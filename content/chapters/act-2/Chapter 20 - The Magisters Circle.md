# Chapter 20 — The Magister's Circle / Path of Trials

## Summary

The party disembarks half a day from the [[Magister's Circle]] and must travel through dangerous forest. At the city gates, they encounter [[Hugo Kerran]] and [[Ashara Everstar]], a princess whose city Sentinel Watch was destroyed by orc sorcerers wielding arcana-based hellfire. The guard refuses entry.

Inside the Circle, the party reunites with [[Ruth]] and [[sheila]]. The two remaining prophecies are shared. [[sheila]] has been tracking Anchor imagery and discovered the [[Valley of Anchors]], where the four anchors were originally held. She reveals the Zeniths contain gem hearts that have been stolen.

The party takes the illegal Path of Trials up the tower -- a series of floating water-slab staircases guarded by massive obsidian winged sentinels. They must defeat wyverns and pass trials of combat (Guardian), faith, and self to reach the Alabaster Seat and ask one question. [[Ashara Everstar|Ashara]] has already used her 3 questions and needs the party as surrogates.
