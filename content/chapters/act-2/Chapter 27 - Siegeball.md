# Chapter 27 — Siegeball

## Summary

The party is teleported back to the Tomb scene, reuniting with [[Captain Liza]], [[Trixie Magnacog|Trixie]], [[Bergs]], and [[Irethia]]. New crew members [[gwendellen]] and [[Boris]] are introduced along with the Red Skipper's crew (including the goliath Hewey and his brothers Dewey and Lewey).

[[Annie Hallowbrook]] formally issues the [[Qual'Xar]] Captain's Challenge, demanding [[Kelik Waystar|Kelik]] as pact-sworn. The Siegeball match is set up on a manicured field between the two camps. Both sides strategize: Liza picks her team of newcomers and deckhands while Annie relies on her practiced crew.

## Key Events

- Party reunited with [[Thornberry Shade]] crew
- Formal Captain's Challenge issued
- Siegeball match preparations
- Crew loyalty and stakes established
