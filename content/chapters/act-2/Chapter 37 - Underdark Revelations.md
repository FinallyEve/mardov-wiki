# Chapter 37 — Underdark Revelations

## Summary

Detailed world-building revelations about the castle (Dvwin'Tar Keep): an Efreeti named [[Ifrin]] is bound there, wanting to free a frozen Phoenix using a fireforged weapon. The 12 [[forgesworn]] items are catalogued (Axe, Breastplate, Shield, Sword, Gauntlets, Boots, Hammer, Helm, Pauldrons, Greaves, Clockwork, Belt) plus the BloodStar meteorite at the ancient site of [[Vag'Mahar]].

Each of the 13 ancient Dueregar fortresses imprisons a Phoenix, used as seals to lock away the First Forge. The Nine Hells have collapsed, dispersing fiends across the planes. The plane of fire is under threat from demons, and Fire Princess Ember Ashborn is missing along with the Pendant of Fire. [[The Sleepless|The Aboleth]] wants to corrupt a Phoenix to achieve parasitic immortality.

The narrative also establishes why [[forgesworn]] are hunted: Thorin Ghost-Eye (Thane of [[Vag'Mahar]]) persecutes them, and the Guilded Rite rebellion exists to protect them.

## Key Events

- 12 [[forgesworn]] items and their known wielders catalogued
- 13 Dueregar fortresses each contain a Phoenix (seals to First Forge)
- Nine Hells collapsed; fiends dispersed across planes
- Plane of fire under threat; Fire Princess missing
- Aboleth seeks Phoenix corruption for immortality
- Efreeti [[Ifrin]] seeks to return to City of Brass
