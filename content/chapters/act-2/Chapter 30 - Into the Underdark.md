# Chapter 30 — Down into the Abyss

## Summary

The Green Tides falls under gnoll attack. Through translation magic, the party learns the gnolls are searching for their captured chieftain [[fa-da-shu]]. An alliance is formed with the gnolls' spiritual leader, Flind Gar-Da-Nar. At the edge of the Underdark bore, [[nyx]] scouts and discovers [[Shi-Shi-Gami]] (the Elk Zodiac) captured by Illithid over a mile below.

Flind plummets into the abyss alone upon learning his chieftain is down there. [[Daina]] pushes [[Nocturnus Wayne|Nocturnus]] and herself after him. The party discovers an Illithid lair with [[Peraxis Phyrexia]], an Illithilich (Illithid Lich), conducting experiments and rituals on captured creatures.

## Key Events

- Gnoll alliance against common Illithid enemy
- [[Shi-Shi-Gami]] captured by Mind Flayers
- Descent into Underdark bore
- [[Peraxis Phyrexia]] revealed as Illithilich ([[Vecna]]'s chosen for the War Zenith)
- [[Daina]] and [[Nocturnus Wayne|Nocturnus]] separated behind enemy lines
