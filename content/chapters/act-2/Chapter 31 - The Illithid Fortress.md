# Chapter 31 — The Illithid Fortress

## Summary

The party discovers the Red Skipper crew imprisoned in the Illithid fortress. [[Annie Hallowbrook]] reveals she was hired by [[Reynardine]] (using a pale tiefling girl [[Xyn]] as messenger) to bring [[Kelik Waystar|Kelik]] to [[windhaven]] in exchange for collapsing the Air Genasi Cloud City and freeing the Water Genasi Princess. The meeting deadline is during the Harvest Festival, 6 weeks away.

Major character-specific plot hooks are established:
- [[nyx]] learns [[Reynardine]] hired Annie
- [[Daina]] discovers [[Peraxis Phyrexia|Peraxis]] has a psychic connection to her father via Thorin Ghost-Eye
- [[Nocturnus Wayne]] learns [[Oblivion Wayne]] (his aunt, the alpha vampire) unleashed Peraxis
- [[brigitte]] receives a psychic note from the stag via the antler
- [[Sprocket]] learns the gnolls have knowledge of True Necromancy (the original 8th circle of magic) and is recognized as "Raven-Touched"

The Alhoons ([[Alhoon Pollivar|Pollivar]] and [[Alhoon Ren|Ren]]) were conducting experiments: vampire/zombie Illithid creation, Mindwitness (beholder forced through ceremorphosis), and studies of the gnolls' connection to True Necromancy.

## Key Events

- Red Skipper crew rescued from prison
- [[Reynardine]]'s manipulation network exposed
- Multiple character-specific plot hooks established
- True Necromancy (original 8th circle) introduced via gnolls
- [[Alhoon Pollivar|Alhoon]] experiments on creating hybrid undead-Illithid creatures
- [[brigitte]] receives Zodiac Locket destiny (requires [[Heart of the Wilde]])
