# Chapter 28 — The Aggregate

## Summary

The party begins traveling back to the shoreline. Along the way, they discover Underdark slime on the surface -- a dangerous sign that underground threats are reaching the overworld. The pathway shows signs of both wagon tracks and mysterious cyclones of leaves. Bones with perfect circles, intact rabbit fur devoid of meat, and silent birds all point to Underdark creatures emerging.

At a bridge, damage suggests previous difficult crossings. The narrative hints at deep gnome villages and gnoll conflicts ahead.

- Underdark contamination reaching the surface

- Signs of underground creatures on the surface
