# Chapter 36 — Lego My Myconid

## Summary

The party splits: one group follows [[Daina]]'s stolen axe (tracking via [[Dierdre Crestfallen|Dierdre]]'s Locate Object), while the other deals with the Drider threat. The axe-hunting group tracks the drider to a bridge over the Purelake where an enormous tentacled creature ([[The Sleepless]], an Aboleth) devours the drider and swallows the axe.

The other group confronts Dusken Wayne's clone. [[Nocturnus Wayne]], gripped by the sword Nightmare's bloodlust, kills his cousin and is thrust into a vision where Dusken explains the dire circumstances of the surviving Drow. Nocturnus is declared King of the Drider. The Myconid Sovereign is rescued from drider captivity.

Key revelations: [[The Sleepless]] (Aboleth, older than gods) has activated Dvwin'Tar Keep using a Blood Star shard, allied with an Efreeti named [[Ifrin]] and Sea Hags. The Aboleth can only be permanently killed by slaying it both in the material plane and the plane of water.

[[Ifrin]] wants [[Daina]]'s fireforged axe to activate the HAMMER beacon, which would mark all [[forgesworn]] with ashen tattoos of their craft. Below the Purelake is a massive lava chamber -- location of the First Forge.
