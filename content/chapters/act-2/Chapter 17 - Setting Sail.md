# Chapter 17 — Setting Sail for the Magister's Circle

## Summary

The party returns to the docks to find [[Captain Liza]] has been selected to take the [[Maturin Pact]], joining the [[Qual'Xar]] -- a maritime order that grants protection from sea creatures, secret ports, and a teleportation circle. The [[Thornberry Shade]] has been upgraded with the Maturin Sigil. Liza warns that traveling with tieflings ([[Tesca]] and [[nyx]]) to the [[Magister's Circle]] will be problematic due to deep prejudice.

Liza reveals a deeply personal secret: she and her brother were spirited to the Fae realm as children and she is not originally from Mardov. She believes tieflings are "Fae-Touched" rather than cursed descendants of [[Tygras]], and the prejudice against them is a tragedy.

The new crew is introduced: [[Nathan Yanagan]] (secretly a spy for Jafar), [[Riley Eversaw]] (mapmaker), [[Dilbert Watacop]] (sarcastic shipwright), [[Kelik Waystar]] (masked wind-controlling navigator), and [[Trixie Magnacog]] (Oswald the Kraken's daughter, portal circle engineer). Below deck, [[Veera Saytrfell]] is a tiefling using glamour magic to appear as an elf, traveling with her son Evan to enlist him at the Magister's Circle.

## Key Events

- [[Captain Liza]] takes the [[Maturin Pact]] / joins [[Qual'Xar]]
- [[Thornberry Shade]] receives Maturin Sigil (sea creature ward)
