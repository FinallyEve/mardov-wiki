# Chapter 24 — Hello From the Other Side

## Summary

Through a painting portal, the party is pulled into [[Vecna]]'s domain within the Tomb -- the "other side" where the air is thick with ashen substance and colors are muted. A voice taunts them: [[Vecna]]'s presence, desperate to use the "Fated" ones marked by the [[Raven Queen]].

The party faces a central room with three levers, each showing stained-glass paintings of their captured allies: [[Trixie Magnacog|Trixie]] slowly disintegrating, [[Captain Liza]] bleeding, and Watcher Fak'Tul with ghosts of his victims ([[M.B. Wells|Dr. M.B. Wells]], Stalin Little, and [[Dallor Dallorbill]]). Each lever requires a colored gemstone (orange, blue, green) collected from other rooms.

Four side rooms reveal hieroglyphic murals representing each of the four Zeniths ([[Forest's Zenith]], [[Mountain's Zenith]], [[Winter's Zenith]], [[Harvest's Zenith]]), each with riddles and detailed imagery hinting at characters, creatures, and events yet to come.

## Key Events

- Party enters [[Vecna]]'s domain within the Tomb
- [[Captain Liza]] revealed to have lost her legs
- [[Dallor Dallorbill]]'s ghost appears (left eye missing, fading)
- Four Zenith rooms with cryptic hieroglyphics
- Gemstone puzzles to unlock rescue paths
- War Room discovered (split between Vecna side and Mardov side)

## Locations Visited

- [[Tomb of the Last Curator]] (Vecna side)
- Four Zenith rooms

## NPCs Encountered

- [[Vecna]] (voice/presence)
- Fak'Tul (hobgoblin captain)
