# Chapter 32 — The Harvest Nightmares (Tiberious's Account III)

## Summary

[[Tiberious D. Rex]], traveling south toward [[windhaven]], experiences a series of prophetic dreams. First, a vision of a Black Hand cradling an unconscious hobgoblin woman ([[Grenda Fak'Tul|Grenda]]). Then a vision from a raven-haired woman with a missing crystalline heart ([[Sabine Ra|Sabine]]) who speaks four words:

- **PESTILENCE**: a small boy in a cold blue aura, tides of foul liquid rising
- **FAMINE**: emissaries of elemental planes (fire woman, wind man, stone boy, water girl) with a man in a top hat ([[Reynardine]])
- **WAR**: the party standing between orc and human armies, with bat-like creatures below and mechanical beings as a buffer
- **DEATH**: a stag and doe upon a locket ripped apart by a skeletal hand

[[Tiberious D. Rex|Tiberious]] manages to contact [[Nocturnus Wayne]] via dream, warning about a naval blockade and informing them of 20,000 gold left at the [[Scarlet Keep]].

## Key Events

- [[Tiberious D. Rex|Tiberious]] receives [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]]'s four-part prophetic vision
- Each anchor visualized: Pestilence (boy/tides), Famine (elemental emissaries), War (armies/bats/automatons), Death (stag and doe locket)
- Dream-contact warning about naval blockade
- 20,000gp left at [[Scarlet Keep]]
