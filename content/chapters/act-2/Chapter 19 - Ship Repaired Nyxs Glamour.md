# Chapter 19 — Ship Repaired, Nyx's Glamour

## Summary

After an orc pirate attack (with a devil among them), the [[Thornberry Shade]] is badly damaged. [[Oswald Magnacog]], the 3rd Kraken of the Sand Seas, arrives to thank the party and grants them the [[Terrapin's Coin]] -- a token that guarantees passage on any [[Qual'Xar]] vessel and can summon aid when thrown into the sea.

Thespus of the Treesingers magically repairs the ship by growing a new mast from an acorn of the Promise Tree. The map room shows a mysterious fire spreading across [[Riley Eversaw|Riley]]'s map from [[Tygras Logath]] northward.

[[nyx]] receives her glamour from [[Veera Saytrfell]] using [[Jorgen]]'s revenant soul (harvested from [[Nathan Yanagan]] who was killed). The glamour transforms her appearance to human, though Jorgen's vengeful voice echoes constantly in the back of her mind ("KILL THE GNOME"). [[Veera Saytrfell|Veera]] connects Nyx to the secret Bardic Circus, an underground school for tiefling mages within the [[Magister's Circle]].

## Key Events

- Ship repaired via Treesinger magic (new mast from Promise Tree acorn)
