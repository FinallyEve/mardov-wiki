# Chapter 33 — Pocket Dimensions (Guest Session)

## Summary

A guest session where the party is split. [[Tiberious D. Rex]], [[Randall]], and [[Daina]] are thrown into [[Reynardine]]'s pocket dimension (a candy-colored realm with chocolate rivers and marshmallow platforms) to rescue [[brigitte]]. Meanwhile, [[Sprocket]], [[Irethia]], [[Nocturnus Wayne]], and [[Tesca]] are summoned to Vodram by [[Varys]] to help Mac and the resistance fighters against V-Mordred's forces.

## Key Events

- Party split across two dimensions
- [[Reynardine]]'s pocket dimension encountered
- Vodram resistance aided
- [[brigitte]] rescued
