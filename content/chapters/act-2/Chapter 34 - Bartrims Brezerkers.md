# Chapter 34 — Bartrim's Brezerkers

## Summary

The party reunites and encounters [[Bartrim Hadrin|Bartrim]]'s Brezerkers, a band of mercenaries traversing the Ways (underground tunnel network). Each member has significant backstory:

- **[[Bartrim Hadrin]]**: Risen from death by [[Tiberious D. Rex]] decades ago; has a diamond where his heart should be, sealed with [[Bahamut]]'s symbol. His wife Evelyn Steelforge was killed by Thorin Ghost-Eye's archer. He cannot sleep and drinks to find peace.
- **[[dune]]**: Earth Genasi royal guard searching for kidnapped Prince Garnet
- **[[Yousef Redvine]]**: Tiefling sorcerer whose wife is [[Veera Saytrfell]]; believes tieflings pre-date [[Tygras]]; has a dragon soul passenger named Redvassyl the Ashen Guard
- **[[Dierdre Crestfallen]]**: [[Bahamut]] cleric who was saved by Bartrim's Platinum Band as a child
- **[[Sylvia Hadrin]]**: Bartrim's daughter, unknowing [[forgesworn]] (her mother was a master armorer)

[[Daina]]'s father's axe is stolen by a drider and tracked to a dueregar fortress. [[Nocturnus Wayne]] kills his cousin Dusken Wayne (leader of the Shattered Drow turned Drider) using the sword Nightmare, inheriting rulership of the surviving drider who chant "All Hail The New King."

## Key Events

- [[Bartrim Hadrin|Bartrim]]'s Brezerkers introduced (rich backstory for each member)
- [[forgesworn]] lore: 12 types of fireforged items, each with a wielder/crafter
- [[Daina]]'s father's axe stolen; chase into the Ways
- [[Nocturnus Wayne]] kills Dusken Wayne; becomes King of the Drider
- Drider/Chitin threat (converting elves into mindless spider-beings)
- [[Yousef Redvine]]'s connection to [[Veera Saytrfell]] revealed
