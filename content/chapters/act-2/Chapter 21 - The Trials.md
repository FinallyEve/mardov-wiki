# Chapter 21 — The Trials

## Summary

Continuation of the Tower of Trials at the [[Magister's Circle]]. The party faces the three keeps: the Keep of the Trial of Friendship (guarded by Brandon Utgar), the Keep of the Trial of Faith (guarded by Landon Utgar), and the Keep of the Trial of Protection (guarded by Trandon Utgar). The Font of Trials restores health to max but not magic slots. [[borris]] the Shield Guardian accompanies them.

The party ultimately reaches the Alabaster Seat, where [[Eon Everstar]] presides.

## Key Events

- Three trials completed (Friendship, Faith, Protection)
- Font of Trials discovered (restores health, not spell slots)
- [[borris]] the Shield Guardian aids the party
- Alabaster Seat reached

## Locations Visited

- Tower of Trials — three keeps
- Alabaster Seat

## NPCs Encountered

- Brandon Utgar — Keep of Friendship
- Landon Utgar — Keep of Faith
- Trandon Utgar — Keep of Protection
- [[Eon Everstar]] — Alabaster Seat
- [[borris]] — Shield Guardian
