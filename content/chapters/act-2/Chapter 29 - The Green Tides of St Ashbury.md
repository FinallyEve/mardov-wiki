# Chapter 29 — The Green Tides of St. Ashbury

## Summary

The party finds themselves at the delta of a mighty river with dead green monstrosities and wreckage from the Red Skipper's caravan. They meet the Sporekeeper, an old deep gnome in a red mushroom hat, who invites them to the Green Tides of St. Ashbury -- a bizarre village of mushroom buildings that are bigger on the inside than outside.

The village appears damaged, with mushroom structures having burst up through former buildings. The party is hosted at an inn run by a one-eyed dwarf. In room 3, a cloaked hobgoblin woman reveals herself as [[Grenda Fak'Tul]] -- the wife of Watcher Fak'Tul and former Black Hand clan chief. She escaped from a prison below using a runed stag horn and warns that the village is not what it appears.

## Key Events

- Deep gnome mushroom village discovered
- [[Grenda Fak'Tul]] appears with warning about the village
- Stag horn connection to the Zodiac
- Shopping at [[Dr. Grakel]]'s Magical Goods
