# Chapter 23 — The Zodiacs Revealed

## Summary

After a druidic ritual with [[Ruth]] and [[sheila]], the party encounters cosmic Zodiac spirits. [[Shi-Shi-Gami]], the Elk Zodiac, has traded his immortality to help the party -- a being of constellations who chose to bet his eternal life on these "fated" champions. [[Manitou]], the Fox Zodiac, arrives to guide them to the Tomb of the Last Curator at the [[Valley of Anchors]].

Key zodiac lore: these cosmic animals serve the [[Raven Queen]]; they can gift powers to mortals; betting on the right champions during [[Gard'Daemon]] means ascension, but failing means eternal death. Other zodiacs who shed immortal form include the Serpent, the Turtle, and Osiris.

The party finds a bridge teeming with hobgoblins. An ogre smashes the bridge. Massive battle ensues with hobgoblins literally raining from the sky. [[Stalwart Little]] captures and brutally interrogates a prisoner, learning the [[Thornberry Shade]] crew ([[Trixie Magnacog|Trixie]], [[M.B. Wells|Dr. M.B. Wells]], and [[Kelik Waystar|Kelik]]) were captured.

[[Sprocket]] charges to rescue [[Trixie Magnacog|Trixie]]. A plan involving 1,000 ball bearings and a pacifist plant works perfectly. But the hobgoblin captain Fak'Tul slits [[M.B. Wells|Dr. M.B. Wells]]'s throat and drags Trixie deeper into the tomb.

## Key Events

- Zodiac spirits revealed ([[Shi-Shi-Gami|Elk]] and [[Manitou|Fox]])
- Zodiacs explained: cosmic animals betting immortality on champions during [[Gard'Daemon]]
- Bridge battle with hobgoblin army
- [[M.B. Wells|Dr. M.B. Wells]] killed by hobgoblin captain Fak'Tul
- [[Trixie Magnacog|Trixie]] captured deeper in tomb
