# Chapter 25 — The Tomb's Secrets

## Summary

The party encounters Fak'Tul performing a terrible ritual -- carving off his own hand and gouging out his eye as sacrifice to [[Vecna]] to bring back his dead wife Grenda and her sisters. He becomes a revenant and must be defeated.

Speak-with-Dead circles allow communion with the fallen: [[M.B. Wells|Dr. M.B. Wells]] asks to have his soul transcribed into his book (succeeding with a DC 20 check), leaving it to [[Sprocket]]. He confirms the City of Snake People exists and the Tomb at the [[Valley of Anchors]] is real. Stalin Little tells [[Stalwart Little|Stalwart]] about the [[Sunless Citadel]] existing in a desert where they worship a serpent, and to take Fak'Tul's [[Boots of Haste]]. [[Dallor Dallorbill]]'s spirit is barely there, left eye missing, form fading.

The hieroglyphics in each Zenith room are fully detailed, providing cryptic visual prophecies for [[Forest's Zenith|Forest]] ([[Sabine Ra|Sabine]]/Ramses/[[Xerxes]] love triangle, the elk and doe, the rabbit zodiac), [[Mountain's Zenith|Mountain]] (dwarven city with 3 walls, the forge, a frozen volcano, clashing armies), [[Winter's Zenith|Winter]] (serpent, dragonborn tribe, dormant turtle zodiac, white dragon, sunless citadel), and [[Harvest's Zenith|Harvest]] (upside-down city, white tiefling girl, fox zodiac, burned fields).
