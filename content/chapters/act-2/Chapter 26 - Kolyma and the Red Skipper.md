# Chapter 26 — Kolyma and the Red Skipper

## Summary

Through the Rod of Sanctuary, the party meets [[Kolyma]], the Last Curator -- a massive bipedal mammoth elder from an ancient race (Mammothborn) eradicated by [[Tiamat]] before the Zeniths were created. He instructs them to place the rod at the entrance of a Zenith.

At the [[Valley of Anchors]], Captain [[Annie Hallowbrook]] of the Red Skipper arrives with her crew. Under [[Qual'Xar]] law, since the [[Thornberry Shade]] lost its oldest crew member ([[M.B. Wells|Dr. Wells]]), Annie can challenge for crew members. She demands a 4v4 Siegeball match. [[Captain Liza]] (now crippled, having lost her legs) accepts, naming [[Malcom Tread]] as pact-sworn.

Behind the scenes: [[Malcom Tread]] is creating a Triton Transmutation requiring exotic ingredients, and Annie/Malcom want [[Kelik Waystar|Kelik]] because he's secretly an air genasi prince whose capture would help free a water genasi princess.
