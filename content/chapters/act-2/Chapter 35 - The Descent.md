# Chapter 35 — The Descent

## Summary

Suspended in the hot air balloon (Chief Grinklesprocket's Magnificent Descension and Ascension Machine), the party and [[Bartrim Hadrin|Bartrim]]'s Brezerkers trade information. [[Bartrim Hadrin|Bartrim]] reveals his full history: the Platinum Band, his death at a [[Sunless Citadel]], resurrection by [[Tiberious D. Rex]], marriage to Evelyn Steelforge, and exile after Ghost-Eye killed his wife.

Deeper in the Ways, they discover Pestilence has flooded through tunnels, contaminating the environment. A treasury room yields powerful items including a Broom of Flying, various scrolls, and gems. The party also discovers a hot air balloon system for traversing the deep shafts. Chitine and Drider ambush the party as they navigate the pestilence-contaminated tunnels.

## Key Events

- Full backstory exchange between party and Brezerkers
- Pestilence contamination in the Ways
- Treasury discovered (powerful magic items)
- Hot air balloon navigation system
- Chitin/Drider ambush

## Locations Visited

- The Ways (Underdark tunnel network)
- Hot air balloon shaft
