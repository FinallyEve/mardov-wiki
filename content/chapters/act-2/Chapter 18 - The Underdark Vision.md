# Chapter 18 — The Underdark Vision (Tiberious's Account II)

## Summary

Again narrated by [[Tiberious D. Rex]] in prayer to [[Bahamut]], the party encounters horrors in the Underdark: a Dueregar woman bearing [[Tiamat]]'s symbol who attacks with a wall of wind, a young drow boy seeking sanctuary from his possessed older brother, and a Mind Flayer that invades their minds.

The pivotal discovery is a teleportation circle in a cramped study. Sleeping within it triggers a vision of an army of Dueregar stretching as far as the eye can see, worshipping a deity in unison. [[Tiberious D. Rex|Tiberious]] recognizes this as evidence of the final war and departs to alert the Silver Scales clergy, leaving the party once more.

## Key Events

- Dueregar [[Tiamat]] worshipper encountered
- Mind Flayer encounter (psychic attacks)
- Teleportation circle discovered in Underdark
- Apocalyptic vision: massive Dueregar army worshipping unknown deity
- [[Tiberious D. Rex|Tiberious]] departs to alert the clergy about the coming war

## Locations Visited

- Underdark tunnels
- Dueregar chambers

## NPCs Encountered

- [[Tiberious D. Rex]] — narrator

## DM Notes

- The Dueregar army vision foreshadows the War Zenith conflict
- Tiamat cult activity among the Dueregar connects to Oblivion Wayne's plots
