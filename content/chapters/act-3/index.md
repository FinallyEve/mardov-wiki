# Act 3 — The Zenith Wars

> Chapters 42-87 plus Reunion. The campaign splits into two concurrent party tracks: **Dwarves** (focused on [[Vag'Mahar]] and the [[Mountain's Zenith]]) and **Tieflings** (focused on [[DawnHaven]], [[Cloud City]], and the Fae/Harvest storyline). Around Chapter 67, the Tiefling track is renamed to **"The Distillery"** (referencing the location in [[vartos]]).

## Party Structure

- **Dwarves**: [[Daina]], [[Bergs]], [[Nocturnus Wayne]] and allies — exploring [[Vag'Mahar]], the Underdark, and the [[Mountain's Zenith]]
- **Tieflings** (later "The Distillery"): [[nyx]], [[Irethia]], [[Tesca]], [[Randall]] and allies — exploring [[DawnHaven]], [[Cloud City]], the Fae, and eventually the Distillery in [[vartos]]
