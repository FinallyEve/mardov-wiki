# Chapter 11 — Batman for Mayor

## Summary

Election day arrives in [[argos]]. [[mandu]] and the [[Golden Reach]] ([[danelle]] and [[markus]]) returned most of the missing children, claiming owlbears took them, and used this heroism to seize political positions. The party discovers that Timmy's soul has been weakened by the hags -- [[cerci]] explains that 4-year-olds are developing their souls and Timmy's has been forged like an iron ingot in fire. She gives him a dreamcatcher to watch for the Night Hag's return.

The party races to save Timmy from a Night Hag's astral projection attempting to steal his soul. Using the dreamcatcher like a flashlight to reveal the astral plane, they drive off the hag. At the Festival of Light, [[Stalwart Little]]'s divine sense reveals [[markus]] and [[danelle]] as fiends (incubus and succubus). They are exposed and flee to the Old Forest. [[Nocturnus Wayne]] stuffs the ballot box.

Returning to the asylum, the party's previous entrance is trapped with Indiana Jones-style boulders. They fight through wights but then deliberately trigger the boulder trap multiple times to seal themselves in a cell for a long rest -- effectively burying themselves alive.
