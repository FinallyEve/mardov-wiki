# Chapter 7 — Night of the WereMoth

## Summary

The party completes the [[Hall of the Raven]]. In the study, [[Sprocket]] uses a vision from [[Sabine Ra]] to find and shatter [[Mordred]]'s original phylactery. A voice identified as Ramses begs them to take his crystal to the [[Forest's Zenith]] so he can finally die. The four liquids are combined at the altar in the ritual order (red, orange, blue, green), and with a drop of blood from each afflicted member, warmth returns -- the cleansing is complete.

Outside, the party meets a hooded [[Finkle Hollingsworth]], now freed from [[Mordred]]'s possession. He offers passage to [[argos]] aboard [[Captain Liza]]'s riverboat the [[Thornberry Shade]], promises them the [[Scarlet Keep]] as a base of operations, and 250gp each for delivering the [[Heart of the Wilde]] (a massive emerald he stole from the [[Forest's Zenith]]).

The riverboat journey becomes a murder mystery when First Mate Gordon is found dead in a silk cocoon. The [[Heart of the Wilde]] is stolen. Through investigation, the party uncovers multiple plots: Ace (really Chuck Wheeler) stole the gem and hid it in his ten-gallon hat; the [[Don Manauchi]] and [[Don Damiachi]] planned to kidnap [[sally]] (a [[machu]] princess); and a WereMoth -- Sally's royal bloodline protector activated by [[cerci]]'s dreamcatcher wards -- killed Gordon and others who threatened Sally.

## Key Events

- Cleansing ritual completed; party freed from [[Mordred]]'s curse
- Ramses's crystal recovered; begs to be taken to [[Forest's Zenith]]
- [[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]] returns as ally; offers [[Scarlet Keep]] and [[Heart of the Wilde]] delivery job
- Boarding the [[Thornberry Shade]] with [[Captain Liza]]
- [[Jorgen]]'s revenant appears chanting "Kill the gnome"
- Murder mystery: Gordon killed, [[Heart of the Wilde]] stolen
- WereMoth revealed as [[sally]]'s royal protector
- Ace/Chuck Wheeler exposed as gem thief
- Dons exposed as kidnappers hired by Jafar ([[sally]]'s uncle)
