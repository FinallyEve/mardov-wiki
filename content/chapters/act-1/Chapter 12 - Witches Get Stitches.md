# Chapter 12 — Witches Get Stitches

## Summary

Trapped in their self-made tomb, the party hears the hags gloating. They learn critical intelligence: the hags are brewing "Pestilence" from corrupted children's souls, and [[markus]]/[[danelle]] need it to "sunder the Winter's Anchor." The party uses synchronized shatter spells to redirect boulders onto one of the hags, crushing her.

After defeating the remaining hag (capturing her broom and forcing a deal for information), they learn: the black liquid is Pestilence (corrupt souls); the hags were recruited by [[Mordred]] through [[danelle]] and [[markus]]; [[Mordred]] has issued a bounty on the awakened; and the Pestilence is needed for the [[Winter's Zenith]].

The captured hag delivers the **SECOND PROPHECY**. In the kindergarten, [[Daina]] uses the Night Hag's soul bag to extract crystalline soul shards and revive the comatose children. [[Don Damiachi]] is found transformed into a dog with a split tongue. The party escapes with the children, brooms, and a transformed Don.
