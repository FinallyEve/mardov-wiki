# Chapter 1 — The Sewer Ritual

## Summary

The party descends into the sewers of the Middle Ward. [[Jorgen]] leads ahead but the door slams shut behind him, leaving the party in darkness with kobolds. [[Stalwart Little]] uses Draconic to keep the kobolds at bay despite their eerie chanting of "SCARLET, SCARLET, SCARLET." The party finds a room with a blood rune button that starts a 180-second countdown -- the ceiling descends while kobolds are thrust into the room each time the button is pressed.

Surviving the ceiling trap, some members phase through a wall marked "RUN LEFT" into the ritual chamber, while others descend a spiral staircase. They discover [[stromble]] and [[grom]] in a control room pulling levers, clearly caught off guard and lying about their presence. The ritual chamber is massive with four endless waterfalls of colored liquid (red blood, green poison, orange mercury, blue liquid souls) and a large phylactery of the [[Raven Queen]] on a gear-like pedestal.

[[Dallor Dallorbill]] had earlier pickpocketed a note from [[Jorgen]] showing [[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]] knew the recruits' arrival time and details -- evidence of a setup. The ritual is completed and a bright light engulfs the room, with the symbol of [[Vecna]]/the [[Raven Queen]] being the last thing seen.

## Key Events

- Party discovers the sewers are a trap designed by [[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]]
- Blood rune button puzzle with descending ceiling
- Discovery that Finkle's henchmen were priming the ritual chamber
- [[Jorgen]] revealed as complicit in leading parties to their deaths
- Ritual of Life chamber found with four streams of magical liquid
- Party completes the ritual; blinding light engulfs the room

## Character Moments

- [[Stalwart Little]] speaks Draconic to the kobolds, establishing his dragonborn heritage
- [[Dallor Dallorbill]] pickpockets Jorgen -- his thieving nature yields critical intelligence
