# Chapter 9 — Never Trust a Hat

## Summary

The party settles into the [[Scarlet Keep]]. [[Tiberious D. Rex]], a cleric of [[Bahamut]], leaves a note confessing to secretly keeping 100gp and leaving healing potions as penance. [[argos]] is under nightly siege by undead at the East and North gates, with the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] down from 100 to 40 members.

[[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]] assigns the party to reinforce the East Gate. They learn that [[cerci]] and [[sally]] have taken up residence in the Keep, and Sally went missing near the North Gate two days ago. Lord Fartheshire's children Hansel and Grettle have also been kidnapped -- they are Underdark royalty with the ability to sense bores into the Underdark.

At the East Gate, the party survives a massive battle with wraiths and wights. [[sheila]] the Witch Hunter arrives on horseback with a radiant potion that drives the undead away. She reveals her backstory: her teacher [[Ruth]], the greatest diviner in the realm, sacrificed herself by willingly wearing a cursed witching hat to save Sheila. Sheila has spent 5 years hunting witches to find a cure (running water can knock the hat off).

The party finds Ruth's hut, uses [[brigitte]]'s rainstorm to remove the hat, and rescues Hansel and Grettle from a ritual involving [[Mordred]]'s fiery gaze. [[Ruth]] delivers the first prophecy.

## Key Events

- [[Scarlet Keep]] established as party's base
- [[argos]] under nightly undead siege (down to ~40 defenders)
- Lord Fartheshire's children (Underdark royalty) kidnapped
- Battle at the East Gate with wraiths and wights
- [[sheila]] the Witch Hunter arrives as ally
- [[Ruth]] freed from cursed witching hat
- Hansel and Grettle rescued from [[Mordred]]'s ritual
- **FIRST PROPHECY** delivered by [[Ruth]]
