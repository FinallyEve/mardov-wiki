# Chapter 2 — The Gnome Eff'n Way

## Summary

[[Finkle Hollingsworth]] reveals his true nature. His body contorts into a hollow, twisted, gaunt figure -- he is actually [[Mordred]], a lich who has been using the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] as a soul-harvesting operation. He has collected 2,000 adventurer souls to fuel his power, creating demand for adventure to lure more victims. He commands [[Jorgen]] to be killed for outliving his usefulness.

The party discovers that the large crystalline object in the ritual chamber is a Demi-Lich skull -- [[Sabine Ra]], trapped inside a phylactery. [[Sprocket]] touches it with mage hand and enters a trance. The party manages to shatter the phylactery despite Mordred's fury, freeing Sabine. The freed Demi-Lich turns Mordred's own ghouls against him and forces him to flee.

However, the party falls in combat. In death, they feel an anchor pulling them back. [[Sabine Ra]] sacrifices her newly restored life to revive them, depositing a crystalline heart in each member -- fragments of her own soul. The party awakens in different places and times, reborn at level 2.

## Key Events

- [[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]] revealed as [[Mordred]], an ancient lich
- [[Mordred]] has harvested 2,000 souls via the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]]
- Party manages to shatter [[Sabine Ra]]'s phylactery
- [[Sabine Ra]] freed, turns on [[Mordred]]
- Entire party killed by [[Mordred]]
- [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] sacrifices herself to resurrect the party with crystalline hearts
- Party reborn at level 2

## DM Notes

- Mordred is actually Ramses, Sabine's former teacher who forced lichdom upon her
- The 2,000 harvested souls fuel Mordred's power and his army of ghouls
- This chapter establishes the campaign's core premise: the party collectively IS Sabine Ra
