# Chapter 13 — Viva La Revolution

## Summary

[[argos]] erupts into civil war. [[vikrum]] (re-elected Minister of Justice) uses propaganda to paint the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] as demons. [[dolf]] the blacksmith rallies supporters to defend the Keep while the party slips out to confront [[mandu]]. [[Jorgen]]'s revenant reappears, heading toward the [[Scarlet Keep]] chanting "kill the gnome."

Inside Mandu's manor, the party finds [[markus]] and [[danelle]] (in demon form) tormenting a petrified orc woman -- [[Mia]], the Earth Mother and last of the Argosian Woods-Witches. They are trying to force a divination from her. MIA was forced to wipe the party's memories at the start of the campaign (explaining why they don't remember joining the Brigade). The Night Hag Tabitha is freed from the astral plane by Markus but the party defeats all three enemies.

[[Mia]] delivers the **THIRD PROPHECY** -- four verses about the four anchors.

## Key Events

- Civil war in [[argos]] between pro- and anti-[[Scarlet Tunic Brigade|STB]] factions
- [[Jorgen]]'s revenant returns (again)
- [[markus]] (incubus) and [[danelle]] (succubus) defeated
- [[Mia]] freed from petrification
- Revelation: [[Mia]] wiped party's memories at campaign start
- **THIRD PROPHECY**: Four verses about the four Anchors ([[Anchor of Famine|Famine]], [[Anchor of Pestilence|Pestilence]], [[Anchor of War|War]], [[Anchor of Death|Death]])
- [[Bergs]] fires a ballista bolt into the forest (natural 16)

## Character Moments

- [[dolf]] the blacksmith rallies common folk to defend the party -- the seeds of revolution
- [[Mia]] reveals she was forced to erase the party's memories, adding moral complexity
