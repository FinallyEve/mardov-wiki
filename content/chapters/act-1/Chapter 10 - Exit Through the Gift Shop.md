# Chapter 10 — Exit Through the Gift Shop, Don't Forget Your Owlbear

## Summary

Two new adventurers, [[Randall]] and [[Tesca]], arrive and are absorbed into the Brigade. Over 100 children have been kidnapped over 2 years, all aged 4. [[mandu]], the corrupt Captain of the Argos Guard, controls the defense budget through bribes.

[[Don Mononuchi]] returns with a deal: he'll lead the party to an asylum where the children and captured [[Artificer's Guild]] soldiers are held, if they rescue [[Don Damiachi]]. The Don explains how his partner was snatched by the WereMoth, rescued by Artificer's Guild soldiers, then captured again when hags attacked the soldiers' camp.

The party infiltrates through an owlbear den, killing Papa Bear (death by monk punch from [[Tesca]]). Using Don Mononuchi's magic violin case containing a summoning circle, they conjure exactly 4 magmin to blast through the wall into the asylum's second basement. They rescue [[Artificer's Guild]] soldiers who reveal [[Xerxes]] has been overtaken by [[Mordred]]'s necrotic madness.
