# Chapter 4 — As the Raven Flies

## Summary

The party awakens in the [[Artificer's Guild]] mobile headquarters, rescued by [[Xerxes]] through teleportation gates in the arena. [[Xerxes]] reveals himself as a 500-year-old being made of living organisms -- moss, plants, countless small lives that inherit his memories and soul. He explains the full history:

[[Sabine Ra]] was the avatar of the [[Raven Queen]] who founded two colleges of magic: the [[Circle of the Murder]] (necromancy/death) and the [[Artificer's Guild]] (animancy/life). She entrusted these to [[Xerxes]] (her husband) and Ramses (her teacher). Ramses grew jealous and attempted to grant both himself and Sabine eternal life through lichdom. When Sabine rejected the ritual, Ramses forced it upon her. Sabine starved her lich form by refusing to consume souls, crystallizing into the Demi-Lich the party found.

When the party shattered [[Mordred]]'s phylactery and completed the ritual, they inadvertently freed Sabine. She used her remaining power to revive the party at the cost of her own existence, depositing crystalline heart fragments in each of them. The party collectively IS [[Sabine Ra]] now. [[Mordred]] seeks to gather these pieces to forcibly resurrect Sabine under his control.

[[Finkle Hollingsworth]] was merely a vessel -- he was possessed when he explored the [[Forest's Zenith]] and activated the seal to Ramses's prison.

## Key Events

- Full exposition of [[Sabine Ra]], [[Xerxes]], and Ramses/[[Mordred]]'s history
- Party learns they carry fragments of [[Sabine Ra]]'s soul (crystalline hearts)
- [[Mordred]] revealed as Ramses, Sabine's former teacher
- [[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]] was an innocent vessel, possessed by Ramses/Mordred
- [[Artificer's Guild]] mobile HQ travels via portals (4 at a time, color-coded)

## Character Moments
