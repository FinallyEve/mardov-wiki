# Chapter 14 — Tiberious's Account

## Summary

Told from [[Tiberious D. Rex]]'s perspective as a prayer to [[Bahamut]], this chapter covers lighter side quests in [[argos]]: finding lost chickens for Farmer Black, entertaining a bored child, capturing a mysterious forest creature ("Bigfoot") for scientist Ghak, and a treasure hunt from a deciphered pirate map leading to an old granite quarry guarded by restless skeletons.

The treasure hunt yields gold and provides character moments for the party as they solve the cipher and clear undead from the quarry. [[Tiberious D. Rex|Tiberious]]'s narration provides comic relief with his obsession with platinum over gold and his bumbling but ultimately effective approach to problem-solving.

## Key Events

- Community side quests completed in [[argos]]
- Bigfoot creature captured for research
- Treasure quarry cleared of undead
- Character building and lighter fare between major arcs
