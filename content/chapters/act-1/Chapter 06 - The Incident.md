# Chapter 6 — The Incident

## Summary

Tiny Rick's time runs out. As his twin souls pour out -- green ([[Mordred]]) and blue (Rick) -- a loud crack fractures reality. [[Varys]], a rebel from another dimension (where [[Vecna]] rules instead of the [[Raven Queen]] and there is no [[Artificer's Guild]]), appears while [[Nocturnus Wayne]] vanishes without a trace.

The party enters the [[Hall of the Raven]] foyer where they find "[[Daina]]" and "Brock Hammer" who claim to have escaped [[Mordred]]. These are actually dopplegangers sent to capture the awakened and steal their crystalline hearts. The party is fooled and enters the cultist lair, discovering a dais with instructions for a ritual requiring colored liquids and blood.

Through a series of rooms with spotlight teleportation and shadow mechanics, the party is separated and paired off. The dopplegangers replace several members through combat in the "Chamber of Shadows" judged by the mechanical watcher [[xj-5]]. Eventually the deception is uncovered when the real [[Daina]] calls out from a cell, and the dopplegangers are defeated.

A massive Gorgon is encountered. Tiny Rick is petrified. The party uses illusions to trick the Gorgon into breathing on a stone dais, activating the Ritual of the Talon. [[Brock Hammer]] and [[corramperre]] each gain a stone talon hand.
