# Chapter 3 — The Murder Circle (Act Un-Natural)

## Summary

The party awakens shackled in holding cells beneath a colosseum, their bodies haggard with spider-tracks of coagulated blood but eyes showing dim natural light rather than undead blue. They have been converted to ghouls by [[Mordred]] but had their consciousness preserved by [[Sabine Ra]]'s gift. [[Mordred]] announces a gladiator tournament to choose his next vessel, offering a drop from his phylactery to the winner.

[[diana]] discovers a letter from [[Xerxes]], Grand Inquisitor of the [[Artificer's Guild]], hidden on her person. It reveals that the Guild has been working to free the awakened and that one of them MUST win the soul tether. However, Diana accidentally reveals the [[Artificer's Guild]] sigil, drawing Mordred's wrath -- he forcibly removes her consciousness and levitates her body into the crowd.

The gladiator battle proceeds while an [[Artificer's Guild]] assault begins in the background. The winner receives a drop from Mordred's phylactery, creating an invisible thread connecting their soul to the vial.

## Key Events

- Party awakens as "awakened" undead in [[Mordred]]'s colosseum
- 2,000 ghouls serve as audience
- Letter from [[Xerxes]] reveals [[Artificer's Guild]] rescue plan
- [[diana]] reveals the sigil and is taken by [[Mordred]]
- Gladiator battle with other risen adventurers
- Soul tether awarded via phylactery drop
- [[Artificer's Guild]] assault on the arena begins

## Character Moments

- The party faces the horror of being undead but conscious, testing their resolve
- Diana's sacrifice demonstrates the stakes of the Artificer's Guild rebellion
