# Chapter 5 — A Matter of Time

## Summary

This chapter recaps and expands on the arena rescue. Five teleportation gates were set up for the [[Artificer's Guild]] invasion, but only three remained active. [[corramperre]] and [[Bergs]] gave their lives delivering [[Sabine Ra]]'s amplification hexbags to the awakened. [[Xerxes]] arrived and [[Mordred]], forced to declare an heir, drenched Tiny Rick with his last phylactery contents -- beginning the process of Tiny Rick's soul being consumed.

The party was freed through the gates and awakened in the Guild's HQ. They must now travel through the Red Door to the [[Hall of the Raven]] to create their own phylactery to contain the lich soul consuming Tiny Rick, and complete a ritual to cleanse themselves of [[Mordred]]'s influence.

At the entrance to the [[Hall of the Raven]], the party encounters a graveyard. [[nyx]] lights a brazier that summons undead from their graves. Using warding torches, they fight through the zombie horde, with [[brigitte]] taking wolf form and [[Sprocket]] using mage hand. [[Dallor Dallorbill]] characteristically loots coins from the zombies' eyes.
