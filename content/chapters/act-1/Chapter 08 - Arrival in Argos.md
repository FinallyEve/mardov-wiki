# Chapter 8 — Arrival in Argos

## Summary

The riverboat journey continues with the full resolution of the murder mystery. The party battles and defeats [[Jorgen]]'s revenant through a combination of [[Sprocket]]'s fireball (cauterizing wounds to prevent regeneration) and [[Nocturnus Wayne]] knocking him off the boat. The WereMoth mystery is solved: the dreamcatcher wards protect [[sally]] from anyone with harmful intent, transforming her shadow into the moth creature.

A giant octopus attacks the ship, drawn by the [[Heart of the Wilde]]'s power to attract creatures. [[Dallor Dallorbill]] drives it off in a rare moment of heroism. The Dons escape through a hole made by the octopus.

The party arrives in [[argos]] with the [[Heart of the Wilde]] and the [[Scarlet Keep]] as their new base of operations.

## Key Events
