**DnD plots**

Reynaldine is trying to use Tiamat against Asmodeus to save himself AND Gabriel at the stone table. The quest to go and open the winters zenith was ultimately to get her kicked out and force her to use the winter fey entrance, directly to him. He also knew the last seal of Asmodeus was there so they could use that to give Gratz the power to ascend and stand a chance against the goddess

What he didn't plan on is them forging a new pillar of Harmony within Bahamuts domain which warrants an another arch angel, Nocturnus Wayne, Archangel of Harmony, Ender of Oblivion and Fated to kill Ashara Everstar (Tiamats host)

At the same time, he didn't anticipate that Rumblesin would pick Josh as his knight and thought it would be Sprocket, Josh being too pure of heart is going to be a problem for him as well.

A new archangel means the possibility to create a new seal, thus "Ender of Oblivion". Josh getting the reins of the feywilde means they have the ability to stop both the Harvest and Winter Zenith from being in play. However though Bergs has the seal of Asmodeus, and I plan for him to get away, I plan on infecting the Jury (his mushrooms) within Oblivion granting him access once they enter the Havests Zenith.

Creating the seal requires an arch angel take their seat within a Zenith, meaning the Forest Zenith is on Nocturnus' radar.

Party is going to fight Gabriel while Gratz is distracted. Then it's a matter of which of the 3 do they want to travel to?

Tiamat is getting them to the stone table by fighting Kelik over the Convergence and instead of using Lapis like she wanted, he will end up putting her right at the stone table as that's halfway between them.

Vecna is the one who killed the Amethyst Dragon and the sphinx. He's going to use that to tip the battle in Tiamats favor. His goal is to get her to kill Bahamut AND Oblivion. He wants to pick up the pieces of both of these deities because they are tied to the sealing technique used in the Zeniths. A fate he fears that the Raven Queen is saving for him.
