Exorcism via Sword

Lolth the Goddess of Lies, taunts the party drawing Nocturnus near enough for an unlikely hero to quickly thwart her plan and immediately use the Ruby Red Rapier of Cleansing left in Barachiel the Righteous’ care to snap the Man Who Would Be King out of his Fated Nightmare! ### Slide 13

Nightmares

The rest of the party is thrust back into the prison that held Nocturnus when they arrived. This nightmare is of Nocturnus’ childhood home, surrounding him there are coffins neatly arranged in a circle, one for each of his family members. This traumatic experience is only undercut by the faint whispers of Nocturnus answering the same question over and over again spoken by his ancestors:“What do we do when we fall?”

“We pick ourselves back up”

Of course that says nothing about the giant skeletal spider that strangely has a swirl of elementals in its thorax where the webbing would be stored?! But not all of them, once Brigit releases the elemental seals back at the treasury however, then the party is freed from their Arachnophobia
