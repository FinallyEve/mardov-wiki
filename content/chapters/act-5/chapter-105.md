### Slide 9

Sometimes You Just Gotta Go Swimming!

Midfight, frustrated at the maddening darkness used to steal the Maghar’s boons. Daina took a leap of faith after seeing the good that Lilly was able to do with the Lava-Swimming boon. If Lilly was able to cleanse the corruption on her back, perhaps Daina could use the network of magma tunnels to head back into the Zenith the way she had the first time. Instead she found herself at the First Forge, but she was not alone. Her father, Thorkon Fireforge and a ghostly image of Reginald were here as well. However this was just the place required to ascend the Anchor of War, and doing so drained the Caldera around the Mountain’s Zenith!?

Plunging the Anchor of War into the lone bonfire, though the Iron Doors keeping Therzadun Chained continued to bang, Daina helped her father hold the door, until it was suddenly stilled?

RIP the OG

Back at the Stone Sanctum, Peraxis and the Vellem-fighters waited in the wings while the whole Sanctum was covered in the darkness that only madness can conjure. His sister nowhere to be seen, Nocturnus blowing up the darkness with his last radiant light bombs, and Lilly still grappling with her corruption, Bergs relied on his recently re-deceased friend to make up for his crippling spore-shambling and closed the gap on Reginald Providence, one of the original four dwarves made from the Sacrifice of Ilmater and Lich of Therzadun. All at once the darkness was dispelled and with a frenzied swing of the Maul of the Phoenix, Reginald dissolved into but a mere tadpole?!
