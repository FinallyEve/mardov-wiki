### Slide 10

What Is the Man Who Wields Nightmare Scared Of?

We opened on a flashback to Nocturnus Wayne’s childhood, the prince of Drow was forced by his parents to learn an ancient discipline of the Four Elements that had been passed down his family line for generations. Though they had lost their original elemental connection as a people, the Wayne’s kept the tradition, instilling the need to learn everything four times in their children:

With the Passion of Fire

With the Calm Power of Water

With the Cool Breeze of Air at their back

With the Subtle Confidence of Earth

All pleasantries ended with poor Nocturnus reliving the night he was cursed to live the rest of his life under the stars instead of mountain.

### Slide 12

Through the Hidden Keyhole

On top of stumbling on the Pride of Da’ Gahar, Sprocket also wondered between the Throne Debaters to socket Reginald Providence’s Demiplane Tuning Fork into its hidden location behind the Throne, revealing his workshop at a place modeled after the Heart Room of the Mountain’s Zenith!? This hidden workshop existed everywhere but the Ethereal Plane like the original. It contained three Fey Iron Spikes and a ritualistic circle in the center of the room where the runoff from four prisoner racks on the walls would converge. Gruesome stuff for someone who became a lich too recently for this to have been set up afterwards.

### Slide 13

We Can’t NOT Open It… Right?

Brigit, Casaval and Varis recruited Nocturnus to help them trigger the Necrotic, Radiant, Fire, Earth, Air and Water Runes on the sarcophagus and released the strange katsuragi sword held within. The sword, named Bane, was sealed away by Lolth specifically so that it would latch onto her chosen and NEVER let go. Despite Varis’ warning to “use protection son,” Nocturnus took to his fate and the sentient magic sword intertwined with his very being.

### Slide 14

Thorkon’s Folly

Meanwhile Daina discovered the dagger that was used to frame her family for the murder of King Thorin Bronzebeard II. The dagger is of Elven make and bears the Moth sigil of the royal house of Machu. Clearly concerned that her father never even made the thing, her suspicions were confirmed after a disappointing interview with her father’s Maghar spirit. The dagger is named Death’s Shadow.

### Slide 16

The Stewardess Becomes the Regent

Timekeeper turned Craftkeeper, Theodosia Garbendan arrived after the majority of the battle at the mines had concluded. After the party describes their next steps to her, she makes it her first point of order as regent to hold council from an envoy from The Court of Bones: Dune?!

### Slide 17

A Begrudging Arrest

Dune insists that the party come with him to the Court of Bones to answer their summons. Despite him knowing that they are heroes and their good intentions, he warns them that if they wait too long the entire Earth Genasi Civilization on Golemdrumn’s back will drown! At this time, Yuri the Jailor, Fen’s Patron, chimes in with a bombshell that in three days, the Elemental Evils will converge, die and suck the entire material plane through to the Feywilde, leaving just the Shadowfell as the sole shard of the plane outside the Autumn Court. This will in practice achieve Reynaldine’s dream of Godlike power, but with him weakened by the Right of the Stone Table, another might try and seize that power. Another like Grazz’t.
