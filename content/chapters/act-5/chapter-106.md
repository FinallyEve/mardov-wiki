### Slide 5

Champion of Dusk

We cut to Joshua Coolbreeze, Champion of Dusk, in the middle of an assault on the ruins of old Uma at the Towers of Twilight. However, he and the Autumn Court were betrayed by their newest recruits Silence and his mother Umbara Skuttle, who used an air elemental attack to ambush Sunlight in a Springtime Glade, killing the Tabaxi turned Tiefling. Using some ancient Right of the Stone Table, Umbara was able to cripple Reynaldine, forcing Josh to find his Commanding Officer, Varis Laidon, Knight of the Dusken Wood!

Reynaldine’s cloak lost its leaves

the Stone Table is where the Diadem of the Dawn was used to bring peace to Autumn and Spring
