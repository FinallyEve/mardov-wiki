### Slide 14

Time Is Of the Essence

In the midst of casting Transport Via Plants, Brigit was interrupted by St. Constantine of the Yellow Gnoll, the Gynosphinx and Keeper of Time. She agrees to ferry the three Progenitors: Breeze, Cycle and Blossom. However, Constantine gives Brigit a warning that she is a Harbinger and/or a Protector. So, even in the midst of an attack by Vecna, himself on her Lair, Constantine agrees to save the spell until it is the correct time. But that there was another soul in the Magister’s Circle she needed to meet. Who could it be?

### Slide 15

An Oath of Vengeance

Stalwart Little in the presence and earshot of The Alabaster Seat, Sprocket Overspark, doubled down on his Oath:

“This Oath of Vengeance I solemnly swear, whomever the Alabaster Seat shows me who is responsible shall die for their part in Stewart’s death” ~ Stalwart

Unfortunately, his request was granted and he was shown a vision of all who were there that day: The Abishai, Ashara Everstar, Tezka, HAL, Daina, Sprocket, Varis, Brigit, Casaval, Nocturnus, and Nyx. The act of doing so and equally including himself caught the attention of Dominel The Just, whose presence triggered a cage match between Stalwart, Nocturnus Wayne/Bane the nearest target of his oath, and Joshua Coolbreeze, Righteousness’ Champion! As if called in a magical arms race, Stalwart was granted the Sapphire Blue Shield of Redemption to aid him in this two v one!

### Slide 16

Deepwater Horizon

While the three of them were busy, Brigit who arrived thanks to a Randal Taxi, managed to plummet Rimefang into the waters of Candle Cove while he was carrying 6 culver's worth of Progenitor-powered Pestilence. On one hand, she saved the city from the Dracolich Attack! On the other, she inadvertently caused the greatest ecological disaster to hit the Isle-Continent of Vartos! Could this potentially come back to haunt us? Almost definitely!

### Slide 18

PeaceForgery Tea Party

Bergs attempted to reason with Peraxis and talk the ancient Illitholich down while the party handled the Pestilliencial waters. Bergs’ mushroom network belonged to St. Ashbury, Peraxis’ former king and best friend and as such, Bergs was able to modify Rimefang’s worth on the myconid network to gain insight into their foe. He learned that Peraxis believed that Redemption was outside his grasp and that this attack is Rimefang and his own last ride.

### Slide 19

Took the Wind from Her Sails

In an effort to clean up the mess in the harbor, Brigit attempted to use her Air Elemental form in order to plug the culvers and reenact the famous finger in the levee fable. However, because this Pestilence was made with a portion of Breeze’s soul, her Air Elemental form became Drab!? Thankfully, Brigit herself was okay!! But, that begs the question: “If Drab is a disease of the soul, is Brigit collecting souls to transform into?!” How will she fix her Air Elemental form before she needs it at the Convergence!? Is it even possible? One thing is certain, this ecological disaster cannot be contained…

### Slide 20

Two Liches, Two Questions, No Answers

During the fight, Varis is taken captive and saved by Casaval, Sprocket and Daina marshal the Magister forces and clear a way to the Birthright Tree, Brigit is drab, the duel of the fates is still in full swing, and peace is not achieved. However Bergs was able to get both the liches questions out of them:

Rimefang ~ “How can I rejoin Tiamat?”
