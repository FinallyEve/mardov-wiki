### Last We Saw Our Heroes...

Last we saw our heroes, they had just done the impossible — killed TWO GODS in the same room, in the same fight, with six people and a turtle carrying a healing potion — and now they're standing in the wreckage of the [[Harvest Zenith]] trying to figure out why the sky is raining ash and why everything feels WRONG.

### The Bringer of Oblivion

The [[Harvest Zenith]] shook with the weight of two gods at war with six mortals.

[[Asmodeus]], Bringer of Oblivion — wearing the skin of [[Cheryl from Accounting]] like a cheap suit — stood at one end of the chamber. [[Tiamat]], the Dragon Queen, five heads screaming in five different registers, stood at the other. And between them, bruised and bleeding and absolutely refusing to die, stood our party.

[[Josh Coolbreeze]] — newly sworn Paladin of [[Bergs]], oath still fresh on his lips — drove his blade into [[Asmodeus]] with everything he had. Stab and slash and the severing of a hand. Blinding white light burned upward from the wound like a star being born in reverse, and the god who had hunted [[Mardov]] since before any of them drew breath... died. The first god to fall on [[Mardov]]. Let that sit.

### Counterspell

One moment deserves its own breath.

[[Tiamat]] — five-headed goddess, dying but not yet dead — called down a meteor storm. The kind of magic that doesn't leave survivors. The kind that turns a victory into a funeral.

[[Sprocket Overspark]] counterspelled it. A gnome. Four feet tall on a good day. Looked a dying goddess in whatever face she had left and said *no*. That is the entire campaign in a single moment.

### Bitten in Half, as Foretold

[[Nocturnous Wayne]] was bitten in half by four of [[Tiamat]]'s heads. This is not a euphemism. Four jaws. Two halves. Dead.

[[Varis]] revivified him — because that is what the [[Shadar-Kai]] DO, they die their destined deaths and they are brought back at the right moment — and his sword shattered, and the threads of fate unraveled, and a pool of red liquid (not blood, something OLDER) formed at his feet, and from it rose [[Promise]], the originator of the [[Darkest Night]] prophecy herself. *"My liege, I swear to you."* [[Nocturnous Wayne]] walked out of the [[Harvest Zenith]] a king. The [[Underdark]] calls him home.

### What Does It Mean For A God To Die?

[[Sprocket]]'s third eye saw [[Asmodeus]]' death fragment into three specters — one pressed itself into the stone of [[Mardov]] (leaving a SCAR on the world), one whisked away into the distance (destination unknown), and the third went directly into [[Nocturnous Wayne]].

Head by head, the Dragon Queen was dismantled. Red, blue, black, green — each fell. Only the white head, [[Evan]], was spared, playing dead under Feign Death while a turtle crawled into [[Brigit]]'s pocket, found a healing potion, and carried it to [[Varis]]. Sometimes the [[Feywilde]] just needs a turtle with good priorities.

[[Hearthstone]] opened the floor beneath [[Tiamat]]'s broken body. The astral dreadnought consumed her. Gone. Two gods dead. Two Zeniths secured.

### Ash Falls on Mardov

Ash is falling. RIGHT NOW. Not the volcanic ash from the [[Mountain]], not the fungal spore of [[Vodram]] — this is something else, something that tastes like two dead gods and a world that doesn't know what to do with all that loose divinity.

It falls on [[Machu]], where [[Jafar]] still sits his throne and [[Vecna]] sits beneath it. It falls on the [[Distillery]]. It falls on [[Faughart]] where [[Brigit]]'s orphans shelter. It falls on [[Dawnpass]]. It falls EVERYWHERE.

Because the [[Forest Zenith]] is active. Something has been set in motion. And whoever set it in motion had VERY good timing.

### The God of Secrets Grows Stronger

[[Vecna]] did not waste the deaths of his rivals. When [[Asmodeus]] died — every secret, every pact, every sealed door and hidden name? [[Vecna]] ate it. When [[Tiamat]] fell — the knowledge of five dragon heads? Added to the collection. The God of Secrets just became the God of EVERYONE ELSE'S Secrets.

And he has NOT been idle. [[Brigit]]'s scrying revealed the [[Anchor Room]] — acrid green liquid spiraling upward into a glass cylinder, and inside it, the demi-lich phylactery of the [[Raven Queen]]. The same artifact that killed [[Brigit]] once before. [[Zombie Finkle]] shambles in circles around it, fused with the [[Heart of the Wilde]], not entirely himself anymore.

But [[Brigit]] still holds the [[Anchor of Death]] — her locket. So how is the [[Forest Zenith]] active? [[Vecna]] rigged a replacement. Because of course he did.

### The Door Was Unlocked

When [[Bergs]] ascended and tapped the guardian powers of the [[Feywilde]], the defense system — [[Mardov]]'s astral dreadnought, its cosmic watchdog — was distracted.

The door was unlocked. [[Vecna]] walked through it.

Not an avatar. Not a lich pulling strings from some distant plane. [[Vecna]] HIMSELF is here, on [[Mardov]], in the [[Forest Zenith]] [[Anchor Room]], with a phylactery and a plan. And every moment the party spends catching their breath is a moment he spends learning what the dead gods knew.

### Dawnpass Reborn

[[Master Tenzin]] — [[Tezka]]'s adopted father — sits the throne of [[Dawnpass]]. Evil queen deposed. The rightful ruler returned. But ash is falling on his kingdom, the [[Orc-Human War]] still rages on his borders, refugees still crowd [[Bridgetown]], and [[Glenda Trammel]] still needs an army.

Peace looks different when the war isn't over.

### The Orcs Without a Master

[[Asmodeus]] is dead. And that means the puppet strings running from [[Tygras Logath]] through [[Logath HellEye]] to every orc chieftain on [[DawnHaven]]? Cut. Just... severed. [[Gordan BlackHand]], [[Grim'Nak]] the Terrible, [[Sledge]] — warlords without a master, an army without a purpose, and a whole lot of rage with nowhere to point it.

An army without purpose is MORE dangerous than an army with one. Who fills the vacuum?

### The Forgotten King Remembered

Deep in the [[White Hills of St. Brutus]] — in the [[Shadowfell]], inside a castle silo lined with a library that stretched floor to sky — [[Bergs]] and Group A found a name that should not exist. A king erased from history. Scrubbed from every record by the same forces that tried to unmake the world once before.

[[Gordon Willingham]]. Former King of [[Dawnpass]]. Descendant of [[Xerxes]] and [[Sabine Ra]].

The founders of the [[Artificer's Guild]] and the [[Circle of the Murder]] — the twin pillars of magic on [[Mardov]] — had a bloodline. And [[Xerxes]] (the colony of living organisms who inherits memories across centuries) HID it, because he understood what [[Mordred]] would do with that knowledge, what [[Vecna]] would do. He made [[Gordon Willingham]] disappear from every record, every genealogy, every book in the [[Monastery of the Dawn]]. Every book except one — because [[St. Brutus]] kept a book bound to every soul. And [[Bergs]] found it.

Now the [[Forgotten King]] steps forward. Not with a crown — with a call to arms.

### The Blood of Sabine Ra

Two bloodlines. Same mother. Two thrones.

[[Xerxes]] + [[Sabine Ra]] = [[Gordon Willingham]] (throne of [[Dawnpass]]). [[Ramses]] + [[Sabine Ra]] = [[Jafar]] (throne of [[Machu]]). The star-crossed three didn't just leave behind a tragedy — they left behind two kingdoms on the same continent, the same ONLY continent, and now both bloodlines are mobilizing.

One throne stands for the alliance. The other sits above [[Vecna]]. Because there is nowhere else to go.

### The War Map

Look at [[DawnHaven]]. Really look.

THE WEST IS FALLING. [[Machu]] is occupied — [[Vecna]] in the [[Forest Zenith]] beneath [[Jafar]]'s throne, the [[Shadow of Death]] blocking the sunlight the prophecy demands. The orcs own the western wilds with no master and no direction. [[Windhaven]] is the last holdout — a single candle in a dark room.

[[DAWNPASS]] IS THE HINGE. [[Master Tenzin]] holds it, but he's holding a door — and something is pushing from the other side.

THE EAST IS LOCKED DOWN. Everything the party built — [[Cloud City]] never fell (no giants for the enemy), the [[Sunless Citadel]] is allied, [[Bynar]] is a stronghold, [[Monethern]] stands with its [[Feywilde]] overlap mapped. Every alliance, every rescue, every impossible choice built THIS side of the board.

THE RIVER IS THE LIFELINE. [[Rimefang]]'s pestilence poisoned the northern sea route. The [[Qual'Xar]] fleet has to go through [[Argos]], up the river — the same river from the [[WereMoth]] episode, the same cursed waterway where [[Sally]]'s affliction was born. And whoever controls that river controls whether reinforcements reach the fight.

136 chapters of choices. Every saved city. Every alliance forged in blood and stubbornness. The party built a war machine, one impossible friendship at a time. And now they need ALL of it.

### What Brigit Saw

[[Brigit]] has been inside [[Machu]]. She went in during [[Operation Elven Freedom]] and walked out with intel nobody else has.

The [[House of Life and Death]] — four locked chests, each guarded by a seasonal [[Casaval]], one chest unlocked. The room itself is a map of the [[Zeniths]] rendered in architecture. And somewhere behind a doorway that not everyone could see, [[Casaval Dekun]] has been trapped since [[Ned'Zailok]] betrayed him.

Her locket still pulses acrid green. Wrong color. Wrong energy. Something in the [[Forest Zenith]] is PULLING on it. And [[Brigit]] — stripped of her elemental powers since the [[Harvest Zenith]], functioning on stubbornness and a turtle that carries healing potions — will have to walk back into [[Machu]] knowing what happened last time she touched [[Sabine Ra]]'s phylactery.

### The Legions of the Dead

Here is the part nobody wants to think about.

[[Reginald Providence]] — [[Maghar of the Belt]], one of the original thirteen dwarven lords — got poisoned by a drab fish, and [[Therzadun]] took personal interest. One drab fish took a hero off the board and put a general at the head of an undead army.

That army has been GROWING. Every battlefield on [[Mardov]] — [[Wesrun Field]] with its corpse flower scarecrows, [[Windhaven]] with its drab-fish reborn, the [[Shadowfell]] beneath [[St. Brutus]] where the dead pile high. [[Argos]] held them back — the wards, the institutions, the sheer magical weight of the city where the campaign BEGAN — but [[Argos]] is weakening. And with every god that dies, every [[Zenith]] that activates, the barrier thins.

[[Reginald Providence]], first Lich of [[Therzadun]], is their general. And the tide is rising.

### The River Road

The [[Qual'Xar]] fleet is missing. [[Captain Rosey Fitzgerald]] was found murdered. Their ships have vanished from the northern shipping lanes.

[[Rimefang]]'s pestilence has poisoned the sea route — there is exactly ONE way for those ships to reach the fight: through [[Argos]], up the river. The same river. The one from the [[WereMoth]] episode. The cursed waterway where [[Sally Lepidoptera]]'s affliction was born.

The river runs from [[Argos]] (where undead press against weakening wards) straight to [[Machu]] (where [[Vecna]] sits in the [[Forest Zenith]] and [[Sally]]'s uncle [[Jafar]] sits on a throne he shouldn't still be alive to occupy). [[Sally]] — princess of [[Machu]], cursed by the [[WereMoth]], the woman who knows what her uncle has become — is the key to BOTH ends of that lifeline.

The fleet hasn't been destroyed. It's been *redirected*. By whom?

### The Winter Zenith Awaits

The prophecy was clear: *"As the snow drifts over the ruined tribe, and serpent plagues the sea, the Anchor of Pestilence shall awake, to answer the curators' plea."*

The party holds the [[Horn of Winter]] (recovered) and the [[Heart of the Faith]] (given to [[Brigit]] by [[Fen]]). But the [[Anchor of Pestilence]] isn't a weapon you just activate — it's a cosmic power that needs to be AIMED.

Inside [[Bynar]]: a frozen fortress, giants in five-dragonhead tabards, the great white dragon [[Frostragan]] clutching a blue gemstone, the turtle [[Maturin]] dormant and waiting, and somewhere in the ice the serpent [[Falak]] — the fae eel that once swallowed the [[Heart of the Faith]] whole. The [[GARD'DAEMON]] configuration demands three champions at the [[Winter Zenith]]: [[Dallor]] as Controller, [[Stalwart]] as Champion, [[Randal]] as Anchor Wielder.

[[Tiamat]]'s seat is empty. And an empty throne attracts pretenders.

### The Scarlet Prophecy

The [[Valley of Anchors]] is not a valley. Not yet.

[[Var'taos]] — the origin of magic itself — is a HILL. The name isn't a description, it's a prophecy. Something is going to turn that hill into a crater, and when it does the name will finally make sense.

Beneath it: the [[Tomb of the Last Curator]] — [[Kolyma]], keeper of knowledge older than the gods who just died fighting over it. And inside the Tomb: the [[Rose of Creation]]. The cosmic original. The thing that was here before anchors, before zeniths, before the word "magic" meant anything.

The party redirected the [[Zeniths]]' conduit, pulled the energy into four elemental stakes, bought time — but the diviner's vision STILL shows the fated explosion. A group standing at the [[Valley of Anchors]] in the moments before it happens. Fire and brimstone. Fighting. And looking into the face of a god they do not yet know — cloaked in black, born from the contest between the [[Raven Queen]] and [[Vecna]] for the Dominion of Death.

The scarlet energy is [[Sabine Ra]]'s. It always was. And [[Vecna]] wants the [[Tomb]] — [[Kolyma]]'s knowledge added to two dead gods' secrets would make him unassailable. This is not a warning. It is a race. Get there first or lose everything.

### The Golden Dragon Egg

During the [[Cloud City]] heist, the party spirited away the [[Golden Dragon Egg]] — the prize [[Tiamat]] herself chased them across the sky to reclaim.

Now [[Tiamat]] is dead. Five heads silenced. Body consumed by the dreadnought.

But the egg remains. What hatches from a golden dragon egg when the goddess who sought it is gone?

### What Waits Outside the Door

The astral dreadnought — [[Mardov]]'s cosmic watchdog, its immune system — was distracted when [[Bergs]] ascended. The door was unlocked. [[Vecna]] walked through.

But the immune system does not stop working because the watchdog is away.

The [[Runic Faced Divine Gargoyles]] have awakened. Stone sentinels older than the gods, carved with runes that predate language. They do not care about alignment. They do not care about intention. They care about unauthorized use of divine power. And [[Bergs]] — sweet, kind-faced, maul-wielding [[Bergs]] — just used a WHOLE LOT of divine power.

The gargoyles are not a boss fight. They are the world asking: *who gave you permission?*

The answer is nobody. The pack does not ask permission.

### What Remains

Two [[Zeniths]] secured. Two to go. An army at your back, a river to sail, and a god to kill.

The [[Mountain]] is sealed. The [[Harvest]] is secured. The [[Winter Zenith]] at [[Bynar]] needs its champions — [[Dallor]], [[Stalwart]], [[Randal]] — and [[Tiamat]]'s empty petal waits for someone to claim it. And above [[Machu]], above [[Jafar]]'s throne, above the [[House of Life and Death]], the God of Secrets sits in the [[Forest Zenith]] with everything he needs — a replacement anchor, a phylactery, and the secrets of two dead gods.

*"As the sunlight warms the forest gate, and madness prisoner freed, the anchor of death shall sway, to bring peace to star-crossed three."*

The madness prisoner is freed — [[Ramses]] is reborn as [[Mordred]]. But the sunlight cannot reach the gate. The star-crossed three — [[Xerxes]], [[Ramses]], [[Sabine Ra]] — left behind two bloodlines on one continent. [[Gordon Willingham]]'s [[Dawnpass]] and [[Jafar]]'s [[Machu]]. One throne for the alliance, one above the enemy.

[[Reginald Providence]] marches the dead from the west. The river is the only lifeline. The [[Qual'Xar]] fleet is missing. [[Argos]] is weakening. The orcs are kingless. And every moment [[Vecna]] sits in the [[Forest Zenith]], he learns more.

But for the first time in this campaign — the party does not face it alone. [[Master Tenzin]] holds [[Dawnpass]]. [[Nocturnous]] rules the [[Underdark]]. The orcs are shaking free. [[Cloud City]] stands because the party refused to let it fall. [[Bynar]] burns bright. The [[Feywilde]] answers to [[Bergs]]. And a king whose name was stolen and returned has called the banners.

Every ally. Every debt. Every promise. Every river sailed and every city saved and every impossible choice that led to this exact moment.

The final war begins.

There is no such thing as a lone wolf.

The pack moves forward.
