# Act 5 — The Ascension

> Chapters 104-136. The reunited party confronts the final conflicts: ascending the Anchors, battling gods, and deciding the fate of Mardov. All four Zeniths must be resolved, Vecna's ascension must be stopped, and the Guard'Daemon must be prevented. Note: Chapters 103 and 129/134 are absent from this act's file set.

## Party Structure

- **Reunited party**: After the Act 4 reunion, the party operates as one group again (with occasional splits for parallel objectives)
- Key PCs: [[Brigit Wildfire]], [[Daina Fireforge]], [[Bergs]], [[Nocturnus Wayne]], [[Varis Laindon]], [[Sprocket Overspark]], [[Fen Darkeyes]]

## Major Themes

- **Anchor Ascension**: The party must ascend each of the four Anchors at their respective Zeniths
  - [[Mountain's Zenith]] / [[Anchor of War]] -- Chapters 104-105 (Vag'Mahar, First Forge)
  - [[Winter's Zenith]] / [[Anchor of Pestilence]] -- Chapters 119-121 (Bynar, Halls of Winter)
  - [[Forest's Zenith]] / [[Anchor of Death]] -- Chapters 123-128 (Machu)
  - [[Harvest's Zenith]] / [[Anchor of Famine]] -- Chapters 132-133
- **Divine Conflicts**: Vecna, Asmodeus, Bahamut, and Tiamat all become active participants
- **Bergs' Apotheosis**: Bergs ascends to godhood, opening a rift between Mardov and [[vodram]]
- **Promise Wayne**: Killing Tiamat/Ashara allows Honor to take final form as Promise Wayne
- **Vecna's Endgame**: With knowledge from fallen gods, Vecna can bring Oblivion and subjugate beings; calls the dragons to [[machu]]
- **The Alabaster Seat Departs**: The Alabaster Seat leaves Mardov, leaving the door wide open
- **Forgesworn Legacy**: All 13 Maghar revealed, their blessings unlocked at the First Forge
