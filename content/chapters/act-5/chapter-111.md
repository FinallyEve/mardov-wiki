Mustard in the Alley?

While the others pop into a magic wand/cruio shop, Sprocket dips into an alley to disguise himself but bumps into his old friend Gideon Clary! ### Slide 16

Cut The Red Tape!

The party bounces over to the Bardic Colleges with Sprocket in disguise as a shorter human. Varis, over the shenanigans of bardic mockery of hierarchy/bureaucracy, banishes the guardian green dragonborn and the party presses on into Randal’s Dressing Room. There they find the Progenitor Spirit, Blossom, still a passenger in this stranger’s head. However this imposter is incapable of saying his name, a uniquely fae affliction. Despite his best efforts to plane shift away (thanks to a quick Casaval Counterspell), Brigit manages to pull Blossom back into the locket and the party reunites Ghostly Randal with his corporeal body! But, if they are taking Randal with them, who will stay and keep the peace in the Magister’s Circle?
