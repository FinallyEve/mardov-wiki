Daina was able to wrestle the gem away from Rimefang at the cost of the dragon getting ahold of The Anchor of War, corrupting a portion of it in the process?! After a quick stop by the Alabaster Seat, the Diamond reveals the location of her sisters: Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Topaz and Amethyst. After a bit more hot-potato, the gem lands in Varis’ hands and turns into a crystalline egg!? They never did catch her name however… Interestingly enough, in Brigit’s hands the gem looked to be a multifaceted progenitor zodiac abomination. ### Slide 21

You’re Not My Real Dad!

During the fight, Casaval is able to get ahold of some of the Fey Iron from Peraxis’ sanctuary. To which Peraxis proclaims “Oh, one of mine?!”

Later, he sees Varis with Curator’s Plea and laments about the death of Friedrich, the Last Curator of Vodram. “He’s here. But so is .. Kolyma” When Varis stabs him with the Fey Iron, his mindflayer affliction seemingly melts off, revealing a deeply grateful, ancient figure.

Through their shared connection in the myconid network employed by St. Ashbury, Bergs is able to determine the weights that Peraxis has placed on his question of “Is it finally enough.” He learns that Peraxis is 4000 years old, and had been researching the Progenitors and their connection with the very Petals of the Rose. He learned that Peraxis is from Therzadun’s petal but was brought here shortly after Vodram. He learns that the most valuable thing in all of Mardov to Peraxis is … BRIGIT?!

This question prompts Brigit’s curiosity, especially coupled with the hint that St. Constantine gave her that there was someone in The Magister’s Circle that she was destined to meet.

### Slide 22

Never Steal From Fey

While the others were focused on Peraxis, Josh, Daina and Nocturnus were all focused on Rimefang. All three wielding magic weapons, Josh’s Ruby Red Rapier of Cleansing was of particular interest to the Dracolich. In a risky gambit, Rimefang decided to willingly take the sword from Josh Coolbreeze by being impaled in the palm. However, Rimefang, in his arrogance, didn’t realize Josh’s fey origin and by stealing from him, he incurred a debt that Josh settled by claiming Rimefang’s very Draconic Essence?! Trading one legendary artifact for another, Josh was able to transform into an Ancient Blue Dragon and dispose of the now humanoid Lich Rimefang!! When the dust settled, Rimefang’s body and the Rapier were missing!! Hopefully, they can take full advantage of Josh’s new power to deal with the consequences!! What we do know is that Tiamat is going to be missing that Blue Essence, and Rimefang the Human Lich is headed back to his Phylactery with the Rapier in tow.

### Slide 23

Sacrifices Must Be Made

Brigit asks Sprocket if Peraxis is the person she was supposed to meet as her Alabaster Seat question, Sprocket responds yes while moved to tears by the vision granted.

The vision is of Peraxis speaking with St. Ashbury in Old Monethern, Peraxis speaks of his research into the Curators saying that Mardov herself sired them for her protection. Then the vision switched to Peraxis after betraying his King where he was sanctioned by Vecna to continue his research but to do so by submitting to Therzadun’s mindflayer influence. After his ceremorphosis, Peraxis surprised Vecna by showing his discovery that Vampirism was a counter to the flaying. Vecna recruited Peraxis to Vodram to try and find an alternative to the Progenitors and the Curators, which he was forced to “distill” down the originals from Vodram. Finally, back in Mardov after his failure at the Mountain’s Zenith, Peraxis was being chastised for being unable to sway Rimefang back from Tiamat and unable to do something about Grazz’t corruption of the archangels. With Therzadun sealed, Vecna no longer needed him to keep his deal with the God of Madness.

It is heavily implied that Peraxis’ experiments on the Progenitors led to Varis, Brigit and even Casaval’s current existence.
