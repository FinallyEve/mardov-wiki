### Slide 5

The Smaller Picture

The Vag Mahar is occupied territory by the Mindflayers beholden to Therzadun. Their leader, “Ghost Eye” Thorin, nowhere to be found

The party is currently trying to ascend the Anchor of War within the First Forge, however there was a trap, half the party now must fight Vellem the Reforged and the other half must fight Reginald Providence, Lich of Therzadun.

The party arrived with the Craftkeeper Rebels who agreed to divert the majority of the cities defenses to a mine known to serve as a well for Drab

### Slide 8

Beaten, Bloody & Nearly Alone

Bergs “Barnabus” Fireforge survived his parties delve into the White Hills of St. Brutus purely on a technicality. His mushroom reanimated body firmly planting itself into the ground with every step, he made his way to the Vag Mahar after being drawn to the hundreds of voices of the Drab within. He carried with him the only other surviving member of his party Kelemtor, though merely the risen’s head remained. Through, luck or fate, he was reunited with his sister Daina and her friends just in time to meet Theosodia and the Craftkeepers for his third attempt at freeing the Dwarven Capital.

His every colored beard now bleached white, unable to control the flow of myconid information from Birtcoin. Our Craftmaster is in a bad way.

### Slide 10

The Troubling Politics of Drow

While sneaking their way through the city, it became clear that there was a tiered system for citizens with Drow in a quasi-foreign dignitary status. Once at the Blackbeard’s Upper Ward, aka Elysium, Nocturnas Wayne was approached by Veyrea Hysteil, leader of a faction of Drow who believe that the Darkest Night prophecy is still possible. The party avoids a fight by declaring fealty to the Wayne Family. They agree to meet later, if there is one.

Note that pre-vamipire/Shadar Kai Nocturnus has a wanted poster up. All Drow except 3 are now Vampires, meaning one of the 3 must be the one to set the Darkest Night.

### Slide 11

Encounter with Plaguebringer

At the ruins of the Dragon’s Carapace Workshop, the party encounters Pyraxis Phyrexia, Vampiric Illithid Lich of Vecna and creator of the Drab Spellplague, who gives them a warning about the two-eyed Beholder Vellem the Reforged, so that they can be better equipped to defeat his rival, Reginald Providence. The runin was tense, however the disgraced Mindflayer left before things could get too violent.

His warning was that if both eyes look upon you, you will be transported to join Reginald within the Stone Sanctum, which would be a trap.
