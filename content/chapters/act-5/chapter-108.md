### Slide 7

A New Paragon Crowned

After Ascending the Anchor of War, Bergs and Daina began divvying up the Maghar boons within the Throne Room.

But declaring war on ALL the Gods had consequences, and Muradin was understandably upset. It took some convincing, but Offensive Boons went to Daina and Defensive to Bergs.

But the Paragon, must sit the Throne and stay, forever, a burden neither wanted to bear. However, HAL, whose brush with Barachiel the Righteous had somehow altered him, was a perfect fit.

And so with Muradin possessing him, a corpseflower tucked into his armor, the Boon of the Belt, and the consensus of Destruction and Creation. HAL was crowned Paragon and the Warforged were ACTIVATED.

### Slide 10

Moving Mountains and Boiling the Seas

Daina, using the power granted to her by the Ascended Anchor of War, decided to mitigate the damage caused by the Elemental Evils before they destroyed her homeland. To do so, she removed the Volcano that had been placed atop the Dwarven Capital during the Second Guard’Daemon, pushing the Mountain into the ocean, creating a new landmass known as The Convergence. Let the Elementals fight where they would do the least harm, at least until the party could get ahold of the Anchor of Famine/Harvest Zenith to seal them away once again: A mirror of the Progenitors shattering Mardov during the First Guard’Daemon.
