# Mardov Campaign — Chapter Index

> 102+ chapters across 4 acts. The campaign of [[Sabine Ra]]'s awakened champions against [[Mordred]], [[Vecna]], and the forces seeking to collapse the barrier between Mardov and V-Mardov.

## Acts

### [[act-1/index|Act 1 — The Awakening]] (Chapters 0-15)
The party is recruited, betrayed, killed, reborn with [[Sabine Ra]]'s soul fragments, and liberates [[argos]]. Three prophecies are delivered, establishing the four Zeniths and Anchors.

### [[act-2/index|Act 2 — The Voyage and the Underdark]] (Chapters 17-41)
Sea voyage to the [[Magister's Circle]], discovery of the Zodiac system, descent into the Underdark to face [[Peraxis Phyrexia]], and the [[forgesworn]] legacy revealed.

### [[act-3/index|Act 3 — The Zenith Wars]] (Chapters 42-87 + Reunion)
The campaign splits into Dwarves and Tieflings (later "The Distillery") for 45+ chapters of parallel play. Strongholds built, followers gained, individual character arcs deepened.

### [[act-4/index|Act 4 — The Final War]] (Chapters 88-102 + Reunion)
Craftkeepers and Distillery tracks continue. The Good Guys Guild forms. [[Gard'Daemon]] approaches.

### [[act-5/index|Act 5 — The Ascension]] (Chapters 104-136)
The reunited party ascends the four Anchors, confronts gods, and fights the final battle for Mardov. Bergs ascends to godhood. Vecna makes his endgame move.
