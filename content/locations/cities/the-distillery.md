# The Distillery

A military-academic compound in [[vartos]], serving as Group B's stronghold. The Distillery contains the [[Actual Size Tower]] (a multi-story arcane research tower), [[Claudere Keep]] (a military fortress), [[faughart]] (a sacred grove), and the [[Temple to the Zodiac]]. The compound sits on a coastal cove and has survived at least one major assault.

## Districts

### Actual Size Tower
Multi-story arcane tower with distinct floors:
- First Floor, Second Floor, Third Floor -- each with unique purpose
- Home to [[Freld Proxtaun]] (later reborn as a Gnoll)

### Claudere Keep
Military fortress with:
- Battle map for tactical encounters
- Vista overlooking the cove
- Quiver Unit guards
- [[Iris Polstrim]] -- New Recruit with catlike eyes
- [[Mac Therden]] -- Rebel from Vodram
- [[Threnson Amundven]] -- Claudere Quiver Guard

### Faughart
Sacred grove settlement with druidic connections:
- [[Brigit]]'s Grove -- Personal sanctuary with visions
- Children's New Home -- Orphan refuge
- Connected to Children of the Forest
- Battle maps for two encounter sites

### Temple to the Zodiac
Sacred site honoring the twelve zodiac spirits:
- Battle maps (day and night versions)
- [[Argent Cerizen]] -- Celestial Spirit of the Mongoose
- Connected to the [[Zodiac Locket]]
