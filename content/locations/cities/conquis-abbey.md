# Conquis Abbey

The holy city of [[Bahamut]] in [[monethern]], Conquis Abbey is a sprawling abbey-town dedicated to the god of justice. It is where the campaign begins -- the party receives the military summons from [[dawnpass]] here. The city is organized into faith-themed districts, each centered on a different aspect of worship and craft.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The reference art shows Conquis Abbey as a medieval European abbey-town spread across rolling green hills. A river runs along one side. The city is dominated by a massive central cathedral complex with red-tile rooftops, multiple spires, and buttressed walls. Surrounding it, orderly rows of stone buildings with terracotta roofs descend the hillside. Green countryside stretches to the horizon.

### Architectural Style
Romanesque-medieval. The main temple complex features pointed arches, flying buttresses, rose windows, and multiple bell towers. The surrounding town is dense but organized -- stone-and-timber buildings with red clay tile roofs, narrow streets, and walled districts. The overall impression is of Cluny Abbey or a similar medieval monastic complex at its height.

### Color Palette
Red terracotta roofs, cream-grey limestone walls, green hills, and blue sky. The Cathedral interiors are golden with stained glass light. At nightfall, the abbey channels glow with warm amber.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Serene and purposeful. Warm pastoral light bathes the exterior. Cathedral interiors glow with devotional warmth. The crypts below shift to cool stone grey with torch-light. An atmosphere of order, faith, and martial readiness.

### Scale & Density
Large abbey-town. Dense within walls, with distinct districts visible from the hilltop approach.

### Scalestone Plaza
The ceremonial heart.

- [[River of Dragons]] -- Commercial street with:
  - Guardians Armorer
  - Jaws of Titans Restaurant
  - Silver Scales Jewels
  - Three Angels Pastries

### Lightwells
The illuminated district.

- [[Guilds of Commerce]] -- Trade district
- [[Luminous Beacon]] -- Lighthouse-shrine with [[Lyserra Pserseph]] (Gilded Lumineer) and [[Ulric Manesbane]] (Luminarch of Pelor)
- Graveyard of Luminarchs

### Ironwork Alley
The craft district.
- [[Groaning Forge Inn]] -- Tavern for smiths
- [[Shrines of the Far Travellers]] -- Shrines to exotic deities

### Tinkertown
The gnome/artificer quarter.
- [[Workshop of Erathis]] -- Shrine-workshop with [[Nathaniel Brendswitch]] (Cleric of Erathis)
- [[Holthbert Burlsbend]] -- Dwarven Ranger resident

### Court of the Creators
Artisan gathering place. Features a grand courtyard.

### Archive of Ioun
Library district.
- [[Patrice Cue]] -- Archivist of Ioun

Sanctuary gardens dedicated to the goddess of travel.

## History

Conquis Abbey is where the campaign begins. Riders arrive with an urgent military summons from [[dawnpass]], requesting aid against orc sorcerers. The party assembles and departs from here, traveling through darkening landscape toward [[Monethern City]] and beyond. The abbey's connection to [[Bahamut]] and the [[Silver Scales of Bahamut|Silver Scales]] makes it a bastion of justice in a world sliding toward chaos.

The Church of the Ashen God and the Temple of the Fallen hint at darker elements even within this holy city. The Crypts beneath the abbey hold ancient secrets.
