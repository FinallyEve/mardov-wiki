# Dawnpass

The political capital of [[DawnHaven]], Dawnpass is a walled city divided between the prosperous Northpass (home to the [[Everstar Palace]]) and the struggling Bridgetown (where Tower Bridge refugees have flooded in). The city is under pressure from the Orc-Human War and internal political intrigue through Her Majesties Magistrates.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
Dawnpass sits at a strategic crossroads in DawnHaven, near the Mortis River. The reference art shows a fortified medieval city with stone walls and gated districts. The East Gate opens onto the DawnHaven countryside. Bridgetown contains the overflow population from the destroyed [[Tower Bridge]].

### Architectural Style
Medieval European administrative city. Stone fortifications, governmental buildings, monastery architecture. The Monastery of the Dawn features underground levels with a lotus room and wellspring. Everstar Palace in Northpass is a grand estate with formal gardens and dining halls.

### Color Palette
Stone grey, warm timber brown, green gardens (Everstar), and the burnt orange of the city in flames (The Burning of Dawnpass).

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Tense and political. The city feels like a pressure cooker -- refugees from Tower Bridge crowd the lower districts while the nobility lives in comfort above. Fire and destruction threaten from without.

## History

Dawnpass is under siege from multiple directions. The Orc-Human War pressures the city militarily, with [[Glenda Trammel]] managing the war effort. Refugees from the destroyed [[Tower Bridge]] have flooded Bridgetown, creating social tension between the displaced and the native population. The Burning of Dawnpass -- a catastrophic fire -- marks a turning point in the campaign. [[Lord Soth]], the Hunter, prowls near the city as an agent of destruction.

The Regal Tour (Dawnpass Royal Vessel) and the visit of "Mr. Happy" to the city are notable campaign events.
