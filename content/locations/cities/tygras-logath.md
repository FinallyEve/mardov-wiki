# Tygras Logath

A sealed crater-city in southern [[DawnHaven]], Tygras Logath is the site of the [[Harvest's Zenith]], containing the Heart of the Progenitors (ruby) and the Anchor of Famine (Diadem of Dawn). The city was sealed after the catastrophic explosion of [[Tygras]]'s superweapon, which destroyed the ancient civilization of Uma and created the desert [[Wastes]].

## Key Features

- Blood of Ancients Flowers and Statue of Old Defender of Uma
- Cornerstone -- Tygras's exile resting place
- Dogs of Logath -- Guardian creatures
- The Scar of Tygras -- The crater itself

## Key NPCs

- **[[Logath HellEye]]** -- Emperor of Tygras Logath (Vecna's agent of Famine)
- **[[Grimnak Norman]]** -- Logath resident
- **[[Tygras]]** -- The ancient tyrant whose echo exists within the Harvest's Zenith

## History

Tygras was a red tiefling who made a deal with a fae demon, gaining power that led to the creation of a draconic-blood destruction superweapon. When it detonated, Uma was destroyed, creating the desert Wastes and forcing survivors into the [[Matrim Pact]]. The explosion sealed Tygras Logath in a crater. Within, the Harvest's Zenith contains Tygras himself (or the arcane being he became).

To revive the Maturin guardian and resolve the Harvest crisis:
1. Remove the Anchor of Famine from the zenith top
2. Return the Heart of the Progenitors to the center of Tygras Logath
3. Speak the words: *"On his back all vows are made, a haven for the continent of dawn paid."*

[[nyx]] inherits the Mark of Tygras (Sable's Beacon) after destroying his echo, which rips a hole in the Fae.
