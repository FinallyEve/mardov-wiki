# Evallan Forest

The sacred elven forest of [[DawnHaven]], Evallan (also spelled Eavallan) is a mystical old-growth woodland that serves as the spiritual heart of druidic and fey power on the continent. It is [[Brigit Loregrammar]]'s homeland and the seat of the [[Emerald Guard]]. The forest contains a waystone portal to the [[feywilde]] and the Grove of the Ancients, including [[DoeFall Glade]] where young Brigit had her first vision.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The reference art depicts Evallan as a fantastical forest of impossible beauty -- towering ancient trees frame a vista of cascading waterfalls, floating islands, a distant castle on a hilltop, and a snow-capped mountain rising behind it all. The vegetation is lush beyond the natural: bioluminescent flowers, giant curling branches, hanging moss, and crystal-clear rivers cutting through the canopy. Rope bridges span ravines.

### Architectural Style
Organic and integrated. Structures are grown from living wood rather than built -- fortress walls incorporate tree trunks, retreat buildings nestle into root systems. The Natural Cathedral (Circle of Avandra) suggests druidic stone circles within forest clearings. Jesabelle's Cabin and Ruth's Hut represent more rustic hermit dwellings.

### Color Palette
Vivid fantasy greens -- emerald canopy, jade moss, lime undergrowth. Accented by bioluminescent purples and pinks, waterfall whites, and the golden light filtering through the canopy. The mountain in the background adds cool blue-white contrast.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Ethereal and dreamlike. Shafts of golden light pierce the canopy, illuminating mist and floating particles. The overall mood is one of ancient enchantment -- this forest is alive, watchful, and deeply magical. The Blight Swamp areas provide darker contrast.

## History

Evallan Forest is the spiritual cradle of the campaign's druidic power. [[Brigit Loregrammar]], tied to the Doe zodiac and the Forest's Zenith, grew up here. The forest's connection to the [[feywilde]] via the waystone portal makes it a crossing point between worlds. The Drab Migration through the forest represents the displacement caused by Mordred's campaigns. The corrupted druid [[Umbgren Ven]] suggests that even this sacred place is not immune to the darkness spreading across Mardov.
