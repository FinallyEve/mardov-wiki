# Argos

A ruined coastal city in northern [[DawnHaven]], Argos was once the seat of the [[Artificer's Guild]] and a thriving port. Now in ruins, the city features the crumbling Scarlet Citadel, a teleportation room still partially functional, and the Argosian Woods pressing in on the collapsed walls. It is where [[Ruth]] delivered the First Prophecy.

## Key Features

- Argos Isometric Map -- Detailed city layout showing ruined districts
- Ruins of Argos -- Crumbling city walls and structures
- Ruins of the Scarlet Citadel -- The former Artificer's Guild headquarters
- Scarlet Keep Map -- Tactical map of the keep
- Asylum (Floor 1) -- Institutional building within the ruins
- Teleportation Room -- Still partially functional
- City by the Sea -- Argos in its prime (reference)

## Key NPCs

- **[[Morticia "Red" Sunwatcher]]** -- Cabal Leader of Argos
- **[[Ruth]]** -- Wilder Diviner who delivered the First Prophecy here
- Artificer's Guild Argos Chapter members
