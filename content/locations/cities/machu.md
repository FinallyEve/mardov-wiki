# Machu

An ancient elven city built into the mountain cliffs of [[DawnHaven]], rising in vertical layers from a hidden canyon harbor to the sky-touching [[Forest's Zenith]] temple above. Massive cliff pillars tower over the city, draped in vegetation, connected by stone bridges and archways. Waterfalls cascade down the rock faces at every level. The city climbs -- from the docks at the canyon floor, through the winding streets and terraced buildings of the city proper, up to the royal temple and the Zenith that hangs in the air above it all.

Machu is ruled by the mad King [[Jafar Lepidoptera]], whose mind was broken by [[Mordred]]/[[ramses]]. The [[Forest's Zenith]] contains the Heart of the Wilde (emerald) and the Anchor of Death (obsidian sword). As of Chapter 137, **[[Vecna]] personally occupies the Forest Zenith anchor room** beneath the city.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape

Machu is a vertical city. Towering cliff pillars of pale stone rise hundreds of feet, their faces carved with elven architecture -- balconies, windows, bridges spanning the gaps between pillars. Mist hangs between the peaks. The city exists in layers:

- **Canyon floor**: The hidden docks, accessible only through a narrow river canyon between sheer cliff walls
- **Lower city**: Stone buildings with arched gateways, markets, the canal district
- **City proper**: Terraced hillside climbing steeply -- whitewashed stone buildings with clay roofs, thick vegetation growing through walls, stone bridges and stairways connecting levels
- **Upper reaches**: The Palace, the royal temple (a monumental ziggurat with waterfalls pouring from its terraces)
- **The summit**: The [[Forest's Zenith]] -- a massive domed temple perched on the highest peak, connected to the city by a stone bridge over a chasm of clouds and waterfalls

### Architectural Style

Elven stonework -- organic and ancient, as if the city grew from the mountain rather than being built on it. Lower levels show more practical construction (arched gateways, timber-framed markets), while upper levels blend seamlessly into the cliff face. The royal temple draws on Mesoamerican ziggurat forms with Egyptian influences -- hieroglyph-covered walls of pale stone that tower above the jungle canopy, a curved horn-like structure crowning the summit. The [[Forest's Zenith]] grand entrance is a rose-colored domed cathedral on a mountain peak, flanked by smaller domes and watchtowers, with a stone causeway lined with guardian statues spanning the gap from the city.

### Color Palette

The city itself is warm stone and green -- pale cliff faces covered in moss, ivy, and flowering vines. The docks glow amber and gold as sunlight streams through the canyon gap. The Zenith shifts to deeper colors -- moonlit green, ancient stone grey, spectral blue. The royal temple is sandstone and gold. The [[Circle of the Murder]] district is cold and dark -- grey cobblestone, raven-black iron, no vegetation.

### Lighting & Atmosphere

The canyon creates dramatic natural lighting -- golden sunset light streaming between cliff walls at the docks, dappled forest light through the canopy in the city proper, and mist-shrouded blue-grey atmosphere in the upper reaches. The Forest's Zenith entrance is often obscured by clouds, visible only when the mist parts. At night, the city glows with lanterns dotting the cliff faces like earthbound stars.

### Scale

Immense. The cliff pillars dwarf everything. A traveler approaching from the river canyon sees only the humble docks; looking up reveals the city climbing impossibly high above, and beyond that, the Zenith in the clouds.

## Districts

### The Docks

The lowest point of Machu -- a hidden harbor at the base of a narrow river canyon. Stilted wooden platforms and simple thatched structures line the calm water, dwarfed by sheer cliff walls rising on either side. Golden light pours through the canyon gap at sunset. Birds wheel overhead. This is the entry point from [[Qual'Xar]] sea routes and the only way in by water.

![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/Docks.jpg]]

### City Proper

The beating heart of Machu, climbing the mountainside in layers. Whitewashed stone buildings with terracotta roofs, linked by stone bridges and stairways. Thick vegetation grows through and over everything -- trees burst through courtyards, vines hang from balconies, moss carpets the ancient steps. Stone archways frame narrow streets busy with foot traffic. Market stalls cluster around the lower gates.

![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/Machu Proper -- a view from the foot of the Ziggurat.jpg]]

### The Palace

Seat of the Lepidoptera dynasty, high on the cliffs.
- **Royalty:** [[Jafar Lepidoptera]] (mad king), [[Hope Lepidoptera]] (son of Jafar)
- **Old Guard:** [[Hysteria Lepidoptera]] (former queen), Korth Deikun, Twallia Deikun
- **The Throne Room:** Contains Jafar and his six-fingered pet Balor -- the demonic corruption underlying the madness made manifest
- Connected to [[Ned'Zailok|Lich Ned'zailok]], Keeper of Death's Anchor

### The Royal Temple

A monumental ziggurat rising above the jungle canopy. Massive stone walls covered in hieroglyphs, waterfalls cascading from its terraced levels. A curved horn-like structure crowns the summit. The approach crosses a bridge over the river, past palm trees and acacia, the temple's shadow falling across everything below. Ancient and awe-inspiring -- this is where the Lepidoptera bloodline connects to the divine.

![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/Machu royal temple.jpg]]

### Circle of the Murder

Gothic horror district -- the stark opposite of the rest of Machu's living, green architecture. Castle Von Vicktonstein and its coven dwell here in cold stone and black iron.
- Raven Altar, ghost town streets, coven interiors
- Laboratories (multiple): alchemical labs, Stomble's lab
- Zombie Artificers workshop

![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/Circle of the Murder/Circle of the Murder.jpg]]
![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/Circle of the Murder/Castle Von Vicktonstein.jpg]]

### City Outskirts & Borders

The approaches to Machu through dense, ancient forest. The border is defended by elven warriors mounted on great stags, fighting in the deep woods against armored invaders. The Mortis River canyon provides the primary approach.
- **Above The Mortis River:** Machu Toad Pools, Mortis River Canyon, mountain peaks, Pride Rock
- Shipwreck Valley
- Machu entrance gates

![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/Border of Machu.jpg]]
![[assets/maps-source/DawnHaven/Machu/machu entrance.jpg]]

### Forest's Zenith

The ancient temple complex at Machu's highest point -- a grand domed cathedral perched on a mountain peak, connected to the city by a stone causeway. Waterfalls plunge from the cliffs on either side. The temple is rose-colored stone with green copper domes, flanked by smaller towers. Guardian statues line the bridge approach.

Inside, the Zenith is a multi-leveled garden temple, overgrown with sacred vegetation, descending in concentric tiers to the anchor room at its heart.

- Grand entrance with sky-reaching architecture
- Temple interiors (day and night versions) -- a tiered garden maze
- Path to Zenith: crosses a bridge beneath the temple's massive hieroglyph-carved walls
- [[ramses]] -- Preserved Mummy within the Zenith
- [[Xerxes]] Reborn -- the soul at the bottom of the Zenith

## History & The Forest Madness

Machu's crisis is the **Forest Madness**. King [[Jafar Lepidoptera]] is trapped in madness -- [[Mordred]]/Ramses and his phylactery are the source. The Forest's Zenith within the city contains the Heart of the Wilde (emerald) and the Anchor of Death (obsidian sword, originally black steel, now appearing as black glass with a lingering curse).

Resolution requires: using a sun-reflecting mirror to access the Zenith, freeing the king from madness, and reconciling the "star-crossed three" -- [[Xerxes]] (soul at the Zenith's bottom), [[ramses]] (soul in Daina's bag, needs to return to phylactery for cleansing), and [[Sabine Ra]] (who appears as a flash when both bodies merge into a demi-lich). After defeating the Forest Zenith boss, the party will be transported to [[V-MARDOV]].
