# Machu

A grand port city in [[DawnHaven]], Machu is a place of faded grandeur and creeping darkness. Its Venetian-inspired architecture -- domed cathedrals, angel-topped columns, ornate bridges over canals -- is rendered in permanent sunset, as if the city exists in an eternal twilight. Machu houses the [[Forest's Zenith]], containing the Heart of the Wilde (emerald) and the Anchor of Death (obsidian sword). The city is ruled by the mad King [[Jafar Lepidoptera]], trapped in madness by [[Mordred]]/Ramses.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The reference art shows Machu as a massive harbor city at sunset. Tall-masted ships crowd the docks in the foreground. Behind them, the city rises in layers -- canal-side warehouses, ornate bridges with winged angel statuary, domed cathedrals and spired towers climbing toward the skyline. Smoke and haze hang over the industrial districts. Beyond the city, mountain peaks and the Forest's Zenith temple complex are visible.

### Architectural Style
Renaissance-Venetian with dark fantasy undertones. Stone bridges with golden angel statues, copper-green domed cathedrals, columned government buildings, and dense urban blocks with terracotta and slate roofing. The Palace features a royal temple and throne room. The Circle of the Murder (Castle Von Vicktonstein) has a distinctly gothic horror feel -- cobblestone streets, raven altars, zombie-filled laboratories. The Forest's Zenith temple is ancient and monumental, with Mesoamerican ziggurat influences.

### Color Palette
Dominated by burning orange and amber -- the entire city appears bathed in a copper-gold sunset that never ends. Dark silhouettes of ships and buildings against the fiery sky. Deep shadows in the alleys. The Palace interiors are gilded. The Forest's Zenith shifts to deep green, moonlight silver, and ancient stone grey.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Perpetual dusk. The reference art is drenched in warm, smoky amber light -- industrial haze and sunset combining to create an atmosphere of decadent decline. This is a city past its prime, beautiful but rotting from within. The Forest's Zenith areas shift to cooler, more mystical lighting -- moonlit glades and spectral green.

### Scale & Density
Large and dense. A major port with extensive harbor facilities, multiple districts, and surrounding outskirts along the Mortis River.

## Districts

### City Proper
The main urban area with docks, palace, and the ziggurat.

### The Palace
Seat of the Lepidoptera dynasty.
- **Royalty:** [[Jafar Lepidoptera]] (mad king), [[Hope Lepidoptera]] (son of Jafar)
- **Old Guard:** [[Hysteria Lepidoptera]] (former queen), Korth Deikun, Twallia Deikun
- Connected to [[Ned'Zailok|Lich Ned'zailok]], Keeper of Death's Anchor

### Circle of the Murder
Gothic horror district -- Castle Von Vicktonstein and its coven.
- Raven Altar, ghost town streets, coven interiors
- Laboratories (multiple): alchemical labs, Stomble's lab
- Zombie Artificers workshop

### City Outskirts
The approaches to Machu along the Mortis River.
- **Above The Mortis River:** Machu Toad Pools, Mortis River Canyon, mountain peaks, Pride Rock
- Shipwreck Valley
- Machu entrance gates

### Forest's Zenith
The ancient temple complex within Machu.
- Grand entrance with sky-reaching architecture
- Temple interiors (day and night versions)
- [[ramses]] -- Preserved Mummy within the Zenith
- [[Xerxes]] Reborn -- the soul at the bottom of the Zenith

### Docks
Harbor district with ships and canal access. Entry point from [[Qual'Xar]] sea routes.

## History

Machu's crisis is the **Forest's Madness**. King [[Jafar Lepidoptera]] is trapped in madness -- [[Mordred]]/Ramses and his phylactery are the source. The Forest's Zenith within the city contains the Heart of the Wilde (emerald) and the Anchor of Death (obsidian sword, originally black steel, now appearing as black glass with a lingering curse).

Resolution requires: using a sun-reflecting mirror to access the Zenith, freeing the king from madness, and reconciling the "star-crossed three" -- [[Xerxes]] (soul at the Zenith's bottom), [[ramses]] (soul in Daina's bag, needs to return to phylactery for cleansing), and [[Sabine Ra]] (who appears as a flash when both bodies merge into a demi-lich). After defeating the Forest Zenith boss, the party will be transported to [[V-MARDOV]].

The six-fingered pet Balor in Jafar's throne room hints at the demonic corruption underlying the madness.
