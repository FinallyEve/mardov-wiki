# Monethern City

The political and commercial capital of the [[monethern]] region, Monethern City is a major port with a distinctive Floating Quarter that rises above the harbor. The city is governed by the **Senatoriate** -- ten "Voices" who sit in the [[Veritasium Palace]]. Fort Undercroft guards the approaches, and the Spillway district manages the bustling docks.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
A port city set on a harbor, with multiple districts arranged from waterfront to hilltop. The Floating Quarter literally hovers above the harbor during festivals, creating a magical spectacle. The South Gate opens to the sea, while canals and bridgeways connect the Market Square to the palace above.

### Architectural Style
A blend of Renaissance port city and fantasy floating architecture. The Floating Quarter features buildings suspended by magic -- visible during the Night of the Fair. The Veritasium Palace and Starstone Cathedral are grand civic structures. Fort Undercroft is a massive coastal fortification with eastern and western entrances.

### Color Palette
Harbor blues, stone grey, wet-weather atmospherics. The Floating Quarter sparkles with festival lights. The Spillway district brings dock browns and ship timber.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Often rainy -- one reference shows Monethern City in the rain, lending a moody, working-port atmosphere. Festival nights transform the Floating Quarter into a celebration of light and color. Fort Undercroft is grimmer -- military torchlight and sea fog.

## History

Monethern City serves as the party's entry point into the [[monethern]] region and the gateway to [[Vag'Mahar]]. The Senatoriate's ten Voices represent a more democratic form of governance than the feudal structures seen in [[DawnHaven]], but political intrigue runs deep. The Red Sails Search Party operating out of Fort Undercroft suggests ongoing military operations, and the Ripper of Peasant Street hints at darker undercurrents in the commercial districts.
