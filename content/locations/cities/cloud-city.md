# Cloud City

A massive floating archipelago suspended in the sky above the [[Storm Peaks]] in northern [[DawnHaven]]. Cloud City is not a single settlement but a constellation of cloud-islands connected by bridges, each hosting distinct communities -- from the avian Aarakocra of [[Laputa Town]] to the five great giant kingdoms. The city floats within the permanent [[everstorm]], accessible from the ground via [[jackfall]] and the great tree Jack.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The Cloud City Map shows an archipelago of cloud-islands floating in a teal sky. **Storm Peaks** sits at the center -- a rocky mountain island surrounded by towers and structures. Around it, connected by arching cloud-bridges:
- **Laputa Town** (northwest) -- A small wooded island
- **Sirrus** (west) -- Cliff-perched settlement
- **Nimbus** (southwest) -- Connected island cluster
- **JackFall** (south) -- Ground-level entry with a windmill
- **5 Cities of Giants** (east) -- Forested islands with towers

Cloud formations serve as both terrain and building material. Bridges arc between islands through open sky.

### Architectural Style
Varied by district. Laputa Town has an avian-academic character -- Booksworth Academy, the Early Worm Cafe, and The Roost casino suggest a civilization of intelligent bird-folk. The Storm Peaks central district has grand stone towers and the Palace of the 9 Winds. The 5 Cities of Giants each reflect their elemental type (cloud, fire, frost, hill, storm). JackFall features a massive magical tree ("Jack -- The Tree to the Clouds") serving as the stairway between ground and sky.

### Color Palette
Soft whites, sky blues, and cloud greys dominate the map. The islands themselves are warm brown earth and green vegetation floating in ethereal whiteness. Storm Peaks adds lightning purple and stone grey. After the Fall, the palette shifts to warmer tones as the city descends -- the Storm Peaks Lantern District glows amber.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Ethereal and diffuse. Light filters through clouds, creating soft, omnidirectional illumination. Mist obscures distances. The Everstorm provides dramatic storm-light -- flashes of lightning, dark thunderheads, and the rage of the Thunder King.

### Scale & Density
Enormous in area but moderate in population. The islands float at various altitudes, creating a three-dimensional city impossible to map on flat paper.

## Districts

### Storm Peaks (Central)
The heart of Cloud City -- a rocky mountain peak floating above all else.
- [[Anvil of Thestrus]] -- Blacksmith forge of Thestrus, brother of Thestra. Home to the sword "Jack's Bane" and the rune weapon "Splinter."
- [[Dr. Grackle's Marvelous Inventions]] -- Inventor's workshop
- [[Kepler's Thunder Roc-ery]] -- Rookery for Thunder Rocs
- [[Magister's Surplus]] -- Magic shop
- [[Palace of the 9 Winds]] -- Grand palace
- [[Upper Strata]] -- Residential area, home to the Poxeese family

### JackFall (Ground Entry)
The point of ascent from the surface.
- **Jack** -- The Tree to the Clouds, a colossal magical tree
- Jackfall Gatehouse
- The Stairway to Heaven -- Monumental ascending architecture

### Laputa Town (Northwest)
Avian Aarakocra community.
- [[Booksworth Academy]] -- School with Mother Makaw (headmistress), Edwin Forebeak (military strategy), Erin Flume (arcane teacher), and Erin's Fledgelings (Mickey, Rouge, Tweetie)
- [[Early Worm Cafe]] -- Run by Breeve Ulson; Vee Portia (seamstress) frequents
- [[The Roost]] -- Casino with Barn Owl Guards, Gorm (bellboy), Mr. Tux (penguin concierge), Woadie & Minnie LaDoux. Contains Guest Services and The Gaming Hall.
- Owl Guard -- City watch of Laputa Town

### Nimbus (Southwest)
Island cluster with specialized venues.
- [[Erlamier's Flask]] -- Alchemist shop
- [[Hanging Gardens]] -- Ancient structure with adamantine emblems, opal walls, and The Apparatus
- [[The Ice-House]] -- Managed by Daodain, later Grobain Habass. Contains Zephreeron (disguised Bronze Dragon) and the 12 Genasi of the Icehouse.

### Sirrus (West)
Cliff-side settlement with port access.
- [[Cliff Temple]]
- [[Callie's Cotton Canderie]]
- [[Tail Feather's Textiles]]
- [[Whaleport]] -- Naval dock with Captain BlackWing (Warfmaster), Edna Grackel (Fleet Captain), and the Thornberry Wilde

### 5 Cities of Giants (East)
Five elemental giant kingdoms:

**[[cloudheim]]** -- Cloud Giant domain. Queen [[threena]] (Queen of Clouds) advised by [[arcanis]].

**[[fireheim]]** -- Fire Giant domain. Notable: Ashen Flamekeeper, [[Ifrin the PactBound]] (Emissary from the Plane of Fire).

**[[frostheim]]** -- Frost Giant domain. Notably absent frost giants: [[elga]] (Frost Talon), [[gorbskull]] (The Frozen Fist), [[olkin]] (Watcher of Winter, Chosen of Grumblesin). A Simulacrum remains.

**[[hillheim]]** -- Hill Giant domain. [[King Vern]] (opera fan). Contains [[Hallan Opera Hall]] with [[ulg]] (The Lady of the Opera).

**[[stormheim]]** -- Storm Giant domain. Ruled by the Forecast Triumvirate: [[gabbatrix]] (Thunder's First), [[Thestra]] (Wife of the Storm), [[zell]] (Wielder of Lightning).

### After the Fall
Post-catastrophe state of Cloud City after it falls from the sky.
- Storm Peaks Lantern District -- Now ground-level, lit by lanterns
- [[Ishara Vane]] -- Reverted Air Genasi, friend of Kelik

### The Plot
Conspiracy thread within Cloud City.
- Connected to the Red Skipper

## History

Cloud City fell from the sky in a major campaign event -- the "post cloud city incident" world map shows the aftermath. Before the fall, it was a marvel of magical engineering, sustained by ancient giant magic and the power of the Everstorm. The fall was catastrophic, reshaping the northern DawnHaven landscape and scattering the giant communities.

The city's internal politics revolve around the five giant kingdoms, the Aarakocra communities, and the genasi populations. The Thunder King's rage threatens periodic destruction, and the Forecast Triumvirate of Stormheim holds the balance of power among the giants.
