# Magister's Circle

The governing magical authority of [[Mardov]], the Magister's Circle is both an institution and a city in [[vartos]]. It houses the Platinum Seats (nine arcane covenant leaders), the Bard Colleges (eight cloak-color schools), and the Alabaster Seat -- the highest magical authority. The city is a bustling harbor town on the Vartos coast, serving as the intellectual and arcane capital of the known world.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
A coastal city built around a natural harbor, with the Circle (the governing complex) on elevated ground. Candle Cove serves as the port. Dense jungle presses in from the east and south. The reference art shows a harbor city with stone towers, gabled rooftops, and a prominent clock tower, with ships in the harbor and forested mountains beyond.

### Architectural Style
Renaissance academic-port. Stone university buildings with arched windows, clock towers, and colonnaded walkways. The town center features a busy marketplace. Below the Circle, older tunnels and chambers suggest ancient construction predating the current city. Shops line Main Street with apothecaries and specialist stores.

### Color Palette
Warm stone, harbor blue, timber brown. The Circle buildings are grander -- white stone and gold accents. Candle Cove at night would glow with lanterns.

## Districts

### The Circle
The governing complex on the heights.
- **[[Eon's Sanctum]]** -- Tower of the Alabaster Seat, [[Eon Everstar]]. Top-floor observatory with panoramic views. Contains Eon's Vision and Gate Guardians.
- **Platinum Seats** -- Nine arcane covenant leaders (see [[Magister's Circle faction]])

### Cabal of Horns
Secret organization operating within the city.
- [[Veera Satyrfell]] -- Tiefling hidden as human / Tiefling Sorceress
- [[Zetanna Farhem]] -- Member
- [[Anemone Nomina]] -- Also in Orchid Cloaks

### Candle Cove -- Port
The harbor district.
- Port facilities, ships, trade
- [[Hugo Kerran]] -- Captain of Whisperwind

### Shops / Main Street
Commercial district.
- [[Allard's Apothecary]]
- Various merchants and specialty stores

### Notable Residents
- [[Yuki Umberbrine]] -- Magister's Assistant, Tiefling
- [[shela]] -- Witchhunter
- [[zoastra]] -- Resident

## Key Events

The Magister's Circle is where the party receives the DawnPass envoy requesting aid against orc sorcerers. The envoy notably contains zero Orcs or Half-Orcs, hinting at the racial politics underlying the Orc-Human War. The city has also suffered pirate attacks and a T-Rex incursion (multiple reference images).
