# Hinderstap

A cursed village in [[monethern]] where visitors should never stay the night. Something terrible happens after dark -- the villagers are trapped in a repeating cycle of violence and death that resets each dawn.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Gideon Clary]]** -- Village resident (connected to Lorilai Clary of the [[Qual'Xar]]?)
