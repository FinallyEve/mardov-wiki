# WindHaven

A walled city of windmills and farmland in the heart of [[DawnHaven]], dominated by the colossal **Windmill Willow** -- an ancient sentient tree whose massive pink-blossomed canopy towers over the entire settlement. WindHaven is ruled by the Androsphinx [[Leonidas]], styled "Leader of Summer's Last Light," who resides within the Willow. The city serves as a major campaign hub where the party first gathers and where the Harvest Festival triggers catastrophic events.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The city map shows WindHaven as a walled settlement on a coastal bluff. The enormous Windmill Willow dominates the center-north, its cherry-blossom canopy spreading over half the city. Multiple windmills dot the landscape both within and outside the walls. To the west, sea cliffs drop to a rocky shoreline with a grotto below. Green farmland stretches south and east, dotted with fringe farms along the Game Trail. Rolling hills and scattered trees surround the settlement.

### Architectural Style
Medieval European with a pastoral character. Stone walls and timber-frame buildings, many with thatched roofs. The city is tiered, with the Upper Crust (nobility) on higher ground and common districts below. The Windmill Willow itself functions as architecture -- its trunk houses the library, lobby, and Leonidas's chambers, while its branches support platforms. Windmills are both functional (grinding grain) and cultural symbols -- they appear on the city's sigil.

### Color Palette
Dominated by the Willow's explosion of pink cherry blossoms against the green of farmland and grey-brown of stone walls. Warm golden-brown timber, green copper rooftops, and the blue-grey of the coastal waters. The city sigil renders in dark iron tones.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Pastoral and warm. The reference art emphasizes golden sunlight filtering through blossom petals. The Windmill Willow painting shows a face-like formation in the ancient bark, with dappled green-gold light -- the tree is wise, watchful, alive. During the Harvest Festival, the atmosphere shifts to twilight purple and festival-fire orange, culminating in the demonic red of the Night of the Equinox.

### Scale & Density
Medium-sized city. Dense within walls, scattered farms outside. The Windmill Willow is impossibly large -- its canopy covers hundreds of meters.

## Districts

### City Proper
The walled inner city, organized around the Windmill Willow.

**Upper Crust** -- Nobility district. Home to [[Cesar Bordesia|Count Cesar Bordesia]], the secretly Yuan-Ti [[Mathia Nuum]], and the Windswept Orphanage. Contains The Rookery.

**North Gate** -- Main entrance district with the North Post trading post, guards, and shoppers. Second Windswept Orphanage location here.

**Temples** -- Religious quarter containing:
- [[Avandra's Gift]] -- Temple to the goddess of travel
- [[Bahamut Makeshift Tent Temple]] -- Temporary temple
- [[Field of Pelor]] -- Temple to the sun god
- Melora Sanctum portal

**Shops** -- Commercial district with Claire Yeendle's pawn shop, Durst Nam's Zoomybrew, Kiersten Vettlegreen's weapons, Melbrum Gam's armor shop, and Oliver Hyrdantos's Willowtop Library.

**Windmill Willow** -- The living heart of the city. Multiple levels within the ancient tree: main lobby, library, Leonidas's chambers, and a summit lookout.

### Brothels, Inns & Taverns

**Traveler's Pony** -- Split establishment with an Elf Half and Human Half, plus Umbertown below. Run by Alice. Features the Orchid Cloaked bard dancer [[Mina Sultrin]], the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]], and [[Sarah Pudnam]]'s Farmer's Daughters.

### The Game Trail
The wilderness road leading out of WindHaven, containing:

**Fringe Farms:**
- [[Fallmora Commune]] -- Fringe farm
- [[Fellfall Farms]] -- Working farm
- [[Navakarra Steading]] -- Family farm with cemetery (Annabelle & Mankato Navakarra)
- [[Serenity's Passing]] -- Farm near WindHaven
- [[Vespeera Ranch]] -- Ranch run by Cauldera Freewind
- [[Wesrun Field]] -- Crow-haunted field with family crypt and battlemap

**Path To the Second Eye:**
- Attack of the Fire Genasi encounter
- Caravan encounters (Farmer's Daughters, Lord Godbrand Fartheshire's caravan)
- Shipment Bearing Lord Nuum's Sigil / Slavers encounter
- [[Skyfall Cemetery]] -- Graveyard with fortune teller No Mi
- The Second Eye -- Canyon with giants
- Tower Bridge Battalion

**North Cliffs:**
- Razelsin Shortcut -- Entrance to the [[feywilde]] via the Four Corner Door

### The Grotto
Coastal cave system below the city. Contains Doldrums Lighthouse, Siren's Cove, and Windsear Bluff (Goddess Path).

### SchroomTop
Mushroom settlement near WindHaven. Features Chanterelle, Tinker's Bench, and views from the Rice Flats.

### Harvest Festival
Annual celebration that serves as a major campaign turning point.

**Hour of Twilight** -- Evening festivities beneath the Windmill Willow's shadow, convergence scene.

**The Night of the Equinox** -- Catastrophic event. Features the Ascended Tygras, Harvest Mother, Harvesters, Reynaldine's true form, and the Shadow of Famine. Gate guardians manifest.

## History

WindHaven is where the party assembles for the first time, drawn by various threads to the city during the Harvest Festival. The festival culminates in the Night of the Equinox -- a catastrophic event where [[Reynaldine]] (Fey Lord of Famine) manifests, the Shadow of Famine descends, and the Harvest's Zenith connection is revealed. This event drives the party out of WindHaven and into the wider campaign.

The city's connection to the [[Scales of Falak]] (Yuan-Ti cult) runs deep -- [[Lady Sessura Vaught]] operates openly in high society, and the secretly Yuan-Ti [[Mathia Nuum]] holds noble status.
