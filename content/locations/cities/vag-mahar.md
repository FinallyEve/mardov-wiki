# Vag'Mahar

The great dwarven fortress-city of [[monethern]], carved into the face of a mountain range. Vag'Mahar is divided into three sectors -- **Blackbeard**, **Brownbeard**, and **Redbeard** -- each governed by a Thane and containing three vertical wards (Lower, Middle, Upper). The [[Mountain's Zenith]] lies within the Stone Sanctum in the Brownbeard Upper Ward, housing the Heart of the Forge (garnet) and the Anchor of War.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The mountain vista reference art shows Vag'Mahar as a monumental vertical city -- towering stone pillars and tiered buildings climbing a narrow mountain valley. Massive cliff faces frame the city on both sides, with golden sunset light catching the peaks above. The architecture is densely stacked, layer upon layer, with the impression of a city carved from the living rock rather than built upon it.

### Architectural Style
The art reveals an Asian-influenced dwarven aesthetic -- pagoda-style rooftops with curved eaves, ornamental archways, and vertical stone pillars. Red and blue accent colors mark different districts against the dominant grey-brown stone. The buildings are monumental in scale, with grand ceremonial gates and columned halls. The sewer system below contains clockwork mechanisms (The Clocktower) and hidden vaults (The Lockbox).

### Color Palette
Stone grey and warm brown dominate, with red accents (Redbeard), dark iron tones (Blackbeard), and earth browns (Brownbeard). Forge-fire orange glows from windows and vents. Golden sunset light on the mountain peaks above.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Forge-lit interiors, with warm amber light spilling from workshops and taverns. The vertical canyons between buildings create dramatic light-and-shadow plays. The Upper Wards catch more natural light; the Lower Wards are darker, lit by torch and forge.

### Scale & Density
Massive and dense. The three-sector, three-ward structure creates nine distinct neighborhoods stacked vertically within the mountain.

- Time Keeping Device Chamber
- Battlemap locations: Cavern secret entrance, gatehouse, mine exit, secret pathway behind waterfall

**Middle Ward -- Military Barracks:**
- [[Hungry Irons Weapon Emporium]]
- [[The Circle of Honor]] -- Arena/dueling ground
- [[The Crucible]] -- with sewers below

**Upper Ward -- Wealthy Estates:**
- Oblivion's Throneroom (battlemap)
- Peraxis' Study (battlemap)

### Brownbeard Sector
The commercial and religious heart.

**Lower Ward -- Docks:**
- [[The Foundry]] -- Lower-tier tavern with the Craftkeepers

Underground tunnels connecting all sectors.

## History

The Dwarven Civil War defines Vag'Mahar's current crisis. On the surface, the three Thanes of Blackbeard, Brownbeard, and Redbeard districts are locked in political struggle. Beneath this, all three secret police forces -- the Blackguard, Truth's Light, and the Silent Flame -- are controlled by the same entity: [[Peraxis Phyrexia]], an Illithilich in the Elder Brain Chamber beneath the Blackbeard mines. Its goal is to use the Anchor of War (a hammer capable of causing earthquakes strong enough to sink the city) to breed better Duergar.

The last trueborn king, **Thorson Bronzebeard II**, was killed by a falling Blood Star meteorite. The only path to resolution is crowning the bastard [[dorrin]] -- connected to [[Bergs]] and [[Daina]] -- as the new paragon. His golem, Roc, is the first new Warforged.

The [[Mag'har|Forgesworn]] -- ancient weapon-makers whose creations consume a portion of their soul -- are scattered but several reside in or near the city.
