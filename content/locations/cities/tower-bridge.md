# Tower Bridge

A once-proud fortress on the shores of [[The Stilllake]] in [[DawnHaven]], now a troll-infested ruin. Tower Bridge's destruction created a wave of refugees that flooded [[dawnpass]], creating the Bridgetown crisis. [[Mordred]] appeared at Tower Bridge as a harbinger of its destruction. The settlement was guarded by the Tower Bridge Battalion before its fall.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
Set on the shores of the Stilllake, Tower Bridge was a fortified crossing point. The reference art shows blasted stone towers, crumbling walls, and burning debris against a dark sky. A ruined fort on a lakeside promontory.

### Color Palette
Destruction tones: fire orange, smoke black, rubble grey, dark water.

## Notable Features

- Troll infestation: Dire Trolls, Mushroom Trolls, Spirit Trolls, and standard Trolls
- Lady of the Lake -- mystical figure associated with the Stilllake
- Mordred's appearance at Tower Bridge
