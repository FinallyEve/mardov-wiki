# Qual'Xar

A half-elf seafaring nation existing primarily as a fleet of ships rather than a fixed settlement. Qual'Xar is one of the three signatories to the [[Matrim Pact]], the ancient alliance between human sailors of old Uma, gnomes of [[faeish]], and the Qual'Xar half-elves. Their fleet navigates the Sand Seas and Serpent Sea, connecting the disparate regions of [[Mardov]].

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
Qual'Xar has no fixed city -- it IS its fleet. The ships anchor at various island ports and travel between the Sand Seas (desert-ocean surrounding the islands) and the wider seas. The Sand Seas Market serves as a floating bazaar.

### Architectural Style
Nautical craftsmanship. Each vessel is unique -- from the elegant [[Thornberry Shade]] to the gnomish [[Flooded Roots]] to the military [[Crimson Driver]]. Ships feature carved figureheads, elaborate rigging, and below-deck living quarters. The Treesinger tradition (led by [[Thespus Sparrow]]) suggests ships are grown as much as built.

### Color Palette
Ocean blues, weathered wood browns, sail whites, and the emerald green of the Emerald Veil.

## History

The Qual'Xar half-elves trace their origins to the cataclysm that destroyed Uma. When [[Tygras]]'s superweapon detonated, a tsunami forced the survivors -- human sailors, gnomes, and half-elves -- onto the back of the giant turtle Maturin. This became the [[Matrim Pact]], with a hidden capital (a buried pyramid containing the [[Harvest's Zenith]]) atop the turtle. The Thornberry Shade's navigational map revealed the five critical campaign locations: [[Valley of Anchors]], [[Vag'Mahar]], [[machu]], [[bynar]], and [[Tygras Logath]].

The StormKing's wrath threatens the fleet at sea, and Krakens patrol the Sand Seas.
