# The Tidepool

A Water Genasi city built into the coastal rock and coral formations of the [[islands]] region. The Tidepool is a hidden settlement accessible through salt cliffs and hidden coves, with architecture that blends seamlessly into the tidal landscape.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
A coastal settlement built into caves, coral formations, and tidal pools. The reference art shows salt cliffs, hidden coves, shipwrecks, a barbed cove, and a Tortle village (Thatch). The Tide Tree -- a massive tree growing from the waterline -- serves as a natural landmark.

### Color Palette
Ocean blues, coral pinks, salt whites, weathered timber, and the green of tidal algae.

## Notable Features

- Barbed Cove -- Resting place of a Young Giant
- Boneyard -- Ship graveyard
- Hidden Cove
- Salt Cliffs
- The Tide Tree
- The Forgotten Shore
- Thatch -- Tortle Village
- Shipwrecked Unladen Swallow
