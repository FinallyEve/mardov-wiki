# Vag'Mahar

![[assets/maps-source/Vag Mahar Mountain View.jpg]]
*The mountain vista of Vag'Mahar -- towering stone pillars and tiered pagoda-style buildings climbing a narrow valley between massive cliff faces.*

The great dwarven fortress-city of [[monethern]], carved into the face of a mountain range. Vag'Mahar is the ancestral seat of dwarven power, home to the [[Mag'har|Forgesworn]], and site of the [[Mountain's Zenith]] -- one of the four elemental Zeniths whose ascension or corruption determines the fate of Mardov. Divided into three sectors -- **Blackbeard**, **Brownbeard**, and **Redbeard** -- each governed by a Thane and containing three vertical wards (Lower, Middle, Upper), the city is a nine-district labyrinth of forges, temples, barracks, and underground tunnels.

The city is currently under martial law. The Blackbeards -- the police force of Vag'Mahar -- have shut down all embassies and sealed the borders to outsiders, especially tieflings. Beneath the political struggle of the three Thanes, the true power belongs to [[Peraxis Phyrexia]], an Illithilich operating from the Elder Brain Chamber beneath the Blackbeard mines, who secretly controls the city's three secret police forces.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape

Vag'Mahar sits in a narrow mountain valley, framed on both sides by massive cliff faces. The city is carved from the living rock -- not built upon the mountain but extracted from within it. Stone pillars and tiered buildings climb vertically through the canyon, layer upon layer, creating a densely stacked fortress-city of monumental scale. A waterfall and frozen water feature mark the Blackbeard sector on the eastern side. A red hexagonal marker -- the Blood Star -- sits above the city.

![[assets/maps-source/Monethern/Vag Mahar/Pathway to Monethern/Vagnahar entrance.jpg]]
*The approach from Monethern -- a traveler looks toward the spired fortress perched on red-stone mountains.*

### Architectural Style

The art reveals an Asian-influenced dwarven aesthetic -- pagoda-style rooftops with curved eaves, ornamental archways, and vertical stone pillars. Red and blue accent colors mark different districts against the dominant grey-brown stone. The buildings are monumental in scale, with grand ceremonial gates and columned halls visible throughout.

![[assets/maps-source/Monethern/Vag Mahar/Pathway to Monethern/Pathway to the Standing Stones/Monethern Entrance to the Vag' Mahar.jpg]]
*Monumental warrior statues flanking the entrance corridor from Monethern -- grand, formal, and ancient.*

### Color Palette

Stone grey and warm brown dominate, with red accents (Redbeard), dark iron tones (Blackbeard), and earth browns (Brownbeard). Forge-fire orange glows from windows and vents. The mountain vista shows a warm pinkish-rose haze across the peaks.

### Lighting & Atmosphere

Forge-lit interiors, with warm amber light spilling from workshops and taverns. The vertical canyons between buildings create dramatic light-and-shadow plays. The Upper Wards catch more natural light; the Lower Wards are darker, lit by torch and forge. The "view of the Gate" approach reveals a starkly different mood -- green-tinted, alien, and oppressive -- possibly representing the Underdark approach or Drow Consulate entrance.

![[assets/maps-source/Monethern/Vag Mahar/Vag' Mahar view of the Gate.jpg]]
*The Gate approach -- green-tinted and oppressive, a stark contrast to the warm dwarven interior.*

## Governance & Police Forces

### The Blackbeards

The Blackbeards are the police force of Vag'Mahar (DM confirmed). Under martial law declared by Thane Thorin Ghosteye, all embassies have been shut down and borders sealed. Tieflings are banned -- the regime blames "the influences of outside gods and tieflings" for the Spell Plague that created the Drab.

## The Forgesworn (Mag'har)

The ancient dwarven order of weapon-makers. Each Forgesworn is a "Maghar" -- a titled leader associated with a specific weapon or tool. Their creations consume a portion of the creator's soul. To revitalize, they must bathe in lava at the Kiln of the First Flame. Apprenticeship is familial -- you must be born into it. Only two Forgesworn exist per symbol at any time: a master and an apprentice.

Long ago, the twelve original Maghar gathered beneath what is now Vag'Mahar and created the Warforged -- beings incapable of being corrupted by Mind Flayers. This drove the Mind Flayers back and brought safety to the dwarven peoples.

To gain your mark, you must activate a Duergar fortress (one of the thirteen D-class fortresses that hold the Mind Flayers back) or inherit it when the master dies.

## History

### The Duergar Origin

Long ago, the Duergar were part of the dwarven clans but were considered lower class. When the Mind Flayers came, the Duergar were the first to fall -- enslaved, their greed driving them deeper underground. **Laduguer** was the first to break the Mind Flayer's bond by making a pact with Asmodeus. This pact led to the creation of tieflings. Together, Laduguer and his followers fought back and established the thirteen D-class fortresses (Duergar fortresses) that hold the Mind Flayers at bay to this day. The Duergar were ultimately ostracized despite their sacrifice.

### The Forgesworn Creation

Once the twelve original Maghar gathered beneath what is now Vag'Mahar and created the Warforged -- beings incapable of Mind Flayer corruption. The Warforged turned the tide and drove the Mind Flayers back. Knowledge was passed from generation to generation, and the Forgesworn were born. Because they put their souls into their weapons, they needed a way to revitalize -- the bath in the lava at the Kiln of the First Flame.

### The Fall of King Thorson

The last trueborn king, **Thorson Bronzebeard II**, was killed by a falling Blood Star meteorite -- a meteorite that turned red as it fell and literally struck the king dead. With no trueborn heir remaining, the succession crisis created the political vacuum that Peraxis Phyrexia exploited.

### The Dwarven Civil War

On the surface, the three Thanes of Blackbeard, Brownbeard, and Redbeard districts are locked in political struggle. Beneath this, all three secret police forces -- the Iron Fist, the Craftsmen Eye, and the Arms of Righteous -- are controlled by Peraxis Phyrexia from the Elder Brain Chamber. The city operates under martial law, sealed against outsiders.

### The Path to Resolution

The only path forward is crowning the bastard [[dorrin]] as the new paragon. He is connected to both [[Bergs]] and [[Daina]] and is the only one who can build and control Warforged. His golem Roc represents the first new Warforged -- a symbol that the ancient compact can be renewed.

The Eldar's power is sealed within the War Zenith. Ascending the Anchor of War (Daina's axe, unascended) is the key to starting the chain that frees Vag'Mahar.

## The Mountain's Zenith

The Mountain's Zenith lies beneath Vag'Mahar, accessed through the Stone Sanctum in the Brownbeard Upper Ward. It houses:

- **Heart of the Forge** -- a garnet, the Zenith's gemstone
- **Anchor of War** -- a hammer-axe-javelin (tri-form weapon) capable of causing earthquakes strong enough to sink the city. Currently unascended as Daina's axe.

The War Zenith is one of four elemental Zeniths whose fate determines whether Gard'Daemon (the apocalypse) can be prevented. Peraxis Phyrexia, as Vecna's Chosen for the War Zenith, seeks to corrupt it.

## Campaign Significance

Vag'Mahar is the destination for the "dwarf party" split -- Daina, Bergs, Nocturnus, Irethia, and Bardin traveled here while the rest of the party pursued the Forest Zenith. Their objectives:

1. Make contact with Decan Smithrunner (knew Daina's mother)
2. Fulfill Nocturnus's prophecy (which dictated he travel to Vag'Mahar)
3. Plead for military aid for the Earth Genasi Clan (Dune's mission)
4. Research the Kiln of the First Flame and the origin of tieflings (Yousef's mission)
5. Secure Daina's birthright as a Fireforge and Forgesworn of the Axe

The assault on Vag'Mahar represents the third such attempt in history. Bergs, as Craftmaster, leads the Craftkeepers (the dwarven resistance). The endgame: sneak into the volcano, ascend the axe, start the chain that frees Vag'Mahar.

A two-eyed creature (scried by Lilith) hovers above the city, controlled by thirteen blind creatures -- the Mind Flayer network maintaining Peraxis's grip.

## Duergar Fortresses (D-Class)

Thirteen fortresses established by the Duergar to hold back the Mind Flayers. Each is tied to a Forgesworn symbol. The thirteenth is a secret fortress beneath Vag'Mahar itself. Known fortresses mentioned in play:

- **D'Poka** -- Above the Kiln of the First Flame, houses the Lockbox
- **D'Bulvark** and **D'Moore** -- Below the Crucible
- **Dvinter Castle** -- Visited by the party (one of thirteen)
- **Greyspear Castle** -- Elder Brain Chamber area, Duergar fortress
