# Sunless Citadel

An ancient citadel buried in [[The Wastes]] of southern [[DawnHaven]], the Sunless Citadel is a shrine to the Serpent's Chosen -- the original Yuan-Ti. Connected to [[Dallor]]'s zodiac (Serpent) and [[Stalwart]]'s quest for the Heart of the Faith. The citadel is where the Yuan-Ti stole the Anchor of Pestilence, triggering the ruin of the [[Winter's Curators]] in [[bynar]].

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
Set in the desert Wastes, the citadel is accessed through sand gnome hills, caravan approaches, and salt flats. The reference art shows a massive ancient structure visible from the sea, with cobra-shaped cloud formations above. The entrance features stone doorways inscribed with warnings -- "What Lies Within." Below the surface, a Water Genasi kingdom lies within the ancient citadel structure.

### Architectural Style
Ancient Near Eastern / Yuan-Ti. Serpentine motifs, cobra-shaped architecture, ritual chambers, and plague waters. The Desert's Zenith (Tomb of Pardona) sits within. The interior includes a cultist arena, serpent lairs, and temples to the serpent deity.

### Color Palette
Desert golds and browns, serpent greens, ritual crimson, and the sickly yellow of plague waters. Salt flat whites. Ancient stone grey within.

- **[[Casstantinople Pserephonie]]** -- Amber-eyed Yuan-Ti leader
- **[[Sessatha Hss'tafi]]** -- Leader of the Scales of Falak
- Serpent Spawn and Serpent's Chosen -- Yuan-Ti elite

## History

[[Dallor]] determines that the Sunless Citadel is the original shrine to the Serpent's Chosen -- the proto-Yuan-Ti. The theft of the Anchor of Pestilence from here ruined the [[Winter's Curators]] in Bynar. [[Stalwart]] discovers that the Heart of the Faith was stolen by a White Dragon, requiring a quest through the citadel's depths and beyond to recover it.
