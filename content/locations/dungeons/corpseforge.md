# Corpseforge

An ancient dwarven temple in the [[underdark]] that once channeled the power of [[moradin]], god of dwarves. Now corrupted, the Corpseforge contains the ghost of [[King Korrin Phyrexia]] and serves as a site of forbidden creation rituals. Its ziggurat architecture predates modern dwarven civilization.

## Notable Features

- Old Monethern Ziggurat -- Ancient stepped pyramid
- Arcane Observation Station
- Sandstone Doorway to the Temple
- Pit Fight arena
- **Chamber Channeling the God of Dwarves Moradin** -- The heart of the temple

## Chamber of Moradin

The innermost sanctum where dwarven creation rituals were performed:
- Arcana Ghost of [[King Korrin Phyrexia]] -- Spectral king
- Dwarf Creation Ritual -- Ancient rite
- [[Flemmin Fireforge]] -- Present-day dwarf
- [[Laudgar Duergar]] -- Duergar connection
- [[Maghar Greyspear]] -- Warrior
- [[Reginald Providence]] -- Maghar of the Belt, Therzadun Sworn Lich

## Key NPCs

- **[[King Korrin Phyrexia]]** -- Ghost of the ancient king, addresses his people
- **[[Peraxis Phyrexia]]** -- Duke of Old Monethern (same family as the ghost king)
- **[[Theodosia Thexis]]** -- Research Assistant to the King
- **[[Ambassador Uriella Moore]]** -- Duchess of Machu, present at the Corpseforge
