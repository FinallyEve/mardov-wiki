# Myconid Grove

A bioluminescent fungal forest in the [[underdark]], the Myconid Grove is a breathtaking but dangerous subterranean ecosystem. Towering mushroom canopies create a canopy layer, while the BloodVine River (a Feywild ley-line) courses through the cavern floor. The grove is home to the Myconid Sovereign and its spore-connected community, but also harbors the Lair of the Corpseforger in its depths.

## Notable Features

- **FungalFall** -- The main grove chamber, a waterfall of spore-laden water
- **BloodVine River** -- Feywild ley-line running through the grove
- **Path of the Many Shadows** -- Dangerous approach route
- **Wandering Spore Path** -- Fungal tunnel network

## Inhabitants

- **Myconid Sovereign** -- Leader of the mushroom-folk
- **Myconid Spores** -- The grove's population
- **Rot Trolls** -- Fungal-corrupted trolls (multiple encountered)
- **The Many Shadows** -- Mysterious entities on the path

## Lair of the Corpseforger

Deep within the grove, the Corpseforger creates undead monstrosities:
- [[The Corpseforger]] -- The entity creating zombie dwarves
- [[jesabelle]] -- Sea Hag, connected to the coven from [[D'Guarda Fortress]]
- Alhoon Fixers -- Mind Flayer undead servants
- Entrance to the Ultroloth's Lair
- "The Missing 30" -- Thirty dwarves turned into zombies
- Fungal Zombies, Zombie Dwarves, Surprise Dead Dwarf
