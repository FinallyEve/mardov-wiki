# Haunted Forest

A cursed woodland in [[DawnHaven]], the Haunted Forest lies between [[argos]] and [[machu]]. It contains the Argosian Woods (remnants of Argos's former territory), Camp Xerxes (an elven war camp), the Forest of Liars (where nothing is as it seems), and the banks of the [[Mortis River]].

## Sub-locations

### Argosian Woods
The forest near ruined [[argos]].
- [[Dallor]] -- Encountered here
- Horned Forest King -- Guardian entity
- Owlbear Cave
- Ruth's Hut -- Home of the Diviner [[Ruth]]
- The Beast from the Forest

### Camp Xerxes
An elven war camp.
- The Last Artificer -- Sole remaining engineer
- Elf warcamp -- Military encampment
- Connected to [[Xerxes]], whose final act was to protect the forest

### Forest of Liars
An enchanted area where deception reigns.
- Forest of Liars -- The forest itself distorts truth
- Great Liar -- Entity within
- Madam Fervor's Caravan -- Passes through

### Mortis River
The dark river flowing through the forest.
- Keeptown -- Settlement on the river
