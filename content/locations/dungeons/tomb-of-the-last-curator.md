# Tomb of the Last Curator

An ancient tomb in [[vartos]] containing the four hieroglyphic rooms that describe each Zenith -- the prophetic heart of the campaign's quest structure. Also known as the Tomb of the First Curator, it is guarded by the [[Clan of the Red Hand]] and connected to the [[Valley of Anchors]].

## The Four Prophecy Rooms

### Forest Room (Death)
Hieroglyphics depicting:
- Seven-point crown of sludge on a small island/throne
- Triad: woman with raven-black hair, two similar men (one black hair unkempt, one long white hair)
- Large antlered beast with glow-stone in its head
- Doorway bathed in moonlight / same building without door and moon
- Two crystalline shapes flanking a pool
- A Lich reaching for a doe
- A great elk with a locket between its antlers
- A rabbit with head in a book, holding phylactery and scythe
- **Riddle answer: Death**

### Mountain Room (War)
Hieroglyphics depicting:
- City with three massive mechanical dwarf walls, largest with hole in heart
- Red-haired dwarf missing right eye
- Hammer-and-anvil crest
- Large bat on stalagmite, wretched woman drinking its drip (vampire)
- Wolf and wolverine beside frozen volcano
- Massive forge with glowing red-eyed giant (transparent, gaunt old dwarf inside)
- Shadowy figure behind modest forge worker
- Tentacle rising from chasm (Mind Flayer)
- Two clashing armies beneath all
- **Riddle answer: War**

### Winter Room (Pestilence)
Hieroglyphics depicting:
- Enormous serpent with angler fish lure
- Snow-colored dragonborn tribe around great rock
- Large turtle with pan flute pattern, dormant
- White dragon clutching blue gemstone
- Dark shadow behind townsfolk
- Shield with vacant gemstone inlay, wielded by a mongoose
- Citadel in desert with cobra-shaped cloud
- Frozen fortress guarded by five-dragonhead-tabard giants
- **Riddle answer: Pestilence**

### Harvest Room (Famine)
Hieroglyphics depicting:
- Upside-down city with glowing gardens
- Snow-white tiefling girl with two tall owl-winged shadows
- Red tiefling shaking hands with floating man with cane
- Fox with silver eyes and ethereal wing-tail
- Building with suspended orbs around three towers
- Woman in headdress on horse before army vs. orc male pointing
- Three mice around flaming mousetrap
- Overflowing well with diadem below surface
- Burned field with nightmarish scarecrows and obsidian guards
- **Riddle answer: Famine**

## Guardians

### Clan of the Red Hand
Tomb protectors:
- [[Grenda Far'Tul]] -- Last Hobgoblin, Founder of the Betrayed
- Garg, Ulfric, Yoshido -- Red Hand Clan members
- [[fa-da-shu]] -- Gnoll Pack Lord
- [[Kolyma]] -- The Original Curator
- [[Manitou]] -- Fox Zodiac
