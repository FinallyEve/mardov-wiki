# The Wastes

The desert region of southern [[DawnHaven]], created by the destruction of the ancient civilization of Uma when [[Tygras]]'s superweapon detonated. The Wastes contain petrified oasis trees, shifting sand seas, the ruins of [[Goldentooth Village]], and the [[Sunless Citadel]] -- an ancient Yuan-Ti temple buried in the desert.
