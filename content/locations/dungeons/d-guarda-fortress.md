# D'Guarda Fortress

The party's stronghold (Group A) in the [[underdark]], D'Guarda Fortress was cleared of hostile creatures and renovated into a functioning base of operations. Originally home to an Aboleth ("The Sleepless") and a sea hag coven, the fortress was reclaimed and upgraded with new fortifications, a teleportation room, and allied followers.

## Before Renovation

The fortress in its original state:
- [[The Sleepless]] -- Aboleth controlling the site
- Jesabell, Rea, and Nagani -- Sea Hag Coven
- Scion of the Deep
- Corrupted Chuul
- [[Fracheal Xen]] -- Inhabitant

## After Renovation

### Group A Stronghold
The renovated fortress.
- D'Vinter Fortress Map -- Tactical layout
- [[Sesame Tower]] -- Defensive tower with battlemap
- Teleportation Room -- Magical transit hub
- Aboleth Lair -- Repurposed sub-level
- The Darkest Knights Runestone

### Farthen Dur Masons
Construction crew for the renovation.
- Hulbran Fren, Lissan Fren (Drab)
- Bensen Halsbry (Victim 1), Shreetra Halsbry (Drab)
- Fallsby Starm (Victim 2), Venthrim Starm (Drab)

### Followers
Allied NPCs at the stronghold.
- [[Halax]] -- Cyclops
- [[Nessa Kaitan]] -- Elven Ambassador from Machu
- [[Pumpernickle Squatsquallow]] -- Gnome follower
- [[Umbara Skuttle]] -- Weaver
- [[Yohan Harveld]] -- Militia Leader
- Dorry, Lorry, Morry -- Dwarf workers
- [[Emofa Ravenbite]] -- Assassin from Vag'Mahar, after the Craftkeeper
