# Feywilde

The plane of nature, emotion, and fae magic. The Feywilde mirrors the material plane but in heightened, exaggerated form -- colors are more vivid, emotions more intense, and time flows unpredictably. It is divided into four seasonal courts, each ruling a domain. Accessible from the material plane through the Evallan Waystone, the Razelsin Shortcut (Four Corner Door), and various natural thin points.

## Planar Characteristics

- **Gravity:** Normal but lighter -- jumping goes further, falling is slower
- **Light:** Perpetual golden-hour light that shifts hue with the season of the local court
- **Atmosphere:** Intoxicating. The air itself carries fae enchantment. Mortals who stay too long may forget their material lives.
- **Time:** Wildly distorted. Hours may pass as years, or years as minutes. No consistency.

## The Four Courts

### Brier Patch -- Spring Court
The domain of growth and new beginnings.
- **Bramble Court** -- The governing seat
- **Hanging Gardens of the Briar Patch** -- Vertical gardens of impossible flowers
- Portal to the Glittering Run via Bramble Bounty

### Flowerfield -- Summer Court
The domain of warmth and passion. Connected to [[winklebottom]] (Fool of the Spring Court, who crosses into Summer).

### Reaper's Bounty -- Autumn Court
The domain of harvest and decline. Connected to [[Reynaldine]] (Fey Lord of Famine) and the [[Harvest's Zenith]] corruption.

### Snowcrest Sanctum -- Winter Court
The domain of cold and endings.
- Frozen Steps
- [[grumblesin]] -- Lord of the Winter Court (also "Chosen" connected to Olkin in [[frostheim]])
- Tomb of Father Winter
- Vale of Ice and Shadow

### Glittering Run
A neutral territory between courts -- a shimmering river of pure fae energy.
