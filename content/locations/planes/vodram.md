# Vodram

A lost world existing as an outer plane, Vodram is a realm of ancient ruins, fey magic, and primal forces. It contains its own pocket Feywilde and the Valheim stronghold. Vodram is connected to the Zodiac Locket and the serpent Ananta, and was once home to the Last Curator, Friedrich.

## Planar Characteristics

- **Gravity:** Normal
- **Light:** Ancient, amber-toned -- like perpetual late afternoon in an old world
- **Atmosphere:** Haunted by echoes. The plane feels like a memory -- populated by remnants and spectres of what once was.

## History

Vodram represents a world that fell -- its Zenith of Peace suggests it once had its own anchor system that failed or was destroyed. The Abbey of St Ashbringer and the remnant of the "last ascension" suggest a catastrophic event. Vecna's Domain is associated with Vodram, implying the god of secrets played a role in its fall. The Vodram Feywilde preserves what the material Vodram lost.
