# Shadowfell

The plane of shadow and death, the Shadowfell is the domain of the [[Raven Queen]] and the resting place of departed souls. It is accessible from the material plane through Gravewind on [[Mt. Dragonspine]] in [[bynar]]. The plane contains RavenFall (the Raven Queen's seat), the Vision Gate (where individual characters receive personal visions), and the Gate of the Broken.

## Planar Characteristics

- **Gravity:** Normal
- **Light:** Perpetual twilight. Pale, diffuse grey-purple illumination with no visible sun. Shadows move independently.
- **Atmosphere:** Cold, still, silent. Sound is muffled. Colors are desaturated except for points of magical light.
- **Time:** Distorted. Time passes differently; visitors may return having aged more or less than expected.

## Visual Description

The reference art shows a landscape of ash-grey salt flats, shattered pathways, and spectral light. The Lake of Souls is a vast still body of water. Doorways and portals glow with faint spectral energy. The overall palette is grey, purple, silver, and black -- with occasional bursts of green or blue where the veil between worlds is thin.

## History

The Shadowfell is central to [[Mordred]]'s plan. He manipulates the Avatar of the Raven Queen into sacrificing her immortality, creating a rift between the material world and [[V-MARDOV]]. The Shadowfell serves as both a destination for the party's personal visions (via the Vision Gate) and a battleground in the cosmic war between death and undeath.
