# Outer Planes of Mardov

The cosmology of [[Mardov]] extends far beyond the material continent. The outer planes are interconnected realms of divine, fae, and shadow power, each accessible through specific portals and rituals.

## The Planes

### Major Planes (Detailed Pages)
- [[shadowfell]] -- Plane of shadow and death, domain of the [[Raven Queen]]
- [[feywilde]] -- Plane of nature and fae magic, four seasonal courts
- [[vodram]] -- Lost world with its own pocket Feywilde

### Other Planes

#### 7 Heavens
The celestial realm. Home to angelic beings and the purest divine power. Connected to [[Bahamut]]'s domain.

#### 9 Hells
The infernal realm. Domain of [[Asmodeus]] and other archdevils. Contains Uma from the 9 Hells ([[hellheim]]).

#### Astral Sea
The space between planes.
- **Betwixt** -- Liminal space within the Astral Sea
- **The Veil** -- Boundary between material and astral

#### Eaden
A plane of paradise or restoration. Minimal reference art.

#### Uvan
A planar location associated with:
- Flooded Temple
- [[Lyra Fairisle]] -- Resident, connected to Tritherion's domain "Petal of the Rose"

#### The Moon
Accessible lunar location.
- **Euclid's Study** -- Scholarly retreat on the moon

#### V-MARDOV
The parallel dimension where [[Vecna]]'s avatar rules. A mirror of the material plane containing a "Death's Zenith" that mirrors the Forest's Zenith. The party will be transported here after defeating the Forest Zenith boss. [[V-MORDRED]]'s invasion originates from this plane.
