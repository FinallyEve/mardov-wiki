# DawnHaven

The largest and most diverse region on the continent of [[Mardov]], DawnHaven stretches from coastal cliffs in the west to the World Spine Mountain Range in the east. It is the political and cultural heart of the known world, containing more settled cities, trade routes, and points of power than any other region. Two of the four Zeniths -- the [[Forest's Zenith]] in [[machu]] and the [[Harvest's Zenith]] in [[Tygras Logath]] -- lie within its borders, making DawnHaven the most contested territory in the campaign against [[Vecna]].

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
DawnHaven occupies the central mass of the continent. The hex map shows it as a patchwork of green forests, golden farmlands, winding rivers, and mountain foothills. The [[MeadowVine River]] and [[Mortis River]] cut through the region, feeding fertile valleys. To the northwest, dense forests give way to the towering peaks where [[Cloud City]] floats above the [[Storm Peaks]]. The southern reaches dry into the arid expanse of [[The Wastes]], while the eastern edge rises toward the [[World Spine Mountain Range]].

### Climate & Vegetation
Temperate and varied. The northern forests ([[Evallan Forest]], [[Haunted Forest]]) are lush and old-growth, heavy with moss and ancient trees. The central plains around [[windhaven]] are pastoral -- rolling farmland, orchards, and scattered windmills. The south transitions abruptly into desert badlands ([[The Wastes]]) with petrified oasis trees and sand seas. The western coast is rocky with hidden grottos and tidal pools.

### Color Palette
Greens and golds dominate the hex map -- forest hexes in deep emerald, farmland in harvest gold and pale wheat. Rivers appear as thin blue-grey lines. The region name is rendered in warm brown serif text against the golden center of the continent.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
DawnHaven feels perpetually in the golden hour. The reference art conveys warmth, bounty, and a pastoral beauty under threat. The Everstorm -- a permanent tempest near Cloud City -- provides dramatic contrast to the otherwise gentle climate.

### Scale & Density
By far the most densely populated region. The hex map shows multiple cities, towns, and landmarks packed into its borders -- more than all other regions combined.

## Notable Features

- **The Windmill Willow** -- A colossal sentient willow tree at the heart of [[windhaven]], home to the Androsphinx ruler [[Leonidas]]. Its canopy of pink blossoms dominates the city skyline.
- **The Everstorm** -- A permanent magical storm above the [[Storm Peaks]], creating a barrier around [[Cloud City]].
- **The Game Trail** -- The wilderness road connecting [[windhaven]] to the wider region, dotted with fringe farms and dangerous encounters.
- **The Mortis River** -- A dark, cursed waterway flowing through [[machu]] and the [[Haunted Forest]].
- **The Forest's Zenith** -- Ancient nexus of cosmic power within [[machu]], containing the Heart of the Wilde (emerald) and the Anchor of Death (obsidian sword).
- **The Harvest's Zenith** -- Buried within [[Tygras Logath]], containing the Heart of the Progenitors (ruby) and the Anchor of Famine (Diadem of Dawn).

## History

DawnHaven's history is shaped by two catastrophes. The first was the destruction of Uma by [[Tygras]]'s superweapon, which created the desert Wastes in the south and forced the survivors into the [[Matrim Pact]] -- an ancient alliance between humans, gnomes, and half-elves who found refuge atop the Maturin Turtle. The second ongoing crisis is the **Orc-Human War**: the Orc Chieftain of [[Stra'Franar]], corrupted by the same fae demon that empowered Tygras, has launched an invasion pressuring [[dawnpass]] and cutting off food from the fertile Harvestlands. [[Mordred]] manipulates this conflict to advance his plan to destroy the keystones and enable [[V-MORDRED]]'s invasion.

The region has seen the party's journey from their recruitment at [[Conquis Abbey]], through the chaos of [[windhaven]]'s Harvest Festival, into the depths of the [[underdark]], and up to the floating islands of [[Cloud City]].
