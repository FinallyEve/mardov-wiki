# Bynar

The arctic mountain region of [[Mardov]], Bynar sits in the northwest of the continent -- a frozen wilderness of jagged peaks, glacial valleys, and permanent snow. The [[Winter's Zenith]] lies within the mountain of Bynar itself, guarded by a White Dragon clutching the Heart of the Faith (sapphire). The region is home to the ruined dragon-kin tribe known as the **Winter's Curators**, destroyed when the Yuan-Ti stole the Anchor of Pestilence.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The Bynar Map reveals an island-continent with a central caldera -- rings of green-forested coastline giving way to barren rocky highlands, then a snow-white frozen interior. The transition from verdant lowlands to frozen wasteland is dramatic and concentric, as if the cold radiates outward from the mountain's heart. On the continental hex map, Bynar occupies the northwest, shown as a separate landmass connected by sea to DawnHaven, with a large dragon perched on its northern peaks.

### Architectural Style
Sparse and rugged. The reference art shows a coastal scene at sunset -- massive sea-stacks rising from tidal flats, a lone campfire on the shore. Inland settlements like Ft. Bynar and the Frozen Village are rendered as simple stone-and-timber structures half-buried in snow. The Halls of Reflection and Temple of Fate suggest more elaborate underground complexes carved from ice and stone.

### Color Palette
Dramatic sunset oranges and pinks at the coast, transitioning to blue-white glacial tones inland. The Bynar Map uses layered greens (coast), tans and greys (rocky highlands), and stark white (frozen center) with a yellow-gold lowland area suggesting geothermal activity.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
The coastal art is suffused with warm sunset light -- golden hour reflecting off wet tidal flats, silhouetted sea-stacks against a peach-and-violet sky. The interior is the opposite: pale, diffuse light filtered through snowfall and ice caverns. A sense of isolation and primordial stillness pervades.

### Scale & Density
Extremely sparse. A few scattered settlements clinging to the edges of an overwhelming wilderness. The interior is almost entirely uninhabited.

## Notable Features

- **The Winter's Zenith** -- Within Mt. Bynar, guarded by a White Dragon, containing the Heart of the Faith (sapphire) and connected to the Anchor of Pestilence
- **The Cradle** -- Mountain range containing Mt. Bynar, Mt. Dragonspine, and Shard Mountain
- **Gravewind** -- Gateway to the [[shadowfell]], located on Mt. Dragonspine
- **The Eternal Flame of the Silver Dragon** -- Sacred site on Mt. Dragonspine
- **Dragonwell** -- Ancient site on Mt. Dragonspine
- **Red Banner Crossing** -- Dangerous mountain pass

## History

The Winter's Curators -- a tribe of snow-colored dragonborn -- once guarded the Winter's Zenith and the Heart of the Faith. Their ruin came when the [[Scales of Falak|Yuan-Ti]] stole the Anchor of Pestilence from the [[Sunless Citadel]], and a fae eel serpent swallowed the Heart of the Faith. The region is now plagued by the serpent and its corruption. Resolution requires [[Stalwart]] to deliver retribution to the serpent, recover the Heart of the Faith, and deliver it to [[Conquis Abbey]]. [[Randal]] must obtain the pan flute (which IS the Anchor of Pestilence) and resurrect the turtle Mia.
