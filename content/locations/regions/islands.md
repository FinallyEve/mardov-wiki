# The Islands

The maritime territories of [[Mardov]], comprising the [[Qual'Xar]] half-elf seafaring nation, the [[Serpent Archipelago]], the [[tidepool]], and scattered volcanic isles. Connected by trade routes across the Serpent Sea and the Kraken Sea, the Islands serve as the naval lifeline between [[DawnHaven]], [[monethern]], [[bynar]], and [[vartos]].

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
The Islands are scattered across the seas surrounding the Mardov continent. On the hex map, they appear as small green landmasses amid teal-blue ocean hexes. The Serpent Sea lies to the north, the Kraken Sea to the south. Volcanic islands punctuate the chain -- one reference image shows a full volcanic cone rising from the sea.

### Architectural Style
Nautical and organic. [[Qual'Xar]] vessels are lovingly detailed -- wooden sailing ships with elaborate figureheads, each with its own character. The Tidepool has a more primordial feel -- water genasi architecture built into coral and tidal rock formations.

### Color Palette
Ocean blues and teals, weathered wood browns, sail whites, and the warm golds of sunset on water. The volcanic island brings fiery oranges and dark basalt.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Maritime golden hour. Many ship and island scenes are rendered at sunset or dawn, with warm light reflecting off calm seas. A sense of freedom and adventure pervades, tempered by the lurking menace of Krakens in the deep.

### Scale & Density
Sparse on land, dense on water. The Qual'Xar nation exists primarily as a fleet, with dozens of named vessels.

## Notable Features

- **The Qual'Xar Fleet** -- Over a dozen named vessels, each with its own captain and crew. Signatories to the [[Matrim Pact]].
- **The Sand Seas** -- Desert-ocean surrounding the islands, home to Krakens
- **The Thornberry Shade** -- Key vessel whose map showed the five critical locations (Valley of Anchors, Vag Mahar, Machu, Bynar, Tygras Logath)
- **The Tidepool** -- Water Genasi city built into coastal rock and coral
- **The Serpent Archipelago** -- Chain of islands where a crashed Nautiloid (Githyanki) ship was found

## History

The [[Qual'Xar]] half-elves are one of the three signatories to the [[Matrim Pact]] -- the ancient alliance formed when a tsunami from [[Tygras]]'s explosion forced human sailors, gnomes of [[faeish]], and Qual'Xar half-elves onto the back of the giant turtle Maturin. They promised sanctuary to each other atop the turtle, creating a hidden capital -- a buried pyramid with the [[Harvest's Zenith]] at its center. The Sand Seas surrounding the islands are home to Krakens, including the notorious Oswald Magnacog, the 3rd Kraken of the Sand Seas.
