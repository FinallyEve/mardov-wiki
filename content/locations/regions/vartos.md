# Vartos

The southwestern region of [[Mardov]], Vartos is a land of deep jungles, ancient ruins, and arcane power. It is home to the [[Magister's Circle]] -- the governing magical authority of the continent -- and the [[Valley of Anchors]], the origin point of all magic in Mardov. The region has a primeval, overgrown character, as if civilization is a thin veneer over something far older.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
On the continental hex map, Vartos (labeled "Var Taos") occupies the southwest, separated from DawnHaven by the Kraken Sea to the south and dense jungle to the east. The terrain is heavily forested with volcanic-looking mountains. A clocktower landmark (the Magister's Circle) is visible on the western coast.

The reference art for the jungles shows towering cliff faces draped in hanging vines and moss, a narrow path winding between massive rock walls. Bioluminescent purple and magenta flowers bloom in profusion, and vine bridges span the canyon overhead. The atmosphere is humid, ethereal, and slightly alien -- more like a fantasy rainforest than anything temperate.

### Architectural Style
Mixed. The [[Magister's Circle]] is depicted as a bustling harbor town with stone towers, gabled rooftops, and a prominent clock tower -- reminiscent of a Renaissance port city. The [[Distillery]] is a military-academic compound with keeps and towers. The [[Tomb of the Last Curator]] and [[Valley of Anchors]] are ancient ruins with hieroglyphic chambers, suggesting a civilization far older than anything currently standing.

### Color Palette
Deep jungle greens, vivid magenta and purple florals, blue-pink atmospheric haze in the canyons. The Magister's Circle brings warmer tones -- golden stone, brown timber, blue sea. The Valley of Anchors shifts to autumnal golds and ancient stone grey.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Mystical and verdant. Shafts of light pierce the jungle canopy. Waterfalls catch prismatic light. The overall mood is one of discovery -- ancient secrets half-hidden beneath creeping growth.

### Scale & Density
Low to medium. Two main population centers (Magister's Circle, The Distillery) with vast uninhabited jungle and ruins between them.

## Notable Features

- **The Valley of Anchors** -- "The origin of magic." Where all Anchors must be lifted and recharged. Ancient Var'Taos ruins.
- **The Tomb of the Last Curator** -- Contains four hieroglyphic rooms describing each Zenith (Death, War, Pestilence, Famine), each with a riddle
- **The Magister's Circle** -- Seat of magical governance, containing the Platinum Seats, Bard Colleges, and Magic Covenants
- **The Distillery** -- Military compound with the Actual Size Tower, Claudere Keep, Faughart grove, and the Temple to the Zodiac
- **Candle Cove** -- Port town serving the Magister's Circle

## History

Vartos is where the party's story truly begins. At the [[Magister's Circle]], an envoy from [[dawnpass]] (containing zero Orcs or Half-Orcs) requests aid against orc sorcerers wielding draconic-blood destruction magic. The Tomb of the Last Curator contains the prophetic hieroglyphics that lay out the entire campaign arc -- four rooms, four Zeniths, four riddles. The Valley of Anchors is where the endgame converges: all Anchors must be returned here to be recharged.

The region is also where the [[Cabal of Horns]] operates covertly, and where the Bard Colleges (eight cloak colors) train their apprentices.
