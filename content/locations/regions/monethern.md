# Monethern

The eastern region of [[Mardov]], Monethern is a land defined by faith, dwarven industry, and political intrigue. It sits across the Serpent Sea from [[DawnHaven]], dominated by mountain ranges, deep mines, and the holy city of [[Conquis Abbey]]. The [[Mountain's Zenith]] lies within the Stone Sanctum of [[Vag'Mahar]], housing the Heart of the Forge (garnet) and the Anchor of War.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
On the continental hex map, Monethern occupies the eastern landmass, connected to DawnHaven by land bridges and sea routes. The terrain transitions from coastal lowlands and river valleys near [[Monethern City]] to towering mountain ranges surrounding [[Vag'Mahar]]. Snow-capped peaks dominate the northern and eastern edges. The region has a more rugged, vertical character than DawnHaven -- cities are built into mountainsides and cliff faces rather than spread across plains.

### Architectural Style
Dwarven stonework dominates. [[Vag'Mahar]] is rendered in the reference art as a massive Asian-inspired fortress city carved into mountain cliffs -- towering stone pillars, tiered pagoda rooftops with red and blue accents, and monumental gateway arches. The architecture is vertically stacked, with buildings layered upon buildings climbing the mountainside. [[Conquis Abbey]] contrasts sharply -- it resembles a medieval European abbey-town with red-tile roofs, stone cathedral spires, and walled districts spread across rolling green hills.

### Color Palette
Stone greys, mountain browns, and iron reds for Vag'Mahar. Warm terra-cotta, green hills, and cathedral gold for Conquis Abbey. The hex map shows Monethern in muted greens and browns with prominent mountain hexes rendered in dark grey and white (snow).

### Lighting & Atmosphere
Forge-light and shadow. The dwarven cities are lit by furnace glow and crystalline veins in the rock. Conquis Abbey basks in pastoral sunlight filtered through stained glass. Monethern City's floating quarter sparkles during festival nights.

### Scale & Density
Medium density. Three major population centers ([[Vag'Mahar]], [[Monethern City]], [[Conquis Abbey]]) with scattered hamlets and connecting roadways.

## Notable Features

- **The Mountain's Zenith** -- Within the Stone Sanctum of [[Vag'Mahar]], containing the Heart of the Forge and the Anchor of War (a hammer-axe-javelin capable of causing earthquakes)
- **The Three Walls of Vag'Mahar** -- Massive mechanical dwarf-shaped walls guarding the city, the largest with a hole in its heart (from prophecy)
- **The Veritasium Palace** -- Seat of the Senatoriate in [[Monethern City]], where the Voices govern
- **The Blood Star** -- A meteorite that struck and killed King Thorson Bronzebeard II, fulfilling prophecy
- **The Frozen Forest** -- Pathway from Vag'Mahar through ice-covered woodland

## History

Monethern's defining crisis is the **Dwarven Civil War** in [[Vag'Mahar]]. The city's three districts -- [[Blackbeard Sector|Blackbeard]], [[Brownbeard Sector|Brownbeard]], and [[Redbeard Sector|Redbeard]] -- are each governed by a Thane, but all three secret police forces (the Blackguard, Truth's Light, and the Silent Flame) are secretly controlled by the same [[Mind Flayer]], [[Peraxis Phyrexia]], an Illithilich seeking to assault the Mountain's Zenith. Its goal: use the Anchor of War to breed better Duergar. The resolution requires crowning the bastard Dorrin as the new paragon -- he alone can build and control [[warforged]].

The region also houses the [[Silver Scales of Bahamut]], the highest priesthood of the god of justice, split between those who judge by Justice and those who judge by Righteousness.
