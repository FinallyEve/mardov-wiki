# Underdark

The vast subterranean realm beneath the mountains of [[Mardov]]. A world of eternal darkness, bioluminescent fungi, underground rivers, and warring factions. The Underdark is divided between Drow-controlled and Mind Flayer-controlled territories, with the party's stronghold ([[D'Guarda Fortress]]) carved out between them.

## Geography & Architecture

### Landscape
A multi-layered cave system with distinct ecological zones. The upper levels connect to the surface via mountain passages, mine shafts, and the Ways Tunnels. The [[Myconid Grove]] is a bioluminescent fungal forest with towering mushroom canopies and the BloodVine River (a Feywild ley-line). The deeper levels descend into the Kaivan Layer (Drow territory) and the Ulthradi Layer (Mind Flayer territory).

### Architectural Style
Varied by faction. The Drow city of Kaivan features web-draped spires and temples to Lolth -- the Shrine of the Bat is a beacon-lit temple. The Mind Flayer city of Ulthradi is organic and alien. The [[D'Guarda Fortress]] (after renovation by the party) is a practical military stronghold with a Sesame Tower, teleportation room, and aboleth lair beneath. The [[corpseforge]] contains ancient dwarven ziggurat architecture channeling Moradin.

### Color Palette
Deep purples, bioluminescent greens and blues, fungal oranges, and the pale grey of raw stone. Lichen-covered doorways glow with ambient light. The Myconid Grove pulses with organic color.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
No natural light. Everything is lit by bioluminescence, forge-fire, magical emanation, or waystone warmth. The mood ranges from claustrophobic menace in the tunnels to eerie beauty in the fungal caverns. Rot trolls and zombie dwarves lurk in the darker passages.

### Scale & Density
Massive but sparsely populated. Great distances of empty tunnel between population centers.

## Notable Features

- **The Ways Tunnels** -- Ancient transit network with waystone stations, used by "Moles" (underground guides)
- **Chamber of the Sleepless** -- Lair of an Aboleth, with its Scion and the "Original Sea Urchin"
- **The Corpseforge** -- Ancient dwarven temple channeling Moradin, now corrupted. Contains the ghost of King Korrin Phyrexia.
- **Vorago Pass** -- Exit point from the Underdark to the surface
- **The Skaagal Forest** -- Subterranean stone forest
- **Green Tides of St Ashbury** -- Hidden gnome settlement with smuggler's waterway exit

## History

The Underdark's power structure is defined by two competing factions. The **Drow** control the Kaivan Layer, led by [[Oblivion Wayne]] -- Nocturnus's vampire aunt who gave her soul to [[Tiamat]] for an ascension ritual. She threatens an invasion of the surface. The **Mind Flayers** control the Ulthradi Layer, with [[Peraxis Phyrexia]] (an Illithilich) secretly manipulating [[Vag'Mahar]]'s three secret police forces from below.

The party established their stronghold at [[D'Guarda Fortress]] after clearing it of the Aboleth known as "The Sleepless" and a sea hag coven (Jesabell, Rea, and Nagani). The Corpseforger -- an entity in the Myconid Grove -- created zombie dwarves from "The Missing 30" and threatened the region until confronted.
