# Clay Levelup plan

Gameplan-

level 1 - fighter 1 (xbows feat)
level 2 - fighter 2 (action surge)
level 3 - rogue 1 expertise (Perception, Thieves Tools), Sneak Attack, Thieves Cant
level 4 - rogue 2 cunning action
level 5 - rogue 3 Scout archetype
level 6 - fighter 3 (battle master)
level 7 - fighter 4 (sharpshooter feat)
level 8 - fighter 5 (second attack)
level 9 - rogue 4 (+2 Dex)


Backstory:
Lost his wife Molly in a murder that turned out to be a vampire claiming her for his brood. 
His wife, alive, had turned on him and left him beaten, bloody, and alone back on their farm at the age of 24. 
He spent 5 years learning how to get revenge on the creatures that ruined his peaceful life of farming and raising a family. 

Molly and Clay had decided to try and have kids, a boy named Ian and a girl named Lilly, 
when he found his wife again she had been with the brood for 5 years and had stolen 2 village children which she re-named after the unborn children. 

Clay had stalked his wife and her adopted children to a sleepy inn at a forgotten crossroads, which now served as a nest for the clan of 20 vampires. 
The vampires were terrorizing the neighboring village, which Clay had mustered a meager set of hunters from. 
Two of the men of note were James Downham and Bartrim Readding, the fathers of the stolen children. 
Their wives having been killed during the kidnapping process, these men were fighting for their children back, the last un-living reminders of the lifes left behind. 

During the raid, James and Bartrim were slain by the Alpha Vampire, Quintin Tyr. 
Bartrim wounded the beast causing it to flee and abandon Molly and the rest of the children. 
Molly who wouldnt part with her adopted family was left in a burning inn with Clay and 2 other good men. 

Having to slay his wife took its toll, the vampires had managed to amass some meager hoard of coin which Clay used to fix up the old inn calling the place, Molly's Revenge. 
His hand-crossbows, taken from the bodies of James and Bartrim are each inscribed with the names of their children, but he never learned their real names. 
So one got, Ian (Downham) and the other is Lilly (Readding). 
They serve as a reminder of the cost of evil.
