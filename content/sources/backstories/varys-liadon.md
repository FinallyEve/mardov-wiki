# Varis Liadon — Backstory

Wilderness outpost childhood. Dad ([[Yeldan Liadon|Yeldan]]) was a hunter, woodsman, guide, scout, and messenger. Mom ([[lianala]]) is a Druid. The city was [[Tower Bridge]], a village outside [[dawnpass]] and [[Eavallan Forest]] — elves and humans alike live there.

Started hunting and learning from both parents. Last big hunt was when [[Varis Liadon|Varis]] was 95 (approximately 17 for a human) — a pack of pseudo dragons that migrated through and killed sheep. After a successful hunt, parents told him to keep a trophy. He kept the skull of his first kill of the pack (tiny enough to be a trinket or piece of jewelry).

2 weeks later, dad spotted undead scouts. The local lord believed him, but couldn't convince farther people (at least, in time). 2 more weeks, and the army arrived — zombies, skeletons, wraiths, mummies — led and created by the lich [[Ned'Zailok]].

Volunteered with dad for militia, and took an adult name at the time. Fought beside dad, and local militia held for a week, hoping for relief from outside. Attacks got worse and supplies got limited after a week. Dad was sent (fastest scout) with trophies to request aid fast.

They broke a day later. The [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] arrived at the same time — too late to hold, but fast enough to cover a retreat and evac. Most of the non-militia got out, including mom. Militia took massive losses when it broke. Never found dad, and he never arrived at the lord's seat.

[[dockson]] — an honorable paladin, high-ranking [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade|STB]] general type — shamed the lord into acting. Looked like he would have challenged him to a duel (or just killed him in his throne room) if he didn't cave.

Militia kept fighting with [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade|STB]] until [[Ned'Zailok]] was defeated — which took another 18 months. Most dispersed after. Mom stayed in or near a larger city when the fight ended. [[Varis Liadon|Varis]] enlisted with [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade|STB]], hoping to find more about dad. Never did.

Been trying to help protect the weak and those screwed over by petty politics with the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade|STB]] ever since (a few years). Always keeps an ear out for word that [[Ned'Zailok]] has resurfaced. Can't ever be sure a lich is gone. Also keeps an ear out for any news of [[Yeldan Liadon|Yeldan]], but stopped expecting to hear anything not long after the end of that war.
