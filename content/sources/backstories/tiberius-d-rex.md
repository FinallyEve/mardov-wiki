# Tiberius D. Rex — Backstory

[[Tiberius D. Rex|Tiberius D Rex]] is a Bronze Dragonkin in the 70th year of his life.

As a young man, [[Tiberius D. Rex|Tiberius]] was one of the [[Bronze Curators]], the nomadic tribe who travel the wastes in the deserts of [[DawnHaven]]. The tribe is structured in a way that the elders are free to travel the world after they have put 50 years of service into wandering the wastes in penance for their shattered pact with [[Ranar Surefoot]].

For 50 long years, [[Tiberius D. Rex|Tiberius]] worked as village clergy training to one day travel the world and spread the prophecy of [[Bahamut]]:

> *"When the sand seas stir, and the [[Matrim Pact|matrim pact]] fulfilled,*
> *[[Bahamut]]'s visage will lead the way, the Lost Nexus again rebuilt."*

Now for the last 20 years he has spent his time spreading the news and exploring the rest of [[Mardov]], however he keeps getting sidetracked along the way.

## Key Characters & Locations

- [[Bronze Curators]] — Tiberius's nomadic tribe
- [[DawnHaven]] — Home region
- [[Ranar Surefoot]] — The figure whose broken pact the Curators atone for
- [[Bahamut]] — Tiberius's deity
- [[Matrim Pact]] — Ancient alliance central to the prophecy
