# Daina Fireforge — Backstory

[[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] was born in the [[Frozen Forest]] in the dead of winter and lived in the mountains behind [[Vag'Mahar]] all of her life.

Unbeknownst to her, her father ([[Thorkon Fireforge]]) was once a royal weaponsmith of amazing renown. He and his wife ([[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]]) lived well within the city of [[Vag'Mahar]], until a rival weaponsmith with the support of one of the [[bronzebeard|Bronzebeards]] sought to blacken his name by sabotaging the shipments of fine weaponry and intricate trinkets that [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] presented to the [[bronzebeard|Bronzebeards]] yearly.

When the weapons arrived all of his items had been laced with a deadly poison which affected the Red [[bronzebeard]] when he picked it up to inspect the greataxe forged for him. Forced to flee after being accused of attempted assassination of a [[bronzebeard]], [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] and [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]] fled in winter to the [[Frozen Forest]] where [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]] gave birth to the baby red haired Diesa Fireforge.

[[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]] died due to blood loss and the cold only 12 hours after naming Diesa. [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]], knowing that he could never return and devastated by the death of [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]], changed his name to Tor Ironforge and renamed Diesa to Daina. Without clan names they were forced to live apart from the other hill dwarves in solitude which suited Tor and [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] just fine. He traded regularly in the [[Frozen Forest]] with the other clans for basic necessities and would forge the weaponry they needed to trade and get by, ensuring they had just enough defects in them to ensure rumors didn't spread. He never forged any fine weaponry again other than the greataxe that he left to [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] when he died of a mysterious illness that destroyed his body in a matter of days.

On his deathbed he bequeathed to [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] the greataxe and the heirloom [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]] brought with her from [[Vag'Mahar]] marking her as an old clan with familial ties to the [[bronzebeard|Bronzebeards]].

[[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] doesn't know any of this. She just heard one of the men from the village talking about how a red bearded stranger with jet black eyes and a strange tattoo came looking for a blacksmith and was sent to Tor — interestingly enough the same day he became terribly ill.

With no family left and without the dexterity of her father, [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] set out on her own, wandering from town to town and keeping an eye out for a strange black-eyed red beard.

## DM Notes (Hidden from Players)

> This backstory is the surface-level version. The full truth — revealed through [[Thorkon's Note inside Daina's Axe]] — is that [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] was a [[forgesworn|Mag'har]], the royal weaponsmith for [[Thorson Bronzebeard II]], who betrayed the king at [[Ghost-Eye Thorrin]]'s urging. [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] also has an older brother, and [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]] died giving birth to both. [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] is later captured by [[Peraxis Phyrexia]] and used as leverage.
