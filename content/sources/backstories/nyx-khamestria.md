# Nyx Khamestria — Backstory

## Timeline

### ~2000 Years Ago
[[Tygras]] makes a deal with an Arch-Fey from [[Vecna]]'s plane, who shares the secret to channeling pure arcana in exchange for [[Tygras]] creating a portal to this world. [[Tygras]] used his power to create a weapon that would open a portal which would transport something from this world to the Fae.

[[Ranar Surefoot]], after making the [[Matrim Pact]], steals the power source for the weapon — [[Diadem of Dawn|Dawn's Diadem]] — by killing the original user of the [[Diadem of Dawn|Diadem]] in the middle of it being used. The weapon backfired and sent the whole city to the Fae, creating a passage for benevolent Fey to enter the world so long as they bond with a human.

### 26 Years Ago
[[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] is born. Her parents have made a deal with the same Fey (without their knowledge) in exchange for her soul — they will be granted the philosopher's stone (ability to channel pure arcana). They agree to have a child to be the host. That child is actually a twin-soul. Both are tieflings, meaning they are also fae-touched.

### 8 Years Ago
Due to a twist of fate, the dominant one ([[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]]) is not the vessel that the arch-fey needed. Instead her sister, who is at this time dormant, is taken. Her powers from this patron are given to her. The deal is revealed. Her parents, who believed that their deal had gone sour, spurned her as a failed experiment.

### 7 Years Ago
The arch-fey returns, this time half-in, half-out of this plane due to the last deal. It agrees to another deal with the parents who are still on the hook for the previous deal. [[Xyn]] is born — the perfect vessel and [[Tygras]]'s true heir. She is easily manipulated and submissive to her parents given her last 19 years as a passenger.

### Now
That arch-fey was busy in the time between gaining some semblance of form in this realm and returning to the parents. He returned to the site where the last plan had failed to find [[Logath HellEye|Logath]], a descendant of the orcs who lived as gladiators in the fighting pits. He put into play the plan that is currently in motion:

1. Get the [[Diadem of Dawn|Diadem]]
2. Use the girl to open a portal to the Fae
3. Merge the Fae and this plane, allowing [[Vecna|V-Mordred]] entrance to this realm and kicking off [[Gard'Daemon]]

**Name of the Arch-Fey: [[Reynardine]], the Madness Bringer**

> **CRITICAL NOTE**: [[Reynardine]] (Nyx's patron, the Madness Bringer) is a DISTINCT entity from [[Reynaldine]] (the Fall Feylord of the Autumn Court). These are separate characters despite the similar names.

## Key Characters

- [[Tygras]] — Ancient tiefling warlord, made the original Fey pact
- [[Ranar Surefoot]] — Stole the Diadem of Dawn
- [[Xyn]] — Nyx's twin sister, the true vessel
- [[Reynardine]] — Arch-Fey patron, the Madness Bringer
- [[Logath HellEye]] — Orc emperor, pawn of Reynardine
- [[Vecna]] — The ultimate beneficiary of Reynardine's plan
- [[Diadem of Dawn]] — The artifact at the center of the conspiracy
