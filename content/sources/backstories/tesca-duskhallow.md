# Tesca Duskhallow — Backstory

## Surface Story (Player Knowledge)

Tezka was born to tiefling parents in a slum in [[dawnpass]]. After crossing the underground crime syndicate, Tezka's parents were slaughtered — a fate Tezka herself narrowly escaped. For several years Tezka lived on the streets, thieving from merchants and travelers until a visiting monk spotted her antics. He offered her safety and stability at the nearby monastery and although wary, Tezka accepted.

At the monastery Tezka was teased and outcast from the other children. She grew to resent her tiefling heritage even more than she did as a street urchin. The elder monk who picked her off the streets encouraged Tezka to redirect her frustration and energy into training, and he personally took her under his wing to offer spiritual teachings and life lessons.

Tezka became a skilled fighter but still retained her propensity towards light crime, often using deceit and stealth to pull pranks on children who were cruel to her or to steal extra food at meal times.

Shortly before Tezka reached adulthood, her long-time mentor passed away of old age. Soon after, Tezka was wrongly accused of stealing offerings and vandalizing the temple. Although the other elder monks had tolerated her and sometimes showed kindness, they still did not completely trust her. They threw her out of the temple.

Tezka took this as an opportunity to start traveling, seeking to experience the same places her mentor had told her about in tales of his own travels.

## DM Notes (Hidden from Players)

The slums of [[dawnpass]] are particularly violent and tragic because it's a crossroads trading town. Born to [[Tenzin Duskhallow]] and [[Kiera Duskhallow]] (two tieflings), there was little way for the family to put food on the table. This led to desperation, which led to crime.

The crime syndicate is the [[Dusker's Initiative]] (founded because of the treatment of migrants in [[dawnpass]]). Her parents needed the money and took a job to try and steal the location of the [[Diadem of Dawn]] (a legendary magic item worth WAY more than a kingdom).

It was a simple job: break into the [[Monastery of the Dawn]], find the office of [[Elder Roku]] and case the joint for any clues as to the location of the [[Diadem of Dawn|Diadem]]. They were caught by [[Elder Roku]] and pleaded for their lives claiming the existence of a small tiefling daughter — Tesca. The Elder heard their desperate pleas and convinced the trio to go seek refuge in the monastery. The couple could seek asylum and their daughter would never go hungry again.

Overwhelmed with gratitude, [[Tenzin Duskhallow|Tenzin]] and [[Kiera Duskhallow|Kiera]] began to make plans with the old man about how to fool the syndicate and start over in the monastery, but they were sloppy...

The parents, upon leaving the monastery, gave the fake location over to the leaders of the syndicate. [[Griff Zealon]], the enforcer at the time, ordered the "loose ends be tied up" and the family's house was lit on fire with everyone inside. As the parents tried to flee with little Tesca, the [[Dusker's Initiative]] agents first killed [[Tenzin Duskhallow|Tenzin]], who told his wife to run with his dying breath. [[Kiera Duskhallow|Kiera]] doubled back towards the burning building, her daughter in tow, the syndicate in pursuit...
