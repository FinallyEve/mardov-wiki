# Session 11 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

SUMMARY CHAPTER 11 - ON THE SUBJECT OF BATMAN FOR MAYOR AND BURYING YOURSELVES ALIVE

Nocturnus Wayne met up with Cerci and Sally four hours before the others woke up in order to ask her to inspect Timmy, son of Mattrum. Cerci had a surprising amount of insight into the true nature of the spells used to \"prepare\" Timmy's soul to be consumed:

1\) Four year old's are just developing their souls (comes with first memories/personality)

2\) Timmy's soul was weakened, the equivalent of putting an iron ingot in the fire to forge

3\) Cerci gave Timmy a dreamcatcher with an \"eye\" to \"watch over him while he sleeps\" but then ordered Nocturnus to watch for the hags to return as he is \"almost ready\".

Later that day when Stalwart Little, Brigit, Tezka and Randall were waking up, they discovered that Nyx as well as the rest of their party had returned from their various other adventures! The reunited group then bumped into an argument between Finkle Hollingsworth and Don Mononuchi. While the news about Timmy seemed suspicious, that is NOTHING compared to the news the group heard next:

Mandu and a rival mercenary company named The Golden Reach returned early this evening with almost all of the children in single file. They claimed that they rescued them from a pack of vicious owlbears in a cave in the Old Forest, sadly they claimed that four souls had already been eaten\... However it turns out that today is election day in Argos, the last one occurred two years ago, and the last city mayor the town had was found dead a year ago. It was then that the party got the breakdown of who is running for which office position:

- Mayor: Mandu (un-opposed)

- Captain of the Guard: Danelle, Commander of The Golden Reach and Linda, the grieving mother and expert leatherworker.

- Master of Commerce: Cervantes, the current master of commerce, a man of numbers and details. and Markus, the quartermaster (and only other member) of The Golden Reach

- Minister of Justice: Vikrum, the sleazy current minister of justice, and Dolf the Blacksmith and owner of the Iron's Whyne.

The breakdown of each of their roles in the town is simple:

The MAYOR makes laws and governs. The CAPTAIN OF THE GUARD protects the citizens from threats to the public both inside and outside the walls. The MASTER OF COMMERCE sets the taxes and manages the port economy. The MINISTER OF JUSTICE is the will of law, with the ability to keep the others in check.

After their triumphant return, Mandu and his new friends from The Golden Reach all declared themselves at the gallows to be running for office. The party immediately set out to find some of the returned children as well as check out the gallows to declare themselves in the running for public office!

Once at the gallows, the town center of a small marketplace, which has literal gallows on behind the speakers on the stage. Nocturnus Wayne, Dallor Dallorbill and Daina all decide to declare themselves running for a particular position.

Nocturnus Wayne ran for Mayor, Dallor ran for Master of Commerce and Daina ran for Captain of the Guard. Even though the elections were closing polls and they announced to a group of people who may have already voted\... During the commotion caused by the charismatic speech by Dallor, Tezka was able to pickpocket a rather smug looking redheaded lord, stealing 100 gold pieces and a note from \...

MANDU!?! the note declared that the two had a good \"business partnership\" and that he would like to continue their \"arrangement\". It is here that the party understands that the last 10 years in Argos has seen a massive rise in the need to bribe for every little thing, the tipping point for a lot of people being bribing Mandu to hire more mercenaries to find their kids.

The party went on their way to the Lost Bolt, the tailor shop owned by Mattrum. Once inside the party found a scared Timmy crying about all the horrible things that he had to do in his dreams like killing puppies and his friends and his family and himself\... It was only in the presence of his heroes was poor Timmy able to finally sleep. However, after the fact the group realized that Nocturnus Wayne had given Mattrum the dreamcatcher and it wasn't currently in the room!

In a flash, Stalwart Little uses divine sense to locate the magic artifact downstairs, but it already too late!! Timmy is unable to be woken up as Tezka jumps to the stairs to catch the dreamcatcher and activate its power. Before the party materialized a wicked hag\'s twisted projection hovering over the captive child. However as soon as Tezka moved the dreamcatcher the hag would vanish from sight, and so the party discovered that the dreamcatcher worked like a flashlight to reveal the astral plane!

After a long battle of \"FIGHTING FIGHTING\", Daina managed to move Timmy's entire bed away from the hags. Meanwhile Bergs who specialized in fighting hags in Artificer's school, knew that this hag was actually a Night Hag and that she needed to bring her Heartstone and Soul bag to enter the astral realm and collect souls respectively.

Nyx, thinking fast noticed that one of the skulls around the night hag\'s waist was a glowing crystal and with an eldritch blast shattered the stone, causing the Night Hag's astral projection to fade!

After saving Timmy as second time, the party departed from the Lost Bolt having earned a number of sheets of paper with the name \"Nocturnus Wayne\" written on them\...

At the festival of light, the first thing the party notices is the eerie feeling of a festival with zero children in it.. absolutely NONE! No laughing kids squealing playing a game of tag, no little figures darting between legs in a crowd. none. Stalwart Little wastes no time and casts divine sense and locates two fiends in the middle of the large crowd of late voters.

In the middle of the crowd is three figures atop three out of four soap crates and four barrels with slits in the top, one marked with each position. The three figures turned out to be Mandu, and two fiends who turned out to be Markus and Danelle of The Golden Reach, who knew!?!

Turns out nobody knew as \"somebody\" lit the boxes Markus and Danelle were standing atop on fire, and SOMEBODY ELSE \"revealed\" their true nature with an illusion of a demon (not after a long drawn out attempt to trap them in a zone of truth). The incubus and succubus revealed for what they truly are, decided to skip town the cool way and flew off into the Old Forest. However during all this, Nocturnus Wayne stuffed the ballot box with the faked votes

The party then decides that the election was well thwarted and decided to search for the remaining four children (all belonging to the other people running for office)

At the fork in the road the party chose to try and re-enter the compound using the same entrance they just blew open yesterday. This proved to be a trap (the hint was the owlbear corpses that The Golden Douchebags brought back as their \"proof\"). The other side of the opening was loaded with an Indiana Jones type boulder trap and luckily everyone ducked into different cells to narrowly evade being crushed, little did they realize that each contained four fully rested wights!

Luckily the party was also rested as they proceeded to beat the wights back into the hallway,

which caused Tezka to set off the boulder trap a second time, revealing its hidden location and producing another deadly boulder, which while crushing Nocturnus Wayne also crushed a couple of wights!

After a long drawn out bloody battle the group found themselves in need of a long rest.

To secure a safe sleeping place, the party decided to purposefully set off the trap two more times to cause the stacked boulders to cover the entrance to one of the cells (and also the trigger).

The party all decided to make camp inside the now buried cell after the sound of crashing boulders stopped shaking the whole floor\...

It is here that we find our adventurers this week!
