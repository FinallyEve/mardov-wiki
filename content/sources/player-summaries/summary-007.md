# Session 7 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary - Chapter 7 Who\'s afraid of the Big Bad Moth aka The Night of the Weremoth!

The party began their treck down the river flowing through the Old Forest, with another set of disappearances and appearances, with a crack Corrumperre vanished from sight as Nocturnus Wayne slipped into existence.

Still missing this time however was Brotor Rootbeard. Nocturnus Wayne begins his first breath of fresh air in a silent scream. PTSD aside, it has become more and more clear that this is the \"easy street\" side of reality.

The whole group was reunited with Finkle Hollingsworth who explained his not so brave escape during the events following the Ritual of Life. He explained that the reason he first travelled the Forest\'s Zenith was to retrieve a gem of great value, this gem is called The Heart of the Wilde. Unfortunately, the last time he tried to travel through the blighted necropolis above Machu, the rest of his expedition force was murdered and he was possessed.

This time however he managed to pilfer the jewel, however it is impossible to teleport as is teleporting with more than one person. For that however, Finkle has the Thornberry Shade and its Captain Liza.

The young ship captain is the Scarlet Tunic Brigade\'s go-to gal for every mission of the treasure variety. Finkle promised his old recruits 3 things.

1\) Passage back to Argos

2\) The run of the Scarlet Keep in Argos, the same guild hall we began the journey in

3\) A promotion and 250 gold pieces if you help deliver the gem safely back

Once aboard the Thornberry Shade it became clear that there were several forces of evil afoot. The first of which was very familiar. The Revenant of Jorgen had a gnome to revenge-kill. The party attempted to make quick work of the former Scarlet Tunic Brigade member but hit after hit was recouped until Sprocket launched a massive fireball, inflicting a wound that would cauterize itself shut after the damage, leaving the undead with nothing to rejuvenate.

It was then that Nocturnus Wayne was able to knock some rigging aside and launch himself in front of the revenant! He delivered a blow that knocked him off the back of the boat, leaving the body burning atop the river.

Next the party was able to meet most of the other passengers and crew aboard the sloop. First, just prior to the Revenant fight the party met First Mate Gordon, who bid them all goodnight.

And goodnight Gordon had, that is until he was \... MURDERED! ! !

\* cue dramatic lighting effect and a cheesy bum - bum - bumMMMMMMMMmm \*

Turns out that during the night Gordon was supposed to be watching the Heart of the Wilde, but was instead paid off to keep lookout. Not a very good lookout as he didn't see the Weremoth coming! The Weremoth murdered the first mate, and cocooned his body in silk in the supposed to be guarded strong room.

However, after some detective work the party found a number of suspicious items in the strong room, none of which were the Heart of the Wilde.

1\) Large male muddy footprints leading in and out of the strong room

2\) A glass cover with a perfect circle cut out of it, with the circle on the floor with a suction cup handle attached

3\) The large silk cocoon with Gordon's body inside, along with a strange lighter, two sacks of 100 gold pieces, and a detailed description of a little elf girl with big eyes and brown hair, a description that exactly matched Sally, the little Machu girl traveling with her guardian, the witchdoctor Cerci.

The party first heard the testimony of Viktoria Brodgesio, the elephant tamer Vestanti traveling with her father. Viktoria mentioned that she saw a weremoth, which is a folk legend of the Machu people and pointed the party to Cerci\'s room.

In Cerci\'s room the party found Sally crying after having a nightmare, the room lined with shrunken heads, dreamcatchers, and other voodoo paraphernalia, like Cerci's spell book. Cerci tells the party the story of the Heart of the Wilde.

The gem began as a guardian of the royal family, an heirloom passed down from generation to generation. But it had a dark side and soon the Machu people were forced into conflict. Not long after the Artificer's Guild imprisoned Ramses, the gem was deemed too dangerous and placed at the center of the temple to keep its curse away from Machu. The gem attracts life, it has some sort of hold on the natural creatures of this world which Cerci could not explain.

Instead the party chose to ask about Sally\'s strange headband, learning that Sally is Machu royalty seeking safe refuge from her murderous uncle, Jafar. Jafar seized power, like so many of the Machu dynasty before him, calling for the eradication of his only true challenge for the throne. The party wished the pair farewell, and traveled down the hall to the Don\'s room.

Don Mananuchi and Don Damiachi are two kidnapping professionals. Wiseguys, the very best. They had business, the likes of which a mister Jafar happened to have slipped their way. Meanwhile these so-called bounty hunters were on the case for a Chuck Wheeler, a gambler with a high bounty back in Argos. So, two birds, two Don\'s\... one stones and all that jazz, badaboom, annnnnd Bob\'s your uncle, the party links these bumbling buffoons with the note and lighter in Gordon\'s pocket.

Turns out the Don\'s paid Gordon to try and kidnap Sally for them while they played cards upstairs to establish an alibi and make it look like the new guys did it. However, what really stuck out about the story was the fact that they only paid Gordon one sack of 100 gold pieces, but Gordon was found with two sacks (and muddy shoes).

And with this information the party returned to Cerci to warn her of the threat of the Don's, lock the Don\'s in the party\'s room, and fetch Captain Liza. The captain commended the group for their quick work in uncovering the Don\'s plot, however the party set out to figure out the two other mysteries

1\) Who gave Gordon the other sack of gold?

2\) Who is, or What is, the weremoth?

To answer the first question, the party gave Ace the classic bad-cop, worse-cop routine. Between Daina and Stalwart Little, poor Ace never stood a chance. Under duress, he admitted that he paid Gordonr to establish both an alibi (watching the crow's nest) and to make sure the gem isn't guarded. In the cover of night, Ace snuck into the strong room and using his glass cutting kit, stole the gem and hid it \.... in \... his\... HAT!!!

Captain Liza didn't take the news of her second crewmate\'s betrayal in one day, she took this as an affront to her reputation and threw Ace off the boat! WHERE HE WAS SAVED by Stalwart Litlle. The party then decided to tie him up and stash him in the crow's nest along with the cocooned body of Gordon! The only thing left of Ace was his wallet revealing himself to really be Chuck Wheeler!

The second question was answered in the archeologist, M. B. WELLS as he interpreted an ancient stone tablet depicting the royal seal of Machu and its shadowy protector! The party then learned of two methods of fighting off the weremoth; either the victim can use silver to defend themselves, or the attacked can toss a light in one direction and run as fast as they can in the opposite direction.

Lastly the party discovered that Viktoria saw the exchange between Ace and Gordon as well and found out that she was supposed to meet her boyfriend Dudley, the son of the ship\'s chef, in the kitchen at midnight.

During the second night, the party decided to watch three rooms, Bergs posted up outside Cerci's room, Nocturnus Wayne hids in the pantry, and Brock Hammer decided to wait in front of Captain Liza\'s quarters, where the captain, Sally and the Heart of the Wilde all rest.

First, Bergs saw Cerci leave her room carrying a dreamcatcher, however Cerci also saw Bergs. And she decided to leave the dreamcatcher outside the Captain\'s quarters and return to her room, with a slam. During this time, Viktoria leaves her room to visit Dudley in the kitchen. As things transition to the only empty room\... the freezer, the weremoth attacks.. from INSIDE the freezer!!!

The weremoth murders the son a chef, and carries the body off into the night, but not before Nocturnus Wayne emerges from the night in time to start the chase\... which Brock Hammer finished with a well-placed arrow, causing both the weremoth and Dudley's body to land amongst the Old Forest.

In the wake of this tragedy, a Giant Octopus attacks the ship, its tentacles clawing at the deck towards the gem! The tentacles follow Captain Liza down below deck, as this isn't the first creature to attempt to nab the gem. The octopus was repelled as Dallor Dallorbill causes it to flee in a rare moment of heroism!

The party then interviewed Cerci about both the dreamcatcher and the gem once again. Revealing that the dreamcatcher is actually a Machu royal warding spell against harm. As it happens, the first weremoth was the ward activating as Gordon attempted to kidnap Sally, the second being Gordon's body reacting to Seamus' murder! (a plot thread that never fully unraveled)

Cerci also revealed that only the removal of the impending threat of the Don's would cause the next weremoth's magic to deactivate (because they still harbored malicious intent to kidnap the elf girl) As the party opened up their old room holding the captured Don's, they discovered a massive hole in the boat from the Giant Octopus' tentacle, the Don's nowhere to be found.

Using the broken strong room door, the party patched the hole and arrived in Argos to a gnome waving welcome!
