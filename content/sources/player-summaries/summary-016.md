# Session 16 Summary

*Written by Sprocket*

Sprocket's Mis-Adventures, Episode 16:

Last week began like most others. With a bar fight.

Me and the group of boneheads known as my "party" were just enjoying some beers when "Guards" from the Scarlet Tunic Brigade were summoned to the Galloping Galoshes to "apprehend" the baddies we beat in the previous bar fight. These guards were immediately suspicious and one even tried to stab a victim of the previous fight.

The loud one in my party was quick to catch on and immediately took up residence in the doorway. He was obstinate as usual and eventually tempers broke and the fight began!

The #FakeNews guards then proceeded to play my favorite game of Ignore-the-Wizard-in-the-back while I summarily roasted them. The rest of the party did, uh, a great job as well I guess.

The Loud One sent a medieval tweet to Dolf summoning him to the bar. Dolf surveyed the situation and it came to our attention that a "Lord Jerrefor" sent these guards. Dolf then graciously invited us back to his place for a night cap. Once at his house he told us that he was disappointed with how poorly the situation was handled. I cannot help but agree. The suspicious dragonborn and the fake guards are still alive, after all. What a mess. Despite the commotion we made, Dolf wrote up a search warrant and gave it to me for safekeeping. He suggested we try to learn all we can about this Lord Jerrefor.

One-by-one, and in their own unique manner, everyone in the party headed back to the Scarlet Keep to go to bed. Some feeling inside me seemed to suggest that I would only be able to cast 2 more spells so I consigned myself to sleep as well.

The following morning at the Scarlet Keep the party once again shook down the only other gnome I've seen in these parts; Finkle. I don't quite remember who said what but there was much ado about owed Gold and some shouting. Before I knew it, we were on our merry way to Lord Jerrefor's manor!

But alas! On the way everyone embraced their own free will and suddenly wanted to do something utterly unrelated! Bergs ran off to the quest board. Daina and friends walked over to the curio shop. Nyx and Professor Lupin beelined it to Jerrefor's house, without the warrant, on some hare-brained quest that definitely was worthwhile.

Time passes strangely at this point. I seem to remember moments from the curio shop, but every once in a while everything would freeze completely still. As if a greater all knowing being had other things to attend to and metaphorically put time and space on hold for 20 minutes.

All I seem to remember from this bizarre time was that we saved a soulless child at the shop and I heard that Jacob Black got squeezed by a mysterious ghost.

The time anomaly seemed to right itself after the curio party rounded up a still inebriated Bergs and finally made it to Jerrefor's manor. Daina, bold as ever, knocked on his door and demanded we search the premises. Lord Jerrefor suspiciously agreed without putting up a fight.

While Nyx and the Loud One searched through all Lord Jerrefor's belongings, I activated my magical radar and sensed that the cellar had much more to hide. Jerrefor, who had remained cooperative up until this point, suddenly wanted to see the warrant as soon as I requested cellar access. That was the point I knew I was one the right track.

Jerrefor showed Daina and I where the cellar was in the backyard but refused to grant us access. He put on his lawyer underpants and claimed the warrant only allowed us indoor access and the cellar is technically outdoors. I don't have much patience for technicalities, so I melted out of existence while Daina led Jerrefor back to the house to investigate a commotion coming from inside.

Back inside the manor, a fight had broken out! Nyx, Bergs, and the Loud One were caught up in a tussle with some form of shadow demon. At first, Jerrefor claimed no knowledge of such foul creatures. But after much yelling and a couple forceful pushes he finally acknowledged their existence but continued to act like he'd been framed. Nyx then laid down the truth for everyone pointing out that Jerrefor tried to kill her and Stuart Little on while their brief side quest. Evidently, not having foreseen this, Jerrefor dropped his old man cane and tried to gag Nyx. This did not go over well with the rest of the party as they easily defeated the shadow demon and began to interrogate Lord Jerrefor.

MEANWHILE! I had blown off the cellar door and was snooping around for the source of magic pinging on my radar. My initial investigation of the room was so successful it was as though I had lived an entire lifetime in each square inch of that cellar. Among the things I instantly knew, I could tell that one of the walls was just an illusion and was protected by a magical alarm spell. After grabbing a few juicy party favors, I approached the pitiful excuse for a magical trap and walked right through. The wall dissolved around me and at the first squeak of the alarm spell I quenched its magic with a well-timed counter spell. Ahead of me opened up a tunnel as long as the eye could see. Walking this tunnel would have taken someone of my considerable size at least 47 years so I abandoned that idea and cast one more glance around the room. I saw that I had forgotten the most important part of this cellar. The plans to the Death Star. I mean the creepy network of tunnels branching out through the city. Once satisfied with my haul, I climbed back up the ladder and rejoined the rest of the party.

Upon re-entering the manor, I learned that the Loud One's zone of truth trick had yielded very little evidence and that Bergs suspected that this Jerrefor character was more than he let on. Curtains close as we order an Uber XL and roll the fuck out.
