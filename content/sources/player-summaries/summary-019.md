# Session 19 Summary

*Written by Bergz*

> *A bottle washes up on shore and two tortle fishermen find it.*
>
> *"Ay, wut dis. All sort o\' scribblin\'. Can you read?"*
>
> *"Yeh! Message from da gods, it is. Tellin\' us the future. Look \'ere"*

I\'m not much of a writer. In fact, my pack never got around to teaching me wolf scratches, let alone book authoring. But if I am ever going to fit in with this new pack, I need to practice my Common. So here goes nothing. I am going to start just by writing down the events that I\'ve witnessed lately.

After some crazy events in town, we headed towards the docks. Lo and behold, our good friend Captain Liza was there. She talks funny, but no funnier than some of the other low-landers I\'ve met recently. She was standing next to the Thornberry Shade and I could feel the universe beckoning us to the get onto the ship. Liza told us to, as well. So, back onto the boat we went. It looks no worse for wear after the last giant squid attack. I\'m guessing they patched it up via magics. There is so much magic around here, it\'s strange. Ever since I acquired my Eldritch powers, I can sense it everywhere. The world is a strange place, no doubt. Magic on a boat, whoulda thunk! I wonder if the boat even touches the water or if it hovers over it.

My suspicions about magic being near were confirmed when we learned that the boat was getting a teleportation circle. This boat is magic, pure and simple. Maybe I can pick up a few spells if I wander through that circle when no one\'s looking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

> "*Magick. Wassat?"*
>
> *"When the Great Tortle makes fish appear in my net, dat\'s magick. Or when the Great Tortle throws the sun into the sky each morning, dat\'s magick"*
>
> *"Oh, ok. Keep reading da note"*

Stalwart is back with someone named Linda. Linda is a chicken. Chicken is food. I\'m going to eat Linda. Flying down from the mast, Stalwart trips and knocks himself on the head. Everyone laughs. Everyone except for the Tieflings, that is. Those people have no sense of humor. Instead, they all seem to be worried about what they\'re going to do when we get to where we\'re going. Which is weird\... why\'d they get on the boat?! We all knew we were going to the Magister\'s Circle and everyone knows mages hate Tieflings. I, for one, am looking forward to finally learning Eldritch Blast. I\'ve heard it can kill anyone with one wave of the hand. That will be useful for later.

The Tieflings in our group blabber on about the Fey and I try to follow along. Apparently, the Fey is like some alternate universe where things don\'t quite work right. Liza is explaining the whole thing to us. There is no sun or wind there. Glowing plants in the sky, floating lights. I think they\'re talking about lightning bugs, but what do I know? I\'m just a dorf. There is something tricky about this Liza character though -- she doesn\'t remember how she got to the Fey and doesn\'t seem too keen about telling us how she got back. We need to keep an eye on her.

We\'ve been away from this ship for so long that they\'ve upgraded the sigil on the front of the boat. The squid attack has inspired a logo of a thornberry being attacked by tentacles. I like it; it depicts the right amount of horror associated with the encounter. Also new to the boat is a new deckhand, but they\'re a dime a dozen, so who cares? More importantly, a captivating young woman has ferried passage\... I\'ve got the feeling she might be a noble, much like everyone else in our party but me. She introduces herself as Irethia, but other than that, isn\'t terribly talkative. I suppose we\'ll get to know her better as this slow boat ride heads towards the Magister\'s Circle.

> *"Da magisser\'s wut?"*
>
> *"Circle"*
>
> *"Wassa circle?"*
>
> *"Shape of our shell is a circle"*
>
> *"And wats a magisser?"*
>
> *"Magick person! I told you dis note was magick."*

The boat is very busy. Much busier than the last time. So many more people than I remember. We decide to go meet them all. There is Vera. She looks elvish, but people in our group mutter that there is something off about her. We\'ll get back to that later. There\'s a lot of hustle and bustle, but one gnome in particular catches our eye going in and out of that room where the elephant used to be. We debate going to see what\'s in that room now, but part of the group gets bored with all the talking and takes off downstairs.

Nate, the first mate, is in the stairwell. Very inconvenient fellow, that guy. He\'s carrying a barrel which makes it tough to move past him as we ignore his pleas not to go downstairs. What a whiny fellow, this guy. He keeps warning us not to go where we don\'t belong and be respectful of the ship. After all, we are but grateful passengers. We show our gratitude by: 1) Tezka getting caught trying to pickpocket him. 2) Stalwart patching his scrapes up and then immediately headbutting him, and 3) Stalwart shoving him down the stairs. I was raised to believe that this type of behavior does not pay off. Strangely though, it does pay off as I notice Irethia manages to obtain a note off of Nate. Magic!

Nate, in his everlasting wisdom, decides to ignore the whole encounter and pretend it never happened. He calls downstairs to guy named Keelick inquiring about something I couldn\'t quite hear. Keelick responds by laughing about something. Whenever I hear laughing, I assume alcohol is involved. My fellow dwarf, Daina, assumes the same thing and heads downstairs trying to find said alcohol. I wish her luck and tell her to bring me some back. She never does.

The lowest deck looks boring, so we stay upstairs. Stalwart tells us he\'s got a Sense of the Divine and that we should stay calm while he investigates why this ship is so strange. I notice him and Irethia peering intently at each other. It looks like they\'re communicating telepathically, but it must be on some wavelength I can\'t comprehend. I hope it works out for them. Not romantically though\... can you imagine a Dragonkin doing anything romantically?

*"Dragonkin?"*

*"Magick! Stop talking"*

The group heads downstairs. I was still thinking about how Dragonkin reproduce, when thankfully some shouting from one of the rooms broke that train of thought. The shouting is high pitched, so it has to be gnomes. Evidently, Dallor knows Gnomish and says something high-pitched to them. I hate that squeaking noise\... how can a race stand to hear each other talk like that all day long?

There\'s a girl gnome and a guy gnome. I\'m thinking we just interrupted their private time; but no matter, the group barges in. From what I gather, and it was tough to gather this considering more than half of the conversation was in pips and squeaks, is that the guy is named Maurice and the girl is named Trixie. The room they are in has been chosen to be the site of the teleportation circle and Trixie is trying to add some magic protection around it to make sure it doesn\'t get co-opted for some nefarious purpose. I\'m sure that won\'t ever be necessary. Sprocket winks at her and makes some other flirty comments. I don\'t understand Gnomish courting rituals, but I think she expresses interest back.

Some of our gang, Tezka and Randall, get bored and decide to go find Daina and see what she got herself into. I assume they are off having fun getting drunk without me. Instead of drinking, I find myself part of the group in the study. It looks crazy! I\'ve never seen so many books in my life. I can feel the magic emanating from them. The others seem equally impressed. It looks like there is a stew going in a cauldron and since we haven\'t eaten in at least an hour, I\'m hungry. I also tend to investigate things by tasting them, since I was raised by wolves and such. The combination of those two factors saw my finger dipping into the stew and trying a bit. What do ya know? It wasn\'t stew! It tasted like stew, a little tangy and a little meaty, but it was really pestilence. Good thing I have a stomach like steel. No clue why\'d you keep pestilence just sitting around like that, but the gnome in the room tells us it\'s an experiment. Probably an experiment to see who\'s stupid enough to eat it.

*"Pessilens? Dat magick, right?"*

*"Nope. It\'s food. Can\'t you read?? You dum dum."*

*"Can we get some? I\'m hungry"*

*"You always hungry. Just wait. Note almost over"*

The door to the study is open, so I can still see into the other room. Out of the corner of my eye, I see a lot of commotion. The teleportation circle is making all sorts of strange sounds and I can feel it pulsing even from all the way over here. I hear someone mark their arrival with a deep gravelly voice, who I can only assume is Nocturnus Wayne. It\'s good to have our mayor back! #NotOurMayor

So I make my way back into that room to see what\'s going on. Gnomish stuff\... magic missiles and counterspells and high-pitched squeeks. Sprocket translates some of what Trixie is saying, but not all of it. From what I could gather, the Gnomes were not expecting this teleportation circle to be active yet. They got freaked out and ran to Stalwart and Brigit for help. I chose a good group of adventuring pals because they came to the rescue. Back to the drawing boards for the Gnomes\... Trixie scratches out one of the runes and that\'s the end of the teleportation circle for now, or at least we hope.

*"Teleportation circle is magick?"*

*"Yes, just like Magisser\'s circle. All circles are magick."*

*"So\... can we make magick by drawing circles?"*

*"I tink so. Let\'s make a circle"*

We get greeted by the Vera lady who is curious about the magic she sensed in the room. Trixie explains what happened, even though we just witnessed her having no idea how it happened. Vera appears to be satisfied with this answer and goes back downstairs. What a strange lady. She is definitely not what she appears to be.

*"Ugh, our magick circle no magick."*

*"I dun know why not. Not big enough, maybe"*

*"Bigger circle!"*

*"Impossible. Get other tortles. We go to magissers circle and find this group. We take their circle."*

Other things are not what they appear to be on this boat, either. For instance, what is that smell? Why does it smell like horse *everywhere*? Why is there is a halfling using an entire room to hang up maps? Why does the glue she uses taste so good? Why is Daina not back yet? Either she found a barrel of rum or she passed out.

*"Rum is magick?"\
"Rum is magick."*

Nyx then scurried off to go tail Vera. I can\'t tell if Nyx trusts Vera or not, but I hope she finds out what she needs to find out. I\'ll follow up with her later to see what she learned.

Irethia talked into my brain! She told me that everyone ought to finish up their explorations and meet back up to discuss what we found. Since I wasn\'t actually doing much at this point, I just waited for everyone to show up. I can\'t say that the following accounts are accurate, but it\'s what they told me.

Nyx learned that Vera is also a Tiefling and that there are a group of Tieflings in the Magister\'s Circle. I didn\'t know this but mages hate Tieflings due to their connection to the Fey. Makes complete sense, we all nod in unison. But the Tieflings still feel the need to learn magic, so they\'ve learned to disguise themselves to sneak in and stay undetected. Of course, magic users can detect magic pretty easily, so this magic the Tieflings use to disguise themselves is really some sort of hybrid between physical disguise and human soul-based magic. Good thing I\'m not a human -- I\'m not gonna give up my soul for Nyx.

During that same time, Daina and Tesca perused the kitchen for some food. They found rancid horse meat boiling in a stew. That explains the smell. Randall and Brigit went to the workshop. They met a ~~blacksmith~~\... ~~metallurgist~~\... scalesmith. I didn\'t catch his name, but he was working with some sort of scales from a sea serpent. It doesn\'t dent -- it just bounces back forth for a bit before settling into its original shape. We had never heard of such a thing, but I imagine it will come in handy for future battles.

Meanwhile, Sprocket was exploring boat jail. They made quite a few enhancements to the brig and it looks much tougher to escape from this time around. That\'s good because there are lots of shady characters on this boat and I\'m guessing we will have to put some people in boat jail. He\'s acting cagey about it, but I think he found something down there that he isn\'t telling anyone about. If I had to guess, it\'s magic.

Stalwart decides he needs the soul bag -- I\'m guessing because of the soul magic that Nyx is interested in. He ran off looking for Daina and I assume they had a cordial discussion regarding the bag.

*"Booooooorrrrinnnng. Get to the good stuff!"*

*"I read ahead. Big fight coming!"*

Now that we were all caught up on each others\' escapades, Irethia explains that the note she found was from Jafar and hints that the first mate might know more about future plans to nab Sally. So, we get the band back together and go to the first mate\'s quarters and since he\'s such a private person, his door is locked. I get irritated by all of the people debating what course of action to take, and I do the first thing that pops into my head. I smash the door with axe and the door explodes open in the most beautiful display of hitting things I have ever seen.

Much to our surprise, we find Nate in his room. Like I said, he\'s a private person, but he\'s acting even more aloof than normal. He\'s swaying around and just staring at the wall. That\'s when we notice that his eyes and mouth are darting all over the place. What a strange man. I don\'t like strange men so I run towards him and tackle him. Up close and personal, I can tell that he is in some sort of trance and he is whispering things only the gods can understand. In his infinite wisdom, or perhaps inspiration from the gods, Stalwart casts a spell on Nate and he wakes up. Finally! Now we can get to the bottom of this whole thing. Or so we thought\.....

Explosions! Lots of noise! Yelling! It feels like the boat got smashed by something quite large. Should we check it out or should we stay down here in the safety of Nate the First Mate\'s quarters? Nate helps make our decision for us by telling us that he\'s been working with some people who have put out a large bounty on a noble woman. Nate thought this noble woman was Irethia and so has called in the pursuers to collect on the reward. We decide that Nate\'s Folly might very well cost us our life and we express as much. Nyx decides to punish him by taking his money. Irethia throws some water in his face. If we were nicer to him before, maybe he wouldn\'t have called the bounty people on us. Either way, that\'s a boat crime, so into boat jail he goes. We can\'t hide down here with him though, so we make our way to where the sound is coming from. In other words, our party opts to run towards the danger.

*"See? About to get good."*

*"Story can\'t be real\...."*

*"Who cares? Makes for good excitement though"*

*"If was real, they\'d make more magic circles for protection"*

Above deck, we find some baddies waiting for us. Orc pirates! "We are going to die" was what I was thinking below my steely exterior. Nate is an idiot. Why would he lead orcs straight to us? They\'ve already got the ship\'s crew tied up to the mast in unbreakable steel chains. Oh, and the mast is on fire. Oh, and there is a demon on top of the mast. Or is it a devil? I can never tell the difference. Either way, the thing demands that we give him the girl. "What girl?", you might ask. And you\'d be right to ask, since we don\'t who she is either. Irethia, the one who Nate confused with the girl, takes it upon herself to clear this little misunderstanding up. At her suggestion, and my desire to extricate myself from this situation, I agree to go with her to get Nate to fess up to his mistake.

Irethia and I are interrogating Nate while we leave the rest of the group to fend for themselves. I\'m sure they\'ll be fine. We manage to get him to agree to come upstairs and admit to his mistake. As we come up the stairs, we can\'t help but notice that a fight has broken out. The demon guy looks angry. So angry that he was misled, in fact, that he decides to turn Nate into ash. RIP Nate.

There are fireballs flying all over the place. Orcs grunting and screaming. People turning into bears. Bears being held down by orcs. Up is down. Down is up. We don\'t know whether the prisoners are on our side or not, since they look possessed. In short, the deck is awash with chaos as Irethia, Nate and I return. RIP Nate.

Irethia decides it\'s a good idea to try to lie to the devil. That goes over poorly. The devil throws fire at us instead. Oh, and then there is an ice storm. Things are not looking good for us. Nocturnus starts punching people. They get hurt. Sprocket is smart and gets the bad guys to waste their counter spells on his cantrips. Stalwart plays whack-a-mole with an orc and wins. Eventually, we get things in control by taking out the mages. No more fireballs flying around; at least not from those two. Oh did I not mention there were mages? There were mages! Magic!

Nyx takes off and does some sneaky stuff, I assume. She\'s invisible so we can\'t really see her. But something happens to the demon where he can\'t move very well and I\'m guessing that was due to her. I\'ll have to thank her for that when I get the chance. Brigit changes into a wolf and bites the wolf captain. As a wolf-spawn myself, I notice the good form by going for the hamstrings. Nocturnus breaks the unbreakable steel chains and lets the prisoners free.

We all get scared as Stalwart decides to climb the mast in order to attack the demon. He weighs a solid two tons, so there is no way that thing is going to hold him. In the mean time, the prisoners break out of their trance and help us kill the orc captain. The rest of the orcs don\'t look like they are in great shape either. I can\'t tell how many are alive or dead at any given time since they\'re so damn ugly. I assume they\'re dead at this point though since they\'ve stopped attacking us.

In order to test the handiwork of the ship\'s carpenters, Nocturnus goes up the mast as well. We\'re thinking this won\'t end pretty as the combined weight of him and Stalwart is surely too much for this conflagrated pine tree to withstand. Yet it manages to hold their impressive weight. Irethia casts something or other on the demon and the demon responds by throwing fire at us. What a jerk; we were only here to collect the bounty. Some eagles fly in out of nowhere and use their powerful talons to rake the demon across the face. He looks worse for wear, but he\'s not dead yet. Sprocket knows just the trick though and shoots some magic missiles at him. Now he\'s dead. RIP demon.

Wow, what a crazy night. So much intrigue, so much action, so many dead orcs. I\'m glad I got to experience that but I am quite thankful for a quiet night\'s rest to reflect on a day gone by. I\'m *really* to get to the Magister\'s Circle and learn Eldritch Blast. I will be unstoppable then! No one is ever going to read this note since I was really just trying to practice my Common. I think I\'ll just throw it overboard.

*"You ready to go to magissers circle with tortle brothers and become wizards and steal circles?"*

*"I am."*
