# Session 20 Summary

*Written by Irethia*

Summary Chapter 20 -- Irethia's Dream

I awoke covered in sweat, my chest heaving rapidly with heavy deep panting... The memories of what had happened the previous evening flooding back into my consciousness. A battle. A devil. Fire. Screams. Calm. Dread. Beware the forest. Laughter. Sleep...

Everything seemed to swirl together, none of it seemed... important. It's almost as if it was trivial. Did I reveal myself? No. They haven't yet seen the true me. Beware the Forest. Not yet... But I must... I must continue with these strange people. Even if it puts us all at risk. What are we doing here? The Magisters Circle. Beware the forest. I sense a hint of evil as I open the door to find the others. The strangers.

They're discussing souls. It's sickening. Why must the gods shove me upon this journey, with such compromised strangers? The moment passes, the sickening feeling moves from conscious to unconscious once they've made up their minds on disguises. Beware the Forest. I really doubt they'll work, can't the mages sense the evil magic being used to support the disguise they so recklessly were sucked into? Eventually they read me in, something about a magic coin, a living mast for the ship, magical shields, Tigris Logath, maps on fire, it all sort of further jumbles together. I'm not sure what we're in for, but we turn in for the night.

Beware the Forest, Stay with the Strangers.

Beware the Forest, Stay with the Strangers.

Seagulls. Land. The Forest.

The Forest. Beware the Forest. I don't tell them. Why didn't I tell them\... I need to earn their trust if I am to trust them.

A sharp stabbing in the back. Assassin. Beware the Forest. Trolls. Rage. Fireballs. Souls. Torture. Fog. Dread. Death.

Fire. Water. Blood. Pain. Rain out of nowhere, perfect for the fire. Silence.

Instructions to kill us. All of us. But a woman especially, a Bounty. Beware the Forest. She looks uncomfortably like me. Someone gave me a sideways look... Will they betray me? What would I do if they did...

We move away from the scene of the battle. We establish a camp to rest, seeing utter destruction find its way to our last location. Beware the Forest. Wounded and exhausted, I must trust them for now...
