# Session 46 Summary

*Written by Nyx*

Summary 46 - Nyx's Version

Once upon a time, there were 4 special people:

1)  **Gabriel** - the kind and powerful ruler of a Celestial Plane,

2)  **Graz'zt** - the Demon Prince of the Abyss / the Lord of Pleasure,

3)  **Juliannia** - the Princess of the Summer Solstice, and

4)  **Reynardine** - the Archfey we are all too familiar with these days.

Long ago, **Juliannia** and **Reynardine** were betrothed to each other, in what I assume might have been an arranged marriage. Because while **Juliannia** was enamored with **Reynardine**, **Reynardine** and **Gabriel** were deeply in love with each other.

However, their love was not meant to be as it turns out the 9 Hells and 7 Heavens get a bit peeved when someone below their station gets involved with a Celestial ruler. While **Reynardine** and **Gabriel** were busy being star-crossed lovers, **Graz'zt** took this as an opportunity to exploit **Juliannia's** growing doubt.

**Graz'zt** bewitched **Juliannia** to fall in love with him, and she grew grim and bitter under the devil's influence. When the time was finally right, **Graz'zt** transformed her into his Demon Amulet in order to grow in power and elevate his status from a mere devil to the Demon Prince.

When **Gabriel** learned of this tragedy, he blamed himself for causing **Juliannia** such great pain. Using the majority of his power, **Gabriel** succeeded in trapping **Graz'zt** within the Demon Amulet. However, this action ended up condensing **Gabriel** himself within the Amulet as well.

**Reynardine** became a broken man after learning of **Gabriel's** sacrifice, and in his grief and guilt and sense of duty, **Reynardine** decided to "take into himself" the Amulet. After a dark and excruciating ritual, ***Reynaldine*** was reborn as a walking graveyard of all 4 entities known as the 'Lord of the Autumn Court' as he became the twisted individual he is today.

\~\~\~

For a long time, I thought Reynaldine was my patron. Then, I thought Reynardine was my patron. But from pretty reliable sources (one of which being Leonidas), I now am fairly certain that Juliannia is actually my patron, which means that the 4 beings within that amulet are still alive, at least enough to grant me my powers. Sidenote: I also think Gabriel has made a pact with Xyn, my sister, to grant her powers and become her patron.

Yesterday, it was **Reynardine** who told me that giving him the amulet would "fix everything." And after everything that's been done, after hearing all the stories\...I decided to believe him. I think somewhere deep inside there's a good person in there that was clearly good enough for an angel to fall in love with, so I'm banking on the good judgement of Gabriel and Juliannia that Reynardine is a trustworthy man, even if *Reynaldine* is a cocky piece of shit.

I think Kelick might still be in his Air Amulet, just like how all these other beings manage to exist within the Demon Amulet. And I also don't think Reynardine would hurt the Genasi's since I'm hoping he'd want to avoid recreating his tragedy with 4 new beings.

Something is going to happen during the Harvest Festival, something that involves Reynardine/Reynaldine, something that I bet requires the 4 amulets. I hope what I've done truly "fixes everything," but if not, I hope you can forgive me.

Nyx out.
