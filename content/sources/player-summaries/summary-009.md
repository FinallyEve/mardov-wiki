# Session 9 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary - Chapter 9 Never Trust a Hat

Some of the party awoke to find that the rest of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade was missing. Were they spirited away to the other reality, did they wander off into some trouble in Argos, or did they just decide to walk off and leave the party due to some perceived slight.

If your answer was D, all of the above, then you would be correct.

In the middle of the barracks of the Scarlet Keep was a pack with a note from Tiberius D. Rex stating that he was sorry for pocketing a secret 100 gold pieces last adventure and to make amends he left each member of the party one healing potion and two lesser healing potions.

From there we explored the Scarlet Keep now that Finkle and the gang has had a chance to settle in. In the tallest tower surrounded by tapestries of Finkle doing impossibly heroic deeds, the Heart of the Wilde is displayed in a solid brick of glass. Instead of putting it in a museum, the administrative powers that be decided to keep it close given its magic effect on giant woodland beasts.

Descending from the tower, the party bumped into Douglas one of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade knights tasked with protecting Argos from its nightly attacks on the North and East gates. Argos had come to expect these nightly raids at intervals of four hours, each attack alternating which gate was under siege. That is until about three days ago, when the attacks stopped all together, only to hit the Brigade where it hurts, taking their number from 100 strong to a mere 40.

With this disheartening news, the party got their next assignment from the gnome with the plan, Finkle Hollingsworth. Inside his study, the party discovered that they were to report to the East Gate to help Rodrick, Scarlet Tunic Brigade\'s Captain of the Watch and provide some much needed backup.

However instead of head right over the party decided to make a stop by Cerci and Sally's quarters. The refugees decided to take up residence in the Scarlet Keep as it is not only the only place/people they know in Argos but in order to keep an eye on the Heart of the Wilde/weremoth threat. On top of these reasons to stick around, Sally took it upon herself to go missing two days ago at the North Gate, which had Cerci in an even worse mood then her usual snarky self.

On the way to the East Gate, the party bumped into a frantic Lord Fartheshire. Turns out that his family was targeted for more than just their position as Argosian Aristocracy. Martha Fartheshire is actually Underdark royalty, or at least she would have been if she hadn\'t been forced to flee to the surface when her Brother was overtaken by a mysterious madness.

That means her children Hansel and Grettle are the 2nd and 3rd children of royal blood to go missing by the North Gate, but Hansel and Grettle have a little something special in their royal blood, the ability to sense bores into the underdark itself (where the arena was). The party decides to swing by the North Wall and get some clues before catching up with the fight at the East Gate.

While at the North Gate, Stalwart Little orders the ONLY guard to not let another child play near the gate, and also to make sure nobody messes with the trail of silver jacks (from Hansel's favorite game).

When they finally arrive at the East Gate the party stumbles on the end of a meeting between Rodrick and Mandu, the Argosian fat-cat who was \"elected\" to be the captain of Argos' non-existent guard. Instead Mandu is more of a glorified bribe middle man between people with real safety concerns and the Scarlet Tunic. But there are dead things to kill so \... BATTLE AT THE EAST GATE \# 2740!

This is a battle the size of which our adventurers haven\'t experienced yet. Wraiths commanding Wights raising fallen brigadiers, each rising the next in the chain. Physical attack after physical attack whirled through the corporeal wraiths, as the wraith sucks the life out of Nocturnus Wayne all but killing him in the process.

Luckily, a shadowy figure on a horse enters the fray and with a single potion, creates a burst of radiant light driving the undead away, killing most in the process. This savior's name? Sheila the Witch Hunter, formerly Sheila the Diviners Apprentice.

The party then explains why they need to get to the North Gate, a direction that Sheila is already headed, in order to confront the witch that forced Sheila to abandon her dream of being a great Diviner instead taking up the Witch-hunting profession. Sheila\'s Teacher was the famous Ruth The Diviner, an Argosian household name, Ruth had many customers and was well respected by her peers in the Magister's Circle, but she had a fatal weak spot for her pupil and this cost her everything.

Five years ago, just before the first of the attacks, Sheila attempted to boost her own intelligence using a bewitched hat as a shortcut through her work. Unluckily for Sheila, shortcuts come at a great cost, this particular hat is one of madness, an all too familiar deal in Mardov. However as soon as the madness overtook Sheila, her selfless teacher offered herself up willingly to remove Sheila from her doomed fate, instead taking on both Sheila's residual madness AND her own deeper madness (the cost of great intelligence).

From that day onward, Sheila vowed to give up her pursuit of Divination, deciding that freeing Ruth was by far the biggest contribution she could ever give to the art.

As this story comes to an end, Sheila, who was too carried away in her past to notice, began to transform into a werewolf! Thanks to some quick moves from the party they managed to subdue her and feed her the wolfsbane she was reaching for right before she fully turned.

But the turning werewolf had an effect on the fog surrounding the party, over a dozen sets of silver eyes, appeared in the darkness to witness the transformation both to and from werewolf.

As mysteriously as they appeared, the eyes (who's color and shape matched that of were-Sheila) vanished\...

STORY TIME with Sheila pt 2.0:

Sheila had traveled far and wide in her five-year training arc, but the first witch she ever hunted had a group of werewolves that turned her in the process of being slayed. This actually worked in Sheila's favor as apart from the occasional mishap, witches never suspect a witch-hunter to be a werewolf! This naturally led to the reason for Sheila's return, the last witch she killed had a hat of madness too! The hat, which typically must be cut off the dead fell off in a stream after Sheila so graciously separated the witches\' head from her body.

This lead to the hypothesis, a large enough force supplied by a large enough quantity of water SHOULD remove the hat without harming the captive below. The second realization to arise out of this conversation is WHY a wild wood witch would want children of royal blood, to raise another three death knights! Death Knights (like the one clearly creating the army of the dead every 4hrs) are extremely powerful undead creatures that can only be commanded by a Lich!

Clearly Mordred, master of madness, is pulling the strings of this elaborate fairy tale.

Soon the party approaches an outcropping marked by two pairs of large tusks, the likes of which are covered in a thin layer of chalk. Getting flashbacks of their fight with the last Gorgon, the party takes great pains to avoid following the markings once inside, choosing instead to use Nyx's owl familiar (who should probably be named at some point) to scout ahead.

Quickly finding the hidden hut inside the cave, the party makes quick work of the traps outside and Stalwart Little bursts through the door! Conjuring a rainstorm, Brigit makes quick work of the hat catching Ruth by surprise! The hat falls into the cauldron that the witch was boiling just prior to the party\'s sudden arrival, creating way too much smoke!

Another wraith, this one stronger, madder, and angrier emerges from the cauldron! Luckily Nyx learned the secret to building the radiant grenades from Sheila as she chucked one across the room. The wraith did not die immediately, seemingly not quite as weak to radiant energy as its cousins. Instead Brigit proves that making it rain is Hella OP and the witches spirit melts under the downpour.

A weak Ruth, then reveals that she has been in a ritual that takes three days to complete and she's on day two. She should be dead but isn't. Instead she is huddled in the middle of a ritual circle and that is only ended by crushing the 1st of three glowing skull shaped talismans around Ruth's waist. The party then keeps looking for Hansel and Grettel below the hut!

The scene they discover is one of cosmic horror, the two children tethered to either side of a well-sized seemingly infinite hole with a large burring eye in the middle. This is the second time we have seen Mordred's fiery gaze and before much can be said, Brigit makes it rain one last time, putting out the fiery eye and filling the seemingly bottomless pit! With two more crushed talismans, the Fartheshire children are saved and the party returns home to reap their rewards!

From Lord Fartheshire, they receive 200 gold pieces each, he also tells the group that he was worried that his children would join the other 100+ missing children in Argos (wonder why there wasn't a trace of them in Ruth's hut?)

From Finkle and the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, they received 100 gold pieces each (after some choice words from Rodrick about ditching the next North Gate battle where they lost ANOTHER 10 men, down to \< 25 now)

From Ruth and Sheila, 100 gold pieces and a free divination:

Four tarot cards were drawn: A tiger (signifying the wild), a forge (signifying creation), a holy knight (signifying faith), a family (signifying progenitors).

Once the tarot cards were dawn the divination began, the lights dimmed and winked out as a great white light burst from the crystal ball and Ruth's eyes rolled back into her skull!

\"THE ANCHORS FALL, THE REALM ASUNDER

HEARTS OF STONE GATHERED FROM PLUNDER

DEATH INCARNATE KNOCKS AT THE DOOR

BELOW THE BLOOD STAR BEGINS THE FINAL WAR!\"

and with that Ruth, clearly uncomfortable with the severity of the reading decides to head off to the Magister's Circle in hopes of consulting the Council of Diviners to decipher this cryptic and apocalyptic prophecy!
