# Session 6 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary - Chapter 6 The Incident aka Planning \> Doing

Following the untimely death of the Tiny Rick, things got cosmic.

With a loud boom, this plane of reality was fractured as it turns out losing 1/10th of a goddess avatar is grounds for interdimensional portals. All at once, a new figure appears and Nocturnus Wayne and Brotor Rootbeard vanish without a trace. The newcomer is named Varys, and he is one of the rebels from another world. A world where there is no Raven Queen to be the goddess of life and death, instead there is a dedicated god of death, Vecna.

In this other world it turns out there is no Artificer's Guild (because there is no Sabine to found it), instead there is only madness. The arbiter of that madness is still Mordred, an out of whack Lich who happens to be the Avatar of Vecna. For the purposes of the summary, we will call him V-Mordred.

V-Mordred also fractured his soul, however he did not fracture the whole thing, instead he passes out crystalline hearts to his enemies to control them. This was the case for our new friend Varys who explains how he and the rest of his party were tricked into a similar ritual, where they completed it as planned and still ended up in the arena. What V-Mordred was not counting on is the sudden appearance of Genevieve who had the Artificer's Guild hex bag still in her pocket. It seems that the more these Avatars weaken their souls by fracturing them, the weaker the boundary between worlds. This seems to be in line with V-Mordred\'s plans to lure as many Raven Queen's blessed to his world as possible, in order to consume her power.

Genevieve, being all too familiar with her own world's Mordred, awoke in the other arena and managed to drag a few of the fellow fighters into consciousness with her. Unluckily for them, there was no Artificer's Guild to rescue them, and instead they found themselves being dissected the same way Daina was supposed to be. Varys attempted escape with some other rebels but was promptly ripped into this world.

Now as for Nocturnus Wayne and Brotor Rootbeard, their side of the story has yet to be discovered.

Once the confusion surrounding the newcomer had subsided, the party promptly decided to travel the path of Light and Dark to collect the 2 remaining liquids required for necromancy: liquid mercury mixed with iron and green acidic poison.

The party devised the first of many plans, the gang was to split up into pairs and enter the spotlight room, but not without checking out what the room does when there isn't a shadow to teleport you. As the first member entered the spotlight room, they travelled down the hallway only to re-appear at the door they entered.

The pairs then split off:

1\) Daina and Brigit decided to play high low, Brigit won

2\) Corrumperre and Nyx decided to play rock/paper/scissors, Nyx won

3\) Sprocket and Bergs decided to \"fight to the death\" and Bergs faked dying, giving up in the process so Sprocket won

4\) Brock Hammer and Varys decided to play the quiet game. Brock lost immediately\...

5\) Stalwart Little and Dallor Dallorbill decided to play a game of dexterity, if Stalwart could pluck the pilfered sigil of Vecna from Dallor\'s hands, then he would win.

But Dallor didn't count on the amount of bad blood between the dragonkin and himself, so Stalwart killed two birds with one punch to the face.

Revenge? check! Win the challenge? Definitely!

Thus, the path of dark was travelled by: Daina, Corrumperre, Bergs, Brock Hammer, and Dallor Dallorbill (who's face seemed to be okay after the punch) and the path of light was travelled by: Brigit, Nyx, Sprocket, Varys and Stalwart Little.

However the path of light and dark have another name, one that was hinted at the beginning of this mess of a haunted mansion.

Enter the Hall of Lies:

The two teams quickly discovered that this was no ordinary raven puzzle, instead there was an added element, XJ-5 and his lies and truths. Lights on? Listen to the robot. Lights off? The door the robot claims to walk through is now 12\'oclock, perform the puzzle as usual based on the number of ravens.

Through trial and error this pattern was figured out, however not without the occasional scrape with skeletons who kept spawning at the first room, the number increasing every time either dark or light group messed up. Once through the Hall of lies, both groups were face to face with the results of failing Mordred. The twisted abominations before the party turned out to be Gromble/Stromble and Strom/Grom, their names no longer just a joke, but instead the names of distinct heads a top-half half-ogre, half flesh-golem bodies.

While it's true that two heads are better than one, that assumes that there were originally two heads to begin with, and not half a brain split between two heads. The party was able to take advantage of the dust for brains golems only after reverting the override XJ-5 triggered remotely, using two levers on the wall. One lever restored consciousness to one head at a time, and the other reverted it.

Luckily for the teams, XJ-5 seemed to be distracted and stopped triggering the override (busy with something on his end?) The teams were able to communicate what they found by restraining the abominations and writing a message to the shadow of their friends on the floor/ceiling. Along with the spigot for the two liquids both teams found a different half of the next riddle:

Light team:

\"Two paths divided must converge within 100 seconds time

Else the ritual be repeated as the ingredients turn to grime

Soul eternal no limits to its life

The blood, the conduit, to cleanse deaths strife.\"

Dark team:

\"The four combined you must make,

upon the shrine you must take

to cleanse the curse which marks his control,

the Gorgon's breath applied to hands upon the keyhole\"

But there was a common element shared between the two liquids, both would turn to grime within 30 seconds of leaving the storage container.

to find the altar where the liquids need to be combined, the party must regroup, share notes and brave the Ritual of the Talon to get stone Talon hands. Where is this Ritual of the Talon? Inside the room with the Gorgon.

This makes the party nervous, how nervous, over an hour of planning how to get into the Gorgon room nervous. The plan however was gold, after attempting to get info from the frightened doppelganger who is trapped in the revenant cell, the party decided to use one of the stone doppelgangers as a distraction while they slipped into the room and locked the Gorgon out. Thanks to Sprocket\'s magic lock, they were able to unlock the door, slip in while the Gorgon ate the stone doppelganger and use Tiny Rick\'s lock picking set to re-lock the door.

Once inside the party found a round arena room, with four large stone pillars and a large stone raven in the middle of the room. Where the belly plumage of the raven should be is a dais with a chalky substance in the murky water. The gang knew what to do next, get the gorgon\'s breath on the dais to activate the ritual. After much debate, plan 2.0 to trick the Gorgon was underway.

The party threw open the doors, and cast two illusions in front of the dais, the first being a gnome and the second being a taunt. The Gorgon seeing the gnome charged full speed into the two front pillars and breathed its petrifying breath all over the illusion. Unfortunately for the group, the breath travels in a cone around the pillars they were hiding behind and manages to start turning Corrumperre to stone, starting at the feet.

Luckily Corrumperre was able to shake it off, and Brock Hammer and Corrumperre managed to each dunk one hand into the dais before fleeing around the stunned bull. Unluckily it seems that both Corrumperre and Brock Hammer have lost the use of a hand each, instead dealing with a stone talon where the hand used to be.

Now the last bit of unfinished business is the nasty doppelganger blood in the foyer dais. Turns out, revenant blood isn't going to cut it for this ritual, so Daina and Stalwart Little attempt to get him to change into one of them. This works after many attempts and backstabs, and results in one angry doppelganger still trapped inside the revenant\'s cell.

The gang somehow manages to open up the study. In the study they find a ton of books lining the wall, various torture devices, and XJ-5 getting his heart ripped out by the released revenant of Jorgen.

Turns out he wants his heart back and doesn't want to be a robot under Mordred, another cautionary tale in the \"don't piss of Mordred\" storybook. The revenant after retrieving his heart strides out of the room, back towards the exit, all the while chanting the second half of his motto:

\"kill the gnome\...\"

At the far end of the room is a raven head overhanging a basin. Sprocket, who had been sitting on a vision from Sabine, Ra of Argos, turned himself invisible and managed to find Mordred\'s original phylactery. The ancient glass vial was all but empty but when Sprocket spoke Sabine\'s name to it, it shattered. A bright light poured out of the shattered glass and enveloped the vial of liquid souls where the green liquid had all but snuffed out the blue. The light seemed to slip through the top and enter the vial, causing the green liquid to swirl and completely take over, that is except for the pure white crystal now suspended inside. A voice you recognize without reason as Ramses begs you to take this crystal to the Forest Zenith so that Ramses can finally die and his madness be imprisoned forever inside the same holding chamber that caused it to take over.

Now that the altar has been found, all that is left is to combine all the different liquids and free the rest of the group from Mordred's icy influence. After combining the liquids in the same order as the ritual of life, red, orange, blue and green, the stage was set to finally rid themselves of Mordred's curse. With a drop of blood from each of the afflicted, the warmth returns to the awakened, fully regaining their previously alive state. With this cleansing, the green liquid suspending the phylactery turned completely white. (Where did the tainted soul go? Was it cured or did it leave on its own?)

With their curse lifted, the party descended from the manor, past the charred corpses of the zombies they had to fight their way through, back to the area where the Artificer's Guild's door had previously dropped them off at.

There in the dark of the night, instead of a door stands a short, hooded figure.
