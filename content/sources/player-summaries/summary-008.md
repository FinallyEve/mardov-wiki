# Session 8 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary 8 - Nyx And The Case of the Not Stolen Phylactery aka Arrival in Argos

The group began their journey on the docks of Argos where they said a brief goodbye to the captain and her boat before heading out to spend their ill-gotten gains. Right before they headed out for that, Stalwart Little introduced his newest friend and compatriot Tiberius, an old and 'wise' cleric of Bahamut. As the introductions were occurring, everyone except Bridgite (sp?), Nyx, Sprocket, and Dallor Dallor grew bored and left for their own lavish homecomings. After introducing Tiberius, Stalwart said a hasty goodbye and also left.

The group of five stuck together and ran around the city, buying miscellaneous items/weapons/armor, managing to anger most of the shopkeepers in Argos, and breaking a few windows with some well-timed Bahamut's. Dallor Dallor also attempted to sell his various, lovingly cared for 'treasures' with mixed success. The group ended this day at THE wine bar in the Upper Ward, called the Raining Pony. It was here that Sprocket requested the finest Argosian wine from the sommelier, but eventually settled on a more practical and affordable vintage of Argosian-adjacent wine simply called 'wine'. Bridgit (sp?) also requested some ale, but instead received a firm lecture on what is available at a wine bar.

After such a tiring day of spending money, the group attempted to return to the Scarlet Tunic Brigade's headquarters. They were met at the front gate by two orphan children whose job it was to not let evil doer's and telemarketers into the building. In a show of force, Brigitee (sp?) transformed herself into a wolf and ran straight through. Tiberius, adding to this spectacle, showed the children what a priest of Bahamut can do: scare orphans. The group was allowed in. After entering the communal sleeping warehouse where they found Daina already fast asleep and reeking of ale, most of the group immediately went to sleep. Dallor Dallor, though, was unhappy with the proximity and accommodations offered by the headquarters and instead chose to seek shelter at one of the local taverns for the night.

In the middle of the night, everyone in the warehouse was sharply awoken by two Drow

interlopers removing the phylactery from Daina's bag. Everyone sprang into action. Without thinking, Brdige (sp?) transformed into a wolf and sprang between the drow and the door, Sprocket arcane locked the door, and Nyx ran as far away from the drow as she could get. After a bit of knocking about, Tiberius attempted to speak with the drow in a zone of truth, only to gain several teeth. Sprocket meanwhile retrieved the phylactery several times and, finally, used his mage hand to hold the phylactery to the ceiling. Little did the group know that the older Drow used to be the vaulting champion of the Drow people before disgracing himself in the Drowlympics and pledging his life to the Raven Queen. Through muscle memory, this man vaulted 15-feet into the air, grabbed the phylactery, and landed on the other side of Tiberius. He then proceeded to sprint towards the door only to be struck in the back by Tiberius and mauled by Bgdrite (sp?). After searching the bodies, the group found studded leather armor, a circular, compass-like object, and a sigil in the shape of an F with a circle around the center. Sprocket recalled that a lord was wearing this sigil the day before at the Raining Pony.

After a short rest, the group was famished and decided to go get some breakfast at the tavern in the lower district, the Galloping Galoshes. This was the same tavern that Dallor had spent the previous night at, and was not thrilled to be back. They were greeted very warmly, given breakfast, and told about the sigil they had found. It belonged to a Lord Farthisher who has been the center of gossip recently. People said that he sometimes answers a question that they did not yet ask. Disregarding this very useful information, the team headed to the upper ward, found Lord Farthisher's house by asking some guards, and proceeded to stand right outside arguing about what to do next. After a few outbursts from Tiberius, a man clad completely in a robe admonishes them and calls the guards.

After retreating a Tiberius distance away, they began to form a plan. Nyx sent her owl familiar through the chimney of the manor to scout out the inside. Plan fully formed and ready, the group went back to the lord's house and used Nyx's brilliant idea of brilliance to pretend to be a messenger of the drow who attempted to steal the phylactery. This got them inside the house. However, Lord Farthisher saw through this plan immediately and both he and the robed man attacked. Lord Farthisher turned out to be a doppelganger (which can read minds) and they all proceeded to have a lovely jaunt of battle. A few SHATTERing revelations from Dallor Dallor brought the evil pair to their knees. The group stopped short of killing the doppelganger and drow (cloaked man) in the hopes of gaining more information. However, no one told Bdfgdaeaghdt (sp?) this, and she proceeded to maul them in the manner that she had mauled their family.

After this engaging sport, the group ran around the house and began searching for the

information that they probably could have gained from their dead adversaries. Sprocket found the floor plan of the first floor and noted something odd about it. He brought this back to the group who all agreed that it was indeed very odd. They then moved on.

On the second floor, they found bedrooms for 2 children and the bedroom of the lord and his lady. They also found an interesting chest that contained rope, fuzzy handcuffs, and a note that read: See you tonight. Meet me in the kitchen. Sprocket, looking out of the second story window, noticed that something was odd about the side of the house near the kitchen. The group headed down to the kitchen and noticed that one of the walls seemed to protrude past where it needed to. Tiberius pulled the metal shelving unit away from the wall, revealing a book attached to the wall. After pulling the book, a secret passage was revealed. Nyx, who had brilliantly remained completely unharmed throughout all the battles, was then squished slightly by the dumb shelving unit as it fell. It was a tragedy for all.

The secret tunnel had great red velvet carpeting that led to a fork. One direction of this fork was covered in velvet carpeting and the other seemed to have been freshly created. The group decided to proceed down the welcoming velvet path, while sending NYX's beloved owl imbued with the light of Bahamut down the other path. The owl both saw and alerted a drow warlock to the presence of the adventuring party. The drow then proceeded to brutally murder Nyx's owl, where Nyx's beloved owl then died a quick and painless death, shrouded in glory and triumph. It died knowing it had done its duty and that Nyx was proud.

Continuing down the other path, the party found the actual lord and his family in some sort of 'prison'. There were several odd things about this dungeon: the walls were padded as though for safety and the 'torturer's' kit on the wall seemed to indicate the creator of this 'dungeon' did not know what torturers needed. There was rope and some fuzzy handcuffs.

The drow in the other tunnel proceeded to rush over to the party and scream at the group for murdering his siblings. He then requested Mordred's blessing. Mordred appeared in the form of a disembodied eye and grouchily admonished the drow for disturbing him during his intense battle with Xerxes that is literally taking forever. To show his displeasure, Mordred sacrificed the drow in order to summon a more useful demon of similar strength. The drow, screaming in agony, is peeled apart and in his place sat a toad. The party, bored by this encounter, turned around to RP with the lord. The toad then transformed into a slightly larger demon toad that attacked Sprocket. Sprocket and Nyx nonchalantly killed the thing.

The group then freed the lord and his family with Tiberius healing one of the sick looking children. The lord was very grateful and rewarded those who saved him with 150 gold pieces. The party then threw a rager at the Galloping Galoshes to end the night.
