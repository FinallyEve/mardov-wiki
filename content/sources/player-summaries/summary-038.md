# Session 38 Summary

*Written by Brigit*

Summary 38 -- Brigit's Account

Did we really only just wake up in the castle this morning? Gods it seems like ages since I last slept. All 13+ of us woke up in Dvinter Castle, an apparent duregard fortress. Supposedly this is one of the 12 fortresses that have to be activated in addition to a weird secret one beneath Vag Mahar (possibly the first forge? I don't know). We had met with Iffrin and made a deal with him that we would kill the Aboleth, The Sleepless, banish it to its realm so that Iffrin can finish it off. Easy right?

Iffrin did give us some important information about the aboleth. They are creatures older than gods, and they were around when the petals were created. Apparently, they are also bitter about the fact that they were passed up and aren't the reigning gods of these realms. They always live in creepy underground lakes. This one is in the lake of pestilence and has the tentacles that we've previously seen.

Before long, we heard a familiar cackling from outside. Could it be the hags we fought ages ago? The party stepped outside and we saw that the ice around the castle has melted. There's also a super ominous shack floating on the water. Lovely. There were three figures around the green smoke. The witches spoke to Finn and thanked him for keeping them informed -- his black eye that someone had spied through.

All at once a few things happened. The witches noticed the rest of us, and immediately Daina's magical soul bag starts trying to pull away. Thankfully she was able to keep a hold of it. The hags look super weird from the other witches we had seen. They look drowned, and they are super gaunt and ugly. And just like Finn, they have giant black fish eyes, but they don't look like Finn's did. It's hard to explain. They decided to strike a deal with us. Daina's axe and our lives for the soul bag, and the two witches broom. They also want us to join them in The Sleepless' lair. Fuck that. I have heard that tale before and I know what happens when we go to the big baddies lair. Nope nope nope. We tried to get them to go to a third party -- the mushroom people, but they're not going for it. Also, Daina keenly notices that they are hurrying us along for no apparent reason. There's got to be some sort of time limit! So we do what we do best and stall.

We conferred with Dr. M B Wells to learn more about aboleth's. Aboleth's have very very old lairs. The water turns foul and is super gross. There's slime everywhere, and the water sources are supernaturally fouled. Don't drink it! There are also able to create a vision of themselves within a mile of their lair, so if we see one, we know we're either screwed or getting close (or both). Stalwart sprung into action and started taking a good hard look at our surroundings. No aboleth, but he did notice that the pestilence coming from the hags is merging with the slime and creating something new and terrible. Bergs notices one of the little mushrooms and we get a message from them "Don't trust the lies, the influence is great. The cauldron, the influence." Let's destroy the cauldron!

Stalwart misty steps onto the platform and shield bashes one of the hags into the water. Weirdly enough, her sisters didn't really care and said that Jezebel would be back? Additionally, he stabbed one of the others, and Rhea's eyes drained and she thanked him. Daina hit the third, and saved her as well. The three of them were under the aboleth's control, but we still have to destroy the cauldron. However, when we damage it, the flotilla shakes. The lake is not a good place, so we need to get everyone there onto shore before we destroy it. Bergs being Bergs grabs the cauldron and tries to take it on his broom, and the entire floatilla comes with him to the shore. Then, our resident evildoer decides to kill the evil hags. We tried to stop him, but he won't listen to us. He kills a hag. Fuck. I decide enough is enough and tried to turn Nocturnous into a bunny, but it didn't work. Finally, Sylvia stuns Nocturnous and Tezka is able to tie him up. We've got to deal with this, it's gotten so bad.

This poor hag who just lost one sister and saw her other one's heart ripped out of her is somehow still willing to help us. She also knows that the aboleth wants Daina for the 13^th^ castle, he wants its phoenix for something. Okay, so definitely killing him. That phoenix was super cool. First though, we definitely need to destroy this cauldron. Now that everyone is safely off the floatilla, Nyx destroys it with Eldritch blast. Bergs watches and tries to learn the spell again (and doesn't). More than 100 souls begin pouring out of the cauldron, Daina tries to catch them with her soul bag, but pulls out a vial of blue liquid instead.

More information from the hag. The Aboleth wants the phoenix because there's trouble in the elemental planes, but he doesn't want to go there. He's not completely connected to the mind flayers, but with the help of the pestilence he can strengthen his hold over them. The hag also hasn't seen Daina's father. She does know of a half-elf, Dierdre. She was bound to the forge below the Aboleth's lair, and we should probably try and unbind her. The hag also know some about Finn's brother, the leader of the new psion's. They are on a ship and working with the aboleth. (Also it's a really nice ship that we should steal). Bergs also wants to know about the Pestilence and why he can drink it. He shows the hag and she is terrified of him -- he should be dead.

Now, to deal with Nocturnous. Sprocket listens to the thoughts of Nightmare and finds nothing good. He's heavily under the influence. Maybe there is a way to save him? Greater Restoration. I take some diamond dust and press it to Nocturnous' forehead. He seems better? Unfortunately, I can only do this once per day, so we've got to stop him from killing things -- not an easy task.

We've got to know where Dierdre, the third hag and the aboleth's lair are. There are multiple entrances to the lair, multiple sinkhole's in the water. But we probably don't want to go that way because it's a trap. Also! The hag gives us 6 water breathing and 6 water speed potions. Irethia connects with the mushrooms and they let us know that it's a trap. However! They can help us out and get us their safely. The can clean up the pestilence, and they owe us for freeing them.

To the mushroom kingdom! We speak to the main mushroom king, the Sovereign. He lets us know that Dierdre has traded her pact and now works for the Aboleth. She's below the lair in a castle. The safest way to get there is not through the Aboleth's lair, the fastest is to go through it. He passes out 10 mushrooms to us. They will purify the sleepless' mucus and will grant us access to attack it. He also gives Nocturnous a pacifying spore. A large mushroom forms over his right eye and covers half of his face. He's not better, but now the super evil part is separate. It's the best the Sovereign can do for now. The spore will also work on Finn's brother.

Suddenly, we hear Nyx's owl scream. A serpent hisses and then pulls both Stalwart and Nyx into Nyx's tome. Varis attempts to pick up the book and is granted the image of a doe. A white glowing doe, "Guide them home"

The hag is leaving us, but before she goes she tells Tezka to meet her in her hut in the Eanvallan forest. (!!!) I wasn't able to hear her in time, so we couldn't ask her why she wants us to return to my home. I'm sure we'll make it there eventually.

We head down to hopefully save Dierdre. The vast room of the castle has a red flame in the middle and green pestilence liquid. She's standing in the middle and has totally given herself to the darkness of the world. Her silver armor has been replaced by dark red menacing armor and she's wearing an eye pendant that's ticking. There are 8 huge pillars that look like dwarf heads.

Dierdre is pretty pissed. She wants to kill Bartrim for not killing her, and now she's a slave again. She summons and controls a hydra that also attacks Bartrim. Finn takes a good look at the columns and notices that they'll create flames in very specific directions. We managed to cut of all but two heads of the hydra, but it grew them back. Sprocket creates the HUGE Bigsby's hand and manages to grapple Dierdre from behind. Dierdre is only focused on Bartrim though and he goes down. We managed to stabilize him, but Dierdre gets there first and severely wounds him. She can't quite bring herself to murder him though. Randall takes a look at Dierdre's medallion and sees that it grants her extra protection. We have also told Nocturnous that he's only allowed to fight non-living things so he takes out the fire columns. Dierdre is finally able to murder Bartrim and kills him with a final blow, cleaving his chest in half. I'm done with this hydra and I turn it into a chicken. I also send one of my giant eagle's to grab it and protect it in the air. Dierdre is not happy with this, so she takes a javelin and hits the chicken to turn it back into a hydra. I turn it back into a chicken.

Daina says enough is enough and strikes out at Dierdre. She cuts off Dierdre's arm and notices a small bauble on the ground -- a diamond that's been shaped into a sphere with Dierdre's heart in the middle. Dierdre's body disappears into black spoke and floats upwards into the lair. With a final blow, Daina crushes the heart. We feel the ceiling above us begin to crack, and the forge goes out.

Oh and also there's the hydra. The eagle bites the chicken, turning it back and then drops it. Banning together we kill it and collect its blood in some vials.

Next time, we'll have to face the Aboleth. Also, I think we have a swamp to drain... We should go talk to the mushrooms real quick.
