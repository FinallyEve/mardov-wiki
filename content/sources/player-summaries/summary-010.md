# Session 10 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

SUMMARY CHAPTER 10 - EXIT THROUGH THE GIFT SHOP, DONT FORGET YOUR OWLBEAR! Aka How do I hold all these gates?

Last week our adventurers, Stalwart Little, Nocturnus Wayne and Brigit awoke to find that Nyx had taken their places at guard duty for the day, so they had the day off\.... until 2 cloaked figures appear outside the barracks!

The two figures are Randall and Tezka a couple of wayward adventurers promised 50 gold pieces upon delivery of the note and 50 gold pieces on delivery of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade to the North Gate (East Gate is the one currently under attack)

It is then that the two newcomers are inadvertently added to the Scarlet Tunic Brigade as there is currently a massive personnel shortage due to the constant battle with wights every night and lack of sleep. On top of assuming that Randall and Tezka were new recruits, Finkle Hollingsworth, C.O.O informed the party that another four children had gone missing while playing near the North Gate. Although none of them are royal (as far as you know), some children were seen to have been carried off by a flying broom! And so Finkle left as quickly as he appeared, funny how quickly he runs off when the subject of money is broached\...

Upon leaving the Scarlet Keep, the party came face to face with an angry mob of parents demanding returns on their \"investigation money\" to find their missing children, some of which started disappearing sometime around two years ago. Turns out about 100 children have been kidnapped in the last two years, the last child kidnapped had been missing for four days!

And so the adventurers learned a few more interesting facts about the missing kids, first that all the children were four years old when they were abducted. Second that Mandu, the elected Captain of the Argos Guard, a company of soldiers amounting to a grand total of one member\.... himself. However, there is something that his position does give him command over, and that is the defense budget. By contracting all the guarding out to the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, Mandu gets to \"prioritize\" which missions are \"funded\" though communal crowd sourcing \*cough BRIBES cough\*.

Third and interestingly enough, election season in Argos comes around every two years, with the last election taking place just a short while after the children went missing. Last and certainly strangest, there were eye witness accounts of a flying broom taking off with the children in a wrapped up bedsheet, like some kind of wooden anti-stork.

And in order to investigate, the adventures headed to the North Gate, where they found Don Mononuchi, frantic and alone. Stalwart Little in the usual fashion slams the wise guy into the city wall, causing him to drop the 100 gold pieces meant for Randall and Tezka.

The Don explains that he has a business proposition for the \"goody-two-shoes\" at the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, after the events aboard the Thornberry Shade, the Don's found themselves walking back towards Machu when Don Damiachi was picked up by the weremoth!

The weremoth was shot down by a group of about 100 soldiers bearing the crest of the Artificer's Guild, Don Mononuchi kept his distance while the battle-weary soldiers shared the tale of their daring escape with Don Damiachi. During the night their camp was attacked by a coven of three crones commanding an army much larger than the force of 100 soldiers. Captured. The soldiers were marched into an abandoned asylum outside Argos.

Don Mononuchi, using a handy magic earing who's twin is worn by Don "The Boss" Damiachi, was able to keep hidden while following the prisoners. On top of that he is able to communicate when within a specific range. During his stakeout, Don Mononuchi managed to see a broom with a crying child in a bedsheet FLY into the chimney of the asylum, as well as Mandu walk out the building right in front of two \"tall-dark and uglies\" who didn't move a muscle.

And so the business deal, is that Don Mononuchi will lead the party to the asylum and help break the party IN, if they promise to rescue Don Damiachi and help him get OUT of the asylum.

Before heading out the party decided to post a sign saying \"KIDS KEEP OUT\" around the North Gate, but quickly saw how a couple of kids walked right by with their little illiterate eyes glossing right over the sign. Instead, the party decided to re-direct the mob outside the Scarlet Hall, to the North Gate in order to have the parents keep the kids away from the North Gate.

With the gate safely guarded by the newly founded \"PTA\", the party swung by the shops to pick up some last minute supplies and headed out!

Once at the fork in the road with the asylum visible in the distance, the party was lead around a lake toward an Owlbear den that descended at least a floor or two below the surface of the ground. Outside the cave, was the half eaten / half rotted corpse of a large stag, ripped apart in so that only the upper torso was left. Inside the cave were five owlbear chicklets, all screaming for \"FOOD\" and \"MAMA\"!

To appease these loud chicklets, as well as attempt to distract them while the party sneaks by, Nocturnus Wayne attempted to throw the deer carcass towards the nest. Instead it landed just short with a loud enough thud. Two adult owlbears appeared to investigate the human smell mixed with loud thud by their nest. Mama and Papa bear were NOT happy that there were strangers near the Baby bears! And like porridge that is much too hot / cold, they had it out for this batch of goldilocks.

During the frey/mauling that ensued, Nocturnus Wayne sustained heavy damage, but Papa bear took the worst of the damage from Tezka resulting in death by punch, the monks secret weapon!

Mama bear was distraught by the death of her mate, choosing to drag the corpse and babies further into the den and out of the way of the dad-murderers.

COMMENSE PHASE TWO of DON MONONUCHI\'s GREAT BREAK-IN PLAN

Producing, a violin case marked with a surprisingly elaborate and well-crafted summoning circle, Don Mononuchi explains that the case summons an indeterminate number of Magmin, tiny fire elementals that produce an explosive charge when killed.

He then further explains that the number of magmin summoned is determined by rolling the magic dice attuned to the circle, which typically just appear to be loaded dice, but are not loaded when they are used in the summoning spell. With sixes between magmin death and BOOM, the party needed to summon at LEAST four and detonate at MOST four magmin in the carved out places on the stone wall. Meaning that if too few were summoned, they would need to summon more, and if too many were summoned then they would more than likely need to NOT detonate them for fear of a cave in!

The MOOCH rolled the dice \... producing EXACTLY four magmin (that is insanely lucky!)

The party made quick work of the weakling elementals and busted their way right into the -2nd floor of the asylum and right into a hallway with cells lining either side and two damaged wights looking confused at the now 5ft tall hole in their base. Killing wights is hard work but the party manages to slay the two wounded guards. On one of their bodies they produce a cell key which opens all the cells on this hallway, which contain four Artificer's each.

The party is greeted by some extremely thankful soldiers who were ecstatic to meet THE AWAKENED! However the soldiers had grave news, Xerxes was completely overtaken with Mordred's necrotic madness, corrupting the cycle of life and death and gaining total control over him immense power! The rest of the guild didn' t make it out as the doorways were all but destroyed while Xerxes was being turned. They also inform the party that there is a \"kindergarten\" upstairs where the children are brought from, and the downstairs where they are brought to and never return. Also, downstairs is a now silent Don Damiachi.

Before they have time to decide where to go next, the sound of boot prints fill the hall as two more guards descend from the upper floor, the party gets ready to take them out and retrieve the child who is screaming and being carried to his nightmare. The party gets the drop on the wights and makes quick work of them, while rescuing a young elven child. With their refugees and retrieved child, the party decides to blow this popsicle stand despite the pleading of Don Mononuchi that you had a deal! Declaring this \"a two-day adventure,\" the party made their way back with new guards to man the gates as well as at least one four year old elf boy named Timmy.

Once back into town, the party stops by Ruth's shop to get her to take a look at Timmy to check him for all manner of evil, instead discovering this note:

Dearest Friends,

I have set out for the Magister's Circle to seek aid in interpreting this grave divination. Your fates are clearly intertwined with this realm, and I must consult my colleges at the great tower.

Sheila has decided to leave Argos one more time with me and sends her regards. Please send word if your path crosses another prophecy as these sorts of things typically come in triplets.

I wish all of you the best of health, not just out of gratitude but for the sake of all that we have come to hold dear. The last war is coming and this world is not ready for what awaits it.

Take Care,

Ruth, Silver Chair of the Diviners Covenant

After discovering that all the PTA members were no longer at the North Gate, the adventurers attempted to return home to the Scarlet Hall, where they were once again greeted by the mob of parents!

Luckily, Timmy's dad, Mattrem a tailor in town, was one of the parents in the crowd. He thanks the party from the bottom of his heart and tearfully follows the adventurers into the Scarlet Hall, where the all collapse and get some much needed rest.
