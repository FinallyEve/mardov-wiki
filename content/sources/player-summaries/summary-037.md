# Session 37 Summary

*Written by Barden*

Summary 37 -- Barden's account

Dreider who stole Daina\'s father\'s Forgesworn axe has fire eyes, gets engulfed by tentacles, fuses together. Speaks into everyones mind forcefully claiming the weapon.

Four cloaked figures appear out of the water behind us. Eyes link with Finn\'s weird eyes

Tentacle monster has a huge mouth underwater, eats the axe

Simultaneously, Finn gets called brother by one of the scions, one of them is Devon (sp?). They follow the Sleepless.

Scions disappear back into the water after Finn pulls off his brother\'s arm

Stalwart heals the dreider to keep it alive while Daina tries to interrogate it

Two hulking red dreadnaughts appear as stalwart tries to pick up the dreider

\-\-- Other Group \-\--

Yousef and Bergs have a moment staring in lava. A cog is moving in the lava. Dune\'s other half of his face informs this group the other group is in trouble.

They decide to go help the other group, Nocturnous brings his Dreider minions along. Varys scouts ahead.

They find tracks from the other group to follow. The other group was clearly not worried about keeping stealthy.

Brigitte in wolf form herds the party to keep them stealthy.

\-\-- Groups Unite on the Bridge \-\--

Start figuring the dreadnaughts, who seem interested in the Dreider. Bardin gets mega fucked. Daina commands them to let them pass, they listen.

Dierdre gets knocked off, killed. Bartrim couldn\'t save her, his heart fades.

Get told to meet at the Castle Divinter.

Attempts are made to save Bartrim. The voice in Sprocket\'s head says Bartrim has served his fate. The head of Bahamat appears on Bartrims new diamond heart.

Sylvia grabs her dad and runs toward the castle, but she and Bartrim are grabbed but group members.

Duergar are on the outside of the castle throwing fireballs into the castle.

Sprocket and Stalwart discover Finn\'s eye is incredibly unnatural, i.e. not biological. It\'s a scrying eye; something is watching through that eye.

A cult of scions following an Aboleth is what put that eye there.

Fire Giants and a djinn with a huge fire sword are waiting inside. Ifrin the Packsworn greets Daina knowing she\'s a Fireforge. He gets angry when she says she can\'t make another axe.

The dwarves are all forced into the castle.

Bartrim says he\'s a Forgesworn, the Magna Har of the Breastplate. He mentions the other Fireforges in the group.

Ifran says the Phoenix is frozen over, gets angry he can\'t steal a Forgesworn weapon. The Dreider was his trap. He wants to freeze the Phoenix.

He\'s interested in hearing that Bergs can break the ice with his maul.

There\'s an armor set on the wall that\'s missing Forgesworn pieces.

Daina\'s dad might be alive; she would have the mark of an axe on her if she was the Forgesworn.

Bergs gives his maul to Ifrin in exchange for help getting Daina\'s axe back. He shatters the ice behind him, causing the being at the top of the tower to fall into a lava pit at the bottom.

A phoenix rises from that pit. It starts to melt the castle. The lava toilet flushes, immolating all but the Fireforges inside.

Ifrin explains that the Duergar used to be part of the dwarven clans, but a lower class than the others. They were the first to fall to Mindflayers, enslaved to go deeper down. Lardegar was the first Duergar to break mindflayer control, and entered a pact with a devil that created Tiefling. They created fortresses like Divinter castle to hold the Mindflayers back.

A group of the 12 Magna Har created a being that could resist the Mindflayers, a Warforged. In the process of driving the Mindflayers back, the Duergar were ostracized and started their own orders apart from the other clans.

Bergs gets the mark of a hammer by activating the fortress. It makes him an apprentice to the master hammersmith, his mother. He needs to find Deacon Smithrunner.

Ifrin says to kill the Aboleth we need to kill it in this plane then travel to the water elemental plane to finish it off. He says the nine hells collapsed.

He gives the group a bead to throw in a fire to call him, then leaves to the fire plane.

The gang hangs before taking a long rest.
