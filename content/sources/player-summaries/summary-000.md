# Session 0 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary of Chapters 0 and 1:

Last time, our adventurers found themselves having signed up for a contract as new recruits of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, despite not remembering when or why,

Their first day on the job, after training in the recruit yard their introduction was cut short by the arrival of Jorgen, a human ranger wearing a gold tunic, being pursued by Kobolds. He claimed that the Kobolds had followed him across town from the sewers and had killed his previous set of recruits. Luckily for Jorgen, the new recruits took care of the flying kobolds while he was able to run in the hall.

But not before Dallor Dallorbill was able to swipe a note written to Jorgen from Finkle with the following information written on it:

\- The precise time of when the recruits were set to arrive at their first day in the Yard\
- The names and classes of each of the recruits

Finkle finished his introduction to the Scarlet Tunic Brigade but not before asking mysterious questions about cults of the Raven Queen. Broden Rootbeard received an inspiration event point and the following information about the Raven Queen Cult:

1\) that they used Necromancy and Animancy as worship 

2\) that they viewed Death as a steroid, and life as potential energy

3\) Look behind their eyelids for members of this cult, as they wish to always sleep under deaths gaze (this is play on, If I die before I wake)

It was then that the new recruits found themselves swept along into the sewers of the Middle Ward. The reason, to activate an ancient ritual housed in a chamber below the city ruins. It was said that the city of Argos was built upon the old city of Argos and that there were 4 sources of ancient magic that needed to be collected.

1\) red blood, hot to the touch

2\) green poison, viscous as sludge

3\) orange mercury, probably actually molten iron

4\) blue, liquid souls.

Upon arrival at the sewers, the adventurers descended the ladder into the sewer waters below. Jorgen pulled a lever on the other end of the hallway and entered the room. Immediately upon entry the door slammed shut and the sewer entrance was sealed. the lever when pulled later lead to nothing, as if it had already served its purpose Alone in the dark the party light up some torches and revealed a horde of Kobolds. The Kobolds were originally just staring at the party until, Daina (not Sprocket), splashed sewer sludge on one of them. Provoked and pissed the Kobolds readied for attack, that was when Stalwart Little stepped in to intimidate them in Draconic.

Something about the Dragonborn was able to keep the Kobolds at bay despite their eerie chanting of \"SCARLET, SCARLET, SCARLET\" and following the party down the hall.

It was there that the party found the door and lever previously used by Jorgon. They then pulled the lever to no avail and discovered the words \"DONT PRESS\" scratched into the door with blood in the trails. The party then decided to search the door for a handle and PULLED the door open, Nocturnus Wayne was the first to enter, but as soon as he joined Jorgen on the other side of the hall (who was slipping is way out of the sight of the Kobolds, and consequently the party) the door slammed shut. It was then that it was discovered that there was a pressure plate on the other end which shut the door and kept it locked, then Broden held the door open as everyone rushed through and left the nest of Kobolds alone.

In the other room was a large depression in the floor and a blood rune on a button, when activated the blood rune starts a counter of 180s which is counting down until when the ceiling will fall and the door will unlock below the button. However, if the button is pressed before the ceiling does, then 4\*the number of presses kobolds would enter the room. The party fought the kobolds and discovered another clue also scratched onto a surface, this surface being the rapidly approaching ceiling, \"RUN LEFT\",

This prompted Stalwart, Nyx, Daina and Brigit, to run into the left wall and fade through just in time for the door to open, kobolds to see this and the ceiling crushed them. A small band survived and had to be fought back by Sprocket, Broden, Dallor, Tiny Rick and Nocturnus Wayne after the ceiling had descended above them but the floor opened up to a spiral staircase drenched in a river of blood from the smooshed kobolds. Meanwhile on the other end of the wall, Stalwart, Nyx, Daina and Brigit find Jorgen and Finkle's Half Ogres henchmen Stromble and Grom or Gromble and Strom in a control room pulling levers (priming the ritual chamber). They are caught off guard by the sudden arrival and struggle to explain why they are here (clearly lying). This half the party slips through another wall into the ritual chamber mentioned by Finkle earlier.

It was at his time that 3 things happened almost simultaneously, 

1\) Jorgen blew a whistle which cause Kobolds to rush into the room, and Grom/Strom put a hand on Stalwart who was more than suspicious by this point.

2\) Stalwart kicked off Strom/Grom and used their bodies as springboard into Jorgon and putting his back to the ritual/kobolds and his eyes on the 2 Half-Ogres.

3\) The other half of the party followed the river of blood into the ritual chamber just in time see the latter half of event 2.

The ritual chamber is a massive open chamber with a steeped roof and four endless waterfalls of orange, red, blue and green liquid/psuedo-liquid into a grate below. In the center of the room on top of a massive gear-like pedestal sits a Phylactery of the Raven Queen. Under the pedestal is grooved with tracks from the center to each of the 4 streams. Additionally, there are mysterious markings on the tracks. along the walls to the room are a total of 12 tunnels to Kobold nests.

Note that the control room is left of where the party following the staircase entered the room. Across the chamber is a steel door with rings in the shape of The Raven Queen\'s Crest!

It is there that we find our heroes right this moment, betrayed, confused, and wrapped up in a web of cultist intrigue and faced with a choice, complete the ritual or fight their way out\...
