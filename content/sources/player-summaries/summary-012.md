# Session 12 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary Chapter 12 -- Witches Get Stitches

Last week our adventurers found themselves in a tomb of their own making as they awakened inside the sealed off cell that served as their camp. After observing the integrity of the walls and floor, Daina borrowed Stalwart Little's trusty hammer and got to work, knocking a tile loose from the floor and illuminating the claustrophobic darkness. The scene below was a great dug out basement with a pool of indeterminate depth that bubbled and burped black tar.

Almost as quick as the sudden appearance of light, the grating sound of a chattering coven reverberated off the cell\'s stone walls:

\" YEHEHHEHEHEHE THOSE FOOLISH ADVENTURERS WENT AND BURIED THEMSELVES ALIVE FOR US!

LITTLE DID THEY KNOW OUR MINIONS ARE TIRELESS AS THEY ARE LOYAL \... AND DEAD, YEHEHEHEHEHE!

AFTER THE WAY THEY STRANDED TABITHA IN THE ASTRAL RELM, I SO LOOK FORWARD TO PERSONALLY FLAYING EVERY INCH OF THEIR SKIN BEFORE OFFERING THEM TO MORDRED.

THAT BLASTED LICH HAS THEM MARKED, YEHEHE BUT THAT DOESNT MEAN THERE CANT BE SOME DAMAGE IN THE SHIPPING AND HANDLING!\"

\" Ah Sister Margaret, you are as stupid as you are powerful, YAHAHAHAH.

We cannot upset Mordred, his influence over madness is omniscient as we are already half mad, YAHAHAHA!

And do you not remember that these undead are a gift! we\'ve already lost too many in your stupid boulder ambush!

How do you expect Markus and Danelle to react when they find out that we lost another 24 wights on top of the five we\'ve already lost?

Don't think that little doll is going to dig you out when they come to collect the pestilence and notice that one\'s soul had been fractured!?

The orders were simple, collect the souls, tainted AND WHOLE and brew the pestilence, those two need it to sunder the winter\' anchor!

Come doggie, lick my feet while we wait for your old minion to deliver our bounty! YAHAHAHAHA!

That's a good boy, if I have a single bunion by the time this boulder is cleared, I\'ll turn your into the worm you are next! YAHAHAHAH! \"

With that the party had heard enough, these witches needed shutting up. And the answer was clear. They needed a good flattening, Wizard of Oz style\... Nyx, Dallor Dallorbill and Sprocket synchronized their shatters to first burrow a large enough hole to clear the runway for the infinite number of boulders that attempted to roll out of the hollow tube above the hallway.

The shatters opened a large enough hole to drop a whole new wave of boulders DIRECTLY on top of one of the two witched who had been trapped in the party\'s last boulder-related shenanigans.

With one of the two witches crushed below the newly formed ramp leading into the black bubling liquid, her sister let out a terrible scream!?!

\" ITS NOT ENOUGH THAT YOU STRANDED POOR TABITHA IN THE AASTRAL RELM, BUT YOU HAD TO SQUASH MY DEAR SWEET SISTER GERTUDE!!!!

NOTHING CAN PREPARE YOU FOR THE SLOW AGONIZING DEATH AS I TURN YOU INTO DIFFERENT ANIMALS AND SLOWLY RIP OFF LIMBS YOU DIDNT EVEN KNOW COULD EXIST!

And with that the party found themselves in combat with the witch. However, what the witch didn't see coming was the party rogue, Brock Hammer, recently returned from the V-Mordred's dimension. Brock nimbly snatched the broom out from under the witch, grounding the hag once and for all\... that is until she shot a beam of lightning so powerful it pieced straight up to the first-floor basement. After almost killing the last hag, the party decided to head her desperate pleas for her life, in the process they learned a great deal of information namely:

1\) The black liquid is called \"Pestilence\", is corrupt souls. This is what the hags were kidnapping the children for, they were turning their souls putrid by forcing them to do evil in their dreams.

2\) The hags as well as the other two branches of the fabled Argosian Woods Withces, a grouping of some of the wildest and most powerful diviners in Mardov\... one divination from these witches is enough to be considered legend

3\) Mordred is using Danelle and Markus as his agents while he rests and recovers within Xerxes' old body

4\) Mordred has issued a bounty on the \"awakened\" and a certain phylactery in theirs or The Scarlet Tunic Brigades' possession

5\) Danelle and Markus need the pestilence for the Winter's Zenith, a mysterious place of unknown origin

But perhaps greatest of all was the deal offered in exchange for her life, one divination for her safety. Bergs reluctantly agreed. The light from the suspended candles tracing the outside of the pit immediately blinked out, their steady glow vanishing for a beat before bursting with a flare of green flames

\" DOUBLE BUBBLE TOIL AND TROUBLE,

GARD\'DAEMON IS NEIGH, THE FIRST ZENITH IS RUBBLE,

PREPARE THE PESTILENCE WITH CHILDRENS SOULS,

THE HUSKS REMAIN UNTIL THEIR TOLD,

DOUBLE BUBBLE TOIL AND TROUBLE,

ANCHORS LIFTED AND HEARTS ARE SMUGGLED \"

After the last syllable boomed from the hags voice, the room was once again plunged into darkness, the hag flashed from sight in the process. Using his quick wit and calculated understanding, Sprocket who refused to descend into the chamber below, found the hag attempting to claw her way up through the hole she had made with the laser. And with the snap of his fingers, ended the coven leader then and there.

The spoils of war were one working magic broomstick, though originally attuned to Bergs was kindly gifted over to Nyx so that she may fully commit to the stereotype which already surrounds her people. Luckily (or almost unluckily) the party was able to recover the second magic broom but the broom was snapped clean in two.

Miraculously, Sprocket was able to mend the broom which was given to Bergs for his previous act of kindness and his innate ability to tame the wild broom. During this time, Daina was interested in attempting to rescue to discarded shell of a little girl, whose undead fingers had snapped in her attempt to dig her captors out of the 3rd floor basement.

While she was able to recover the soul shard which could have revived her, the shard was already tainted with pestilence and the shard upon being thrust back in its vessel immediately resulted in the unnamed four-year-olds second painful death as the pestilence consumed her further. Her numerous wounds too much for her poor body to take, the little girl died with a scream, a moment that would replay itself in Daina's mind in the quiet moments for the rest of her days.

The party using their new toys managed to ascend through the broken ceiling, up into the kindergarten. The kindergarten was a locked room lined with four beds, only one of which is unoccupied. At the far end of the room slumped the hunched over physical form of the night hag.

Defenseless in her current state, Nyx and Daina proceeded to make quick work of the lasting physical body, further stranding the hag in the astral plane. On her body the party found a number of interesting goodies:

1\) A soul bag, a tainted bag of holding crafted by night hags from the skin of their victims, which Daina took responsibility for.

2\) The physical heartstone which Sprocket held for safe keeping, with a bit of information from Bergs the resident hag expert, it became clear that this stone had a power that was less sinister than what it had been used for: the ability to cure any disease, even that of the soul

3\) a letter from Markus and Danelle stating that they had lent another 30 wights to the coven, and that despite their shortcoming, the pestilence is needed in order to assault the Winter's Zenith

Using her new soul-bag, Daina began to produce the crystalized soul shards for each of the comatose children. These shards were clean of the tainted pestilence which plagued the poor girl below, and as such the children awoke from their endless nightmare. It was then that Stalwart Little looted the toy-chest at the end of the hall, producing a doll for Emily, a ball for Ian and Craigor, and a familiar set of silver jacks with the ball missing.

After pocketing these silver treasures, Dallor Dallorbill proceeded to check the markings on the door, knocking on the wood and producing a loud enough sound to rile up the wights behind.

Having already determined that the walls of this abandoned asylum are thin enough to bust, Bergs used his axe to create an opening wide enough to see behind the magically locked door, and was met with a flurry of arrows. Stalwart Little breathed fire on the unwanted visitors, catching the whole building on fire, and creating a sense of dire urgency to bail quickly from this deathtrap.

Quickly bailing back down to the second-floor basement, the party relied upon their new favorite spell, shatter to clear an opening back into the owlbear cave, meeting up with Don Mononuchi who was more than surprised to find the three children atop the new brooms as well as a naked Don Damiachi who had been transfigured into a dog, his tongue split down the middle. Not having time to explain, Stalwart Little head locked the wise guy and proceeded to hoof it all the way back to Argos with a confused mobster under each arm, but not before sealing the wights inside the burning building with a bit of pestilence produced by Daina's new bag.

The scene as they returned to the North Gate was both comical and tragic. Comical as the party looked ridiculous, everyone covered in all manner of dust and ash, one child riding on Nyx's broom, a naked Don Damiachi riding on the front of Bergs' broom, a child in each of Daina's arms, and a strangely silent Don Mononuchi being carried by Stalwart Little who out of habit was still head locking him. Tragic because the scene they arrived at was that of three desperate parents watching the tree line of the Old Forest, silent and still, as the rest of the gate bustled around them. When the adventures arrived at the gate they greeted Dolf, Linda and Cervantes by returning their children and showing them the note they pilfered from the dead crone.

Upon reading the note, the blacksmith and would be Minister of Justice, traded his anvil for a sickle and called upon his new heroes to help him rid Argos of the plague which had crippled it, the corruption brought by Mandu must be stopped. He vowed to rally up his supporters along with Linda and Cervantes and told the adventurers to meet them outside Mandu's manor at dusk.

But after traveling back to the Scarlet Keep and talking with Finkle they came face to face with the rumors that had been surrounding them since their departure to the asylum. The common folk with more than enough prodding by Mandu had been lead to believe that these Scarlet Tunic \"heroes\" were actually demons who turned The Golden Reach into demons as well with their hellfire and on top of stuffing the mayoral ballot box, split town to avoid justice. Now with their return, the adventurers are seen by half the town as demons and the other half as saviors.

Who will come to their aid in the liberation of Argos? Will Mandu's propaganda forever defame the Scarlet Tunic Brigade? Will we meet the last Argosian Woods-Witch, The Earth Mother? What became of the Golden Reach's Danelle and Markus?
