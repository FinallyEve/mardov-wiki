# Session 13 Summary

*Written by Brigit*

Summary 13 -- Brigit's Account

Dear Diary,

Today was an interesting day. I mean it\'s not every day that you kiss a Succubus, reveal your destiny, and save a town from corruption, right? The group and I started the day off fairly normal, waking up in the Scarlet Keep, getting yelled at by townspeople, the usual. Nyx, Bergs and Varys snuck out the back entrance to avoid the fight, and Stalwart, Daina, Hedwig and myself went out the front, but not before Daina grabbed Sprocket by his neck and hoisted him outside. We exited the keep find an angry mob led by Vikrum shouting outside the keep:

\"DEMONS THE SCARLET TUNIC BRIGADE IS FULL OF DEMONS! STAND STRONG GOOD PEOPLE OF ARGOS,

MY FIRST ORDER OF BUISNESS SINCE RE-ELLECTION AS MINISTER OF JUSTICE IS TO CALL FOR THESE CRIMINAL\'s TO STAND FOR THEIR EXORSISM!

THEY FRATENIZE WITH A DAUGHTER OF TYGRAS WHO RIDES A WITCHES BROOM!

THEY CONJOURED A VISION OVER THE CAPTAIN OF THE GUARD, TRANSFIGURING HER INTO A FIEND!

THEY CAN TRAVEL AMONGST THE DEAD IN THE OLD FORREST WITHOUT BEING ATTACKED BY UNDEAD!

WHY ELSE WOULD THEY ONLY BOTHER TO RESUCE ONE OF THE MISSING CHILDREN WITHOUT SEARCHING FOR THE REST! WHAT MAKES ANOTHER CHILDS LIFE GREATER THAN YOUR OWN!

FIND NOCTURNUS WAYNE AND HIS DEMON CONJOURING FIENDS!\"

However! Good old Dolf was also outside defending the scarlet keep with his own mob of craftsmen and craftswomen covered with their protective gear and using their tools as weapons (hammers, forging tools, even knitting needles from Matron). Dolf defended us and shouted to the angry mob:

\" HOLD FAST, DO NOT HARM YOUR OPPONENTS! DISARM THEM SO THAT WE MIGHT SETTLE THIS WITH WORDS NOT VIOLENCE, ONLY MANDU AND HIS DEMONS NEED DIE TODAY! TOO MUCH ARGOSIAN BLOOD HAS BEEN SPILLED!

THERE WILL BE GOOD MONEY IN IT, I PLAN TO SELL BACK EVERY STOLEN WEAPON AT TRIPLE THE PRICE AND SPLIT THE EARNINGS 50/50! HAHAHA! \"

Now that we had a moment to take it all in, we noticed that most of the angry mob was parents of the children who were taken and later returned by the Golden Reach. Luckily, Daina pointed out that their children weren\'t the same, that they were broken and that we could fix them. Matron was also able to confirm that Timmy had been fixed by us, and only us.

As luck would have it, we had gotten the situation under control when our old pal, The Revenant, showed up again and headed straight inside the Scarlet Keep. The mob of parents had no clue what to do, blaming us, confused as always:

\" THEY SUMMONED ANOTHER DEMON IN ARGOS! COMRADES TAKE BACK YOUR ARMS AND ARREST THESE VIAL FIENDS! \"

Despite the craziness, Dolf still had our back, promising us to hold them off as he yelled to his warriors to hold the line. We quickly headed inside to hold off the Revenant yet again. He had found Finkle and was once again trying to kill him. Why we actually need Finkle is probably something we should figure out, but he did spill some useful information. Finkle doesn\'t actually remember most, if any, of the things he did while he was possessed by the Lich. He and the gnome of his life, Yvonne, went to the Forest Zenith to find the Heart of the Wilde. Yvonne had split up from Finkle and had gone to the lower floors while he had gone to the upper ones. Apparently, she awaked something and by the time she had found Finkle, it wasn\'t her.

Luckily for us someone, Sprocket I think, remembered that the Revenant can regenerate, but not if the damage is from fire damage - which he is also weak to. Firebolt after firebolt hit him, we threw oil at him and lit it on fire, Stalwart remembered that he was a dragon and blew fire on him, literally every which way the Revenant could be burned, we burned him. Finkle ran away, again as always, with his only concern being for his precious tapestries. Finally, Varys fired 2 arrows and they arched downward into the prone Revenant\'s eye sockets as the lights of his eyes, finally, finally went out. Daina grabs her soul bag and starts pulling cloth out of it, way more cloth than the bag could ever physically contain and it slowly surrounds Jorgon\'s soul, grabbing it and pulling it into the soul bag to become some sort of magical battery.

We headed out of the Keep to Mandu\'s manor. We figured that not only was he behind all this, he was probably hiding out at his manor, the coward. We spent\... an embarrassingly long time if I\'m being frank, trying to get inside of the manor and failing to be sneaky until Stalwart ripped a door off its hinges and scaring a guard half to death. Inside, we found some booby traps. There were hollow tubes on the ground and the walls that shot out poison arrows. There was also a room with runes on the floor that needed a specific amount of weight on a panel to open up another door. This whole time we were exploring the manor, Bergs had decided to stay outside, follow the guards, and generally keep a lookout. We were passing by a window when he flew by on his broom and knocked. Unfortunately, we couldn\'t let him in due to some iron bars on the window, but we asked him to go take care of the scorpions so we wouldn\'t be bothered when we left.

In front of us, we were faced with two double doors covered by pleated red leather. Before we entered, we heard haunting a voice from the room:

\" Dearest sweet MIA, have we not punished you enough for your transgressions against the master\'s plans? We require the last divination from you, the other WOODS-WITCHES were so much more \... agreeable.

You\'ve always claimed the title of EARTH MOTHER, what will you do now that we\'ve taken your child and turned you to stone? It seems your more EARTH than MOTHER these days.

\" You were supposed to wipe the memories of those chosen so that they could be easily tricked into being the last few souls for SABINE\'s RESURRECTION but they continue to be one step behind us, now all they need is your divination and they will have all the keys to find the last zenith!

YOU WILL DIVINE FOR US, EVEN IF I HAVE TO TURN YOUR PRETTY STATUE INTO A PILE OF RUBBLE! WHERE IS THE HARVEST\'s ZENITH!? \"

We burst into the room to see Markus and Danelle up on a platform. In between them was a bleary-eyed, dazed, drooling Mandu. And in the corner of the room we see the supposed Mia, a statue of a lady with light around her eyes holding a lantern in front of her. Markus immediately tried to get the Heartstone by summoning it to him, but Sprocket was quick on his tiny toes and caught it midair! Nyx, Varys, and I immediately began attacking Danelle, but Sprocket had other plans. With his mage hand, he rubbed the heartstone on Mia and magically, she came back to life! Markus was turned into stone almost immediately, frozen in agony. The heartstone is shattered into shards that turn into gas before they hit the ground. From the gas, the Night Hag Tabitha appeared to join the fight.

Danelle ran up and kissed me, charmed me to do her bidding. I had no control, I had no notion of myself, all I heard was her voice, urging me to kill Mia, KILL MIA. Daina and Stalwart both attacked the succubus trying to bring her down. Nocturnus Wayne was finally able to throw his radiant bomb on target and hit all of the bad guys, including myself. Bergs came in right on time for comedic relief and decided to stab the succubus with his broom. But as the Night Hag magics the broom away from him, he gets a swing from his great axe and hits the succubus. I twitched into a wolf, and attacked Mia, before being told once again to obey Danelle, Bring Mia TO HER.

The Night Hag faded into the floor like a shadow and appeared directly behind Nyx before touching her with an icy touch, freezing her before being hit by Sprocket\'s magic missiles. Danelle once again had a chance to attack and tried to charm Stalwart Little, but because he is a paladin he\'s immune and I was snapped out of my charm. Stalwart was then able to smash the succubus to the ground with his shield, killing her. Daina shattered the Markus statue ensuring that he could never come back to haunt us, and Nyx, without looking, throws an Eldritch Blast over her shoulder, hitting the witch in the face as she fades into existence never to return.

Mia finally spoke, and told her tale:

\" It\'s about time I got my revenge for imprisoning me and my daughter for 5 long years, but you should know that my divinations always come at a price. For I derive my power from the EARTH itself and the laws of nature are simple, everything has a cost!

BUT UNDER YOUR CONTROL I WAS FORCED TO BREAK THAT EXCHANGE, YOU COST ME MY OWN LIVING FORM wiping these adventurer\'s memory and compelling them to join the SCARLET TUNIC BRIGADE back when you were so confident in your scheming, well the joke is on you!

For I have an outstanding bill with nature that's long overdue! And these adventurers gave up their memories for more than just fate, their divination has already been paid for!\"

And additionally, the third Divination:

\"As the rain falls on the fallow harvest, and the matrim pact fulfilled,

the anchor of famine shall be lifted, the echo of TYGRAS stilled

As the snow drifts over the ruined tribe, and serpent plagues the sea

the anchor of pestilence shall awake, to answer the curators plea

As the fire bellows on the forgotten forge, and the blood star is found

the anchor of war shall be silenced, as the new paragon is crowned

As the sunlight warms the forest gate, and madness prisoner freed

the anchor of death shall sway, to bring peace to starcrossed three \"

Mandu then woke up from his trance, right in time for Stalwart to give him the old Stalwart SpecialTM and smacking him into the wall. Mandu revealed that the children\'s souls have been consumed and turned into pestilence, pestilence that was at this moment heading toward the Winter Zenith. We dragged Mandu out of the room and back to Argos to reveal everything that happened with the election, the Golden Reach and us, and how everything we had been saying was all true. Vickrum was arrested, and Nocturnus Wayne is now the mayor of Argos.

Until next time,

Me.
