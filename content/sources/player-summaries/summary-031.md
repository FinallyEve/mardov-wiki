# Session 31 Summary

*Written by Stalwart*

Summary 31 -- Stalwart's State of Mind

I find myself, yet again, leading the Scarlet Tunic brigade into a foray into the unknown. After

tasking Irethia, Varys, Brigit, and Tezka to begin exploring this new mind-flayer abyss (carefully, I tell them, though I have never known this group to be careful), I take Nyx and her broom to look closer at the hovering cage where the deer used to be. Inside the cage are some mad scribblings that neither Nyx nor I can decipher. Usually if I can't read it, it means the scribblings are meaningless, but just to be sure I command Nyx to get Randall. Randall pops down (I must learn how he is doing that) and reads something about a hobgoblin and... hobGOBLIN! Perhaps this one will finally know where it's brethren is hiding out. Perhaps...

Where is Daina going? By herself? Again?!!!

I tell Nyx to take us over to Daina. I swear that woman is trying to kill herself. Sometimes I

wonder what this group would do without me watching over them. How will I find the time to

locate these goblins, when I can't let my comrades out of my sight for ONE second?!

As we enter, something begins speaking to us. I would swear it was Linda again, but she seems

back to her old self right now. Man, I kind-of miss her creepy, psychotic, intelligent self. I feel a

bit bad that I tried to replace her.

We find a mind-flayer that has been imprisoned by the other mind-flayers, which I think makes

him a good guy? He just wants to be free and is willing to give us information for it. So we tell

him to talk. He tells us about a guy, Vectraxiffsus Fricjfixa, that created a "spell plague." Also, he tells us that the ill-thid's here are a mixture of a bunch of shitty stuff like lich-shit, and the human prisoners are also experiments like this. Also, they were looking for a hobGOBLIN! Finally! I shall finally find the rest of the goblins and have my revenge! Nothing shall stand in my wa....

... Where is Daina going?

As I catch up to Daina and Nyx, we find the Thid's friends in the back of this room and Daina

attacks (as she does). While we fight, one of these Thid's attempts to mind control me! Telling

me that the ill-thid will betray us. Does he think we don't know this? What's the fun if he doesn't

betray us?

These Thid don't know who they are fighting! As we carve our way through them, Nyx begins to

question them about Raynardeen. They know something, but aren't talking. Ha! If they won't

talk, then we will beat it out of them! What idiots! Coming here alone to fight the Scarlet Tunic
