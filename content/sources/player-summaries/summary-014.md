# Session 14 Summary

*Written by Tiberious*

Summary Chapter 14:

MAY THE SILVER SCALES MEASURE THE BALANCE OF GOOD AND EVIL IN THIS WORLD, OH BAHAMUT THIS IS MY ACCOUNT OF GOOD DEEDS AS THIS BRONZE ONE HAS PERFORMED IN YOUR NAME,

PLEASE LEND YOUR EAR TO AN OLD MAN IN THE TWILIGHT OF HIS LIFE, SO I MAY TIP THE SCALES ONCE AGAIN FROM DISCORD TO ORDER:

The day began much like any other, I awoke from my slumber after arriving the night before.

Finally, reunited with a band of merry youngsters who, while misguided, seem to be at the forefront of justice being brought to an unjust world.

We emerged from the Scarlet Keep, I must talk to that Gnome about re-naming the place, really Scarlet, how unholy a color. It doesn\'t even match a precious metal! There was a delightful young man working on a contraption of some sorts, I tried my hand at fixing the hunk of metal but it was broken beyond my abilities as a cleric. This young man had a board of INJUSTICE which while seeming minor, gave these old scales a chance to do some real good for the people of Argos as well as inform them of their divine duty to justice and order.

And so in the town square we took the first and obviously most import mission of all: FINDING THE LOST CHICKENS

I took the job immediately, rolling the dice as they say, and asking nearest shopkeeper, a fine young gem seller who's tent was all too dark and who seemed to be very busy and distracted by the inevitable flood of ball bearings which would later plague the jeweler. Stumped I looked to the aid of companions who managed to round up most of the lost fowl, save a few, who's locations were kindly pointed out. How do they manage to navigate this world without your guidance and still manage to be effective force of order!?! Truly I have found a company of your CHOSEN!

Once properly captured the chickens were put away and goodbyes were given. Stalwart, the caring young dragonkin had a particular attachment to one of the lost flock and offered to purchase this new found familiar! Now I know that a good deed is its own reward, however the kind farmer had his own good deed to fulfil and so he insisted in giving me 25 GOLD pieces! GOLD! CAN YOU BELIEVE THAT! I still find myself, baffled that society has chosen GOLD to be the metal of highest value! PREPOSTEROUS!

However, this misplaced priority did manage to work out in my favor, as I was able to purchase the rest of Farmer Black\'s supplies AND have enough eggs to cook a grand breakfast in celebration of reuniting with your CHOSEN! However, in my wisdom, I attempted to carry half of my ten dozen eggs in one basket! HOW FOOLISH! and like the fool I am, I lost half of breakfast right then and there. Randall, kind young man he is, managed to mend a few, however some things are beyond mending, a lesson well worth the a few gold pieces.

Now for next bit of order, we popped by to help a poor bored little girl. I attempted to console her with one of the dolls I had picked up in the Fartheshire mansion, but I was foolish to think that trick would work twice in a row. For every bout of order, there is an equal bout of chaos. Luckily the fast fingers and excellent powers of your CHOSEN made quick work of boredom and the proverbial princess was saved! Again the parent attempted to pay me in GOLD! Like it has value!?! Well we\'ll see what the clergy can melt this down for or if opportunity arises to trade it for something truly good!

Now the next item, well items, on the list pertained to two opposing sides. One oddly was a half-orc woman named Ghak, who was interestingly enough apart of the scientific community! There looks to be once again, trouble in the Old Forest, as there is a beast living amongst the mists, with HUGE FEET! We simply had to capture the majestic animal in the name of research! However, it turns out this animal is DANGEROUS! or as dangerous as a man named Zanmorn Droverson could describe. The misguided man wanted the beast dead for a crime long committed!

In the name of peace and justice, we decided to mislead Zamorn and put his mind at ease, claiming we would rid the Old Forest of this threat, and rid it we would! BY CAPTURING IT!

Now this old head of mine has trouble remembering the details but we agreed to meet the scientist after determining what the last entry on the justice board truly meant!

Luckily, and strongly we found the halfling woman playing a game of cards against some less than reputable sailors. This halfling woman had lost her property in a game of chance, however she was not prepared for the quick wit and faster fingers of one Dallor Dallorbill. Normally I don't approve of his methods but credit where credit is due, without me even paying attention to the game, he managed to have already won map back AND solved the impossible riddle it held!!

Somehow the poor confused sea folk had mixed up their letters when noting where this particular bit of treasure was hidden, but Nyx, Tezka, and Brock Hammer made quick work in unscrambling the symbols.

No doubt that divine intervention played its hand during this speaking of tounges!

The message was relatively clear:

\"FIND THE LIGHTNING BLASTED OAK TREE

THEN GO NORTH FOR 2 MILES

UNTIL YOU FIND THE HILL SHAPED LIKE A SADDLE

THEN GO NORTHEAST FOR 1 MILE

UNTIL YOU FIND THE OLD GRANITE QUARRY

THEN GO NORTHEAST HALF A MILE

YOU\'LL FIND THE TREASURE GUARDED BY SPIRITS\"

Now typically I am un-interested in the material gains of this world, but the map claimed there were malevolent SPIRITS haunting that quarry, and being a man of faith, I cannot let such an abomination continue on this plane!

My talents ferrying the undead to the next life were once again called upon! However, we had already agreed to find this large footed creature to two people and despite my drive to rid the world of these affronts to your creation, a promise is only silver when kept!

And so we met with Ghak and found the clearing with the most footprints. It was here that my time tested experience saved the day entirely! You see I dug a hole in the ground, just like that time I captured 15 wondering vampires in the streets the of Connis Abbey! But this time, I learned from the mistakes of the past and personally made sure the mechanism that dropped the beast into the hole would go off! And there is nothing quite like a job well done, especially when you do it yourself. The beast distracted, fell right on top of my shield which was obscuring the entrance to my trap! Once properly subdued, Randall managed to communicate with the beast, explaining that he must come with us back to Argos for a few tests. It turns out the beast is a wonderfully pleasant young man who happily obliged!

After delivering our promised specimen, we once again took the forest and found our way to the old quarry! Inside the quarry, to ward off any evil spirits I lead the way guided by your light! and you light has NEVER led me astray! Barring a brief tussle with some rather displeased snakes, we found ourselves in the middle of a large room with a mysterious pool of water with a submerged chest. Much more pressing a matter were the seemingly endless skeletons which had been unceremoniously entombed in the cave. Why they were missing their silver coins! How might they transition to the afterlife without being able to pay the ferryman! Most tragic of all, the only money I had was GOLD! Those poor souls, forced to pay the ferryman at long last with nothing but worthless gold! My heart breaks for them.

It was no wonder that the skeletons were restless and attacked! They undoubtable were gravely upset at being buried so close to a treasure trove and yet didn't have a coin between the lot to speak for. Dallor using a brand of magic I have not seen before, managed to entrance the whole lot, making purifying these desecrated skeletons rather easy, for none of the defiled may stand in your light unscathed! There was one skeleton that didn't look quite human, which was odd, however given a few more bops on the head, he did remember that he was supposed to be dead, proving that the map was mistaken in blaming the guarding of this treasure on sprits! BAH, these were merely possessed skeletons, nothing a little holy symbol can't fix!

Now I know what you're thinking, that the others cutting the poor halfling woman out of her own treasure hunt was an act of malice, however you and I both know that they gave me a larger portion that was needed in order to provide a larger boon for your true CHOSEN. And so I took my allotted cut, and returned to the halfling woman to pay her finder's fee. Oddly enough, Stalwart also had business in the area, and the halfling was so blessed by your scales that she offered nearly her entire portion as tithe to your name! It is because of this great deed, that I am recalling this tale of divine justice! Truely, Stalwart and the rest of your CHOSEN move mountains in your name!

MAY YOUR PLATINUM NAME RING THROUGH THIS LAND FOR ALL TIME OH AGELESS ONE!

\~ The Bronze to your Silver, Tiberious D. Rex
