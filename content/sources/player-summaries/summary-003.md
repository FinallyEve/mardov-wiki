# Session 3 Summary

*Written by Unknown*

Summary of Chapter 3 and 4 Act Unnatural:

Daina waved her hex bag around the arena and announced herself as one of the awakened. The result being she was immediately rag dolled by Mordred\'s ghastly visage, her body removed from the gladiator battle as if lifted by the air itself.

As soon as she left the arena, the command to begin the Gladiator Battle was issued. The prize was to be selected to serve as Mordred\'s next vessel. The last vessel, Finkle Hollingsworth is mysteriously missing.

The awakened quickly learned to tell friend from foe by looking at the lights in their eyes. The first targets were easy choices as there were plenty of ghouls to keep everyone busy. There was however, unfinished business between Stalwart Little, Nyx, Nocturnus Wayne and the accused turn clock, Dallor Dallorbill.

Dallor was quick to plead for his life, claiming that he was only trying to save his skin when Mordred clearly had everyone dead to rights. It was, after all the smart thing to do. Those were Dallor's last words as he was quickly knocked unconscious by his former party.

Luckily, Brotor Rootbeard decided to honor those old ties and stabilize Dallor\'s condition.

Meanwhile the ghouls seem to be searching out the awakened and attacking the same target. Whoever draws the attention of the undead crowd is immediately targeted. Realizing this, some members of the awakened, like Bergs, an agent of the Artificer's Guild and bloodthirsty fighter, tried camouflaging themselves in Ghouls' blood.

But there was more that. The party found out the hard way as a brilliant move by Brotor caused all eyes to follow a magic luminescent pair of manacles was thrown into the center of the arena. There, etched into the sand to avoid detection, spelled the markings of a long-distance teleportation spell gate.

It was then that the arena shook with a blast of green flames that seemed to snap into being. Under the light of the blast, Brigit noticed that the audience of 2000 ghouls had trickled down to a mere 200. With the audience literally decimated, the farce of a match continued as the gladiator ghouls began targeting the teleportation gates. The gates were arranged in an X around the arena to mark the spot for an invasion which began the day that Corrumpere and Bergs gave their lives delivering Xerxes' amplification hex bags to as many of the awakened as they could.

Out of the five gates, three remained intact, one was never activated, and one was somehow tainted by the blood of the fallen ghouls. Midway through the struggle the party was interrupted by the cavalry arriving. Xerxes and the Artificer\'s Guild magicians used the three active gates to still complete the spell, but the conduit only was able to travel both ways. This meant that all of the awakened must be ascended out and if the rest of the army tried to invade, there would be no hope for retreat. A one-way ticket.

However this was no ordinary magician. Xerxes is not just a being with 500 years mastery of the animatic arts but he is also a being with 500 years without proper revenge against the man who took his queen from him. There was an entire necropolis of bones to be picked with the shade that is now Mordred. The arrival of Xerxes marked the need for Mordred to immediately declare an heir, he quickly appeared before Tiny Rick and drenched him with the contents of Mordred\'s last phylactery. Immediately Tiny Rick felt the change as all warmth left his body. His very soul was being infected fragment by fragment.

Meanwhile, Sprocket upon being imbued with Sabine\'s magic for the second time, unlocked a message planted in his psyche from Sabine, a message for Xerxes which has yet to be revealed.

Xerxes was still able to free all of the awakened with the help of the amplification hex bags and Sabine\'s gift. It seems that fortune finally smiled on the ex-Scarlet Tunic Brigader's as they awaken from the portal inside the Artificer's Guild mobile headquarters. The headquarters travels via portals and the base can only be attuned to 4 portals at a time, each represented by a color.

Black being the color of the arena where the party was rescued 24 hours ago. This color was the dimmest of colors and seemed to be flickering as if not calibrated correctly.

Xerxes told the tale of Ramses, himself and Sabine, The Ra of Argos. Sabine was thought to be the avatar of the Raven Queen herself, and to honor her deity she founded two polar colleges of magic. These colleges were to serve as pillars in the arcane arts for all magicians in Argos and beyond. They were the Circle of The Murder to study necromancy, the study of death, and the Artificer's Guild to study animacy, the study of life. Sabine\'s goal was to bring the best of both Arcanum together in Argos to facilitate research about the balance between life and death.

Sabine trusted these organizations to her two closest and most powerful friends. The first being Xerxes, Sabine\'s husband and true love, an easy first choice, and the second fell to her old teacher, a man of incredibly arcane knowledge and a deep love of Sabine. This posed a problem for the Queendom that was Argos, as Ramses grew more knowledgeable as the new head of the Circle of the Murder. But as his knowledge grew so too did his jealousy. Soon it became an overwhelming ache which itched its way into a plan.

Ramses would gift himself and Sabine with eternal life in order to spend eternity together without Xerxes, as Ramses believed that this could only be achieved through the clearly superior school of magic, necromancy. Ramses spent years searching for the key to everlasting life, and upon finding his solution, becoming a Lich, he offered this to Sabine. But the cost was too great. Sabine rejected the ritual, but Ramses performed the conversion against her will.

Upon finding herself a newly minted Lich, Sabine starved the Lich portion of herself by refusing to consume the souls of other humanoids. Her body crystalized and she became the Demi-Lich that the party found inside the large phylactery in the ritual of life room.

With the containment phylactery destroyed by a brave company of adventurers, Sabine was reborn with her still possessing her own free will. With that will she decided to revive the party that saved her, however he did so at the cost of her being. Depositing a small portion of her newfound life in each of her new children, in the form of a new crystalline heart.

Because of Sabine\'s gift the group was able to have their consciousness restored. Despite Sabine\'s best efforts, she was unable to destroy and deter Mordred and instead he fled after his vessel was revived apart from him. He soon returned to discover the former Scarlet Tunic Brigade unconscious, still recovering from their rebirth. Instead of outright killing the adventurers, Mordred chose this opportunity to bolster his numbers by converting them into ghouls.

Months passed as the Artificer's Guild searched for Mordred\'s lair and they created a plan to revive Sabine\'s new chosen, the awakened.

Luckily, they had planted 2 of their members amongst the other adventurer\'s captured by Mordred, Corrumpere and Bergs who were able to be located and restored thanks to some preparation by Xerxes. Xerxes sent in a few spies to plant the hex bags and draw the teleportation gates, and which is how the events from earlier began.

Now the awakened must travel through the red door to the Hall of the Raven, equipped with only a few vials to create their own phylactery to contain the Lich\'s soul which is slowly consuming Tiny Rick. Additionally, the awakened must complete the ritual to cleanse all of themselves from Mordred's grasp and finally be whole again.

The party discovered a graveyard at the entrance to the Hall of the Raven, and as such decided to continue through the gate, there they discovered a brazier surrounded by four torches on long poles. Nyx immediately lit the brazier which cause a horde of undead to emerge from their graves as if answering the call.

The party was able to let the gate down and keep the brunt of the undead forces out, however they still managed to stream in through the gatehouse. Quick thinking by Bergs caused one of the four warding torches to be used to light the main force of zombies on fire. Despite seeming like a good idea at the time, it was discovered that the torches were needed in order to keep the onslaught of zombies back.

It was then that the team sprang into action, making quick work of the closest torches, dunking one by one into the fire. Brigit taking on the form of a giant wolf managed to take a bite out of the undead, while Sprocket used his trusty mage hand to retrieve the tossed torch from underneath the writing pile of burning zombies.

The party finished up the fight and Dallor Dallorbill being the upstanding team player he is, proceeded to loot the coins from eyes of each of the zombies.
