\(1\) Travel to the Vagnar Har.

\(2\) Enter the mountain zenith.

\(3\) Find the kiln of the first flame and activate the first forge.

\(4\) Upon activation, an invasion will occur led by his aunt.

\(5\) Use the war forged against the army and defeat them.

\(6\) He will find his aunt at his childhood home hiding, and she will be defenseless and you will have your revenge
