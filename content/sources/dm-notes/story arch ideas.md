notes for overarching story:

The forrest's zenith contains the anchor of death magic to this world, the desert's zenith holds the life magic anchor once both are severed the avatar of the raven queen wil cease to exist, 
sabine's crystaline heart will be removed from the adventurer's and they will no longer be under threat of being spirited away off to V-MORDRED's universe. 

this is the only way to force V-MORDRED back into the realm from which he came. 
The anchors are a pair of swords thrust in the center of each zenith. 

Death's Anchor: 
	is a magic sword which has been thrust in the forest's zenith since time memorium. The sword has long since been turned to obsidian through the test of time, 
	though legend tells of its black steel blade, the Anchor now looks to be made of black glass. 
	
	Whenever you roll a 1-5 on its attack roll, a lingering curse inside it lashes out at you dealing 4D10 force damage to you, you may make a charisma save (DC 15) for half damage. 
	If you spend a long rest with the blade, you may make a charisma check (DC 20). 
	On a failure the curse deals 2D10 force damage to you and until your next long rest the cursed blade gains no bonus to attack or damage rolls. 
	On a success you gain control over the curse until your next long rest where you lose control of it again. 
	While in control of the curse bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to 2+ and the curse can't lash out at you. Also the cursed blade has 10 charges. 
	When you succeed on a attack roll with the cursed blade, you may expend charges to activate the curse on the target, when you do choose a curse effect: 

	Damage curse: 
		deal an extra 2D10 force damage to the target, they may make a charisma save (DC 15) for half damage. Charge cost: 1.
	Weakening curse: 
		the target makes a charisma save (DC 15), on a failure the target becomes poisoned, has 1- on attack and damage rolls, and has 2- to AC for 1 hour. 
		The target may repeat the saving throw each turn, ending the effects on a success unless you spend a charge each turn to prevent them. Charge cost: 2.
