Each Element has its own Fey realm it is closely aligned with, additionally every Dragonborn/Dragon also falls upon this spectrum:

Winter:

- Fey Leader = Grumblesin of the Winter Court

- Dragonborn = White, Blue, Silver

  - Chieftain: Nic

  - Location: Bynar

  - Tumnus Driftwalker

- Element = Water

  - Amulet Holder: Lapis Poseidon, Heir to the Drowned Throne

    - Last seen as a political captive of the Storm King during the last war (started by Reynaldine when he collapsed the Heavens and Hells)

    - In the process of losing their isle, they sunk the Citadel of the Sun, corrupting it and the now drowned Citadel has been named the Sunless Citadel

  - Location: Destroyed Isle, know the location of the Tidepool

  - Elemental Lord: Poseidon

Summer:

- Fey Leader = Juliannia of the Summer Court

- Dragonborn = Red, Gold, Brass

  - Chieftain: Lit

  - Location: Used to be the Citadel of the Sun until it was sunken by the water Genasi in the war that followed Juliannia's death, now this order of Curators are lost though there still might be some wandering the desert wastes with the Autumn Tribe

  - Stalwart Little

- Element = Fire

  - Amulet Holder: Ember Sinder, Heir to the Flaming Throne

    - Daughter of Pyre Sinder, current King of Fire

    - Lives Within the Mountain Beneath the Vag'Mahar

  - Elemental Lord: Sinder

Spring:

- Fey Leader = Evelyandrin of the Spring Court

- Dragonborn = Green, Copper

  - Leader named Eld

  - Location: From the Frozen Forest to the Deserts outside Bael' Vun, they mark the end of Winter and Beginning of Summer in their march, fauna blooming in their wake, spend Autumn and Winter fighting in the Feywilde

  - Fortuna Veridiance

- Element = Earth

  - Amulet Holder: Garnet Golemdrumn, Heir to the Stone Throne, Son of the Late King Quartz

    - Taken from the throne room during his coronation by the sudden appearance of black metal rods

    - Age: The Equivalent of human years 11 -\> 600 years old

  - Elemental Lord: Golemdrumn

  - Location: StoneHearth within Bael' Vun

  - Known Earth Genasi:

    - Dune, Royal Guard and Druid

      - Last seen at Vag'Mahar seeking help from dwarven king

Fall (Autumn):

- Fey Leader = Reynaldine of the Autumn Court

- Dragonborn = Bronze, Black

  - Chieftain: Malik

    - Tiberious D. Rex: the last Malik of Autumn

  - Location: Wandering Deserts of South-Eastern Dawnpass

  - They are under a self inflicted exile after refusing to attack Summer, Tiberious (a young dragonborn at the time) had agreed to merge following Reynaldine and Juliannia's betrothal

  - Tiberious is waiting for a way to redeem Reynaldine and re-unite the Dragonborn to their true cause, preventing Guard' Daemon.

- Element = Wind

  - Amulet Holder: Kelik Temptus, Son of the Wind and Heir to the Storm Throne

  - Elemental Lord: Temptus

  - In the war which granted Autumn and Spring their Equinoxes, the wind genasi and the Dragonborn Curators split their allegiance, each taking a different approach to the war, where the Dragonborn sought peace, the genasi found war.

    - They originally struck a deal with the Fire Genasi to team up on the Water, however after defeating the Poseidons' and destroying their isle, The Water Genasi sunk the Citadel of the Sun and claimed it for their own

    - The Water Genasi Princess Lapis was captured in the assault, her amulet hoarded and kept.
