### Randal and Apotheosis

- Question: What does it take to become a god?

  - Answer: Every diety in the pantheon were the first to uncover a particular universal truth

    - Bahamut: God of Justice and Righteousness. \"True Justice cannot be attained without Righteousness\"

    - Avandra: God of Change, Luck and Travel. \"To travel is to change\"

    - Asmodeus: God of Tyanny and Domination. \"All beings serve.\"

    - Tiamat: Goddess of Greed and Envy. \"Anything may be obtained\"

    - Vecna: God of Death and Secrets. "There is no safer place for secrets than a grave"

    - Raven Queen: Goddess of Fate, Life and Death. "All things serve fate, even death"

  - Once a being has uncovered their truth, they are able to undergo Apotheosis

    - Apotheosis - The process of ascending to godhood, requires some key elements

      - The being must preform a miracle in service of their ideal, the mannor and method determines their domain

        - Example: The Raven Queen and Vecna ascended together, they are considered twin gods for this reason. Vecna was a lich before his ascention, his miracle was the destruction of all living knowledge in his prime plane. However, the Raven Queen's miracle was to determine this fate beforehand and CHANGE it. She did this by creating the Shadowfell for the souls of those whose fate had yet to be finished. And so there is a stalemate, for Vecna seeks to own the secrets of the grave and yet not all who die are sent to oblivion for their fate is not finished.

      - The being must sacrifice their mortal form and assume responsibility of a Petal of the Rose.

        - This is dangerous as it envokes the ire of The Gods of Destruction

        - Each petal has the potential to create infinite more, however each new petal will be a shadow of the original, this is why Vodram and Mardov are twin worlds. Their gods were from the same prime plane of existence. The Gods of Creation who created the Rose and the Curators who cultivate its secrets were the original by which all other planes spawned. The original split was between the Gods of Creation and the Gods of Destruction on the plane know as Prime.

- Moments:

  - Reynaldine of the Autumn Court's plan to assume control of Mardov.

    - Dismayed at the state of his home petal, Reynaldine seeks to right the wrongs left behind by the Dead God who created him. He wants to collapse all pocket planes (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Material, Feywilde, Shadowfell, 7 Heavens, 9 Hells) within his domain so he gains absolute power over them all. This will be his miracle. His ideal is "Fate is the death of freedom" His domain will be Knowledge and Trickery. Once all planes are collapsed he seeks to segment everything into a Matrix like paradise of their own.

  - Pacificism is a goal not a lifestyle

    - Randal's decision to become a pacifist is a valiant effort but is ultimately flawed for he claimed victory while he still has goals outstanding.

      - His ideal at the moment is "Victory is only satisfying if achieved without violence"

    - Need foils to bring this theme out:

      - "Violence is Victory" - Logath, Orcish Leader and Ex-Gladiator

      - "Peace is what I will achieve after all my enemies have been defeated by my hand" - Eveylandrin of the Spring Court

      - "Some things are worth fighting for" - Reynaldine

      - "Nothing is worth fighting for, I will endure and adapt" - Grumblesin of the Winter Court

### Sprocket and The Pursuit of Fun

- Question: Is it possible for responsibility to be fun?

- Sprocket's arc so far:

  - Sprocket was a Silver Seated Diviner who was allowed to leave the Magister's Circle at Lillith's command to study a village who's inhabitants are stuck in an endless cycle of life and death.

  - He studied within his yurt nestled in the bluffs outside *Insert Village Name Here* for the better part of 5 years, however his discovery of a portal to the Shadowfell forced him out of his semi-retirement

  - This discovery led Lillith to require the diviner to head to Argos, chasing the only magical signiture similar to that of the village, there he was bewitched and forced to join the Scarlet Tunic Brigade

  - WIthout understanding his quest, he stumbled upon the ruins of old Argos, the location of the artifact tied to the Shadowfell... Sabine Ra of Argos, Demi-lich.

  - There Sprocket died. However he was risen with a shard of the Raven Queen's divine essence inside him and with this he became Fated. Sprocket's death is unique as he died in an attempt to touch The Avatar of the Raven Queen using his magehand and paid the price. This set him on a path which fated him to become the next Avatar of the Raven Queen.

  - Sprockets Fate, along with the others in the room is to protect this petal from the Gods of Destruction. We have met one other mortal who has attempted this: Eon Everstar, The Allabaster Seat. He has withered under the weight of undertaking this task on himself.

- Moments and Themes:

  - Anonimnity

    - Sprocket thrives in an enviornement where he is under the radar, he purposely seeks out opportunities to be under-estimated and lives for moments where he can catch an opponent offguard

      - The purpose of the members of the Thornberry Shade and Trixie in particular is to show that the deeds that he deems "fun" provide him notoriety. With nortoriety, it becomes more and more difficult for Sprocket to break through the expectations set in front of him.

      - Rise of infamy Trixie -\> Thronberry Shade -\> Qual'Xar -\> Magister's Circle && Conquis Abbey -\> Entire Material Plane

  - Expectations

    - The fate of Sprocket as the next Avatar of the Diety of this plane is something that cannot be downplayed. No matter how Sproket tries, this will be the overarching conflict of the character.

    - "You cannot run from your fate"

      - To learn this is to understand what it means to become the next Raven Queen.

#### Brigit and Self-Discovery

- Question: "What makes me special?"

- Brigit's Arc so far:

  - Brigit was born to two human parents who were killed because of something innate about her connected her with nature around here.

  - Despite the fact that her village was nestled in Eavallan Forest the townsfolk feared her based on some superstition that was not shared with her

  - She took refuge in the Forest, creating a den for herself where she could gain access to the Villages of Man and Orc as well as the safety of the Forest.

  - While stealing from Faeish (orc village) she met a Shaman who took a liking to her, granting her the last time of Wildfire.

  - While running errands with her suprising new friend, she stumbled upon the oldest civilization on Mardov, Eavallan. There she learned that there were a race of elves who are waiting for the return of the Progenitors who need to be reborn. However that means the child inhabitants of Eavallan are never allowed to leave or grow up until their return.

  - She took a mushroom from the gigantic Redwood Tree with Golden Leaves at the center of town and gifted it to Lorgrammar.

  - Lorgrammar's tribe found the village as a direct result of Brigit's actions and destroyed the village, slaughtering the adults and binding the children in manacles of iron

  - The children eventually returned, malnourished and beaten, the adults too, only the mushroom circles that had galvanized Lorgrammar and Brigit's friendship refused to return. They were banished from the forest after that, the elves within had begun to spread and conver more territory.

  - The human village was burned shortly after the two of them settled outside its walls

  - Brigit headed to Argos to try and find a woods witch name Mia who was of great interest to the Earth Mother worshippng Lorgrammar, however, when she arrived her memories of the Argosian woods were erased and she joined the Scarlet Tunic Brigade.

  - While combatting Mordred, the lich who had sought to revive Sabine Ra of Argos and Avatar of the Raven Queen, Brigit came into contact with the Heart of the Wilde as it had been stolen from the Forest's Zenith. She had a unique connection with this gem of unimaginable power.

  - The Heart was placed in The Scarlet Keep until it was stolen shortly after Brigit and the others left in an attempt to meet with the Diviner's of the Magister's Circle to help decipher a tripplet of prophecies about Guard'Daemon, the last of which comming from Mia herself.

  - Once there, Brigit was ushered to meet Eon Everstart, Alabaster Seat and being responsible for protecting this petal from attacks from without. When parting ways, Eon placed the party in the presence of a being that would help them: The Stag Zodiac: Shi-Shi-Gami.

  - Brigit was chosen by the Stag, a process for a Zodiac that ties the nature spirit to the soul of the chosen, and oppening the imortal being up to being killed.
