The Collector:

- Paints portraits of those who wrong him, the portraits portray the manner in which justice was served to the figure

- A gnome who is on a rack to make him taller

- A half-elf whose ears were cropped and his lower half removed and replaced with an elf's legs

- A skinned Tabaxi

- A half-orc with his tusks clipped and a human with great tusks sewn to his mouth

The Builder:

- Working towards building a tower on the corpses of innocents

- Masquerades as an undertaker, never actually buries his dead and instead feeds them to the pit inside his hidden mage's tower

- Secretly funding a war, in order to gain more bodies

- The more powerful the life and more brutal the death the greater potential energy to harness

- Probably the king of Machu, madness plagues him

- Building a tower to the floating Forest Zenith
