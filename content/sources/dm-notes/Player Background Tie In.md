1.  Sprocket

    a.  Has a yurt outside Hinderstap village in Monethern.

    b.  Sent there by Lillith, the Platinum Seat of the Diviners Covenant to investigate mysterious city filled with normal people in the day who turn ravenous and murderous in the night

    c.  Being killed in the village forces the murdered to stay there permanently, they are reborn in the morning (this happened to a man whom Sprocket stole a pocketwatch from and told him to get it back in the morning, when he didnt show up he found the man pacing at the village border)

        i.  Man was named Giddean Clary, Mustard Bard of College of Lore

        ii. He had a Basset Hound named Darling

    d.  There is a portal, some kind of wound there, that links Hinderstap to the Shadowfell.

2.  Brigit

    a.  Brigit's background grants her expertise in sneaking and stealing when in wolf form. Particularly when it pertains to her in a forest.

    b.  She has a criminal contact Firekeeper Lorgrammar, an older orc shaman who's people betrayed him and assulted Eavallan Forest, Stra' Franar, and last Tower Bridge looking for something in the forest...

    c.  Brigit has a lore heavy backstory that explains that she is a progenitor. One of the original beings created in Eden, this grants her the ability to open the Harvest Zenith without destroying the elemental seals.

    d.  Eavallan Forest is of the Faewild. It is the earliest location where elves first came to Mardov. They are keepers of the forest, though they have lost their way and are no longer as connected to the progenitors.

    e.  Brigit is one, there is no reason for this other than they were fated to return to help stop Guard' Daemon. The original sacking of Eavallan by the orcs set into motion the steps to open the Harvest Zenith.

    f.  Progenitors blood was originally thought be in Uma, though it seems that the bloodline of old Uma was still strong, and Brigit's parents were drawn to Eavallan because it was the closest to the Zenith.
