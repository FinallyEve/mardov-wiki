Its is a handscanner, each level in the dungeon requires a higher level minions hand

There is a collection bowl with a hole that leads down to a sacrificial basin, the bad guy brings a pet with him that eats the sacrifice, if the party decides to use their own blood to open the door, then the monstrous pet will attempt to eat the sacrifice.
