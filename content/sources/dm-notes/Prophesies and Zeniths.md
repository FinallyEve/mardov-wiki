Prophesies and Zeniths (as of Chapter 26)

We took the prophesies, with Ruth and Sheila, to a magic giant elk, and used his antlers to answer some questions about them. Every time we got something right, the papers or words would light up.

[First Prophecy]{.underline}:

For the first prophecy, four tarot cards were drawn: (1) A tiger, signifying the wind, (2) A forge, signifying creation, (3) A holy knight, signifying faith and (4) A family (signifying progenitors).

The divination began and this was heard from Ruth in Argos:

"The anchors fall, the realms sunder

Hearts of stone gathered from plunder

Death incarnate knocks at the door

Below the blood star begins the final war"

- The anchors fall refers to something that is with each of the Zeniths and as they fall, the realms will fall into chaos.

- Hearts of Stone: We've seen a heart of stone, the Heart of the Wilde, plus the other slots in the tower of the Red Tunic Brigade. We also know from Tiberious that this stone has been stolen and "gathered from plunder"

- The Blood Star: One was seen before when Tygras came to power. It's a star that'll turn red and literally fall. Meteorite (?)

- The Final War: refers to Gard'damon (Anagram for Armeggedon)

[Second Prophecy]{.underline}:

This was heard from the hags:

"Double bubble toil and trouble,

Gard'damon is neigh, the first zenith is rubble,

Prepare the Pestilence with children's souls,

The husks remain until they're told.

Double bubble toil and trouble,

Anchors lifted and hearts are smuggled."

- The first zenith: Always refers to the Forest Zenith. We're not quite sure how its rubble.

- Pestilence: What happened to it? We tried to get rid of it but couldn't, so we demolished a building on top of it. We've also seen it in other places.

- The husks: The bodies of the children who's souls were taken.

- Anchors: being lifted from the Zeniths

- Hearts are Smuggled: We know of a smuggler in Argos: Lord Jerefor.

[Third Prophecy]{.underline}:

This was heard from Mia after we rescued her.

"As the rain falls on the fallow harvest, and the maturin pact fulfilled,

The Anchor of Famine shall be lifted, the echo of TYGRAS stilled.

As the snow drifts over the ruined tribe, and serpent plagues the sea

The Anchor of Pestilence shall awake, to answer the curators' plea

As the fire bellows on the forgotten forge, and the blood star is found

The Anchor of War shall be silenced, as the new paragon is crowned.

As the sunlight warms the forest gate, and madness prisoner freed

The Anchor of Death shall sway, to bring peace to star-crossed three."

- The four horseman: Famine, Pestilence, War and Death are universally known as bringers of the apocalypse, of Armageddon.

- Locations: the four verses refer to four points on a map

- The map we saw on the Thornberry Shade bubbled up in five places:(1) Var'taos ruins, also known as The Valley of Anchors, the origin of magic, (2) Vagna Har - The dwarven city, (3) Macchu, (4) Bynar, (5) Tygras Logra - This point additionally had a burning ember on its location.

  - In order, the verses refer to: (1) Tygras Logar, (2) Bynar, (3) Vagna Har and (4) Macchu.

  - Currently we know that Macchu is the "Forest Zenith" and Vagna Har is the "Mountain Zenith". The other two are still a mystery/we haven't heard about them as much. I would assume that Bynar is the Sea/Ocean Zenith, and then I don't know about Tygras Logar.

  - From what Manitou told us there is a zodiac that realms over the desert lands, could this be another zenith? Maybe Tygras Logar\....

  - From the Tomb of the Last Curator, we know that Bynar is the "Winter Zenith" and Tygras Logar is the "Harvest Zenith"

- The Valley of Anchors: Absolutely has something to do with these Anchors associated with each Zenith. We should travel here to gain more information.

- Matrim Pact: Gnomes, Humans and Half-elfs created a utopia on the back of the turtle Mia. We know of a qualashahar that has more information about this.

- Tygras: Created Tieflings, Blew Up Uma with a superweapon, Reason for Covenants of Magic so that no being save for the Alabaster Seat, may posses that amount of power again, superweapon along with Tygras was thwarted by Matruin Pact which caused all of the surrounding area to explode. The last threat to this universe.

- Serpent Sea: Literally the north sea. And it has a serpent. We know of someone, Dilbert, who has fought the serpent and collected the scales, Liza was swallowed by the serpent.

- Ruined Tribe: We know that there is a ruined tribe in Bynar (Dragon-kin history check)

- Winter's Curators: We know that the dragon kin tribe in Bynar is known as these.

- Forgotten Forge (aka First Forge): A forge in dwarven legend. It has to be lit once again. This falls into place with a few of our backstories and answers about our futures.

- Blood Star is found: Found does not mean rise.

- Paragon: The Bronze beards, the rulers of Vagna Har.

- Forest and Sunlight: Finkle will have more information about this.

- Madness Prisoner: Mordred/Ramses and his phylactery.

- Star-crossed three: Sabine, Mordred/Ramses and Xeres. Once we bring their souls/phylacteries to the forest, and complete the trial of the zenith, we will bring peace to them.

[Tomb of the Last Curator Zenith rooms:]{.underline}

In the Tomb of the Last Curator, we came across four rooms that contained hieroglyphics describing the four Zeniths, who is associated with them, things that have happened, things that are happening and things that have yet to come. Here's what we know\...

The Forest Room Hieroglyphics - The Anchor of Death in Macchu and associated with the Green Eye of Veccna.

- A seven point crown made of sludge, dripping to the floor and surrounding the man wearing it, creating a small island where the throne stands.

- A triad of people in ancient garb, one female with raven-black hair and two men who look similar - one with black hair unkempt and curly, the other with long white hair pulled back.

- A large antlered beast with a glow-stone in the center of its head.

- An open doorway bathed in moonlight, and another picture that is the same building but without the doorway and the moon.

- Two crystalline shapes on either side of a pool of water, one beneath the water and one above.

- A Lich, reaching for a doe.

- A great elk with a locket suspended between its antlers.

- A rabbit with its head buried in a book, holding a phylactery in one hand and a scythe off to the side in another.

- The doe and the elk stand side by side above a forest of mangrove trees.

In addition to the hieroglyphics, there is a riddle that we have to solve to leave the room:

"I am the fate of all things, large and small

Many run from me, though I eventually find them all.

Who am I?"

Answer: Death

The Mountain Room Hieroglyphics - The Anchor of War in Vagna Har and associated with the Red Eye of Veccna.

- A city with three massive walls, the walls are carved in the pattern of some kind of mechanical dwarves, and the largest has a hole in his heart.

- A dwarf with red hair, older, and missing his right eye.

- A crest of some kind with a hammer and an avil.

- A large bat, hanging on a stalagmite. Below the bat is a wretched looking woman drinking whatever is dripping off this bat. (Blood? Vampire bat?)

- A wolf and a wolverine, both are standing beside a frozen volcano.

- A massive forged worked by a glowing red eyed giant, transparent but with a smaller gaunt old dwarf inside, seemingly unconscious.

- A shadowy figure on the wall behind a man working on a much more modest forge.

- An image of a tentacle rising from a chasm.

- All of the above sits above two massive clashing armies.

- The symbol on the Cthulu's head is on the wall between the districts of the bronze beards and the other beards that make up the Vagna Har. The symbol is of the first bronze beard, who originally united the clans.

In addition to the hieroglyphics, there is a riddle that we have to solve to leave the room:

"Children and rich men play me like a game,

But poor men fear even the whisper of my name.

What am I?"

Answer: War

The Winter Room Hieroglyphics - The Anchor of Pestilence in Bynar and associated with the Blue Eye of Veccna.

- A serpent with an angler fish lure (A huge goddamn serpent, big enough to swallow at least 10 people at once)

- A tribe of snow-colored dragon-born, surrounding a great rock of some sort.

- A large turtle with a pan flute in the pattern on his back, the turtle's head and legs are tucked within his shell while dormant.

- A great white dragon clutching a blue gemstone.

- A dark shadow lurking behind all the images of townsfolk in the corners of the painting.

- A shield with an inlay for a large gemstone, but it is vacant. And wielded by a mongoose.

- A grand citadel in the desert, sunlight shines on all the surrounding land, but a cloud hangs over the building in the shape of a cobra.

- A frozen fortress guarded by giants wearing a five dragonhead tabbard.

In addition to the hieroglyphics, there is a riddle that we have to solve to leave the room:

"I ride on the wind and roll like a wave,

Those who know me have one foot in the grave.

What am I?"

Answer: Pestilence

The Harvest Room Hieroglyphics - The Anchor of Famine in Tygras Logar and associated with the Orange Eye of Veccna.

- An upside down city, its gardens glowing faintly.

- A snow-white tiefling girl with two too tall shadows looming behind. Both shadows have great big owl wings. (Nyx's sister?)

- A classic red tiefling is shaking the hand of the image of a man in a hat and a cane. The cane touches the ground, but his feet do not. (Red tiefling - Tygras?)

- An image of a fox with silver eyes, wings folded from its back ethereal and they flow backwards forming the tail of the fox.

- A large building with several suspended orbs revolving around the three larger towers. The building is cracked, but still completely functional\...barely. It looks ancient and brand new at the same time.

- There sits a woman wearing a headdress on a horse before an army, she does not wield a sword. Across from her is an orc male, pointing at her.

- Three mice around a flaming mousetrap - one is safe and two are burning.

- A well overflowing with water, a head-gem, a diadem, sets at the water's origin just below the surface.

- A burned field, patrolled by nightmarish scarecrows and obsidian guards. In the distance there is thunderclouds.

In addition to the hieroglyphics, there is a riddle that we have to solve to leave the room:

"You can read me between the line on many,

Though to find me yourself, you must wade through nothing a plenty.

What am I?"

Answer: Famine
