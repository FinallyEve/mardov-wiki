What was lost shall now be found,

Entombed in rock which once grew from the ground.

A gift handed down through hands of four,

Only to be returned to he who lost it before.

The power to break the mountains spine,

Frozen in the forest for all to find

The mantle of power thou shall that bear,

The burden of war can still be prepared
