On the Subject of Magic:

	- Is some magic common? 
		- magic is governed by the Watchful Order (a guild of magicians who govern, teach, and occasionally put down forbidden forms of magic)
			- common magic: standard elemental magic (fireball, illusions), standard cleric miracles (healing the sick)
			- forbidden magic: necromancy, animancy, anything that breaks the universal laws of the natural world
	- Forbidden magic is anything that follows the teachings of the follow dieties: 
		- Asmodeus (tyranny, mindcontorl magic/charm)
		- Gruumsh (distruction, Chaos)
		- Raven Queen (death, necromancy)
		- Vecna (evil secrets, amnimancy)
	- Magic items are often thought of as monkey's paw's, in the laws of the universe nothing can be gained for free
		- bag of holding? some small percentage of items get lost in the aether between uses of the bag
		- belt of giant strength? causes the user disadvantage in charm spells (giants are dumb)
	- creatures wielding magic or creatures created of magic are managed by the Watchful Order and determined to be destroyed if evil
		- most dark magician creatures stay hidden or are powerful enough to rival a petty band of Watchful Initates
		- dark magic objects are worshipped by cults and mistaken for dieties
