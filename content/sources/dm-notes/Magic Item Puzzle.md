title: "Magic Item Puzzle"
source: ["NFS/Ideas/Magic Item Puzzle.docx"]
category: idea
tags: [idea]
---

Give the party over 5 items that are magic and 10 minutes of time before the bad guys break down the door and come for them. The 10 min allows one cast of identify, or the spellcaster can spend extra spell slots to identify the items.
