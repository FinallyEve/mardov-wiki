Dearest Daina, 

I am writing ya this note to warn ya of a danger that I was unable to handle
on me own. Me lifes greatest fear was that you would be thrown into this mess
because of my foolish actions, and for that I am deeply sorry. 

I'll start with the most important things first. Yer mother was a wonderful
woman who in one of lifes cruelest twist, you'll never be able to meet.
Ye deserve to know the name she chose for ya... Diesa. 

I know I never liked to mention her, but Hlin was a fierce warrior, best
fighter the I've ever had the pleasure to see in the arena! There's so much
about her that ye don't know and I don't think I'll ever have the courage to
tell ye in person. This axe was hers and she treated it so well, I always knew
she would make a great mother.... 

I had 12 hours with her before she died giving birth to you and your older
brother while we fled from Vag'Mahar. In that time, she named ya after her
grandmother and made me swear to give you 'er axe... and never tell ya of
yer birthright. 

It seems that I was only able to keep one of those promises Diesa. I only have
you left in the world and it only seems right to tell you.... 

Did ye ever wonder why all those days I spent in me workshop, I never once
asked for yer help? I love ya, Diesa. And it would have meant so much more
than you know to pass me craft onto me daughter. But our blood is dangerous,
and without understanding what it means to be an Fireforge, you could put
yerself in danger... 

Me name is not Tor Ironforge, no, it's Thorkon Fireforge and I am a FORGESWORN.
That sigil yer mother left you, that is our house's brand. Yer a desendant of
the 12 Maghar, and our burden is the creation of weapons... 

Our kind were hunted to the brink of extinction, forced to create great
marvels for Kings of all nations. Some of the greatest artifacts in history
bear yer birthright! But a true FORGESWORN understands the cost of their
craft... A bit of yerself is imparted in each of yer creations.

There is nothing worth more than yer soul, Diesa. NOTHING. Never forget
that...

And now it's time to learn of yer father's sins, me hope is that they
never fall upon yer shoulders and ye never need to find this note... but...

I am a criminal. Make no mistake Diesa, yer pa is not a good man. I've worked
to attone for what I've done but that cannot make up for the damage I've
caused. 

I was once the royal weaponsmith for THORSON BRONZEBEARD II, the last great
benevolent leader of the Vag'Mahar, but BRONZEBEARD had an obsession with the
first of his lineage, THORIN THE BRONZE, and his WARFORGED. It was then that
I was approached by GHOST-EYE THORRIN, a chief adviser to the current REDBEARD
THANE. I was convinced to betray the King, I refused at first, but their
arguement was convincing.

See THORSON believed that there was a war coming, and he aimed to create more
Warforged, but I learned a terrible secret. One that I had shared with the
Council.... The Warforged CONSUMES Dwarven souls when created. Each
machination a melding of a sacrafice and a great portion of the creator. And
the only ones capable of creating them... were the FORGESWORN. 

And so with Hilns encouragement, I agreed to create a soul-forged dagger, one
that was cursed and gave it to THORSON as a gift. It was pure treason. But one
whose price was supposed to ensure the safety and freedom of the FORGESWORN.
But I was betrayed, The Ghost Eye used the information I'd presented and
attempted to round up all of the FORGESWORN. Luckily, we were able to escape
before they got to us, but some were not so lucky. 

I cant detail everything in this note, but if your reading this message, then
I am dead. There is one ye can trust in the capital, find DECAN SMITHRUNNER.
One thing I can be sure of is that ol' Decan will be at his shop, the DRAGONS
CARAPACE. Show him yer mothers seal. He'll tell ya the whole story and might
be able to show you yer mothers old arena, she'd have wanted to show ye that. 

At the arena, find yer mothers statue. You'll know what to do lass. 
I love you Diesa Fireforge, watching you grow up has been the happiest time of
my life. 

Love, 
Thorkon Fireforge, Forgesworn Weaponsmith
