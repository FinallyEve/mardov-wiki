My beloved Thorkon,

It is so cold here. An icy wind howls and all I can feel is loss. A loss for Barnabus. For Diesa who will have to stomach your endless nights at the forge alone. For you, my husband who was framed for the crime of good craftsmanship and lost more than half his family as a prize, I weep.

The Raven Queen of Fate is anything but kind. Yet, still I find myself trusting her. You remember the wolverine I fought in the colosseum? It seems it has a sibling on the Zodiac pantheon, the wolf.

It's name is Fenrir and his kindness alone is why I am able to write you this note despite this mortal wound. I don't have much time. I've slain your pursuers thanks to the pack, but I have spent too long outside the arena. Some Maghar of the Hammer I turned out to be.

Your Mag'Har craftsmanship created one of the 4 anchors of our world. Nothing can take that away from you. You may be creating this compartment even now. For all the grief, honing your abilities in spite of all the pain IS worth it.

Thank you.

It is the instrument of War. Once used to preserve peace, it can raise mountains and boil oceans. That is your legacy. But it is not the greatest.

Our children are alright. I have made a bargain with the Zodiac. It seems our son has already gained the wolfs favor but I have had a chance to speak with the Wolverine. Our daughter will be tested and will earn the wolverine's favor. Raise her right as I know you will and it is all but guaranteed.

In return I must guard the Anchor in Fenrir's form as it needs to recover. I will watch over our son. I hope he has the opportunity to meet you someday soon. If you're reading this note, I will watch over him off the trail where we fell. Both will be safe.

Murder Ghosteye for me. Be well. You are my Paragon.
