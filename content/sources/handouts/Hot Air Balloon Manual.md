CHIEF GRINGLESPROCKET's MAGNIFICENT DESCENTION AND ASCENTION MACHINE!

WELCOME USER TO THE WORLDS FIRST AERONAUTICAL ELEVATION SYSTEM!

HOW TO ASCEND: 
	1) FIND THE NEAREST, DUAL CHAMBERED DEFLATED VESSEL AND OPEN THE SEPARATION VALVE (DC strength 16)
	2) LIGHT A FIRE IN THE FORGE-ENGINE, THIS VESSEL REQUIRES 120 CUBIC STONES OF AIR, EACH 10 CUBES OF AIR MUST BE 10 DEGREES WARMER THAN THE LAST
	3) WHEN THE AIR IS THE RIGHT TEMPERATURE, CLOSE THE SEPATATION VALVE AND PRESS THE RELEASE TO TRANSFER THE WARMER AIR
	4) ENJOY YOUR ASCENTION

HOW TO DESCEND:
	1) FIND THE NEAREST, DUAL CHAMBERED INFLATED VESSEL AND CLOSE THE SEPARATION VALVE
	2) GENLTY DRIP WATER OVER THE FLAME, THIS VESSEL REQUIRES 120 CUBIC STONES OF AIR, EACH 10 CUBES OF AIR MUST BE 10 DEGREES COOLER THAN THE LAST
	3) WHEN THE AIR IS THE RIGHT TEMPERATURE, OPEN THE SEPARATION VALVE AND PRESS THE RELEASE TO DISPENSE 10 STONES OF AIR
	4) CLOSE THE VALVE AND REPEAT
	5) AVOID PUTTING THE FIRE OUT BEFORE REACHING THE BOTTOM, A FULL SYSTEM SHUTDOWN WILL OCCUR
	6) DESCEND SAFELY!






*notes in dwarvish* 
- water descent average time, 3damage per 6s, so -12+5=7+3 = 4 turns * 12 = 48 rounds * 6s = 4.8min
- possible to keep the noise down if you brace the edges of the basket from swinging and hitting the side

*notes in gnomish*
- there has been reports of a colony of great cave spider and something more ... powerful leading them inside the balloon shaft
- need to keep the basket quiet when near the 50 Stone height (DC 26 atheltics/stealth) the major nest is there, they can only chase you if you are descending, possible to ascend too quickly
- if you alert them by going down too quickly after going up too noisily the spiders are likely to use webbing to try and slow down the basket.
- Each 10-foot cube of giant webs has AC 10, 15 hit points, vulnerability to fire, and immunity to bludgeoning, piercing, and psychic damage.
- cave spider blood is alcoholic, quick 9 health added to fire if you add cavespider blood, if you hit them with a javelin in the center (+24 to hit with javilin) they will die immediately, tie a bucket to ceiling to collect blood
