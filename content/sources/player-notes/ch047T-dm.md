# Chapter 47T: Chapter 47T - Dungeon Master Lead In

*Notes by DM*

Chapter 47T - Dungeon Master Lead In

Last we saw our heroes:

After intercepting a shipment of children out of Windhaven, the party decided to turn Reela, Rory and Squat over to the authorities to be returned to the orphanage they were taken from. Randal said farewell to the Farmer's Daughters and decided to use his commision with them to make sure the children made it home safe and sound and to describe the situation to the town guard.

Nyx rejoined the party, hot on the heels of a somber, yet insightful, talk with Leonidas of the Summer's Last Light where she learned a bit more about this ill fated and mysterious *Juilannia of the Summer Court*. Once together, the party decided to accompany Kelik towards the entrance to Cloud City also known as the New Eye of the ever-present storm surrounding Windhaven\... after all there were bigger fish to fry than orphan-nappers.

Fen, sure that their destination was a trap, grilled Kelik for more information about the circumstances of his need to return home and the various scrys that were discovered to be up him. The party learned that Kelik's father and him are essentially the same, however Kelik was segmented off from the Storm King, gifted free will by the Amulet around his neck. After Brigit touched the cool metal, she suddenly transformed into an air elemental, earning a "So what are you exactly?" from the air genasi prince\... Brigit recieved no answers as to why this power was bestowed upon her, however it seems to come as naturally as her other wildshape forms.

Using the newfound knowledge of Kelik's link to his "father", Nyx scryed the Storm King to find that he IS the ever-present storm encircling Windhaven! However after some time, she was able to find his ... more corporeal form inside his throne room, seemingly alone, save for the imprisoned blue-skinned woman inside a gemstone suspended at eye level to the giant despot. Further investigation led to the party learning that this was Lapis, the Water Genasi Princess and counterpart to Kelik. Nyx was unable to determine if she still possessed her amulet, the party presses on with a bit more fire in their step.

Sheltered from the storm by Kelik and Wind-Brigit, the party made their way out onto the fields of Windhaven despite warnings of a thunderstorm rolling in. Things went rather smoothly at the beginning of their journey, that is until Fen noticed a large shape in the distance, there is a flash of yellow light and the far-too-big-to-be-a-bird shape dive-bombed something perhaps 3 miles ahead on their path.

Sprocket and Stalwart decided to team up while keeping and eye out for strange things on their path and came across a Mammoth print with a massive feather in it. The feather was easily the size of Sprockets arm and covered in a strange black ichor that reacts to their presence. The duo pocket a vial of the strange ooze after watching it eat the metal of the knife used to remove it from the feather.

The party then came across the scene of a great battle between orcs and humans, however NONE of the bodies were wearing armor. Suspicious that there might be more of this ooze and pressed for time, Stalwart decides to grab a body at random and rejoin the group instead of picking through the remains. Carrying what he now knows is only the upper torso of an orc, Stalwart finds himself attacked by a fully realized black pudding only successfully escaping after it damages his armor.

All the commotion of dealing with the slime seemingly caught the attention of a roc flying overhead, the party presses on at a slower and more cautious rate. Again Fen Darkeyes spots a flash of light high in the storm ahead, however this time it was a cool blue lightning and a distinctly larger shape producing it. The lightning felled the roc following the party, however it is unknown if this is a good or bad thing.

Back on track, the party comes to a calm grassy knoll, which is strange as its location is still within the Storm King's Maelstrom. However it becomes evidence shortly that this location is under the effect of a dapper dressed slender man with a shifting autumn skin-tone, *Reynaldine of the Autumn Court*. Reynaldine greets the party by commanding Kelik to turn over his amulet, however Kelik manages to resist... barely. Reynaldine conscripts the help of the party in acquiring the Amulet of Poesidon, which he believes to be with Lapis in Cloud City, Sprocket attempted to dodge the deal dimension doored to above the storm coming face to face with the larger shape spotted by Fen... Rimefang, the Blue Dracolich.

The resulting "fight" was over within 10 seconds, the conclusion being a second suggestion from Reynaldine which seems to bypass the free will of its listeners causing them to "Why don't you put your weapons down and we can talk this through in a civilized manner." The overwhelming force of the enchantment is enough for even those who managed to resist to take it seriously. Except for Brigit who decided to try and take the skeletal dragon out of the fight by turning him into a bunny.. She fails and Rimefang looks hungry... the shifts into a stag in response to her fight or flight reflex to discover that her stag form is ... altered. Her usual brown stag, replaced with a larger albino version with one antler... and the locket proudly displayed around its neck.

Nyx, armed with the information she gained from Leonidas, decides to take a leap of faith and trust her patron, retrieving the amulet from Kelik who in turn is trusting her. Reynaldine and Rimefang take their leave ordering the party to retrieve the Posiedon Amulet for him. He brandished the Earth Amulet and Kelik's Temptus Amulet before leaving getting the last word...

Beaten, the party rests inside a tiny hut, the storm surrounding them...
