# Chapter 45T: Chapter 45T - Dungeon Master Lead In

*Notes by DM*

Chapter 45T - Dungeon Master Lead In

Last we saw our heroes, they had split up by the sudden arrival of the very strange custodian of WindHaven, Leonidas of Summer's Last Light with a very mysterious sense of punishment. With a great and terrible roar, the majority of this half of the former Scarlet Tunic Brigade and Bartim's Berzerkers \<Please for the love decide on a name for the party\> are transported to the Sphinix's domain. The rest were not near the others when they were apprehended, to them, the others disappeared, absorbed into the great sapphire necklace that adorned its mane. Those judged were forced to give justification for "committing crimes in WindHaven" their worthiness awarded afterwards along with their vindication. Those worthy were allotted a boon for completing the trials offered upon entering the Sphinx's domain. Those unworthy would have their worth re-evaluated and they are offered a boon upon a worthy conclusion. It followed that judged each in turn: Stalwart "worthy", Nyx "worthy", Varys "worthy", Tiberious "worthy", Sprocket "unworthy" and Fen initially "unworthy" though his opinion was changed later in the trials. Brigitte, Randal, Tesca and Kelik were left to wander the city for a day and an hour until the others appeared unchanged with a distant look in their eyes...

The first trial was a roar from Leonidas which was meant to strike fear in the hearts of all who hear, but Stalwart's courage guided the group through their fear and allowed everyone to pass the first trial without fail.

The second trial is effectively a 220 ft dash to cross a finish line which acts more like a timeline. However across the field were the Spectators, a series of beholder-esque entities which surrounded the first out the gate; Varys quickly hasted 100ft of the way before being faced with those he's deemed hes betrayed: His father, Mac, Clicker among the standout. Each ghost of his past rendered the elf more paralyzed. Seeing this, Fen decides to wish himself to the end of the end of the first trial, his request granted at the cost of 14 years of his life, no further affirmation needed Fen is then vanished from sight. Nyx grabs Tiberious and dimension doors them to safety quickly, Sprocket quickly follows the same strategy although none were small enough to take the same doorway. Stalwart, in a blaze of hasted speed charges forward grabbing Varys and deposits him on the other side of the timeline. The cost of his heroism seemingly another 18 year from his life as well as he his blasted just before crossing the line himself.

The first to arrive in the "reflection room" was Fen as his wish aged him 18 years, his punishment the requirement to spend out the total of his sentence by 18 years. Over the course of his time, he experimented with the veil of falling sand behind him and lost a finger as he attempted to touch it. The finger was disintegrated on touch. With nowhere to explore but ahead, Fen took a step on the grassy knoll which quickly faded to sand falling into an abyss below. Each step forward met with an island of sand, some larger than others. The larger islands contained a regret from the past to present to the future, Fen explored one of each. The past was the moment in time in which an argument with his brother led him to join the cult of the abyss. When he first explored the isle of the past, he experienced the event exactly as it had occurred, unable to change the past. It took a second visit, 5 years later, to determine that he would never be able to stop his brother from joining and you with him, mostly out of guilt. He does it a third time just to be sure, clearly affected by this cornerstone of his past. 14 years later the others join him in turn, that is except Stalwart who vanished upon saving Varys from the Spectators. Each of the 5: Fen, Nyx, Sprocket, Tiberious, Varys had to take a step into the grass and face their regrets. Guided by Fen's 3 previous attempts, Sprocket convinces Nyx to banish him, granting him freedom! He heads to the bar to grab a pint and wait for it all to blow over, free of guilt from his crime of attempting to enchant someone and remove their free will. Varys decides to skip the isle of the past and head to the isle of the present: he is faced with the death of Daina as she grabs a large ruby and jumps into a pool of lava, sinking deep below the molten earth. Fen decided to skip every isle, partly out of fear of reliving his biggest regret a fourth time. Instead he decides to walk as far and as long as it took, nothing would deter him. Tiberious as if by reflex, set his shoulders, adopted a grim frown and took a step into the grass, determined. That left Nyx who was flying on her broom searching the seemingly empty demi-plane. She sought help on other planes, madness waited for her. As she hit the sand, she was greeted by a woman with long blonde hair and warm colored leaves all over her body. The vision was brief and unsure if it was onset by madness or time, Nyx could swear she hear this summery woman ask, "Have you been enjoying my power?" Stalwart, spared the reflection hallway for his heroism in the previous 2 trials, arrived in the blink of an eye to find Fen aged 14 years and others (minus Sprocket) with a distant look in their eye, perhaps most of which was Fen who looked as if he'd been walking constantly for 18 years.

Before proceeding to the Third Trial, the remaining members were granted the opportunity to ask a boon or attempt the next trial and increase the power of their wish. Nyx, Stalwart and Varys opt out now. Nyx wished for "whatever aid to save her sister". Varys wished for "whatever aid to stop the 6 fingered necromancer" Stalwart seeing he had been aged 14 years in the first trial decided it was enough to put this all behind him and go back to how he was before the trials. His wish was granted quickly. The lot of them head to the bar to grab a pint and wait for this all to blow over.

Tiberious and Fen remained for the final trial. Tiberious revealed he has performed this trial 79 times, each time giving a year of his natural lifespan in exchange for being de-aged to when he first undertook the trials at 30. That would put him at possibly thousands of years old??

The last trial was BRUTAL, Tiberious was instantly blasted by the Spectators, relenting his quest upon saying something about "Juliania" and vanished. Fen, perhaps still haunted by the ghosts of his past, proceeded to use a simple rope trick to take a breather and make it to the finish timeline. However, he suffered incredible wound which all opened as he endured the last of the lions terrible roars, collapsing unconscious to the ground in the agony of it all. It was here, at death's door that something within Fen held on. He rose from ground and wished to be able to convince his brother not to join the cult, upon which his eyes returned to normal and somewhere there is a cargo-vessel with Devin Brighthallow as its captain. The Verdant Horizon would never shepard victims to the lair of the Sleepless, however, Fen still was apart of Bartrims Brezerkers, a group of mercenaries who joined the Scarlet Tunic Brigade after they stumbled around in the Ways of the Underdark... He too arrived back in WindHaven and headed for the nearest bar to have a pint and wait for it all to blow over... all too aware of the duality of his new and old memories.

Meanwhile, Brigitte and Kelik all headed for the gate far north of town to prepare for their journey into the maelstrom and answer the summons of the Storm King! Randal and Tesca head instead to the Traveler's Pony for some bardic espionage.
