# Chapter 41: Chapter 41 - Dungeon Master Lead In - The Breaking of The Fellowship

*Notes by DM*

Chapter 41 -- Dungeon Master Lead In -- The Breaking of The Fellowship

Last we saw our heroes they had managed to get Captain Brownbeard Rummy's ship The Sea Urchin out of the Sleepless's lair through the use of another case of dimension door shenanigans, but not before gaining a boatload of gold. After paying reparations for the damage done, they agreed to set a path towards Windhaven despite the knowledge that an orc blockade was between them and their destination.

During the downtime of travel, the party had devised a plan between them. It had become evident after talking amongst yourselves that not all of you were needed to travel to the dwarven capital. Especially the tieflings in the party. Rummy, a fellow Tiefling had talked with the party and explained that Vag Mahar was under martial law. Thorin Ghosteye has shut down all embassies and has begun marshaling the three police forces: The Iron Fist, The Craftsmen Eye, and the Arms of Righteous. They claim that it was the influences of outside gods and tieflings that brought upon the spellplague creating the drab. And so, a split in the party was decided.

Nyx, Tezka, Randal, Sprocket, Varys, Brigit, Stalwart Little, and Fin Darkeyes all decided to split from the party and travel to Dawnpass, through Windhaven before trying to tackle the Forest Zenith. This way they could swing by the Harvest Festival and say hello to Tiberious, traverse the Eavallan Forest, old stomping ground of both Varys and Brigit, investigate what happened to the Heart of the Wilde in Argos, and finally reach Machu to find the Forest Zenith.

Daina, Bergs, Nocturnous, Irethia, and Bardin all would travel to the Vag Mahar and make contact with Decan Smithrunner, he supposedly knew Daina's Mother and could share the story of the arena. Additionally, Nocturnous' prophecy dictated the he must travel to the Vag Mahar, so he agreed to accompany the dwarves. Yousef suggested to Irethia that she should stay with the Dwarves in order to avoid running into Ashara Everstar in Dawnpass as contact with the former owner of her draconic passenger could cause it to flare up out of control. Dune and Yousef agreed to part ways with Fin Darkeyes with silent understanding. There was nothing holding Fin here in the underdark. He needed to start fresh, and Dune needed an audience with the king to plead for aid for the Earth Genasi Clan. Yousef needed to continue his research in light of Randal's recent discovery of the origin of a particular type of tiefling. To learn more he plans to travel to the kiln of the first flame where the creation of tiefling is rumored to have happened.

The plan set the two groups said goodbye and were packing their supplies \*anything posted before the session today at 7pm in the channel can be done to interact with the opposite party\*

A familiar ripping sound strikes the eardrums like broken glass. A swirling of black and a neon purple opening to a cold ashen sky. Out from this portal falls a humanoid cloaked shape, performing a last minute flip narrowly avoiding a disastrous fall.

Before the party knew what hit them, a loud BOOM shakes the boat originating from the other side of the ship. The Boom turned out the accompany the arrival of a technicolor cavern of swirling red, yellow, purple, pink, orange, blue, and green lights. This spectacular display of magic is in stark contrast to the open ocean air that it leaps out from. Plunging headfirst quickly followed by a flash of fluttering wings, a large 6ft tall hairy firbolg creature lands with a thud knocking both the tabaxi and himself below deck, into the packing dwarven party getting a briefing from Captain Liza and Brownbeard Rummy. In response to these new arrivals, without warning the teleportation circle linking the Sea Urchin to the Qual'Xar teleportation hub known as the sand seas. This was discovered too late as the 2 opened portals were still opened, held that way through some strange unseen force. The portals gravitated towards the now active circle all while slowly orbiting each other, violently flexing its borders against the side of the ship.

The entire team was caught in their path and were forced to travel through.. It is here that we find our heroes. Hit with the dry arid desert air and overwhelming light of the sun. The sounds of a market of some sorts chattering on the wind.
