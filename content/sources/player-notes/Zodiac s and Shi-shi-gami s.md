# Zodiac s and Shi-shi-gami s

Zodiac's/Shi-shi-gami's (as of Chapter 26)

- Zodiac's are immortal beings that live in the world of Mardov. They hide in plain sight, taking on a mortal form until they die and are reborn.

- Shi-shi-gami

  - Close to shinigami - god of death

  - Might mean "forest spirit" or "deer god". Potentially just "nature spirit"

- Each Zodiac has a gift they can bestow upon mortals to help us prevent Gard'damon. And if they pick the right champion, the champion that survives to the end and prevents Gard'damon, the zodiac will be granted a power beyond their wildest dreams. If their champion fails, they will face the eternal death.

- From speaking with Mr. Fantastic Talking Cosmic Fox aka Manitou, we have learned some things about what have been bestowed on us.

  - The elk is the second almost-diety level entity to trade their immortality to help us with Sabine being the first (by saving us from death and bestowing us with crystal hearts)

  - The elk granted us the ability to speak with the Zodiac's in their ethereal form.

  - And he appointed Manitou to be our guide to an ancient tomb - The tomb of the Last Curator (potentially related to the curators mentioned in the third prophecy?)

- Other zodiac's

  - The serpent and the turtle are zodiac's that have already shed their immortal form.

    - Does this mean that someone else is their champion? Or did their champion fail? Or was their champion the person to defeat Tygras? Would that even be a thing?

    - Also, from the prophesies and from what we've heard it seems that the serpent is dangerous. It "plagues" the sea. But every zodiac we've come across has been friendly. Did it lose its mind when it gave up its immortality? Or is it just naturally dangerous? Does this mean some zodiac's are more powerful than others?

  - Osiris - mentioned as a zodiac, but we don't have information on her form. She rules over the desert sands.

  - There is also another zodiac that has taken the form of a rabbit.
