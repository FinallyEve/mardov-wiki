Summary 33A - Dance Like No One’s Watching

[From Randall’s perspective]

Having had enough adventuring for the moments and being unable to make heads or tails of current events, I decided to write a song to gather my thoughts. The tempo was an ocean wave, The notes fell into place, so easily...too easily. I began to form the verse in my mind, no remember the verse, an image of a turtled appeared and began to whisper...

Just then a portal appeared, displacing my body and misplacing his thoughts. Oh well, like they say, inspiration always strikes all the time.

After collecting myself I took a quick head count. “Let’s see there’s Daina, and Tiberius, and….no one. Great well, As long as there are no giants with sensitive ears or big red shiny buttons I’m sure we’ll be fine.

A well dressed man appeared and seemed to be delighted to see us, though I was almost certain he was a baddy. The man, Renaldine, admitted, he had trapped us in a pocket dimension of his own design to test our abilities. Ah yep, there it is, definitely a baddy, didn’t even ask first.

He shows us a bird in a cage. As our prize. Clearly he doesn’t know that the party has several already. Oh, it’s Brigit, that’s embarrassing. Do druids eat bird food when they’re birds? What do birds eat...worms? Does Brigit eat worms? I’ll have to ask her when she doesn’t have a beak.

Oh he’s still talking. He wants to know about Nyx’s companions. Are they friends? I better write all these questions down. He’s presenting us a series of challenge rooms. This should be cake. I played intramural challenge room at the circle.

He also tells us that if we can choose to wear a dunce cap in place of a challenge. That sounds fun, but I haven’t had great luck with hats recently. Though if it’s anything like his it’s probably pretty swank.

He also mentions that he’s a god in this realm. Now, that’s intriguing. I’ll have to look into this, if only to practice. He displays his power by reducing any proclamation of Bahamut to a whisper. The party seems to be split on the morality of this action.

The first room has a sphinx. You know what a sphinx is right? Isn’t that weird? The sphinx has riddles for us. It turns out that we are each able to solve. Who knew we were all so good at it. I guess everyone needs to cross a bridge now and again.

The next room is a test of silence. I knew it. They’re not giants, but they do have sensitive ears. Tiberius express his esteem for Bahamut by creating flatulence on the other side of the room distracting the beasts.

I won’t bore you by bragging, but I will tell you that we:

Floated across a chocolate river on marshmallow

Played on a jungle gym

Drank potions and herbs with varying effects

Went back an knocked out those creatures we snuck by earlier

Woke a very in the way troll and asked it, very politely,  to move out of the way.

Ran from boulders without dropping any hats

Hid from our own reflections

About the potions...You may have noticed that Tiberius is now the shiniest blue color. I think it looks great, but he’s a little sensitive about it. Apparently Dragons are very racist.

The final challenge was a capture the staff scenario, but with flags. I raised an army of squirrels, deer, and other forest creatures to defend our base in return for some Marshmallow Daina had tucked away. Tiberius in an equally impressive feat summoned a hoard of a angels to defend our flag. Daina offered to scout ahead by running in a straight line for the enemy’s flag. As good scouts do, she found several traps, and going above and beyond set them off to mark their locations.

I activated my invisibility. Did you know I could do that? I could have been listening to any of your conversations, you never would have known. After booking across the field and dancing between two angry trolls, I brought the flag home without a scratch. Renaldine seemed miffed at this, but let us go on his bad guy words with some bag guy prizes.
