# The Mardov Intrigue Web

*A comprehensive map of every conspiracy, betrayal, hidden agenda, and political thread across 136 chapters.*

---

## Part 1: The Conspiracy Web

### THE COSMIC ENGINE: Vecna vs. Sabine

The entire campaign is driven by one act of divine jealousy. Vecna and Sabine were the original **twin souls** -- both gods of death, but Sabine also ruled life and fate. Vecna's envy of that additional dominion is the cosmic engine driving every conspiracy in Mardov.

Sabine's avatar (the Raven Queen) founded two colleges of magic: the **Circle of the Murder** (necromancy/death, entrusted to her teacher Ramses) and the **Artificer's Guild** (animancy/life, entrusted to her husband Xerxes). Ramses grew jealous and attempted to grant both himself and Sabine eternal life through lichdom. When Sabine rejected the ritual, Ramses forced it on her. Sabine starved her lich form by refusing to consume souls, crystallizing into a Demi-Lich trapped inside a phylactery for centuries.

This created the fracture that Vecna exploits across the entire campaign.

### THE SPELL PLAGUE

**Origin:** "Pure genius" -- discovered by Ebon Wayne (Oblivion's husband).

**Mechanism:** Without the Heart of the Faith, even humans undergo "crisis of faith." The plague has an unknown origin, possibly from another plane.

**Research chain:**
1. Alhoon Ren combines ground Umber Hulk eyes + Oblivion's blood
2. This restores individual thought to Illithid hosts
3. Vampirism is the key to liberating mindflayer puppets from Elder Brain
4. Next generation of Alhoon can be Ullitharid (elite Illithid)
5. Combined with the spell plague: "dawn of a new empire"

**Supply chain:**
- Hags brew Pestilence from corrupted children's souls (aged 4, developing souls)
- Supply wagons deliver pestilence to Rimefang
- Rimefang ordered to "finish the Spellplague" at Winter's Zenith
- Reynaldine threatens to disrupt supply lines as leverage against Vecna

**Who benefits:** Peraxis (defeats dwarves), Oblivion (liberates Illithid for vampire army), Vecna (destroys Winter's Zenith barrier)

**Who fights it:** The party, Conquis Abbey (Heart of the Faith delivery), the Winter's Curators (ruined dragon-kin tribe)

### JORGEN'S BETRAYAL

**The Seeds (Chapter 0):**
- Jorgen arrives at the Scarlet Tunic Brigade as a human ranger in a gold tabard
- Comes sprinting in pursued by flying kobolds
- Claims to have lost his previous party in the sewers
- Immediately trusted by the new recruits

**The Evidence (Chapter 1):**
- Dallor pickpockets a note from Jorgen showing Finkle knew the recruits' arrival time and details -- proof of a setup
- Jorgen led the party through the sewer trap, then the door slammed shut behind him
- He was complicit in leading adventuring parties to their deaths for Mordred's soul-harvesting operation

**The Execution (Chapter 2):**
- Mordred reveals himself: "2000 souls, to be exact... They could have flourished if we hadn't betrayed them"
- Mordred commands Jorgen to be killed for "outliving his usefulness"
- Jorgen dies but becomes a revenant, chanting "KILL THE GNOME"

**The Aftermath:**
- Jorgen's revenant pursues Finkle repeatedly (Chapters 7, 8, 11, 13)
- The party defeats his revenant through fireball cauterization + knocking overboard
- His revenant soul is eventually harvested by Veera Saytrfell for Nyx's human glamour (Chapter 19)
- Even as a soul powering Nyx's disguise, Jorgen's vengeful voice echoes: "KILL THE GNOME"
- Jorgen's betrayal is the players' first taste of the campaign's core theme: nothing in Mardov is what it appears

**Pilot significance:** Jorgen's arc from trusted companion to revealed traitor to undying revenant to soul-fuel is the campaign's opening intrigue thread. It establishes the pattern of trust, betrayal, and consequence that defines every subsequent conspiracy.

- **Dallor's pickpocket (Ch. 1):** Players caught the Jorgen/Finkle setup before the DM's reveal, via Dallor stealing a note

- **Brigitte's zodiac connection:** Players began connecting zodiac lore to their character arcs before the DM fully revealed the system
- **"What the fuck is going on?" (Ch. 100):** Brigitte's chapter title -- the Shadowfell/time dilation arc genuinely baffled players, showing DM succeeded in maintaining mystery even 100 chapters deep
- **Players tracking the Forgesworn conspiracy:** By chapter 34, players had pieced together Ghost-Eye's betrayal chain from Thorkon's note + Bartrim's backstory before the DM connected all dots

### Red Herrings That Worked

- **The Scarlet Tunic Brigade's innocence:** Players genuinely believed they were joining a legitimate adventuring company
- **Finkle as a friendly NPC:** The gnome COO persona was so convincing that even after the reveal, the party eventually accepted the REAL Finkle back as an ally
- **The Sunless Citadel as Winter's Zenith:** Stalwart expected to find the Zenith there; instead found the Shield of Retribution. The real Zenith was inside Mt. Bynar
- **Reynaldine as a pure villain:** His Vecna-servant persona lasted the majority of the campaign before his true motivation (saving Gabriel) was revealed

### Red Herrings That Didn't Work

- **MIA's memory wipe:** The DM used this to explain why players didn't remember joining the Brigade, but it was more of a retroactive patch than a planned mystery
- **The WereMoth:** Players solved this faster than expected on the Thornberry Shade

## Part 4: Pilot Intrigue Seeds

Based on the campaign's opening (Chapters 0-2 as the pilot's core scope), here are the intrigue threads that must be planted and the foreshadowing the opening panels should contain:

### Threads Planted in Pilot Scope

1. **Finkle/Mordred's Disguise** -- The entire pilot operates under the deception that this is a standard adventuring company. Every friendly interaction with Finkle is dramatic irony.

2. **Jorgen's Complicity** -- His arrival (sprinting from kobolds, gold tabard, lost his previous party) must read as heroic on first pass and sinister on rewatch. The gold tabard is Mordred's color. "Lost his previous party" means he led them to death.

3. **The Sewer Ritual** -- The four colored waterfalls (red blood, green poison, orange mercury, blue liquid souls) and the Raven Queen phylactery are the visual spine of the pilot's climax.

4. **Sabine Ra's Sacrifice** -- The Demi-Lich's choice to die so the party can live is the emotional core. The crystalline hearts deposited in each member are the campaign's MacGuffin.

5. **Dallor's Pickpocket** -- The note revealing Finkle knew the recruits' arrival time. This is the evidence thread -- the moment the audience (through Dallor) learns the truth before the full reveal.

6. **The 2,000 Souls** -- Mordred's line about harvesting 2,000 adventurer souls establishes the scale of evil. The undead audience in the colosseum (Chapter 3) visualizes this.

### Foreshadowing the Opening Panels Should Contain

**Visual seeds (background details the audience won't notice until rewatching):**
- The Vecna symbol hidden in architectural details of Argos
- Four-colored motifs (red, green, blue, orange) appearing subtly -- matching the four Zeniths
- A blood star barely visible in the sky (the recurring omen)
- Kobold eyes with the unnatural blue glow of Mordred's undead magic
- Finkle's shadow slightly wrong -- too tall, too gaunt, not matching his gnome body

**Dialogue seeds:**
- Finkle asking about "ancient cults" and the Raven Queen cult -- seemingly academic, actually reconnaissance
- Finkle noting that cult members "mark themselves with symbols under their eyelids" -- he knows because he IS one
- "SCARLET, SCARLET, SCARLET" chanted by kobolds -- they know the Brigade's true nature

**Thematic seeds:**
- The Monkey's Paw principle: the party signs up for adventure and gets death
- Twin souls: Sprocket's connection to Sabine begins at the ritual chamber
- The four waterfalls mapping to the four Zeniths (blood=Death, poison=Pestilence, mercury=Famine, souls=War)
- Jorgen's gold tabard echoing Mordred's colors, contrasting with the party's scarlet

**Character seeds for long-term arcs:**
- Nocturnus Wayne's aristocratic bearing (foreshadows Drow royalty)
- Daina's connection to her axe (contains Thorkon's hidden letter)
- Brigitte's wild-shape affinity (zodiac destiny)
- Sprocket's magical sensitivity (Raven-Touched, Sabine's vessel)
- Nyx's tiefling features hidden or remarked upon (foreshadows the prejudice arc)
- Dallor's thievery (his pickpocket of Jorgen is the pilot's detective moment)
- Stalwart's righteous fury (will later confront the Sunless Citadel)

### What the Pilot Must NOT Reveal

- Mordred is Ramses (save for Chapter 4 exposition)
- The four Zeniths exist (let the colored waterfalls be mysterious)
- Sabine Ra's full cosmological significance (she's just a trapped Demi-Lich for now)
- The Zodiac system
- V-MARDOV
- Any of the four Horsemen
- The prophecies

The pilot's job is to make the audience feel safe, then rip that safety away. Finkle's reveal is the first knife. Sabine's sacrifice is the first heartbreak. The crystalline hearts are the first mystery. Everything else unfolds from there.
