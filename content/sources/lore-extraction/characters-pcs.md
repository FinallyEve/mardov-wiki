# Clay Glennbrook

## At a Glance
A crossbow-wielding vampire hunter who lost his wife Molly to a vampire brood, slew her and the nest, and now runs an inn called Molly's Revenge -- carrying twin hand-crossbows named after stolen children as reminders of evil's cost.

## Backstory
Clay was a farmer who lost his wife Molly when a vampire claimed her for his brood. She turned on Clay, leaving him beaten and alone at age 24. He spent 5 years learning to fight the creatures that ruined his life.

When he found Molly again, she had been with the brood for 5 years and had stolen 2 village children, renaming them after the children she and Clay had planned to have: Ian and Lilly. Clay tracked her to a forgotten crossroads inn serving as a nest for 20 vampires.

He mustered hunters from the neighboring village, including James Downham and Bartrim Readding -- the fathers of the stolen children, whose wives had been killed in the kidnapping. During the raid, James and Bartrim were slain by the Alpha Vampire Quintin Tyr, though Bartrim wounded the beast enough to make it flee.

Molly refused to leave her adopted children and was trapped in the burning inn. Clay was forced to slay his own wife. He used the vampires' hoard to rebuild the inn as "Molly's Revenge." His twin hand-crossbows, taken from James and Bartrim's bodies, are inscribed "Ian" (Downham) and "Lilly" (Readding) -- he never learned the children's real names.

# Diesa "Daina" Fireforge

## At a Glance
A red-haired hill dwarf woman raised in exile in the Frozen Forest, unknowingly the daughter of a legendary royal weaponsmith, carrying a greataxe and a clan heirloom that mark her as having ties to the Bronzebeard dynasty -- searching for the black-eyed, red-bearded stranger who murdered her father.

## Backstory
Daina was born in the Frozen Forest in the dead of winter, in the mountains behind Vag'Mahar. Her father, Thorkon Fireforge, was once a royal weaponsmith of legendary renown in Vag'Mahar. He and his wife Hlin lived well until a rival weaponsmith, backed by one of the Bronzebeards, sabotaged Thorkon's shipments by lacing his weapons with deadly poison. When the Red Bronzebeard was affected by the poisoned greataxe, Thorkon was accused of attempted assassination.

Thorkon and Hlin fled into the Frozen Forest in winter, where Hlin gave birth to Diesa -- a red-haired baby girl. Hlin died of blood loss and cold only 12 hours after naming her daughter.

Devastated, Thorkon changed his name to Tor Ironforge and renamed Diesa to Daina. Without clan names, they lived as outcasts among the hill dwarves, trading in the Frozen Forest. Thorkon forged weapons with deliberate defects to avoid recognition, never making fine weaponry again except for the greataxe he left to Daina.

On his deathbed, Thorkon bequeathed to Daina the greataxe and an heirloom from Hlin marking her as old clan with familial ties to the Bronzebeards. Daina knows none of this history.

What Daina does know: a red-bearded stranger with jet black eyes and a strange tattoo came looking for a blacksmith and was directed to Tor -- the same day he fell mysteriously ill and died. With no family left and lacking her father's smithing skill, Daina set out wandering, searching for the black-eyed red beard who killed her father.

## Personality & Traits
- Independent, self-reliant loner raised in the wilderness
- Driven by vengeance for her father's murder
- Unaware of her noble heritage and the political conspiracy behind her exile
- Tough, practical, accustomed to hardship

## Relationships
- Thorkon Fireforge / Tor Ironforge (father, deceased -- murdered by the stranger)
- Hlin (mother, died in childbirth)
- The Bronzebeards (unknown familial connection)
- Bergs (shared Fireforge surname -- possible relative)
- The black-eyed red-bearded stranger (father's murderer, target of vengeance)

## Character Arc
- Born in exile, raised in ignorance of her heritage
- Father murdered by a mysterious assassin
- Set out to find the killer, carrying heirlooms she doesn't understand the significance of
- Potential for revelation about her noble lineage and the conspiracy that destroyed her family

## Visual Description
- "Daina Reborn" portrait: Dark-skinned dwarf woman with white/platinum hair pulled back, glowing amber/orange eyes. Wears heavy plate armor with glowing orange runic inscriptions and geometric patterns. Carries a large warhammer/maul. Armor has orange crystal/gem inlays. Imposing, battle-hardened appearance with a fierce expression. Suggests she has undergone a "rebirth" or empowerment.
- Original description from backstory: Red-haired hill dwarf woman.

# Nyx Khamestria

## At a Glance
A tiefling warlock with curly dark purple hair and small horns, bound by a deal her parents made with the archfey Reynardine -- she is a twin-soul whose sister Xyn was taken to the Fae, and who now carries powers she was never meant to receive.

## Backstory
2000 years ago, the wizard Tygras made a deal with an archfey from Vecna's plane, gaining the secret to channel pure arcana in exchange for creating a portal. Tygras created a weapon powered by Dawn's Diadem. Surefoot, after making the Matrim Pact, stole the Diadem by killing its user mid-operation. The weapon backfired, sending an entire city to the Fae and creating a passage for benevolent fey to enter the world (bonded with humans).

26 years ago, Nyx was born. Her parents had made a deal with the same archfey (unknowingly): in exchange for their child's soul, they would receive the philosopher's stone (ability to channel pure arcana). The child was actually a twin-soul, both tieflings and fey-touched.

8 years ago, a twist of fate revealed that the dominant soul (Nyx) was not the vessel the archfey needed. Instead her dormant sister was taken. Nyx received the powers from this patron, the deal was revealed, and her parents -- believing the deal had gone sour -- spurned Nyx as a failed experiment.

7 years ago, the archfey returned, half-in/half-out of this plane. It made another deal with the parents. Xyn was born as a separate being -- the perfect vessel and Tygras's true heir -- easily manipulated and submissive after 19 years as a passenger soul.

Now: The archfey Reynardine (the Madness Bringer) has been busy. He found Logath, a descendant of gladiator orcs, and set the current plan in motion: (1) get the Diadem, (2) use the girl to open a portal to the Fae, (3) merge the Fae and material plane, allowing V-Mordred entrance and kicking off Gard'Daemon.

## Personality & Traits
- Unwanted child, rejected by parents as a "failed experiment"
- Carries powers she was never intended to receive
- Twin-soul -- her sister Xyn is somewhere in/from the Fae

At age 9, Riven discovered his great-grandfather Vandallan Fendrim's panflute below deck of the ship Words of Reconciliation. The elven panflute was broken and silent, but bore an inscription in elvish: "Follow the Astral Wind to find your Boon." When confronted, his parents dismissed Vandallan as a failure who abandoned his quest for a life of revelry and sin.

Disillusioned with his parents' diplomatic pretensions, Riven determined to surpass his great-grandfather. He was sent to the College of Magic but lacked the work ethic for wizardry. He excelled only at manipulating classmates into doing his work and researching the panflute in the library. Riven was a dropout failure. Then the College of Mages was attacked in a brutal orc raid. Weaponless and alone, with only the panflute around his neck, Riven fled to the shoreline where a single orc blocked his escape. He tried talking, lying, begging -- the orc didn't understand Common.
