Journal Entry, Day 1568 since I've last seen you,

After so many years, I think I received a message from you, sire. Last night, I suffered from a

strange, drug-induced dream, courtesy of Randall and his sketchy dealer. Within the dream

were Mother and Father...

But at the end of this dream, I thought I heard your mischievous laugh as the scene faded! What

does this mean, sire? When will I see you again? I won't pretend to understand your intentions -

I can only hope that I am using the powers you've gifted me in the way you intended them to be

used. Until you tell me otherwise, I'll continue to record my daily actions in case you require

them someday.

After my nightmare faded away, the mist coalesced into a new dream that featured Daina’s younger self. We sat by helplessly as we watched a sinister, tattooed shadow stalk Daina’s

father. When the younger Daina left to go retrieve something, the shadow stabbed something

into the neck of her father, leaving him face down in the dirt. I don't know what happened next,

but I'm pretty sure it explains why Daina is always scowling...

The dream then shifted to the completely nonsensical hallucinations of Tiberius. The old

dragon must be more demented than I thought if his nightmares involve devout mice praying to

a dragon statue. Tiberius seemed absolutely petrified much to the confusion of the rest of us.

Sprocket at least had a good bit of fun pretending to be a giant among mice--I suppose it

must be the only time he feels that way.

Despite this absurdity, I did find the cure to my tinnitus! Mice are apparently the only thing that

quiets that damn old dragon. I do feel bad exploiting such a severe phobia, but at the same

time, I'm fairly certain that I'm losing hearing in my right ear...

Thankfully, Tiberius convinced the mice to scribble out the mistakes in their scripture, and we

finally woke up. We seemed to have passed out in a bar for part of the night, so we slowly

crawled our way back to our chambers. While we slept, there was some horrific incident that

involved Sprocket’s indigestion that I would like to erase from my memory forever. After the worst sleep of our lives, we groggily started the day with Stalwart galloping off into the distance with a chicken in his arms...never to be seen again. It was nice knowing ya, buddy.

Anyway, the next morning, a mysterious letter was slipped to Brigit by a messenger. The

letter was from Dolph, asking us to meet him at his office. Despite this very clear quest line

request from a trusted friend, our party bickered for a bit before deciding to split up. Half of our

party went to the curio shop to inquire about the bad drugs we took while the other sensible half

went to Dolph's office to follow the quest line see what Dolph wanted.

When we finally all met up at Dolph's place, Dolph surreptitiously snuck Brigit another note

saying that he couldn't speak freely here and to meet him at the bar at midnight, also commonly

known as the justice hour according to Tiberius. We headed to the quest board to kill some time while we waited to hear the news from Dolph. The quest board had 2 quests:

"Please help, my child hasn't been the same since Mondu. He keeps having nightmares." And

"Wolves are terrorizing the farms."

The heartless bastard Sprocket decided that children are boring as fuck, and he's done saving them. Somehow, the rest of us were completely on board with this, so off we went to the wolves!

As we got to the farm, the farmer rambled on about some demon chicken that I probably could

have heard more about if "BAHAMUT!!!!" wasn't still ringing in my ears. Anyway, the farmer

pointed us into the forest to find the wolves and off we went again.

As we crept deeper into the woods, my sharp eyes and keen senses noticed a trip wire that would have surely killed us all if I hadn't been my usually brilliant self. Despite all of our careful efforts to set up a plan, Bergs (unknowingly to all of us) was raised by wolves and somehow felt more allegiance to these random wolves than to us--the people who have saved his life multiple times . Bergs attempted to warn these wolves but Sprocket quickly cast a spell that exploded rocks everywhere, including straight into Tiberius’ face because who needs a cleric anyway? Not us apparently! As Tiberius limped away from the action, the wolves were slowly taken out one by one. Bergs managed to convince the alpha female to surrender but Nocturnus decided to slaughter her anyway since someone woke up on the homicidal side of the bed today. I spared the last wolf out of pity, and we go back to the farmer to collect our reward.

The farmer rewarded us generously with either 20 gold or a chicken. I have more gold than I need at this point, so I opted for a chicken! I now have a chicken named Cornelius. He was in a fowl mood at first, but I think it was just because Hedwig doesn't seem to like him very much. Hedwig has been very jealous ever since Stalwart said Linda was his familiar and is convinced that Cornelius will steal his spotlight. I told him that was ridiculous, but he's been pouting ever since.

Meanwhile, Nocturnus decided that he gets both 20 gold and a chicken due to fact that he is

the mayor, and he cited the chicken as a "tax." This almost immediately bit him in the ass when

we got back to town as townspeople started to complain about the taxes.

Nocturnus continued to have a terrible day as we noticed a giant painted portrait of Nocturnus high on drugs with the words "Count The Votes!" underneath it. Luckily, we caught the painter and Tiberius cast the Zone of Truth to make him confess who's hiring him to do this. The painter hemmed and hawed for a bit until I "bribed" him with 2000 gold to tell us who was paying him to do this. I used a bit of careful wording to get around the Zone of Truth, but I doubt the fool noticed. He told us that he was being paid by Vagran Roguestaff, the celebrity guild leader, to paint Nocturnus in a bad light.

It was almost midnight at this point, so we went to meet Dolph at the bar to continue on the

quest line to see what our friend wanted to discuss. Dolph told us that he had been tracking

some mysterious unaccounted payments in Mondu’s account. Mondu apparently has been

building strange buildings in the lower ward, but Dolph nees proof of Mondu's suspicious

activity. Dolph also told us about some suspicious people:

Lord Jerrifer, who has been building the mysterious buildings

Lord Castro, who oversees building codes and permits

Lord Everdone, who oversees the ships

Lord Finnerwhiffle, who has information

Right after this information, three dragonborn thugs suddenly started getting nosy about our

whispering. Apparently, they had been following us all day! I tried to charm two of them to

deescalate the situation, and they told me that they were hired by Lord Jerrifer to kill us.

Nocturnus managed to persuade the third one to back down. Thank goodness we avoided

such a completely unnecessary fight, is what I thought as an extremely drunk Bergs slammed

his battle-axe down on the head of the third dragonborn. Dolph told us to avoid killing anyone, so we decided to beat these dragonborn to an unconscious pulp and then try to revive them later.

That should have absolutely no consequences, right?

Based on Tiberious and Stalwart, I'm pretty sure brain damage is a given if you're a dragonborn anyway. We managed to knock two of these thugs out, but the third one decided to bolt and squeal to the guards outside. Brigit was crouched over the third dragonborn's body with me standing at a distance when the guards marched up and told us to halt - By the gods! That twice-damned dragon just started worshipping again! I’ll finish the rest of this later when my headache stops.

Your humble servant,

Nyx
