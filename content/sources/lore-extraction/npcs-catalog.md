### Underdark & War Zenith

Key lore from `Broad Strokes.txt`:
- **Oblivion Wayne** is Nocturnus's Aunt and Leader of the Drow. Married **Ebon Wayne**, a vampire drow.
- Oblivion is obsessed with the ancient Warforged army. She believes warforged are key to dominating the Illithid.
- Ebon is captor of the Bat Zodiac. Uses it to bestow the "gift of the first bite" creating a new alpha vampire.
- Oblivion uses vampire alpha power to depose her brother and unite the Drow. Each turned has innate loyalty to her.
- **Peraxis Geth** is an Illithilich raised by Vecna's power, gifted a shard of the Raven Queen. Has all of Tiny Rick's memories.
- The Elder Brain has been corrupted by Vecna's influence through Peraxis. Only Ulitharid elite escaped.
- Peraxis's one command from Vecna: "Assault the War's Zenith."
