Chapter 36 Dungeon Master Lead In

Last we saw our heroes, they were descending into the WAYS, a network of tunnels and caverns which span the entirety of MARDOV about a mile below the surface.

During the difficult descent, the party got to know BARTIM'S BERSERKERS, learning a bit more about the motivations of its various members

- Bartrim Hadrin has a mysterious wound over his heart which is under constant maintenance by Dierdre Crestfallen, a fellow cleric of Bahamut, who claimed that she wished "Tibbers" was here

- Yousef Redvine, the party learned is a Draconic Soul Sorcerer who had his "passenger" split from himself and placed inside a ring of protection by his wife, Veera Saytrfell. Randall had a conversation with the "passenger," a red dragon soul named: REDVASSYL THE ASHEN GUARD, who claimed that he was resting after "Tiamut is dead," it is learned that Yousef, due to the influence of his passenger, registers as a fiend, he finds Irethia very interesting as she should also be a "fiend" but her passenger is seemingly nowhere to be found

- Dune, the Earth Genasi Royal Guard, is looking for his prince, GARNET who was taken by a mastermind who used black iron rods to control Dune's fellow guards, while Dune was off duty

- Fin, a human with mysteriously dark eyes, is looking for his brother, who was taken by some form of Cult, he is gifted a ring by Yousef, due to their bond as fellow Berserkers

- Bardin, a fellow exiled dwarf obsessed with count, also revealed himself to be a forgesworn, hinted that his specialty is boots

Once down within the ways, we discovered a camp of 2 skeletons around a fire as well as a cute mini myconid who asked for release from the "SLEEPLESS" and pointed the party back towards the darkness, briefly lighting the way.

Additionally, the party found a volcano vent which was strangely and magically "frozen" over in a stalagmite, one good whack from THORKON FIREFORGE's old axe shattered the vent but then NOCTURNOUS WAYNE comes running out from the darkness, with a pack of drider/cave spiders hot on his heels.

NOCTURNOUS's first act was to plunge NIGHTMARE, into the spore's head, killing the seemingly innocent creature and transforming NOCTURNOUS, causing him to become gaunt and lose his hair. But there was no time to talk, for they had spiders on them in an instant. As the tide of battle shifted in the party's favor, one of the last remaining drider stole, THORKON FIREFORGE's ax and ran off into the magic darkness.

Daina, chased after the fiend, relying on SPROCKET's Mind spike to direct the two of them as they chase down the stolen heirloom…
