Character Backstories:

Brigit:

1) Where is she from?

a. Ireland ish area, woodsy and wild.

b. Bears, wolf, etc.

c. A small small village outside of Windhaven

2) Parents?

a. Lived in a village with them, pretty simple life. Survivalist, and living off of the woods close by.

b. When she started developing her powers, they weren't really controlled. She would change unpredictably and uncontrollably; usually as a quite fearsome wolf, but sometimes as other animals.

c. The villagers and council didn't understand when she started developing powers and rejected her in small ways at first. Eventually the fear was too great. Her parents stood up for her, and were killed in her stead (a teenager at this point), and she ran off to the woods to survive.

d. She lived in the woods for a while, befriended some creatures in the meantime, honed her skills.

3) What drives them?

a. Survival

b. A want to be accepted as she is

c. Vengeance for her parents

d. She's interested in learning more about her powers and why she is this way,

4) What were they doing before the character was committed to paper?

a. She stayed in the forest for a while, lost a bit of touch with civilization (beast vs. non-beast). She honed her skills, became someone that survives no matter what.

b. Sometimes she needed things she couldn't get, so she would sneak into nearby villages and steal them. Nothing big, nothing unnecessary, just little things. She was too wild at this point to properly trade for it, or really even care about anything but surviving.

c. Eventually, she turned around and became more civilized, she grew up and realized there were some things that she couldn't get on her own and she would actually need to trade for. She started trading in the small village outside of her forest; small things at first that should couldn't get on her own (metal weapons, some better crafted armor) and eventually befriended a trader there. Well, befriended is the wrong word, she really just tolerated his presence, due to her wild nature.

d. Every now and then she would get asked to do things for people in the town, gather some specific herbs, lead them to a specific part of the forest, little things like that. For the most part she rejected them, not wanting any ties (because she knew what happened the last time she had an emotional connection to someone/something).

e. A lot of the time it was easier to be in beast form than human form, a wolf normally, but sometimes a bird, bear or deer. Her mind stayed the same, but her senses were better and she could more easily move around. She was very very careful to not let anyone see her like this. Or at the very least not make the connection that she and the beasts were one being.

f. She always had a soft spot for outcasted children. Children that didn't quite fit in with their peers, or that were neglected by their parents. Once she was more comfortable in the village (while still being an outsider, sleeping in her den in the forest, etc), she would leave them little presents or gifts. A rare flower here, a perfectly carved toy sword, little things like that.

5) Why did your character leave their previous life?

a. One night while she was prowling the forest as a wolf, she heard a scream from the village, a terrified little child scream. She ran to the village, forgetting that she was still in wolf form. She came across bandits burning the village, killing the townspeople, but gathering and stealing the children. She tried to save them, tried to do something, but the fight was essentially over when she got there. The children were taken, the village destroyed and she was left bleeding and unable to do anything. The bandits left her dying in her wolf form, not realizing what she was. She woke up from her unconscious state human again after they left badly badly wounded. Somehow a few villagers had managed to hide and had also seen her transformation, knowing what she was now. She was once again driven out, despite who she was, despite what she had done and barely escaped to the thickest part of the forest where she recovered and mourned.

6) What did they leave behind?

a. See above.

b. She left once she was well enough to travel, searching once again for a reason she was like this. She came to Argos and learned all she could about Druids and what she was. She eventually accepted what she is and the power that came with it. She began using her powers, no longer scared of them, just scared or wary of what people thought about them. Eventually she got to the mindset that she didn't care if people were scared of her, she was powerful enough to destroy anyone stupid enough to attack her.

c. She kept trading things of the forest to people, kept doing little quests here and there. She was careful to stay very very detached this time.

d. (Not sure if we know this part) Eventually a member of the Scarlet Tunic brigade spotted her, had heard of her skills and proposed the cause to her. She didn't accept at first, not wanting to be associated with anything or anyone.

i. Maybe one day she heard a rumor about those bandits from the STB and joined just for that one thing. (hasn't happened yet, she's just biding her time)

7) What does your character want?

a. Survival

b. Vengeance for he parents, herself.

c. Acceptance

d. Justice for those who don't deserve what life dealt them

e. To know why she is this way, why she has these powers.

Daina:

Daina was born in the Frozen Forest in the dead of winter and lived in the mountains behind Vag'Mahar all of her life. Unbeknownst to her, her father (Thorkon Fireforge) was once a royal weapon smith of amazing renown. He and his wife (Hlin) lived well within the city of Vag'Mahar until a rival weapon smith with the support of one of the Bronzebeards sought to blacken his name by sabotaging the shipments of fine weaponry and intricate trinkets that Thorkon presented to the Bronzebeard's yearly. When the weapons arrived all of his items had been laced with a deadly poison which affected the Red Bronzebeard when he picked it up to inspect the great axe forged for him. Forced to flee after being accused of attempted assassination of a Bronzebeard, Thorkon and Hlin fled in winter to the Frozen forest where Hlin gave birth to the baby red haired Diesa Fireforge.

Hlin died due to blood loss and the cold only 12 hours after naming Diesa. Thorkon, knowing that he could never return and devastated by the death of Hlin, changed his name to Tor Ironforge and renamed Diesa to Daina. Without clan names they were forced to live apart from the other hill dwarves in solitude which suited Tor and Daina just fine, he traded regularly in the Frozen Forest with the other clans for basic necessities and would forge the weaponry they needed to trade and get by, ensuring they had just enough defects in them to ensure rumors didn't spread. He never forged any fine weaponry again other than the great axe that he left to Daina when he died of a mysterious illness that destroyed his body in a matter of days. On his deathbed he bequeathed to Daina the great axe and the heirloom Hlin brought with her from Vag'Mahar marking her as an old clan with familial ties to the Bronzebeards.

Daina doesn't know any of this, she just heard one of the men from the village talking about how a red bearded stranger with jet black eyes and a strange tattoo came to looking for a blacksmith and was sent to Tor, interestingly enough the same day he became terribly ill. With no family left and without the dexterity of her father, Daina set out on her own, wandering from town to town and keeping an eye out for a strange black eyed red beard.

She said farewell to the one friend she had within the village Flint Ungart, village elder's son who was apparently infatuated with her and the village elder Adrik, a fair if grumpy black bearded hill dwarf. Along her travels she made allies of the beastly barbarians of Monetham by beating them in arm wrestling contest, she travelled with them a while where together they embarked across the sea to Dawnhaven where Urk and the 4 Bob's parted ways with her. Here and there she travelled, doing work as she travelled, never taking up residence for too long or getting too involved.

Nyx:

Nyx was born to two humans - Vana and Al Khamestria - who were renown alchemists in Dawnhaven. Driven by a need for power and glory, her human parents decided to sell the soul of their unborn child to an old God in exchange for the Philosopher’s Stone. This dark transaction turned her into a tiefling, and therefore she was born with small horns and purple hair. For the entirety of Nyx’s childhood, her parents treated her as an object to be traded and bartered. They kept her alive only to be used in the deal they made with the old God. However, the old God that the parents believed they had made this deal with turned out to be a mischievous archfey, Reynardine. On Nyx’s 18th birthday, he arrived and gave her fey-like warlock powers for his own entertainment. The power her parents so desperately longed for was now hers. Yet as these powers manifested for the first time, Nyx lost control and accidentally cast Fey Presence on her human parents, causing them to become terrified of her. They fled, never to return, so Nyx decided to start afresh elsewhere with her newfound powers.

Stalwart Little:

Stalwart grew up in Dawnpass at the Wandering Orphan orphanage. He never knew his family, only his ‘siblings.’ As they grew up, the children of the Wandering Orphan decided that if no family wanted them then they would be forced to make their own. So all children at the orphanage began to take on the last name Little once they reached maturity. No one knows why they chose the name, but they all agree that it makes them feel a Little better.

As Stalwart grew up, he became restless and eventually decided to see the world by becoming a sailor on the merchant ship, the Narwhal. Aboard the Narwhal, Stalwart found his second home. However, while on land leave one night, Stalwart received word that the Narwhal had been attacked and destroyed by roving goblin pirates. A week into mourning the loss of his new home, Stalwart received word from his siblings in Dawnpass that Stewart Little, one of his closest brothers who had sought the life of a cleric, had never returned from delving into a mine called the Sunless Citadel. A mine that is said to be inhabited solely by goblins.

Nearly driven mad by grief, Stalwart vowed that he would do whatever it takes to get vengeance for his shipmates and his brother. Some god must of have heard him. So, with his new found powers, Stalwart set out on his quest for vengeance by heading towards the only goblin he had heard about, the first in command of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade.

Tezka:

Tezka was born to tiefling parents in a slum in Dawnpass. After crossing the underground crime syndicate, Tezka's parents were slaughtered, a fate Tezka herself narrowly escaped. For several years Tezka lived on the streets, thieving from merchants and travelers until a visiting monk spotted her antics. He offered her safety and stability at the nearby monastery and although wary, Tezka accepted.

At the monastery Tezka was teased and outcasted from the other children. She grew to resent her tiefling heritage even more than she did as a street urchin. The elder monk who picked her off the streets encouraged Tezka to redirect her frustration and energy into training, and he personally took her under his wing to offer spiritual teachings and life lessons. Tezka became a skilled fighter but still retained her propensity towards light crime, often using deceit and stealth to pull pranks on children who were cruel to her or to steal extra food at meal times.

Shortly before Tezka reached adulthood, her longtime mentor passed away of old age. Soon after, Tezka was wrongly accused of stealing offerings and vandalizing the temple. Although the other elder monks had tolerated her and sometimes showed kindness, they still did not completely trust her. They threw her out of the temple. Tezka took this as an opportunity to start traveling, seeking to experience the same places her mentor had told her about in tales of his own travels.

Varis Laindon:

Wilderness outpost childhood. Dad (Yeldan) was hunter/woodsman/guide/scout/messenger. Mom (Lianala) is a Druid. Started hunting and learning from both. Last big hunt was when Varis was 95 (think ~17 for human) was for a pack of pseudo dragons (they migrated through and killed sheep) and after a successful hunt, parents told him to keep a trophy. Not usually trophy takers. Kept the skull of his first kill of the pack. 2 weeks later, dad spotted undead scouts. Local lord believed him, but couldn’t convince farther people (at least, in time). 2 more weeks, and the army arrived. Zombies, skeletons, wraiths, mummies, led and created by the lich Ned'zailok. Volunteered with dad for militia, and took an adult name at the time. Think high school senior after 9/11 or Pearl Harbor. Fought beside dad, and local militia held for a week, hoping for relief from outside. Attacks got worse and supplies got limited after a week. Dad was sent (fastest scout) with trophies to request aid fast. We broke a day later. Scarlet tunic brigade arrived at the same time - too late to hold, but fast enough to cover a retreat and evac. Most of the non-militia got out, including mom. Militia took massive losses when it broke. Never found dad, and he never arrived at lord’s seat. STB (specifically Dockson, an honorable paladin, high ranking STB general type) shamed lord into acting. Looked like he would have challenged him to a duel (or just killed him in his throne room) if he didn’t cave. Militia kept fighting with STB until Ned'zailok defeated - which took another 18 months. Most dispersed after. Mom stayed in/near larger city when fight ended. I enlisted with STB, hoping to find more about dad. Never did. Been trying to help protect the weak and those screwed over by petty politics with the STB ever since (a few years). Always keep an ear out for word that Ned'zailok has resurfaced. Can’t ever be sure a lich is gone. Also keeps an ear out for any news of Yeldan, but stopped expecting to hear anything not long after the end of that war.
