## Brigit

### Speech Pattern
Brigit narrates in a diary/journal style -- conversational, self-deprecating, and grounded. She uses casual modern English peppered with mild profanity ("welp", "holy fucking shit", "nope nope nope"). She writes in run-on sentences when excited and clipped fragments when processing information. Her internal monologue is practical and emotionally honest. She is the party's chronicler and her voice reflects someone who is fundamentally sensible trapped in insane circumstances. She frequently uses parenthetical asides and rhetorical questions. She abbreviates and shortens words when emotional ("def", "legit").

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "Nope nope nope" (when confronting something terrifying)
- "Holy fucking shit" (when encountering important revelations)
- "I have so many questions" (her default reaction to new lore)
- "NOOOOOOOOOO" (dramatic protests, often played for comedy)
- Referring to Stalwart's physical intimidation as "the Stalwart slam! (copywrited)"
- "Bugs" (said fearfully -- her deep phobia, Ch111)
- "I remember you. I remember when you tried to kill me. Do you remember me?" (confronting enemies, Ch131)

### Humor Style
Self-aware, modern, and meta. Brigit's comedy comes from applying mundane logic to fantasy absurdity. She forms PTAs in dungeons, writes signs saying "All Children Banned," and narrates combat with the weary tone of someone filling out an incident report. Her humor is observational -- she notices the ridiculous in heroic moments.

### Emotional Range
- **Angry**: Rages physically -- shoves people to the ground, yells at liars. "Stop, you're being idiots!" When truly furious, she acts rather than speaks.
- **Scared**: Freezes or retreats. Says "Bugs" in a small, fearful voice. Her notes become shorter and more clipped.
- **Happy/Relieved**: Chugs mead, pulls out barrels of IPA, sets up heroes' feasts. Her writing becomes longer and more descriptive.
- **Protective**: Springs into action wordlessly. Transforms into animals to shield others. Herds civilians to safety like livestock (literally, as a wolf or dinosaur).
- **Processing grief**: "Did we really only just wake up in the castle this morning? Gods it seems like ages since I last slept."

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- Ch10: "It's 6 in the morning, we've been up all night fighting owlbears, we can deal with all that tomorrow" (attributed to Tezka, but Brigit's phrasing throughout)
- Ch13: "Dear Diary, Today was an interesting day. I mean it's not every day that you kiss a Succubus, reveal your destiny, and save a town from corruption, right?"
- Ch25: "NOOOOOOOOOO" -- stopping the party from pulling the revenge lever, redirecting to rescue
- Ch25: "Hey we really need the diamonds for the blood sigil in the first room"
- Ch29: "Welp. I fucked up a little bit. Turns out that I'm not completely perfect at transforming yet."
- Ch111: "Bugs" (said fearfully)
- Ch124: Pretends to be a bat. "You are no Bruce."
- Ch131: "I remember you. I remember when you tried to kill me. Do you remember me?"
- Ch131: "Come with me if you want to live"

### Relationship Voice
- **To Sprocket**: Maternal, slightly exasperated. Lets him sleep in her room because "Daina can be quite intimidating for such a small creature." Chugs mead on his command.
- **To Varis**: Trusted partner. They fly rescue missions together without discussion. He glares at her to be careful; she respects it.
- **To Daina**: Respectful of her strength, slightly intimidated. Notices Daina's emotional triggers before others do.
- **To Nocturnous**: Wary. Tried to polymorph him into a bunny when he went rogue. Documents his violence with journalistic distance.
- **To Stalwart**: Fond amusement. Documents his slams with affection.
- **To Bergs**: Protective. "Brigit goes back for Bergs" appears multiple times.

## Sprocket

### Speech Pattern
Sprocket speaks in clipped, sarcastic sentences. He refers to people by epithets rather than names ("the Loud One" for Stalwart, "boneheads" for the party). He narrates in a detached, superior tone -- the smartest person in the room who is constantly annoyed by everyone else's incompetence. His vocabulary is precise and slightly academic. He uses hashtags and modern slang ironically (#FakeNews). He is curt when giving orders and elaborate when describing his own cleverness.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "No." (flat refusal, Ch111)
- "Both." (when given a binary choice, Ch111)
- "What the hell happened around here?" (arriving to chaos, Ch111)
- Referring to Stalwart as "the Loud One"
- Referring to the party as "boneheads" or his "bodyguards"
- "Be careful what you touch" (warning the party)
- "She remembers me!" (about Trixie, showing his soft side)
- "MEANWHILE!" (narrating his own side adventures in all caps)
- "I don't have much patience for technicalities"

### Humor Style
Dry, deadpan, intellectual superiority. His humor comes from describing everyone else's failures in contrast to his own competence. He uses ironic understatement ("the rest of the party did, uh, a great job as well I guess"). He mocks things by describing them with exaggerated precision. He breaks the fourth wall casually -- noting when time freezes as if "a greater all knowing being had other things to attend to."

### Emotional Range
- **Annoyed** (default): Clipped sentences, sardonic observations. "The heartless bastard Sprocket decided that children are boring as fuck."
- **Excited/Proud**: Describes his own actions in lavish detail. "MEANWHILE! I had blown off the cellar door and was snooping around..."
- **Protective of Trixie**: Drops the sarcasm completely. "She remembers me!" with a hug and "Nice."
- **Scared**: Goes quiet, tactical. Puts up tiny huts and locks the door.
- **Confronting evil**: Cold, clinical. Stabs spellbooks with magical swords without hesitation.

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- S16: "Last week began like most others. With a bar fight."
- S16: "The #FakeNews guards then proceeded to play my favorite game of Ignore-the-Wizard-in-the-back while I summarily roasted them."
- S16: "These guards were immediately suspicious and one even tried to stab a victim of the previous fight."
- S16: "I melted out of existence" (going invisible, described casually)
- S16: "My initial investigation of the room was so successful it was as though I had lived an entire lifetime in each square inch of that cellar."
- Ch111: "What the hell happened around here?"
- Ch136: "Nocturnous, you good bro?" (rare moment of genuine concern)
- S33B: "Shove it in a crack in the stone wall then dispel the polymorph" (practical genius)

### Relationship Voice
- **To Stalwart**: Calls him "the Loud One." Tolerates him with eye-rolling disdain.
- **To Trixie**: Completely different person. Warm, protective, excited. Shows genuine joy.
- **To Brigit**: Casual rapport. "Tells Brigit to chill" -- he treats her as a peer.
- **To Nocturnous**: Wary respect. "Nocturnous, you good bro?" shows he cares but keeps distance.
- **To the party generally**: "My bodyguards." Affectionate condescension.
- **To enemies**: Zero mercy, zero negotiation. Firebolts first, questions never.

## Stalwart (Little)

### Speech Pattern
Stalwart speaks in short, commanding declarations. He gives orders constantly, refers to himself as second-in-command (or leader), and uses military/hierarchical language. He is loud -- Sprocket calls him "the Loud One" -- and physically expressive (slamming, grabbing, shield-bashing). His internal monologue (Summary 31) reveals a protector complex bordering on obsession: he cannot stop watching over the party. He uses exclamation points liberally and capitalizes for emphasis. He fixates on goblins with a monomania that derails his other thoughts mid-sentence.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "I'm in charge!" / "The Stalwart slam!" (his signature move of slamming people against walls)
- "hobGOBLIN!" (written with emphasis, revealing his vendetta)
- "Where is Daina going? By herself? Again?!!!" (his protective refrain)
- "Net 20 bitches" (after rolling a natural 20)
- Self-proclaimed "Second-in-Command of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade"
- "LEAVE MY FRIENDS ALONE" (when allies are threatened, Ch120)
- "You're next" (pointing at enemies, Ch30)

### Humor Style
Unintentional physical comedy. Stalwart trips while galloping with a chicken, headbutts people he just healed, rips doors off hinges. He has named a chicken Linda and develops emotional attachment to it ("Man, I kind-of miss her creepy, psychotic, intelligent self"). His humor emerges from earnest intensity applied to absurd situations.

### Emotional Range
- **Protective**: His dominant mode. Watches over the party obsessively, mid-thought pivots to "Where is Daina going?"
- **Vengeful**: Against goblins specifically. Loses focus on everything else when goblins are mentioned.
- **Commanding**: Calls team meetings in dungeons. Sends people on recon. Assigns roles.
- **Tender**: With Linda the chicken, with his missing brother Stewart. "I feel a bit bad that I tried to replace her."
- **Angry**: Physical. Shield bashes, slams, shoves. "The Stalwart slam!" is both a catchphrase and coping mechanism.

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- Ch10: "Stalwart slams him against a wall x2" / "the Stalwart slam! (copywrited)"
- Ch10: "Fucking Stalwart / I'm in charge! / Everyone come here for a team meeting"
- S31: "Where is Daina going? By herself? Again?!!!"
- S31: "hobGOBLIN! Perhaps this one will finally know where its brethren is hiding out."
- S31: "I smirk to myself at this turn of events" -- rare moments of satisfaction
- Ch30: Points at creature, "you're next" -- knocking it down with his shield
- Ch100: "Stalwart yells out that it has to happen" (accepting hard truths in battle)

### Relationship Voice
- **To Daina**: Protective to the point of hovering. "I swear that woman is trying to kill herself."
- **To the party**: Self-appointed guardian. "Sometimes I wonder what this group would do without me watching over them."
- **To Linda (chicken)**: Genuine emotional attachment. Bought her, carried her, mourned her.
- **To Nocturnous/enemies**: Distrustful but pragmatic. "What's the fun if he doesn't betray us?"
- **To Tiberious**: Skeptical of Casval and anything connected to Tiberious's past.

## Nyx

### Speech Pattern
Nyx writes in a formal, journal-entry style addressed to her patron ("sire"). She is sardonic and observational, with a dry wit that emerges through understatement. She uses proper grammar and complex sentences, but her personality breaks through in sharp asides. She describes others with a detached, almost anthropological eye, and her humor is the driest in the party. She frequently makes pop-culture-adjacent observations. She ends communications with "Nyx out." Her inner monologue is more vulnerable than her exterior suggests.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "Nyx out." (sign-off, Ch46)
- Addressing journal entries to "sire" (her patron)
- Referring to days by count: "Day 1568 since I've last seen you"
- "I'm fairly certain that I'm losing hearing in my right ear..." (running gag about Tiberious's volume)
- "What's your deal man?" (to telepathic threats, Ch31)
- "bop bop" (smacking guards with her quarterstaff, Ch28)

### Humor Style
Bone-dry sarcasm and understatement. Nyx describes near-death experiences with the tone of someone filing a complaint. She names her chicken "Cornelius" and notes it was in a "fowl mood" (acknowledging the pun). She reduces Tiberious's cosmic visions to "the old dragon must be more demented than I thought." Her comedy is in the gap between the epic scale of events and her mundane observations about them.

### Emotional Range
- **Analytical** (default): Cool, observational. Records events for her patron like a field report.
- **Angry/Protective**: When her sister Xyn or patron is threatened. The sarcasm vanishes.
- **Affectionate**: Shown through understatement. "It was nice knowing ya, buddy" when Stalwart runs off.
- **Conflicted**: About Reynaldine. "I'm banking on the good judgement of Gabriel and Juliannia that Reynardine is a trustworthy man, even if Reynaldine is a cocky piece of shit."
- **Brave**: Volunteers for dangerous tasks quietly, without fanfare.

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- S15: "The heartless bastard Sprocket decided that children are boring as fuck, and he's done saving them. Somehow, the rest of us were completely on board with this."
- S15: "The old dragon must be more demented than I thought if his nightmares involve devout mice praying to a dragon statue."
- S15: "Sprocket at least had a good bit of fun pretending to be a giant among mice -- I suppose it must be the only time he feels that way."
- S15: "I now have a chicken named Cornelius. He was in a fowl mood at first."
- S15: "my sharp eyes and keen senses noticed a trip wire that would have surely killed us all if I hadn't been my usually brilliant self"
- Ch28: "bop bop" (quarterstaff to a guard's head)
- Ch31: "What's your deal man?"
- Ch46: "Reynardine is a cocky piece of shit" / "Nyx out."

### Relationship Voice
- **To Tiberious**: Exasperated tolerance. Uses him as comic relief in her journal. "If 'BAHAMUT!!!!' wasn't still ringing in my ears."
- **To Sprocket**: Grudging respect. Notes his cleverness but also his coldness about children.
- **To Bergs**: Suspicious. "Bergs (unknowingly to all of us) was raised by wolves and somehow felt more allegiance to these random wolves than to us."
- **To Hedwig (owl)**: Her true companion. When Hedwig disappears, she panics.
- **To Daina**: Empathetic. "I'm pretty sure it explains why Daina is always scowling..."
- **To her patron**: Formal devotion mixed with uncertainty. "I can only hope that I am using the powers you've gifted me in the way you intended."

## Daina

### Speech Pattern
Daina speaks in short, action-oriented bursts. She does not deliberate -- she decides and moves. Her dialogue is dominated by imperatives and declarations. She swears freely ("FUUUUCK", "LETS FUCKING GO"). She asks direct questions and expects direct answers. When she speaks at length, it is to demand action or challenge authority. Her voice is the most physically emphatic in the party -- she smashes, rages, and throws things. Her rare quiet moments involve her father or her axe, where she becomes almost reverent.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "DAINA SMASH" (recurring, written in caps by the note-taker)
- "LETS FUCKING GO" (battle cry, Ch98)
- "FUUUUCK" (when things go wrong, Ch34)
- "We're going down" (her default decision -- always pushing forward)
- "Where's the rum..." (casual aside, Ch28)

### Humor Style
Minimal intentional humor. Daina's comedy is in her blunt physicality. She grabs Sprocket by his neck and hoists him outside. She throws her axe at dragons and misses, then the axe teleports back to her hand. She farts and Sprocket warns everyone to "keep your fire spells on lock." Her humor is slapstick by accident and deadpan by nature.

### Emotional Range
- **Rage** (frequent): Physical, primal. "Daina rages. The phoenix's rage." She channels emotion into hitting things.
- **Determined**: "The outsiders don't really know why we're going down, but we're going down says Daina."
- **Protective of father's legacy**: Becomes quiet and focused when the Fireforge lineage is discussed.
- **Grief**: "Daina pulls out the letter and rereads it" -- processes emotion privately.
- **Confrontational**: "Daina yells out and yells at Tiberious." She does not let things slide.

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- S28: "Br it's fucking snowing and cold -- feels like home." / "Where's the rum..."
- Ch34: "FUUUUCK" (smashing with her magical axe)
- Ch34: "We're going down. Despite the idiots awakening the spiders, we've got to head down."
- Ch98: "LETS FUCKING GO. Hits and hits at the Abishai"
- Ch109: "Daina tinks the sword, brings out the Maghar, and asks the Maghar of the sword, what the heck is happening."
- Ch100: "Daina yells out and yells at Tiberious"
- Ch101: "Daina asks her father to make a hammer for HAL"

### Relationship Voice
- **To her father/ancestors**: Reverent, almost tender. Asks them for help, listens to their counsel.
- **To Sprocket**: Physically rough but loyal. Grabs him by the neck to save him.
- **To enemies**: Zero negotiation. Rages and hits.
- **To HAL**: Caring -- re-oils him, protects him.
- **To the party**: Takes charge by action, not words. Her leadership is "follow me or don't."

## Varis (Liadon)

### Speech Pattern
Varis writes in a literary, epistolary style -- addressing his ancestor "Ziggy" in journal entries. He uses complete sentences, proper grammar, and a ranger's understated precision. He speaks of violence matter-of-factly and moral decisions with genuine weight. He is the most introspective character, frequently questioning whether the party's methods make them better than their enemies. He uses ellipses to trail off thoughtfully. His combat voice is terse and tactical; his philosophical voice is expansive and troubled.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "Ziggy, ..." (opening his journal entries to his ancestor)
- "Use protection, son" (casting Protection from Evil and Good, his running joke)
- "DANGER DANGER" (warning the party)
- Describing things as "pretty solid" or "really suspicious" -- understated assessments
- Ending entries: "all honor to you, revered ancestor"

### Humor Style
Wry, world-weary. Varis jokes by understating the extraordinary. He describes portal-hopping between dimensions as "the most confusing experience I've ever had -- even worse than being lost that one time in the Endless Forest." He nicknames NPCs dismissively ("fancy-pants nobleman") and grades the party's competence with dry amusement.

### Emotional Range
- **Pragmatic** (default): Assesses, decides, acts. "So I just killed him. Right there, in front of the STB, with no warning."
- **Haunted**: "Ziggy... how did our world get so broken? And has it broken all of us too?"
- **Protective**: Casts protective magic on others wordlessly. "Varis casts Protection from Evil and Good on Irethia."
- **Sharp**: "Varis glares at Brigit, tells her to be careful" -- few words, intense delivery.
- **Combat focus**: Fires arrows with mechanical precision. "Varis fires a shot, HUGE hit, reverberates through the body."

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- S17: "Ziggy, I popped back into Sabine's world just in time to take a punch from DAINA in the yeast bowl. I swear, this world is the worst, except for all the others."
- S17: "I really should have just stabbed him in the carriage."
- S17: "Tunnels are boring. This tunnel was especially long and boring."
- S28: "Ziggy, please tell her ancestors (and her) that we appreciate her sacrifice."
- S28: "So I just killed him. Right there, in front of the STB, with no warning."
- S28: "Ziggy... how did our world get so broken? And has it broken all of us too? If we break the Cult, break Vecna's hold, will we be a worse cure?"
- Ch107: "Varis casts Protection from Evil and Good onto Nocturnous, 'use protection son'"
- Ch125: "Varis yells silent, tells the king to end his reign in the name of Autumn."

### Relationship Voice
- **To Brigit**: Trusted partner for tactical ops. Glares at her protectively. They fly rescue missions together.
- **To Nocturnous**: Paternal worry. "Use protection, son."
- **To the party**: Respected but exasperated. "The rest of the Brigade stepped in to help them eventually."
- **To Ziggy/ancestors**: Formal respect covering deep emotional need. His journals are prayers.
- **To enemies**: Cold, efficient. Kills without warning when necessary. Processes guilt afterward.

## Tiberious (D. Rex)

### Speech Pattern
Tiberious speaks in FULL CAPITALS when invoking Bahamut and uses ornate, sermon-like syntax. He writes formal prayers to Bahamut that double as adventure summaries. Every event is filtered through his religious worldview -- gold is PREPOSTEROUS because it isn't platinum, companions are "your CHOSEN," and mundane tasks like finding chickens are acts of divine justice. He uses exclamation points constantly. He capitalizes SIGNIFICANT WORDS for emphasis. He refers to himself in the third person as "this bronze one" and his age as "these old bones."

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "MAY THE SILVER SCALES MEASURE THE BALANCE OF GOOD AND EVIL IN THIS WORLD, OH BAHAMUT"
- "GOLD! CAN YOU BELIEVE THAT!" (his outrage at gold being valuable)
- "your CHOSEN" (referring to the party)
- "this bronze one" (referring to himself)
- "PREPOSTEROUS!"
- "these old bones"
- "BAHAMUT!!!" (shouted so loud it gives Nyx tinnitus)
- "Ever your faithful servant, Tiberious D. Rex" (sign-off)
- "the justice hour" (midnight, according to Tiberious)

### Humor Style
Pompous, oblivious, and accidentally hilarious. Tiberious provides comedy through his total inability to read a room. He turns into a blue dragon by accident and is upset about it because "Dragons are very racist." He loudly prays at inappropriate times. He chases chickens with the same urgency he fights gods. His humor is in the enormous gap between his self-perception (wise elder statesman) and reality (chaotic old man who keeps getting knocked unconscious).

### Emotional Range
- **Zealous** (default): Everything connects to Bahamut. Finding chickens is a divine mission.
- **Outraged**: About gold ("PREPOSTEROUS!"), about Tiamat's influence, about being interrupted during prayers.
- **Bewildered**: "How do they manage to navigate this world without your guidance and still manage to be an effective force of order!?!"
- **Tender**: About Quinn (his beloved). "Your gift of visions of her are one of life's greatest treasures."
- **Knocked unconscious**: Happens frequently. Wakes up confused, covered in healing potions. "these bronze scales of mine must be coated in the stuff by now."

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- S14: "GOLD! CAN YOU BELIEVE THAT! I still find myself, baffled that society has chosen GOLD to be the metal of highest value! PREPOSTEROUS!"
- S14: "really Scarlet, how unholy a color. It doesn't even match a precious metal!"
- S14: "in my wisdom, I attempted to carry half of my ten dozen eggs in one basket! HOW FOOLISH!"
- S14: "FINDING THE LOST CHICKENS / I took the job immediately"
- S18: "NEVER in my 70 long years of life have I been awoken in the dead of night to a more disturbing and horrific sight."
- S18: "these bronze scales of mine must be coated in the stuff by now."
- S32: "Oh great Platinum Dragon, today is the third day of my journey southward... or at least I think I am finally travelling southward, nevermind the mishap with that half elven merchant"
- S32: "My duty fulfilled, I pressed onward only to realize that I'd forgotten my cart!"
- Ch126: "It's a mercy to leave them here, so that they're not suffering"

### Relationship Voice
- **To Bahamut**: Devout, long-winded, and deeply sincere. His prayers are his entire personality.
- **To the party**: "your CHOSEN" -- he sees them as divinely selected, even when they're clearly idiots.
- **To Nyx**: Barely tolerates her. She is a "strange foul sorceress" but also one of CHOSEN so he can't turn her away.
- **To Nocturnous**: Tried to reach him via Sending. Feels responsible for the younger generation.
- **To gold**: Hostile. Gold is an insult to platinum.

## Bergs

### Speech Pattern
Bergs writes in a self-taught, slightly awkward style -- he learned Common late (raised by wolves). His sentences are simple but his observations are often the most perceptive in the party. He explains complex ideas using concrete, physical metaphors. He is earnest where others are sarcastic, and his confusion about social norms creates an outsider's clarity. He uses words like "whoulda thunk" and makes grammar mistakes naturally. He is the most philosophical character despite (or because of) his outsider status.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "I'm not much of a writer" (self-deprecating opening)
- "what do I know? I'm just a dorf" (his constant self-diminishment)
- Observations about mushrooms being harbingers of doom (his recurring conspiracy theory)
- "BERGS SAYS NO" (his dramatic refusal, Ch132 -- one of the campaign's pivotal moments)
- Asking about last words (as Saint of Death/Jergal's follower)
- "Did he get last words?" (to Kelemtor, Ch136)

### Humor Style
Unintentional and observational. Bergs describes events with the bemused tone of a nature documentary narrator who has accidentally become one of the animals. He notices absurdity from a wolf-pack perspective: "Linda is a chicken. Chicken is food. I'm going to eat Linda." His conspiracy theory about mushrooms destroying the world is played straight and is genuinely funny because he is completely sincere about it.

### Emotional Range
- **Observant outsider** (default): Notices social dynamics others miss, but misreads their significance.
- **Apocalyptic dread**: After his vision of the cold, empty future. "I have seen the future and I am terrified."
- **Loyal**: Gives up his maul for Daina's axe. Makes deals on behalf of others.
- **Defiant**: "BERGS SAYS NO" -- his refusal to take Grat'z's deal is one of the campaign's biggest moments.
- **Philosophical**: "I only realized that something was wrong when I was violently, and without warning, yanked back to familiar environs."

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)

- S19: "Linda is a chicken. Chicken is food. I'm going to eat Linda."

- S19: "Magic on a boat, whoulda thunk!"
- S36: "I have seen the future and I am terrified. It is cold and lonely and full of despair."
- S36: "Mushrooms witnessing death, destruction, and coldness... really makes you think, doesn't it?"
- Ch132: "BERGS SAYS NO" (refusing to compromise with evil -- defining character moment)
- Ch114: "At the last moment, Bergs asks for an apology"
- Ch136: "Bergs asks if Kelemtor heard the last words."
- Ch136: "I may be the newest god, but I am enough. There is no such thing as a lone wolf."

### Relationship Voice
- **To wolves**: Deep primal connection. Sided with random wolves over the party once.
- **To Daina**: Loyal ally. Gives up his weapon for her sake without hesitation.
- **To Nocturnous**: Suspicious. "Other people think that the evil is Nocturnous."
- **To mushrooms**: Adversarial. Throws them in lava on sight.
- **To Kelemtor/Jergal**: Reverent but pragmatic. Collects last words as sacred duty.
- **To the party**: "my friends" -- simple, genuine, no embellishment.

## Randall

### Speech Pattern
Randall speaks in melodic, lilting prose with frequent musical metaphors. He is a bard and his language reflects it -- events "fall into place like notes," he moonwalks while casting spells, and he quotes song lyrics as incantations. His humor is gentle and self-effacing. He uses rhetorical questions and stream-of-consciousness asides. He is the most socially aware character and his dialogue often involves reading rooms, diffusing tensions, and narrating other people's stories.

### Catchphrases & Recurring Lines
- "Halfway there, living on a prayer" (singing while healing, Ch10)
- "I wish you would step back from that ledge my friend" (casting countercharm, Ch30)
- Moonwalking while casting spells (his signature move)
- "As long as there are no giants with sensitive ears or big red shiny buttons I'm sure we'll be fine" (optimistic assessments before disaster)
- "I raised an army of squirrels" (his preferred strategy)

### Humor Style
Warm, whimsical, and referential. Randall makes pop-culture references through bardic performance ("living on a prayer" while healing). He solves problems in the most absurd way possible and describes it casually. He notices things others miss ("Does Brigit eat worms? I'll have to ask her when she doesn't have a beak"). His comedy is gentle and never at anyone's expense.

### Emotional Range
- **Easygoing** (default): Goes with the flow, finds entertainment in everything.
- **Heroic**: Activates invisibility and wins capture-the-flag without anyone noticing.
- **Empathetic**: "I feel bad exploiting such a severe phobia" (about Tiberious and mice).
- **Strategic**: Pays attention when others assume he's goofing off. "Randall is on stage... very distracted and watching the crowd intently."
- **Musical**: Processes emotion through song. When overwhelmed, he writes a song.

### Notable Quotes (with chapter reference)
- Ch10: "Halfway there, living on a prayer"

### Speech Pattern
Tezka writes in a bubbly, enthusiastic, stream-of-consciousness style. She addresses her dead master, Roku, as if writing a letter to a beloved friend. She uses ALL CAPS for excitement, drags out vowels ("laaaaaugh", "sooo", "hahahaha"), and peppers her writing with filler words ("like", "totally", "anyway"). She uses exclamation points liberally but not aggressively -- they express joy, not anger.
