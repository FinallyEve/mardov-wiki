Summary Chapter 18 – Tiberious’ Account:

MAY THE SILVER SCALES MEASURE THE BALANCE OF GOOD AND EVIL IN THIS WORLD, OH BAHAMUT THIS IS MY ACCOUNT OF GOOD DEEDS AS THIS BRONZE ONE HAS PERFORMED IN YOUR NAME,

PLEASE LEND YOUR EAR TO AN OLD MAN IN THE TWILIGHT OF HIS LIFE, SO I MAY TIP THE SCALES ONCE AGAIN FROM DISCORD TO ORDER:

Excuse this bronze ones previously unfinished prayer, we were rudely awakened from within Randall’s strange walking hut. Another denizen of the depths managed to slip into the chamber and spirited away of the bodies from our last encounter. Even agents of chaos deserve some funeral rites, let's hope his soul is judged righteously!

This did however shed your light on a rather large oversite in our plans to rest, without some manner of hiding our strange hut, someone would need to take watch. And it takes a denizen of the depths to keep watch, thus Nocturnus Wayne decided to post first watch, did you know he only needs 4 hours of sleep? However clearly this hut would not do, despite us mentioning this to the rest of the party, only Nyx, Nocturnus Wayne, Sprocket, and Bergs had the presence of mind to leave the hut.

I do not know what manner of foul magic caused the tragedy that shortly followed but the hut began to sick INTO THE FLOOR! Was this some form of underdark magic? Did they go to this "Other Side?"

We retreated into some convenient barracks branching off from the chamber, I retired to my room to resume my payer and ask for guidance to these great mysteries!

I was however... interrupted by Nyx, the strange foul sorceress decided that it was safer to near your influence and it is not my place to turn away one of your CHOSEN!

It was then that we were abruptly awakened by our ever vigilant Wayne.

NEVER in my 70 long years of life have I been awoken in the dead of night to a more disturbing and horrific sight.

The Duregar woman in the other room wore the sigil of the NEMISIS OF THE GODS herself, Tiamat...

Clear as day the woman displayed her sacrilegious blight, as if she were PROUD of the of spreading Tiamat's dominion! I could feel the rage that had once plagued me as lost young man returning! The influence of the goddess of vengeance and greed is not something that has harrowed me for very long time... Wielding Tiamat's tainted favor, the Duregar conjured a wall of wind to rebuke my every attempt at destroying this lost soul.

My advance thwarted, my every attempt to batter through left me a bit more exhausted as if not resting on top of the pure nightmare unwinding around me had zapped all the energy I had left, until everything faded to red ... and then black.

I awoke to find the injuries I do not remember receiving, had begun the quick but painful process of being repaired by the primitive potions we had picked up in town. I do not remember how I was knocked unconscious, I only remember that broach and being overwhelmed by zealous wrath.

Yet again your chosen have performed miracles as when I came to, I discovered the body of that tainted soul, I attempted to re-consecrate the ground by performing your burial rights on the body, but it will be some time before true peace can be found in those tunnels.

My prayer was ONCE AGAIN interrupted by a knock at the door, this time it was a much younger drow, though his eyes had that same dead blank stare, the influence of Tiamat to be certain. Before I had a chance to bless this child, he plead for sanctuary from someone he claimed to be his older brother and some other creature that seemed to frighten him more.

As if summoned by our words the brother appeared, this one fully taken by Tiamat’s foul grasp!

Clearly Tiamat's blight in this room interrupts any amount of prayer in your name, no matter, we dispatched the possessed creature but not before learning what had possessed him...

A MIND FLAYER, the twisted creature of legend had a power we were not fully prepared to deal with! The last thing I remember was suffering an invading force in my mind, picking apart my scattered memories, and turning out the lights!

Again, I was awoken by the burning pain of a healing poultice, these bronze scales of mine must be coated in the stuff by now. The mind flayer thwarted the question on everyone's mind was what fresh terror would await us down the corridor! And following your light, I lead the way back to the origin or so much evil, making a point to leave the weeping boy back in the chamber to mourn the loss of his brother and flee these blighted tunnels!

It was there that we happened upon the reason for this prayer, a teleportation circle inside a cramped and clearly well used study. But it is not purely the existence of this portal that warrants my plea, no it is the vision we gathered by sleeping within the circle.

I knew almost immediately upon entering this austral state, that we had happened upon the most significant premonition of modern faith! Thousand? Millions? I cannot say, but the rank and file of duregar stretched farther than these eyes could see! But the sheer number of them is not quite the reason for this message, no! it is that they were worshipping a deity in unison.

Luckily this vision was sent to me by your grace, as you already know the hellscape of which I speak. This was the moment you lead me to the CHOSEN to see! The silver scales must be alerted, and every clergyman of the pantheon must be mobilized for this was clearly the beginning of the final war, the one to kill a god and bring balance, either good or evil to MARDOV!

WE MUST WIN! WE MUST PREPARE! THE ENEMY IS ALMOST UPON US! I hate to leave your CHOSEN again but I know that I will find them and that they will be instrumental in thwarting Tiamat and his invasion!

MAY YOUR PLATINUM NAME RING THROUGH THIS LAND FOR ALL TIME OH AGELESS ONE!

~ YOUR VASSEL AND MESSENGER,

Tiberious D. Rex
