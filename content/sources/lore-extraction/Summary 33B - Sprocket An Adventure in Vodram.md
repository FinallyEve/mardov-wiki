Summary 33B: Sprocket’s Account of an adventure in Vodram

Session DM: Scott Petersen

PCs: Tezka, Nocturnis, Irethia, and Sprocket

Our heroes found themselves in the unfortunate realm of Vodram this time. A new experience for some, but sadly familiar for others who were more accustomed to the plane. They appeared in front of a battle worn fort known as Ryre Citadel. Varis rushed out to meet the party.

Varis was accompanied by a Kenku friend named Clicker. Clicker was initially wary of the otherworldly visitors, but after Varis vouched for their valor he lightened up. Varis was noticeably relieved that well equipped party members had arrived. He quickly explained that a small brigade of resistance soldiers had gone missing in one of the several tunnels that led to the city of Kholkoss. Needing every capable hand they could muster, we were to go save this Captain Mack and his recruits while Varis and Clicker stayed to defend Ryre Citadel.

Varis explained that the “Eye of Vecna” in Kholkoss was a creature named Degarre and that he may send all manner of foes our way to stop us from reaching the lost party. Never to be dissuaded, we headed out towards the shorter of two tunnels and began our search. Partway through this tunnel, we came across two dead corpses that had been mauled by some fierce creature and a fork in the tunnel. After consulting the vague map that Varis drew, we knew that the side route we had come upon did not lead to Kholkoss but still very well might hide danger in its depths. The team decided to string up the bodies on the cave ceiling at the intersection and set a cunning tripwire trap below them in an effort to be notified of intruders from behind as they moved along the main route.

After a few more hours in the main tunnel, the party came across Captain Mack and some recruits engaged in a devastating battle with a Behir. The Behir was a huge beast. Dark blue and serpent like with 12 legs. It could crack a man in two or swallow them whole. Just as a resistance recruit was broken in half, the party jumped into action. Nocturnis went directly in to wail on the beast while Irethia hatched a plan in her mind. With a loud crack and some sparks the Behir was nowhere to be seen and in its place was a small, slow, and unusually ugly baby sloth.

One of the most effective way to deal with huge scary beats, Irethia explained, is to simply polymorph them. The recruits and Mack were understandably petrified of such magic, but Sprocket and Tezka assured them that everything is ok. Sprocket then applied his impressive brain power to the situation and though of a beautifully simple way to dispose of the beast. “Shove it in a crack in the stone wall then dispel the polymorph” he suggested. After some further thought and planning, Tezka and Nocturnis found a small fissure in the cave ceiling and wedged the polymorphed Behir into it.

Everyone was told to back away at least 40 ft and then Irethia unceremoniously dispelled her polymorph. With a huge bang and plenty of falling rubble, a severely injured Behir plummeted 15 ft from the ceiling. It only took 3 further attacks to put the massive beast out of its misery.

After regrouping, the party plus Captain Mack and his recruits headed back to their rendezvous at Citadel Ryre. On the way, they noted that their earlier tripwire trap had been sprung without anyone noticing. Nocturnis and Irethia stealthed ahead of the party to scout for hidden dangers. They found members from the Cult of Vecna attempting to storm the gates of Citadel Ryre. Irethia and Nocturnis immediately engaged to prevent them from breaching the defenses.

Soon the party and the Captain Mack’s crew caught up and joined in the battle. The Vecna cultists along with some last minute reinforcements were easily dispatched by the two trained monks and magic wielders. Varis and Clicker then emerged from the Citadel unscathed to congratulate the party and award them with plenty of loot!

The end
