## Vecna's Four Zeniths

Vecna's conquest is organized into four "Zeniths" — each a domain of power:

| Zenith | Domain | Key Agents | Location/Target |
|--------|--------|------------|-----------------|
| **Pestilence** | Disease/Spell Plague | Rimefang, Garth, Umangi Shiva (Yuan-Ti), Essy Lin | Bynar Mountains, Winter's Zenith |
| **Death** | Undead/Necromancy | Mordred, Ned'zailok (Lich), Lord Soth, Salina, Reva, Tatiana | Machu |
| **Famine** | Starvation/Conquest | Reynaldine (Fey Lord), Logath HellEye, Gordan BlackHand, Grim'Nak, Jafar, Sledge | Tygras Logath |
| **War** | Military/Domination | Peraxis Phyrexia (Illithilich), Oblivion Wayne, Ebon Wayne, Ghost Eye Thorrin | Underdark, Vag'Mahar |
