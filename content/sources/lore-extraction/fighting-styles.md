## Brigit Wildfire

**Class**: Druid (Circle of Wildfire) -- high level (access to 7th+ level spells: Reverse Gravity, Transport via Plants, Polymorph into Air Elemental)
**Primary Stats**: WIS > CON > DEX (based on spellcasting focus, d8 hit die from Roll20, and survival in frontline chaos)

### Weapons & Equipment
- **Druidic spellcasting focus**: Her primary weapon is magic itself -- she rarely if ever swings a weapon
- **Wild Shape forms**: Bat (scout/travel), Spider (infiltration with Varis), Pangolin (healing cuddle mode), Stag (ceremonial/divine form as Chosen of the Stag)
- **Heart of the Faith**: Received from Fen, a divine relic

### Signature Abilities
- **Wall of Fire** (4th level): Her go-to battlefield control spell. Throws it up reflexively when threatened -- against beholders, around spider legs, to melt gold revealing hidden runes. She uses fire as a barrier, not just damage (Ch103, Ch108).
- **Conjure Animals -- Giant Spiders** (3rd level): Summons pairs of giant spiders to swarm enemies. Uses them as independent attackers while she repositions (Ch100).
- **Reverse Gravity** (7th level): Coordinated simultaneous casting with Sprocket -- both reverse gravity at the same moment, used to redirect projectiles mid-flight (Ch101).
- **Polymorph** (4th level): Transforms herself into an Air Elemental for flight/escape, and transforms allies -- notably polymorphing Sprocket into a spider so they can both ride on Varis's shoulder as spiders (Ch103).
- **Wild Shape**: Not used for combat tanking like a Moon Druid. Used for utility -- bat for scouting, spider for infiltration, pangolin for healing cuddles. The Stag form is ceremonial/divine.
- **Transport via Plants** (6th level): Her party's primary long-range teleportation method.
- **Feign Death** (3rd level): Cast on Varis when he was alone and about to be killed by Ned'zailok -- creative defensive use (Ch100).
- **Druidcraft**: Used creatively in non-combat -- carving maps, making leaves fly to reveal invisible creatures (Ch107).
- **Healing Word** (1st level): Quick-action battlefield healing, often reviving downed allies mid-combat.
- **Skywrite**: Used to broadcast warnings over entire towns -- "Evil coming northward, evacuate now" (Ch103).
- **Progenitor Form**: A dark, ethereal transformation -- an ancient, godlike alternate form with glowing energy cracks. Triggered during the Birthright tree event (Ch112).

### Combat Philosophy
**Battlefield architect and force multiplier.** Brigit does not stand in one place and blast. She reshapes the entire combat space -- walls of fire to split enemy formations, conjured creatures to create secondary fronts, polymorph to turn allies into unexpected forms, reverse gravity to weaponize physics. She is the player who looks at the map and thinks "how do I change what this room IS" rather than "what do I hit." When the party is in danger, she grabs people (literally grabbed Daina mid-fall from a dragon, Ch100) and creates escape routes. Her damage comes through environmental control, not direct attacks.

### Signature Moves (from player notes)
- Ch100: Conjures 2 giant spiders to attack the Casval eladrin while simultaneously running to Varis to cast Feign Death -- fighting on two fronts with summoned creatures while she personally saves an ally.
- Ch101: Simultaneous Reverse Gravity cast with Sprocket, redirecting an arrow at the peak of its arc -- precise, coordinated gravity manipulation.
- Ch103: Polymorphs Sprocket into a spider, then Wild Shapes into a spider herself, and they both ride on Varis's shoulder for stealth infiltration. Three PCs, one shoulder.
- Ch108: Casts Wall of Fire on 4 spider legs simultaneously, melting the gold covering to reveal hidden primordial runes -- using fire as archaeology.
- Ch112: Turns into a pangolin and heals the entire party to full health "with hugs and a cuddle puddle" -- the most Brigit possible use of a druid feature.
- Ch125: Stands behind Varis as a stag in front of the Winter Court army -- ceremonial intimidation through Wild Shape.

### Fighting Animation Notes
Brigit fights like a conductor, not a soldier. She stands back (or overhead, or in animal form on someone's shoulder), and the battlefield moves around HER choices. Her hands are always doing something -- throwing up walls of flame, shaping conjured creatures into position, reaching out to grab falling allies. When she uses fire, it is deliberate and architectural -- lines and walls, not explosions. Her Wild Shape transitions should be fluid and organic, shifting mid-motion from woman to bat to spider without breaking stride. In her Progenitor Form, she becomes still and terrible -- the chaos she normally creates externally now radiates from her body as cracking energy. She talks during combat -- shouting orders, casting Skywrite warnings, yelling at enemies. Never silent.

### Party Combat Role
**Controller / Support / Battlefield Architect.** She is the party's strategic brain in combat, reshaping the environment to give advantages. She also serves as the emergency extraction specialist -- Transport via Plants for retreat, Feign Death for faking death, polymorph for escape.

### Synergies
- **Varis**: Her most common combat partner. They infiltrate together (spider-on-shoulder), she buffs his combat (Healing Word to keep him shooting, Haste via Casaval coordination). She shapes the battlefield; he exploits the openings.
- **Sprocket**: Coordinated spellcasting (simultaneous Reverse Gravity). They think alike as high-level casters and double-team magical solutions.
- **Daina**: Brigit catches Daina when she falls (literally, Ch100). She creates the conditions (walls, conjured distractions) for Daina to rage safely into melee.

## Varis Liadon

**Class**: Ranger (likely Hunter or Gloom Stalker) -- Roll20 shows Ranger 1 base, but in-play he has access to Guardian of Nature (4th level, Ranger 13+), Silence, Wind Wall, Haste (via items/allies), Healing Spirit, and multi-attack. Likely level 15-17+.
**Primary Stats**: DEX > WIS > CON (archer ranger, proficiency in STR and DEX saves from Roll20)

### Weapons & Equipment
- **Longbow ("Horn")**: His primary weapon. Named weapon, fires magical arrows. Later upgraded with a bronze dragon bowstring (Ch112) that lets him shoot lightning or fire -- Call Lightning through arrows, AoE fire splash.
- **Curator's Plea**: A relic from the mammoth-hand Curator who pulled him from a grave (Ch100).
- **Wand of Fireballs**: AoE capability from an item, not a spell (Ch112, Ch121).
- **Quiver of magical arrows**: Glowing cyan/teal arrows, including the "arrow of the cephalopod" -- a tentacle-summoning arrow (Ch104).
- **Pseudo-dragon skull trinket**: His first kill trophy, carried always.

### Signature Abilities
- **Hunter's Mark** (1st level): Cast on nearly every target before firing. He marks, then unloads. Always the opening move (Ch104, Ch105, Ch108).
- **Guardian of Nature** (4th level): Transforms into a nature-empowered state, dramatically increasing his damage output. When he casts this, he becomes a machine gun -- "Hits and hits and hits at the dragon" (Ch100).
- **Multi-attack with Longbow**: His core loop. Mark, shoot, shoot, shoot. When he has Guardian of Nature or Haste active, the volume of arrows is overwhelming.
- **Silence** (2nd level): Tactical use for stealth approaches (Ch101).
- **Wind Wall / Wind Gust**: Creates barriers of wind to deflect projectiles -- "shield of wind to blow the rocks away" (Ch101).
- **Revivify** (3rd level): Emergency resurrection -- he carries diamonds and can bring back the dead.
- **Healing Spirit** (2nd level): Used creatively -- cast it as a "Patronus Tezka" for thematic healing (Ch124).
- **Bronze Dragon Bowstring upgrade** (Ch112): "Now Varis can pull lightning or fire. The lightning can shoot through somebody (Call Lightning) and the fire can do an AoE and splash." His bow becomes a multi-element weapon.

### Combat Philosophy
**Precision striker and volume shooter.** Varis has one mode in combat: acquire target, mark it, and fill it with arrows until it stops moving. He is not flashy or clever -- he is relentless. When Guardian of Nature is active, he becomes the party's highest sustained DPS by far. He positions at range, finds high ground or clear sight lines, and shoots. His tactical flexibility comes from his upgraded bow (lightning/fire arrows) and items (Wand of Fireballs for AoE), but his instinct is always "more arrows, faster." He does not retreat easily -- he holds position and keeps shooting.

### Signature Moves (from player notes)
- Ch100: Pulls out Horn and takes multiple shots at Ned'zailok, then casts Guardian of Nature and opens up on the dragon -- "Hits and hits and hits at the dragon." Pure volume fire.
- Ch104: Moves to clear a sightline, Hunter's Marks, and fires with the longbow -- "hits and hits." Then sprouts the arrow of the cephalopod for area denial.
- Ch105: Hunter's Mark, shoots with bow, NAT 20 -- "hits and hits and hits." Three hit descriptions in one sentence. This IS Varis.
- Ch108: Hunter's Marks Lolth herself, starts shooting -- "black icarus blood drips down from the wound." He shoots GODS.
- Ch112: Attacks Casaval with the bow, gets Silvery Barbs'd, fires again -- persistent through magical interference.
- Ch116: Casaval casts Haste on Varis -- and now the already-relentless shooter has an extra attack per round.

### Fighting Animation Notes
Varis is mechanical and efficient. He does not flourish or pose. He draws, nocks, aims, and releases in one smooth motion, then immediately repeats. His stance is grounded -- feet planted, shoulders squared, breathing steady. When Guardian of Nature activates, nature energy wraps around him (vines, wind, green glow) and his rate of fire visibly increases. His arrows should have distinct visual effects: cyan magical trails normally, crackling lightning or trailing fire with the dragon bowstring. He fires from distance and elevated positions when possible. He talks rarely during combat -- a called target, a grunt of effort, maybe a warning shout. His face is focused, not emotional. The pseudo-dragon skull on his belt swings with each draw.

### Party Combat Role
**Primary DPS / Ranged Striker.** He is the party's main damage dealer at range. With Hunter's Mark, Guardian of Nature, and Haste stacking, he outputs more consistent damage than anyone. He also provides emergency healing (Revivify, Healing Spirit) and utility (Silence for stealth ops).

### Synergies
- **Brigit**: She creates the conditions (fire walls splitting enemies, conjured creatures drawing attention) and he exploits the isolated targets with focused fire.
- **Sprocket**: They scout together on Sprocket's broom with Pass Without Trace. Sprocket handles magical threats; Varis handles physical ones.
- **Casaval**: Casaval Hastes Varis, turning him from dangerous to terrifying. This is a known and practiced combo.
- **Daina**: She holds the frontline to protect his shooting position. She tanks; he damages.

## Sprocket Overspark

**Class**: Wizard (Divination school -- has Portent) -- high level (access to Disintegrate 6th, Phantasmal Killer 4th, Counterspell 3rd, Arcane Lock 2nd, Shield of Faith). Likely level 15+.
**Primary Stats**: INT > DEX > CON (wizard, divination specialist, d4 hit die in Roll20 data but likely homebrew adjusted)

### Weapons & Equipment
- **Destiny** (magical sword): A silver-to-black gradient blade pulled from the chest of Envy. Extremely dangerous to Sprocket as a champion of Fate. Used to stab Ned'zailok's spellbook (Ch100).
- **Spellbook**: His most precious possession. Multiple factions have tried to steal it -- Casaval, Ned'zailok. Contains Mordred's spells after he stabbed Ned's book.
- **Flying Broom**: His primary mobility tool. Gets on the broom for aerial combat, scouting, and escape. Lost it once in a near-death (Ch102).
- **Potions**: Chugs Health and Shield of Faith potions before combat (Ch100). Always carries consumables.
- **HAL**: His warforged companion/construct -- not equipment exactly, but fights alongside him as a paired unit.
- **Knob**: His gnome artificer companion, provides trap-setting and melee support.

### Signature Abilities
- **Portent** (Divination Wizard feature): His defining ability. Uses precognitive dice to force outcomes -- makes sure Daina escapes safely (Ch100), forces attack rolls on enemies (Ch104). "Lily also portent's with an attack" -- other party members have Portent too, but Sprocket is the original.
- **Counterspell** (3rd level): His clutch defensive spell. Shuts down enemy casters at critical moments (Ch100, Ch108).
- **Disintegrate** (6th level): His big damage spell. Cast WITH a Portent to guarantee the hit -- "Sprocket moves behind the cover of the wall, and then attacks Vellum, disintegrate WITH A PORTENT" (Ch104).
- **Phantasmal Killer** (4th level): Fear-based single-target elimination. Used from broom-back against abishai (Ch100).
- **Arcane Lock** (2nd level): Locks doors to control the battlefield -- "impassable until the spell is fixed" (Ch100).
- **Reverse Gravity** (7th level): Coordinated dual-cast with Brigit (Ch101).
- **Pass Without Trace** (item or multiclass?): Used with Varis for stealth scouting (Ch101).

### Combat Philosophy
**Fate-manipulating glass cannon on a broom.** Sprocket's fighting style is defined by two things: he flies above the combat on his broom and rains destruction, and he uses Portent to guarantee that the most important moments go his way. He is also the party's anti-caster -- Counterspell shuts down enemy wizards. He is fragile (chugs potions pre-combat, takes shelter in towers) but enormously impactful. He thinks in terms of "what is the most important roll this fight" and uses Portent on that. His willingness to stab Ned'zailok's spellbook with Destiny -- destroying an irreplaceable magical artifact to deny it to the enemy -- shows he thinks strategically about the long game, not just the current fight.

### Signature Moves (from player notes)
- Ch100: Gets on his broom, Phantasmal Killer's the green abishai from the air, then Counterspells an enemy caster -- aerial magical superiority.
- Ch100: Portents a tentacle roll to guarantee Daina escapes safely -- using divination defensively for allies.
- Ch100: Stabs Ned'zailok's spellbook with the sword Destiny, denying Mordred's spells to the enemy permanently. Strategic destruction.
- Ch101: Simultaneous Reverse Gravity with Brigit, redirecting an arrow at its arc apex -- coordinated physics manipulation.
- Ch104: Moves behind wall cover, then Disintegrate WITH A PORTENT on Vellum the Beholder -- guaranteed annihilation from behind cover.
- Ch100: "Chugs some potions. Health and shield of faith, and then takes shelter in the tower. However upon seeing 5 abishai, comes right back up." -- retreats to buff, then comes right back out when needed.

### Fighting Animation Notes
Sprocket is a small gnome on a flying broom -- visually chaotic and fast. He zips around above the fight, hair wild, goggles reflecting spell-light. His casting is theatrical -- big hand gestures, crystalline mechanical wings flaring on his back. When he Counterspells, it is a sharp, cutting hand gesture that snaps the enemy's magic apart. When he Disintegrates, he points with both hands and a beam of green energy lances down from above. Portent moments should be shown as a brief pause -- his eyes flash with precognitive light, then he acts with absolute certainty. He chugs potions mid-flight, tossing empty vials behind him. He TALKS constantly during combat -- quips, warnings, tactical calls to HAL and Knob. His body language oscillates between "terrified glass cannon hiding behind a wall" and "reckless maniac flying directly at 5 abishai."

### Party Combat Role
**Arcane Artillery / Anti-Caster / Fate Manipulator.** He provides burst damage (Disintegrate), battlefield control (Reverse Gravity, Arcane Lock), and the single most important tactical ability in the party: Portent. He is also the counter-magic specialist.

### Synergies
- **HAL & Knob**: His personal combat squad. HAL throws bombs and removes masks; Knob sets traps and fights in melee. Sprocket provides air support.
- **Brigit**: Coordinated spellcasting -- they cast the same spell simultaneously for doubled effect.
- **Varis**: Scouting duo -- broom + stealth ranger. Varis shoots; Sprocket counterspells.
- **Daina**: Sprocket uses Portent to protect Daina when she is in danger (Ch100).

## Daina "Diesa" Fireforge

**Class**: Barbarian (Path of the Berserker or Ancestral Guardian -- "13 dwarves haunting" Reginald suggests Ancestral Guardian, but Reckless Attack and pure rage behavior suggest Berserker). High level.
**Primary Stats**: STR > CON > WIS (melee barbarian, axe-thrower, incredible toughness)

### Weapons & Equipment
- **Anchor of War** (greataxe): Her father Thorkon's legacy weapon. Ascended by plunging it into lava (Ch105). Contains a shard of her father's lifeforce (Ch101). Can "move mountains or boil the sea" (Ch101). Has one charge remaining in later chapters. Lights up when she gets angry (Ch101). Also known as the "Anchor of Yeet" when thrown (Ch108). Produces ancestral Maghar dwarves when "tinked" (Ch109).
- **Throwing capability**: She throws the axe at range -- "throws the axe at the dao, hits both times!" (Ch101). This is a returning weapon.
- **Heavy armor / rage**: Standard barbarian build. Climbs towers, rides on avatar's backs, dives into lava.

### Signature Abilities
- **Rage**: Her defining combat state. "Daina breaks free and rages" (Ch100). "Daina rages, hits Reginald, there are now 13 dwarves haunting him" (Ch104). "Daina is riding on Bigsby's hand and enters a rage" (Ch105). She rages as naturally as breathing.
- **Reckless Attack** (implied): The volume of hits per turn -- "starts swinging at the dragon. Hitting a lot." -- suggests Reckless Attack with advantage on every swing.
- **Ancestral Dwarves**: When she "tinks" the Anchor of War, Maghar dwarves appear as spectral guardians (Ch109). "13 dwarves haunting him" when she rages against Reginald (Ch104). This is either Ancestral Guardian barbarian or a weapon-specific feature.
- **Throwing the Anchor of War**: "Throws the axe at the Beholder" (Ch105). "Throws the Anchor of Yeet and hits Lolth" (Ch108). She can throw her magical greataxe at range and it returns.
- **Lava diving**: "Daina dived into the lava and ascended the axe" (Ch105). She is supernaturally tough.

### Combat Philosophy
**Berserker wrecking ball.** Daina has one mode: rage, close distance, and hit things with the biggest axe in the room until they stop moving. She climbs towers to reach dragons (Ch100), dives into lava to power up her weapon (Ch105), rides on Bigsby's Hand while raging (Ch105), and throws her axe at gods (Ch108, Lolth). She does not think tactically -- she thinks emotionally. Her rage is fueled by real anger (murdered father, stolen heritage, threats to family). She does not wait for the right moment; she creates the moment by being so aggressive that the enemy has to deal with her. "Daina hacks at the box holding Tiberius and Nocturnus and it cracks in two swings" -- she solves problems with axe damage.

### Signature Moves (from player notes)
- Ch100: Climbs up a tower to reach the dragon, still raging, and starts swinging -- "Hitting a lot." Doesn't wait for the dragon to come down; she goes up.
- Ch101: The Anchor of War lights up when she gets angry, she throws the axe at the dao -- "hits both times!" Emotional state directly powers weapon.
- Ch102: Hacks at the magical box holding Tiberius and Nocturnus -- "cracks in two swings." Brute force problem-solving.
- Ch104: Rages, hits Reginald -- "there are now 13 dwarves haunting him." Ancestral spirits manifest through combat fury.
- Ch105: Dives into lava to ascend the Anchor of War. Then plunges it into the volcano to make it dormant. Rides Bigsby's Hand while raging. Throws the axe at a Beholder.
- Ch108: "Throws the Anchor of Yeet and hits Lolth." She threw her axe at a GOD and named it accordingly.

### Fighting Animation Notes
Daina is power and fury. She is a stocky dwarf woman who fights like a natural disaster. Her swings are massive overhead arcs with the greataxe, using her whole body -- feet planted, hips rotating, arms extending fully. When she rages, her eyes glow amber/orange (matching her "Daina Reborn" portrait), and spectral dwarven ancestors flicker around her like heat haze. She CLIMBS things -- towers, monsters, avatars -- to get to enemies above her. She throws the axe overhand like a tomahawk, and it arcs back to her hand trailing orange light. She screams and shouts in combat -- dwarven war cries, curses at enemies, calls to Bergs. Her Anchor of War glows brighter the angrier she gets. She takes hits and keeps going -- she does not dodge, she endures.

### Party Combat Role
**Primary Tank / Melee DPS / Wrecking Ball.** She draws fire by being the most dangerous and aggressive thing on the field. She soaks damage with rage resistance while dealing enormous melee damage. She also provides the "break things with axe" problem-solving approach.

### Synergies
- **Bergs**: The Fireforge siblings fight as a unit. Both melee, both forge-themed weapons. They "bang their weapons and call the warforged to arms" together (Ch107).
- **Brigit**: Brigit creates the conditions (fire walls, distractions) and catches Daina when she overextends (literally catching her mid-fall, Ch100).
- **Stalwart**: Daina rides on Stalwart's avatar's back (Ch100). She redirected the avatar's attention to the dragon by "pushing up his head and pointing it" -- she literally aims the paladin.
- **Sprocket**: He uses Portent to protect her when she is in danger.

## Bergs "Barnabus" Blunderbeat

**Class**: Fighter (Eldritch Knight -- has Hex, Misty Step, erupts into fire) with possible Warlock multiclass (Hex is a warlock spell). Has Action Surge. High level.
**Primary Stats**: STR > CON > CHA/INT (melee fighter with spellcasting, heavy maul user)

### Weapons & Equipment
- **Maul of the Phoenix** (maul): His signature weapon through mid-campaign. Made the final blow on Reginald (Ch105). Melted in his hand during a major event (Ch107).
- **PeaceForger** (maul/hammer): Replacement weapon forged when Bergs and Daina "jumped in without looking" and Daina released Anemone -- "his maul turned into the PeaceForger" (Ch108). Later split back into the Maul of the Phoenix + an old sword + Kelemtor (Ch119). He brings weapons into existence through sacrifice.
- **White Hammer**: After the Phoenix maul melted, "arrives back up with a shiny new hammer, white like the white light of a forge" (Ch107). His weapons keep evolving.
- **Broom** (flying): Has access to a flying broom for mobility (Ch113).
- **Kelemtor**: His skull-faced death knight companion -- can be summoned, provides Locate Creature and Eldritch Blast at 300ft range (Ch112).

### Signature Abilities
- **Hex** (Warlock spell): Cast before melee attacks to add necrotic damage -- "casts hex on Timothy (DEX), and then hits with the Maul of the Phoenix" (Ch105).
- **Misty Step** (2nd level): His gap-closer. Uses it aggressively to teleport into melee range -- "Bergs walks and misty steps out of darkness, hits Reginald with his maul" (Ch104). "Bergs misty steps toward an enemy, action surge's and hits with his maul" (Ch104). "Bergs moves and misty steps to Lolth, then hits and hits" (Ch108).
- **Action Surge** (Fighter): His burst damage feature. Misty Step + Action Surge + Maul attacks is his signature kill combo (Ch104).
- **Erupting into fire**: "Bergs erupts into fire" (Ch104). Some Phoenix-themed fire ability, possibly tied to the Maul of the Phoenix.
- **Forging/Creating weapons**: Bergs makes things. He forges in combat downtime, he creates weapons from sacrifice, his weapons evolve through story beats. He is a weaponsmith who fights with his own creations.

### Combat Philosophy
**Misty-stepping wrecking ball with evolving weapons.** Bergs' fighting style is teleport-and-smash. He sees an enemy, Misty Steps into their face, and hits them with the biggest hammer he has, often Action Surging for a second full round of attacks. He pre-buffs with Hex for extra damage. His combat identity is inseparable from his weapons -- they are not just tools, they are characters in their own right (Phoenix, PeaceForger, Kelemtor). He is emotionally invested in every swing. The fight against Reginald is his defining combat: Misty Step through darkness, Action Surge, maul strikes while dwarven fire erupts from his body. He fights his father's killer with forge-fire fury.

### Signature Moves (from player notes)
- Ch104: Misty Steps out of magical darkness, hits Reginald with his maul. Then Misty Steps toward another enemy, Action Surges, hits with maul again. Two Misty Step + attack combos in one fight.
- Ch105: Made the final blow on Reginald, unleashing the Maul of the Phoenix. The kill-shot that defined his weapon.
- Ch108: Slams down PeaceForger and hears Muradin say "ow" -- his weapon is so connected to the divine forge that hitting things makes gods react.
- Ch108: Moves and Misty Steps to Lolth, then hits and hits -- teleporting into melee with a GODDESS.
- Ch113: Hexes Peraxis, flies over on the broom, and attacks -- "Sacrifices must be made, it has to be done." Deliberate, purposeful violence.
- Ch116: Sent to the Cradle on Mount Bynar to cleanse the Anchor of War and PeaceForger -- his weapon maintenance is a QUEST.

### Fighting Animation Notes
Bergs fights with a dwarf's low center of gravity and a forgemaster's deliberate strength. His Misty Step should be visually dramatic -- he dissolves into silvery mist, reforms RIGHT in the enemy's face, and is already mid-swing when he solidifies. His maul strikes are overhead slams, not side-swings -- bringing the full weight of the weapon down like a forge hammer on an anvil. When he erupts into fire (Phoenix mode), flames ripple outward from his body with each impact, and the maul head glows white-hot. He grunts with effort on every swing. He is stocky, grounded, and hits like a train. When he Hexes, his eyes briefly flash with dark energy before the necrotic curse settles on the target. His beard (massive, braided, red) swings with each hammer blow.

### Party Combat Role
**Melee Burst DPS / Teleport Striker.** He deals enormous spike damage through Misty Step + Action Surge + Hex + Maul combos. He is not a tank (that is Daina) -- he is an assassin with a hammer.

### Synergies
- **Daina**: The Fireforge siblings. They fight side by side, bang weapons together to rally warforged (Ch107). Both melee, both forge-themed. Daina tanks while Bergs bursts.
- **Kelemtor**: His summoned companion provides Locate Creature (finding targets for Bergs) and Eldritch Blast at 300ft (Ch112). Bergs is the melee half of a melee-ranged duo.
- **Sprocket**: Lily/Sprocket provides Portent to boost Bergs' attacks (Ch104).

## Stalwart Little Goldentooth

**Class**: Paladin (Oath of Vengeance, later Oath of Redemption) -- has Vengeful Glare, Smite, Shield spell, and divine avatar summoning. High level.
**Primary Stats**: STR > CHA > CON (paladin with heavy armor, shield, smite, and oath abilities)

### Weapons & Equipment
- **Shield of Redemption**: His defining item. Originally tied to his Oath of Vengeance, later corrupted and then redeemed. A sapphire shield that tracks enemies (Ch121). Rock-paper-scissors mechanics (Ch113).
- **Morningstar/Mace**: His melee weapon, paired with the shield.
- **Blade** (for Oath of Vengeance): "Attacks him with his blade" (Ch111). Secondary weapon for oath-powered strikes.

### Signature Abilities
- **Vengeful Glare** (Oath of Vengeance): His avatar casts it to paralyze enemies -- "casts vengeful glare and the dragon is currently paralyzed" (Ch100). Hold-person equivalent for oath targets.
- **Divine Smite**: "Josh double attacks Stalwart, but doesn't hit, and gets smacked by Stalwart" -- implied smite damage on counterattacks. "CRIT AND SMITE" from Josh suggests this is a known party mechanic (Ch112).
- **Shield** (spell): Casts Shield to block attacks -- "he casts shield and fails" (Ch112, against Josh's attack on his shield).
- **Avatar Summoning**: Summons a massive divine avatar of his oath. "Stalwart's avatar climbs upward, with Daina on his back" (Ch100). The avatar is large enough to carry a dwarf barbarian on its back.
- **Oath Declarations**: "I, Stalwart Little, declares redemption upon your death" (Ch121). His combat is ceremonial -- he announces his oaths and intentions formally.
- **Oath Transitions**: Started with Oath of Vengeance (for Steward Little's death), Bane tried to grant him peace from vengeance (Ch112), eventually shifted to Oath of Redemption with the Shield of Redemption (Ch121).

### Combat Philosophy
**Oath-bound divine knight.** Stalwart does not fight for himself -- he fights for vows, for the dead, and for justice. Every swing is a prayer.
