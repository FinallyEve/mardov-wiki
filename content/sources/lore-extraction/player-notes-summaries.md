# Mardov Campaign - Player Summaries

Source: Mardov DnD Reference Guide
Total summaries: 38

These are player-written recaps of sessions, each from a specific character's perspective.

---

## Summary 0 and 1

Summary of Chapters 0 and 1:

Last time, our adventurers found themselves having signed up for a contract as new recruits of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, despite not remembering when or why,

Their first day on the job, after training in the recruit yard their introduction was cut short by the arrival of Jorgen, a human ranger wearing a gold tunic, being pursued by Kobolds. He claimed that the Kobolds had followed him across town from the sewers and had killed his previous set of recruits. Luckily for Jorgen, the new recruits took care of the flying kobolds while he was able to run in the hall.

But not before Dallor Dallorbill was able to swipe a note written to Jorgen from Finkle with the following information written on it:

- The precise time of when the recruits were set to arrive at their first day in the Yard
- The names and classes of each of the recruits

Finkle finished his introduction to the Scarlet Tunic Brigade but not before asking mysterious questions about cults of the Raven Queen. Broden Rootbeard received an inspiration event point and the following information about the Raven Queen Cult:

1) that they used Necromancy and Animancy as worship

2) that they viewed Death as a steroid, and life as potential energy

3) Look behind their eyelids for members of this cult, as they wish to always sleep under deaths gaze (this is play on, If I die before I wake)

It was then that the new recruits found themselves swept along into the sewers of the Middle Ward. The reason, to activate an ancient ritual housed in a chamber below the city ruins. It was said that the city of Argos was built upon the old city of Argos and that there were 4 sources of ancient magic that needed to be collected.

1) red blood, hot to the touch

2) green poison, viscous as sludge

3) orange mercury, probably actually molten iron

4) blue, liquid souls.

Upon arrival at the sewers, the adventurers descended the ladder into the sewer waters below. Jorgen pulled a lever on the other end of the hallway and entered the room. Immediately upon entry the door slammed shut and the sewer entrance was sealed. the lever when pulled later lead to nothing, as if it had already served its purpose Alone in the dark the party light up some torches and revealed a horde of Kobolds. The Kobolds were originally just staring at the party until, Daina (not Sprocket), splashed sewer sludge on one of them. Provoked and pissed the Kobolds readied for attack, that was when Stalwart Little stepped in to intimidate them in Draconic.

Something about the Dragonborn was able to keep the Kobolds at bay despite their eerie chanting of "SCARLET, SCARLET, SCARLET" and following the party down the hall.

It was there that the party found the door and lever previously used by Jorgon. They then pulled the lever to no avail and discovered the words "DONT PRESS" scratched into the door with blood in the trails. The party then decided to search the door for a handle and PULLED the door open, Nocturnus Wayne was the first to enter, but as soon as he joined Jorgen on the other side of the hall (who was slipping is way out of the sight of the Kobolds, and consequently the party) the door slammed shut. It was then that it was discovered that there was a pressure plate on the other end which shut the door and kept it locked, then Broden held the door open as everyone rushed through and left the nest of Kobolds alone.

In the other room was a large depression in the floor and a blood rune on a button, when activated the blood rune starts a counter of 180s which is counting down until when the ceiling will fall and the door will unlock below the button. However, if the button is pressed before the ceiling does, then 4*the number of presses kobolds would enter the room. The party fought the kobolds and discovered another clue also scratched onto a surface, this surface being the rapidly approaching ceiling, "RUN LEFT",

This prompted Stalwart, Nyx, Daina and Brigit, to run into the left wall and fade through just in time for the door to open, kobolds to see this and the ceiling crushed them. A small band survived and had to be fought back by Sprocket, Broden, Dallor, Tiny Rick and Nocturnus Wayne after the ceiling had descended above them but the floor opened up to a spiral staircase drenched in a river of blood from the smooshed kobolds. Meanwhile on the other end of the wall, Stalwart, Nyx, Daina and Brigit find Jorgen and Finkle’s Half Ogres henchmen Stromble and Grom or Gromble and Strom in a control room pulling levers (priming the ritual chamber). They are caught off guard by the sudden arrival and struggle to explain why they are here (clearly lying). This half the party slips through another wall into the ritual chamber mentioned by Finkle earlier.

It was at his time that 3 things happened almost simultaneously,

1) Jorgen blew a whistle which cause Kobolds to rush into the room, and Grom/Strom put a hand on Stalwart who was more than suspicious by this point.

2) Stalwart kicked off Strom/Grom and used their bodies as springboard into Jorgon and putting his back to the ritual/kobolds and his eyes on the 2 Half-Ogres.

3) The other half of the party followed the river of blood into the ritual chamber just in time see the latter half of event 2.

The ritual chamber is a massive open chamber with a steeped roof and four endless waterfalls of orange, red, blue and green liquid/psuedo-liquid into a grate below. In the center of the room on top of a massive gear-like pedestal sits a Phylactery of the Raven Queen. Under the pedestal is grooved with tracks from the center to each of the 4 streams. Additionally, there are mysterious markings on the tracks. along the walls to the room are a total of 12 tunnels to Kobold nests.

Note that the control room is left of where the party following the staircase entered the room. Across the chamber is a steel door with rings in the shape of The Raven Queen's Crest!

It is there that we find our heroes right this moment, betrayed, confused, and wrapped up in a web of cultist intrigue and faced with a choice, complete the ritual or fight their way out...

## Summary 2

SUMMARY of Chapter 2 The Gnome Eff’n Way:

Last week we found our heroes the midst of an epic battle beneath the sewers of Argos. Before combat we discovered that Finkle Hollingsworth was actually a Lich named Mordred who chose to reveal himself to you all and command Stalwart Little to kill Jorgon because he had outlived his usefulness as a mercenary/kobold trainer. After 2000 souls, Mordred finally had enough souls to complete the ritual of rebirth, a ritual that requires the large phylactery. The phylactery contains a Demi-Lich of current unknown origin and allegiance dunked beneath the four endless steams of liquid essence:

1) RED blood, hot to the touch

2) ORANGE mercury mixed with molten iron

3) BLUE liquid souls

4) GREEN poison, corrosive like acid

Finkle underestimated the ingenuity of the adventuring party. First, the party decided to spare Jorgen and listen to his warning to not complete the ritual. Once again being able to speak Draconic came in handy, as Stalwart could convince the angry Kobolds to forgo their need for revenge on Jorgen for imprisoning them and decide to join in the fight against Mordred and his two half-ogre flunkies, Gromble/Stromble and Strom/Grom.

Mordred was less than amused and decided to collect the last souls needed himself. He raised his gnarled fingers and every Kobold in the room was immediately drained dry, their corpses contorted and fused together to form an army of Ghouls, hungry for flesh. He also revealed the enslaved bodies of three of his last batch of adventurer's, Corramperre a Tiefling Sorcerer and two unnamed meat shields.

During all this hullaballoo, Daina notices the large crystalline Demi-Lich skull inside the phylactery and Sprocket attempts to touch the suspended object using mage hand. As soon as the magic connection was made with the Demi-Lich, Sprocket was thrust into a deep trance. By all accounts this feat would normally have resulted in Sprocket’s soul being immediately consumed by the being of pure necromancy.

Brotor and Tiny Rick made quick work of Stromble/Gromble only to dismay as Grom/Strom pushed the phylactery of the Demi-Lich underneath the first liquid, RED blood. The liquid mixed with the bubbling semi-clear liquid that was already occupying the container, however the massive vial never overflowed despite the endless waterfall that continued to pour into it.

After receiving a knock on the back of the head from Nocturnus Wayne, searing the half ogres face on contact, Nyx tried to disrupt the order of the ritual by pulling the wrong lever, causing the phylactery to reject the green acid, creating a large pool on the floor.

Just when things looked to be turning grim, Dallor Dallorbill decided to cut a deal with Mordred to save himself from being murdered before the ritual. The ritual inched ever closer to being completed as the already risen adventurers pulled the orange lever.

The adventurers turned their efforts towards shattering the phylactery encasing the Demi-Lich. Sprocket dealt the first blow, creating a small crack in the eye of the Raven Queen’s sigil Daina also managed to widen the crack, that only invoked Mordred’s anger. The barbarian was obliterated by a powerful spell from the phylactery. Despite this loss, the party managed to destroy the phylactery shortly after. The Demi-Lich was freed and immediately the Ghouls turned on their former master, as if the balance of power in the room shifted. The Demi-Lich lashed out at Mordred, and the only witness to this was Stalwart who had decided to play dead instead of joining them. Stalwart completed the ritual and a bright light engulfed the room.

## Summary 3 and 4(1)

Summary of Chapter 3 and 4 Act Unnatural:

Daina waved her hex bag around the arena and announced herself as one of the awakened. The result being she was immediately rag dolled by Mordred's ghastly visage, her body removed from the gladiator battle as if lifted by the air itself.

As soon as she left the arena, the command to begin the Gladiator Battle was issued. The prize was to be selected to serve as Mordred's next vessel. The last vessel, Finkle Hollingsworth is mysteriously missing.

The awakened quickly learned to tell friend from foe by looking at the lights in their eyes. The first targets were easy choices as there were plenty of ghouls to keep everyone busy. There was however, unfinished business between Stalwart Little, Nyx, Nocturnus Wayne and the accused turn clock, Dallor Dallorbill.

Dallor was quick to plead for his life, claiming that he was only trying to save his skin when Mordred clearly had everyone dead to rights. It was, after all the smart thing to do. Those were Dallor’s last words as he was quickly knocked unconscious by his former party.

Luckily, Brotor Rootbeard decided to honor those old ties and stabilize Dallor's condition.

Meanwhile the ghouls seem to be searching out the awakened and attacking the same target. Whoever draws the attention of the undead crowd is immediately targeted. Realizing this, some members of the awakened, like Bergs, an agent of the Artificer’s Guild and bloodthirsty fighter, tried camouflaging themselves in Ghouls’ blood.

But there was more that. The party found out the hard way as a brilliant move by Brotor caused all eyes to follow a magic luminescent pair of manacles was thrown into the center of the arena. There, etched into the sand to avoid detection, spelled the markings of a long-distance teleportation spell gate.

It was then that the arena shook with a blast of green flames that seemed to snap into being. Under the light of the blast, Brigit noticed that the audience of 2000 ghouls had trickled down to a mere 200. With the audience literally decimated, the farce of a match continued as the gladiator ghouls began targeting the teleportation gates. The gates were arranged in an X around the arena to mark the spot for an invasion which began the day that Corrumpere and Bergs gave their lives delivering Xerxes’ amplification hex bags to as many of the awakened as they could.

Out of the five gates, three remained intact, one was never activated, and one was somehow tainted by the blood of the fallen ghouls. Midway through the struggle the party was interrupted by the cavalry arriving. Xerxes and the Artificer's Guild magicians used the three active gates to still complete the spell, but the conduit only was able to travel both ways. This meant that all of the awakened must be ascended out and if the rest of the army tried to invade, there would be no hope for retreat. A one-way ticket.

However this was no ordinary magician. Xerxes is not just a being with 500 years mastery of the animatic arts but he is also a being with 500 years without proper revenge against the man who took his queen from him. There was an entire necropolis of bones to be picked with the shade that is now Mordred. The arrival of Xerxes marked the need for Mordred to immediately declare an heir, he quickly appeared before Tiny Rick and drenched him with the contents of Mordred's last phylactery. Immediately Tiny Rick felt the change as all warmth left his body. His very soul was being infected fragment by fragment.

Meanwhile, Sprocket upon being imbued with Sabine's magic for the second time, unlocked a message planted in his psyche from Sabine, a message for Xerxes which has yet to be revealed.

Xerxes was still able to free all of the awakened with the help of the amplification hex bags and Sabine's gift. It seems that fortune finally smiled on the ex-Scarlet Tunic Brigader’s as they awaken from the portal inside the Artificer’s Guild mobile headquarters. The headquarters travels via portals and the base can only be attuned to 4 portals at a time, each represented by a color.

Black being the color of the arena where the party was rescued 24 hours ago. This color was the dimmest of colors and seemed to be flickering as if not calibrated correctly.

Xerxes told the tale of Ramses, himself and Sabine, The Ra of Argos. Sabine was thought to be the avatar of the Raven Queen herself, and to honor her deity she founded two polar colleges of magic. These colleges were to serve as pillars in the arcane arts for all magicians in Argos and beyond. They were the Circle of The Murder to study necromancy, the study of death, and the Artificer’s Guild to study animacy, the study of life. Sabine's goal was to bring the best of both Arcanum together in Argos to facilitate research about the balance between life and death.

Sabine trusted these organizations to her two closest and most powerful friends. The first being Xerxes, Sabine's husband and true love, an easy first choice, and the second fell to her old teacher, a man of incredibly arcane knowledge and a deep love of Sabine. This posed a problem for the Queendom that was Argos, as Ramses grew more knowledgeable as the new head of the Circle of the Murder. But as his knowledge grew so too did his jealousy. Soon it became an overwhelming ache which itched its way into a plan.

Ramses would gift himself and Sabine with eternal life in order to spend eternity together without Xerxes, as Ramses believed that this could only be achieved through the clearly superior school of magic, necromancy. Ramses spent years searching for the key to everlasting life, and upon finding his solution, becoming a Lich, he offered this to Sabine. But the cost was too great. Sabine rejected the ritual, but Ramses performed the conversion against her will.

Upon finding herself a newly minted Lich, Sabine starved the Lich portion of herself by refusing to consume the souls of other humanoids. Her body crystalized and she became the Demi-Lich that the party found inside the large phylactery in the ritual of life room.

With the containment phylactery destroyed by a brave company of adventurers, Sabine was reborn with her still possessing her own free will. With that will she decided to revive the party that saved her, however he did so at the cost of her being. Depositing a small portion of her newfound life in each of her new children, in the form of a new crystalline heart.

Because of Sabine's gift the group was able to have their consciousness restored. Despite Sabine's best efforts, she was unable to destroy and deter Mordred and instead he fled after his vessel was revived apart from him. He soon returned to discover the former Scarlet Tunic Brigade unconscious, still recovering from their rebirth. Instead of outright killing the adventurers, Mordred chose this opportunity to bolster his numbers by converting them into ghouls.

Months passed as the Artificer’s Guild searched for Mordred's lair and they created a plan to revive Sabine's new chosen, the awakened.

Luckily, they had planted 2 of their members amongst the other adventurer's captured by Mordred, Corrumpere and Bergs who were able to be located and restored thanks to some preparation by Xerxes. Xerxes sent in a few spies to plant the hex bags and draw the teleportation gates, and which is how the events from earlier began.

Now the awakened must travel through the red door to the Hall of the Raven, equipped with only a few vials to create their own phylactery to contain the Lich's soul which is slowly consuming Tiny Rick. Additionally, the awakened must complete the ritual to cleanse all of themselves from Mordred’s grasp and finally be whole again.

The party discovered a graveyard at the entrance to the Hall of the Raven, and as such decided to continue through the gate, there they discovered a brazier surrounded by four torches on long poles. Nyx immediately lit the brazier which cause a horde of undead to emerge from their graves as if answering the call.

The party was able to let the gate down and keep the brunt of the undead forces out, however they still managed to stream in through the gatehouse. Quick thinking by Bergs caused one of the four warding torches to be used to light the main force of zombies on fire. Despite seeming like a good idea at the time, it was discovered that the torches were needed in order to keep the onslaught of zombies back.

It was then that the team sprang into action, making quick work of the closest torches, dunking one by one into the fire. Brigit taking on the form of a giant wolf managed to take a bite out of the undead, while Sprocket used his trusty mage hand to retrieve the tossed torch from underneath the writing pile of burning zombies.

The party finished up the fight and Dallor Dallorbill being the upstanding team player he is, proceeded to loot the coins from eyes of each of the zombies.

## Summary 5

Summary – Chapter 5 A matter of time or Tiny RIP

The party entered the foyer of the Hall of the Raven (the current headquarters for what is left of Mordred’s Circle of the Murder) to find Daina and Brock Hammer.

Daina and Brock claimed that they escaped from Mordred's clutches all on their own. Brock Hammer, a human rogue, claimed he was tasked by the Artificer’s Guild to save Daina, a statement that while true, is also completely false as the two figures in the foyer were actually doppelgangers!

The truth is that these doppelgangers had one task: capture each of the awakened alive in order to steal Sabine's Gift, the crystalline heart which now resides in every party member who was originally in the ritual room when Sabine awoke from her 500 year slumber. Sabine, as the avatar of a deity with 500 year of free time, did something that only occurs on a few planes of the multiverse, she died. Dying for an avatar of a deity is complicated work, the rule of avataring being when the current avatar dies the next is born, in one world or another.

But the Raven Queen was not reborn, instead she shattered her soul so that those who were sacrificed in her awakening may be awakened as well. And awakened they were. Mordred seeks to gather these pieces of heart and forcibly bring back his Sabine in order to control her power and rule with her by his side.

The awakened, bless their crystalline hearts, were fooled by the doppelganger’s impersonation and immediately began searching for clues in the cultist lair. To their credit, Stalwart Little decided to search the room for evil, but doppelgangers while being infamously despicable tricksters are not inherently evil, and thus the wolves wandered free amongst the sheep. The middle of the room sported a large metal dais held up by 3 talons. On the dais was the following inscription:

"Find the Raven As the Crow Flies, else continue in a field of lies,

Collect Four Vials, different colors found, mix them wisely upon hallowed ground

This the first of trials you must complete, to gain the liquids you all must seek.

Red the color we bleed till we die, red you must offer from a single supply"

Corumperre, not-Daina, not-Brock, Tiny Rick and Nocturnus Wayne all approached the dais the same way, it became clear that only one person may fill up the dais with their blood. Despite Tiny Rick being on a time limit, making him the least attached to his blood in the party, not-Daina decided to drip some of her doppelganger blood into the dais.

The foyer immediately jumped to life as the whole room filled with chiming from four mysteriously stuck grandfather clocks lining the hallway. These clocks were of particular note to the curious Stalwart Little, Nyx, and Brotor Rootbeard, who spent their time attempting to get the hands which were stuck at 12'oclock, 3'oclock, 6'oclock and 9'oclock. But clocks were of no great importance to Dallor Dallorbill, after collecting all of the silver candlesticks in the hallway, Dallor decided to go straight into the next room "as the crow flies" where he was joined by the majority of the party later.

Nocturnus Wayne, not-Brock and Brigit decided to check out the upper floor before joining their fellow awakened, the upper floor of the foyer has a set of large double doors covered in red leather. Try as they might they could not get the door to budge but they did discover a pair of recessed 3 taloned prints in the wall to either side, which only covered their hands in a dusty chalk when they tried to fit them in.

After all this investigation, the awakened entered the next room through a small unlocked wooden door with a clock carved into it. Everyone but Brotor Rootbeard who decided to stay on the other side of the door to watch the clocks when the door closed. Alone in the lair of his enemies, Brotor was quickly snatched up by the other doppelgangers lurking about.

The party entered a room with 4 identical doors and 12 painted ravens circling overhead.

The gang once again split up, with Nyx, Stalwart Little, Tiny Rick, Sprocket, and Nocturnus Wayne going through the room across from the entrance while Dallor Dallorbill, not-Daina, Bergs, not-Brock Hammer, Corrumperre and Brigit all exited through other doors in that order. As Dallor Dallorbill ran into the next room, the spotlight above the door cast a long shadow down the seemingly infinite hallway, this is exactly what the doppelganger not-Daina was waiting for as she entered next, quickly cast her shadow over not-Daina and both disappeared. Then it was Bergs turn to run in and be whisked away by not-Brock who also had the same idea. That left a very confused Corrumperre and Brigit. Luckily Corrumperre had decided to cast invisibility on himself prior to entering the room but was still caught in Brigit's shadow.

Meanwhile the other group entered a room that was identical to the previous except for 2 facts.

1) this room had 9 painted ravens flying overhead

2) this room contained not-Brotor, who looks to have been teleported there when he opened the door after everyone had left.
