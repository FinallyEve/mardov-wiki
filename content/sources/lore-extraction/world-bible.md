## Cosmology

### The Gods and Primal Forces

The cosmology of Mardov revolves around a central divine conflict between twin souls: **Vecna** (god of evil secrets, animancy) and **Sabine** (goddess of life, death, and fate). They are the original twin souls, sharing a domain over death. Sabine's additional dominion over life made Vecna jealous, and his campaign to steal her powers is the cosmic engine driving the entire narrative.

**Key Deities Referenced:**
- **Sabine Ra** — Goddess of life, death, and fate. "Your time comes when it comes." Her spirit was shattered; her gifts scattered across the world. She will return as a demi-lich through the merging of Ramses and Xerxes.
- **Vecna** — God of evil secrets and animancy. His avatar rules a parallel dimension (V-MARDOV). He is "death incarnate knocking at the door."
- **The Raven Queen** — Goddess of death and necromancy. Her avatar's sacrifice creates a rift between worlds.
- **Bahamut** — God of justice, worshipped at Conquis Abbey and throughout Monethern. His highest priesthood is the Silver Scales: "All that is Just is not Righteous! All that is Righteous is not Just!"
- **Asmodeus** — God of tyranny and mind control magic.
- **Gruumsh** — God of destruction and chaos.
- **Tiamat** — Worshipped by the vampire queen of the Underdark, who gave her soul to Tiamat for an ascension ritual.
- **Pelor** — Temple in WindHaven (Field of Pelor).
- **Avandra** — Temple in WindHaven (Avandra's Gift) and Conquis Abbey (Gardens of Avandra).
- **Ioun** — Archive of Ioun at Conquis Abbey.
- **Erathis** — Workshop of Erathis in Tinkertown, Conquis Abbey.
- **Moradin** — God of dwarves, channeled at the Corpseforge in the Underdark.

### The Planes

**Material Plane:** Mardov — the continent, surrounded by seas. Contains the four Zeniths.

**Outer Planes:**
- **7 Heavens**
- **9 Hells**
- **Astral Sea** — Contains "Betwixt" and "The Veil"
- **Eaden**
- **Fae Wildes** — Four courts: Brier Patch (Spring), Flowerfield (Summer), Reaper's Bounty (Autumn), Snowcrest Sanctum (Winter). Also contains the Glittering Run.
- **ShadowFell** — Contains RavenFall, Gate of the Broken, Path of the Watchers, Shadar's Gift, Vision Gate (with rooms for individual characters: Casaval, Daina, Stalwart)
- **Vodram** — Contains Valheim and Vodram's FeyWilde (Hengefield, Serpent Meadows, Ursa Rock)
- **Uvan**
- **The Moon** — Has its own location folder, implying it's reachable/significant.
- **V-MARDOV** — The parallel dimension where Vecna's avatar rules. Mirror of the material plane. Contains a "Death's Zenith" that mirrors the Forest's Zenith.

### The Four Zeniths and Keystones

The Zeniths are ancient nexus points of cosmic power. Each contains a **Heart** (a gemstone keystone) and an **Anchor** (a weapon tied to one of the Four Horsemen). Mordred's plan is to destroy all keystones, enabling V-MORDRED's invasion.

| **Mountain's Zenith** | Heart of the Forge | Garnet | Anchor of War (hammer-axe-javelin) | War | Vag Mahar, Monethern | Red Eye |

### The Valley of Anchors
Located in Var'taos ruins, this is "the origin of magic." Anchors must be lifted and recharged here.

### Guard'Daemon (Gard'Daemon)
Anagram for Armageddon. A prophesied apocalyptic event heralded by a bleeding/blood star visible around Mardov. "Below the blood star begins the final war."

### Twin Souls
A cosmic mechanic originating from Vecna and Sabine. When twin souls find each other, their power increases (boost the stat where their combined bonuses are highest). Known twin soul pairs:
- Vecna and Sabine (the originals)
- Daina and Bergs
- Nyx and her sister Xyn

## Geography

### The Continent of Mardov

Mardov is a continent with diverse regions connected by trade routes, mountain passes, rivers, and seas. The world has undergone cataclysmic reshaping — notably the destruction of Uma which created a desert, and the sealing of Tygras which created a crater.

**Major Regions (from Places/ directory):**

1. **DawnHaven** — The largest and most detailed region. A continent-spanning domain.
2. **Monethern** — A region centered on faith and dwarven culture.
3. **Vartos** — Home of the Magister's Circle, the Valley of Anchors, and the Distillery.
4. **Bynar** — A mountainous/arctic region, the Cradle mountains.
5. **Islands** — Including Qual'Xar and the Serpent Archipelago.
6. **Underdark** — The subterranean realm beneath the mountains.
7. **Outer Planes** — Extraplanar locations.
8. **The Moon** — An accessible location.

*(See `locations-catalog.md` for the full directory breakdown.)*

### Key Cities and Locations

**WindHaven (DawnHaven):** A major city with districts including the Upper Crust, North Gate, temples to Bahamut/Pelor/Avandra, the Windswept Orphanage, and the Windmill Willow. Surrounded by the Game Trail with its Fringe Farms.

**Dawnpass (DawnHaven):** Political capital. Contains Bridgetown with the Tower Bridge Refugees, Umbratown, Her Majesties Magistrates, Monastery of the Dawn, and Northpass with the Everstar Palace.

**Machu (DawnHaven):** Home of the Forest's Zenith. Contains the Palace with its Old Guard royalty, the Circle of the Murder, and the City Outskirts above the Mortis River. The king is trapped in madness (like Jafar).

**Cloud City / Storm Peaks (DawnHaven):** A massive floating location. Contains the 5 Cities of Giants (Cloudheim, Fireheim, Frostheim, Hillheim, Stormheim), plus Laputa Town, Nimbus, Sirrus, and the Palace of the 9 Winds.

**Evallan Forest (DawnHaven):** The mystical forest. Contains the Emerald Guard, Grove of the Ancients with DoeFall Glade and Ursa Major.

**Vag Mahar (Monethern):** The great dwarven city, divided into three sectors: Blackbeard (mines, military, wealthy estates), Brownbeard (docks, taverns, temples), and Redbeard (carpenters, residential, artisans). Contains the Mountain's Zenith within the Stone Sanctum. The sewer system has the Clocktower, Shed, and Lockbox. Pathway leads to the Standing Stones with Revolutionaries, the Frozen Forest, and ultimately to Monethern City.

**Monethern City (Monethern):** A port city with the Floating Quarter, Fort Undercroft, Veritasium Palace with its Senatoriate, and the Spillway.

**Conquis Abbey (Monethern):** A holy city dedicated to Bahamut. Districts: Scalestone Plaza (Platinum Temple of Bahamut, Fountain of Promise, River of Dragons), Lightwells, Ironwork Alley, Tinkertown, Court of the Creators, Villas of the Devoted, Archive of Ioun.

**Magister's Circle (Vartos):** A magical academy/governing body. Contains the Circle with Eon's Sanctum, the Cabal of Horns, Candle Cove port, and shops.

**The Distillery (Vartos):** Contains the Actual Size Tower, Claudere Keep, Faughart, and the Temple to the Zodiac.

**Bynar / The Cradle:** Mountain region with Mt. Bynar, Mt. Dragonspine (FrostFang Village), and Shard Mountain. Home to the Winter's Zenith and the ruined dragon-kin tribe (Winter's Curators).

**Underdark:** Multi-layered. D'Guarda Fortress (renovated into a stronghold), Da'Gahar, Green Tides of St Ashbury, Myconid Grove with the Lair of the Corpseforger. Lower Levels divided between Drow-controlled Kaivan Layer (Shrine of the Bat) and Mind Flayer-controlled Ulthradi Layer.

## Magic System

### The Watchful Order
A guild of magicians who govern, teach, and occasionally put down forbidden forms of magic.

**Common Magic (permitted):**
- Standard elemental magic (fireball, illusions)
- Standard cleric miracles (healing the sick)

**Forbidden Magic:**
- **Necromancy** — Associated with the Raven Queen
- **Animancy** — Associated with Vecna
- **Tyranny/Charm/Mind Control** — Associated with Asmodeus
- **Destruction/Chaos** — Associated with Gruumsh

### The Monkey's Paw Principle
In the laws of Mardov's universe, nothing can be gained for free. All magic items carry hidden costs:
- Bag of Holding: some small percentage of items get lost in the aether between uses
- Belt of Giant Strength: causes disadvantage on charm spells (giants are dumb)

### Dark Magic
- Creatures wielding or created from dark magic are managed by the Watchful Order and destroyed if evil
- Most dark magicians stay hidden or are powerful enough to rival the Watchful Initiates
- Dark magic objects are worshipped by cults and mistaken for deities

### Sources of Power (The Four Hearts)
Each Zenith channels a different fundamental force:
- **Arcane** (Heart of the Forge) — Raw magical energy, channeled through artifice
- **Radiant/Divine** (Heart of the Faith) — Raw divine power, channeled without a deity
- **Natural** (Heart of the Wilde) — Cosmic zodiac powers, source of druidcraft
- **Fae/Warlock** (Heart of the Progenitors) — Raw fae magics

### The Covenants of Magic
After Tygras's superweapon destroyed Uma, covenants were established so that no being save for the Alabaster Seat may possess that amount of power again.

## The Overarching Story

### Mordred's Plan
Mordred is the campaign's primary antagonist. His arc:

1. **Origin:** Originally wanted to resurrect Sabine, his lost love. After her spirit was shattered, he shifted to collecting her gifts and controlling her power (this mirrors V-MORDRED in the parallel dimension).
2. **The Sacrifice:** Gets the Avatar of the Raven Queen to sacrifice her immortality, creating a rift between the material world and V-MARDOV (where Vecna's avatar rules).
3. **The Body:** Gave up his phylactery to take out the Artificer's Guild. This grants him Xerxes's body (corrupting the individual beings that make up Xerxes).
4. **The Endgame:** With his new body, Mordred has one goal: bring about the invasion by V-MORDRED by destroying all the keystones.

### The Anchor System
Each Zenith houses both a Heart (power source) and an Anchor (weapon). The Anchors are pairs of swords (or other weapons) thrust into the center of each Zenith. When both the Forest and Desert anchors are severed, the Raven Queen's avatar will cease to exist, Sabine's crystalline heart will be removed from the adventurers, and V-MORDRED will be forced back to his realm.

**Death's Anchor:** An obsidian sword in the Forest's Zenith. Originally black steel, now appears made of black glass. Carries a lingering curse with complex mechanics (charges, curse control on charisma saves).

**Life's Anchor:** Paired with Death's Anchor in the desert (incomplete in notes).

### The Three Prophecies

**First Prophecy (Ruth, Argos):**
> "The anchors fall, the realms asunder / Hearts of stone gathered from plunder / Death incarnate knocks at the door / Below the blood star begins the final war!"

**Second Prophecy (The Hags):**
> "Double bubble toil and trouble / Gard'Daemon is neigh, the first zenith is rubble / Prepare the pestilence with children's souls / The husks remain until they're told / Double bubble toil and trouble / Anchors lifted and hearts are smuggled"

**Third Prophecy (Mia):**
Four verses mapping to four locations, each describing the quest to resolve one Zenith:
- **Harvest Zenith** (Tygras Logath): Rain on fallow harvest, Matrim pact fulfilled, echo of Tygras stilled
- **Winter Zenith** (Bynar): Snow over ruined tribe, serpent plagues the sea, curators' plea answered
- **Mountain Zenith** (Vag Mahar): Fire on forgotten forge, blood star found, new paragon crowned
- **Forest Zenith** (Machu): Sunlight warms forest gate, madness prisoner freed, star-crossed three find peace

### The Matrim (Maturin) Pact
An ancient alliance between human sailors of old Uma, gnomes of Fae'ish, and Qual'Xar half-elves. They promised sanctuary to each other atop the Maturin Turtle. A tsunami in the wake of Tygras's explosion forced these pilgrims onto a giant turtle, creating a hidden capital — a buried pyramid with the Harvest's Zenith at its center.

### The Blood Star
A recurring omen. One appeared when Tygras came to power. A meteorite that turns red and literally falls. It struck and killed King Thorson Bronzebeard II, last trueborn heir of Vag Mahar.

## Conflicts

### 1. The Orc-Human War (DAWNHAVEN)

**Factions:** DawnPass/WindHaven/Eavallan vs. Stra'Franar/Fae'ish/Tygras Logath

**Root Cause:** The Orc Chieftain (former gladiator pit champion from before Tygras's empire fell) made a deal with the same Fae demon as Tygras, causing bloodlust and madness. Mordred is using this to pressure DawnPass and cut off food from the fertile Harvestlands.

**Reveal:** At the Magister's Circle, an envoy from DawnPass (notably containing zero Orcs or Half-Orcs) requests aid against orc sorcerers wielding draconic-blood destruction similar to the Tiefling army that destroyed Uma.

**Resolution Path:** The party discovers the Harvest's Zenith contains Tygras himself (or the arcane being he became). Destroying his echo rips a hole in the Fae. Nyx inherits the Mark of Tygras (Sable's Beacon). To revive the Maturin guardian: (1) remove Anchor of Famine from zenith top, (2) return Heart of Progenitors to Tygras Logath's center, (3) speak the words: "On his back all vows are made, a haven for the continent of dawn paid."

### 2. The Dwarven Civil War (VAG MAHAR)

**Factions:** Three Thanes of the three districts (Blackbeard, Brownbeard, Redbeard)

**Root Cause:** All three secret police forces (the Blackguard, Truth's Light, and Silent Flame) are actually run by the same Mind Flayer seeking to assault the Mountain's Zenith. Its goal: use the Anchor of War (a hammer capable of creating earthquakes strong enough to sink Vag Mahar) to breed better Duergar.

**Resolution:** The bastard Dorrin (Bergs/Daina's connection) is the only one who can build/control Warforged. His golem is named Roc. He must be crowned as the new paragon.

### 3. The Winter Curator Crisis (BYNAR)

**Root Cause:** The Yuan-Ti stole the Anchor of Pestilence from the Sunless Citadel, ruining the dragon-kin tribe (Winter's Curators). A fae eel serpent swallowed the Heart of the Faith.

**Resolution:** Stalwart delivers retribution to the serpent, recovers the Heart of the Faith, delivers it to Conquis Abbey. The Winter's Zenith is within the mountain of Bynar, guarded by a White Dragon.

### 4. The Forest's Madness (MACHU)

**Root Cause:** The king of Machu is trapped in madness. Mordred/Ramses and his phylactery are the "madness prisoner."

**Resolution:** Use a sun-reflecting mirror to access the Zenith. Free the king. The star-crossed three (Xerxes, Ramses, Sabine Ra) must find peace — Xerxes's soul is at the bottom of the Zenith, Ramses's soul is in Daina's bag and needs to return to the phylactery for cleansing. Sabine appears as a flash when both bodies merge into a demi-lich.

### 5. The Underdark Invasion

**Factions:** Nocturnus's vampire aunt (who gave her soul to Tiamat) threatens an invasion of the overlands from the Underdark.

## DM's Ideas & Future Plans

### Unused Location Concepts
- **Colosseum City:** Forces inhabitants to fight their way to the center circle. Citizens can only be saved by making it to the center and winning single combat.
- **The Dome:** Giant dome of blackness. Open at top. Party must find a MacGuffin and escape.

### The Silver Scales of Bahamut
The highest order priesthood of Bahamut. Two sides: one judges by Justice, the other by Righteousness. "All that is Just is not Righteous! All that is Righteous is not Just!" — a philosophical split within the faith itself.

### Story Idea Bites
- **Underground Crime Network:** Literal underground network. NPC tells party about "the underworld" — it's a trap. Railcart getaway with bombs in back carts to cover tracks. The bombs are actually breaches for an invasion. **The party is framed for the invasion.**
- **Misty Step Rogue:** Grimnak
- **Badass Ally Rescue:** An ally dimension doors in, saves the party, and dimension doors out before they come to.
- **Elemental Battlefield:** A battlefield with elemental planes and affinities.

### Video Study Notes
The DM has studied armor mechanics, weapon rules, advantage/disadvantage — foundational D&D mechanics to build the homebrewed magic system on top of.

### Production Adaptation
A comprehensive pacing and storytelling study has been compiled for adapting Mardov as a **gothic anime motion webtoon** (see `pacing-and-storytelling-study.md`). Sources studied include One Piece, Berserk, FMA:B, Frieren, Vinland Saga, Tower of God, Chainsaw Man, and others. Key recommendations:
- 12-player ensemble requires rotating spotlight (3-4 characters per arc, others as support)
- Gothic visual language with cathedral-scale environments
- Power system transparency (make the Zenith/Heart/Anchor mechanics legible to readers)
- Arc structure following the proven manga formula (Arrival → Descent → Convergence → Climax → Aftermath)

## Appendix: The Final Battle Structure

The campaign is designed to culminate in a massive "final war" where:
1. Each Zenith has a **champion** who mans its control room
2. The four Hearts combine their power (Voltron-style beams)
3. The Anchors serve as weapons in the final fight
4. Bergs (as a Warforged powered by Heart of the Forge) is the central power conduit
5. Guard'Daemon (Armageddon) is the name of this final event
6. The blood star falls, V-MORDRED's invasion begins, and the 12 must hold all four Zeniths simultaneously
