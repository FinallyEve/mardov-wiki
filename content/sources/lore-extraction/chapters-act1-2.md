### Summary

The campaign opens in the town of Argos, newest bastion of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, an adventuring company founded by Chairman Slitz Gnard. The Brigade was formed after necromancers moved into the Old Forest and traditional tavern-hired adventurers kept disappearing. Over a year, the Brigade fought back the undead hordes until they retreated.

The party arrives for their first assignment as recruits. They are introduced by Finkle Hollingsworth, the Brigade's Chief Operating Officer, a bookish gnome flanked by two half-ogre henchmen, Stromble and Grom. During introductions, Finkle asks mysterious questions about ancient cults in the area, particularly the Raven Queen cult -- revealing that cult members use necromancy and animancy, view death as a steroid, and mark themselves with symbols under their eyelids.

The introduction is interrupted when Jorgen, a human ranger in a gold tabard, comes sprinting in pursued by flying kobolds. After the party dispatches the kobolds, Finkle assigns them their first mission: descend into the sewers beneath the Middle Ward to reach an ancient ritual site and collect four sources of ancient magic to disperse the kobold infestation.

### Key Events
- Party signs on as Scarlet Tunic Brigade recruits
- Finkle reveals information about the Raven Queen cult and excommunicated gods (Asmodeus, Gruumsh, Raven Queen, Vecna)
- Flying kobold attack during orientation
- Jorgen arrives having lost his previous party in the sewers
- First mission assigned: clear the sewer ritual chamber

### Characters Present

- Jorgen (human ranger, gold tabard)
- The party (new recruits)

### Summary

The party descends into the sewers of the Middle Ward. Jorgen leads ahead but the door slams shut behind him, leaving the party in darkness with kobolds. Stalwart Little uses Draconic to keep the kobolds at bay despite their eerie chanting of "SCARLET, SCARLET, SCARLET." The party finds a room with a blood rune button that starts a 180-second countdown -- the ceiling descends while kobolds are thrust into the room each time the button is pressed.

Surviving the ceiling trap, some members phase through a wall marked "RUN LEFT" into the ritual chamber, while others descend a spiral staircase. They discover Stromble/Grom in a control room pulling levers, clearly caught off guard and lying about their presence. The ritual chamber is massive with four endless waterfalls of colored liquid (red blood, green poison, orange mercury, blue liquid souls) and a large phylactery of the Raven Queen on a gear-like pedestal.

Dallor Dallorbill had earlier pickpocketed a note from Jorgen showing Finkle knew the recruits' arrival time and details -- evidence of a setup. The ritual is completed and a bright light engulfs the room, with the symbol of Vecna/the Raven Queen being the last thing seen.

### Key Events
- Party discovers the sewers are a trap designed by Finkle
- Blood rune button puzzle with descending ceiling
- Discovery that Finkle's henchmen were priming the ritual chamber
- Jorgen revealed as complicit in leading parties to their deaths
- Ritual of Life chamber found with four streams of magical liquid
- Party completes the ritual; blinding light engulfs the room

### Characters Present
- The party, Jorgen, Stromble/Grom, Finkle (remotely orchestrating)

### Locations
- Argos Sewers (Middle Ward)
- Ritual Chamber (beneath the old city of Argos)

### Plot Threads
- Opened: The four magical liquids and their significance; Finkle's betrayal
- Advanced: Raven Queen cult presence in Argos
- Closed: Kobold sewer mission (though the true purpose is revealed)

### Summary

Finkle Hollingsworth reveals his true nature. His body contorts into a hollow, twisted, gaunt figure -- he is actually Mordred, a lich who has been using the Scarlet Tunic Brigade as a soul-harvesting operation. He has collected 2,000 adventurer souls to fuel his power, creating demand for adventure to lure more victims. He commands Jorgen to be killed for outliving his usefulness.

The party discovers that the large crystalline object in the ritual chamber is a Demi-Lich skull -- Sabine Ra, trapped inside a phylactery. Sprocket touches it with mage hand and enters a trance. The party manages to shatter the phylactery despite Mordred's fury, freeing Sabine. The freed Demi-Lich turns Mordred's own ghouls against him and forces him to flee.

However, the party falls in combat. In death, they feel an anchor pulling them back. Sabine Ra sacrifices her newly restored life to revive them, depositing a crystalline heart in each member -- fragments of her own soul. The party awakens in different places and times, reborn at level 2.

- Finkle revealed as Mordred, an ancient lich
- Mordred has harvested 2,000 souls via the Scarlet Tunic Brigade
- Party manages to shatter Sabine Ra's phylactery
- Sabine Ra freed, turns on Mordred
- Entire party killed by Mordred
- Sabine sacrifices herself to resurrect the party with crystalline hearts
- Party reborn at level 2

They have been converted to ghouls by Mordred but had their consciousness preserved by Sabine Ra's gift. ### Summary

The party awakens in the Artificer's Guild mobile headquarters, rescued by Xerxes through teleportation gates in the arena. Xerxes reveals himself as a 500-year-old being made of living organisms -- moss, plants, countless small lives that inherit his memories and soul. He explains the full history:

Sabine Ra was the avatar of the Raven Queen who founded two colleges of magic: the Circle of the Murder (necromancy/death) and the Artificer's Guild (animancy/life). She entrusted these to Xerxes (her husband) and Ramses (her teacher). Ramses grew jealous and attempted to grant both himself and Sabine eternal life through lichdom. When Sabine rejected the ritual, Ramses forced it upon her. Sabine starved her lich form by refusing to consume souls, crystallizing into the Demi-Lich the party found.

When the party shattered Mordred's phylactery and completed the ritual, they inadvertently freed Sabine. She used her remaining power to revive the party at the cost of her own existence, depositing crystalline heart fragments in each of them. The party collectively IS Sabine now. Mordred seeks to gather these pieces to forcibly resurrect Sabine under his control.

Finkle Hollingsworth was merely a vessel -- he was possessed when he explored the Forest's Zenith and activated the seal to Ramses's prison.

### Key Events
- Full exposition of Sabine Ra, Xerxes, and Ramses/Mordred's history
- Party learns they carry fragments of Sabine's soul (crystalline hearts)
- Mordred revealed as Ramses, Sabine's former teacher
- Finkle was an innocent vessel, possessed by Ramses/Mordred
- Artificer's Guild mobile HQ travels via portals (4 at a time, color-coded)

### Characters Present
- Xerxes, the party

### Locations
- Artificer's Guild Mobile HQ (portal-based)

### Notable Dialogue/Moments
- Xerxes: "Unlike the organisms that make up ME, you now collectively make up SABINE the avatar of the RAVEN QUEEN."
- Ramses (from phylactery): Begs party to take his crystal to the Forest's Zenith so he can finally die

### Plot Threads
- Opened: The Red Door to the Hall of the Raven; Creating a new phylactery to contain the lich soul consuming Tiny Rick; The Raven Queen's true nature
- Advanced: Mordred's pursuit of Sabine's heart fragments; Understanding of the crystalline hearts

### Summary

Tiny Rick's time runs out. As his twin souls pour out -- green (Mordred) and blue (Rick) -- a loud crack fractures reality. Varys, a rebel from another dimension (where Vecna rules instead of the Raven Queen and there is no Artificer's Guild), appears while Nocturnus Wayne vanishes without a trace.

The party enters the Hall of the Raven foyer where they find "Daina" and "Brock Hammer" who claim to have escaped Mordred. These are actually dopplegangers sent to capture the awakened and steal their crystalline hearts. The party is fooled and enters the cultist lair, discovering a dias with instructions for a ritual requiring colored liquids and blood.

Through a series of rooms with spotlight teleportation and shadow mechanics, the party is separated and paired off. The dopplegangers replace several members through combat in the "Chamber of Shadows" judged by the mechanical watcher XJ-5. Eventually the deception is uncovered when the real Daina calls out from a cell, and the dopplegangers are defeated.

A massive Gorgon is encountered. Tiny Rick is petrified. The party uses illusions to trick the Gorgon into breathing on a stone dias, activating the Ritual of the Talon. Brock Hammer and Corrumpere each gain a stone talon hand. The chapter ends with Tiny Rick's death, his soul separated from his body as a safety measure against Mordred's full takeover.

### Key Events
- Tiny Rick dies; soul separated into phylactery vials
- Varys arrives from alternate V-Mordred dimension
- Nocturnus Wayne/Brotor Rootbeard vanish interdimensionally
- Doppelganger infiltration uncovered
- XJ-5 mechanical watcher introduces Hall of Lies
- Gorgon encounter; Tiny Rick petrified
- Stone Talon ritual begun (Brock Hammer and Corrumpere get stone hands)

### Plot Threads
- Opened: The alternate V-Mordred dimension; Interdimensional rifts from avatar soul fragmentation

### Summary

The party completes the Hall of the Raven. In the study, Sprocket uses a vision from Sabine to find and shatter Mordred's original phylactery. A voice identified as Ramses begs them to take his crystal to the Forest's Zenith so he can finally die. The four liquids are combined at the altar in the ritual order (red, orange, blue, green), and with a drop of blood from each afflicted member, warmth returns -- the cleansing is complete.

Outside, the party meets a hooded Finkle Hollingsworth, now freed from Mordred's possession. He offers passage to Argos aboard Captain Liza's riverboat the Thornberry Shade, promises them the Scarlet Keep as a base of operations, and 250gp each for delivering the Heart of the Wilde (a massive emerald he stole from the Forest's Zenith).

The riverboat journey becomes a murder mystery when First Mate Gordon is found dead in a silk cocoon. The Heart of the Wilde is stolen. Through investigation, the party uncovers multiple plots: Ace (really Chuck Wheeler) stole the gem and hid it in his ten-gallon hat; the Don Manauchi and Don Damiachi planned to kidnap Sally (a Machu princess); and a WereMoth -- Sally's royal bloodline protector activated by Cerci's dreamcatcher wards -- killed Gordon and others who threatened Sally.

### Summary

The party settles into the Scarlet Keep. Tiberious D. Rex, a cleric of Bahamut, leaves a note confessing to secretly keeping 100gp and leaving healing potions as penance. Argos is under nightly siege by undead at the East and North gates, with the Scarlet Tunic Brigade down from 100 to 40 members.

Finkle assigns the party to reinforce the East Gate. They learn that Cerci and Sally have taken up residence in the Keep, and Sally went missing near the North Gate two days ago. Lord Fartheshire's children Hansel and Grettle have also been kidnapped -- they are Underdark royalty with the ability to sense bores into the Underdark.

At the East Gate, the party survives a massive battle with wraiths and wights. Sheila the Witch Hunter arrives on horseback with a radiant potion that drives the undead away. She reveals her backstory: her teacher Ruth, the greatest diviner in the realm, sacrificed herself by willingly wearing a cursed witching hat to save Sheila. Sheila has spent 5 years hunting witches to find a cure (running water can knock the hat off).

The party finds Ruth's hut, uses Brigitte's rainstorm to remove the hat, and rescues Hansel and Grettle from a ritual involving Mordred's fiery gaze. Ruth delivers the first prophecy: "THE ANCHORS FALL, THE REALM ASUNDER / HEARTS OF STONE GATHERED FROM PLUNDER / DEATH INCARNATE KNOCKS AT THE DOOR / BELOW THE BLOOD STAR BEGINS THE FINAL WAR!"

Election day arrives in Argos. Mandu and the Golden Reach (Danelle and Markus) returned most of the missing children, claiming owlbears took them, and used this heroism to seize political positions. The party discovers that Timmy's soul has been weakened by the hags -- Cerci explains that 4-year-olds are developing their souls and Timmy's has been forged like an iron ingot in fire. She gives him a dreamcatcher to watch for the Night Hag's return.

At the Festival of Light, Stalwart Little's divine sense reveals Markus and Danelle as fiends (incubus and succubus). They are exposed and flee to the Old Forest. Nocturnus Wayne stuffs the ballot box.

Returning to the asylum, the party's previous entrance is trapped with Indiana Jones-style boulders. They fight through wights but then deliberately trigger the boulder trap multiple times to seal themselves in a cell for a long rest -- effectively burying themselves alive.
