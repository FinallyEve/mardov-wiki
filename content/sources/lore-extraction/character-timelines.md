## BRIGIT (Wild Spirit of Fire)

**Player**: Liz
**Class**: Druid (Circle of the Moon) / Barbarian multiclass
**Race**: Changeling/Shapeshifter
**Key Items**: Anchor of Death (locket), Stag Zodiac
**Track**: Tieflings -> Distillery -> Good Guys Guild

### Arc Summary

Brigit begins as a chaotic, multi-formed druid struggling with the many voices in her head. She becomes a Progenitor through a ritual in Eden with the Children of the Forest, gains custody of the Anchor of Death in her locket, and carries the Stag Zodiac. Her arc is one of identity -- learning to channel her rage and multiplicity into purpose. She is the Wild Spirit of Fire, and through her shapeshifting, serves as the party's most versatile combatant and emotional anchor. By Act 4 she has ascended the locket, lost her elemental powers during the Harvest Zenith battle, and must find her way forward without her primary coping mechanism.

**Key Items**: Third Eye (ethereal sight), Portal Gun, Robe of the Archmagi, Mordred's cursed spellbook

### Arc Summary

Sprocket is the party's divination wizard who becomes embroiled in a cosmic chess match. Cursed by Mordred's spellbook, nearly framed as a necromancer, he rises to become the Alabaster Seat (the conduit of all arcane magic on Mardov). His third eye gives him unique sight into the ethereal plane, and his crystallization is slowly killing him. He becomes the Knight of Spring/Flower Field, and his journey is about bearing impossible burdens with humor and stubborn gnomish resolve. "The Gnome Yoinks" is his signature move.
