# Visual Moments Catalog -- Mardov Campaign

Art director's sourcebook. 100 most visually striking scenes from Brigit's player notes, ranked by visual potential. Sourced from Chapters 28-136.

## Tier 1: Iconic Campaign-Defining Images (1-20)

### 1. The Locket Ascends -- All Thirteen Zodiac Souls Unite (Chapter 120)
**Scene**: Brigit holds the locket aloft as the final progenitor soul enters it. The locket ascends to full power, radiating light that turns every undead creature in her presence. The twelve zodiac forms swirl within the locket's inner realm -- wolf, stag, doe, fox, pangolin, serpent, rabbit, and more -- orbiting four elemental cores.
**Visual**: Central figure (Brigit) arms raised, golden locket blazing with twelve distinct animal silhouettes spiraling outward in concentric rings. Undead creatures recoiling and disintegrating in waves from the light. Cathedral-scale radiance.
**Characters**: Brigit (center), zodiac spirits (ethereal)
**Pilot relevance**: No
**ComfyUI prompt sketch**: `fantasy woman barbarian holding glowing golden locket above head, twelve spectral animal spirits spiraling outward, undead creatures dissolving in holy light, epic divine power moment, dark battlefield background, volumetric god rays, golden and white light against darkness`

## Notes for Art Direction

### Recurring Visual Motifs
- **The Locket**: Golden, zodiac animals swirling within. Grows in power throughout the campaign.
- **War Paint**: Brigit applies mud-and-berry henna before every major battle. Different styles for different characters.
- **Hero's Feast**: Barbarian charcuterie -- primitive, earthy, always under a tree at dawn. Recurs three times identically.
- **Transformations**: Brigit shifts forms constantly (wolf, fire elemental, dragon, air elemental, owl, scorpion, pangolin, horse, giant ape, quetzalcoatlus).
- **Scale Contrast**: Sprocket (gnome) against massive opponents is a constant visual -- beholder, nightwalker, titan.
- **Ethereal Overlay**: Many scenes have an ethereal plane view overlapping the material, showing hidden truths.
- **The Four Zeniths**: Inverted pyramid structures, each elemental-themed (forest/earth/winter/harvest).

### Color Palettes by Arc
- **Early Campaign (28-49)**: Winter whites, warm tavern ambers, blood reds
- **Tournament Arc (72-82)**: Azure blues, golden sand, purple magic, the deep green of the Valley of Anchors
- **Distillery Arc (83-90)**: Coastal greys, zombie greens, dawn golds, fire oranges
- **Endgame (100+)**: Deep purple shadowfell, blood-red beams, ice-blue zeniths, volcanic oranges, divine gold
