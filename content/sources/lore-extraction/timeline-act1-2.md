# Mardov Campaign — Timeline (Acts 1 & 2)

> Chronological key events extracted from DM chapter notes, Chapters 0-37.

---

## Prehistory / Deep Lore

- **Ancient era**: Mammothborn civilization exists; Tiamat eradicates them. Kolyma, the Last Curator, survives in the Rod of Sanctuary.
- **Ancient era**: The Raven Queen establishes the four Zeniths (Forest, Winter, Mountain, Harvest) with corresponding Anchors (Death, Pestilence, War, Famine) to maintain cosmic balance.
- **Ancient era**: 13 Dueregar fortresses built, each imprisoning a Phoenix as seals to lock away the First Forge beneath Vag'Mahar.
- **Ancient era**: The Sleepless (Aboleth), older than gods, takes residence in the Purelake deep beneath the surface.
- **Centuries ago**: Sabine Ra, avatar of the Raven Queen, founds two colleges of magic: the Circle of the Murder (necromancy/death) and the Artificer's Guild (animancy/life). She entrusts them to Ramses (her teacher) and Xerxes (her husband).
- **Centuries ago**: Ramses grows jealous; attempts to grant himself and Sabine eternal life through lichdom. Sabine rejects the ritual; Ramses forces it upon her. Sabine starves her lich form by refusing to consume souls, crystallizing into a Demi-Lich.
- **Centuries ago**: Ramses becomes Mordred, an ancient lich. Xerxes and the Artificer's Guild begin centuries-long war against him.
- **~500 years ago**: Xerxes achieves his unique form -- a colony of living organisms (moss, plants) that inherit his memories and soul.
- **Decades ago**: Tiberious D. Rex resurrects Bartrim Hadrin using divine magic of Bahamut, placing a diamond where his heart should be.
- **Years ago**: Bartrim marries Evelyn Steelforge (master armorer). Thorin Ghost-Eye's archer kills Evelyn; Bartrim is exiled.
- **Years ago**: Finkle Hollingsworth explores the Forest's Zenith, activates a seal, and is possessed by Ramses/Mordred.
- **~2 years before campaign**: Mordred (as Finkle) founds the Scarlet Tunic Brigade in Argos as a soul-harvesting operation, recruiting and killing adventurers. Over time, harvests 2,000 souls.
- **~2 years before campaign**: Children begin disappearing in Argos -- hag coven kidnapping 4-year-olds to brew Pestilence from their developing souls.
- **Sometime before campaign**: The Nine Hells collapse, dispersing fiends across the planes. The plane of fire comes under threat from demons. Fire Princess Ember Ashborn goes missing.

## Act 1 — Argos and the Awakening

### Chapter 0: The Scarlet Tunic Brigade
- Party arrives in Argos and is recruited into the Scarlet Tunic Brigade
- Finkle Hollingsworth (secretly Mordred) briefs them on their mission
- Flying kobold attack during orientation
- Jorgen arrives, having lost his previous party in the sewers
- First mission assigned: clear the sewer ritual chamber

### Chapter 1: The Sewer Ritual
- Party descends into Argos sewers; Jorgen leads them into a trap
- Blood rune button puzzle with descending ceiling
- Discovery that Stromble and Grom were priming the ritual chamber
- Jorgen revealed as complicit in leading adventurers to their deaths
- Party discovers the Ritual of Life chamber with four magical liquid streams (blood, poison, mercury, liquid souls)
- Dallor pickpockets evidence of Finkle's betrayal from Jorgen
- Ritual completed; blinding light engulfs the chamber

### Chapter 2: The Gnome Eff'n Way
- Finkle's body contorts -- revealed as Mordred, an ancient lich
- Mordred has harvested 2,000 adventurer souls via the Brigade
- Party shatters Sabine Ra's phylactery, freeing the Demi-Lich
- Sabine turns Mordred's ghouls against him; Mordred flees
- **The entire party is killed by Mordred**
- Sabine Ra sacrifices her restored life to resurrect the party
- Each party member receives a crystalline heart fragment of Sabine's soul
- Party reborn at level 2, scattered across time and place

### Chapter 3: The Murder Circle
- Party awakens shackled in Mordred's underground colosseum as "awakened" undead
- 2,000 ghoul spectators; Mordred announces gladiator tournament
- Diana discovers Xerxes's letter revealing the Artificer's Guild rescue plan
- Diana accidentally exposes the Artificer's Guild sigil; Mordred takes her
- Gladiator battle; winner receives soul tether (drop from Mordred's phylactery)
- Artificer's Guild assault on the arena begins

### Chapter 4: As the Raven Flies
- Party rescued by Xerxes via teleportation gates to Artificer's Guild mobile HQ
- Xerxes reveals the full history of Sabine, Ramses/Mordred, and himself
- Party learns they collectively ARE Sabine -- her soul fragmented among them
- Mordred seeks to gather the heart fragments to forcibly resurrect Sabine
- Finkle revealed as an innocent vessel, possessed when he opened the Forest's Zenith seal

### Chapter 5: A Matter of Time
- Corramperre and Bergs sacrifice themselves delivering hexbags during arena rescue
- Mordred drenches Tiny Rick with his last phylactery contents -- soul consumption begins
- Sprocket receives a psychic message from Sabine
- Party enters the Hall of the Raven
- Graveyard battle at the entrance; Nyx lights a brazier summoning undead

### Chapter 6: The Incident
- **Tiny Rick dies** -- twin souls (green/Mordred, blue/Rick) pour out
- Reality fractures; Varys arrives from an alternate dimension (V-Mordred's world)
- Nocturnus Wayne vanishes interdimensionally
- Doppelganger infiltration in the Hall of the Raven (fake Daina and Brock Hammer)
- XJ-5 mechanical watcher oversees the Chamber of Shadows
- Gorgon encounter; Tiny Rick petrified
- Stone Talon ritual begun (Brock Hammer and Corrumpere get stone hands)

### Chapter 7: Night of the WereMoth
- Hall of the Raven completed
- Sprocket uses Sabine's vision to find and shatter Mordred's original phylactery
- Cleansing ritual performed (four liquids combined at altar with blood from each member)
- **Party freed from Mordred's curse**
- Finkle Hollingsworth returns, freed from Mordred's possession
- Party boards the Thornberry Shade (Captain Liza's riverboat)
- Finkle offers the Scarlet Keep and a job delivering the Heart of the Wilde
- Jorgen's revenant appears chanting "Kill the gnome"
- Murder mystery begins: First Mate Gordon found dead in silk cocoon
- Heart of the Wilde stolen by Ace (actually Chuck Wheeler)
- WereMoth revealed as Sally's royal Machu bloodline protector
- Dons exposed as kidnappers hired by Jafar (Sally's uncle)

### Chapter 8: Arrival in Argos
- Jorgen's revenant defeated (fireball + knocked overboard)
- Giant octopus attacks, drawn by the Heart of the Wilde
- Dons escape through hull breach
- **Party arrives in Argos; receives the Scarlet Keep as their base**

### Chapter 9: Never Trust a Hat
- Scarlet Tunic Brigade reduced from 100 to ~40 members; nightly undead siege
- Over 100 children kidnapped over 2 years, all aged 4
- Lord Fartheshire's children (Hansel and Grettle, Underdark royalty) kidnapped
- Massive battle at East Gate against wraiths and wights
- Sheila the Witch Hunter arrives with radiant potion
- Ruth the Diviner freed from cursed witching hat (Brigitte's rainstorm)
- Hansel and Grettle rescued from Mordred's ritual
- **FIRST PROPHECY delivered by Ruth**: "THE ANCHORS FALL, THE REALM ASUNDER / HEARTS OF STONE GATHERED FROM PLUNDER / DEATH INCARNATE KNOCKS AT THE DOOR / BELOW THE BLOOD STAR BEGINS THE FINAL WAR!"

### Chapter 10: Exit Through the Gift Shop
- Randall and Tesca join the party
- Don Mononuchi returns with a deal: lead party to asylum in exchange for rescuing Don Damiachi
- Party infiltrates through owlbear den (Papa Bear killed by Tesca's monk punch)
- Magmin summoning breach into asylum basement
- Artificer's Guild soldiers rescued; reveal Xerxes is corrupted by Mordred's madness
- Young Timmy (4-year-old elf) rescued
- Ruth departs for the Magister's Circle to interpret the prophecy

### Chapter 11: Batman for Mayor
- Election day in Argos; Mandu elected Mayor through corruption
- Golden Reach (Danelle and Markus) returned most kidnapped children, claiming owlbears took them
- Timmy's soul weakened by hags; Cerci gives him a dreamcatcher
- Night Hag attacks Timmy's soul in his dreams; party drives her off
- Stalwart Little's divine sense exposes Markus and Danelle as fiends (incubus/succubus)
- Golden Reach demons flee to Old Forest
- Nocturnus Wayne stuffs the ballot box
- Party deliberately buries themselves alive in the asylum for a long rest

### Chapter 12: Witches Get Stitches
- Hag coven destroyed (Gertrude crushed, Margaret killed, Tabitha stranded in astral plane)
- Party learns: hags are brewing Pestilence from corrupted children's souls for Mordred
- Mordred has issued a bounty on the awakened
- **SECOND PROPHECY delivered by captured hag**: "DOUBLE BUBBLE TOIL AND TROUBLE, / GARD'DAEMON IS NIGH, THE FIRST ZENITH IS RUBBLE..."
- Children rescued from kindergarten using Night Hag's soul bag
- Don Damiachi found alive but polymorphed into a dog
- Magic brooms and heartstone acquired

### Chapter 13: Viva La Revolution
- Civil war erupts in Argos (pro- vs anti-Scarlet Tunic Brigade)
- Dolf the blacksmith rallies defenders of the Keep
- Jorgen's revenant returns again
- Party storms Mandu's manor
- Markus (incubus) and Danelle (succubus) defeated in combat
- MIA, the Earth Mother and last of the Argosian Woods-Witches, freed from petrification
- MIA reveals she wiped the party's memories at the start of the campaign
- **THIRD PROPHECY delivered by MIA** -- four verses about the four Anchors:
  - Famine: "as the rain falls on the fallow harvest..."
  - Pestilence: "as the snow drifts over the ruined tribe..."
  - War: "as the fire bellows on the forgotten forge..."
  - Death: "as the sunlight warms the forest gate..."
- Argos liberated from Golden Reach corruption

### Chapters 14-15: Side Quests in Argos
- Community building: finding chickens, entertaining children, capturing Bigfoot for scientist Ghak
- Treasure quarry cleared of skeletons
- Party reputation in Argos solidified

## Act 2 — The Wider World

### Chapter 17: Setting Sail
- Captain Liza takes the Maturin Pact, joining the Qual'Xar maritime order
- Thornberry Shade upgraded with Maturin Sigil (sea creature ward)
- New crew introduced: Nathan Yanagan (secretly Jafar's spy), Riley Eversaw, Dilbert Watacop, Kelik Waystar, Trixie Magnacog
- Liza reveals she is from the Fae realm, not originally from Mardov
- Veera Saytrfell (tiefling with glamour) discovered below deck with son Evan
- Veera reveals the Bardic Circus (underground tiefling mage school)
- Tiefling prejudice at Magister's Circle identified as obstacle

### Chapter 18: The Underdark Vision
- Tiberious D. Rex narrates Underdark exploration
- Dueregar woman with Tiamat's symbol encountered
- Mind Flayer attacks the party psychically
- Teleportation circle triggers apocalyptic vision: massive Dueregar army worshipping unknown deity
- Tiberious departs to alert the Silver Scales clergy about the coming war

### Chapter 19: Approach to Magister's Circle
- Orc pirate attack damages the Thornberry Shade (devil among them)
- Oswald Magnacog (3rd Kraken of the Sand Seas) grants the Terrapin's Coin
- Ship repaired via Treesinger magic (new mast from Promise Tree acorn)
- Mysterious fire spreading on Riley's map from Tygras Logath northward
- Nyx receives human glamour (using Jorgen's revenant soul, harvested from Nathan Yanagan)
- Nathan Yanagan (Jafar's spy) killed; soul used for the glamour
- Nyx connected to Bardic Circus (secret tiefling school)

### Chapters 20-21: The Magister's Circle
- Party arrives at the Magister's Circle

### Chapter 23: Hobgoblins and the Zodiacs
- Druidic ritual with Ruth and Sheila

- **Dr. M.B. Wells killed** by hobgoblin captain Fak'Tul
- Trixie dragged deeper into the Tomb of the Last Curator

### Chapter 24: Hello From the Other Side
- Party pulled into Vecna's domain within the Tomb
- Four Zenith rooms discovered with hieroglyphic murals (prophetic imagery)
- Captain Liza revealed to have lost her legs
- Dallor Dallorbill's ghost appears (left eye missing, fading)
- Gemstone puzzles to unlock rescue paths for captured allies

### Chapter 25: The Tomb's Secrets
- Fak'Tul performs sacrifice to Vecna (cuts off hand, gouges out eye) and becomes revenant
- Speak-with-Dead circles: Dr. Wells's soul transcribed into his book (given to Sprocket)

- Full hieroglyphic prophecies decoded for all four Zeniths:
  - **Forest**: Sabine/Ramses/Xerxes love triangle, elk and doe, rabbit zodiac
  - **Mountain**: Dwarven city with 3 walls, the forge, frozen volcano, clashing armies
  - **Winter**: Serpent, dragonborn tribe, dormant turtle zodiac, white dragon, sunless citadel
  - **Harvest**: Upside-down city, white tiefling girl, fox zodiac, burned fields

### Chapter 26: Kolyma and the Siegeball Challenge
