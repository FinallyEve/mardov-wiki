## Chapter 0 -- Opening Exposition

### DM's Prose

Our Adventure this evening takes place in the town of Argos, this is the newest bastion for the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, a company that puts the VENTURE in adventure! The COIN in coincidence... the MONEY in harmony!

Before the Scarlet Tunic Brigade came to Argos, none of the market projection models showed any discernible gain from organized adventuring. In fact, the conventional model of adventurers in the tavern for hire was quite effective...

That is until the necromancers moved in.

Soon, party after party had disappeared fighting the onslaught of undead hoards.

Enter the big cheese himself, the chairman of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade SLITZ GNARD, the goblin with a plan and that plan was his patented triangle management structure. And so Chairman GNARD hired out every last adventurer in the area and put them to work.

After generous donation upon generous donation Argos' new guardians fought the good fight. Over the course of a year, the town was able to fight back the hoards of the undead, until they retreated back into the Old Forest. And during that time the Brigade grew and was able to maintain its budget by taking on the same odds and ends adventuring jobs that used to be discussed in inns and taverns across the township.

Now Argos' up and coming adventurers must work their way up the patented TRIANGLE management scheme of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade.

### The Hook

The adventuring party arrives for their first assignment. We open on our heroes practicing their crafts in the Recruit Training grounds, a short bookish gnome flanked by two lumbering Half Ogres waddles his way towards you.

"Oh my, Greeting adventurers, My name is Fikle Hollingsworth. I am the Scarlet Brigade's Chief Operating Officer at your service!"

"And these numbskulls are Stromble and Grom, or was it Gromble and Strom, I can never get your moronic names straight!"

The party learns about cults in the area: Asmodeus (tyranny), Gruumsh (destruction), Raven Queen (death/life, necromancy/animancy), and Vecna (evil secrets/death). Followers of the death cults use the dead as a means to furthering their power -- look for their symbols under the backs of their eyelids.

Suddenly a Human in a gold Tabard comes sprinting into view, pursued by a flock of flying kobolds.

JORGEN: "Blasted things followed out the sewers, bad enough you got me going down there, worse that my whole party was murdered by these bastards, DONT JUST STAND THERE HELP ME!"

After the encounter, Jorgen is reluctantly assigned to lead the new recruits into the sewers to deal with an ancient ritual focus buried deep below. The mission: get to the ancient ritual site below, fill a large bobble with one dose of each of the 4 pipes of ancient magic. Take away the magic, the kobolds will disperse.

### DM's Planning Notes

- Inspiration Events system introduced: when the party triggers a plot-related event or puzzle, they open up a challenge involving skill checks
- Class-specific dialogue written for each class introduction
- Event 1: clergy characters identify cult symbols
- Detailed NPC responses for every class

## Chapter 1 -- Into the Sewers

### DM's Prose

You come to the entrance of the sewers at the northernmost crossroads of the middle ward, a seemingly calm place well except for the ancient ritual focus buried deep below. The citizens of the middle ward know well that IT lives in the sewers, and IT is no laughing matter.

The manhole to the sewers is housed in the cellar of the capital building, below is a large hole with what looks like a clockwork ladder. The citizens like to wind back up the ladders when kobolds inevitably take up residence.

JORGEN: "I'm going in, be sure to follow quickly behind me, I'm not going to spend all day dragging you recruits through the SEWERS!"

**Room 1: The Hallway** -- The adventurers descend into an empty sewerway with a bright light at the other end. As soon as Jorgen enters the room, everything suddenly goes dark as a loud thud resonates off the walls. They discover the darkness, light sconces, and a horde of kobolds.

**Room 2: The Button Puzzle** -- A large depression in the floor with a button bearing a magic symbol of unknown origins emitting extremely bright green light. The blood rune starts a 180-second counter until the ceiling falls and a door unlocks below. Pressing the button spawns waves of kobolds. On the ceiling: "RUN LEFT" -- running into the left wall teleports through to the ritual chamber.

**Room 3: The Ritual Room** -- A HUGE glass container in the middle with 4 draining liquids falling from the ceiling. A mechanical pedestal with tracks leading to each of the 4 drains. The four liquids:
1. Red blood, hot to the touch
2. Green poison, viscous as sludge
3. Orange mercury, probably actually molten iron
4. Blue liquid souls

On completion: the glass glows with a bright light, kobolds are torn apart molecule by molecule, the last thing anyone sees is the symbol of Vecna/the Raven Queen.

### Summary Points (from Chapter 1 Summary)

- DALLOR DALLORBILL swiped a note from Jorgen revealing Finkle had the precise arrival time and names/classes of all recruits
- BRODEN ROOTBEARD received information about the Raven Queen Cult: they used necromancy and animancy as worship, viewed death as a steroid and life as potential energy

- Half the party found Jorgen and Finkle's half-ogre henchmen in a control room pulling levers (priming the ritual chamber), caught off guard

## Chapter 2 -- The Lich Reveals Himself

### DM's Prose

JORGEN struggles while in STALWART's grasp.
JORGEN: "Let go of me, do you realize what is going on here?"

At that moment, from behind the door strolls in FINKLE HOLLINGSWORTH.

FINKLE: "It seems this latest batch of fresh souls is proving a bit stronger than the average kobold horde. No matter, I'm confident we can fix them."

With that FINKLE's body begins to snap into different un-natural poses. Each pose is announced with a loud "CLICK" as FINKLE contorts from one position to another. Where the bookish butler gnome stood is now the body of something slightly taller. The first thing you notice is its cold presence, as if a physical wall of cold snapped into the room before your eyes could register. FINKLE's face has contorted into this hollow twisted figure, his body seems to have exploded at each of his joints making him seem drawn out and gaunt.

FINKLE: "Kill him: I have all the life energy I need, my undead empire will flourish beneath this city, for the RAVEN QUEEN smiles upon this place."

JORGEN: "But boss, I've done everything you've asked! I've led countless parties to their deaths down here, why kill me NOW!"

FINKLE: "2000 souls, to be exact, 2000 promising souls of adventurers, souls with the most potential energy. They could have flourished if we hadn't betrayed them. But they will be stronger now that I have risen them in the name of my RAVEN QUEEN. Now they serve ME, I create demand for adventure, which causes towns to collect coin, which lures adventurers, which brings me POWER!"

**FINKLE IS A LICH.** This is a hopeless fight for the adventurers. Finkle raises his fingers and every kobold stops dead -- from within each kobold, a GHOUL emerges from the spine, shedding its form like taking off clothing.

Inside the phylactery is a DEMI-LICH skull. Destroying the phylactery releases the DEMI-LICH but stops the ritual.

After the last member is dead: "The final recruit from the scarlet tunic brigade fell, the rush of the gears of the ritual filled the chamber as the rest of the hall falls silent. In the void that exists after life, you feel something anchor you back into being. The anchor wrenches you bit by bit back from the brink. For the second time in your existence you are 'born' beneath a bright white light. You feel stronger and yet not quite whole, as you each awaken in a different place in a different time."

### Summary Points (from Chapter 3 Summary)

- The Lich is revealed as MORDRED, who chose to possess FINKLE HOLLINGSWORTH
- MORDRED needed 2000 souls to complete the Ritual of Rebirth
- STALWART convinced kobolds to join the fight against MORDRED (speaking Draconic)
- SPROCKET touched the DEMI-LICH through mage hand, was thrust into a deep trance
- The party destroyed the phylactery, freeing the DEMI-LICH (SABINE)
- The DEMI-LICH turned on MORDRED
- STALWART completed the ritual and a bright light engulfed the room

## Chapter 3 -- The Murder Circle (Gladiator Games)

### DM's Prose (from Intro.txt)

Suddenly, after what feels like both an eternity and an instant, you awaken. The room around you is dimly lit as light leaks in through a grate above, there are 6 bodies including your own strapped to the walls of the small DEVAR-TETE.

You note the bodies of your fellow adventurers, their bodies haggard, their skin crawling with spider-tracks of coagulated blood. But their eyes are lacking the vibrant blue light that accompanies the stereotypical undead, instead the light of their eyes seems dim but natural.

BRIGIT WILDFIRE is the first to check her manacles and discovers that none of them are locked.

A booming voice:

"WELCOME, WE STAND TODAY ON THE ANNIVERSARY OF MY SHATTERING. 500 YEARS AGO TODAY, ARGOS WAS UNDER MY CONTROL! I RULED THIS CITY UNTIL THE BLASTED ARTIFICER'S GUILD BACKED ME INTO A CORNER. BUT DEATH SERVES MY RAVEN QUEEN, NOT SOME CIRCLE OF 2 BIT FOOLS! NOW WE CELEBRATE MY ASCENSION, TODAY I CHOOSE A LIEUTENANT TO SERVE AS MY NEXT VESSEL."

The holding cell lurches upward into a colosseum fighting pit. 2000 hollering GHOULS fill the stands. The ceiling is framed with GREEN FLAMES.

DIANA discovers a letter from XERXES, Grand Inquisitor of the Artificer's Guild:

"Play his game for now, but know that there are those who fight to free you as you stand. I have restored as many of your party's minds as I could manage. We've put pressure on MORDRED, in hopes he would need to conduct this very ceremony. One of the awakened MUST win the soul tether at all costs! Without you, the Artificer's Guild will be eradicated!"

DIANA flashes the hexbag with the Artificer's Guild sigil. MORDRED's voice:

"This particular band of adventurers cost me centuries of work. My beloved SABINE RA was awakened outside her phylactery, she shall never be whole again."

Upon the last contender standing, MORDRED descends and flicks a droplet from his phylactery onto the champion. Immediately a connection is felt with an ancient and boundless life force, bitter and filled with malice.

## Chapter 4 -- As the Raven Flies (XERXES Explains Everything)

### DM's Prose

You awaken to a room that is finally warm. In the center is a large pillar of light, and within that pillar is the shadow of a large man in white and gold plate. He removes his plate-helm to reveal orange glowing eyes framed on a rough brown face. Where hair should be is a jumbled mess of moss and leaves.

XERXES: "It never gets old meeting new people. Yes I am Xerxes, 500 year old bag of plant parts. No I am no longer human, I performed a desperate act a little over 450 years ago. I am a being of beings. My individual parts are organisms of their own."

**How he revived the team:** The arena AND the ritual room were infiltrated by spies well before the party arrived. They broke MORDRED's phylactery. The ritual was originally to resurrect SABINE -- but SABINE never died. She was tricked by RAMSES into accepting his curse and instead of subsisting on souls of others, she consumed her own, crystallizing within that chamber for an eternity.

**The History of SABINE, XERXES, and RAMSES:**

SABINE was thought to be the avatar of the RAVEN QUEEN herself. She founded two colleges of magic:
- The CIRCLE of THE MURDER -- to study necromancy (death)
- The ARTIFICER'S GUILD -- to study animancy (life)

SABINE trusted XERXES (her husband) and RAMSES (her old teacher). RAMSES grew jealous. He searched for everlasting life, became a LICH, and forced the conversion on SABINE against her will. SABINE starved the lich portion of herself, crystallized into the DEMI-LICH.

When the party broke the phylactery, SABINE was reborn with free will. She sacrificed her being to revive the party -- depositing a portion of her life in each awakened adventurer as a crystalline heart.

**Why he hates FINKLE:** "Any adventurer who defiles an ancient prison should know better than to try and TAKE anything! He was RAMSES when we put him in there but 500 years of subsisting on the ambient lifeforce of the ZENITH drove him mad."

Four rooms with riddles and ancient paintings, each corresponding to a Zenith:

**The Forest Room** -- "I am the fate of all things, large and small, many run from me, though I eventually find them all." Paintings of: a crown of sludge, a triad of people (SABINE, XERXES, RAMSES), a great elk with a locket, a rabbit with a phylactery, a lich reaching for a doe.

## The Central Tragedy: SABINE, XERXES, and RAMSES

SABINE RA of ARGOS, avatar of the RAVEN QUEEN, founded two colleges to study the balance of life and death. She married XERXES and trusted her teacher RAMSES. RAMSES grew jealous, became a LICH, forced lichdom on SABINE. She starved herself and crystallized for 500 years.

When the party broke her prison, SABINE sacrificed her being to give them crystalline hearts. MORDRED (the mad form of RAMSES) seeks to gather these hearts and forcibly resurrect SABINE to control her power.

XERXES, a 500-year-old being of plant organisms, leads the Artificer's Guild against MORDRED.

## GARD'DAEMON

The ultimate convergence event that the prophecies foretell. The anchors are falling, the zeniths contain gem hearts being exploited. If all anchors fall, GARD'DAEMON occurs -- a war between cosmic forces that could kill a god and bring either good or evil to MARDOV.
