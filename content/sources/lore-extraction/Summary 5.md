Summary – Chapter 5 A matter of time or Tiny RIP

The party entered the foyer of the Hall of the Raven (the current headquarters for what is left of Mordred’s Circle of the Murder) to find Daina and Brock Hammer.

Daina and Brock claimed that they escaped from Mordred's clutches all on their own. Brock Hammer, a human rogue, claimed he was tasked by the Artificer’s Guild to save Daina, a statement that while true, is also completely false as the two figures in the foyer were actually doppelgangers!

The truth is that these doppelgangers had one task: capture each of the awakened alive in order to steal Sabine's Gift, the crystalline heart which now resides in every party member who was originally in the ritual room when Sabine awoke from her 500 year slumber. Sabine, as the avatar of a deity with 500 year of free time, did something that only occurs on a few planes of the multiverse, she died. Dying for an avatar of a deity is complicated work, the rule of avataring being when the current avatar dies the next is born, in one world or another.

But the Raven Queen was not reborn, instead she shattered her soul so that those who were sacrificed in her awakening may be awakened as well. And awakened they were. Mordred seeks to gather these pieces of heart and forcibly bring back his Sabine in order to control her power and rule with her by his side.

The awakened, bless their crystalline hearts, were fooled by the doppelganger’s impersonation and immediately began searching for clues in the cultist lair. To their credit, Stalwart Little decided to search the room for evil, but doppelgangers while being infamously despicable tricksters are not inherently evil, and thus the wolves wandered free amongst the sheep. The middle of the room sported a large metal dais held up by 3 talons. On the dais was the following inscription:

"Find the Raven As the Crow Flies, else continue in a field of lies,

Collect Four Vials, different colors found, mix them wisely upon hallowed ground

This the first of trials you must complete, to gain the liquids you all must seek.

Red the color we bleed till we die, red you must offer from a single supply"

Corumperre, not-Daina, not-Brock, Tiny Rick and Nocturnus Wayne all approached the dais the same way, it became clear that only one person may fill up the dais with their blood. Despite Tiny Rick being on a time limit, making him the least attached to his blood in the party, not-Daina decided to drip some of her doppelganger blood into the dais.

The foyer immediately jumped to life as the whole room filled with chiming from four mysteriously stuck grandfather clocks lining the hallway. These clocks were of particular note to the curious Stalwart Little, Nyx, and Brotor Rootbeard, who spent their time attempting to get the hands which were stuck at 12'oclock, 3'oclock, 6'oclock and 9'oclock. But clocks were of no great importance to Dallor Dallorbill, after collecting all of the silver candlesticks in the hallway, Dallor decided to go straight into the next room "as the crow flies" where he was joined by the majority of the party later.

Nocturnus Wayne, not-Brock and Brigit decided to check out the upper floor before joining their fellow awakened, the upper floor of the foyer has a set of large double doors covered in red leather. Try as they might they could not get the door to budge but they did discover a pair of recessed 3 taloned prints in the wall to either side, which only covered their hands in a dusty chalk when they tried to fit them in.

After all this investigation, the awakened entered the next room through a small unlocked wooden door with a clock carved into it. Everyone but Brotor Rootbeard who decided to stay on the other side of the door to watch the clocks when the door closed. Alone in the lair of his enemies, Brotor was quickly snatched up by the other doppelgangers lurking about.

The party entered a room with 4 identical doors and 12 painted ravens circling overhead.

The gang once again split up, with Nyx, Stalwart Little, Tiny Rick, Sprocket, and Nocturnus Wayne going through the room across from the entrance while Dallor Dallorbill, not-Daina, Bergs, not-Brock Hammer, Corrumperre and Brigit all exited through other doors in that order. As Dallor Dallorbill ran into the next room, the spotlight above the door cast a long shadow down the seemingly infinite hallway, this is exactly what the doppelganger not-Daina was waiting for as she entered next, quickly cast her shadow over not-Daina and both disappeared. Then it was Bergs turn to run in and be whisked away by not-Brock who also had the same idea. That left a very confused Corrumperre and Brigit. Luckily Corrumperre had decided to cast invisibility on himself prior to entering the room but was still caught in Brigit's shadow.

Meanwhile the other group entered a room that was identical to the previous except for 2 facts.

1) this room had 9 painted ravens flying overhead

2) this room contained not-Brotor, who looks to have been teleported there when he opened the door after everyone had left.

Despite all the confusion, NYX saved the day after she summoned her owl familiar, Hedwig, to guide the party through each doorway until they came across an unsettling sight.

The paired-off group found themselves smack dab in the middle of a smoky almost-room, alone. A mechanical voice that filled the room:

"Greetings, I am watcher XJ-5 welcome to the Chamber of Shadows!

Both contestants must determine a winner and loser in order to be sorted onto the light and dark paths.

All consequences of combat will be forgiven"

This was the doppelgangers time to shine as the only way to knock out and take the place of the other contestant was to engage in physical touch. Dallor Dallorbill despite asking for a contest of will was duped into a rock paper scissors contest with not-Daina while not-Brock got Bergs with some good old fashioned arm wrestling!

Interestingly enough however, Brigit and Corrumpere were pleasantly surprise to declare no contest as Corrumpere was deemed to be invisible and not competing. Instead of traveling to light and dark path the no-contestants were instead put back on the original path and quickly made their way to the same room as the rest of the party.

It was then that the party found that they had been played. All of the now doppelganger party members were in the room that the others took so long to get to not-Daina, not-Brock Hammer, not-Brotor Rootbeard, not-Bergs, and not-Dallor Dallorbill stood in the middle of the room silent and staring at the door.

When the other half of the party entered the room and found their so called friends standing there, they heard the voice of Daina call out from one of the 6 cells that lined the room:

"That’s not me, it was a doppelganger the whole time!"

With that the doppelgangers morphed back to their original form and attempted to take the party on. However they weren’t accounting for the cells to be opened by the awakened, except for the 6th cell. Once things took a turn for the worse for team doppelganger, the final cell was opened to reveal a revenant who merely repeated the same phrase over and over: "Retrieve the heart, kill the gnome".

With that the Revenant, never taking his gaze off the door behind the party, exited his cell and as touched by a doppelganger who also assumed the revenant form. Luckily for the awakened the real revenant had much better things to do after being freed and he disappeared leaving only his likeness behind.

Outmatched and out of their league, Tiny Rick attempted to throw open the door to the next room only to be run down and petrified by a massive Gorgon! The Gorgon with no regard for humanoid life began indiscriminately petrifying and goring anything in range. Thanks to some quick suggestions by Corrumpere, the Gorgon was willed into retreating back behind his door and locked out of the room just in time as the door received a full charge from the mechanical bull.

Luckily for the team, the doppelgangers, their numbers decimated attempted to flee the Gorgon and party, including the not-revenant who went back into a cell long enough to be locked in.

The encounter over, the team looked with dismay as the progress bar on Tiny Rick’s phylactery box passed the 51% mark. With his last bout of free-will Tiny Rick said goodbye to his former party members and opened the box.

A bright blue liquid was draw from deep within Tiny Rick as his soul separated completely from his body, a safety measure to keep Mordred from fully forming while his soul remained partly Tiny Rick’s. The liquid soul, filled the glass phylacteries within the box which looks like a wash between light blue and vibrant light green. The two liquids repel each other like oil and water.

It is here that the party finds themselves, this week on DND
