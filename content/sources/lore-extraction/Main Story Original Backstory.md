Main Story Original Backstory:

The Scarlet Tunic Brigade is an adventuring party run by Slitz Gnard, a greedy goblin of morbidly obese size and a healthy relationship with the town mortician.

Time is money friends, and right now you’re out of both, so you and your fellow Scarlet Tunics signed up to be fresh meat! Funny how none of the higher ups in the Brigade have been on more than 3/4 missions, that's one hell of a turnaround... revolving doors are clearly ruining the adventuring business

The company has a simple and easy to follow hierarchy, everyone listens to the goblin in charge unless on missions, then whoever survived the most previous missions calls the shots. Any attempts to question the little green man will be met with a visit to the storage warehouse with Gogrut and Hurtle, the two highest ranking Ogres on the team, who are in charge of ... the HR department?

Think you have what it takes for leadership? Additional mission points are awarded for good old-fashioned bribery! It's not corruption if it’s in broad daylight

Each mission is concluded by paying the survivors tax (67% to Slitz) and the awarding of mission points!
