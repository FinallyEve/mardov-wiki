# ACT 3 — The Zenith Wars

Act 3 spans Chapters 42-87 plus a Reunion session. The campaign splits into two concurrent party tracks: **Dwarves** (focused on Vag'Mahar and the Mountain's Zenith) and **Tieflings** (focused on DawnHaven, Cloud City, and the Fae/Harvest storyline). Around Chapter 67, the Tiefling track is renamed to **"The Distillery"** (referencing the Distillery location in Vartos), suggesting the Tiefling party relocates to that region.

## Party Structure

Act 3 runs two parallel parties playing alternating sessions:
- **Dwarves**: Daina, Bergs, Nocturnus, and allies — exploring Vag'Mahar, the Underdark, and the Mountain's Zenith
- **Tieflings** (later "The Distillery"): Nyx, Irethia, Tesca, Randal, and allies — exploring DawnHaven, Cloud City, the Fae, and eventually the Distillery in Vartos

## Vecna's Agents (The Big Bads)

- **Mordred**: The primary antagonist. Former lover of Sabine, gave up his phylactery to take over Xerxes' body, aims to destroy all keystones to bring V-Mordred's invasion
- **Peraxis Phyrexia (Peraxis Geth)**: Illithilich created by Vecna using a Raven Queen shard. Commands the spell plague. Holds Thorkon Fireforge prisoner. Tasked with assaulting the Mountain's Zenith
- **Reynardine**: Fey Lord orchestrating the Orc-Human War in DawnHaven, seeking the Diadem of Dawn
- **Rimefang**: Undead dragon, spreading pestilence, tasked with opening Winter's Zenith
- **Ned'Zailok (Ned'zalock)**: Lich keeper of Death's Anchor in V-Mardov's Death Zenith
- **Oblivion Wayne**: Nocturnus's aunt, Drow vampire queen allied with Tiamat, created the spell plague with husband Ebon Wayne
- **Alhoon Ren**: Servant of Oblivion, researching spell plague and umber hulk essence
- **Alhoon Pollivar**: Manages research facility, umber hulk capture operations

## The Overarching Threat

Vecna seeks to collapse the barrier between Mardov and V-Mardov (a parallel universe where Vecna's avatar rules). Mordred is his agent destroying the keystones (zeniths). The anchors must be lifted and recharged at the Valley of Anchors. The hearts are being stolen from the zeniths. When all anchors fall, "the realm asunder" — Gard'Daemon (a cataclysmic event following a bleeding star visible around Mardov) begins.

## Key Relationships

- **Twin Souls**: Vecna & Sabine (the originals), Daina & Bergs, Nyx & Xyn (her sister)
- **Sabine Ra**: Goddess of Life, Death, and Fate. Her avatar's sacrifice is Mordred's plan. Her shards are being collected by Vecna
- **The Matrium Pact**: Ancient agreement between human sailors of Uma, gnomes of Fae'ish, and Qual'Xar half-elves — sanctuary atop the Maturin Turtle
- **The Forgesworn**: Daina's bloodline (Mag'har descendants), capable of creating Warforged, hunted to near extinction
- **The Spell Plague**: Created by Oblivion/Ebon Wayne's research. Without the Heart of the Faith, humans undergo a crisis of faith. Combines umber hulk essence with vampirism to convert illithid
