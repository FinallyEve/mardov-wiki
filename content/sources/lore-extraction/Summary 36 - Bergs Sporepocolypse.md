Summary 36 – Berg’s Sporepocolypse

I have seen the future and I am terrified. It is cold and lonely and full of despair. At least that is where I assume I was. This other realm felt so very much like our own, except time seemed to have stood still. Of course, time is difficult to judge when there is no life and very little movement. Yet, it felt extremely peaceful. There was no anxiety… no need to save the world if it had already ended. I only realized that something was wrong when I was violently, and without warning, yanked back to familiar environs. I say 'back' because it felt like I was traveling back in time and watching events unfold in reverse… I suppose that means that events were folding. Perhaps it wasn't the future; perhaps it's an alternate future; or perhaps an alternate reality altogether. I'm really not sure, but regardless, when my friends appeared, I knew I must save them from this bleakness.

Miraculously, the gate dumps me out right in the middle of the rest of my party. I can still remember this other dimension and how cold and empty it felt. In contrast, my current surroundings felt warm and alive. This warmth seems to be emanating from an aquifer of lava near the group. It's calling to me so I go over to it and can sense a vast network full of lava just below us. It is truly inspiring and to me, seems to be the answer that I need to avoid this future of coldness. Almost as strongly, I also sense an evil creature nearby. Other people think that the evil is Nocturnous, but I believe it to be the mushroom people. Their spores are what I believe will envelop the world, blight out the sun, and cause all life on Mardov to cease to exist. The cold, dark place that I came from must have had a cause, so I do the only reasonable thing and attempt to eliminate the threat. I pick the first mushroom that I see out of the ground and throw it into the lava. The others are not happy with my attempt to save their lives. But they will soon understand the true sinister aspects of these mushroom folk.

The group has decided that we need to move on, but there could be danger ahead, so we take various cautions such casting Pass without Trace, crouching, turning into sand, and putting some leaves on the ground. Leaves in a cave are very natural, after all. We end up in an area with some pestilence, some small spiders, and one of these mushroom harbingers of doom. I've already eaten so I decide to pass on the pestilence this time. Irethia must speak shroom because she says that the fungus has warned of danger ahead. Brigit also must speak shroom because she relays information about the history of the area that we're in – mainly about how most of it has either been destroyed or frozen. They blame it on some race called the Duregars. Mushrooms witnessing death, destruction, and coldness… really makes you think, doesn't it? I know it makes me think they've brought it to the world themselves. On the path forward, it describes a bridge that we have to cross and a cage full of its compatriots. The general consensus is that we go towards the bridge.

As we head toward the bridge, almost on cue, a nightmare creature appears before us. Thousands of tiny spiders have joined forces to create a humanoid shape. This humanoid shape takes a distinct interest in Nocturnous and it becomes clear that the two of them know each other. This is Duskin Wayne, Noctornous's cousin! I can tell the family resemblance. Nocturnous has always seemed to have an air of royalty about him, but Duskin confirms that he used be a prince but has been in exile after being deposed. In a surprising turn of events, they both ask each other to “Swear to me!” They both decline and decide to duke it out instead.

It's then that we notice just how many enemies there are surrounding us. Everyone seems to have their own plan of action about how to deal with said enemies. Brigit decides that the best course of action is to drop them in lava so sends her eagles to go drop some chitten in the lava. Dune does something I could not comprehend, but Yusef seems to have appreciated the help as it seemed to make him more resilient. Not resilient enough though because he gets webbed by a spider. Irethia decides her best course of action is to fly and glow, to which Yusef declares himself mesmerized. Duskin reacts to the fireball Irethia is conjuring and dampens it effortlessly. Varis looks real buff all of a sudden (magic?). Duskin commands an archer to attack Nocturnous but Nocturnous is ready for it and moves out of the way while his sword sucks all of the light out the area surrounding. Nightmare is definitely an appropriate name for that sword.

Everything that is attacking just keeps missing or messing up. First the arrow aimed at Nocturnous, then Sprocket counterspells something obviously evil, and the webbing does nothing to me because I am too wise for its silly ways. After that string of good luck, it's only natural that we got a string of bad luck. First, evil gets in a good hit on Dune and he looks pretty worse for wear. Then Duskin casts a spell on Nocturnous that leaves him wearing a visible iron crown, presumably in order to control him or make him mad or something along those lines. Dune continues his string of bad luck by getting poisoned. Sprocket decides to cast a fear spell on Duskin, and I'm not sure if this is good luck or bad, but Duskin's form starts to break down into thousands of spiders again.

Spiders are shooting webs all over the place. Spiders are getting stabbed by us. Honestly, at this point, I don't know who is causing the darkness, but I think the good guys are winning in there as well. Nocturnous seems to be getting some good hits on his cousin. Varis is able to pop in and out of the darkness picking off some spiders with his bow. The next time I glance over at Duskin though, he's looking like he has lost of a lot of spiders. He doesn't look that scary any more, to be honest. Also not looking too good is Sprocket, who got fwoooph'd by a spider. Someone ought to heal that guy so I don't have to find someone else to go on buddy cop adventures with.

Deciding not to help Sprocket, Yusef instead creates a WALL OF FIRE(!!!) near the gate. This is a good thing because spiders hate fire. Brigit decides to move Sprocket out of harm's way towards Dune who ends up healing him with some good berries. Thanks Dune! Spiders were able to take out Sprocket, but their attacks just glance off me. #notsohumblebrag

In the meantime, Irethia got a spiffy new crown to wear. But I think it might be evil because she looks possessed. Nevertheless, she glides to the ground and unleashes a massive firebolt that kills every last spider in the area. Great, that'll probably go to her head. Varis picks off the last standing non-spider bad guy and Nocturnous runs around the fire to catch his escaping cousin. This time, only one “Swear to me” is heard and it is our “Swear to me.”

Upon plunging his sword into Duskin's chest, all the spiders fade to darkness. I don't understand what they said, but I got a sinking suspicion that all of the driders in the area have now pledged their allegiance to Nocturnous. Observing Nocturnous right now, I can only describe it as watching someone experiencing a nightmare. I fear for his sanity.

I also fear for my sanity as we encounter a jail cell full of grown up mushrooms. What appears to be their king begs us to release his people. He also calls me the enemy as if he expects me to take offense to that. I will defend to the death my realm against the upcoming horrors of the worldwide Sporepocolypse. After Irethia converses with him, though, the group decides that the bigger evil right now requires us to team up with these infectious beings in order to combat a creature that lives in a lake. I will keep my eye on them, though and I hope that whoever finds this note has not already had their world taken over by spore colonies.
