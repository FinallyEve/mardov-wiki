## Chapter 34 - DM Lead-In

Chapter 34 Dungeon Master Lead In

Last we saw the party together, they had just managed to defeat a pair of Alhoon and a Mindwhitness. The Alhoon were Ulilitharid Necromancers, elite form of mindflayer, and they were doing a series of experiments on natural creatures (hobgoblins and gnolls) as well as on their own kind (Vampire Illithid & Zombie Illithid) and perhaps their most horrifying experiment of all was the mindwhitness, a BEHOLDER which was maimed and forced to undergo ceremorphisis, the process of becoming a mind flayer. These creatures act as some sort of

network for one of the 4 figures within the stags last vision, Peraxis Phyrexia, the Illithilich (Illithid Lich!).

Irethia, Nocturnous Wayne, Sprocket and Tezka were all summoned to VODRAM, to help out MAC and the Resistance Fighters! After a trip down their perspective rabbit holes, the party finds themselves returned to the scene

of their last great group battle.

THIS BETTER HAVE ANSWERS! and if it doesn’t? well she still needs to see where this Heart of the Forge is. after all, Tor spent the last of his free will to tell her that he was dying, even though he was already dead in her eyes, she'd already shed those tears, they had dried, why now. What is so great about this key?!? She knows one thing though. She's going to do one thing her father wanted for sure. she was going to FIGHT! Fight her way all the way back to the dwarven capital and find more answers!

- Nocturnus spent his watch strangely drawn to the strange black sword with the glass pommel, even if it was in Brigit bag.... it was calling HIM, clearly it thought he was the only one who was worthy. And worthy he felt as over in VODRAM the sword was strangely more powerful, and it was GLORIOUS, those cultists deserved every bit of what’s coming to them. But now he’s been teleported back, the blade in hand in front of BRIGIT who spent the whole time as Reynaldine's pet bird... But he has the perfect weapon to challenge his Aunt, that bitch. She deposed him. She is the reason he must roam as an exile, unable to watch over his people. If he couldn’t be their dark knight, he would be a symbol of vengeance. The will of his people. Reynaldine even went so far as to kill Annie by walking her into the quickly rising tide of pure pestilence! Even as Annie died, Reynaldine was able to take over Lorelei’s body without her even having said his name 3 times. Though strangely she was also exempt from that rule. And just as she was starting to think she was special to Reynaldine, she was NOT sent to Reynaldine's pocket dimension with chocolate and marshmallows and capture the flag, no... she was sent to VODRAM, the land ruled by the dead and a stark reminder that things can get so much worse if GARD' DAEMON is allowed to happen! Why? All of these questions, and only strange answers. He wants her to steal the earth genasi prince from the clutches of PERAXIS, in return for a favor. And so she has decided to go DOWN into the WAYS and cross to beneath VAG'MAHAR, the dwarven capital city.

## Chapter 37 - DM Lead-In

Chapter 37 – Dungeon Master Lead In – The Death of Duskin Wayne

Last We Saw Our Heroes, they had decided to split up:

- Following the drider that made off with Daina's Father's Axe: Daina, Tezka, Randall, Nyx, Stalwart, Finn, Bardin, Bartim, Slyvia, and Dierdre all worked together to chase down the lost fireforged weapon. They managed to track the drider to an extremely long bridge over open water, the drider was eventually caught thanks to a mix of Daina's determination, Dierdre's Locate Object, and Nyx's Flying broom. However, the drider had fallen into the clutches of a group of psions of the Sleepless, the creature just walked into the deep, something horrible, large, and possessing a body of tentacles ripped the drows body from its spider half, the ax dropping into the maw of the beast. The battle with the scions was bloody but the creature faded into the near black water. The party had interrogated the last remaining psion, who gave up his name after Stalwart, Bardin, and Daina succeeded in ripping off his left arm, the sleeve of his leather sailor’s jerkin bearing the symbol of a White Whale with the silhouette of a ship sailing into it. The name left behind: Devin Darkeyes...

- Sprocket, Nocturnous, Brigit, Varis, Bergs, Irethia, Dune, and Yousef all decided to deal with the drider threat... sneakily. They were doing pretty good, that is until they are spotted by a spider that happened to be more than your average spider, in fact it was made up of hundreds, when the mass re-coagulated it was in the form of DUSKEN WAYNE, Dusken asks NOCTURNUS to swear to him and abdicate his father’s throne to Dusken in exchange for being turned into a drider and joining his cousin at the head of the last mutated remnant of the Drow race. However, Dusken was not prepared for the bloodlust which gripped Nocturnous, his sword of nightmare dripping with black tendrils fog as he lunged at his cousin, initiating a bout of combat which nearly led to the death of SPROCKET after divining a even more mortifying nightmare for Dusken, the image of OBLIVION. Dusken fades back into the darkness and attempted to flee, Nocturnous hot on his heels.

As the battle raged, IRETHIA revealed her true form, reining fiery death on spiders below catching the ire of the Drider king, who bestow a crown of madness upon her in retaliation, and the fearful curiosity of YOUSEF despite the distraction that rescuing DUNE from certain death with a Greater Invisibility. Under the cover of invisibility Dune, received communication that the others were gripped in battle with a large machination made of metal and wielding 2 massive sheets of lava tempered steel creating a wall between the party and moving forward on the bridge.

Dashing around a wall of fire, summoned by YOUSEF in a futile attempt to break the grip that Dusken had on Irethia, Nocturnous Wayne slams his sword down through the chest of the second to last of his kin, accompanied by an almost uncharacteristically primal roar of "SWEAR TO ME!" Darkness descended upon Dusken Wayne as the tendrils of black fog burst through Dusken’s chest, seemingly growing stronger as the thousands of tiny spiders were consumed. Nocturnous is thrust into a vision, as the surviving drider surrendered chanting: "All Hail The New King"

Darkness... then NOCTURNOUS was thrust into an immaculate white marble room. There in the center of the room, stood Dusken, now in the form Nocturnous remembered, The Drow Leader called out to his usurper "You have a responsibility cousin, Slay her."

Dusken tells Nocturnous of the dire circumstances that caused the last loyal followers of his father to abandon their race in a misguided attempt to please Lolth, their Spider Goddess. Dusken also tells of a dueregar forge, the first of its kind, which is supposed to be buried deep below a pocket of lava. Nocturnous is woken from his madness, just as Dusken warned him of the sleepless.

Pressed for time to reunite and reinforce the others in their fight with the metallic automatons, the party agrees to head back over to the bridge discovered during a particularly interesting commune with nature performed by Brigit, as it brought to light the existence of a massive chamber of lava which rests below the PURELAKE. However before setting out, there was the matter of the mushroom people held in the drider cages, rescuing the Myconid Soverign and his followers. They showed their gratitude by revealing that the sleepless is an aboleth, immortal and older than the gods themselves. There’s only one way to kill and Aboleth...

You need to slay it once in the material plane and once in the plane of water before it can be reborn.

## Chapter 40 - DM Lead-In

Chapter 40 – Dungeon Master Lead In – Cleaning Up and Getting Out

Last we saw our heroes they were faced with the aftermath of having slain Dierdre Crestfallen, the paladin of Bahamut who’s capture led to her exchanging her oath of protection sworn to Bartim Hadrin for a new oath to the D’Guarda Forge sworn to an aboleth known as the Sleepless. The room was still as Sylvia Hadrin mourned her father’s demise within the metallic storage tank that makes up most of D’Guarda castle. A black memory swirling above each of the remaining living beings within the now pactless forge, endlessly reciting each of the predecessors oaths to the forge.

After Randal activated the archives upon the anvils placed in a ring before the forge, it became clear that the party was tricked by the Efreeti, Ifrin the Pactbound, into releasing him from his prison in D’Vinter Castle, and at the same time damned poor Dierdre to her fate as the new pactbound for BOTH castles. It became clear once the party tried to leave to take out their anger on the mastermind of this plot, the Sleepless, that one of their party must make the same oath that Diedre and countless others have made before, including one Thorkon Fireforge.

Deciding to stay behind, Sylvia agreed to being bound to the D’Vinter Forge claiming that her life ended the day her father’s did. Alone for seemingly an eternity within D’Vinter Castle, Sylvia remained.

Without a leader, the remains of Bartrims Brezerkers ascended with the party to the lair of the Aboleth who had caused them no end of suffering. It was time for revenge.

Daina would have her father’s axe back, securing her birthright as a Fireforge and Forgesworn fo the Axe.

Fin would have revenge for taking his eyes, brother and peaceful life in Monethern away from him.

Bardin, Dune, and Yousef would avenge the loss of founding members of their band of misfit mercenaries.

Randal would meet a being who is older than the Gods and discover the key to him joining the pantheon.

Stalwart and Tezka could not release Sylvia from her Oath but they can sure as hell kill the being that orphaned the poor girl.

The fight was BRUTAL, with a number of the party facing what seemed like insurmountable odds. Even with Sylvia’s newfound ability to sense the party through the duergar statues, a number of party members nearly succumbed to death’s relaxing embrace. Tezka and Brigitte now seemingly poisoned by the Aboleths mucus, help arrived from the most unlikely of places.

As the fight began to look more and more dire, Sprocket devised a plan to squash this abomination before it had a chance to claim any of his friends. Hopping into a dimension door and reaching past the lint in his pocket, he produced a wooden coin with a ship engraved upon it. Now falling from 45 ft above the beast, he licked the coin to hydrate the odd trophy. There 40 ft above the aboleth who had been thrust out of the water due to Nocturnous’ quick thinking, a sailing ship with a crew of 20, weighing a total of 20 TONS, magically was summoned and materialized. Now falling at the same speed, Sprocket was able to swing his way aboard the vessel, coming face to face with a wide-eyed pink haired gnome just as the boat hits the iceblock just as Nocturnous released his hold, the icewater and Aboleth crushed beneath it.

Irethia, seeing the aboleth return to its home dimension of the Plane of Water, quickly banished the dying creature BACK to Mardov in order for this death to be its final. The body slumped up on the deck, our heroes come face to face with Captain Brownbeard Rummy of the Sea Urchin.

The fight is still not up, as the banished hydra and bubbled marid are still hostile and their imprisonment is limited…

## Chapter 41 - DM Lead-In

The entire team was caught in their path and were forced to travel through.. It is here that we find our heroes. Hit with the dry arid desert air and overwhelming light of the sun. The sounds of a market of some sorts chattering on the wind.

Using the newfound knowledge of Kelik’s link to his “father”, Nyx scryed the Storm King to find that he IS the ever-present storm encircling Windhaven! However after some time, she was able to find his … more corporeal form inside his throne room, seemingly alone, save for the imprisoned blue-skinned woman inside a gemstone suspended at eye level to the giant despot. Further investigation led to the party learning that this was Lapis, the Water Genasi Princess and counterpart to Kelik. Nyx was unable to determine if she still possessed her amulet, the party presses on with a bit more fire in their step.

Sheltered from the storm by Kelik and Wind-Brigit, the party made their way out onto the fields of Windhaven despite warnings of a thunderstorm rolling in. Things went rather smoothly at the beginning of their journey, that is until Fen noticed a large shape in the distance, there is a flash of yellow light and the far-too-big-to-be-a-bird shape dive-bombed something perhaps 3 miles ahead on their path.

Sprocket and Stalwart decided to team up while keeping and eye out for strange things on their path and came across a Mammoth print with a massive feather in it. The feather was easily the size of Sprockets arm and covered in a strange black ichor that reacts to their presence. The duo pocket a vial of the strange ooze after watching it eat the metal of the knife used to remove it from the feather.

The party then came across the scene of a great battle between orcs and humans, however NONE of the bodies were wearing armor. Suspicious that there might be more of this ooze and pressed for time, Stalwart decides to grab a body at random and rejoin the group instead of picking through the remains. All the commotion of dealing with the slime seemingly caught the attention of a roc flying overhead, the party presses on at a slower and more cautious rate. Again Fen Darkeyes spots a flash of light high in the storm ahead, however this time it was a cool blue lightning and a distinctly larger shape producing it. The lightning felled the roc following the party, however it is unknown if this is a good or bad thing.

Back on track, the party comes to a calm grassy knoll, which is strange as its location is still within the Storm King’s Maelstrom. However it becomes evidence shortly that this location is under the effect of a dapper dressed slender man with a shifting autumn skin-tone, Reynaldine of the Autumn Court. ## Chapter 48T - DM Lead-In

Chapter 48T - Dungeon Master Lead In

Last we saw our heroes:

The party was stuck in a great maelstrom without their guide, the party had turned down the offers of a guide and were starting to regret that decision. Luckily Brigit’s new form was enough to provide most of them cover until they buddied up and teleported ABOVE the storm, relatively smooth sailings.

Bypassing some rather nasty foes on the road, including a distant red dragon seen by Fen dive bombing something off in the distance, the party stealthily attempted to descend within the Second Eye of the Everpresent Storm. However a few of them were spotted by a pair of trained Roc’s who immediately gave away their position.

The cracked surface of the Second Eye is marred with a network of canyons plunging deeper into the sandy landscape juxtaposed with the grassland of the surrounding area. Announcing their arrival with a pair of large boulders flying in the air towards the party, a pair of storm giant scouts also enter the fray. After a series of increasingly more dangerous brushes with death, the party finally managed to push their assailants off the edge of the disconnected plateau and into the canyon below.

Taking their time, the party then managed to hide their presence well enough to find a grove in the middle of the eye. The grove itself was decorated with a single ancient birch tree that at one time was struck by lightning causing the tree to split down the middle creating an archway as the two halves grew to reconnect at the top. All along the tree were a network of druidic runes and the porous nature of the surrounding earth gave walking in this area a lighter than air gravitas. Written amongst the runes was a phrase in druidic:

“Tel' alusfaen Ath Tel' first shall welsa Tel' ya, Kaweh ent pay Tel' toll”

Or in common: “The blood of the first shall open the way, speak destination and pay the toll”

It was then that Fen and Varys both decided to add their blood to the soil causing the tree to activate and open a portal …. To cloud city!
