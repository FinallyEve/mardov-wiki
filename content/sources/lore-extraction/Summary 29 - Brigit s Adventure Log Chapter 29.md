Brigit’s Adventure Log Chapter 29

Welp. I fucked up a little bit. Turns out that I’m not completely perfect at transforming yet. When I was rushing to grab the Nightmare sword and the stag antlers from Veccna’s world, I quickly transformed back out of my wolf form. In my haste, something went wrong and I lost my voice! I was stuck without the ability to speak, and instead I had to write everything down for my companions – It was exhausting not being able to explain myself, or offer my opinion. Hopefully, it’ll be fixed with my next rest, or maybe my next transformation? I don’t know, I’m just hoping it comes back soon.

We were transformed back to our group somewhere new. When we were transported away, we had just left the Tomb of the Last Curator, and had gone into Eden to meet Cloyma. Now, we were on the banks of a river, near the Valley of The Anchors (!!!) There’s a leathery and old gnome in front of us – with a strange mushroom on his head. Maybe this is some sort of tribal thing? I wonder if Sprocket knows. He said he was a sporekeeper, and simply put they keep spores to grow a village. I have so many questions. Do they live in the mushrooms? Do they eat their dwellings? I don’t know. And no one is reading my notes.

As a group, we followed the gnome down to his village, where there is some strange landscaping. There are almost pimples growing from the ground causing it to look topsy turvy, like a rug was pulled out from underneath it, causing it to lean one way or another. The gnome explained that it’s the “green tides of St. Ashbury” and that it looks strange because it’s currently under development. These people are not very forthcoming. I don’t know about this place. Tezka, polite as ever, asks for some spores from the sporekeeper and she and Randall are granted two spores!

We arrive at the village where there are lots of gnomes and non-gnomes of various shapes and sizes. It’s the middle of the night, and 7 gnomes come to greet us. Some of us notice that among the “hills” there is a strange shadowy figure wave at us, hold up 3 fingers and leave. Irethia also reveals that she saw the shadowy figure when we teleported in! She messaged the figure, who told Irethia to not trust anything we see – and meet her in the inn later that night. ALSO SHE HAS THE OTHER STAG ANTLER. HOLY FUCKING SHIT GIVE ME.

Back at the village, all of the strangers surrounding us are wearing their strange mushroom hats. I am reminded of a story I once was told about blood sucking beings. This seems pretty suspicious. I tried to alert the others, but Tezka, Randall and Linda all don a magical hat, and we find out that while they are very stylish and fit ever so perfectly, they don’t come off and the gnomes don’t understand why we would ever want them off.

A gnome with goggles introduces herself as Darla to Nyx. She is the secretary for Dr. Grackle, the man with the hats. She says to come visit them in the morning, they have many wares that we can buy. Eventually, we head to the tavern/inn, The Tetherpole! The door inside is less than 3 feet tall, so some of us have a bit of trouble ducking through, but when we enter we find a huge dome. It’s a little musty, but it’s actually quite pleasant. We drink, and eat (or in some cases pretend to), and Randall sings us the tale of Fun Gus! Despite my suspicions, it was actually quite a pleasant night. Eventually, everyone slinks away to bed. Irethia, Varis and I wait up for the stranger, and we also take pity on Sprocket and let him sleep in our room. Daina can be quite intimidating for such a small creature.

As I opened the door to my room, it finishes opening by itself, and we’re meted by Grinda Fuk’tul! The hobgoblin from the tomb. She warns us that the village is not what it appears to be. She was the leader of a clan of hobgoblins, and she just woke up missing her left hand. The last thing she remembers is her husband meeting a strange fellow who came into town talking about an underground fortress. She left her village to fight gnols, the enemies of gnomes. She pulls out her stag horn, and I showed her the matching set! She also knows where the stag is! She can take us to see him tomorrow, apparently she talked to him, and even knows that he is Shishigami. She leaves quickly, promising to meet us by the river tomorrow, she doesn’t want to stay in this unnatural structure for longer than she has to.

We set up a watch, and sleep. During the night (or so I’m told), Varis wakes and notices smoke coming from my pack, and wakes everyone up, but I didn’t stir. After some time, the smoke faded into me and disappeared. I awoke feeling slightly different, but nothing really changed, at least not then.

In the morning, I pass a note to everyone without a hat – The village is not what it seems to be and we need to keep our eyes open. Don’t put on a hat. And after breakfast, we head to the curio shop. Randall buys some fabulous sequined boots, Tezka buys a helm and wants to take off her mushroom hat, but the only help the doctor can give is the idea that a spell of Greater Restoration might help. Stalwart alerts me that he’s hearing strange things from Linda (via his earring), faint whispers of something. And Tezka and Randall also look preoccupied by something, but we’re not sure yet. Suddenly, Varis screams fire and pushes everyone out before going around back to see what happens. Also, Tezka decides to burn off her mushroom hat and it works! She still feels weird, but she’s no longer hearing voices.

We see a group of gnol’s and hyena’s (gnol/knoll, get it?). The Gnoll’s are nomadic and attack these defenseless villages. Varis is on top of his game and fires off the first shot, followed quickly by Daina smashing out at a hyena angrily. Tezka comes in with some punches to back up Daina. Nocturnous McWayne also decides to punch a hyena. Randall, wanting to use his new dancing shoes, does a quick jig and Eldridge Blasts! Daina hits out again at the hyena and kills it! Varis shoots the main gnoll dude and hits! But definitely takes some fire from the enemy team. Some hyena came out to bite at me – oh just you wait until I get my turn. Nyx hexes a hyena and blasts him! The various gnols and hyenas snap and hit out at us, but mostly miss. I turn into a wolf, and a shadow emits from me, and surrounds my claws and teeth, I bite out at the hyena and take a chunk out of him and hear a voice in the back of my head “excellent”. This sword might not be worth it. I’m definitely going to have to pay attention. Irethia obliterates one of the gnols. Stalwart hits out at a hyena and hits! Sprocket springs into action and shatters the ground, hitting 3 at once! Tezka kills the first gnol with her furry of blows! Nocturnous Wayne punches out at a gnol and punches straight through his skull killing it!

Randall takes a moment to breathe and starts speaking at the head gnol. They’re looking for their captain. We have 5 minutes to produce the captain, so Daina runs inside to grab Darla and the doctor, but they’re gone, so instead she steals a potion of climbing. Meanwhile, Randall and Tezka notice that the whole village is on fire. Irethia sends a message to Darla, who says we should run and they’re not telling us where the captain is. Before Irethia can get more information, a voice cuts off Darla and tells Irethia that she is not the only one here.

Daina is exploring the huts and pulls out a halfling from the last one. She doesn’t acknowledge or move in any way, but her mushroom hat has safely burned off. She simply just doesn’t seem to be there anymore. Randall, with his magical hat powers, gets an image of an underground cavern and an entrance, but doesn’t recognize it. There’s a massive spiral going down into the ground, down into oblivion. The gnol grabs his shoulder, and says that they are going to warn us if this is a trap. Before we leave, the hyena’s go over to the dead gnol and eat it. When they’re finished, one of the gnols cuts a hyena’s neck and from the beast, a gnol, fully formed appears. Additionally the gnol corpse also rises and is commanded to march. Why the hell are we aligning ourselves with these monsters?

After Randall explains the cavern that he saw, Nocturnous reveals that he also knows about it. It’s burnt and ashy, but Nyx cleans it! He found a letter to Grinda. There is a hidden cave underneath the Valley of the Anchors. He has gone to go check it out to find something to defeat the gnols. He’s taken most of the clan and his sisters, and Grinda needs to join them when she’s ready. He’s says to go to the tree with the handprint, and the stone will open.

Stalwart goes up to Randall and without warning breathes fire on his hat. It’s really hard to laugh as a wolf, it does not come out clean. Randall apparently hears a faint whisper of a scream, but is feeling mostly back to himself. Unfortunately, we no longer have any idea where we are going. Despite this, we head away from the Valley of the Anchors until we see the edge of a sinkhole. Daina is carrying the halfling while Stalwart carries Sprocket so he can concentrate on reading the halfling’s thoughts. She is in the corner of her mind, rocking in a ball. “Gotta get away, gotta get away…”. Nyx tries to message her, and the halfling reveals that whatever had her, whatever has her, had tentacles and got into her brain, and she can’t feel anything anymore. It came from below. Nyx gets a quick flash of a holding cell on the cliff. A mind flayer.

Varis and wolf-me sneak off to go find Grinda. We’re late and she’s angry, but Varis manages to convince her to come with us. She’s going to lead us as best as she can to the cave, and she will follow behind our ragtag group. We almost successfully sneak back, with only Nocturnous noticing. While we were gone, the party had sent hyena’s into the cave. Since Varis knows the way to go, but can’t reveal his source, he tries to explain that he found hidden tracks, but it doesn’t work. I’m still in wolf form, so I try to emulate those disgusting hyena’s as best as I can. I run along the path, “sniffing out” my way forward, and bark back at the group, pointing out the way. Luckily the gnols are too stupid to notice that I’m a wolf, not a hyena, and blindly follow. Randall recognizes where we are.

We come to a huge abyss, headed straight down. Varis senses out that there are tons of undead beings below us and somewhat back towards the village. It’s like the group that was coming for us while we were in the tomb, but luckily this world does not have nearly as many undead beings as Veccna’s world. Nyx sends Hedwig down the abyss to recon. There’s a tunnel coming out of the wall about 200 ft down, but the owl goes further and further and further down, it’s almost never ending. There are holding cells all along the walls, empty and with their doors closed. Many of them contain starving gnols who have tried to tear through the walls in their desperation. Hedwig notices one huge gnol sitting patiently, watching her with a keen eye, which we assume is their captain. And at the very very bottom (5000 ft?) the stag is patiently waiting for us. We have got to get to him.

The gnols are still with us as we’re discussing the situation, and one of them decides that the best course of action is to jump down. I told you, stupid beasts. In a similar fashion, Daina grabs Nocturnous, catching him off guard, screams at him to slow fall and rides him down to safety. I don’t know how we’re going to get down this cliff, but I am sure as shit glad I can fly.

Until next time,

Brigit.

P.S. Alright I’ve got a plan: I’m going to turn into a giant eagle and run interference for the party. I would love to catch up with the stag, and get some insight into the greater adventure, but I’ve got to be a safety net for these guys. We’ve been through way too much together.
