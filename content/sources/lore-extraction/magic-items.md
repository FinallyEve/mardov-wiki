## 1. Nightmare (Sentient Cursed Longsword)

**Source:** `Nigtmare/Nightmare.txt` + `Effects of Nightmare.jpg`

A cursed, sentient blade that is a mixture of the Eye of Vecna and Vecna's own power manifest. Acts similarly to a Lich's phylactery.

### Stats (In Vecna's World / Our World)

| Property | Vecna's World | Our World |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Enhancement | +2/+2 longsword | +1/+1 longsword |
| Darkness | Cast at level 4, 1/day | Cast at level 3, 1/day |
| Blindsight | In Darkness | In Darkness |
| Overwhelming Darkness DC | 15 | 14 |
| Nightmare damage | 8d6 (DC 15) | 6d6 psychic (DC 14) |

**Overwhelming Darkness:** Bonus action, target up to 2 creatures within or adjacent to Darkness. Athletics/Acrobatics check or grappled.

**Nightmare:** Once/day action. All creatures grappled by Overwhelming Darkness must save or take damage (half on success).

### Sentience

| Stat | Score |
|------|-------|
| STR | 10 |
| DEX | 10 |
| WIS | 16 |
| INT | 14 |
| CHA | 20 |

Saving throws: +9 CHA, +8 WIS. Spell save DC: 17.

**Spellcasting (Level 4, 2x Lv4 slots/day):**
- Charm Person
- Suggestion
- Dominate Person (last resort -- users lose trust in the blade afterward)

### Curse Mechanics

**Nightly Attunement:** Each night, Nightmare attempts forced attunement on nearest person. Contested Wisdom check. Failure = attuned. Success = nightmares but full rest benefits.

**Initiative Hijack:** 25% chance when rolling initiative that Nightmare forces itself to be drawn (shadow wraps around wielder's weapon).

**Goal:** Find a willing host and slowly corrupt them, eventually transferring the Eye of Vecna's soul into the new host.

### Corruption Points

Gained by killing a being while attuned (max 1/day). Lost by: Greater Restoration, or 3 days sleeping un-attuned.

| Points | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 1 | Becomes cruel toward enemies, aims to cause maximum pain |
| 2 | Cannot show mercy in combat (DC 14 WIS save to spare enemies) |
| 3 | Loses hair, appears slightly gaunt |
| 4 | Roll on short-term madness table |
| 5 | Hears the dead Eye's voice -- sounds like a good friend offering "sound" advice |
| 6 | Roll on long-term madness table |
| 7 | Loses remaining hair, appears very gaunt |
| 8 | Insatiable need to cut off left hand (DC 10 + days at 8 WIS save). Cannot gain more corruption until done. |
| 9 | Insatiable need to cut out left eye (DC 8 + days at 9 WIS save). Cannot gain more corruption until done. |
| 10 | **Becomes the dead Eye of Vecna.** Character becomes an NPC. Possible salvation for the original soul, but unlikely. |
