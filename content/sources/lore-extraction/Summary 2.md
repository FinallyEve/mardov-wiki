SUMMARY of Chapter 2 The Gnome Eff’n Way:

Last week we found our heroes the midst of an epic battle beneath the sewers of Argos. Before combat we discovered that Finkle Hollingsworth was actually a Lich named Mordred who chose to reveal himself to you all and command Stalwart Little to kill Jorgon because he had outlived his usefulness as a mercenary/kobold trainer. After 2000 souls, Mordred finally had enough souls to complete the ritual of rebirth, a ritual that requires the large phylactery. The phylactery contains a Demi-Lich of current unknown origin and allegiance dunked beneath the four endless steams of liquid essence:

1) RED blood, hot to the touch

2) ORANGE mercury mixed with molten iron

3) BLUE liquid souls

4) GREEN poison, corrosive like acid

Finkle underestimated the ingenuity of the adventuring party. First, the party decided to spare Jorgen and listen to his warning to not complete the ritual. Once again being able to speak Draconic came in handy, as Stalwart could convince the angry Kobolds to forgo their need for revenge on Jorgen for imprisoning them and decide to join in the fight against Mordred and his two half-ogre flunkies, Gromble/Stromble and Strom/Grom.

Mordred was less than amused and decided to collect the last souls needed himself. He raised his gnarled fingers and every Kobold in the room was immediately drained dry, their corpses contorted and fused together to form an army of Ghouls, hungry for flesh. He also revealed the enslaved bodies of three of his last batch of adventurer's, Corramperre a Tiefling Sorcerer and two unnamed meat shields.

During all this hullaballoo, Daina notices the large crystalline Demi-Lich skull inside the phylactery and Sprocket attempts to touch the suspended object using mage hand. As soon as the magic connection was made with the Demi-Lich, Sprocket was thrust into a deep trance. By all accounts this feat would normally have resulted in Sprocket’s soul being immediately consumed by the being of pure necromancy.

Brotor and Tiny Rick made quick work of Stromble/Gromble only to dismay as Grom/Strom pushed the phylactery of the Demi-Lich underneath the first liquid, RED blood. The liquid mixed with the bubbling semi-clear liquid that was already occupying the container, however the massive vial never overflowed despite the endless waterfall that continued to pour into it.

After receiving a knock on the back of the head from Nocturnus Wayne, searing the half ogres face on contact, Nyx tried to disrupt the order of the ritual by pulling the wrong lever, causing the phylactery to reject the green acid, creating a large pool on the floor.

Just when things looked to be turning grim, Dallor Dallorbill decided to cut a deal with Mordred to save himself from being murdered before the ritual. The ritual inched ever closer to being completed as the already risen adventurers pulled the orange lever.

The adventurers turned their efforts towards shattering the phylactery encasing the Demi-Lich. Sprocket dealt the first blow, creating a small crack in the eye of the Raven Queen’s sigil Daina also managed to widen the crack, that only invoked Mordred’s anger. The barbarian was obliterated by a powerful spell from the phylactery. Despite this loss, the party managed to destroy the phylactery shortly after. The Demi-Lich was freed and immediately the Ghouls turned on their former master, as if the balance of power in the room shifted. The Demi-Lich lashed out at Mordred, and the only witness to this was Stalwart who had decided to play dead instead of joining them. Stalwart completed the ritual and a bright light engulfed the room.
