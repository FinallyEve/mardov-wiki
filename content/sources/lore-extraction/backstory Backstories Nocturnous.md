Nocturnus Wayne:

Drow nobility where my parents were assassinated by a ninja in the guise of a thief while we were on the way back home from the theater. We were ousted due to the struggle that was happening with the other drow nobility.

Martha and Thomas Wayne were killed during the time of the ousting and the reason the ninja was in disguise was to hide this fact.

Nocturnus and his 4-year-old sister, Barbarella, ran away from the underdark. During that time, Barbarell was killed as we were continually hunted prompting Nocturnus to train to never again be weak to protect the ones he loves. Starting with a regiment of doing 100 push ups, 100 sit ups, 100 squats, 10km running every single day.

The death of my sister is why I care about the welfare of children and will do anything for the greater good.
