## 1. Hot Air Balloon Manual

**Author:** Chief Gringlesprocket (Gnome)
**Type:** Technical manual

A manual for the world's first "Aeronautical Elevation System" -- a hot air balloon system inside a vertical shaft. Key details:

- **Ascent:** Open separation valve (DC 16 Strength), light forge-engine fire, heat 120 cubic stones of air in increments of 10 degrees per 10 cubes, close valve, transfer air.
- **Descent:** Drip water over flame to cool air in controlled increments. Full system shutdown if fire goes out before reaching bottom.

**DM Notes (in Dwarvish):**
- Water descent: ~3 damage per 6s, approximately 4 turns total (~4.8 minutes)
- Brace basket edges to reduce noise from swinging

**DM Notes (in Gnomish):**
- Colony of great cave spiders inside the shaft, led by something more powerful
- Major nest at 50-Stone height (DC 26 Athletics/Stealth to pass quietly)
- Spiders only chase during descent; ascending too fast avoids them
- Spider webbing: AC 10, 15 HP, vulnerable to fire, immune to bludgeoning/piercing/psychic
- Cave spider blood is alcoholic -- adds 9 health to fire. Kill spiders with javelin to center (+24 to hit), collect blood in bucket

## 9. Thorkon's Note inside Daina's Axe

**Author:** Thorkon Fireforge
**Recipient:** Daina (Diesa Fireforge)
**Type:** Hidden letter -- the campaign's core backstory document

**Key revelations:**
- Daina's real name is **Diesa**
- Her mother was **Hiln** (Hlin) -- fierce warrior, arena champion, Maghar of the Hammer
- Hiln named Daina "Diesa" after her grandmother
- Hiln died giving birth to Daina and an **older brother** during their flight from Vag'Mahar
- The axe was Hiln's, promised to Daina
- Thorkon's real name is **Thorkon Fireforge**, not "Tor Ironforge"
- He is a **Forgesworn** -- descendant of the 12 Maghar
- Forgesworn burden: creation of weapons. Each creation takes a bit of the creator's soul.
- Forgesworn were hunted to extinction, forced to create artifacts for kings
- **Thorkon's crime:** He was royal weaponsmith for **Thorson Bronzebeard II**, last great leader of Vag'Mahar
- Bronzebeard wanted to create more Warforged. Thorkon discovered **Warforged consume dwarven souls** when created -- only Forgesworn can make them
- **Ghost-Eye Thorrin** (adviser to the Redbeard Thane) convinced Thorkon to betray the king
- Thorkon created a cursed soul-forged dagger as a gift to Thorson
- Ghost-Eye then betrayed the deal and tried to round up all Forgesworn
- Most escaped, but some were captured
- **Trust Decan Smithrunner** at the Dragon's Carapace shop. Show him the mother's seal.
- Find the mother's statue at the arena -- "You'll know what to do"
