Summary 23 - Nocturnous Log

*Gruff Voice Activated*

It was a dark December night. I’m still traveling with the Scarlet Tunic Brigade for reasons unknown to even me. Everything I do is for the greater good and these mutinous bastards still refuse to see me as I am, their rightful Mayor. #YesImYourMayor

Everyone is essentially exhausted from the Trial of the Heart. It was odd having to best myself with another’s abilities. But now I know what I need to train to become even stronger. As soon as the trail was done, we all appeared on a platform with either X’s or O’s drawn on themselves. As I have an O on me, I assume the others with O’s were able to best themselves.

The platform begins to rise, and continues to rise up. Some of the others seem to be nervous, but I was born to be at the peak of the world!

There was a bright purple lightning, but as soon as I saw it, it was gone. And before us is that useless bard that I killed before, Dallor. How he stays living is a wonder. If he betrays us, I’ll get the pleasure of disposing of him. Again.

As I walk out onto the walkway, I just see the loud dragonborn throw Ashara off of the ledge for no reason. Doing what heroes do, I use my trusty grappling hook to save her and hoist her unconscious body back up. Since we can’t keep her near that thieving, murderous Stalwart, I tie her up and leave her outside against a wall.

As I’ve finished leaving Ashara behind, I hear this booming voice say, “Let me heal your wounds”! I feel rested as I’m teleported into a large room. I look around and see that Ashara is teleported in this enclosed room. I’ll have to keep an eye on Stalwart to make sure he doesn’t try to murder her again.

Before us is haggard man with purple magic pulsating through his tattoos. Many try to speak, but only those with O’s may speak. We may have a surrogate ask a question for us, but we have 5 minutes to decide. No one but the asker will know what question is asked, but everyone will hear the answer to the question.

From here, Ashara is awake and is willing to sell her question. Dallor, willing to take any opportunity to make money wants 30k gold to ask her question. If she fucks with Dallor, he said that he’s going to tell the Platinum Council, which she seems oddly scared of.

The others ask their questions, but I pay no mind to them. Because before the time for my question comes up, the moment of my parents’ death flash within my mind. The show that we just saw, the drizzle as we were walking out, the terrible moment that the assassin posing as a mugger murdered my parents. I swore I would get revenge for their deaths.

Today, I shall have the means.

It comes to me to ask my question. My uncle and cousin are already dead, but they are just pawns for the true mastermind. My aunt. I ask specifically what I need to do to get revenge, interrogate, and for me to survive the ordeal against my aunt.

His answer were specific in return, which was to my satisfaction. Travel to Vagnarhar, Enter the Mountain Zenith, Find the Kiln of the First Flame and activate the First Forge. This will prompt my aunt to lead an invasion from which I will use the War Forge against the army to defeat them. I’ll then find that murderous bitch hiding in my childhood home. I will have my answers.

After that, the others drone on with their questions. I can neither hear the questions, nor care about the answers.

Finally, the last question is asked and answered. I see that the Alabaster Seat snaps his fingers after saying something about being disappointed. From there Ashara shatters into a thousand shards. I hear Dallor yell out in agony as he sees his golden ticket shatter.

I smirk to myself at this turn of events for Dallor. Although we eventually find out that she is still alive and waiting in Dawnpass.

As we tell the Alabaster Seat about the deal with the Lich, we’re under attack. We get transported into a forest where I see a giant Elk and then hear the roar of a T-Rex right behind it.

It becomes evident that I’ll have to save these fools. Again.

Irethia Firebolts the T-Rex which causes her to call out to her mate. Everyone gets into their fighting stances. Daina gets angry and runs up to hit it in the face while Dallor shoots a crossbow at the T-Rex. To protect Daina, Stalwart casts Shield of Faith on Daina which is just in time, because the T-Rex bites down on Daina and slaps Stalwart with his tail. Sprocket tries to spring into action, but whatever he does just fizzles out. Varys tells the Giant Elk to go hide and it bolts away. After this happens Brigit turns into an Elk and tries to ram the T-Rex. Somehow she misses and has the T-Rex’s leg in between her antlers. The T-Rex takes this opportunity to bite at Brigit and rips out one of her antlers. I have to come in to deal the majority of the damage to the beast with my flurry of consecutive punches and end up underneath it. Tezka takes this opportunity to damage the already wounded T-Rex with 4 punches. Thinking that she was somehow superior, said that’s how a real monk hits. I’ll have teach her how monks work later. *cracks knuckles*

Out from the woods, two hooded figures appear. They come in and turn towards the Remaining T-Rex and attack it. Turns out these two are Ruth and Sheila. Sheila comes in with her crossbow while Ruth casts Eldridge Blast.

Irethia then Banishes the T-Rex to a plane of nothingness for 60 seconds. We take this time to attack the non-banished T-Rex. Dallor Heats the metal arrow that is already in the T-Rex’s back. Daina cleaves it’s right toe and then disgustingly picks it up and drinks from it….. The dragonborn then tries to ram it. Failing this, he then Smites the T-Rex and breaks its other large toe, causing the monster to fall over! The monster then goes after Stalwart and snaps at him, but misses. Sprocket finally does something and fireballs the T-Rex. Varys marks the T-Rex and hits it once. Brigit, not being used to being an Elk, tries and fails again to ram the T-Rex. Nyx comes in and casts Eldridge blast right in its mouth, killing it. Daina pulls a grey vial of viscous gel out of her bag and Sheila gasps and says “Gorgon’s breath”.

Now that one T-Rex is down, we surround the area where the other should pop back into existence and ready our weapons. Nyx is back to Eldridge blast this one as well!

I come in with my water whip and pull it to the ground. It’s weight crushing in on itself, breaking bone. Tezka comes in to deal the finishing blow by punching it three times in the T-Rex’s eyeball, causing blood to spray everywhere.

The battle is done. We start to relax while Sheila and Ruth call out to the Elk that ran away earlier. Ruth then turns to us to ask why we’re here. The others tell her about the Alabaster Seat and asking an open-ended question with regards to the prophesies. I let them explain it as I slink into the shadows. Watching. Waiting. For when these fools need saving again. As their mayor, I feel it is my duty.

Side note: none of them have paid their taxes for the greater good. Stalwart seems to have a chicken named “Linda”. Be sure to taking that as collateral.

Nocturnous, out.

*gruff voice deactivated*
