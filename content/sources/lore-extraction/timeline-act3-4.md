## Pre-Act 3 Context

The campaign began in Acts 1 & 2 (Chapters 0-41) as a single unified party. Key events from the DM notes that precede Act 3:

- [HIGH] The party received three divinations (from Ruth, the Hags, and Mia) foretelling the Zenith Wars
- [HIGH] Ruth departed for the Magister's Circle with Shela, leaving a farewell note
- [HIGH] Mordred broke free of his prison and began his campaign to destroy the keystones
- [HIGH] Vecna collected shards of the Raven Queen to raise Peraxis Geth as an illithilich
- [HIGH] The Forest's Zenith (first zenith) was already plundered by Mordred — he used the Heart of the Wilde to awaken the forest guardian
- [HIGH] The Orc-Human War was ignited in DawnHaven by Logath (under Fae demon influence)

## PROPHESIED FUTURE EVENTS (from DM Planning Notes)

The following events are planned but their placement in the chapter timeline is unknown:

### Forest's Zenith Sequence
1. Party defeats the final boss of the Forest's Zenith (awakened forest guardian)
2. Heart of the Wilde pops off the beast and transports the entire party to V-Mardov
3. Inside V-Mardov's Death Zenith, they fight Lich Ned'Zailok and steal his Anchor of Death
4. Varris sits in the control room for the Forest's Zenith in the final fight

### Mountain's Zenith Sequence
1. Travel to Vag'Mahar
2. Enter the Mountain's Zenith (in the Stone Sanctum, Brownbeard Upper Ward)
3. Find the Kiln of the First Flame and activate the First Forge
4. Daina and Bergs create/awaken the Warforged army
5. Oblivion Wayne leads a Drow/Illithid vampire invasion
6. Nocturnus uses the Warforged against the invasion army
7. Nocturnus confronts his aunt in his childhood home
8. Nocturnus mans the Mountain's Zenith in the final war

### Winter's Zenith Sequence
1. Party finds the Sunless Citadel in the Shifting Sands (Yuan-Ti fortress)
2. Stalwart discovers the Shield of Retribution
3. Randal uses the panflute to resurrect Mia (the turtle)
4. Mia surfaces and grants the Anchor of Pestilence (a magical tune on the panflute)
5. Deliver the Heart of the Faith to Conquis Abbey to break the curse
6. Dallor mans the Winter's Zenith in the final battle

### Harvest's Zenith Sequence
1. Remove the Anchor of Famine from the top of the zenith (guarded by the Harvest Effigy)
2. Return the Heart of the Progenitors to the crevasse in Tygras Logath
3. Speak the words: "ON HIS BACK ALL VOWS ARE MADE, A HAVEN FOR THE CONTINENT OF DAWN PAID"
4. The turtle skeleton beneath the bay is revived — massive turtle carrying an Atlantean city appears
5. Nyx inherits the Mark of Tygras (Sable's Beacon), chooses: rid world of tieflings OR rebuild the lost empire
6. Irethia mans the Harvest's Zenith in the final battle

### The Final War (Gard'Daemon)
1. All four anchors are charged/positioned at the Valley of Anchors
2. All four hearts are wielded by their champions
3. The four zenith controllers (Varris, Nocturnus, Dallor, Irethia) man their positions
4. The four champions (Brigitte, Bergs, Stalwart, Nyx) wield their hearts
5. Sprocket serves as the vessel for Sabine Ra's return
6. V-Mordred's invasion is repelled
7. The anchors stabilize the barrier between Mardov and V-Mardov
