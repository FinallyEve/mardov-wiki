Summary 21 – Randall’s Account

Act 1 of 5 Chapter 20 - Paging Dr Manhattan

We last left our party in the forest outside the Magister’s Circle.

It’s dress-up time for the more stand-out members of the group. To avoid notice Nyx traded the tortured revenant soul of Jorgan and a fair amount of gold for a disguise. The end result is the visage of a slightly disturbed human thanks to the cries revenge yelling in her head.

Tezka has constructed the devout form of a blind and def Priestess of Sarenrai from a disguise kit a wish and a prayer. Will it be enough?

Realizing his similarity to the wanted poster, Irethia rummages through the disguise kit for a wig to cover his blatantly silver hair.

With their wardrobes settled the adventurers settle down into a tiny hut for a solid 8 hours.

Dollar and Berg emerge from a portal surprisingly well rested and take watch for the party.

After a quick nap Nocturnus joins them.

The forest is oddly silent as if everything is trying its best to not be heard or seen in the forest. No insects no creature noises.

Nocturnus spots two hooded figures running full speed toward the Circle gates in the middle of the night. He abruptly calls out to them, probing for identification. They claim to be “Jerry” and “Sally” though Nocturnus suspects otherwise.

He notices that “Sally” looks remarkably like Irethia.

They tell him not to mention them to the orcs or better yet, to avoid them entirely. And they hurry on their way.

Shortly afterwards the group awakes and they make their way toward the circle.

Dallor is first to notice the gate spotting “Jerry” and “Sally” from the night before. He pulls the team into a huddle to plan their next move.

Sprocket explains that there are 7 sects of the circle, the 8th unrepresented is necromancy. They intend to visit the bardic circle, and order the devil’s kiss to talk to the necromancer from the ship. With the extra soul Dana procured from the previous day, they should be able to provide Tezka with a better disguise in a mere 3 days.

They also have a secondary goal to discuss the prophecy with Ruth telling her about the other two locations they discovered.

And lastly, they would like to get more information about “The Anchor” and to find out what’s up with the Zenith.

Irethia personally wants to learn about his powers that aren’t traditional Wizard powers

Moving ahead toward they gates they are stopped by the guards who are arguing with “Jerry”

Irethia sends a message to “Sally”.

“Ive seen the wanted poster and we look similar.”

She responds curtly,  “I don’t believe you, stay away from me”

The guard warns that a disease is spreading in the circle. The locals have lost all reservations. They have no sense of community or belonging. They’ve let in too many clerics and paladins in from Conquees abbey and they believe they are the cause.

Nocturnus, for no particular reason, reveals to the guard that Dollar has died at least once . The guard raises an eyebrow, but Dollar quickly covers up his mistake.

When they mention they’re here for Ruth, he lets us know they’ve actually been waiting for us.

“Sally” is demanding to see the Alabaster mage. She gestures to the top of the tower.  She is miffed that the party is welcomed in while they need to stay outside.

A man with a pipe, Grimnak, saunters up around that time. At the same time a large vibration comes through the forest.  He passes the guards a fair bit of coin while mentioning that “Jerry and Sally” are wanted criminals.

When sprocket mentions that they’re worth a quarter of a million., Grimnak raises his hand and cuts himself. The vibrations in the forest get louder. He reappears behind “Jerry” and slits his throat. “Sally” shouts, “My brother will hear about this!”. Her torn clothes reveal she’s wearing noble clothes.

Dana says “Oh hey, I bet she’s talking about the guy in the tower.”

At that moment a pair of orcs each accompanied by a freakin’ T-rex burst out from the tree line. They smell blood and Berg is covered in it. The first lizard, with one ferocious tail swipe, beats the tar out of Berg. The second narrowly misses Tezka as she backflips over its sweeping tail, like so many leg sweeps.

Sprocket knocks a lizard literally senseless, blinding and deafening the beast. It immediately smells Grimnak’s blood, snapping its head toward him.

Stalwart freezes Grimnak in place to seal his fate.

The guard, Ryan, looks surprised as they run through the gate. The party yells “T-rex!” and he pulls a lever to close it.

Randall messages Grimnak..

“Hey, how’s it going there?”

Grimnak replies without hesitation, “If you know what’s good for you, don’t travel with that girl...and don’t trust her. Meet me at the docks at midnight. Come alone and bring the girl.”

Randall passes this message along to each of the party members without alerting “Sally”.

Sally has silver hair, looks kind of like Irethia. She tells the group, “My name is Ashara Everstar, I am from Dawnpass.”

Stalwart knows she is the Steward of Dawnpass. “Dawnpass and the rest of the human elf alliance are in danger. We lost our furthest outpost to orc sorcerers. Orcs shouldn’t be able to cast magic and especially not at that level. They seemed to have tiefling level magic. Orcs may have made the same deal that the tieflings had for magic. I’m here to tell the Alabaster mage”

“I’m magically gifted but not on the level of my brother.” “My brother, the Alabaster Mage, has barred my entry to the circle because of the disease.” “It’s a strange magic that causes the weak willed to become immoral. Hugo “(Jerry) was my guard, though not a great one. It seems as if Qual-shahar guards are a dime a dozen. Actually, I believe we started our journey on the same ship.”

As the party walks toward the tower they come across a great tree. Hanging from the tree are a number of tieflings.

Stalwart explains to the party about the tree:

“This tree is the birthright. If you are a tiefling and you’re caught trying to learn magic you are hung at this tree. It’s hollow inside. The tower used to be built here. After the war with the tieflings they planted this tree, formed the circles, and promised to prevent anyone from reaching the power level of Tigris without being checked.”

He also explains that, while in the Magister’s Circle, when a spell is cast, a glowing symbol appears on the ground under the caster. The symbol displays the spell’s school of magic.

Randall notices a distinct lack of magic glowing circles despite the density of the population.

Stalwart senses desecration from the entire population.

At the tower the party runs into an envoy people leaving lead by Lilith, the platinum seat of the diviner’s circle.

She tells Sprocket that someone is looking for him and that the source of the disease seems to be religious in origin.

She narrows her eyes at him and says, “Be sure to let us know why you’ve been gone for so long.”

Randall sings a greeting for Lilith. She says “good to see you too”, though she doesn’t seem to mean it. “Beware visiting the Bardic circle. I hear the Devil’s Kiss is terrible.”

At this point Irethia realizes the wanted poster says to contact the Orc nation.

The party decides to climb the climb the stairs circling the outside of the main tower, which are seemingly made from water.

The party gets about 40 stories when some flying creatures swoop in and grab Nocturnus.

Sprocket seems unphased and he says “Let the wyverns have him and attempts to walk away”.

Dollar bill manages politely asks the wyvern to drop Nocturnus which he does, then proceeds to beat Dollar within an inch of his life. Nocturus returns the favor with a “double meaningful punch”.

A screaming Dana manages to whelm another with wyvern with extreme prejudice.

Ashara runs to the other side of the tower claiming it’s safer on the other side.

After a rousing rendition of “Staying Alive” Randall revives Dollar then immediately bolts for the safe side of a tower getting a nasty swipe from Dollar’s attacker for his trouble.

Tezka knocks another Wyvyrn towards the earth. It fails to grasp the watery platform and crashes into the invisible barrier below, disintegrating on contact.

Stalwart takes the hint and knocks another into the barrier. It pulls up in time but loses its tail to the barrier bleeding profusely.

Stalwart’s nemesis attempts to take him a second time, but misses, but manages to knock Dana down with the same swipe.

One Wyvyrn, seeing the tides turn flies off. The party runs into the safety of the barrier after Randall and Ashara

After the rest of the party runs to safety, Stalwart bravely but needlessly wrestles the final beast to the ground and drags it into the barrier disintegrating as if feeding a log into a woodchipper. He stops before the tail and pockets it.

Berg hobbles over to the other chimera corps and splashes the blood to half his body.

Everyone in the party takes a sample of chimera blood, as it’s known to be good for making superior healing potions.
