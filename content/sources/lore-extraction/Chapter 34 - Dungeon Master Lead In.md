Chapter 34 Dungeon Master Lead In

Last we saw the party together, they had just managed to defeat a pair of Alhoon and a Mindwhitness. The Alhoon were Ulilitharid Necromancers, elite form of mindflayer, and they were doing a series of experiments on natural creatures (hobgoblins and gnolls) as well as on their own kind (Vampire Illithid & Zombie Illithid) and perhaps their most horrifying experiment of all was the mindwhitness, a BEHOLDER which was maimed and forced to undergo ceremorphisis, the process of becoming a mind flayer. These creatures act as some sort of

network for one of the 4 figures within the stags last vision, Peraxis Phyrexia, the Illithilich (Illithid Lich!).

Then the party was split, with two party members, Brigit and Varris, being captured early, some party members actually disappearing completely, and the arrival of Tiberious with Daina and Randall inside Reynaldine's Pocket Dimension. Irethia, Nocturnous Wayne, Sprocket and Tezka were all summoned to VODRAM, to help out MAC and the Resistance Fighters! After a trip down their perspective rabbit holes, the party finds themselves returned to the scene

of their last great group battle.

So much had changed since they were last here:

- Daina had been cleaning her father's ax after being reminded of how much it meant to her, and found a note hidden in a compartment inside. She crossed a chocolate river using giant marshmallows, and now she’s ready to read it! It was time for answers! Why was her father calling her Diesa? What does it mean to be a FireForge, why does he want her to be a ForgeLord? Why was he alive and not with her? How is he alive if she saw the shadow in that dream, and the man with a red beard but jet black eyes coming to visit him.... and his sudden illness? Pa's hint about the axe was something. She'd cleaned that ax a hundred times and never once found the chamber with that note in it, but the very time after receiving a vision from him, she finds it! THIS BETTER HAVE ANSWERS! and if it doesn’t? well she still needs to see where this Heart of the Forge is. after all, Tor spent the last of his free will to tell her that he was dying, even though he was already dead in her eyes, she'd already shed those tears, they had dried, why now. What is so great about this key?!? She knows one thing though. She's going to do one thing her father wanted for sure. she was going to FIGHT! Fight her way all the way back to the dwarven capital and find more answers!

- Nocturnus spent his watch strangely drawn to the strange black sword with the glass pommel, even if it was in Brigit bag.... it was calling HIM, clearly it thought he was the only one who was worthy. And worthy he felt as over in VODRAM the sword was strangely more powerful, and it was GLORIOUS, those cultists deserved every bit of what’s coming to them. But now he’s been teleported back, the blade in hand in front of BRIGIT who spent the whole time as Reynaldine's pet bird... But he has the perfect weapon to challenge his Aunt, that bitch. She deposed him. She is the reason he must roam as an exile, unable to watch over his people. If he couldn’t be their dark knight, he would be a symbol of vengeance. The will of his people. He's collected a prophecy from an all powerful conduit of magic and now with this magic sword, he can cut her down and clear the Wayne family name, his fathers name. The last King of the Drow. "The Drow are dead. Long Live the Vampire Queen!" echoing in his ear, there’s only one way he’s going, down. Home. To finish what OBLIVION started...

- Nyx, who found others who were benefitting from Reynaldine’s power, was shocked to find that her "deal" had much better "terms" than whatever Annie Hallowbrook or even Lorelei had. Reynaldine even went so far as to kill Annie by walking her into the quickly rising tide of pure pestilence! Even as Annie died, Reynaldine was able to take over Lorelei’s body without her even having said his name 3 times. Though strangely she was also exempt from that rule. And just as she was starting to think she was special to Reynaldine, she was NOT sent to Reynaldine's pocket dimension with chocolate and marshmallows and capture the flag, no... she was sent to VODRAM, the land ruled by the dead and a stark reminder that things can get so much worse if GARD' DAEMON is allowed to happen! Why? All of these questions, and only strange answers. He wants her to steal the earth genasi prince from the clutches of PERAXIS, in return for a favor. And so she has decided to go DOWN into the WAYS and cross to beneath VAG'MAHAR, the dwarven capital city.
