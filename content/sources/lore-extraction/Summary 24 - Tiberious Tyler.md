Summary 24 – Tiberius’ Account

SUMMARY - CHAPTER 23 HOBGOBLINS HOBGOBLINS ... you guessed it! MORE HOBGOBLINS!

Having just completed a strange and ancient druidic ritual with the great elk, Sheila and Ruth spend the next few minutes dumbfounded at the utter miracle that has occurred! Instead of dwelling on the mysteries of the past, Daina decided her time was better spent inspecting a mysterious dull light stalking below the bushes, the shape and size of this newfound entity expanding and contracting to perfectly fit the cramped underbrush.

Having pointed out this oddity to the rest of the group (Ruth and Sheila not withstanding) ... and come to think of it another set of members shifted in and out of the plane, perhaps the most unfortunate being Brigit as this whole situation was just dripping with strange druidic magic. How do we know? Well you can thank the Fantastic Mr. Talking Cosmic Fox for that..

Fantastic - because he has a wonderful party trick and loves to show it off

Mr. Talking - because well this object talked with a strange male voice

Cosmic - because despite having a recognizeable form, the substance he was made of was almost exactly the same as the prophetic energy between the elk's horns complete with its own set of tiny constellations!

and

Fox - because well, he was a Fox ... kind of?

Why you may ask, is there a strange talking fox wandering in the woods? Well that cryptic Alabaster Seat dropped the party off directly in front of one of the few remaining Zodiac’s/Shi-shi-gami, the Elk Zodiac. But here’s the thing about zodiacs, they hide in plain sight, decidedly taking on a mortal dull form until they die and are reborn. Eternal Life, an endless cycle of death and rebirth. Fitting for a force of cosmic animals on the Raven Queen's plane of existence.

Yeah yeah yeah, another pantheon of strange cosmic entities who happen to talk

to this motley crew of formerly dead people.... what else is new.

WELL, this is decidedly the second almost deity level entity to trade their immortality to help these fated few. Shi-shi-gami explains that on top of helping out with the prophecy’s, the elk has also granted the party the ability to speak with these Zodiac’s in their ethereal form as well as appointed his old friend the fantastically clever, Manitou, to be their guide to an ancient tomb, The Tomb of the Last Curator.

Other useful information includes:

1) There are a few zodiacs who have already shed their immortal form: the serpent and the turtle being the poster children, however there also appears to be another named Osiris (no clue which zodiac she is, only that she now commands the desert sands?)

2) Other than the fox, it is also known that there is rabbit zodiac

3) These Zodiac’s have a gift they can bestow upon mortals to help them toward their fate, namely either triggering or preventing Gard’damon, either way if the Zodiac picks the right champions and survives to the end of Gard’damon then it will be granted a power beyond even the Zodiac’s wildest dreams. It seems that this is quite the gamble because death during/before Gard’damon is an eternal death.

4) Grim’nak (the half-orc rogue who attacked with the T-Rex's and was left for dead in the forest) survived and managed to send the T-Rex's after the elk, but the elk doesn’t know how he was discovered and decides that he has already spent enough time with the party as its likely that the elk can still be tracked.

Manitou thinks that dilly-dallying is a bother and doesn’t want to hold the party's hand the whole way to the tomb, so decides to meet everyone amidst the Valley of Anchors, mysteriously the destination that the party was already on the way to check out with the help of the rest of the Thornberry Shade, especially Dr. M. B. Wells, resident archeologist and brewer of things beyond

his understanding.

As luck would have it, the party was not very far from excitement as they came to a bridge over a canyon. Across from the canyon stands a massive plateau, unfortunately there would be quite a bit more climbing to do before the party could reach the top, as the bridge only placed travelers on the trail upwards. Fortunately, there wasn’t any boring hiking to do as the bridge was teeming

with hobgoblins. Emphasis on the Goblin part according to the frenzied Stalwart Little.

As soon as the party was spotted by some scouts, a deep guttural voice from the darkness proceeded the resident bridge ogre (aren’t bridges supposed to always have a troll?) to break the only bridge over the ravine.

After a bloodbath of epic proportions, during which hobgoblins were literally raining from the sky, the party, and by party I mean Stalwart Little, self-proclaimed Second-in-Command of the Scarlet Tunic Brigade, took a prisoner for questioning and torture.... mostly torture.

After comparing the captive's ears with the severed one which serves as Stalwart’s holy symbol, Stalwart was not pleased to find that there were larger differences between hobgoblins and goblins, so he proceeded to extract as many answers and limbs as he could.

Things we learned from the newly minted quadruple amputee:

1) He definitely does not know where the Sunless Citadel is.

2) There have been four captives in the last week: two male and two female.

3) First captive was a human male with jet-black hair.

4) The others were a pink-haired gnome female, young. An older male gnome and

a red-haired female human. All captured in the same day.

5) The hobgoblins were living in the Tomb of the Last Curator and were attempting to use the prisoners in some manner of sacrifice to get farther into the tomb

Like the valiant hero some pink-haired gnome girl believe him to be, Sprocket immediately picked up on the description of Trixie and the rest of the Thornberry Shade and charged off to the rescue!

Stalwart proceeded to take things to a dark place, carving a message into the chest of the hobgoblin nugget. Having delivered his message, punctuated by a lightning bolt straight through the ogre on the other side of the ravine, the hobgoblin captain bailed back into the mountain.

Bergs, seeing one friend go full hero while the other went full villain, decided to muster forward with Sprocket and toss him across the ravine, with a rope.. both ends.... classic.

The rope situation covered, Sprocket continued to blaze a trail ahead, this time invisible, but recklessly he charged directly into caltrops, was rescued by Bergs, then discovered the entrance to the Tomb (it was covered in warning signs written in hobgoblin to "STAY OUT")

The rest of the party jolts up from a short rest to the sound of battle coming from the other side of the canyon. Using ultra-sweet ninja moves, Nocturnus Wayne vaults the ravine. Everyone else used the leftover rope ...

Meanwhile, invisible wizard and his trusty fighting wizard friend are confronted with four hobgoblins and do the first thing that comes to their heads.... come up with a plan that involves 1000 ball bearings and a sentient pacifist plant. This is one of the few plans in DND that goes 100% as intended, complete with maul-attached spine whipping.

The last hobgoblin attempt to flee and warn the others of the intruders.. and is greeted with a ballista bolt through the chest. Legend tells he's still serving as wall art to this day...

After some much deserved looting the party finds a number of items:

1) Trixie's chalk. suspicions confirmed, Sprocket is going full hero mode

2) Dr. M. B. Wells’ diary (notably with such greatest hits as categorized descriptions of the Valley of Anchors and the recipe for brewing pestilence, there is more mumbo-jumbo here that's going to take some time to figure out)

3) An emblem with the symbol of the orphanage from which the Little’s keep pouring out

So if matters weren’t dire enough it seems that Stalwart might lose another brother to the goblin menace, the crew of the Thornberry Shade is definitely somewhere in here, AND there is decidedly no trace of Captain Liza.

The party goes in, GUNS A BLAZING. Irethia spends a significant amount of time picking up the ball bearings to determine that this Tomb has multiple other directions it could go, up ... and down being the other two, but as it's impossible to cast light on them all, that remains a mystery.

What isn’t a mystery is the amount of ass-kicking that ensues. There just one issue, Sprocket took the gun's-a-blazing thing to heart and decided to use everything in his strangely well-stocked pockets to burn this motherfucker to the ground in an attempt to rescue Trixie. The motherfucker he was burning to the ground, well that turned out to be Stalin Little, the black haired youth, hell-bent to make as big of a name for himself as his older brothers Stalwart and Stewart. The key here was not to have that name be a scorch mark in a hobgoblin nest.

Getting out of this particular pickle requires finesse, the kind of finesse that comes with rushing a spent ballista, somehow already understanding how it works despite being raised by wolves, dismantling the ballista, and then getting knocked unconscious can provide, you know the Bergs classic.

Stalwart took this moment to finally rescue his misguided brother and send him off to safety! This is a job for a sailor, not his kid-brother!

It is somewhere around this time that a Triceratops joins the fight after being whipped into a frenzy, but that’s exactly the kind of test of strength that gets Daina up in the morning. Toe to toe with one of the strongest time-tested fighters Mardov can produce. RAGE V RAGE CAGE MATCH BBY!!

The triceratops attempts to gore Daina, like they do. However, Daina had been more than ready to test her metal against sterner stuff than the likes of puny hobgoblins and STOOD HER GROUND! TAKE THAT POMPOUS VAG'MAHAR DWARVES! And just as she has the beast right where she wanted it... It was banished before the fight could continue.

Irethia was much more interested in keeping these mysterious strangers alive than stroking their already cosmic egos, now where had that fox spirit stalked off too again?

Most of the current wave of hobgoblins handled, Bergs still unconscious. Sprocket once again springs into action, loosing a second blind bolt of lightning DIRECTLY at the dis-embodied voice of the hobgoblin captain (the bolt stopping inches from a bound and gagged Trixie). But in his desperate attempt to save his biggest fan, Sprocket pours a healing potion down the throat of the friend who's always got his back, Bergs.

Perhaps it’s because he thought he could use the back-up. Perhaps it was so that Trixie could hear that he saved not only her from near-certain death but his friends as well. Me? Well... I believe that Sprocket’s heart grew three times as big that day. (though it was still smaller than an average humans)

As the party re-focused on the task at hand, they were greeted by a familiar taunting coming from the defeated captain:

"You may have fought your way in here and beat a few dozen of my best men, but there is no way you’re going to be able to fight your way out! I only need one more sacrifice anyway, so you can have this one!"

Not even wasting the effort to pull out his dagger completely, the hobgoblin captain used the motion of opening the large door to slit the throat of Dr. M. B. Wells and lock the door behind him. Dragging poor Trixie into the darkness, her muffled crying fading off behind the door.
