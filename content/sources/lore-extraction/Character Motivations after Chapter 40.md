Character Motivations after Chapter 40:

1) Tezka Duskhallow, life has a strange way of sweeping you on the wind. It's been ages since you stumbled into Master Tenzin's arms as a misguided beggar with nowhere to go, You've been wondering why your parents were killed for as long as you can remember and now that you know the answer... there are just more questions. You're parents MET Master Tenzin and were tasked with stealing the Diadem of the Dawn by the criminal organization known as the Duskers. Why did nobody at the Monastery tell you? They always treated you like an outcast because of your appearance, you can’t help being a Tiefling. Sure, you survived a fire that would kill a normal human, but do you have to pay for the sins of your parents AND every tiefling who messed up before you? Where's the justice in that? At least Tenzin treated you kindly, but if he knew your parents and what would happen to them, why didn’t he save you from years on the streets of Dawnpass? And now you find out that the Current Ruler of Dawnpass ended up taking the Diadem supposedly after Tenzin's death, rest his kind old bones... You'll just have to remember Tenzin's teachings: "An enemy is just a friend you've yet to understand" and Ashara Everstar/The Duskers have a lot of explaining to do...

2) Stalwart Little, an orphaned dragonborn has a rough life ahead of him, especially when he’s orphaned in the middle of a HUMAN city surrounded by HUMANs who have no idea what it’s like to molt their scales. If it weren't for Gran and the Little Orphanage, you'd have starved and gone mad all at the same time. But you’re lucky, you grew up surrounded by more family than you knew what to do with a menagerie of all kinds of races all under one roof, each sibling looking out for the younger generation. None were as cool as Stewart though. Stewart was an adventurer and more importantly your hero. To hear that he died to GOBLINS in that Sunless Citadel, it was enough to awaken the dragon within. That rage channeled to vengeance by an oath. You finally have enough power to avenge him but before you head out following in your brother’s footsteps to the Sunless Citadel, you need to pay respects to the ashes of your now lost home. Is Gran okay? Why did the Orphanage burn down? Where are the others? Dawnpass has the answers and it’s up to this sailor to find them.

3) Finn Darkeyes, it’s a name you never wanted, and the price you paid to get it.. well it wasn’t worth it. You wonder if you can go back to being a BrightHallow after Devin tainted it. Killing your brother left a black mark on your past, a stain. Returning to Monethern knowing that Devin wanted to go back there just seems childish, the Cult of the Sleepless can rot for all you care. The Sleepless... the first stain. The last remaining eye he forced into your skull twitching as you remember that monsters voice booming in your head, always watching, always calling to you.... with Bartrims Brezerkers decimated and your debt to Bartim for rescuing you left unpaid, the stains are becoming too much to bear. Perhaps you can start over, but where. Where can a bloodstained soul with borrowed eyes find a future. Jesabelle, the sea hag you freed from the fate that would have been your own, offered to show you in the Eavallan Forest. Seems as good a place to start as any

4) Varys Laidon, a man of two worlds and one quest, find the stag and save it from its terrible fate. Ned'Zalok may have taken it but thwarting his plans has been your life’s work. The doe chose you for a reason. Before, you couldn’t save your father from that monster. But before you were weak. Before you didn’t understand that there were other worlds of possibilities. Now, you’re prepared. You've studied the fabric between worlds and have learned to traverse it. Vodram may be doomed, but Mardov can be the home you’ve always fought for and these chosen or whatever the doe called them are the key. First stop Dawnpass, then on to Eavallan, your old hunting ground. And what a hunt it will be...

5) Randall <last name redacted>, there’s something to be said for the power of ignorance. For better or worse, the ignorant shape the world and it’s up to people like you to seek the truth. TRUTH beyond violence. That's what eats at you about this last battle with the sleepless. The creature knew so many secrets ... perhaps THE secret! But he attacked in ignorance, like so many battles before it, and well he got what he deserved. Your search continues.... every day is another that slips away from you. Tick Tock. Tick Tock. Death is closing in. If only there was a way to grant your wish, then the real truth can be recorded. Those fools at the Magister's Circle are going to learn what a true mage can do.

6) Brigitte, the signs are everywhere. Mardov is reacting to the evil which moves atop it, nature itself calls to you, "Save Me, Brigitte." Ever since you've learned of the Zodiacs you felt a kinship with these forces of nature. The Stag, the king of the forest with a crown of thorns. To think that a majestic creature such as that can be captured, his power exploited and drained. Nature has a new protector, and these zodiacs will be saved. For this outlander from the Eavallan forest is going home to her old stomping ground. Hopefully something there can help her uncover the mystery of the zodiac and save the Stag!

7) Sprocket the Diviner. "Yes," the ultimate answer to a question you ask yourself every fight. Every day seems sweet with the knowledge that it might be your last. The Alabaster Seat gave you a gift that day, freedom. As a divination wizard, you know full well how difficult it can be to see the future, but once you latch onto a possibility, really understand that one moment in time, then a true master of time itself can KNOW. Its final. But being marked for death isn't all bad, one need only embrace their fate and lean into the path set before them. Things may be strange, but when the goddess of fate rides shotgun, ignoring her request gets ... tricky. So off to the Forest Zenith this gnome will go, a shard of a lich's soul in his pocket...

8) Nyx, you haven't forgotten, you haven't forgiven, you are finally ready to return home to Dawnpass. Since discovering your sister was alive, you've thought you were ready, but after this latest brush with Euclid and Archimedes, armed with the knowledge that you carry Juliania's Tome. It's time to rescue your sister from the fate that could have very easily been yours. Your parent be damned for whatever deal they made, you’re going to save this innocent from a violent life and Gabriel's Pactbound will be free. Onward to the Bubbling Cauldron, onward to save Xyn.

9) Pickle and Gareth have a mysterious set of magnetic gears to deliver to Oswald Magnacog, Kraken of the Sand Seas

10) Nocturnus Wayne's next step of the Alabaster Seat's prophecy is to enter the Mountains Zenith and Nightmare, a sword from Vodram with a terrible past, has given Nocturnus a lazer focus on this task in order to ultimately kill his Aunt Oblivion Wayne

11) Daina (Diesa) Ironforge (FireForge) was told by her dying father to seek out Decan Smithrunner in order to get information about what it means to complete her birthright as a Maghar, she was told that he could be found within the Dragons Carapace and would trust her once she shows him her mother's seal

12) Bergs (last name not currently known) has discovered that despite being abandoned by his parents and raised by wolves, somehow he’s been roped into this strange Maghar Dwarven mumbo-jumbo. If it means discovering who he really is, he’s happy to play along for now. Although there is some level of guilt for having accidentally bound Dierdre Crestfallen to the D'Vinter/D'Guarda castle while forging his hammer and getting this strange new tattoo

13) Irethia, a woman of two worlds one lost and the other falling, has discovered that there is a sinister lineage to her draconic powers that inhabit this human body of hers. Under the guidance of another with a "draconic passenger", she discovered that there is a mystery as to how she can exist while Ashara Everstar, the previous owner of this power, still lives and breathes. Yousef is convinced the mystery to the origin of the Tieflings is linked to the origin of the draconic sorcerers as well. Perhaps she can find out more in due time

14) Bardin Caveskin, after the loss of Bartrims Brezerkers, Bardin has been frustrated and lost. He's been down this discovery of learning to be a Maghar and what it entails, and all of this lead to the loss of his wife and the red stinging burn of his firey beard. But a man can only run so far from his responsibilities. Someone needs to let the Craftsmen know that Bartrim is dead, and Slyvia is forgebound. So it’s time to return back to the place he was exiled, he knows his crimes and the consequences of returning. Time for these old boots of his to take him on one final journey

15) Whispers of the Morning Breeze (Breeze), time is short. This world is doomed to become the one you were born into, that molded you into the fighter you are now. This mission is not one you can fail, there might be hope for your people here in this reality, if you can only ensure that Zail Edok be found and killed before Ned'Zalok gets his claws on him. You witnessed the systematic extinction of your people before and were powerless to save them. You’re much much stronger now, with or without the Doe's champion Varys wherever the fool is.
