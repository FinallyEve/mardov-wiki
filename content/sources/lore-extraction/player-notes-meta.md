## Main Story Original Backstory

Main Story Original Backstory:

The Scarlet Tunic Brigade is an adventuring party run by Slitz Gnard, a greedy goblin of morbidly obese size and a healthy relationship with the town mortician.

Time is money friends, and right now you’re out of both, so you and your fellow Scarlet Tunics signed up to be fresh meat! Funny how none of the higher ups in the Brigade have been on more than 3/4 missions, that's one hell of a turnaround... revolving doors are clearly ruining the adventuring business

The company has a simple and easy to follow hierarchy, everyone listens to the goblin in charge unless on missions, then whoever survived the most previous missions calls the shots. Any attempts to question the little green man will be met with a visit to the storage warehouse with Gogrut and Hurtle, the two highest ranking Ogres on the team, who are in charge of ... the HR department?

Think you have what it takes for leadership? Additional mission points are awarded for good old-fashioned bribery! It's not corruption if it’s in broad daylight

Each mission is concluded by paying the survivors tax (67% to Slitz) and the awarding of mission points!

## Prophesies and Zeniths

Prophesies and Zeniths (as of Chapter 26)

We took the prophesies, with Ruth and Sheila, to a magic giant elk, and used his antlers to answer some questions about them. Every time we got something right, the papers or words would light up.

First Prophecy:

For the first prophecy, four tarot cards were drawn: (1) A tiger, signifying the wind, (2) A forge, signifying creation, (3) A holy knight, signifying faith and (4) A family (signifying progenitors).

The divination began and this was heard from Ruth in Argos:

“The anchors fall, the realms sunder

Hearts of stone gathered from plunder

Death incarnate knocks at the door

Below the blood star begins the final war”

The anchors fall refers to something that is with each of the Zeniths and as they fall, the realms will fall into chaos.

Hearts of Stone: We’ve seen a heart of stone, the Heart of the Wilde, plus the other slots in the tower of the Red Tunic Brigade. We also know from Tiberious that this stone has been stolen and “gathered from plunder”

The Blood Star: One was seen before when Tygras came to power. It’s a star that’ll turn red and literally fall. Meteorite (?)

The Final War: refers to Gard’damon (Anagram for Armeggedon)

Second Prophecy:

This was heard from the hags:

“Double bubble toil and trouble,

Gard’damon is neigh, the first zenith is rubble,

Prepare the Pestilence with children’s souls,

The husks remain until they’re told.

Double bubble toil and trouble,

Anchors lifted and hearts are smuggled.”

The first zenith: Always refers to the Forest Zenith. We’re not quite sure how its rubble.

Pestilence: What happened to it? We tried to get rid of it but couldn’t, so we demolished a building on top of it. We’ve also seen it in other places.

The husks: The bodies of the children who’s souls were taken.

Anchors: being lifted from the Zeniths

Hearts are Smuggled: We know of a smuggler in Argos: Lord Jerefor.

Third Prophecy:

This was heard from Mia after we rescued her.

“As the rain falls on the fallow harvest, and the matrim pact fulfilled,

The Anchor of Famine shall be lifted, the echo of TYGRAS stilled.

As the snow drifts over the ruined tribe, and serpent plagues the sea

The Anchor of Pestilence shall awake, to answer the curators’ plea

As the fire bellows on the forgotten forge, and the blood star is found

The Anchor of War shall be silenced, as the new paragon is crowned.

As the sunlight warms the forest gate, and madness prisoner freed

The Anchor of Death shall sway, to bring peace to star-crossed three.”

The four horseman: Famine, Pestilence, War and Death are universally known as bringers of the apocalypse, of Armageddon.

Locations: the four verses refer to four points on a map

The map we saw on the Thornberry Shade bubbled up in five places:(1) Var’taos ruins, also known as The Valley of Anchors, the origin of magic, (2) Vagna Har - The dwarven city, (3) Macchu, (4) Bynar, (5) Tygras Logra - This point additionally had a burning ember on its location.

In order, the verses refer to: (1) Tygras Logar, (2) Bynar, (3) Vagna Har and (4) Macchu.

Currently we know that Macchu is the “Forest Zenith” and Vagna Har is the “Mountain Zenith”. The other two are still a mystery/we haven’t heard about them as much. I would assume that Bynar is the Sea/Ocean Zenith, and then I don’t know about Tygras Logar.

From what Manitou told us there is a zodiac that realms over the desert lands, could this be another zenith? Maybe Tygras Logar....

From the Tomb of the Last Curator, we know that Bynar is the “Winter Zenith” and Tygras Logar is the “Harvest Zenith”

The Valley of Anchors: Absolutely has something to do with these Anchors associated with each Zenith. We should travel here to gain more information.

Matrim Pact: Gnomes, Humans and Half-elfs created a utopia on the back of the turtle Mia. We know of a qualashahar that has more information about this.

Tygras: Created Tieflings, Blew Up Uma with a superweapon, Reason for Covenants of Magic so that no being save for the Alabaster Seat, may posses that amount of power again, superweapon along with Tygras was thwarted by Matruin Pact which caused all of the surrounding area to explode. The last threat to this universe.

Serpent Sea: Literally the north sea. And it has a serpent. We know of someone, Dilbert, who has fought the serpent and collected the scales, Liza was swallowed by the serpent.

Ruined Tribe: We know that there is a ruined tribe in Bynar (Dragon-kin history check)

Winter’s Curators: We know that the dragon kin tribe in Bynar is known as these.

Forgotten Forge (aka First Forge): A forge in dwarven legend. It has to be lit once again. This falls into place with a few of our backstories and answers about our futures.

Blood Star is found: Found does not mean rise.

Paragon: The Bronze beards, the rulers of Vagna Har.

Forest and Sunlight: Finkle will have more information about this.

Madness Prisoner: Mordred/Ramses and his phylactery.

Star-crossed three: Sabine, Mordred/Ramses and Xeres. Once we bring their souls/phylacteries to the forest, and complete the trial of the zenith, we will bring peace to them.

## Zodiac_s and Shi-shi-gami_s

Zodiac’s/Shi-shi-gami’s (as of Chapter 26)

Zodiac’s are immortal beings that live in the world of Mardov. They hide in plain sight, taking on a mortal form until they die and are reborn.

Shi-shi-gami

Close to shinigami - god of death

Might mean “forest spirit” or “deer god”. Potentially just “nature spirit”

Each Zodiac has a gift they can bestow upon mortals to help us prevent Gard’damon. And if they pick the right champion, the champion that survives to the end and prevents Gard’damon, the zodiac will be granted a power beyond their wildest dreams. If their champion fails, they will face the eternal death.

From speaking with Mr. Fantastic Talking Cosmic Fox aka Manitou, we have learned some things about what have been bestowed on us.

The elk is the second almost-diety level entity to trade their immortality to help us with Sabine being the first (by saving us from death and bestowing us with crystal hearts)

The elk granted us the ability to speak with the Zodiac’s in their ethereal form.

And he appointed Manitou to be our guide to an ancient tomb - The tomb of the Last Curator (potentially related to the curators mentioned in the third prophecy?)

Other zodiac’s

The serpent and the turtle are zodiac’s that have already shed their immortal form.

Does this mean that someone else is their champion? Or did their champion fail? Or was their champion the person to defeat Tygras? Would that even be a thing?

Also, from the prophesies and from what we’ve heard it seems that the serpent is dangerous. It “plagues” the sea. But every zodiac we’ve come across has been friendly. Did it lose its mind when it gave up its immortality? Or is it just naturally dangerous? Does this mean some zodiac’s are more powerful than others?

Osiris - mentioned as a zodiac, but we don’t have information on her form. She rules over the desert sands.

There is also another zodiac that has taken the form of a rabbit.
