Summary 32 - Tiberious’ Account: The Harvest Nightmares

Oh great Platinum Dragon, today is the third day of my journey southward towards Windhaven...

or at least I think I am finally travelling southward, nevermind the mishap with that half elven merchant, his blasphemy will be weighed heavily and his journey will end in justice!

My attempts to contact your chosen have been rather ... unsuccessful...

However, I know your silver scales will settle back in favor once more,

now that I am once again on a noble quest!

The harvest festival is set to begin in but a few weeks but the fields remain fallow!

But that is not why I write you now, NO! Instead I write to you about a black hand...

Late last night, there was a great force that knocked me off my cart...

I was having the most wonderful dream of my youth, but oddly QUINN was not there....

Your gift of visions of her are one of life's greatest treasures..

Instead my beloved QUINN was replaced by an unconscious hobgoblin woman with the most peculiar mark behind her.

A Black Hand... massive and open, as if cradling the lost soul

I tried my best to contact her, but my voice was missing and her ears were deafened

The hand as quickly as it appeared, closed around my visitor and vanished

The sheer force of it all caused me to be thrown off my cart and into the road!

A most unpleasant way to wake these old bones!

=====================================================================

Upon my inevitable descent back into sleep I was greeted by another woman,

this one with raven black hair and a matching cloak!

Her heart, oh BAHAMUT! Her heart was MISSING, the place where it should reside was crystalline and open.

With 4 single words, a whole host of scenes erupted around me!

Her first word was PESTILENCE! As it rang in the void that encompassed her,

the form of a small boy in a strange necklace a cold blue aura burst into view!

The tides of foul liquid rose around him as he cried for his mother's embrace...

The second was FAMINE! As the first scene burst into a bright green jungle the likes of which I've never seen!

The green was so vibrant and light that the vines looked to glow!

As they pulled back I was greeted by emissaries of the elemental planes.

A woman with hair a blaze clad in a blood red dress with accents of ash.

A man whose form was difficult to tell where it ended and breeze began, his song melancholy as the wind

A boy no older than 13 but solid with feet that melded with the stone beneath him

And a girl whose tears formed a globe around her as her blue skin shifted with an unseen current

Between them all stood a man in a top hat, admiring his menagerie

And there was WAR. It was then that I was reunited with your CHOSEN!

They stood in the middle, above them were orc and men. The damage was unparalleled,

the blood soaked the ground and seeped into the cavern below,

where your chosen stood amidst an ocean of bat-like creatures, but fear not!

For there was a buffer! A wall made of strange mechanical beings bearing a crest I do not know...

And last there was DEATH,

What I saw next brought me great pain, a stag and doe upon a locket which was ripped apart by a skeletal hand...

The stag fought but was unsuccessful instead, it left behind a souvenir.. a single horn to be remembered by!

I do not know why this great sadness overcame me,

it was as if the woman knew these creatures and was mourning their passing

=====================================================================

My sleep sufficiently ruined, I decided to try once again to reach NOCTURNUS as I promised DOLF and FINKLE!

I was astounded that FINALLY after the gauntlet of dreams beneath the Harvest Moon,

there I was able to make contact!

I told NOCTURNUS of the terrible blockade which now plagued their journey home,

and of the 20,000 gold I left in the Scarlet Keep where the HEART OF THE WILDE once was held before it was STOLEN!

I told him of my quest to find the source of the desecrated harvest which now plagues the countryside.

The most pressing matter was the warning I was able to impart upon them,

beware the blockade and travel northward before East!

My duty fulfilled, I pressed onward only to realize that I'd forgotten my cart!

No matter that irritable merchant should realize my napping has finished any day now and you...

BAHAMUT will be my guide once again!

Ever your faithful servant,

Tiberious D. Rex
