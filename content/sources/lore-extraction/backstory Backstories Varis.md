Varis Laindon's backstory:

Wilderness outpost childhood. Dad (Yeldan) was hunter/woodsman/guide/scout/messenger. Mom (Lianala) is a Druid. Started hunting and learning from both. Last big hunt was when Varis was 95 (think ~17 for human) was for a pack of pseudo dragons (they migrated through and killed sheep) and after a successful hunt, parents told him to keep a trophy. Not usually trophy takers. Kept the skull of his first kill of the pack. 2 weeks later, dad spotted undead scouts. Local lord believed him, but couldn’t convince farther people (at least, in time). 2 more weeks, and the army arrived. Zombies, skeletons, wraiths, mummies, led and created by the lich Ned'zailok. Volunteered with dad for militia, and took an adult name at the time. Think high school senior after 9/11 or Pearl Harbor. Fought beside dad, and local militia held for a week, hoping for relief from outside. Attacks got worse and supplies got limited after a week. Dad was sent (fastest scout) with trophies to request aid fast. We broke a day later. Scarlet tunic brigade arrived at the same time - too late to hold, but fast enough to cover a retreat and evac. Most of the non-militia got out, including mom. Militia took massive losses when it broke. Never found dad, and he never arrived at lord’s seat. STB (specifically Dockson, an honorable paladin, high ranking STB general type) shamed lord into acting. Looked like he would have challenged him to a duel (or just killed him in his throne room) if he didn’t cave. Militia kept fighting with STB until Ned'zailok defeated - which took another 18 months. Most dispersed after. Mom stayed in/near larger city when fight ended. I enlisted with STB, hoping to find more about dad. Never did. Been trying to help protect the weak and those screwed over by petty politics with the STB ever since (a few years). Always keep an ear out for word that Ned'zailok has resurfaced. Can’t ever be sure a lich is gone. Also keeps an ear out for any news of Yeldan, but stopped expecting to hear anything not long after the end of that war.
