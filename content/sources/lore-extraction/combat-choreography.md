### The Kobold Tavern Brawl (Chapter 0/1) -- Pilot Panel 44
**Combatants**: The full party of new recruits (Brigit, Sprocket, Stalwart, Nyx, Dallor, Tiny Rick, Nocturnus Wayne, Daina, others) vs. a swarm of kobolds
**Setting**: A tavern in the port town of Argos. Warm golden interior light, wooden tables, ale tankards. Evening. Jorgen crashes through the door bleeding, kobolds swarming behind him through the door and windows.
**Play-by-play**:
1. Jorgen -- human ranger in a torn gold military tunic -- SLAMS through the tavern door, gasping, bleeding, one hand on the doorframe. Behind him, the night is full of moving shapes.
2. Kobolds swarm through windows and the open door. Tables overturn. Ale spills. The recruits who were drinking grab whatever weapons are at hand.
3. Chaotic melee erupts -- this is the party's first fight together as strangers. No coordination, no tactics. Pure survival instinct.
4. The recruits manage to fight off the flying kobolds while Jorgen runs further into the hall.
5. In the aftermath, Dallor swipes a note from Jorgen -- written by Finkle, listing the precise arrival time and names/classes of every recruit. The first hint of betrayal.
**Signature moves**: None established yet -- this is the party's raw, unpolished debut. Brigit likely shifted to wolf form. Stalwart's physical presence dominated the space.
**Emotional stakes**: Strangers forced to fight together for the first time. The fragile social bonds from the recruitment yard are forged in blood. Jorgen's arrival shatters the tavern's warmth and safety.
**Pilot relevance**: YES -- Panel 44, the pilot's first action sequence
**Key frame moments**:
1. Jorgen slamming through the tavern door, backlit by kobold shapes in the night
2. The moment the first table flips -- recruits grabbing weapons mid-drink
3. Dallor's hand closing on the stolen note in the chaos

### The Kobold Standoff in the Sewers (Chapter 0/1) -- Pilot Panels 51-54
**Combatants**: The party vs. hundreds of kobolds (no actual combat -- resolved through diplomacy)
**Setting**: Underground ancient corridors beneath Argos. Ankle-deep sewer water. A single torch illuminating geometric stonework predating civilization. Hundreds of reptilian eyes reflecting torchlight from ledges and alcoves.
**Play-by-play**:
1. The party descends through a hidden sewer entrance, led by Jorgen. The architecture shifts from crude sewer to something ancient, precisely carved.
2. A single torch reveals dozens -- possibly hundreds -- of kobolds crouched on ledges and in alcoves. Eyes reflecting torchlight like scattered stars.
3. The standoff crystallizes. Neither side moves. The kobolds begin chanting: "SCARLET, SCARLET, SCARLET."
4. Daina splashes sewer sludge on one of them, provoking the horde. They ready for attack.
5. Stalwart Little steps forward and speaks in Draconic. Something about the dragonborn's heritage carries authority -- his voice echoes through the stone.
6. The kobolds retreat, parting like a tide. Safe passage earned through lineage, not violence.
**Signature moves**: Stalwart's Draconic speech -- the moment the paladin becomes the leader. His golden scales catching torchlight as he commands the swarm.
**Emotional stakes**: The party learns that brute force isn't always the answer. Stalwart's heritage matters. This is the first real moment of party cohesion -- following someone's lead.
**Pilot relevance**: YES -- Panels 51-54, the kobold standoff and Stalwart's defining moment
**Key frame moments**:
1. Wide shot: hundreds of kobold eyes reflecting a single torch in absolute darkness
2. Low angle on Stalwart speaking Draconic, golden scales lit by torchlight, mouth open with authority
3. The kobolds retreating into darkness like a receding tide

### The KNEEL Ceiling Trap (Chapter 0/1) -- Pilot Panels 58-59
**Combatants**: The party vs. the dungeon itself (environmental hazard + kobold stragglers)
**Setting**: A large underground room with a descending stone ceiling. Ancient architecture. The word "KNEEL" carved in dried blood on the ceiling surface. Also inscribed: "RUN LEFT."
**Play-by-play**:
1. A blood rune on a button starts a 180-second countdown. The ceiling begins to descend.
2. Pressing the button spawns kobolds (4x the number of presses). The party fights waves of kobolds while the ceiling drops.
3. The party spots "RUN LEFT" scratched onto the rapidly approaching ceiling.
4. Stalwart, Nyx, Daina, and Brigit run into the left wall and phase through -- just in time.
5. The remaining party (Sprocket, Dallor, Tiny Rick, others) fight off surviving kobolds as the ceiling crushes the rest. A river of blood from crushed kobolds floods down a spiral staircase.
6. Through the wall, Stalwart's group discovers Jorgen and Finkle's half-ogre henchmen (Stromble/Grom) in a control room pulling levers -- priming the ritual chamber.
**Signature moves**: Sprocket's mage hand retrieving items under pressure. Brigit's wolf form biting through undead. The party splitting and improvising under time pressure.
**Emotional stakes**: Pure claustrophobic terror. The dungeon is trying to kill them. The discovery of Jorgen in the control room confirms the betrayal is real.
**Pilot relevance**: YES -- Panels 58-59, the most claustrophobic sequence in the pilot
**Key frame moments**:
1. POV straight up at the descending ceiling, the word KNEEL carved in dried blood, close enough to touch
2. Characters phasing through the left wall at the last possible second
3. The river of blood cascading down the spiral staircase

### Jorgen's Betrayal and the Ritual Chamber (Chapter 0/1 & 2) -- Pilot Panels 67-70
**Combatants**: The party vs. Jorgen, Stromble/Grom (half-ogres), summoned ghouls, and ultimately Mordred (as Finkle)
**Setting**: The ritual chamber -- a cathedral-scale underground space with four supernatural waterfalls: blood-red, acid-green, mercury-orange, soul-blue. A central gear-like pedestal holds a phylactery. The Raven Queen's crest on a massive sealed steel door.
**Play-by-play**:
1. Jorgen's mask drops completely. Standing at the chamber threshold lit by four waterfalls, his expression shifts from rescuer to predator. He draws his weapon against the party.
2. Jorgen blows a whistle causing kobolds to rush in. Grom/Strom grabs Stalwart.
3. Stalwart kicks off the half-ogre, uses their bodies as a springboard to launch into Jorgen, putting his back to the ritual chamber and eyes on the half-ogres.
4. The other half of the party follows a river of blood into the chamber just in time to witness the betrayal.
5. Mordred reveals himself (Finkle was his disguise all along). He raises gnarled fingers -- every kobold in the room is instantly drained dry, their corpses contorting and fusing into ghouls.
6. Mordred also reveals three enslaved adventurers, including Corrumpere the Tiefling Sorcerer.
7. Sprocket touches the phylactery with mage hand and is thrust into a deep trance, connecting with the Demi-Lich (Sabine).
8. Brotor and Tiny Rick fight Stromble/Gromble while the ritual progresses -- levers pulled, liquids poured.
9. Nyx disrupts the ritual order by pulling the wrong lever, causing green acid to pool on the floor.
10. Dallor cuts a deal with Mordred to save himself -- the party's first internal betrayal.
11. The party shatters the phylactery. Sprocket deals the first crack. Daina widens it. Mordred obliterates Daina with a powerful spell.
12. The Demi-Lich (Sabine) is freed. Ghouls turn on Mordred. Stalwart plays dead and completes the ritual. A bright light engulfs the room.
**Signature moves**: Stalwart's springboard tackle off the half-ogres. Sprocket's mage hand touching the phylactery. Nyx pulling the wrong lever to sabotage. Dallor's treacherous deal. The party's desperate teamwork to shatter the phylactery.
**Emotional stakes**: Maximum. Betrayal on multiple levels -- Jorgen was never a rescuer, Finkle was Mordred all along, and Dallor sells out his own party. Daina's apparent death. The discovery that they've been pawns in a necromantic ritual since before they even signed up for the brigade.
**Pilot relevance**: YES -- Panels 67-70, the pilot's climax. This is the reason the pilot exists.
**Key frame moments**:
1. Jorgen drawing his weapon, four-colored waterfall light casting his shadow in four directions simultaneously
2. Mordred draining every kobold -- hundreds of bodies contorting and fusing into ghouls in a wave of necrotic energy
3. Sprocket cracking the phylactery -- the first blow against the glass revealing Sabine's crystalline skull inside

### The Gladiator Arena (Chapters 3-4)
**Combatants**: The Awakened (party) vs. ghouls, each other (forced gladiatorial combat), and ultimately Mordred's forces
**Setting**: Mordred's arena. 2,000 ghoul audience (later decimated to 200). Sand floor with hidden teleportation gates arranged in an X pattern. Underground, lit by green flames.
**Play-by-play**:
1. Mordred declares a gladiator battle -- the winner becomes his next vessel. The party must fight while appearing to comply.
2. Ghouls target the Awakened specifically -- whoever draws attention is swarmed.
3. Bergs camouflages himself in ghoul blood. Brotor throws luminescent manacles into the center, revealing teleportation gate markings etched into the sand.
4. Green flames blast, revealing the audience has dropped from 2,000 to 200. The gates are an invasion preparation by Xerxes and the Artificer's Guild.
5. Corrumpere and Bergs sacrifice themselves delivering amplification hex bags.
6. Three of five gates activate. Xerxes arrives -- 500 years of mastery and 500 years without revenge against Mordred.
7. Mordred panics and forces a phylactery onto Tiny Rick, draining all warmth from his body. His soul begins fragmenting.
8. Xerxes frees the Awakened through the gates. The party escapes as Mordred's arena collapses around them.
**Signature moves**: Bergs' ghoul-blood camouflage. Brotor's manacle throw revealing the gates. Xerxes' 500-year-old vengeance entrance.
**Emotional stakes**: The party is property, fighting for survival in a rigged game. Tiny Rick is cursed to become Mordred's next vessel. The rescue comes from an ancient love story -- Xerxes seeking revenge for what Mordred did to Sabine.
**Pilot relevance**: NO (post-pilot, but seeds for future episodes)
**Key frame moments**:
1. Green flames illuminating the arena, revealing the ghoul audience has been decimated
2. Mordred forcing the phylactery onto Tiny Rick -- warmth leaving his body
3. Xerxes' arrival through the teleportation gate -- 500 years of rage materialized

### Wraith Attack at the East Gate (Chapter 9)

5. Stalwart uses Divine Smite but forgets to actually curse.
6. A stranger on horseback arrives -- Sheila, a witch hunter. She kills EVERYTHING "like a goddamn badass."
**Signature moves**: Sheila's dramatic cavalry entrance. Brigit's Flaming Sphere. Nocturnus's failed acrobatics (comedic beat).
**Emotional stakes**: The party is outmatched -- the wraith is immune to their physical attacks. The arrival of Sheila shows them there are powers beyond their current ability.
**Pilot relevance**: No
**Key frame moments**:
1. Arrows passing harmlessly through the wraith's feathery form
2. Sheila's horseback entrance, killing everything in one pass
3. The wraith draining Nocturnus's life force -- visible energy extraction

## COMBAT STYLE NOTES FOR ANIMATION

### Character Combat Signatures (recurring across all fights)

**Brigit**: Shifts between human and wolf/animal forms mid-combat. As a wolf, she bites, tears skin, and body-checks enemies. As a human, she casts nature magic (Spike Growth, Call Lightning, Wall of Fire, Thunderwave). Later gains dinosaur (Quetzalcoatlus) and dragon forms. Her signature transition is liquid and organic -- fur dissolving into freckled skin. Combat note: she often self-Thunderwaves to reposition. Always described as "goddamn ruthless" in wolf form. Says "BORK BORK."

**Sprocket**: Never fights physically. Uses Mage Hand, Bigsby's Hand, Firebolt, Disintegrate, Dimension Door, and creative item usage. His greatest moments are lateral thinking -- dropping a boat on an aboleth, throwing books for thrust in anti-magic, launching dwarves as projectiles. Small gnome body, enormous spellcasting presence. Gap-toothed grin. Brass goggles.

**Stalwart**: Shield bashes, warhammer slams, Divine Smite. Speaks Draconic to command/intimidate. Breathes fire. Physical anchor -- described as taking up most of the room. His combat stance is immovable wall. Signature move: "The Stalwart Slam" (trademarked).

**Nyx**: Eldritch Blast is her bread and butter. Uses Hex, Mage Hand for utility. Sends her owl Hedwig for reconnaissance. Fights at range, cobalt cloak billowing.

**Daina**: Rages with battle axe. Gets thrown by allies as a projectile. Dives into lava. Cuts off limbs. Raw, furious, physical combat. Her axe "screams" when she Tarzan-swings. Later ascends the Anchor of War.

**Nocturnous Wayne ("Batman")**: Monk punches ("one punch man"), uses Nightmare (sentient dark sword), casts darkness, runs up walls, does Van Damme splits. Stealth attacks. Elements-based combat (fire fists, water whip, ice platforms). Called "Batman" by the notetaker.

**Bergs**: Maul/hammer user. Eldritch Blast from his beard. Flies on a broom. Throws allies. Later becomes a god of the feywilde, gaining Hex and cosmic powers.

**Randall**: Bard -- Eldritch Blast, inspiration, dancing (literally moonwalks while casting). Once cast countercharm by singing "I wish you would step back from that ledge my friend." Support role.

**Tezka**: Monk -- Flurry of Blows, stunning strikes, Hellish Rebuke (tiefling). Punches through armor. NAT 20 killshots with bare fists.
