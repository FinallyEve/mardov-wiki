# Villain Arcs — Mardov Campaign

A comprehensive trace of every villain's arc across the 136-chapter campaign, organized by tier. Cross-referenced from chapter files, faction pages, intrigue web, cosmology, prophecies, NPC catalog, and player notes.

## Tier 1: Cosmic Antagonists (Gods and God-Level Entities)

---

## Vecna (God of Evil Secrets)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced from Chapter 0 onward; operates through agents throughout
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Vecna Sworn (commands all four Zenith operations)
- **Motivation**: Divine jealousy -- Vecna and Sabine Ra were the original twin souls, both gods of death, but Sabine also ruled life and fate. Vecna's envy of that additional dominion drives every conspiracy in Mardov. His universal truth: "There is no safer place for secrets than a grave."
- **Key Events**:
  - Ancient history: Exploited the fracture created when Ramses forced lichdom on Sabine
  - Raised Peraxis Geth as an Illithilich, gifting a shard of the Raven Queen
  - Raised Rimefang from death into a white dragon body via the Black Hand Clan rebirth ritual
  - Commands V-Mardov (parallel dimension mirroring the material plane) through his avatar V-MORDRED
  - Organized conquest through the Four Zeniths, each with a lieutenant and nested agent web
  - Four Eyes of Vecna distributed (Green/Death, Red/War, Blue/Pestilence, Orange/Famine)
  - Threatened Reynaldine directly: "I OWN YOU" -- threatened the Feywild with corpses
  - Corrupted the Elder Brain through Peraxis's influence
  - The Death Pact with the Raven Queen hinges on harvesting the regent Goddess's limitless potential; both believe they will best the other at the moment of revelation
- **Relationships**:
  - Master of: Mordred/Ramses, Peraxis Geth, Oblivion Wayne, Reynaldine, Rimefang, Ned'Zailok
  - Twin soul with: Sabine Ra (the cosmic engine of the campaign)
  - Allied with: Therzadun Sworn (entangled but separate agendas)
  - Opposed by: The Awakened (the party), Artificer's Guild, Zodiac spirits, Silver Scales of Bahamut
- **Current Status**: Active -- rules V-Mardov, final confrontation at Gard'Daemon
- **Threads**:
  - V-MORDRED invasion of the material plane (the endgame)
  - The Death Pact resolution with the Raven Queen
  - All four Zeniths must be held simultaneously to prevent Gard'Daemon

## Asmodeus (The Bringer of Oblivion)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced throughout; direct presence in late campaign
- **Faction/Affiliation**: God of Destruction (independent, sealed)
- **Motivation**: Total Oblivion -- the strongest God of Destruction. His march is so relentless it led to the creation of the Rose. Swore to destroy the Rose and end all being. Seeds his blood on petals to create competing fiends worthy of wielding his power.
- **Key Events**:
  - Sealed with 10 of 13 seals; missing only the 2 Angel Seals held by Dominel the Just and Barachiel the Righteous (who hate each other)
  - Graz'zt needed Asmodeus's 13th seal for his Apotheosis scheme
  - **Chapter 136**: Killed by Josh Coolbreeze channeling his oath through Bergs
  - At death, three specters escaped: one into the distance, one upward, **one entered Nocturnus Wayne**
- **Relationships**:
  - Allied with (unwillingly): Graz'zt (possessed 10/13 seals)
  - Opposed by: Lolth (made deal with Raven Queen against him), the party
  - Hearthstone was coerced triple agent: oath to Asmodeus, helped Tiamat, then helped party
- **Current Status**: DEAD (Chapter 136) -- but three specters escaped; one now inhabits Nocturnus Wayne
- **Threads**:
  - The specter inside Nocturnus Wayne -- what does it mean for his future?
  - Two other specters at large (one fled into the distance, one went upward)
  - His death may have consequences for the seals and cosmic balance

## Tiamat (Chromatic Dragon Goddess)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced throughout; avatar awakened in late campaign
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Chromatic dragons; connected to Oblivion Wayne
- **Motivation**: "Anything may be obtained" -- her universal truth. Seeks dominion over dragonkind and beyond.
- **Key Events**:
  - Oblivion Wayne gave her soul to Tiamat via bat-worship ascension
  - Avatar awakened: Ashara Everstar (the template for Irethia's creation)
  - Present at the Harvest/Autumn Feywilde (Chapters 135-136)
  - Frozen fortress guarded by giants wearing a five-dragonhead tabard (Winter Zenith hieroglyphics)
- **Relationships**:
  - Connected to: Oblivion Wayne (soul-bond), Irethia (created in image of her avatar)
  - Hearthstone served Tiamat as part of triple-agent scheme
  - Opposed by: Bahamut, the party
- **Current Status**: Avatar Awakened -- active threat in the endgame
- **Threads**:
  - Her avatar (Ashara Everstar) and connection to Irethia's identity
  - The soul-bond with Oblivion Wayne -- what does it mean if Oblivion falls?

- **First Appearance**: Referenced; sealed behind Iron Doors at Mountain's Zenith
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Therzadun Sworn
- **Motivation**: Destruction -- seeks freedom from the Iron Doors to unleash annihilation

  - Entangled with but separate from: Vecna Sworn

- **Threads**:
  - The Iron Doors still exist; Therzadun is not destroyed, only contained

## Mordred / Ramses (Primary Mortal Antagonist)

- **First Appearance**: Chapter 0 (disguised as Finkle Hollingsworth)
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Vecna Sworn -- Machu & Death Zenith division
- **Motivation**: Complex four-phase evolution:
  1. **Lover**: Originally wanted to resurrect Sabine Ra, his lost love
  2. **Collector**: After Sabine's spirit shattered, pivoted to collecting her gifts/fragments
  3. **Conspirator**: Convinced the Raven Queen avatar to sacrifice her immortality, creating a rift to V-Mardov; gave up his phylactery to infiltrate and destroy the Artificer's Guild, taking Xerxes's body
  4. **Herald**: With his newfound body, his single goal became enabling V-MORDRED's invasion by destroying all keystones
- **Key Events**:
  - **Chapter 0**: Infiltrated the Scarlet Tunic Brigade disguised as Finkle Hollingsworth (a 3-foot gnome bureaucrat)
  - **Chapters 0-2**: Orchestrated the recruitment, kobold attacks, sewer descent, and ritual chamber. Drained kobolds to create ghouls, revealed three enslaved adventurers, attempted to complete necromantic ritual
  - **Chapter 2**: "2,000 souls, to be exact... They could have flourished if we hadn't betrayed them." Commanded Jorgen's execution for "outliving his usefulness." Party shattered the phylactery, freeing Sabine Ra
  - Possessed the Heart of the Wilde and used it to awaken the Forest Guardian (the zenith boss)
  - Ancient history: Was Sabine Ra's teacher (Ramses), grew jealous, forced lichdom on Sabine. When she rejected the ritual and starved her lich form, he shattered the cosmic balance
  - Manufactured the Orc-Human War alongside Reynaldine and Logath HellEye
  - Took Xerxes's body after corrupting the Artificer's Guild
  - Deployed agents across the Death Zenith: Salina (Death Knight Captain), Hag Coven, Golden Reach demons, Mandu, Jafar, Costa Una, Reva, Tatiana
- **Relationships**:
  - Master: Vecna
  - Former love/teacher: Sabine Ra
  - Rival: Xerxes (Sabine's husband, whose body Mordred took)
  - Agent: Jorgen (lure, turned revenant after betrayal)
  - Co-conspirator: Reynaldine (Orc-Human War), Logath HellEye
  - Disguise victim: Finkle Hollingsworth (real Finkle later freed and became ally)
  - The starcrossed three: Xerxes, Ramses/Mordred, and Sabine Ra
- **Current Status**: Alive -- operating through Xerxes's body, pursuing keystone destruction
- **Threads**:
  - Resolution of the "starcrossed three" (Xerxes, Ramses, Sabine Ra)
  - Forest Zenith prophecy: "the anchor of death shall sway, to bring peace to starcrossed three"
  - His presence at V-Mardov and the Death Zenith endgame
  - What became of the real Finkle?

## Peraxis Geth / Peraxis Phyrexia (The Illithilich)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced throughout; revealed Chapter 30
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Vecna Sworn -- Underdark & War Zenith division
- **Motivation**: Complex -- received one command from Vecna ("Assault the War's Zenith") but developed his own agenda. Wanted to make the new Paragon part of a dwarven god, NOT Therzadun. In some scenarios, wanted to SAVE the party.
- **Key Events**:
  - Origin: Raised by Vecna's power, gifted a shard of the Raven Queen
  - Has all of "Tiny Rick's" memories (connecting to ancient campaign history)
  - Created the Spell Plague (with Alhoon Ren's research)
  - Captured the Stag Zodiac (Shi-Shi-Gami) to locate Mountain's Zenith; suspects the Stag can "truly divine the future"; plans to use it as vessel for Vecna's latest creation
  - Holds Thorkon Fireforge captive; plans Ceremorphosis (mindflayer conversion)
  - Traded one conversation between Thorkon and Daina for Thorkon's compliance with Ceremorphosis
  - Cruel mind games with Daina via beholder relay: "Now that we know you're alive, we have a spare"
  - Corrupted the Elder Brain via Vecna's influence (only Ulitharid elite escaped)
  - **Chapter 30**: Revealed to the party
  - **Chapter 66**: Homunculus Thraalm killed -- portion of Peraxis's soul destroyed
  - **Chapter 106**: Revealed he wanted to save the party in some scenarios; wanted a dwarven god, not Therzadun's agenda
  - **Chapter 113**: Direct confrontation; Rimefang's sacrifice in same arc
  - The three Thane secret police forces (Blackguard, Truth's Light, Silent Flame) are ALL run by a single Mind Flayer working under Peraxis's Underdark dominion
- **Relationships**:
  - Master: Vecna
  - Allied agent: Oblivion Wayne (Vampire Queen)
  - Allied agent: Ghost-Eye Thorrin (Vag'Mahar puppetmaster)
  - Research arm: Alhoon Ren (double agent, secretly taking notes), Alhoon Pollivar (Umber Hulk facility)
  - Captive: Thorkon Fireforge, Stag Zodiac
  - Primary target: Daina Fireforge (psychological warfare)
  - Conflicted with: Therzadun Sworn (different agendas at the Mountain Zenith)
- **Current Status**: Alive (undead) -- described as "THE Central Villain" of the War Zenith arc
- **Threads**:
  - The captured Stag Zodiac and Vecna's plan to use it as a vessel
  - Thorkon Fireforge's Ceremorphosis -- did it happen?
  - His complex motivations (wanting a dwarven god rather than pure destruction)
  - The corrupted Elder Brain
  - Daina's reckoning with him

## Oblivion Wayne (The Drow Vampire Queen)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced throughout; depicted in Tomb hieroglyphics
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Vecna Sworn -- Underdark & War Zenith division
- **Motivation**: Total Drow/Illithid domination. Obsessed with the ancient Warforged army as the key to dominating the Illithid. Plans a Drow/Illithid vampire invasion of the overlands.
- **Key Events**:
  - Married Ebon Wayne (who discovered the Spell Plague and captured the Bat Zodiac)
  - Used the Bat Zodiac's "gift of the first bite" to create alpha vampires with innate loyalty
  - Deposed her own brother (Nocturnus's father) to unite all Drow under vampire rule: "The Drow are no more. Long live the Vampire Queen!"
  - Built a vampire-converted Drow army
  - Gave her soul to Tiamat via bat-worship ascension
  - Alhoon Ren serves her while secretly taking notes; his research (Umber Hulk eyes + Oblivion's blood) restores individual thought to Illithid hosts
  - Plans overland invasion using combined vampire-Drow and freed-Illithid forces
  - Tomb hieroglyphics depict: "a large bat hanging on a stalagmite; below, a wretched woman drinking what drips from the bat"
- **Relationships**:
  - Husband: Ebon Wayne (captured Bat Zodiac, discovered Spell Plague)
  - Nephew/nemesis: Nocturnus Wayne (prophesied to confront her)
  - Master: Vecna (War Zenith operations)
  - Allied with: Peraxis Geth
  - Soul-bonded to: Tiamat
  - Research: Alhoon Ren (serves her, but is a double agent within the villain side)
- **Current Status**: Alive (undead) -- Nocturnus's entire arc leads to confrontation with her
- **Threads**:
  - Nocturnus vs. Oblivion: the prophesied confrontation
  - Nocturnus was cleansed of vampirism (Ch. 109) -- how does this change the dynamic?
  - The Warforged army obsession -- collision with the party's need for Warforged at Gard'Daemon
  - Tiamat soul-bond implications
  - The vampire army's planned overland invasion

## Reynaldine (Fey Lord of the Autumn Court -- TRIPLE AGENT)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced; direct encounters from Act 3 onward
- **Faction/Affiliation**: Autumn Court / Vecna Sworn (serving) / Independent (true agenda)
- **Motivation**: The most complex villain motivation in the campaign. Ostensibly serves Vecna, but his true plan was to orchestrate bringing ALL villains together at the Convergence + Stone Table so he could ascend to godhood and save his beloved Gabriel (archangel of kindness). His ideal: "Fate is the death of freedom." His domain would be Knowledge and Trickery.

> **CRITICAL**: Reynaldine (Fall Feylord, Vecna's servant) is DISTINCT from Reynardine (Nyx's patron, "the Madness Bringer"). Similar names, different characters.

- **Key Events**:
  - Collecting Elemental Seals to open the Harvest's Zenith -- claimed to have Fire and Earth seals, took the Air seal from Kelik Temptus (who was absorbed into his amulet and died)
  - Hired Annie Hallowbrook (Red Skipper captain) to bring Kelik to Windhaven -- offered to collapse Cloud City in exchange
  - Used Xyn (Nyx's twin sister) as messenger
  - Threatened to disrupt pestilence supply shipments to Rimefang as leverage against Vecna
  - Manufactured the Orc-Human War with Logath HellEye and Mordred
  - Wanted DawnHaven cleansed of Uma's remnants to find the Diadem of Dawn
  - Invoked the Ritual of the Stone Table through Umbara Skuttle
  - **Chapter 106**: "Crippled" -- cloak fell revealing leeches forming a portal
  - **Chapter 133**: True plan revealed -- "Reynaldine had a plot the whole time." Orchestrated everything for his own ascension to save Gabriel
  - **Chapter 135**: Plotted with Hearthstone to open Harvest and Winter Zenith using Book of Shadows
  - Randal discovered via Legend Lore that Reynaldine is actually 4 beings: Juliannia (Summer Court), Graz'zt (Demon Prince), Gabriel the Kind (Archangel), and Reynardine/Prince of Harvest
  - Reynaldine's Apotheosis plan: collapse all pocket planes (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Material, Feywild, Shadowfell, 7 Heavens, 9 Hells) within his domain for absolute power, then segment everything into a "Matrix-like paradise"
  - Vecna warned him: "I own you"
- **Relationships**:
  - Ostensible master: Vecna ("I OWN YOU")
  - Beloved: Gabriel the Kind (archangel of kindness)
  - Composite being containing: Juliannia, Graz'zt, Gabriel, Reynardine
  - Co-conspirators: Logath HellEye, Mordred (Orc-Human War)
  - Tool: Xyn (messenger), Annie Hallowbrook (agent), Umbara Skuttle (Stone Table ritual)
  - Opponent: Tiberius D. Rex (waits for a way to redeem Reynaldine)
  - Victim: Kelik Temptus (absorbed into amulet)
- **Current Status**: "Crippled" (Ch. 106) -- true plan revealed but status after Stone Table uncertain
- **Threads**:
  - The Convergence aftermath -- what happened to the four beings within Reynaldine?
  - Gabriel's fate after being swapped with Graz'zt at the Stone Table
  - Tiberius D. Rex's hope to redeem Reynaldine
  - Only the Water seal (on Lapis Poseidon) was unaccounted for

## Tygras (Ancient Tiefling Warlord)

- **First Appearance**: Referenced in lore; sealed within Harvest's Zenith

- **Key Events**:
  - Ancient history: Made a pact with [[Graz'zt]] (the same entity Reynardine dealt with 2,000 years ago -- Reynardine shared the secret of channeling pure arcana in exchange for a portal between worlds)
  - His superweapon destroyed Uma, creating the desert wasteland
  - Sealed inside the Harvest's Zenith but it was "never fully sealed" -- the fourth opened, never closed
  - All tieflings descend from his fae corruption
  - His echo must be destroyed to free the Zenith
  - Destroying his echo rips a hole in the Fae and reveals [[Graz'zt]], the fae demon who gave Nyx her powers
  - Tomb hieroglyphics depict: "a classic red tiefling shaking the hand of a man in a hat and cane -- the cane touches the ground but his feet do not"
- **Relationships**:
  - Connected to: Reynardine (shared the channeling secret), Logath HellEye (same [[Graz'zt]] pact)
  - Legacy: All tieflings, including Nyx Khamestria
  - Opposed by: Nyx, Tesca (must wield Anchor of Famine at Harvest Zenith)
- **Current Status**: Sealed (alive?) -- feeding on Zenith energy, echo must be destroyed
- **Threads**:
  - Nyx's choice: destroy the echo to prevent future tiefling children, or rebuild the lost empire
  - The Harvest Zenith must be opened and Tygras's echo destroyed
  - Connection to [[Graz'zt]] (the fae demon, formerly independent, now under [[Vecna]]'s command) that is central to multiple villain schemes
