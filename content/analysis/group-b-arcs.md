# Group B -- The Tieflings / The Distillery

> The second adventuring party formed when the campaign split in Chapter 41 ("The Breaking of the Fellowship"). Originally called "The Tieflings" because of its tiefling-heavy roster, the group was renamed "Team Distillery" around Chapter 67 when they relocated to the Distillery in Vartos. In production notes (Ch. 64), the party is referred to as "Group B" (the Dwarves being Group A).

**Active chapters**: 42-87 (Act 3 split), continuing as the Distillery track into Act 4 (88-102), reuniting fully in Act 5 (103+).

## Group Dynamics and Themes

### The Distillery as Found Family
Where the Dwarves track is driven by heritage and destiny (Fireforge legacy, Mountain's Zenith), Group B is defined by choice. These characters -- many of them outsiders, tieflings, orphans -- chose each other. They brew beer together (Ch 64T), build a stronghold together, and defend the Distillery together. The name itself reflects this: "The Distillery" is a place they made their own, not a birthright.

### The Knowledge Bearers
Group B contains the campaign's primary knowledge-seekers: Sprocket (divination/fate), Randall (truth/bardic lore), Varis (inter-planar intelligence), Brigit (natural/progenitor knowledge). The Dwarves track is about forging and fighting; the Distillery track is about knowing and protecting.

### Trust and Betrayal
The Diadem of Dawn theft (Nyx stealing from Tesca, Ch 79T) is the Distillery's defining internal crisis -- mirroring Dallor's betrayal in the unified party. Unlike Dallor's, Nyx's betrayal is motivated by genuine belief that she's doing the right thing. The group's response -- sending Tesca after Nyx rather than condemning her -- shows how far they've grown from the party that beat Dallor unconscious.

### The Harvest Connection
Five of Group B's eight members are prophetically linked to the Harvest/Famine arc: Nyx (Owl), Tesca (Mouse), Irethia (Fox) at the Harvest Zenith, plus Brigit (Stag) and Varis (Doe) at the Forest Zenith. The Distillery's geographic proximity to DawnHaven and Tygras Logath is not coincidental -- these characters were drawn to their destiny.
