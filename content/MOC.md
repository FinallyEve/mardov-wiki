# Mardov Campaign Wiki

> The world of Mardov -- a 136-chapter D&D campaign spanning 5 acts, 24 player characters, hundreds of NPCs, and a continent's worth of locations. 665 vault pages and growing.

---

## Chapters (141 pages)

The campaign is structured in 5 acts with parallel party tracks starting in Act 3.

### [[chapters/act-1/index|Act 1 — The Awakening]] (Chapters 0-15)
The party is recruited by the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]], discovers [[Mordred]]'s soul-harvesting, dies and is reborn with [[Sabine Ra]]'s soul fragments, and liberates [[argos]].

Key chapters:
- [[Chapter 00 - The Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] -- Party origin
- [[Chapter 02 - The Gnome Effn Way]] -- Party killed, Sabine's sacrifice
- [[Chapter 09 - Never Trust a Hat]] -- First Prophecy
- [[Chapter 13 - Viva La Revolution]] -- Argos civil war, Third Prophecy

### [[chapters/act-2/index|Act 2 — The Voyage and the Underdark]] (Chapters 17-41)
Sea voyage to the [[Magister's Circle]], Zodiac revelation, Underdark descent, [[forgesworn]] legacy.

Key chapters:
- [[Chapter 20 - The Magisters Circle]] -- Arrival at the Circle
- [[Chapter 23 - The Zodiacs Revealed]] -- Zodiac spirits appear
- [[Chapter 30 - Into the Underdark]] -- Peraxis Phyrexia revealed
- [[Chapter 36 - Lego My Myconid]] -- First Forge, Nocturnus crowned
- [[Chapter 41 - The Party Splits]] -- Dwarves/Tieflings diverge

### [[chapters/act-3/index|Act 3 — The Zenith Wars]] (Chapters 42-87)
45+ chapters of parallel play: **Dwarves** (Vag'Mahar, Mountain's Zenith) and **Tieflings/Distillery** (DawnHaven, Cloud City, Fae).

Key chapters:
- [[Chapter 60 - Cloud City Parachute]] -- Cloud City
- [[Chapter 67 - Team Distillery Begins]] -- Tieflings renamed
- [[Chapter 75 - Birt Solo Mission]] -- Birt solo
- [[Chapter 87 - The Emerald Veil]] -- New tracks emerge

### [[chapters/act-4/index|Act 4 — The Final War]] (Chapters 88-102)
Craftkeepers and Distillery converge. The Good Guys Guild forms. Guard'Daemon approaches.

Key chapters:
- [[Chapter 088 - Act 4 Begins]] -- Craftkeepers established
- [[Chapter 100 - The Centennial]] -- Centennial chapter
- [[Chapter 101 - The Good Guys Guild]] -- Guild formed

### [[chapters/act-5/index|Act 5 — The Ascension]] (Chapters 104-136)
The endgame: Anchor ascensions, divine conflicts, the final battle for Mardov.

Key chapters:
- [[chapter-104|Chapter 104]] -- Death of Reginald Providence, First Forge
- [[chapter-113|Chapter 113]] -- Sacrifices, A Matter of Life and Death
- [[chapter-116|Chapter 116]] -- Convergence and Betrayal
- [[chapter-120|Chapter 120]] -- Winter's Chill, Zodiac souls unite
- [[chapter-132|Chapter 132]] -- Penultimate, the Stone Table
- [[chapter-136|Chapter 136]] -- The Final Battle
