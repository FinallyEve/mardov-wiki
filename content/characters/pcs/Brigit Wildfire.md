## Description

Brigit Wildfire is the emotional center of the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] and the campaign's primary narrator, having authored 109 of the party's 176 chapter notes. She is a druid of wildfire renewal who channels the ancient power of the [[Stag Zodiac]], chosen vessel of the Progenitors, and custodian of the [[Anchor of Death]]. More than any other party member, Brigit is the one who remembers, who documents, and who refuses to let any moment be forgotten.

## Appearance & Costume

### Normal Form
A young woman with vibrant red hair, partially shaved on one side, swept back and falling in loose waves. She has bright green eyes, fair skin dusted with freckles across her cheeks and nose, and slightly pointed ears. Her expression in repose is watchful and intelligent, lips slightly parted as though mid-thought.

She wears a **green bodice** with decorative stitching along the neckline, laced up the front with brown leather cord over a darker undershirt. A **dark cloak** drapes over one shoulder, clasped at the throat with a simple metal pin. Her forearms are wrapped in **tan leather bracers** with visible stitching. A sturdy **leather belt** with a metal buckle sits at her waist. Her overall palette is forest greens, warm browns, and dark earth tones -- the colors of someone who lives closer to the woods than to any city.

Her most striking visual feature is the **glowing molten fire orb** she conjures above her open palm -- a crackling sphere of orange-red magma and embers that illuminates her face from above, casting warm light across her freckled skin and catching the green of her eyes. This is her wildfire magic made manifest, and she holds it with casual familiarity.

### Progenitor Form
A dramatic transformation into something ancient and otherworldly. In this form, Brigit appears as a **dark ethereal elven woman** with wild, flowing dark red-brown hair whipping upward as though caught in an invisible storm. Her ears are sharply pointed, her skin has shifted to a pale grey-blue tone, and her eyes blaze with pure glowing white light -- no pupils, no iris, only radiance.

She wears **dark ornate armor** with metallic chains draped across her chest and shoulders. The most striking element is the **glowing red-orange energy** that cracks through her skin and armor like magma breaking through cooling stone -- luminous fissures running along her shoulders, arms, and chest. Embers and sparks trail from these cracks, drifting upward into darkness. A small amber pendant or jewel hangs from her neck. The overall effect is terrifying and beautiful: the freckled druid girl is gone, replaced by something primal, volcanic, and ancient.

### Visual Evolution
Brigit's appearance charts her arc from uncertain young druid to Progenitor. Early campaign Brigit is the green-clad girl with the fire orb -- warm, approachable, grounded. Late campaign Brigit in Progenitor form is cold, alien, and wreathed in cracking light. The two forms share red hair and fierce eyes, but little else. This contrast is the visual story of the character.

## Personality

**ENFJ (The Protagonist)** -- Brigit is the emotional center of gravity. She organizes, documents, worries, and rallies. She leads not through authority but through sheer force of caring.

**Motivations**: Protect her found family above all else. Understand and master the wild forces inside her -- the Stag, the Progenitor form, the zodiac connections. Secretly, she is terrified that the fire inside her is not renewal but destruction.

**Coping mechanisms**: Brigit copes by doing. She writes notes obsessively, makes plans, skyrites warnings over towns. She processes emotion through transformation -- literally shifting into animals when overwhelmed. Her humor is dry, exasperated, and grounded. She is the "mom friend" who hides exhaustion behind competence.

**Under pressure**: Frighteningly focused. The warmth drops out of her voice. She stops writing notes and starts giving orders. Embers trail from her hair. At peak stress she shifts into Lox or the Progenitor -- when she stops being Brigit entirely and becomes something ancient and terrifying.

**Moral compass**: Will always protect innocents. Will never abandon a party member. Frames morality through renewal: fire that burns to let new growth emerge. She would burn a forest to save the people inside it.

## Backstory

Brigit was an orphaned druid from the wilds outside [[windhaven]]. Her parents were killed by fearful villagers who did not understand her nature. She grew up trusting animals more than people, living on the edges of civilization. When the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] put out a call for recruits, she answered -- arriving at the town walls in wolf form before shifting to human, establishing her duality from the very first moment.

## Relationships

- **[[Daina Fireforge]]** -- Battle sisters. Brigit is cerebral and cautious; Daina is impulsive and violent. They complement each other perfectly. Brigit plans, Daina executes. The campaign's bedrock relationship.
- **[[Sprocket Overspark]]** -- Big sister / little brother energy. Brigit is exasperated by Sprocket's recklessness but deeply protective. They are the magical backbone of the party. Both assigned to the [[Forest Zenith]] (Elk and Rabbit).
- **[[Nyx Khamestria]]** -- Fire and Darkness. Reluctant allies who became deeply loyal friends. A warlock of darkness and a druid of fire who shouldn't work together, but do.
- **[[Varis Laindon]]** -- The Forest Bond. Fellow protectors of the wild, cosmologically linked through the [[Stag Zodiac]] and [[Doe Zodiac]]. The campaign's most harmonious pairing.
- **[[Stalwart Little Goldentooth]]** -- Mutual respect between two protectors. Brigit's notes shift from discomfort with Stalwart's brutality to acceptance that he channels rage productively.
- **[[Nocturnus Wayne]]** -- The party fought to save Nocturnus from the [[Nightmare Sword]] rather than abandon him. Brigit tried to polymorph him into a bunny. Uses Greater Restoration on him daily.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Druid (Circle of the Moon) / Barbarian multiclass, high level with access to 7th+ level spells.

**Primary weapon**: Magic itself -- she rarely swings a physical weapon.

**Signature abilities**:
- **Wall of Fire**: Her go-to battlefield control. Uses fire as a barrier, not just damage.
- **Conjure Animals (Giant Spiders)**: Summons pairs to swarm enemies while she repositions.
- **Reverse Gravity**: Coordinated simultaneous casting with [[Sprocket Overspark]].
- **Polymorph**: Transforms herself into an Air Elemental for flight; transforms allies for tactical advantage.
- **Wild Shape**: Used for utility (bat for scouting, spider for infiltration, pangolin for healing, Stag for divine ceremony), not combat tanking.
- **Transport via Plants**: The party's primary long-range teleportation.

**Combat philosophy**: Fluid, druidic. She moves like water around obstacles, then strikes like a crashing wave. In animal forms, she fights with genuine animal ferocity -- no human hesitation. She is the party's most versatile combatant, shifting between healer, controller, and damage dealer as the situation demands.
