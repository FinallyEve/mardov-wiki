## Description

Birt is a bird who chose to be wise instead of free -- a powerful high-level wizard wrapped in feathers, carrying humor as his shield and knowledge as his weapon. He deflects the weight of his power with jokes and teleports in and out of existence with deliberately comedic timing.

## Appearance & Costume

A **humanoid aarakocra** with an eagle-like head featuring sharp eyes and a curved beak. His plumage is striking: **crimson and dark blue** primary feathers with **cream-white** accents. His flight feathers bear distinctive **red eye-spot markings**. His lower body features **scaled bird legs** ending in **taloned feet**.

He wears **dark red-maroon vestments** trimmed with **gold** -- chevron patterns along the edges with gold dots and studs. The robes are formal, almost priestly, contrasting with his avian nature. Perched on his wing-hand is a **white dove companion**, providing visual contrast to his crimson plumage -- calm when Birt is excited, fluttering when Birt is calm.

## Personality

**INTP (The Logician)** with a comedic streak. The party's high-level spellcaster who deflects the weight of power with humor. Smarter than he lets on, funnier than he needs to be.

**Motivations**: Master magic. Protect the phoenix. Secretly, he misses flying just to fly, without purpose or destination.

**Under pressure**: Stops joking and starts casting. High-level wizard magic deployed with avian precision -- he sees the battlefield from above and drops spells like a raptor drops on prey.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Wizard (high level, access to 8th-9th level spells).

**Signature abilities**: Teleportation (blip in/out leaving a scatter of crimson feathers), blindness/light manipulation, simulacrums, phoenix healing through feather magic.

**Combat philosophy**: Birdlike precision from elevation. He drops spells like a raptor drops on prey. When the comedy evaporates, what remains is devastating.
