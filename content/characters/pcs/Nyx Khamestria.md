## Description

Nyx Khamestria is the unwanted twin -- a tiefling warlock who was sold before she was born, rejected by both buyer and seller, and who found power in being the one nobody planned for. She is the party's wildcard: chaos engine, lateral thinker, and the person who convinces enemies to throw away their swords mid-fight. Her quest to save her sister [[Xyn]] from the archfey [[Reynardine]] drives everything she does.

## Appearance & Costume

### Portrait Form
A striking **tiefling woman** rendered in shades of purple and violet. Her most prominent feature is her **voluminous curly dark purple-violet hair**, thick and luxurious, cascading around her face and shoulders in heavy ringlets that seem to catch and hold starlight. Two small **curved horns** emerge from her hair like ram's horns, their surface smooth and pearlescent.

Her skin is **light pink-fair**, her features soft and youthful with full lips and dark, melancholic eyes that hold intelligence and sadness in equal measure. Her expression is guarded -- arms crossed, chin slightly down, the posture of someone who learned to be small.

She wears a **white button-up blouse** with a small collar, tied at the throat with a **dark purple ribbon**. Over this she wears a **purple blazer jacket** in a rich violet tone. The overall aesthetic is unexpectedly modern and scholarly -- more bookish student than demon-touched warlock. This disconnect between her appearance and her nature is the character in a single image.

### Knight of the Flower Field Form
A complete visual inversion. In this persona, Nyx appears as a **cheerful, whimsical witch girl** with curly **pink-light hair** and small horns peeking out from beneath a **wide-brimmed blue witch hat** decorated with charms. Her skin is **rosy pink**, her expression open and laughing -- the opposite of her portrait's guardedness.

She wears a **blue cloak** over a **white blouse** and **dark skirt**, with **blue lace-up boots**. A **pink tail** curls playfully behind her. She rides a **wooden broomstick** through the sky, one hand raised in a wave, her **owl companion** (Hedwig) perched on the broomstick beside her. Green bead charms dangle from the broom handle.

The Knight of the Flower Field is the life Nyx would have lived if she had been loved. The contrast between this bright, joyful persona and her true guarded self is heartbreaking.

## Personality

**INFP (The Mediator)** -- Idealistic, deeply feeling, and perpetually at war with a world that told her she was a mistake. She processes everything internally. Her melancholy is not performative; it is the natural state of someone who learned as a child that her parents considered her a "failed experiment."

**Motivations**: Save her sister [[Xyn]] from [[Reynardine]]. Prove that being the "wrong twin" does not make her wrong. Secretly, she wants her parents to see what she has become and regret everything.

**Under pressure**: Her defiance surfaces. The shy posture straightens. Warlock powers manifest as fey energy crackling along her horns and tail. Her voice drops and becomes eerily calm. She is most dangerous when most still.

**Moral compass**: Would never do to anyone what was done to her. Would never sell a child, never reject someone for failing to be useful. Extends empathy to monsters because she knows what it feels like to be called one.

## Backstory

Two thousand years ago, [[Tygras]] made a deal with an archfey from [[Vecna]]'s plane -- [[Reynardine]], the Madness Bringer -- exchanging the secret of channeling pure arcana for a portal between worlds.

Twenty-six years before the campaign, Nyx's parents made a deal with the same fey, unknowingly. In exchange for the philosopher's stone (the ability to channel pure arcana), they agreed to offer a child as vessel. That child was actually a twin-soul -- both tieflings, both fey-touched.

Eight years before the campaign, a twist of fate revealed that the dominant twin (Nyx) was not the vessel Reynardine needed. Instead, her sister (dormant until then) was taken. Nyx received the patron's powers as a consolation, the deal was revealed, and her parents -- who believed the deal had gone sour -- spurned her as a failed experiment.

Seven years before the campaign, Reynardine returned. [[Xyn]] was born as a separate being -- the perfect vessel, easily manipulated after nineteen years as a dormant passenger. Reynardine's plan: obtain the [[Diadem of Dawn]], use Xyn to open a portal to the fae, merge the planes, and allow [[V-Mordred]] entrance to the realm.

Nyx's Summary 46 reveals the full depth of her internal struggle: she has pieced together the cosmic puzzle of her patrons ([[Gabriel]], [[juliannia]], Reynardine, [[Graz'zt]]) and made a leap of faith -- trusting Reynardine because she believes in the good person he once was. "I hope you can forgive me."

> **CRITICAL NOTE**: [[Reynaldine]] (Fall Feylord) and [[Reynardine]] (Nyx's patron, the Madness Bringer) are distinct entities.

## Relationships

- **[[Tesca]]** -- The Tiefling Sisters. Both tieflings in a world that hangs tieflings for practicing magic. Instant kinship through shared persecution. Both assigned to the [[Harvest Zenith]] alongside [[Irethia]].
- **[[Brigit Wildfire]]** -- Fire and Darkness. Reluctant allies who became deeply loyal. Nyx's chaos and Brigit's order create a balance neither expected.
- **[[Xyn]]** -- Her twin sister, the "correct" vessel, trapped by [[Reynardine]]. Saving Xyn is Nyx's reason for everything.
- **[[Reynardine]]** -- Her patron, her abuser, the being who holds her sister. Accepting his power feels like proving her parents right.
- **[[Irethia]]** -- Prophetically linked through the Harvest Zenith. Owl, Fox, Mouse.
- **Hedwig** -- Her owl familiar. Murdered in Ch. 8. The loss was devastating.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Warlock (Archfey patron -- [[Reynardine]]).

**Signature abilities**:
- **Eldritch Blast**: Primary ranged attack, twin beams of fey energy.
- **Invisibility**: Makes allies invisible for stealth operations.
- **Fey magic**: Involuntary-looking -- purple-green light bleeds from her eyes, fingertips, and the cracks in her composure.
- **Persuasion mid-combat**: Convinced an enemy to toss his darkness sword away in the middle of battle.

**Combat philosophy**: Lateral thinking that looks reckless but is often brilliant. She solves problems from angles nobody else considers. Her magic manifests as something leaking out rather than being cast -- involuntary, fey, and unsettling.
