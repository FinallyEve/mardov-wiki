## Description

A farmer who became a killer because love demanded it -- he slew his own wife and carries the weight in a pair of crossbows named after children he could not save. Clay Glennbrook rebuilt a vampire nest into an inn because that is what you do: you take the worst thing that happened to you and you make it useful.

## Appearance & Costume

*No commissioned portrait available.* From backstory inference: a human male, late 20s to early 30s, with a farmer's build hardened by combat. Weathered face, lines around the eyes, jaw always slightly clenched. Practical worn clothing in earthy tones and dark leather.

His defining props are **twin hand-crossbows** inscribed "Ian" and "Lilly" -- named after two children whose real names he never learned. They are always accessible: Ian on the left hip, Lilly on the right. He draws them in a single fluid motion that speaks of thousands of hours of practice.

## Personality

**ISTJ (The Logistician)** hardened by trauma. Methodical, practical, haunted. He does what needs doing without flair or hesitation.

**Motivations**: Kill Quintin Tyr, the Alpha Vampire who started everything. Run Molly's Revenge well enough that no one who sleeps there is ever harmed. Secretly, he wants forgiveness -- not from Molly, but from himself.

## Backstory

Clay was a farmer whose wife Molly was turned by an alpha vampire named Quintin Tyr. He killed his own wife to save others. He named his crossbows after two children ("Ian" and "Lilly") he found in the vampire nest -- children whose real names he never learned. He rebuilt the vampire nest into an inn called [[Molly's Revenge]], keeping the silver polished and the holy water stocked.
