## Description

A dragon who chose the lute over the lance. Frozen Song is a dragonborn bard whose name is a paradox -- frozen things do not sing, songs do not freeze -- and whose entire existence is about reconciling contradictions. Purple dragonborn are imposing; bards are gentle. He holds both without contradiction.

## Appearance & Costume

A **blue-purple scaled dragonborn** with a **rounded, horned head** featuring **multiple horn spines** extending backward from the skull like a natural crown. His scales are **purple-lavender** with a lighter underbelly. The horns vibrate slightly when he plays, adding natural resonance to his music.

He wears a **simple dark blue-purple robe** with **white sleeves**, tied at the waist with a cloth belt. The simplicity of his clothing contrasts with the dramatic nature of his draconic form. He holds a **wooden lute** in playing position, fingers on the strings. The background is a **warm sunset**, orange and amber against his cool purple scales.

## Personality

**INFP (The Mediator)** -- Contemplative, artistic, at peace with paradox. He seeks the song that his name promises -- the one that exists at the intersection of ice and melody.

When he plays, his eyes half-close and his scales soften in color. The music manifests as frost patterns in the air that melt as the notes fade.
