## Description

Diesa "Daina" Fireforge is the party's unstoppable force -- a hill dwarf barbarian forged in exile and hardened by loss, who carries a lineage she does not understand and a rage she channels into protecting everyone she could not protect before. She is heir to the [[First Forge]] and the [[bronzebeard]] dynasty, twin-soul of [[Bergs]], and the woman whose heartbeat commands warforged.

## Appearance & Costume

### Reborn Form
The portrait shows Daina after her transformation -- **Daina Reborn**. Her skin has shifted to a **dark purple-grey** tone, striking and otherworldly. Her hair, once red like her father's, is now **white-platinum**, pulled back in a tight bun that emphasizes her fierce, angular features. Her eyes blaze with **glowing amber-orange** light -- no pupils visible, just radiance.

She wears **heavy plate armor** of dark grey steel, covered in **glowing orange runic inscriptions** -- geometric patterns and dwarven symbols that pulse like a heartbeat. The armor has angular, segmented plates across the shoulders and chest, with **orange crystal gem inlays** set into key points of the breastplate and pauldrons. These runes are not decorative; they are the Fireforge legacy made visible, carved into her armor the way her father once carved weapons.

She carries a **large warhammer or maul** over one shoulder, its head heavy and brutal, the handle wrapped in dark leather. The weapon's silhouette against the burning orange sky behind her creates an image of absolute martial authority.

The overall effect is a forge at full heat -- dark metal glowing from within with contained power. She does not look peaceful. She looks like the moment before a hammer strikes an anvil.

### Pre-Reborn (inferred)
Before the transformation, Daina was a red-haired hill dwarf woman, compact and coiled with power. Her father's greataxe was her primary weapon, and she carried the Fireforge heirloom -- a piece marking her family's ties to the [[bronzebeard]] dynasty. She dressed practically in worn traveling clothes and armor, keeping an eye out always for a "strange black-eyed red-bearded stranger."

## Personality

**ISTP (The Virtuoso)** -- Practical, action-oriented, emotionally guarded. Shows love through doing, not saying. The person who just throws herself up a tower because the math says it is faster than the stairs.

**Motivations**: Find and destroy the black-eyed, red-bearded stranger who murdered her father. Understand what she is -- the Fireforge name, the heirloom, the warforged that respond to her heartbeat. Secretly, she wants a home.

**Under pressure**: Becomes a weapon. The rage kicks in -- muscles swell, eyes narrow, breathing goes deep and rhythmic. After the Reborn transformation, her runes glow orange and she becomes genuinely terrifying: a platinum-haired dwarf wreathed in geometric fire.

**Moral compass**: Operates on survival and reciprocity. Protects those who have earned protection and hunts those who have earned hunting. Would never betray kin. Would never let a child suffer what she suffered.

## Backstory

Daina was born in the [[Frozen Forest]] in the dead of winter. Her father, [[Thorkon Fireforge]], was once a royal weaponsmith of renown in [[Vag'Mahar]], married to Hlin. A rival weaponsmith, supported by one of the Bronzebeards, sabotaged Thorkon's weapon shipments by lacing them with deadly poison. When the Red Bronzebeard was affected, Thorkon was accused of attempted assassination and forced to flee.

Hlin gave birth to Daina in the Frozen Forest and died of blood loss and cold twelve hours after naming her "Diesa." Thorkon, devastated, changed his name to Tor Ironforge, renamed Diesa to Daina, and raised her in solitude. He traded with forest clans, forging weapons with deliberate defects to avoid recognition. He never forged fine weaponry again -- except for the greataxe he left to Daina.

On his deathbed, poisoned by a mysterious visitor -- a red-bearded stranger with jet black eyes and a strange tattoo -- Thorkon bequeathed the greataxe and the Fireforge heirloom to Daina. She set out alone, wandering from town to town, hunting the stranger who killed her father.

## Relationships

- **[[Brigit Wildfire]]** -- Battle sisters. Brigit plans, Daina executes. The campaign's bedrock relationship.
- **[[Bergs]]** -- Twin-soul connection. Both Fireforges, both apprentice [[Magna Har]]. Their parallel arcs toward discovering their heritage is a major slow-burn revelation.
- **[[Nocturnus Wayne]]** -- Combat partners. Mutual respect expressed through violence. She grabs him, screams "slow fall!" and rides him down cliffs.
- **[[Stalwart Little Goldentooth]]** -- He casts Shield of Faith on her instinctively. She hits things that get near him.
- **[[Dallor]]** -- Daina hits Dallor. Repeatedly. This is not a relationship; it is a running gag with a body count.
- **[[Thorkon Fireforge]]** -- Her murdered father. The quest to avenge him expanded into the Fireforge dynasty revelation.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Barbarian (Path of the Berserker / Ancestral Guardian).

**Primary Stats**: STR > CON > DEX.

**Weapons**: Greataxe (father's heirloom, later replaced/supplemented); warhammer/maul (Reborn form).

**Signature abilities**:
- **Rage**: Literal barbarian rage. Muscles swell, eyes narrow. In Reborn form, runes glow orange.
- **Reckless Attack**: "Just throws herself up a tower." No hesitation, maximum violence.
- **Warforged Command**: Her heartbeat syncs with warforged, visible as a shared orange pulse. She commands six simultaneously.

**Combat philosophy**: Quick for a dwarf. Moves in bursts -- still, then sudden explosive action. She launches, leaps, charges. No hesitation. She is the party's id: pure action, raw emotion, unfiltered violence. But her rage is grief, and her violence is love expressed through protection.
