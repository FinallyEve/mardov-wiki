## Description

Tiberius D Rex is a seventy-year-old bronze dragonborn priest who has seen too much and still believes. He wandered the deserts of [[DawnHaven]] for fifty years in penance and emerged wanting to see everything else. He is the party's grandfather: wise, rambling, occasionally saying something that changes the course of the entire campaign, then forgetting where he put his staff.

## Appearance & Costume

### Old Tiberius
A **massive bronze-green-brown dragonborn** with a powerful, weathered frame. His head is crowned with **heavily ridged horns and spines** running from his snout to the back of his skull, giving him an ancient, prehistoric silhouette. His scales are **greenish-bronze with gold undertones**, dulled by age like old bronze that has developed a rich patina. **Whisker tendrils** hang from his chin like sea grass, swaying gently and serving as his most expressive feature.

His eyes are **green** and carry the knowing warmth of seventy years of life and fifty years of prayer. His scales are thick, weathered, and creased with age -- the face of someone who has walked deserts and studied scripture in equal measure.

He wears a **red cloth scarf** at his neck -- possibly the only thing he kept from his younger self. His armor is minimal, a worn dark garment beneath. The red scarf is his visual anchor, the warm point of color against the bronze-green palette.

### Young Tiberius
A dramatically different figure: **slimmer, golden-yellow dragonborn** with clean, bright scales and a single prominent horn on his snout. He wears **white and blue plate armor with gold accents**, a **blue tabard sash**, white boots and gauntlets, and carries a broadsword. His bearing is confident, upright, and cold-eyed. He moves with the swagger of someone who has never been wrong.

The contrast between Old and Young Tiberius is unsettling -- same species, zero shared warmth. Young Tiberius was an arrogant warrior who destroyed the water genasi kingdoms. The fifty years of penance transformed him completely -- or so he believed, until the campaign's time fractures de-aged him and forced the party to meet the terrible young man inside the kind old one.

## Personality

**ENFP (The Campaigner)** -- Despite his age and the weight of his mission, Tiberius is fundamentally curious, warm, and distractible. He spent fifty years in a desert and came out wanting to see everything else.

**Motivations**: Fulfill the [[Bahamut]] prophecy. See everything, talk to everyone, experience the world. Secretly, he was an asshole in his youth and is terrified of the young man he used to be.

**Under pressure**: Stops being the kindly old dragonborn and becomes the Bronze Curator. Voice drops to a rumble that vibrates in chests. He speaks in prophecy and scripture. Under maximum pressure, the young Tiberius surfaces: golden, armored, ruthless.

**Moral compass**: Believes in kindness as a discipline, not an instinct. He was punished with immortality "to teach him kindness," and he accepted the punishment. Would never destroy another civilization.

## Backstory

As a young man, Tiberius was one of the [[Bronze Curators]] -- a nomadic tribe wandering the deserts of [[DawnHaven]] in penance for their shattered pact with [[Ranar Surefoot]]. Young Tiberius was responsible for the destruction of the water genasi kingdoms, an act of arrogance and cruelty.

The tribe requires fifty years of desert service before elders may travel the world. Tiberius served his penance and spent the last twenty years spreading the prophecy of Bahamut: "When the sand seas stir, and the matrim pact fulfilled, BAHAMUT's visage will lead the way, the Lost Nexus again rebuilt." He keeps getting sidetracked along the way.

## Relationships

- **[[Stalwart Little Goldentooth]]** -- The Dragonborn Brotherhood. Both devout followers of Bahamut, both loud. Tiberius is the comic relief version of Stalwart's righteous fury.
- **[[Daina Fireforge]]** and **[[Randal]]** -- The pocket-dimension trio. Randal's account: "Daina offered to scout ahead by running in a straight line for the enemy's flag."
- **[[Brigit Wildfire]]** -- She consistently misspells his name in her notes. This is somehow affectionate.
- **[[Bahamut]]** -- His god, his purpose, his anchor. The prophecy drives everything.
- **Narissa Sunspot** -- A changeling. "THIS IS MY HEART" -- enough to undo him.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Cleric (War Domain / Bahamut).

**Weapons**: Staff; divine magic.

**Signature abilities**:
- **Divine Authority**: Exercised dominion over a primordial dragon through pure divine power -- the closest thing the party witnessed to direct intervention from Bahamut.
- **Prophecy**: Speaks in scripture that seems pointless until three days later when you realize it contained exactly the information you needed.
- **Bahamut's Light**: When channeling, scales shimmer with platinum light underneath the bronze-green, whisker tendrils straighten upward, eyes go fully gold, draconic script carves itself into the air.

**Combat philosophy**: Old Tiberius fights with the unhurried patience of seventy years. Every motion is deliberate, not slow -- he is still powerful. When the young Tiberius surfaces, the patience vanishes and the power becomes ruthless.
