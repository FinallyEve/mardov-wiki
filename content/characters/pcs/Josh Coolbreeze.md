# Josh Coolbreeze

A half-elf paladin who entered the campaign serving the [[Autumn Court]] as the Champion of the Dusk. Blonde hair, pointed ears, silver plate armor trimmed in green and gold, a brown cloak, and a shield at his side -- Josh carries himself like someone who was trained for purpose, even before he understood what that purpose would be.

He killed a god.

![[assets/npc-art-source/FeyLords/Autumn Court/Josh Coolbreeze -- Champion of the Dusk.jpg]]

## Description

### Appearance
Young half-elf with blonde hair and a calm, open face. He wears polished silver plate armor with green-gold trim and leather accents -- practical but well-made. A brown riding cloak hangs from his shoulders. He carries a shield and a blade. The armor and bearing mark him as a knight, but there's a farm-kid earnestness underneath the formality that never quite goes away.

### Personality
Josh is the party member who does the right thing because it's the right thing. No calculation, no hesitation, no tortured internal debate. When [[Nocturnus Wayne]] was consumed by vampirism, Josh cleansed him. When [[Asmodeus]] stood before the party as the Bringer of Oblivion, Josh drove his blade in. He acts with conviction in moments where everyone else is weighing odds.

## Backstory

Sent on a quest to find [[Varis Laindon|Varis]] by [[Umbara Skuttle]], an agent of [[Lolth]]. The reasons behind this mission run deeper than they first appeared -- Lolth seemed to know exactly what chain of events Josh's arrival would set in motion, and she was amused rather than threatened by it.

Josh entered the campaign through the [[Autumn Court]] of the [[Fey Courts|Feywilde]], serving as Champion of the Dusk. His connection to the fey courts gave him standing in a world where mortals rarely earn the attention of archfey.

## Relationships

- **[[Bergs Fireforge]]** -- Josh's patron god. Bergs ascended to godhood and Josh swore his paladin oath to him. The relationship between a mortal champion and a god who used to fight beside him as an equal.
- **[[Varis Laindon]]** -- The one Josh was sent to find. Their meeting set everything else in motion.
- **[[Nocturnus Wayne]]** -- Josh cleansed his vampirism, an act that triggered a prophecy and changed Nocturnus's trajectory permanently.
- **[[Umbara Skuttle]]** -- Agent of Lolth who sent Josh on his quest. Lolth chuckled at his "misguided sense of Righteousness" but let events play out.
- **[[Autumn Court]]** -- His original faction. Champion of the Dusk.
