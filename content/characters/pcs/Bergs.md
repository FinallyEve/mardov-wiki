## Description

Barnabus "Bergs" Fireforge is a dwarf who became a god and never stopped being a dwarf. He forges connections the way his bloodline forges metal, and he would rather make a friend than win a fight. He ascended to become the sovereign of the [[feywilde]] -- simultaneously the newest god and the Demon Prince of Pleasure -- carrying all of these contradictions with the same steady hands that always forged and fought.

## Appearance & Costume

A **stocky, broad-shouldered hill dwarf** with a completely **bald head** and a face dominated by a **massive bushy red beard** -- a truly magnificent facial mane that is a character unto itself. The beard features **multiple thick braids** secured with **metal clasps and rings**, the braids hanging well past his chest. The rest of the beard is full and wild, red-brown and meticulously maintained despite its volume.

His face is **weathered and stern** with deep-set eyes beneath heavy brows, a prominent nose, and the hardened expression of someone who has worked with fire and metal his entire life. There is warmth there, but you have to earn it.

He wears **heavy scale mail armor** -- overlapping metal scales in dark grey-silver tones. **Studded leather pauldrons** protect his shoulders, reinforced with **dark metal shoulder plates** studded with rivets. The armor is functional, well-worn, and practical -- no ornamentation, no gold trim. This is a working dwarf's armor, made for survival.

After ascension, the visual shifts subtly: a faint warmth enters rooms with him, mushroom spores drift in his wake like tiny constellations, and his beard braids pulse with faint bioluminescent light. He still looks like a dwarf. The godhood makes that ordinariness feel startling.

## Personality

**ISFJ (The Defender) evolving into ENFJ**. Bergs starts as the quiet, loyal protector who acts through his hands. As the campaign progresses and he ascends to godhood, his sphere of care expands from "my people" to "all people," but he never loses the fundamental dwarven groundedness.

**Motivations**: Protect the network -- first the myconid network, then the party, then all of Mardov. Forge something that matters. Secretly, he wants to be enough: "I may be the newest god, but I am enough."

**Under pressure**: Becomes the immovable object. Voice drops lower, movements slow down, plants his feet. He anchors. In extreme moments, connects to the full network and speaks with the weight of every mind linked to his.

**Moral compass**: Tried to feed a mushroom spore to Tiamat's heads to redeem them rather than kill them. Will always try diplomacy first, connection second, violence last. Gave himself to the Stone Table to save everyone. No one gets abandoned.

## Backstory

Bergs was planted by the [[Artificer's Guild]] among the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] as a covert agent. His true heritage as a Fireforge was unknown even to himself. Abandoned by his parents and raised by wolves, he was "roped into this strange Maghar Dwarven mumbo-jumbo" as the campaign revealed his connection to the [[Magna Har]] and the [[First Forge]].

Through the campaign, he died and was resurrected by the myconid network, becoming something more than dwarf -- a living node in a fungal consciousness. He carried [[Kelemtor]] (a talking skull) as a partner. He discovered his sibling connection with [[Daina Fireforge]], learned he was heir to the First Forge, and ultimately sacrificed himself on the Stone Table to become sovereign of the Feywilde.

## Relationships

- **[[Daina Fireforge]]** -- Sister. Both Fireforges (siblings), both apprentice [[Magna Har]] (Daina the axe, Bergs the hammer). The slow-burn revelation of their shared heritage.
- **[[Sprocket Overspark]]** -- The Buddy Cop Duo. "Not looking too good is Sprocket, who got fwoooph'd by a spider. Someone ought to heal that guy so I don't have to find someone else to go on buddy cop adventures with."
- **[[Kelemtor]]** -- A talking skull carried on his belt. They are a buddy comedy about death and connection. The skull narrates everything and judges lovingly.
- **[[Brigit Wildfire]]** -- She is the heart, he is the anchor. Both protect, but through different elements.
- **The Myconid Network** -- His resurrection source and extended consciousness. When connections drop, he panics.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Fighter (Champion/Battle Master) evolving into divine combatant.

**Weapons**: Maul (traded to Ifrin); post-ascension weapon Peacemaker (hovers above him, radiating light).

**Signature abilities**:
- **Network Connection**: Pings connected minds like checking a phone; speaks with the weight of every linked consciousness.
- **Forging**: Uses blacksmith precision in combat -- every swing is a practiced strike.
- **Myconid Spores**: Post-resurrection, deploys fungal abilities for communication and control.
- **Divine Presence**: Post-ascension, light bends around him, spores drift, warmth enters with him.

**Combat philosophy**: Economical, brutal, efficient. Every swing is a blacksmith's practiced strike. After ascension, his weapon Peacemaker hovers above him radiating light. He still fights like a dwarf, which makes the godhood feel startling.
