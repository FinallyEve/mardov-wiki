## Description

Randal is the party's smoothest operator -- a bard who watches instead of performing, using music as cover for something deeper. He sings, charms, dispels, and inspires. Where [[Stalwart Little Goldentooth|Stalwart]] leads through force and [[Brigit Wildfire|Brigit]] through knowledge, Randal leads through charisma. He is dying, and the awareness that every day slips away gives him an urgency that manifests as gentle wisdom rather than panic.

## Appearance & Costume

A **young man** with **tan-olive skin** and slightly pointed ears suggesting half-elf heritage. His **messy dark brown-black hair** is tousled and unkempt, with slight stubble on his jaw. His expression is alert and watchful, eyes scanning the room even mid-performance.

He wears a **green and brown patterned cloak-poncho** with spiral and geometric designs over dark inner layers in brown-maroon tones. His legs are wrapped in **cloth bindings** above simple **sandal-boots**. A **backpack** sits on his shoulders and a **short blade** hangs at his hip.

He carries a **tall wooden staff** with an ornate **glowing lantern** hanging from its top, casting warm amber light. The lantern swings with his walking rhythm -- his personal lighthouse.

The overall effect is traveler, not warrior. Earth tones, warm lantern glow, muted palette. He looks like someone who belongs on the road, moving between towns, earning his keep before accepting charity.

## Personality

**ISFP (The Adventurer)** -- Observant, adaptable, more comfortable blending in than standing out, despite being on stage. The music is not the point.

**Motivations**: Find the [[Sunless Citadel]] and the [[Anchor of Pestilence]]. Earn his place through observation and timing. Secretly, he is watching someone specific in every crowd.

**Moral compass**: "There's something to be said for the power of ignorance... TRUTH beyond violence." His search continues. Every day is another that slips away. Death is closing in.
