## Description

A con artist who conned a god -- a rich kid who never earned anything in his life until he earned a cosmic debt. Riven Fen is the most interesting fraud in the world trying to become genuine before the bill comes due. His blade fights for him through "the depth of his aspirations" rather than skill, and the disconnect between his terrified face and his body's perfect precision is his visual signature.

## Appearance & Costume

*No commissioned portrait available.* From backstory inference: a half-elf male, lean, well-groomed (wealthy upbringing), with an aristocratic slouch -- nobleman's bearing that he refuses to use properly. One shoulder higher than the other, weight on one foot, casually leaning.

He carries a **broken panflute** on a cord around his neck -- his most treasured possession, inherited from his great-grandfather [[Vandallan Fendrim]]. The inscription changed from "Follow the Astral Wind to find your Boon" to "Seeker of the Wind's Secrets, Property of the Kingmaker." He wields a **rusty rapier** that materializes from the Astral Wind -- pitted, ugly, but alive when it fights.

## Personality

**ENTP (The Debater)** -- Silver-tongued, lazy, brilliant, fundamentally dishonest with himself. Talks his way out of everything because he never developed the discipline to fight his way out. The Kingmaker's pact is the first thing he cannot talk his way out of.

**Motivations**: Discover the Wind's Secrets and the Boon his great-grandfather abandoned. Become worthy of the power given to him. Secretly, he wants to be the person he pretends to be.

**Under pressure**: His blade fights for him. Eyes go wide, charm drops, raw desperation surfaces. The rusty rapier materializes and fights with grace he does not possess. After intense combat, he faints from the toll.

## Backstory

Riven was born to a human mother (Dierdra Fen) and half-elven father (Gan Fen), next in line for the family's ambassadorial tradition. His childhood was upper-crust but desperately lonely, dragged from island to island aboard the Words of Reconciliation.

At age nine, he found his great-grandfather's broken panflute below deck -- elven-made, unable to produce sound, inscribed in elvish: "Follow the Astral Wind to find your Boon." His parents refused to explain, dismissing great-grandfather [[Vandallan Fendrim]] as a failure who abandoned his quest.

Riven's image of his parents shattered. He saw their diplomacy as a sham. He would follow the Astral Wind and restore the family name. But he lacked the fundamental requirement of wizardry: work ethic. At the college of magic, he convinced classmates to fill out his spellbook, spent hours in the library researching the panflute, and picked up rapier fighting (inspired by his teacher Armineas) only to drop it when it required sustained effort.

A dropout and failure like his great-grandfather, Riven kept the panflute as a reminder to try again. When orcs attacked the college, weaponless and alone, he pleaded to anything listening: "I will give you anything you ask, please save me from this pathetic talentless death."

A single elderly voice answered, carried on an Astral Wind: "Play the flute and I shall grant you the talents you so desperately seek. I accept your offer..."

The panflute produced its first note. A rusty rapier materialized. The wind fought for him. Riven fainted from the toll. He awoke aboard the [[Black Croc Ale Company]], the inscription changed: "Seeker of the Wind's Secrets, Property of the Kingmaker."
