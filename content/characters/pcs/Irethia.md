## Description

Irethia is the party's enigma -- a child of the forest who carries something alien inside her. Fey-born, nature-bound, and host to a draconic passenger ([[Emni the Undimmed]]), she represents the intersection of purity and corruption. She hears a voice saying "Beware the Forest" and resembles the wanted fugitive [[Ashara Everstar]], creating suspicion she never fully dispels.

## Appearance & Costume

A **young woman** with fair skin and dark hair, shown **partially emerging from dense foliage** -- ivy, broad leaves, vines, and small red berries frame her face. Her features are delicate with **large, intense eyes** that hold a direct, knowing gaze. Her lips are red, her skin pale against the deep green surroundings. She is less a person standing in a forest than a person who is part of the forest.

In her hand she holds a **small glowing green gem or seed** that pulses with her heartbeat -- light flowing between them like an umbilical cord. This seed is her visual signature: the living connection between her human self and whatever she is becoming.

## Personality

**INFP (The Mediator)** -- Gentle, intuitive, profoundly connected to the natural world. Communicates more easily with plants and fey than with people. Her Summary 20 is written entirely from internal monologue: "Why must the gods shove me upon this journey, with such compromised strangers?"

**Motivations**: Understand what she is. Bridge the fey-touched and the material plane. Secretly afraid the tadpole inside her is winning.
