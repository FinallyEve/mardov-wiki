## Description

The quiet bard -- a musician who plays the drum because rhythm is structure, and structure holds the chaos together when everyone else is casting fireballs. In a party of godslayers and titan-fighters, Fovis is the one who keeps the beat. His sacrifice in Chapter 117 suggests he gave something profound -- possibly his life -- for the party.

## Appearance & Costume

A **young man** with a slight build and **fair skin**. His **pale blonde-platinum hair** falls softly around a calm, pleasant face. He wears a **tan-brown tunic** over a lighter shirt, with a **dark teal-green cloak** draped over one shoulder. Light trousers, dark boots, dark gloves, and a simple leather belt with pouches complete the ensemble.

At his side hangs a **large drum** -- his bard instrument and visual signature. The palette is muted and naturalistic: tan, brown, teal-green. Fovis is visually the plainest party member by design. No glowing runes, no golden armor. His visual consistency grounds him.

## Personality

**ISFP (The Adventurer)** -- Understated, genuine, present. Useful without being the center of attention. Experiences the world one song at a time.
