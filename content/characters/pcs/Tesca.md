## Description

Tezka "Tesca" Duskhallow is a street kid in a monk's body -- she learned discipline from the monastery but her soul was forged in the slums. A tiefling who handles her heritage with defiance rather than shame, she is the party's scrapper: fast, fearless, and allergic to hypocrisy. Her quest leads to the [[Diadem of Dawn]] -- the very artifact that got her parents killed.

## Appearance & Costume

A **lean, athletic tiefling woman** with **purple-violet skin** that catches light with an inner luminescence. Her build is clearly that of a trained fighter -- toned, functional muscle with a low center of gravity, always balanced on the balls of her feet.

Her **lavender-white hair** is pulled back and gathered into a single **long braid** that falls well past her waist, ending in a **decorative tassel**. Two small **curved horns** rise from the top of her head like a natural crown -- she never tries to hide them. A **pointed, spaded tail** extends behind her, used consciously as a tool for balance during kicks and climbing.

She wears a **purple-blue crop top** that leaves her midriff exposed, showing defined abdominal muscles. Her arms are wrapped differently: one forearm is covered in **cloth wraps**, while the other bears a **silver armband**. Her **loose flowing purple-indigo harem pants** are gathered at the ankles with dark fabric, cinched at the waist with a **blue sash-belt** whose ends trail behind her. A **simple necklace with a pendant** hangs at her throat. She wears **dark purple slippers** -- flexible, silent, perfect for a monk's footwork.

The overall silhouette is flowing and graceful -- the billowing pants and trailing sash create movement even when she is still. Her color palette is entirely purple-violet-indigo-lavender, with blue accents. She looks like twilight given form.

## Personality

**ESTP (The Entrepreneur)** -- Action-first, instinct-driven, allergic to hypocrisy. She thinks with her body before her mind. Charming in a rough, unpolished way -- the kind of person who picks your pocket while making you laugh.

**Motivations**: Experience the world her mentor described. Prove that a tiefling street urchin can be as worthy as any temple-raised acolyte. Secretly, she wants to find [[Elder Roku]] and thank him. She has never thanked anyone.

**Under pressure**: Becomes pure movement. Her monk training takes over -- flows between targets like water. She is most dangerous when silent, because Tesca is usually talking.

**Moral compass**: Hates bullies. Steals from those who have too much and gives to those who have too little (sometimes keeps some). Would never betray someone who shared their food with her.

## Backstory

Tezka was born to tiefling parents [[Tenzin Duskhallow|Tenzin]] and [[Kiera Duskhallow|Kiera]] in the slums of [[dawnpass]]. The family was desperate, which led to crime. Her parents took a job from the Dusker's Initiative to break into the [[Monastery of the Dawn]], find the office of [[Elder Roku]], and locate clues to the [[Diadem of Dawn]].

They were caught by Elder Roku, who showed mercy. He convinced the family to seek refuge in the monastery. But the parents were sloppy -- the Dusker's Initiative discovered the fake location they provided and ordered the "loose ends tied up." The family's house was set ablaze. Tenzin was killed first, telling Kiera to run. Kiera doubled back toward the burning building with young Tesca in tow.

Tesca survived and lived on the streets for years, thieving from merchants until a visiting monk spotted her. He offered safety at the monastery. She accepted warily. There she was teased and outcasted but trained, channeling her frustration into fighting under the elder monk's personal guidance. She retained her "propensity towards light crime" -- pulling pranks and stealing extra food.

When her mentor died of old age, Tesca was wrongly accused of stealing offerings and vandalism. The monks threw her out. She took it as an opportunity to travel, seeking the places her mentor had described.

The discovery that the Diadem of Dawn is actually the [[Anchor of Famine]] -- an artifact granting one wish at the cost of all spellcasting ability -- and that her parents died trying to find it, transformed her from someone running from her past to someone running toward the truth.

## Relationships

- **[[Nyx Khamestria]]** -- The Tiefling Sisters. Both tieflings in a world that hangs them for magic. Two responses to oppression: Nyx through cunning and pacts, Tesca through discipline and patience. Both assigned to [[Harvest Zenith]] alongside [[Irethia]].
- **[[Randal]]** -- They arrived together. Both outsiders, both with hidden depths.
- **[[Irethia]]** -- Prophetically linked through the Harvest Zenith. Fox, Owl, Mouse.
- **[[Elder Roku]]** -- The monk who saved her parents and later trained her (through his monastery). She wants to find him and thank him.
- **[[Tenzin Duskhallow]]** and **[[Kiera Duskhallow]]** -- Her murdered parents. Their crime and death led to her life.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Monk (Way of the Open Hand / Shadow).

**Primary Stats**: DEX > WIS > CON.

**Weapons**: Unarmed strikes, fists, feet, horns, tail.

**Signature abilities**:
- **Flurry of Blows**: Continuous, flowing strikes -- kicks, punches, elbow strikes, headbutts, tail sweeps.
- **Ki-Powered Strikes**: Contact points glow briefly with faint violet light.
- **Stunning Strike**: Precise pressure-point attacks.
- **Patient Defense / Step of the Wind**: Evasion and mobility that makes her nearly untouchable.
- **Tail Mastery**: Used consciously for balance during kicks, wrapping objects when climbing, sweeping legs.

**Combat philosophy**: She fights like a dancer who learned violence -- every strike flows into the next. Fluid, continuous, beautiful. She never stops moving. Her breathing stays controlled even as she shatters bones. Silence is her tell -- Tesca is usually talking, and when she stops, bones break.
