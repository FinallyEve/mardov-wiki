## Description

Casaval is Josh's character (Chad's brother), who remote-pilots this NPC. He works for a magic item procurement company -- though Chad knows this, while Josh's character thinks Casaval actually runs one. He is practical and proper, the perfect foil to the ragtag [[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]].
