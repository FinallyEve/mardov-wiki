## Description

An inventor and his best friend who happens to be made of gears. Knob builds things because the world keeps breaking, and Scamp runs beside him because loyalty does not require a heartbeat. Together they are a two-body tactical unit -- Knob creates conditions for victory while Scamp executes.

## Appearance & Costume

### Knob
A **small gnome** with a wiry build and large pointed ears. His **wild dark hair** frames an alert, confident face. He wears a **dark green patterned cloak-poncho** over **layered brown and maroon traveling clothes**, with **wrapped leg bindings** and **simple boots**.

He carries a **long wooden staff** with a **glowing lantern** hanging from its end -- their campfire, their lighthouse, their signal. The lantern swings gently when they walk and steadies when they stop. A **backpack** sits on his shoulders and a **blade** hangs at his hip. His **brown leather tool belt** holds pouches filled with components and gadgets.

### Scamp
A **mechanical clockwork fox** with a **golden-brown metallic body**. Visible **gears, panels, and mechanical joints** articulate his form. Through a **transparent torso panel**, a **glowing blue energy core** pulses -- his "heartbeat." He moves like a real fox but with mechanical precision: each step clicks, each turn is slightly sharper than organic.

## Personality

**INTP (The Logician)** -- Knob is the quiet problem-solver who says little but sees everything. His bond with Scamp reveals his deepest truth: he would rather build a friend than risk the vulnerability of finding one.

**Motivations**: Understand how things work. Protect Scamp. Secretly, he wants someone to build something for him, for once.

## Fighting Style

Knob deploys gadgets, traps, and inventions from pouches in the right order at the right time. Scamp is his offensive arm -- darting and biting while Knob creates conditions for victory. Together, a two-body tactical unit that functions through silent coordination.
