## Description

HAL is Zach's second character -- a "new game+" after [[Randal]]. Ostensibly a Warforged fighter, HAL is secretly a robotic raccoon sent back from the future after major upgrades to look more like a normal warforged. He is the D&D equivalent of a Terminator.
