## Description

Stalwart Little Goldentooth is the self-appointed leader of the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] -- a title nobody contests because he is an enormous golden dragonborn paladin who will argue until everyone gives up. He is the party's anchor: righteous, stubborn, divinely empowered, and capable of channeling divine magic without a deity through pure conviction. His personal quest to avenge his brother Stewart's death expanded into a cosmic role linked to the [[Pestilence Zenith]] at [[bynar]].

## Appearance & Costume

A **gold-scaled dragonborn** of imposing stature, standing well above most humanoids. His scales are a warm, burnished gold that catch and reflect light, giving him a naturally regal presence. His head features a rounded snout with small facial horns, and his eyes are a piercing yellow-amber. His clawed gold feet and thick tail complete the draconic silhouette.

He wears **gleaming silver plate armor** with ornate gold filigree -- swirling decorative patterns etched into the pauldrons, breastplate, greaves, and gauntlets. The armor is pristine and well-maintained (he polishes it obsessively). A **blue cloth tabard or sash** is draped around his neck and across one shoulder, providing a bold warm accent against the silver steel. His belt is dark leather with a purple-toned buckle.

In his right hand he wields a **longsword** with a gold crossguard and red inlaid detail, its blade long and polished. In his left he carries a large **kite shield** -- silver-grey steel decorated with elegant gold filigree scrollwork. The shield is as much a character as a weapon; it faces threats before Stalwart consciously does.

His overall silhouette reads as classic paladin -- armored, shielded, upright, commanding. The gold scales against silver armor create a visual metaphor: he is both the treasure and the vault that protects it.

## Personality

**ESTJ (The Executive)** with a deep ISFP wound. Stalwart presents as the classic paladin -- righteous, organized, certain. But his obsession with retribution masks a person who has been deeply hurt by injustice and is terrified of being powerless again.

**Motivations**: Deliver divine retribution justly and completely. Prove that faith without a deity is the purest form of belief. Secretly, he is still searching for Stewart, and the search has become less about finding his friend and more about proving that loyalty means something.

**Under pressure**: Stops arguing and starts swinging. His morningstar becomes a metronome of justice. He shouts battle commands that are half tactics and half sermon. His faith manifests as radiant energy pulsing from his shield in concentric waves.

**Moral compass**: Believes in cosmic justice -- wrong must be answered, evil must be punished, scales must balance. Struggles with mercy. His Shield of Retribution is both his weapon and his worldview: when struck, strike back. He is learning, slowly and painfully, that retribution and redemption can coexist.

## Backstory

Stalwart grew up at the Little Orphanage, raised by Gran alongside his brothers, including Stewart. When goblins attacked the [[Sunless Citadel]] and killed Stewart, Stalwart's life gained a singular purpose: find the goblins responsible and deliver justice. He was a sailor before the brigade, an orphan before that, and a paladin of [[Bahamut]] in his soul from the very beginning. His name -- "Little" -- is a source of tension for a dragonborn who stands tallest in any room.

## Relationships

- **[[Dallor]]** -- Natural enemies. Stalwart is the moral arbiter; Dallor the opportunist. The Betrayal in Ch. 2 was never forgiven.
- **[[Nocturnus Wayne]]** -- Two loners who respect each other's competence. Stalwart is order and justice; Nocturnus is vengeance and darkness. The Nightmare Sword arc forced the party to choose Nocturnus's soul over safety.
- **[[Brigit Wildfire]]** -- Mutual respect between two protectors. Brigit chronicles; Stalwart acts.
- **[[Tiberius D Rex]]** -- The Dragonborn Brotherhood. Both devout followers of [[Bahamut]], both loud, both proud. Tiberius is the comic relief version of Stalwart's righteous fury.
- **[[Daina Fireforge]]** -- He casts Shield of Faith on her instinctively. His protective instinct extends to everyone, but Daina draws it out most reliably.
- **Linda** (the chicken) -- His emotional support animal. He carries her everywhere and refuses to leave her behind.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Paladin (faith without a specific deity -- pure conviction).

**Weapons**: Longsword and kite shield (primary); spiked morningstar (referenced in some sources).

**Signature abilities**:
- **Shield of Faith**: Cast reflexively to protect allies in danger.
- **Shield of Retribution**: When struck, strikes back -- radiant energy pulses from the shield.
- **Divine Sense**: Used constantly to detect evil.
- **Lay on Hands**: Healing through faith.
- **Draconic Heritage**: Speaks Draconic with natural authority; can command kobolds and other draconic creatures through lineage.

**Combat philosophy**: Heavy, committed strikes. Every swing is a judgment. He does not dodge -- he blocks with the shield and punishes the attacker. His fighting style is a sermon delivered in steel. He leads from the front, always, and positions himself between danger and his party.
