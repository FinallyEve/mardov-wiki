## Description

Dallor Dallorbill is the party's first traitor and ultimate cautionary tale -- a greedy survivor whose deal with [[Mordred]] in the ritual chamber established a party norm that betrayal is not forgiven. He eventually ascended (or was cursed) into the Newest Titan, representing what happens when power outgrows the body that holds it and greed outgrows the person who feeds it.

## Appearance & Costume

### Titan Form
The only existing portrait shows Dallor as the Newest Titan -- a **colossal dark-skinned humanoid** towering over buildings in a destroyed cityscape. His skin is **smooth and featureless**, glowing faintly. His eyes blaze with **glowing white light**, inhuman and vast. The setting is apocalyptic: dusty brown and grey tones, ruined buildings, a desolate atmosphere.

The tragedy is in the scale: he cannot touch, cannot sit at a table, cannot be embraced. The person still exists somewhere inside, and [[Sprocket Overspark]] found the crystalline heart beating when he targeted the center of mass.

## Personality

**ENTJ (The Commander) corrupted**. His ascension reflects unchecked ambition. He represents "greed and a desire to avoid one's own fate." His zodiac is the serpent.

**Motivations**: Avoid fate. Accumulate power sufficient to never be vulnerable again. Secretly, lonely -- Titans do not have friends.
