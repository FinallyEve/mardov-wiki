## Description

The recordkeeper of last words -- a skull who has been dead longer than most characters have been alive, who finds purpose in witnessing endings and remembering them. Carried by [[Bergs]] on his belt, Kelemtor is the party's conscience, historian, and memento mori. He is "stoked" when there are "lots of last words" because abundance means significance.

## Appearance & Costume

A **skeletal skull** with exposed bone -- deep, dark eye sockets (sometimes with glowing red pinpoint lights, sometimes empty voids), prominent teeth, and a bony neck. He wears a **dark purple-maroon hood** draped around the skull, with a wet-dripping appearance on the fabric. Below the skull, **dark ornate armor** with **bat-wing flame motifs** on the shoulders is visible -- remnants of whatever he was before becoming a skull.

The background is warm **orange firelight**, casting dramatic shadows across the bone. One version shows him in close-up portrait -- intimate, almost conversational. The other shows more of his armored remains. Both convey the same thing: someone who found purpose in death that they perhaps never had in life.

## Personality

**ISTJ (The Logician of the Dead)** -- Methodical, dutiful, profoundly patient. His humor is dry, gallows-dark, and perfectly timed. He copes by being needed.

**Motivations**: Collect and preserve last words. Guide [[Bergs]] toward the right choices. Secretly, he wants to speak his own last words someday and finally rest.

## Relationships

- **[[Bergs]]** -- A buddy comedy about death and connection. The skull on the dwarf's belt, narrating everything, judging lovingly.
