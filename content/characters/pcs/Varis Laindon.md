## Description

Varis Laindon is the party's sentinel -- a half-elf ranger who fights because he could not save his father, protects because he could not protect his village, and keeps watch because if he stops watching, it happens again. He arrived through a portal from [[vodram]], an alternate reality destroyed by [[Vecna]], and carries that apocalyptic weight with quiet, devastating competence. His 200+ damage single-turn is the culmination of every sunrise he watched and every watch he kept.

## Appearance & Costume

### Standard Form
A **muscular half-elf male** with **golden-brown shoulder-length hair** and slightly pointed ears marking his elven heritage. His face is ruggedly handsome with a **short beard and stubble**, strong jawline, and watchful brown eyes that never stop moving -- tracking, assessing, counting.

He wears **brown leather armor** with ornate **gold-copper trim** -- scrollwork designs embossed into the chest piece and shoulder guards. **Fur-lined shoulders and cloak** give him a ranger's woodsman silhouette, warm and practical. Multiple **leather pouches** hang from his belt. His overall palette is earthy: brown leather, gold-copper accents, green forest behind him.

He holds a **longbow** at the ready, with **glowing cyan-teal magical arrows** that emit an ethereal light. A full **quiver** is visible on his back. The cyan arrow glow is his visual signature -- when Varis draws his bow, cyan light reflects in his eyes and the world goes still.

### Knight of the Dusken Wood Form
A weathered, grizzled transformation. His hair has shifted to **long silver-white**, his face more lined and hardened. He wears a **dark green-brown cloak** over leather armor, firing his longbow mid-combat in a **misty forest** setting. The palette shifts from warm golds to cool greens and greys. He moves like fog between trees -- visible, then not, then there is an arrow where you were standing.

## Personality

**ISTJ (The Logistician)** with a buried INFJ core. Varis presents as practical and duty-bound. He organizes defenses, commands guards, makes tactical assessments. But beneath the military bearing is someone who watches sunrises alone, carries a pseudo-dragon skull as a talisman, and dumps diamonds on dying strangers without being asked.

**Motivations**: Protect the weak from those with power and without conscience. Find Yeldan -- or the truth of what happened to his father. Secretly, he wants to believe his father is alive.

**Under pressure**: Becomes terrifyingly competent. Voice goes flat, movements become economical. Channels the Knight of the Dusken Wood -- silver-haired, battle-hardened, a figure out of legend.

**Moral compass**: Hates petty politics with visceral fury. Will always fight for those "screwed over by petty politics." Will break rules for righteousness but never for convenience.

## Backstory

Varis grew up at [[Tower Bridge]], a wilderness outpost near [[dawnpass]]. His father Yeldan was a hunter, scout, and guide; his mother Lianala a druid. At age 95 (roughly 17 in human years), they hunted a pack of pseudo-dragons together. He kept the skull of his first kill.

Two weeks later, Yeldan spotted undead scouts. The local lord believed him but could not convince others in time. Two more weeks, and the army arrived -- zombies, skeletons, wraiths, mummies, led by the lich [[Ned'Zailok]]. Varis and his father volunteered for the militia.

They held for a week. Yeldan was sent as the fastest scout to request aid. The militia broke a day later. The [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] arrived -- too late to hold, fast enough to cover retreat. Most civilians escaped. The militia took massive losses. Yeldan was never found.

STB commander [[dockson]] shamed the lord into acting. Varis enlisted with the STB, fighting alongside them for eighteen months until Ned'zailok was defeated. He has been protecting the weak and keeping an ear out for the lich ever since.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Ranger (Hunter).

**Primary Stats**: DEX > WIS > CON.

**Weapons**: Longbow with magical cyan-teal arrows (primary); short sword (backup).

**Signature abilities**:
- **The Draw**: His signature moment. When Varis pulls his bow, everything slows. Breathing steadies, pupils contract, body perfectly aligned. Cyan glow reflects in his eyes. Release. Arrow flies true.
- **Multi-Attack**: In sustained combat, fires volleys of arrows with devastating speed.
- **Colossus Slayer / Hunter's Mark**: Bonus damage that stacks to 200+ in critical moments.
- **Natural Explorer**: Moves through forests like fog between trees.

**Combat philosophy**: Military precision. He sees the battlefield from above (metaphorically), identifies targets, and calls them. His fighting style is vigilance weaponized -- every watch he kept, every sunrise he guarded, condensed into the moment the arrow leaves the string.
