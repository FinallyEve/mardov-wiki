## Description

Sprocket Overspark is the party's divination wizard -- a gnome who hides world-ending power behind a wide grin and mismatched boots. He is the vessel for [[Sabine Ra]], goddess of fate, carrier of the cloak of the [[Raven Queen]], and the living conduit between the world of the dead and the world of the living. He uses humor to deflect from the fact that he is carrying the weight of fate itself, and he builds Nocturnus-themed maid outfits with the same enthusiasm he brings to channeling cosmic necromancy.

## Appearance & Costume

A small **gnome** of diminutive stature, standing perhaps three feet tall with a wiry build and an oversized personality. His **wild grey-white hair** sprays outward in all directions, framing a face dominated by a **wide, gap-toothed grin**, a large bulbous nose, and bright, alert eyes behind **large round brass goggles** pushed up onto his forehead.

He wears a **tall pointed yellow hat** that adds significant height to his silhouette. His outfit is a chaotic assemblage of colorful layers: a **red vest-jacket** with silver-grey metallic accents and buckles over a teal-green inner layer, secured with various straps and clasps. A **thick brown leather tool belt** wraps around his waist, heavy with pouches, vials, gears, and gadgets. His pants are **off-white or cream**, stuffed into **mismatched boots** -- one green with brass buckles, the other brown -- a detail that speaks volumes about his priorities (mechanisms over matching).

His hands are covered in **brown leather work gloves**. He carries a **wooden staff topped with a blue crystal orb** that glows faintly with arcane energy. Behind him spread **golden crystalline geometric wings** -- translucent, mechanical, and beautiful, radiating light in sharp angular patterns. These wings are expressive: they flutter when he is excited, fold tight when scared, and spread wide for dramatic moments.

The overall effect is part mad inventor, part court jester, part cosmic conduit -- exactly what Sprocket is.

## Personality

**ENTP (The Debater/Inventor)** -- Sprocket is the mad scientist with a heart. He argues, tinkers, experiments, and pushes boundaries. Beneath the chaos is someone who understands cosmic-level magic and uses pocket sand and simulacrums with equal enthusiasm.

**Motivations**: Understand everything -- every mechanism, every spell, every cosmic secret. Keep his friends alive using increasingly improbable gnomish ingenuity. Secretly terrified of [[Sabine Ra]]'s cloak and what it means that it chose him.

**Under pressure**: Becomes the smartest person in the room and stops pretending otherwise. The grin stays but the eyes go sharp. Casts from pure mathematical precision -- Disintegrate to center of mass, Hypnotic Pattern on exactly the right heads.

**Moral compass**: Casual, instinctive generosity wrapped in chaotic behavior. Gave a beggar genie two gold coins and was rewarded with a dragon ally. Would never experiment on the unwilling. Believes knowledge should be free and power should be earned.

## Backstory

Sprocket was marked for death and freed simultaneously by the [[Alabaster Seat]]. He is a divination wizard who knows his end is coming -- "the ultimate answer to a question you ask yourself every fight." He carries a shard of a lich's soul in his pocket and hears [[Sabine Ra]]'s voice. His recklessness is not stupidity: it is the behavior of someone who knows exactly when and how they will die and is racing to accomplish everything before the clock runs out. His romantic interest in Trixie (a pink-haired gnome captured by hobgoblins) adds a personal stake to his cosmic burden.

## Relationships

- **[[Brigit Wildfire]]** -- Big sister / little brother. She is exasperated by his chaos; he is grounded by her organization. Both assigned to the [[Forest Zenith]] (Rabbit and Elk). Their fates are cosmically intertwined.
- **[[Bergs]]** -- The Buddy Cop Duo. Bergs is the muscle, Sprocket is the brain. Bergs's summary explicitly calls them "buddy cop adventures."
- **[[Nocturnus Wayne]]** -- Sprocket builds simulacrum servants dressed as Nocturnus in frilly maid outfits. He also "listens to the thoughts of Nightmare and finds nothing good." Comic antagonism masking genuine concern.
- **Trixie** -- Romantic interest. A pink-haired gnome whose capture sent Sprocket charging into a hobgoblin-infested tomb.
- **[[Sabine Ra]]** -- His patron/possessor. The goddess of fate chose him as vessel, and the cloak of the Raven Queen sits on his shoulders. He jokes about it because the alternative is screaming.

## Fighting Style

**Class**: Wizard (Divination), high level with access to 9th level spells.

**Primary Stats**: INT (primary), DEX/CON (secondary).

**Weapons**: Staff with blue crystal orb (arcane focus); pocket sand (legitimate tactical option).

**Signature abilities**:
- **Portent Dice**: Divination wizard's core -- he knows the future and deploys foreknowledge at maximum impact moments.
- **Disintegrate**: Targeted to center of mass with mathematical precision.
- **Hypnotic Pattern**: Deployed on exactly the right targets.
- **Counterspell**: The party's anti-magic defense.
- **Mage Hand**: Used for everything -- retrieving torches, holding phylacteries to ceilings, picking locks from a safe distance.
- **Simulacrum**: Creates copies (often dressed inappropriately) for tactical purposes.
- **Reverse Gravity** (7th level): Coordinated with Brigit for devastating combined casting.

**Combat philosophy**: The Swiss army knife. Sprocket does whatever the situation demands with the precision of someone who has already seen the outcome. When quiet and calculating, he is the most dangerous thing in the room. His crystalline wings and tiny stature make him easy to underestimate -- a mistake enemies make exactly once.
