## Description

Mirvess is an enchantress who makes everyone look at her so that no one looks too closely. Gold chains and sheer fabric serve as armor against genuine connection. She is charismatic, magnetic, and performing constantly -- living in the space between audience and performer, controlling every interaction through beauty and mystery.

## Appearance & Costume

A **beautiful woman** with **golden-tan skin** that glows warmly in any light. Her **long dark blue-black hair** is partially veiled, framing a face with **dark, expressive, piercing eyes** -- eyes that track everyone in the room simultaneously -- and **full lips** with dark lipstick.

Her jewelry is her defining visual element: a **gold chain headpiece** with a **teardrop forehead pendant**, **multiple layered gold necklaces**, **arm bands**, and **gold chain draping** across her shoulders and chest. Everything about Mirvess catches and reflects light.

She wears **sheer translucent fabric** with **gold embroidery** -- clothing that moves like water and reveals like smoke, never quite enough to be explicit, always enough to be distracting. This is deliberate armor. The background is **red curtain drapery**, suggesting a stage or court -- she is always performing.

## Personality

**ENFJ (The Protagonist)** -- Commands attention and uses it as weapon and shield. Under all the gold and glamour, she is looking for someone who sees her without the performance and stays anyway.
