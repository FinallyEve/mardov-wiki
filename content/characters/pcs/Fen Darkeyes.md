## Description

Fen Darkeyes -- later Fen BrightHallow -- is a shadow who chose the light. He began hidden, hooded, and faceless, and slowly allowed the world to see him, changing his name to match the person he became. His companion [[Mehen]], a fairy dragon who ascends into a spectral ice figure, is his emotional externalization.

## Appearance & Costume

### Normal Form
A **hooded figure** whose face is completely hidden -- animate darkness where features should be, with only occasional glints of eyes. He wears a **dark brown-green cloak** over **layered robes** in muted earth tones. Simple **leather boots** complete the ensemble. On his back, a **longbow** and **quiver of arrows**.

The color palette is deliberately subdued: dark brown, green, sandy backdrop. He is designed to disappear into backgrounds. The hood is everything -- when up, Fen has no face. When it drops, the reveal should feel significant every time.

### Mehen (Companion)
A **tiny fairy dragon** with **purple-lavender scales** and **pink iridescent butterfly wings**, perched on a flowering branch. Beautiful, bright, vocal -- everything Fen is not.

### Mehen Ascended
A **tall spectral figure** with **crystalline antler growths** from the head, **glowing cyan eyes**, and **dark ornate robes** flowing with **cyan-teal energy streams**. A blade in hand. The hidden thing becoming the most visible thing in the scene.

## Personality

**INTP (The Logician)** -- Analytical, reserved. Operates best from observation. Communicates through Mehen rather than directly. Sees patterns everyone else misses.

**Motivations**: Right the wrongs committed as "Darkeyes." Protect Mehen. Secretly, he wants to be seen.
