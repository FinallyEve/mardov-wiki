## Description

Columbia the Closer is one of [[Tygras|Tygras Firebrand]]'s Thirteen Damned -- a fiend strike force operating across Mardov. He is physically present at [[The Roost]] Hotel and Casino in [[Cloud City]], stationed on Floor One near The Pool. His portrait is filed in Euclid's Study (Floor Four), suggesting he pushed deep into the Roost's upper levels during the Cloud City crisis.

He commands two lieutenants: [[Goadie LaDoux]] (a succubus) and [[Minnie LaDoux]] (an incubus), both disguised with Cajun accents and operating under Dominate Person. They posed as ordinary Roost staff while Columbia's fiends infiltrated the casino basement and assaulted [[Euclid]]'s Study.

## Plan A

Columbia's mission at Cloud City was to reach and kill [[Euclid]], which would collapse Cloud City and free the Storm King ([[Terrell Temptus]]) from the Temple of the 9 Winds. Euclid's Study contains notes on the ritual needed to save [[Xyn Khamestria]]/[[Gabriel]], making Columbia's assault a direct threat to multiple campaign objectives.

Columbia intended to exploit the "now childless" Storm King's situation to his advantage. His plan ran parallel to [[Lorelai Clary]]'s Plan B -- Lorelai sought to sabotage Plan A to gain [[Reynaldine]]'s favor rather than Columbia's.

## In-Session Events

- The party discovered fiends throughout the Roost basement and identified Minnie LaDoux as a fiend (gold eye underneath her blue eye).
- [[Goadie LaDoux|Goadie]] was captured and interrogated in the Zone of Truth, revealing two factions at work: fiends and Lorelai.
- [[Randall]] impersonated Goadie to infiltrate a room with [[Lorelai Clary|Lorelai]] and defeated the occupants.
- Lorelei had a fiend pact with Columbia the Closer, gaining warlock powers through him. A brooch protected her from his influence; without it, she was not safe.
- After the party rescued Euclid, Lorelei reported she "doesn't feel Columbo. He's very far away on this plane, but very far up. Is it the moon or the sun?"
- Later reference: "It's gonna be bad. Like Columbo." -- used as shorthand for a serious fiend threat.

## The Thirteen Damned

The Thirteen Damned are [[Graz'zt]]'s main strike force. Three of them are Abashai on loan from [[Tiamat]] and [[Asmodeus]]. Known members include Almeera the Clairvoyant, Goballan the Wanderer, a Rakshasa (Juliesive), Abishai, an Ultroloth, and a Goristro. No other group of villains has the same kill count on party members. Columbia is confirmed as one of the Thirteen.
