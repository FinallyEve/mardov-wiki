## Description

![[assets/npc-art-source/Reynardine/reynardine-ref-autumn-portrait.png]]


Reynardine, the Madness Bringer, is the archfey patron of [[Nyx Khamestria]]. Two thousand years ago, he made a deal with [[Tygras]] -- sharing the secret of channeling pure arcana in exchange for a portal between worlds. Twenty-six years before the campaign, he made a deal with Nyx's parents for a twin-soul vessel, leading to the birth of Nyx and [[Xyn]].

> **CRITICAL DISTINCTION**: Reynardine (Nyx's patron, the Madness Bringer) is NOT the same entity as [[Reynaldine]] (the Fall Feylord serving [[Vecna]]).

## The Plan

1. Obtain the [[Diadem of Dawn]]
2. Use [[Xyn]] to open a portal to the fae
3. Merge the fae and material planes, allowing [[V-Mordred]] entrance to the realm and kicking off GARD'DAEMON

## Significance

Nyx's Summary 46 reveals she has chosen to trust Reynardine, believing in "the good person he once was." This leap of faith -- trusting a manipulative archfey because she believes in redemption -- is the campaign's most psychologically complex character moment. Her sign-off: "I hope you can forgive me."

Reynardine is part of Nyx's patron chain: [[Gabriel]] -> [[juliannia]] -> Reynardine -> [[Graz'zt]].
