## Description

![[assets/npc-art-source/Gods/sabine ra.jpg]]


Sabine Ra is the goddess of fate, formerly imprisoned as a Demi-Lich in a phylactery beneath [[argos]]. When [[Sprocket Overspark]] touched the phylactery with mage hand in Chapter 2, he was thrust into a deep trance, connecting with Sabine for the first time. The party shattered the phylactery, freeing her.

She chose Sprocket as her vessel. Her cloak -- merged with the power of the [[Raven Queen]] -- settled on the gnome's shoulders, transforming him from a clever divination wizard into the living conduit between the world of the dead and the living.

Depicted at "the last battle" and alongside [[ra]].

## Significance

Sabine's liberation is the pilot's pivotal moment -- the act that sets the entire campaign in motion. Her choice of Sprocket as vessel is one of the campaign's great ironies: the most powerful cosmic force chose the smallest, most chaotic, most unlikely carrier.
