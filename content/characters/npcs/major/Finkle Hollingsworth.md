## Description

Finkle Hollingsworth is a three-foot **gnome** bureaucrat who commanded the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] recruitment through institutional authority. He was the party's first shared antagonist -- all PCs were unified in discomfort with him before they trusted each other.

> **CRITICAL CORRECTION**: Finkle is a **Gnome** (NOT a Goblin).

In the ritual chamber, [[Mordred]] revealed that "Finkle" was his disguise all along. The real Finkle may or may not exist separately -- "Young Finkle" art in the NPC collection suggests there was a real person whose identity Mordred stole.

## Role in the Campaign

Finkle represents institutional betrayal -- the first authority figure the party encountered was already compromised. His note listing the precise arrival times and classes of every recruit (pickpocketed from [[Jorgen]] by [[Dallor]]) was the first hint that the brigade's recruitment was a trap.

[[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] (compromised by [[Vecna Sworn]]).
