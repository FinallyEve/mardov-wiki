## Description

Thorkon Fireforge was [[Daina Fireforge]]'s father -- a Maghar of the Axe, royal weaponsmith of amazing renown, and a Forgesworn who served the [[bronzebeard]] dynasty. He was framed for attempted assassination of a Bronzebeard, forced to flee to the [[Frozen Forest]] where his wife Hlin died after giving birth to Daina, and was ultimately murdered by a mysterious "black-eyed, red-bearded stranger" who poisoned him.

His true name was hidden behind the alias "Tor Ironforge." He renamed his daughter from "Diesa" to "Daina" and lived in exile, deliberately forging weapons with defects to avoid recognition. The only fine weapon he ever made again was the greataxe he left to Daina.

## Significance

Thorkon's murder is the catalyst for Daina's entire character arc. His connection to the Magna Har, the Bronzebeard dynasty, and the First Forge makes him central to the campaign's dwarven mythology. [[Ghost Eye Thorrin]] betrayed him. [[Decan Smithrunner]] was his trusted contact who still runs a rebellion and forge.
