## Description

Peraxis Geth is an Illithilich -- an illithid lich raised by Vecna's power, gifted a shard of the [[Raven Queen]]. He is the creator of the Spell Plague, possesses all of "Tiny Rick's" memories (connecting to early campaign events), and has a single command from Vecna: "Assault the War's Zenith."

He has captured the [[Stag Zodiac]] (Shi-Shi-Gami), directly threatening [[Brigit Wildfire]]'s cosmic connection. The Elder Brain has been corrupted by Vecna's influence through Peraxis, with only the Ulitharid elite escaping corruption.

## Role in the Campaign

Peraxis is the key antagonist of the War Zenith arc and a major threat to the Underdark faction. His Spell Plague research, conducted through servants Alhoon Pollivar and Alhoon Ren, threatens all of Mardov.
