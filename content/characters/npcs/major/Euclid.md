## Description

Euclid is an owl humanoid creature -- an Owl Aarakocra and the actual leader of Laputa Town in [[Cloud City]]. The title of Leader of the Roost goes to Euclid. He resides in Euclid's Study, a vast library "glowing blue somehow" filled with piles of books, accessible via a gate in the basement of [[The Roost]] Hotel and Casino. The study is physically located on the moon.

He is one of the forms/aspects of the Owl Zodiac in [[Kolyma's Twelve]], alongside [[Archimedes]], Helm (Precursor of Winter), and [[Alexandria]].

## Roles and Titles

- **Leader of Laputa Town** -- The Roost.docx confirms "the title of Leader goes to Euclid."
- **Keeper of the Divine Seal** -- He was the keeper of the Divine Seal, though by the time the party meets him he states he "is no longer" its keeper. [[Reynaldine]] took Gabriel's Divine Seal and caused the collapse of the Celestial Realm.
- **Keeper of the Ragnarok Protocol** -- The exit strategy for the Aarakocra race during an apocalyptic event. Requires a copper-tipped tube to reinstate. Contains the essence of [[Zell]] and [[Terrell Temptus]].
- **Original discoverer of the Resurgence of the Progenitors** -- referenced in DM notes.
- **Old mentor of [[Reynardine]]** -- responsible (through [[Archimedes]]) for Reynardine becoming the Knight of the Dusken Wood.

## In-Session Events

### Chapter 38X -- Euclid's Study

[[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] and Stalwart were pulled through a jarring portal into Euclid's Study. They saw visions of an owl. Euclid greeted them -- an owl humanoid creature among piles of books. He had only wanted one of them. Behind him, a window blinked yellow-white, and a huge voice (identified as [[Archimedes]]) told Euclid to calm down.

Euclid called Nyx "the Tome-Sworn" and warned she was on thin ice. He shared critical lore:

- [[Reynaldine]] was once a Prince of Harvest, now Ruler of the Feywilde West of the Glittering Run. Those under the Queen of Thorns call him an Abomination.
- The 7 Heavens were ruled by Barachiel the Just, Dominel the Righteous, and Gabriel the Kind.
- Reynaldine took Gabriel's Divine Seal and caused the collapse of the Celestial Realm and the 9 Hells -- they merged into the Feywilde.
- Euclid believes it possible to bring [[Juliannia]] back with Gabriel, but they would need [[Xyn Khamestria|Xyn]], [[Tygras]], and Reynaldine's pactbound.

He warned: "Be careful -- we might not want this information, for knowledge is power. He might not know, but he'll know that Euclid talked to Nyx."

### The Roost -- Fiend Assault

[[Mr. Tux]] reported that Euclid "hasn't left his study in quite some time" and the fiend problem had been ongoing for three to six weeks. The pool had been shut down. Fiends (under [[Columbia the Closer]]'s command) were assaulting Euclid's Study from the Roost basement on a tip from [[Lorelai Clary|Lorelei]].

The party fought through the fiends. When [[Brigit]] was forcibly transformed into [[Archimedes]] by the Zodiac Locket, she phased in and out onto Euclid while the fiends had no idea what was happening. Euclid was confused that Archimedes was "now calling himself Brigit."

Euclid needed a vial of water (with copper caps) to keep the city up -- which the party had. He met Nyx, pointed out his book, and was "very confused." He sent Stalwart off to deal with the fiend. The party saved Euclid.

### On the Moon (Chapter 58T)

The party traveled to the moon with Euclid. He lectured Nyx as they headed to a dais inside the library. [[Archimedes]] was found dead (due to the feather the party had found). Brigit faced a choice: give up Archimedes' remains, or stay at the dais for 24 hours to get Ragnarok back in place.

Later, Archimedes' will worked through Brigit's locket, pulling her to a sigil on a pan flute. Brigit fell into Euclid's study -- referred to as "Alexandria" -- where Euclid was talking to [[Xyn Khamestria|Xyn]]. Euclid said he would call Brigit back if he needed her.

## Euclid's Study

The study is a huge library on the moon, glowing blue. It connects to The Roost via a gate in the basement. The study contains notes on the ritual needed to save Xyn/Gabriel. A bifrost-like technicolor portal connects the study to the material plane.

The study itself may also be called "Alexandria" (per player notes: "Brigit suddenly falls into Euclid's study -- i.e. Alexandria").
