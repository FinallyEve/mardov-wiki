## Description

Mordred is Vecna's primary mortal agent and the campaign's first villain. He infiltrated the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] recruitment disguised as **Finkle Hollingsworth**, a three-foot gnome bureaucrat, orchestrating the pilot's ritual chamber betrayal. His true form is depicted in multiple versions ("Mordred Completed", "New Mordred"), suggesting ongoing transformation.

> **CRITICAL CORRECTION**: Finkle Hollingsworth is a **Gnome** (NOT a Goblin). He is the disguise Mordred used to infiltrate the Scarlet Tunic Brigade.

## Role in the Campaign

Mordred orchestrated the events of Chapters 0-2: the recruitment, the kobold attacks, the sewer descent, and the ritual chamber. He drained every kobold in the room to create ghouls, revealed three enslaved adventurers, and nearly completed the necromantic ritual before the party shattered the phylactery and freed [[Sabine Ra]].

His plan continues through the [[Vecna Sworn]] organization, with agents at each Zenith working toward Vecna's ascension.
