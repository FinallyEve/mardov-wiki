## Description

Archimedes is the Owl Zodiac of [[Kolyma's Twelve]] and the former protector of [[Cloud City]]. He operated from [[Euclid]]'s Study on the moon and is one of the forms/aspects of the Owl Zodiac alongside Euclid (Keeper of Divine Seal), Helm (Precursor of Winter), and [[Alexandria]].

He was recently deceased by the time the party reached the moon. His death was connected to a feather the party found -- a feather "with the marking of the stars" that fell from a defeated monster's body along with electrum, platinum, a pliable hat, and a glass vial with copper caps.

## The Deal with Reynardine

Archimedes made a deal with [[Reynardine]]: neither would enter Cloud City while the other lived. This pact was major lore -- it meant Archimedes protected Cloud City from Reynardine's direct interference, while Archimedes himself could not enter the city proper, operating instead from the moon.

Archimedes was responsible for Reynardine becoming the Knight of the Dusken Wood.

## In-Session Events

### Chapter 38X -- Voice from the Window

When [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] and Stalwart visited [[Euclid]]'s Study, Archimedes was heard as a "huge voice" from behind a window blinking yellow-white. He told Euclid to calm down, and relayed that [[Inchu]] (Mongoose Zodiac) had asked for help reclaiming the Sunless Citadel from snakes.

### The Search for Archimedes

During the Cloud City arc, the party determined "We need to search for Archimedes -- he might be at The Roost." [[Brigit]] concentrated on finding Archimedes using the Zodiac Locket, but had to fight to keep from transforming into the stag again.

### Manifestation Through Brigit

When the party found a feather with stellar markings, Brigit's Zodiac Locket activated. A voice called out: "Locket holder, please let me out of here." Brigit was forcibly transformed into Archimedes and moved up the stairs automatically toward a light of constellations and stars. She saw the world as starlight. In this form, she reached down with talons, phased in and out onto [[Euclid]], while the fiends attacking the study had no idea what was happening.

[[Euclid]] was "quite confused that Archimedes is now calling himself Brigit."

Later, Euclid examined the Brigit/Archimedes locket and noted the book might have more information about [[Reynaldine]] and Brigit's locket.

### Death and the Moon (Chapter 58T)

On the moon, the party found Archimedes dead -- "due to the feather that we found." [[Euclid]] lectured [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] as they headed to a dais inside the library.

Brigit faced a choice: give up Archimedes' remains, or stay at the dais for 24 hours to get the Ragnarok Protocol back in place.

### Archimedes' Will Persists

Even after death, Archimedes' will continued to work through Brigit's locket. The locket was pulled toward a sigil on a pan flute, operating on its own. After the process completed, Brigit fell into Euclid's study (Alexandria), where Euclid was talking with [[Xyn Khamestria|Xyn]]. "Archimedes" headed over to return the book.

## The Zodiac Locket

The Zodiac Locket served as Archimedes' vessel and connection to the material plane. Through it, Archimedes could:
- Communicate with the locket holder (Brigit)
- Forcibly transform Brigit into his owl form
- Phase between planes to reach [[Euclid]]
- Continue operating even after his physical death

The locket is connected to other Zodiac artifacts -- when it absorbed the stellar feather, [[Varys]]'s Doe's Tear began to glow, and a portal sensation emerged. The locket, the Doe's Tear, a pseudodragon skull, and a pseudodragon egg all seemed able to open portals together.

## Connections

- **[[Euclid]]** -- Fellow Owl Zodiac aspect. Shared the study on the moon. Archimedes spoke from behind a window in Euclid's study.
- **[[Reynardine]]** -- Mutual pact: neither enters Cloud City while the other lives. Archimedes made Reynardine the Knight of the Dusken Wood.
- **[[Brigit]]** -- Zodiac Locket holder. Archimedes manifested through her, transforming her into his owl form.
- **[[Inchu]]** (Mongoose Zodiac) -- Relayed a request for help through Archimedes to Euclid.
- **[[Columbia the Closer]]** -- The fiend assault on Euclid's Study threatened both Euclid and Archimedes' seat of power.
- **[[Alexandria]]** -- Another Owl Zodiac aspect. Euclid's study itself may be named "Alexandria."
- **[[Kolyma's Twelve]]** -- The twelve Zodiac spirits. Archimedes is the Owl alongside Euclid, Helm, and Alexandria.
