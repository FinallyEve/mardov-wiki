## Overview

Kolyma's Twelve are zodiac spirit animals of cosmic significance. Each is bonded to one or more PCs, and their capture or corruption by [[Vecna]]'s agents is a central conflict of the campaign. The zodiac groupings determine which characters share cosmic fate.

## Zenith Groupings

The zodiac groupings reveal which characters' fates are cosmically intertwined:

| Zenith | Anchor | PCs | Zodiacs | Location |
|--------|--------|-----|---------|----------|
| **Forest** ([[Macchu]]) | Death | [[Brigit Wildfire]], [[Varis Laindon]], [[Sprocket Overspark]] | Elk/Stag, Doe, Rabbit | The naturalists |
| **Mountain** ([[Vagna Har]]) | War | [[Daina Fireforge]], [[Nocturnus Wayne]], [[Bergs]] | Wolf, Bat/Wolverine, Bat | The warriors |
| **Winter** ([[bynar]]) | Pestilence | [[Randal]], [[Stalwart Little Goldentooth]], [[Dallor]] | Serpent, Turtle, Mongoose | The seekers |
| **Harvest** ([[Tygras Logar]]) | Famine | [[Nyx Khamestria]], [[Irethia]], [[Tesca]] | Owl, Fox, Mouse | The outsiders |

These groupings foreshadow the endgame: the party will split into four quests across four continents.
