## Overview

The Vecna Sworn are the campaign's primary antagonist faction -- agents of [[Vecna]], the God of Secrets, working to assault the four Zeniths and bring about Vecna's ascension. They are organized by Zenith target.

## Key Lore

- The **Elder Brain** has been corrupted by Vecna's influence through Peraxis. Only Ulitharid elite escaped.
- Oblivion uses **vampire alpha power** to depose her brother and unite the Drow. Each turned has innate loyalty to her.
- Peraxis has **all of Tiny Rick's memories** (a mysterious detail connecting to early campaign events).
