## Overview

The gods of Mardov are active participants in the campaign's cosmic conflict. [[Vecna]] seeks to ascend through the four Zeniths, while [[Bahamut]], [[Sabine Ra]], the [[Raven Queen]], and others work through mortal agents to prevent it.

## Major Deities

| Name | Role | Significance |
|------|------|-------------|
| **[[Bahamut]]** | Creator of the Celestials | Patron of [[Stalwart Little Goldentooth]] and [[Tiberius D Rex]]. His prophecy drives the Bronze Curators. |
| **[[Sabine Ra]]** | Goddess of Fate | Chose [[Sprocket Overspark]] as vessel. Her cloak and the Raven Queen's power merged through him. Freed from the Demi-Lich phylactery in Ch. 2. |
| **[[Vecna]]** | God of Secrets, primary antagonist | Operates through agents ([[Mordred]], [[Peraxis Geth]], [[Reynaldine]]) to assault the four Zeniths. Ascended form depicted. |
| **[[The Raven Queen]]** | Goddess of Death | Her crest seals the ritual chamber door. Her cloak passed to [[Sprocket Overspark]]. |
| **[[Tiamat]]** | Dragon Queen | Five-headed dragon goddess. [[Bergs]] tried to feed spores to her heads to redeem them. Silver Head depicted separately. |
| **[[Graz'zt]]** | Demon Prince | Disguised as "Chief Baxtal Grusher." Connected to Nyx's patron chain. |
| **He Who Walks Below** | Unknown horror | Depicted on "His Throne of Stone." |
| **[[poseidon]]** | Sea god | Referenced in campaign. |

## Gabriel's Arc

Gabriel appears in three forms across the NPC art:
1. **Gabriel the Kind** -- the original, benevolent celestial
2. **Angry Gabriel** -- corrupted or enraged
3. **Fallen Gabriel** -- fully corrupted

This progression connects to the campaign's themes of corruption and redemption.

## Key Antagonist Deities

- **[[Mordred]]** / V-Mordred -- Vecna's primary mortal agent. Disguised as [[Finkle]] in the pilot. Multiple depictions including "Mordred Completed" and "New Mordred."
- **[[lolth]]** -- Drow god. Tasked [[Nocturnus Wayne]] with killing other gods.
- **[[auril]]** (Goddess of Winter) -- Connected to the Winter Zenith.
