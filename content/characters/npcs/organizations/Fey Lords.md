## Overview

The Fey Lords govern the four seasonal courts of the [[feywilde]]. They are ancient, manipulative, and deeply entangled in the campaign's central conflict through their connections to [[Vecna]]'s agents, the [[Diadem of Dawn]], and the mortal world.

> **CRITICAL DISTINCTION**: [[Reynaldine]] (Fall Feylord, Vecna's servant) and [[Reynardine]] (Nyx's patron, the Madness Bringer) are distinct entities.

## The Reynardine Problem

[[Reynardine]], the Madness Bringer, is [[Nyx Khamestria]]'s patron -- the archfey who made a deal with her parents for a twin-soul vessel. His plan:
1. Obtain the [[Diadem of Dawn]]
2. Use [[Xyn]] to open a portal to the fae
3. Merge the fae and material planes, allowing [[V-Mordred]] entrance

Nyx's Summary 46 reveals she has chosen to trust Reynardine, believing in "the good person he once was."
