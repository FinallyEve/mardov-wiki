# Underdark & War Zenith

Oblivion Wayne is Nocturnus's Aunt and Leader of the Drow
She has recently taken a new husband Ebon Wyane, a vampire drow.

Oblivion was obsessed with the imagery of an ancient army created by the old dwarven king who united the clans.
That army is that of the Warforged, she is convinced that the warforged are the key to dominating the illithid, her true competition for dominion over the underdark.
In her research to own all of the underdark, she is put into contact with Ebon, who is a vampire and the current captor of the bat zodiac.
He uses the zodiac to bestoy upon Oblivion the gift of the first bite, a way of creating a new alpha vampire. 

Oblivion uses this power to depose her useless brother and his weak spawn in order to unite the drow under her rule. 
She begins to turn her inner circle and realizes that each turned has an innate loyalty to her as their alpha, not unlike the illithid and the elder brain. 
Ebon continues to research the bat zodiac and is scheming to try and be the bat's chosen. 

Mordred breaks out of his prison and expands his influence, alerting Vecna that this petal of existance is up for the taking. 
Vecna uses his agency once he collects the first of many of The Raven Queens Shards to raise a Illithilich, answering the call of Ebon Wayne who asked his god for a method to control the elder brain. 
PERAXIS GETH is a lich risen with Vecna's power which he gifted the shard of the Raven Queen to, this creature has all of TINY RICK's memories.

The illithid was chosen because he was a vampire already and was being experimented on by Oblivion and Ebon.
An outcast of his kind, this illithid was a vampire who was purposely inflicted with the mind flayer tadpole in order to find out which had control, the alpha vampire or the elder brain.
In their study, it seemed that the vamipre illithid was obedient, however that could have been the elder brain playing along, they never got to find out which.

Now the Elder Brain has been corrupted. When the influence of Vecna poured through PERAXIS GETH, the shock to the link to the elder brain proved too much. 
Twisted and risen, only the ulitharid elite managed to cut themselves off from the familiar drone of whispers in time to escape PERAXIS/VECNA's control.
