# Winter Court

Kobolds and Goblins and Blue and White Dragonborn

Goblins are denizens of the winterlands. There is a portal to the feywilde in the sunless citadel and it leads to the Frozen Warren. They are sacrificed to create more Yuan-ti as souls are required to fuel their transformation.

Kobolds are byproducts of psuedodragons and the dragonpact.
