# Grovewardens of Avandra

God of Halflings, Luck and Adventure

This Goddess is considered one of the 3 Gods of Fate (along with Ioun and Raven Queen)

In Mardov, the grovewarden of Avandra are halflings who take pride in planting whichever seeds they happen to find. The seeds are thrown in a large mixer and planted in paddies. The paddies resemble tiered rice patties but there is no rhyme or reason as to which things are planted there.

If a grovewarden seeks answers then he/she is set to wander the paddies, whatever they feel drawn to is Avandra's Bounty, the destiny chosen by their god for them.
