# Scarlet Cloaks




Previous leader Meyer Fannie went missing when researching a tale of forbidden love between the Lady of the Night, Oblivion Wayne and King "Ghost Eye" Thorin. The working title of the book, "Where the sun doesn't shine" is now a rare collectible, unfinished and under published.

Cirin Lovelace, previously known as The Stringed Prince, is the new Scarlet Cloaked Bardic Leader after revealing himself to be a Tiefling in the most Scarlet Cloaked manner possible, Mardov's first sex-tape/movie. In which, the masked stringed prince was playing violin in a brothel where woman after man after woman would throw themselves at him while he played. However, only after a deaf pink haired human patron then placed her hand on his violin while he played did anyone manage to crack his facade. Only once the two had fallen in love, in the final moments of the film, did the two "reveal" themselves to each other to be tieflings. The programmed illusion is on display at the brothel owned by Cirin called: **The Twined Tails**.


Hey, I'm Cirin Lovelace, nice to meet you.

I am shocked, \*chuckles\* and more than a bit proud to introduce myself as the newly elected leader of the Scarlet Cloaks.

It's an odd feeling, being upstaged. Never once in my career has anyone upstaged me, and yet over the course of one tournament, I am bested by not one, not two but four people?! I mean, bravo to you, Head Musician, and to your friends. I have never been so ... *inspired.*

To think that my latest performance could go from being the first tiefling to be elected in the Magister's Circle in its history, to the third. To go from tied for the highest position imaginable for a bard in our world to achieve, to the *third*?!

But, perhaps the most unimaginable thing though, is how happy it would make me.

So, my apologies for missing your initial coronation. I did however manage to catch your performance this morning at the Circus and I must say, I was impressed.

My expertise is in a different form of artform, pushing the boundaries of society.

There is a special art to firsts. And, *you,* and Anemone. And... Mallory, and even Olivia. The four of you have done something truely, inspiring.

My latest performance was to be the crescendo by which my story would ascend through history. The ultimate tale of love and sacrifice. To achieve the highest station for a tiefling to have held in the magic community and be the martyr for change that would spark the fires of rebellion in my fellow Sons and Daughters of Tygras. To be "ousted" by love and sacrificed in its name. Perfection.

Because I believe that together, you and I can shape the course of history for generations to come. The two of us can make real change.
