# Vigó, Pale Horse, Specter of Eternal Rest

**AC** 16 | **HP** 475
**Stats**: STR 18 | DEX 26 | CON 28 | INT 20 | WIS 22 | CHA 20
**Senses**: Truesight 120ft
**Skills**: Stealth +16, Insight +13, Medicine +18, Deception +13, Intimidation +18
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious

## Special Traits

Incorporeal Movement.&nbsp;The grim reaper can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
Horrifying Visage.&nbsp;Each non-undead creature of the creature's choice within range must make a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute.
Regeneration.&nbsp;The grim reaper regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. If the&nbsp;grim reaper takes&nbsp;radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the grim reaper's next turn.
Everlasting.&nbsp;Upon "death" the grim reaper returns to the astral plane.
Innate Spellcasting.&nbsp;The grim reaper's innate spellcasting ability is&nbsp;Intelligence&nbsp;(spell save DC 21). The&nbsp;grim reaper can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:
At will:&nbsp;detect evil and good, greater invisibility, revivify
3/day each: blight,&nbsp;dispel evil and good,&nbsp;soul cage
1/day each: true resurrection, finger of death&nbsp;
Magic Resistance.&nbsp;The&nbsp;grim reaper has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day).&nbsp;If the&nbsp;grim reaper fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Turn Immunity.&nbsp;The Grim Reaper is immune to all effects that turn undead.
Soul Reaper.&nbsp;&nbsp;A creature killed by&nbsp;the grim reaper&nbsp;is immediately sent to the wall of the faithless, regardless of their faith.

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;The&nbsp;grim reaper can use its Horrifying Visage. It then makes three attacks: one with its scythe, one with the unholy blade, and one with life drain.
Great Scythe.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;22 (2d12 + 8) slashing damage plus 27 (6d8)&nbsp;necrotic damage.&nbsp;If the target is a creature that has 100 hit points or fewer, it must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or die.
Ludvig's Unholy Blade.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (4d6 + 4) slashing damage plus (2d8) necrotic damage.&nbsp;If the target is a creature that has 50 hit points or fewer, it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or die.
Life Drain.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+16 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;35 (6d8 +8 ) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until magic such as the&nbsp;greater restoration spell removes this effect. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.
Create Specter.&nbsp;The&nbsp;grim reaper targets a&nbsp;creature within 120 feet of it that has been dead for no longer than 1 minute. The target's spirit rises as a specter in the space of its corpse or in the nearest unoccupied space. The specter is under the grim reaper's control. The grim reaper can have no more than&nbsp;fourteen specters under its control at one time.

## Legendary Actions

The grim reaper can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The solar regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Teleport.&nbsp;The&nbsp;grim reaper magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
Impending Doom (Costs two actions).&nbsp;The grim reaper exudes a sense of impending doom. Each creature of its choice in a 60-foot radius of the grim reaper must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be paralyzed for one round.
Soul Bag (Costs three actions). The grim reaper lunges forward up to 10 feet towards a living entity and attempts to capture their soul in his bag. The creature must succeed a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or be instantly killed.
