# Famine Spirit

**AC** 20 | **HP** 65
**Stats**: STR 10 | DEX 22 | CON 16 | INT 16 | WIS 16 | CHA 10
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +13, Perception +10
**Condition Immunities**: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious

## Special Traits

Legendary Resistance (3/day).&nbsp;If the famine spirit fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. Input the number of uses per day.
Avoidance.&nbsp;If the famine spirit is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a saving throw to only take half damage, it instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it.
Invulnerability. The famine spirit, if reduced to 0 hit points and destroyed, reforms in 70 days. The only way to permanently destroy this creature completely is destroy a body it is possessing, and destroy the heart of its victim with a silver weapon, scattering the pieces of the heart and burying them in holy ground.

## Actions

Draining Touch. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach&nbsp;15 ft.,&nbsp;one target. Hit:&nbsp;14&nbsp;(4d6)&nbsp;psychic damage.&nbsp;
Famine's Affliction.&nbsp;The famine spirit chooses one creature that has been hit by its draining touch since the last sunset. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw, (DC 15) or lose 1d4 points from their Wisdom score. A creature whose Wisdom score reaches 0 dies, if that creature is a humanoid, the famine spirit can possess the creature;&nbsp;the famine spirit then disappears, and the target is arises under the control of the famine spirit. The famine spirt can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except ones that turn undead, and it retains its immunity to being charmed&nbsp;and frightened.
The possession lasts until the body drops to 0 hit points, or the famine spirit ends it as a bonus action. When the possession ends, the famine spirit reappears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the body. If the target survives 7 days, but remains possessed it will transform into a Famine Taken, and the possession will end.
A creature that has lost Wisdom to famine's affliction feels intense, insatiable hunger. The creature suffers from the effects of starvation if they do not eat within a numbers of hours equal to 3 + their Constitution modifier. Humanoids that die of starvation arise by Famine Taken within 1d10 days. A humanoid that eats the flesh of another humanoid while afflicted by this condition dies within 1d4 hours and arises as a Famine Taken within 1d6 days.&nbsp;This condition can be removed only by Remove Curse cast at 5th or higher level, Wish, or Divine Intervention. Wisdom lost to the affliction can be restored by a Greater Restoration, restoring 1d4 points of Wisdom per casting only after the affliction has been removed.
