# Fenrir

**AC** 15 | **HP** 105
**Stats**: STR 18 | DEX 14 | CON 14 | INT 10 | WIS 14 | CHA 16
**Senses**: Darkvision 60
**Skills**: Nature +8, Survival +8


## Special Traits

Alpha's Presence. While within 60 feet of Fenrir, friendly creatures have advantage on saving throws against being frightened and gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with melee attacks.
Cold Resilience. Fenrir has resistance to cold damage.
Pack Tactics. Fenrir has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of Fenrir's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
Zodiac's Fury. Fenrir's bite deals an extra 7 (2d6) cold damage.
1. Ice Knife. 1st-level conjurationFenrir hurls a shard of ice at a creature it can see within range. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 2d6 piercing damage and 2d6 cold damage. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage.
2. Sleet Storm. 3rd-level conjurationFenrir creates a 40-foot-radius sphere of swirling sleet centered on a point it chooses within range. The area is heavily obscured, and exposed flames in the area are doused. The ground in the area is covered with slick ice, making it difficult terrain. When a creature enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it falls prone.
3. Cone of Cold. 5th-level evocationA blast of cold air erupts from Fenrir's mouth in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d8 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Actions

Multiattack. Fenrir makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) cold damage.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage.
