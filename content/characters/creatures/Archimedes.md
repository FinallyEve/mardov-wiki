# Archimedes

**AC** 14 | **HP** 40
**Stats**: STR 14 | DEX 17 | CON 14 | INT 10 | WIS 16 | CHA 12
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +4, Perception +5


## Special Traits

Flyby. The owl doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.
Keen Hearing and Sight. The owl has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or sight.
Astral Form.&nbsp;The owl has the ability to travel on the Ethereal Plane, however the owl may only occupy places where it could travel on the Material Plane. It is a bonus action to switch forms. If the owl is grappling a Large or smaller and has moved more than half their speed prior to shifting, the owl may bring the grappled creature with them to the Ethereal Plane. The strain causes the grappled to make a DC 15 Str Saving Throw or else take 2d10 force damage. Once shifted the owl must be able to move an additional 10ft or else both the owl and any passenger must make DC 16 Const Saving Throws else take 2d10 + 2 force damage.

## Actions

Talons. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit:&nbsp;12 (2d10 + 1) slashing damage.
