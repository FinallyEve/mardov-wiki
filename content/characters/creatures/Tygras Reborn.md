# Tygras Reborn

**AC** 19 | **HP** 262
**Stats**: STR 22 | DEX 15 | CON 24 | INT 20 | WIS 16 | CHA 22
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.

**Condition Immunities**: Poisoned

## Special Traits

Death Throes. When Tygras dies, it explodes, and each creature within 30 feet of it must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, taking 70 (20d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The explosion ignites flammable objects in that area that aren't being worn or carried, and it destroys Tygras' weapons.
Fire Aura. At the start of each of Tygras' turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) fire damage, and flammable objects in the aura that aren't being worn or carried ignite. A creature that touches Tygras or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) fire damage.
Magic Resistance. Tygras has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Magic Weapons. Tygras' weapon attacks are magical.Legendary Resistance (3/Day).&nbsp;If Tygras fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Innate Spellcasting.&nbsp;The warlock&rsquo;s innate spellcasting ability is Charisma. It can innately cast the following spells (spell save DC 15), requiring no material components:
At will: alter self, false life, levitate (self only), mage armor (self only), silent image
1/day each: feeblemind, finger of death, plane shift
Spellcasting.&nbsp;Tygras is a 20th-level spellcaster. He spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 20, +12 to hit with spell attacks).&nbsp;He regains its expended spell slots when it finishes a short or long rest. It knows the following warlock spells:
Cantrips (at will): eldritch blast, fire bolt, friends, thaumaturgy, minor illusion, prestidigitation, toll the dead
1st&ndash;5th level (6 5th-level slots): banishment, burning hands, counterspell, hellish rebuke, infernal calling, scorching ray, wall of fire, scrying, suggestion, hold person
6th level (1 6th-level slot): soul cage (can be used on non-humanoids with a soul, that he has killed)
7th level (1 7th-level slot): forcecage
8th level (1 8th-level slot): maddening darkness
9th level (1 9th-level slot): true polymorph
Dark One&rsquo;s Own Luck (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).&nbsp;When&nbsp;Tygras&nbsp;makes an ability check or saving throw, he&nbsp;can add a d10 to the roll. It can do this after the roll is made but before any of the roll&rsquo;s effects occur.

## Actions

Multiattack. Tygras&nbsp;makes two attacks: one with his longsword and one with his Eldritch whip.
Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d8 + 8) slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) lightning damage. If Tygras scores a critical hit, it rolls damage dice three times, instead of twice.
Eldritch Whip. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 80ft., up to 4 beams. Hit: 13 (1d10 + 8) force damage plus 10 (3d6) fire damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 20 feet toward Tygras. Targets within 20 ft of Tygras who fail this save are restrained and must make a Athletics, Acrobatics or Arcana DC 20 to break free, may be attempted as a bonus action with DC 25.&nbsp;&nbsp;
Teleport. Tygras magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

## Legendary Actions

Tygras can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Tygras regains spend actions at the start of his turn.
Cantrip.&nbsp;Tygras casts a cantrip.
Flare Whip.&nbsp;Tygras burns all held creatures in his Eldritch Whip. Each creature currently restrained by the whip must make a DC 20 Constitution save or take (3d6) fire damage and are reeled 5ft closer to Tygras.&nbsp;
Teleport.&nbsp;Tygras uses his teleport ability.
Invoke Pact Magic. Tygras uses one of his pact magic spell slots to cast a 5th level spell.&nbsp;
