# Shi Shi Gami

**AC** 16 | **HP** 44
**Stats**: STR 20 | DEX 17 | CON 15 | INT 10 | WIS 16 | CHA 12

**Skills**: Perception +4


## Special Traits

Charge. If the elk moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 10 (2d6) damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
Spirit of the Glade.&nbsp;The elk may use an action to produce a summer glade expelling light using the daylight, the elk may use a bonus action while standing in the light of the spell to&nbsp;expend&nbsp;a hit dice to get 1d12 +3 health to all friendly creatures within the light.&nbsp;

## Actions

Ram. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d12 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
Hooves. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone creature. Hit: 22 (4d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
