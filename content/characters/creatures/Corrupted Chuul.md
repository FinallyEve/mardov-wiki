# Corrupted Chuul

**AC** 16 | **HP** 136
**Stats**: STR 18 | DEX 12 | CON 16 | INT 5 | WIS 11 | CHA 5
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Condition Immunities**: Poisoned

## Special Traits

Amphibious. The chuul can breathe air and water.
Sense Magic. The chuul senses magic within 120 feet of it at will. This trait otherwise works like the detect magic spell but isn't itself magical.
Mucous Cloud.&nbsp;While underwater, the&nbsp;Corrupted Chuul&nbsp;is surrounded by transformative mucus. A creature that touches the Chuul or that hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is diseased for 1d4 hours. The diseased creature can breathe only underwater.

## Actions

Multiattack. The chuul makes two pincer attacks. If the chuul is grappling a creature, the chuul can also use its tentacles once. If the chuul manages to poison a creature with its tentacle, the chuul can drain its victim.
Pincer. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (4d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 16) if it is a Large or smaller creature and the chuul doesn't have two other creatures grappled.
Tentacles. One creature grappled by the chuul must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. Until this poison ends, the target is paralyzed. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
Drain.&nbsp;One creature&nbsp;paralyzed&nbsp;by the Corrupted Chuul takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage, and the&nbsp;Corrupted&nbsp;regains hit points equal to the damage the creature takes.
