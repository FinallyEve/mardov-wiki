# The Iron Revenant

**AC** 22 | **HP** 400
**Stats**: STR 24 | DEX 14 | CON 20 | INT 18 | WIS 16 | CHA 22
**Senses**: Darkvision
**Skills**: Athletics +13, History +10, Perception +9, Deception +12, Intimidation +12, Persuasion +12
**Condition Immunities**: Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned

## Special Traits

Rise of Darkness. If The Iron Revenant is able to deal damage to 3 or more enemies&nbsp;on his own turn of combat (not counting reactions or legendary actions), he becomes surrounded by a&nbsp;maelstrom of necrotic energy and metal shrapnel. This aura extends 10 feet in all directions, lasting until the end of the&nbsp;his next turn and deals 1d8 necrotic damage and 1d8 piercing damage to all enemies immediately, and again to any enemy that starts their turn in that space, or moves through the space at any point during their turn.&nbsp;

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;The Iron Revenant makes two attacks with his Mace of Terror, the damage of which is considered magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance.&nbsp;
Mace of Terror. Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1 target. Hit:&nbsp;19 (2d8 + 9) bludgeoning&nbsp;damage.&nbsp;The Iron Revenant may forego these attacks in favor of using one of three charges on the Mace of Terror, as shown in the magic item list.Obliterate. The Iron Revenant swings his mace in a single mighty blow to the ground, sending necrotic power through a 20 foot cone, centered on himself. All targets must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 20) or suffer 14 (4d12) necrotic damage, with half as much on a success.&nbsp;
Death's Grasp.&nbsp;The Iron Revenant casts out a spectral hand in a line 5 feet wide and 30 feet long. All enemies in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 22) or be immediately pulled to an available space within 5 feet of him, and suffer 18 (3d12) force damage.&nbsp;
Realm of Death (Recharge 6). The Iron Revenant calls out to the spirit of a single target, forcing them to make a Charisma saving throw, or be cast into the Shadowfell with him, leaving behind a small glowing orb where they had previously stood. This ability can be cancelled with a Counterspell (Equal to a 6th level casting).
Both the target and The Iron Revenant appear instantly in a 25 foot radius, circular, shadowy arena for a duel - lasting up to 3 total turns (ending early if the orb can be dispelled, or if either target dies). During this duel, The Iron Revenant gains a +1 to all stat modifiers and immediately gains the benefit of his Indestructible reaction.

## Legendary Actions

The Iron Revenant&nbsp;can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. He regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.Attack (Costs 1 action). The Iron Revenant makes an attack with his Mace of Terror.&nbsp;
Obliterate (Costs 2 actions). The Iron Revenant uses his Obliterate ability.&nbsp;Call to Arms (Costs 3 actions). The Iron Revenant raises 1d4 Shadow Assassins from his fallen armies to the field at any point he chooses within 30 feet of himself, allowing them to immediately roll into the current initiative order.
