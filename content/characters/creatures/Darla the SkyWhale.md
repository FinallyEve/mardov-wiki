# Darla the SkyWhale

**AC** 14 | **HP** 111
**Stats**: STR 19 | DEX 10 | CON 15 | INT 3 | WIS 14 | CHA 7
**Senses**: Blindsight 120 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3
**Condition Immunities**: Grappled

## Special Traits

Echolocation. The whale can't use its blindsight while deafened.
Hold Breath. The whale can hold its breath for 30 minutes.
Keen Hearing. The whale has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

## Actions

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (5d6 + 4) piercing damage.
Slam.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack +10 to hit, reach 5ft, one target.&nbsp;Hit: 73&nbsp;(14d8 + 10) bludegoning damage. If the creature is Large or smaller, all damage is dealt to the creature. If the creature is of equal size to Darla, then the damage is split between them (Darla is resistant to bludgeoning damage). This ability requires Darla move at least half her speed directly at the target, all creatures aboard Darla must make a DC 16 Animal Handling Check or a DC 20 Dexterity Save or else be thrown 20 ft off the edge of the Thornberry Wilde.
