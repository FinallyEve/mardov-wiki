# Balto

**AC** 15 | **HP** 48
**Stats**: STR 17 | DEX 15 | CON 15 | INT 3 | WIS 12 | CHA 7

**Skills**: Stealth +4, Perception +3


## Special Traits

Keen Hearing and Smell.&nbsp;Balto has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
Pack Tactics.&nbsp;Balto has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the Balto's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated. If Balto's ally is also the target of the calming bond, Balto gets a second attack.&nbsp;
Calming Bond.&nbsp;Once per short rest, Balto may spend a bonus action to bond with an Ally. While within 5ft of the bonded ally, the ally is under the effects of the Calm Emotions.&nbsp;

## Actions

Multiattack. Balto attacks once per turn with a Bite attack. Then if Balto is within 5ft of its bonded ally, then Balto may also take another Bite attack or give up&nbsp;the attack&nbsp;to cause the bonded ally to spend their reaction on an attack against the same target as Balto's first Bite.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
