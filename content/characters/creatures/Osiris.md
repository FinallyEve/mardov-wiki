# Osiris

**AC** 19 | **HP** 66
**Stats**: STR 16 | DEX 12 | CON 20 | INT 12 | WIS 12 | CHA 12




## Special Traits

Defender's Scales.&nbsp;The Pangolin Zodiac is covered almost completely in large, sharp, overlapping plates. These provide a formidable natural armor and a dangerous, slashing tail attack. Whenever an attacker hits the pangolin, they must make a DC 14 Dex saving throw or take 1d10 + X slashing damage, where the X the pool of healing scales still on Osiris.

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;Osiris can make either 2 claw attacks, a claw and tail attack, or a tongue and claw attack.
Claw&nbsp;Attack.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+7 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1 target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;9 (1d8 + 4)  damage. Damage tripled against structures.&nbsp;
Tail&nbsp;Attack.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+7 to hit, reach 10 ft., 1 target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;11 (2d6 + 4)  damage.&nbsp;
Tongue Attack.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack&nbsp;+3 to hit, reach 10 ft, 1 target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;target grappled and knocked prone.
