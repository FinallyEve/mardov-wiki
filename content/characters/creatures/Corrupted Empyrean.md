# Corrupted Empyrean

**AC** 22 | **HP** 313
**Stats**: STR 30 | DEX 21 | CON 30 | INT 21 | WIS 22 | CHA 27
**Senses**: Truesight 120
**Skills**: Insight +13, Persuasion +15


## Special Traits

Innate Spellcasting. The empyrean's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
At will: greater restoration, pass without trace, water breathing, water walk
1/day each: commune, dispel evil and good, earthquake, fire storm, plane shift (self only)
Legendary Resistance (3/Day).&nbsp;If the empyrean fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Magic Resistance.&nbsp;The empyrean has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.&nbsp;
Magic Weapons.&nbsp;The empyrean's weapon attacks are magical.

## Actions

Maul.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 31 (6d6+10) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Costitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of the empyrean's next turn.
Bolt.&nbsp;Ranged Spell Attack: +15 to hit, range 600ft., one target. Hit: 24 (7d6) damage of one of the following types (empyrean's choice): acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, radiant, or thunder.

## Legendary Actions

The empyrean can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The empyrean regains spent legendary actions at he start of its turn.
Attack. The empyrean makes one attack.&nbsp;
Taunting counterattack (Cost 2 Actions).&nbsp;The empyrean forces any hostile creatures within 40 ft. to make an attack against him (if possible). Combos:&nbsp; 1.&nbsp;If all creatures hit nothing happens. 2. If no creatures hit the empyrean and more than 2 creatures have missed he heals 1d100 hit points. 3.&nbsp;If some miss and some hit the empyrean redirects as many of the succesful attacks as he can, to any available creatures that missed. The rest hit him.
Trembling Strike (Cost 2 Actions).&nbsp;The empyrean strikes the ground with its maul, triggering an earth tremor. All other creatures on the ground within 60 feet of the empyrean must succeed on a DC 25 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
