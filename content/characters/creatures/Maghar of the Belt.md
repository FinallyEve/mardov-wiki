# Maghar of the Belt

**AC** 16 | **HP** 30
**Stats**: STR 16 | DEX 12 | CON 16 | INT 10 | WIS 14 | CHA 11

**Skills**: Athletics +5, Perception +4, Survival +4


## Special Traits

Warforged Resilience. The warforged has advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and is immune to disease. Magic can&rsquo;t put it to sleep.

## Actions

Channel Divinity - Bless Artifact: Belt.&nbsp;Reginald Providence blesses the Belt of a target within a 20ft of his statue. The Blessed Belt grants the wearer&nbsp;the ability to hold two sets of concentration&nbsp;while within range. By passing ownership of a spell currently being concentrated on while touching the statue. Only one Belt may be blessed at a time. If the target is also under the effects of the other blessings of Muradin, these effects last until the end of the next long rest. The full blessing may only be granted once per week.
