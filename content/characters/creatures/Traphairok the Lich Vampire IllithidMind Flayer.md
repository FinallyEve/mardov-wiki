# Traphairok the Lich Vampire Illithid/Mind Flayer

**AC** 23 | **HP** 356
**Stats**: STR 28 | DEX 28 | CON 28 | INT 30 | WIS 30 | CHA 28
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft, Truesight 120 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +18, Arcana +24, History +24, Insight +18, Perception +18, Deception +18, Persuasion +24
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned

## Special Traits

Legendary Resistance (5/Day). If Traphairok fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.
Rejuvenation. If they have a phylactery,&nbsp; Traphairok gains a new body after dying in 1d10 days, regaining all they're hit points and becoming active again. The new body appears within 5 feet of the phylactery.
Innate Spellcasting. Traphairok is an innate spellcaster. They're spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 22, +18 to hit with spell attacks). Traphairok has the following spells prepared:
At will: Mage Hand, Prestidigitation,&nbsp; Ray Of Frost, Unseen Servant, Magic Missiles (9th), Teleport, Levitation
10/Day Each: Detect Magic, Shield, Thunderwave, Detect Thoughts, Fireball,
5/Day Each: Acid Arrow, Detect Thoughts, Invisibility, Mirror Image, Animate Dead, Dispel Magic
3/Day Each: Blight, Dimension Door, Cloudkill, Scrying, Disintegrate, Globe Of Invulnerability, Finger Of Death, Plane Shift, Dominate Monster, Power Word Stun
1/Day Each: Power Word Kill, Wish
&nbsp;
Shapechanger.&nbsp;Traphairok&nbsp;can use their action to polymorph into a Tiny bat or a Medium cloud of mist, or back into they're true form.
While in bat form, Traphairok can't speak, they're walking speed is 5 feet, and they have a flying speed of 30 feet. Their statistics, other than its size and speed, are unchanged. Anything they're holding or wearing transforms with it. They revert to their true form they die.
While in mist form, they can't take any actions, speak, or manipulate objects. They are weightless, has a flying speed of 20 feet, can hover, and can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. In addition, if air can pass through a space, the mist can do so without squeezing.&nbsp;They have&nbsp;advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws, and are immune to all nonmagical damage, except the damage they take from sunlight.
Turn Resistance. Traphairok has advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.
Regeneration.&nbsp;Traphairok regains 20 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point. If Traphairok takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of Traphairok's next turn.
Spider Climb.&nbsp;Traphairok can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
Magic Resistance.&nbsp;Traphairok has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
&nbsp;Turn Resistance.&nbsp;Traphairok has advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;Traphairok makes or casts a total of five attacks/spells, only one of which can be a bite attack and only one of which can be a vorpal barrage.
Paralyzing Unarmed Strike (Humanoid Form Only). Melee Weapon Attack Attack: +18 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit:&nbsp;13(2d8+4) bludgeoning damage plus an additional 10 (3d6) cold damage. The target must succeed on a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.&nbsp;Instead of dealing bludgeoning damage, Traphairok can grapple the target (escape DC 24).
Vorpal Barrage. Ranged Weapon Attack. +12 to hit, reach 180 ft., up to 5 creatures. Sends 5 Vorpal Longswords at up to 5 creatures. Each one deals 4 (1d8) slashing damage plus an additional 6 (1d12) necrotic damage plus an additional 4 (1d8) cold damage.&nbsp;When they attack a creature that has at least one head with this weapon and roll a 20 on the attack roll, the&nbsp; creature takes an extra 6d8 slashing damage from the hit.
Bite. (Bat or&nbsp;Humanoid&nbsp;Form Only).&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one&nbsp;creature.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains hit points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0. A humanoid slain in this way and then buried in the ground rises the following night as a vampire spawn under Traphairok's control.
Charm.&nbsp;Traphairok&nbsp;targets one humanoid it can see within 30 feet of it. If the target can see the vampire, the target must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw against this magic or be charmed&nbsp;by the vampire. The charmed target regards the vampire as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected. Although the target isn't under they're control, it takes the vampire's requests or actions in the most favorable way it can, and it is a willing target for the vampire's bite attack. Each time Traphairok or Traphairok's companions do anything harmful to the target, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. Otherwise, the effect lasts 24 hours or until&nbsp;Traphairok is destroyed, is on a different plane of existence than the target, or takes a bonus action to end the effect.
Tentacles.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+12&nbsp;to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;21 (3d10 + 5) psychic damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it is&nbsp;grappled&nbsp;(escape DC&nbsp;15) and must succeed on a DC&nbsp;20&nbsp;Intelligence saving throw or be&nbsp;stunned&nbsp;until this grapple ends.
Extract Brain.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+12&nbsp;to hit, reach 5 ft., one&nbsp;incapacitated&nbsp;humanoid&nbsp;grappled&nbsp;by the lich.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;55 (10d10) piercing damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the lich kills the target by extracting and devouring its brain.
Mind Blast (Recharge&nbsp;5&ndash;6).&nbsp; The illithilich magically emits psychic energy in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC&nbsp;18&nbsp;Intelligence saving throw or take 27 (5d8 + 5) psychic damage and be&nbsp;stunned&nbsp;for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

## Legendary Actions

The lich can take 5 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The lich regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Spell. Traphairok casts a spell like normal.
Frightening Gaze (Costs 2 Actions). Traphairok fixes its gaze on one creature it can see within 10 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw against this magic or become frightened for 1 minute. The frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to Traphairok's gaze for the next 24 hours.
Disrupt Life (Costs 3 Actions). Each non-undead creature within 20 feet of the lich must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw against this magic, taking 21 (6d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
Move.&nbsp;Traphairok moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
Unarmed Strike.&nbsp;Traphairok makes one unarmed strike.
Bite.(Costs 2 Actions). Traphairok makes one bite attack
Tentacles.&nbsp;The illithilich makes one attack with its tentacles.
&nbsp;
Extract Brain (Costs 2 Actions).&nbsp;The illithilich uses Extract Brain.
&nbsp;
Mind Blast (Costs 3 Actions).&nbsp;The illithilich recharges its Mind Blast and uses it.
