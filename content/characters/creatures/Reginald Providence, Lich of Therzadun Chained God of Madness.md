# Reginald Providence, Lich of Therzadun Chained God of Madness

**AC** 17 | **HP** 231
**Stats**: STR 11 | DEX 18 | CON 20 | INT 22 | WIS 14 | CHA 14
**Senses**: Truesight 120ft.
**Skills**: Arcana +14, Perception +7
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned

## Special Traits

Far Realm Parasite. Inside the lich&rsquo;s torso dwells a wormlike parasite that contains the lich&rsquo;s soul. When the lich dies, it implodes into the parasite, which then vanishes into the Far Realm. In 2d4 days, the parasite causes the lich to reappear within 1d4 miles of where it died. If the lich died inside a magic circle cast to contain Undead, the lich instead reappears as an otyugh with all the lich&rsquo;s memories.
Legendary Resistance (4/Day). If the lich fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Unusual Nature. The lich doesn&rsquo;t need air, food, drink, or sleep.

## Actions

Multiattack. The lich makes one Parasitic Tentacle attack or uses Spellcasting. The lich also uses Psychic Whisper twice.
Parasitic Tentacle. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. Hit: 25 (6d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 25 (6d6 + 4) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned. The poisoned target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. The third time the target fails the save, the target dies and dissolves into a gibbering mouther that obeys the lich and uses the target&rsquo;s initiative.
Psychic Whisper. The lich targets one creature it can see within 120 feet of itself. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or take 25 (6d6 + 4) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of the lich&rsquo;s next turn as incomprehensible whispers fill the target&rsquo;s mind.
Spellcasting. The lich casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):
Spell slot: 5 additional 5th level spells.
At will: detect magic, mage hand, prestidigitation, armor of Agathys, mage armor, mass suggestion, shadow of moil
2/day each: dispel magic, hunger of Hadar, lightning bolt
1/day each: arcane eye, dimension door, plane shift (self only), crown of stars (13 total motes), maddening darkness

## Legendary Actions

The lich gains the following legendary action options, which replace all of the lich&rsquo;s legendary actions.
Tentacles.&nbsp;The lich makes one attack with its tentacles.
Whispers of Madness.&nbsp;The lich uses mass suggestion which targets creatures currently in darkness. Creatures have +5 to the spell save if they have already passed a check against suggestion before.
Cast Spell (Costs 1&ndash;3 Actions).&nbsp;The lich uses a spell slot to cast a 1st-, 2nd-, or 3rd-level spell that it has prepared. Doing so costs 1 legendary action per level of the spell.
