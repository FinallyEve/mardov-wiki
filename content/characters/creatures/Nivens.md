# Nivens

**AC** 15 | **HP** 97
**Stats**: STR 10 | DEX 18 | CON 16 | INT 14 | WIS 14 | CHA 16
**Senses**: Darkvision 60
**Skills**: Acrobatics +8, History +6


## Special Traits

Fey Ancestry. Nivens has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put Nivens to sleep.
Evasion. If Nivens is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, Nivens instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.
Quick Footed. Nivens can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.
Favored Terrain: Feywild. Nivens has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while in the Feywild.
Zodiac's Blessing. Nivens can cast haste at will, targeting itself only, without expending a spell slot or requiring concentration.
1. Slow. 3rd-level transmutationNivens targets up to six creatures within a 40-foot cube originating from a point Nivens can see within range. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be affected by the spell's effect for the duration. An affected target's speed is halved, it takes a -2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws, and it can't use reactions. On its turn, it can use either an action or a bonus action, not both. Regardless of the creature's abilities or magic items, it can't make more than one melee or ranged attack during its turn. If the creature attempts to cast a spell with a casting time of 1 action, roll a d20. On an 11 or higher, the spell doesn't take effect until the creature's next turn, and the creature must use its action on that turn to complete the spell. If it can't, the spell is wasted.
2. Blink. 3rd-level transmutationNivens can cast blink at will, targeting itself only, without expending a spell slot or requiring concentration.
3. Time Stop. 9th-level transmutationNivens can cast time stop once per day, without expending a spell slot. When it does so, Nivens stops the flow of time for everyone but itself. No time passes for other creatures, while Nivens is free to act for 1d4 + 1 rounds. During this time, Nivens can use its actions and move as normal. This spell ends if one of the actions Nivens uses during this period, or any effects that it creates during this period, affects a creature other than itself or an object being worn or carried by someone other than itself. In addition, the spell ends if Nivens moves to a place more than 1,000 feet from the location where it cast the spell.

## Actions

Multiattack. Nivens makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage.
