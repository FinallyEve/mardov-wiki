# True Tiamat

**AC** 32 | **HP** 840
**Stats**: STR 30 | DEX 16 | CON 30 | INT 27 | WIS 27 | CHA 30
**Senses**: Blindsight 360 ft, Darkvision 600 ft., Truesight 240 ft.
**Skills**: Arcana +17, Investigation +29, Religion +17, Insight +28, Perception +26
**Condition Immunities**: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Stunned

## Special Traits

Discorporation. When Tiamat drops to 0 hit points or dies, her body is destroyed but her essence travels back to her domain in the Nine Hells, and she is unable to take physical form for a time.
Innate Spellcasting (7/Day). Tiamat can innately cast divine word,&nbsp;Power word kill,&nbsp;Time Stop,&nbsp;Meteor swarm,&nbsp;Forcecage,&nbsp;Gate, Command(spell save DC 30). Her spellcasting ability is Charisma.
Legendary Resistance (7/Day). If Tiamat fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
Limited Magic Immunity. Unless she wishes to be affected, Tiamat is immune to spells of 8th level or lower. She has advantage on saving throws against all other spells and magical effects.
Magic Weapons. Tiamat&rsquo;s weapon attacks are magical.
Multiple Heads. Tiamat can take one reaction per turn, rather than only one per round. She also has advantage on saving throws against being knocked unconscious. If she fails a saving throw against an effect that would stun a creature, one of her unspent legendary actions is spent. She also gains an Extra reaction because if her awareness.
Regeneration. Tiamat regains 100 hit points at the start of her turn.
Chromatic Wrath (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).&nbsp;If Tiamat would be reduced to 0 hit points, her current hit point total instead resets to 650 hit points, she recharges her Chromatic Flames and breath weapons, and she regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance. Additionally, Tiamat can now use the options in the "Mythic Actions" section for 1 hour. Award a party an additional 155,000 XP (310,000 XP total) for defeating Tiamat after her Chromatic Wrath activates.

## Actions

Multiattack. Tiamat can use her Frightful Presence. She then makes Five attacks: two with her claws and one with her tail, and two with her Bite.
Bite.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+28&nbsp;to hit, reach 25 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;31&nbsp;(3d12 + 10)&nbsp;piercing damage plus 23&nbsp;(3d12)&nbsp;force damage.
Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +28 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (4d8 + 10) slashing damage.
Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +28 to hit, reach 25 ft., one target. Hit:&nbsp;36 (6d8 + 10) piercing damage.
Frightful Presence. Each creature of Tiamat&rsquo;s choice that is within 600 feet of Tiamat must succeed on a DC 37 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature&rsquo;s saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to Tiamat&rsquo;s Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

## Legendary Actions

Tiamat can take 7 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature&rsquo;s turn. Tiamat regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.
Tiamat&rsquo;s legendary action options are associated with her five dragon heads (a bite and a breath weapon for each). Once Tiamat chooses a legendary action option for one of her heads, she can&rsquo;t choose another one associated with that head until the start of her next turn.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +28 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 32 (4d10 + 10) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) acid damage (black dragon head), lightning damage (blue dragon head), poison damage (green dragon head), fire damage (red dragon head), or cold damage (white dragon head).
Furious Bite (Costs 2 Actions).&nbsp;Tiamat makes one Bite attack. If the attack hits a creature, the target must succeed on a DC 32 Wisdom saving throw or become&nbsp;frightened&nbsp;of the aspect until the end of its next turn.
Black Dragon Head: Acid Breath (Costs 2 Actions). Tiamat exhales acid in a 300-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 28 Dexterity saving throw, taking 73 (15d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
Blue Dragon Head: Lightning Breath (Costs 2 Actions). Tiamat exhales lightning in a 300-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 33 Dexterity saving throw, taking 93 (16d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
Green Dragon Head: Poison Breath (Costs 2 Actions). Tiamat exhales poisonous gas in a 300-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 34 Constitution saving throw, taking 83 (22d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
Red Dragon Head: Fire Breath (Costs 2 Actions). Tiamat exhales fire in a 300-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 33 Dexterity saving throw, taking 98 (26d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
White Dragon Head: Cold Breath (Costs 2 Actions). Tiamat exhales an icy blast in a 300-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 33 Dexterity saving throw, taking 79 (16d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
