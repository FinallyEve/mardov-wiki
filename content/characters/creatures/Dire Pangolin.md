# Dire Pangolin

**AC** 19 | **HP** 53
**Stats**: STR 16 | DEX 9 | CON 16 | INT 4 | WIS 9 | CHA 14
**Senses**: Blindsight out to 30 ft



## Special Traits

Defender's Scales.&nbsp;The Dire Pangolin is covered almost completely in large, sharp, overlapping plates. These provide a formidable natural armor and a dangerous, slashing tail attack. Whenever an attacker hits the pangolin, they must make a DC 12 saving throw or take 1d6 slashing damage.&nbsp;

## Actions

Claw&nbsp;Attack. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1 target. Hit:&nbsp;9 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage. Damage tripled against structures.&nbsp;
Tail&nbsp;Attack.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+7 to hit, reach 10 ft., 1 target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.&nbsp;
Tongue Attack.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack +3 to hit, reach 10 ft, 1 target.&nbsp;Hit: target grappled and knocked prone.
