# Infected Render

**AC** 21 | **HP** 291
**Stats**: STR 24 | DEX 17 | CON 20 | INT 13 | WIS 8 | CHA 14
**Senses**: Blindsight 60ft, Darkvision 60 ft.
**Skills**: Arcana +5, Perception +7


## Special Traits

Shapechanger.&nbsp;The Render can use its action to polymorph into a&nbsp;Medium&nbsp;humanoid, or back into its true form. Its statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn&rsquo;t transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.
Innate Spellcasting.&nbsp;The Render&rsquo;s innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). The&nbsp;Render can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
At will: detect magic, detect thoughts, invisibility (self only), major image, Compelled Duel, Expeditious Retreat,&nbsp;
2/day each: fear, fly, fireball, tongues, Ashardalon&rsquo;s Stride
1/day: Reverse Gravity (self only)
Magic Resistance.&nbsp;The&nbsp;Render has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Magic Weapons. The Renders weapon attacks are magical.
Regeneration.&nbsp;The Render regains 20 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point.
Possession.&nbsp;The Render has as many reactions as eyes. The Render starts with 6 eyes. Eyes may be destroyed with a targeted strike. Eyes have an Armor Class of 26 and 10 hp. The Armor Class is the byproduct of natural armor and cover (+5) and may be negated with Sharpshooter.
Magic Affinity.&nbsp;The Render Regains spell slots on short rest.

## Actions

Multiattack. The gray render makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d12 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, the target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage, plus 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage if the target is prone.
