# Manitou

**AC** 15 | **HP** 85
**Stats**: STR 12 | DEX 18 | CON 18 | INT 14 | WIS 14 | CHA 10
**Senses**: Darkvision 60
**Skills**: Stealth +7, Perception +7, Deception +9


## Special Traits

Keen Senses. The fox has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or sight.
Ambush Tactics. The fox has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn in the combat yet.
Zodiac Cunning. The fox can add its Intelligence modifier to its Armor Class.
1. Illusory Script. 1st-level illusion (ritual)Manitou can cast Illusory Script as a ritual, creating a hidden message that can be read only by a creature with truesight or a creature affected by the spell see invisibility.
2. Mirror Image. 2nd-level illusionThree illusory duplicates of Manitou appear in its space. Whenever a creature targets Manitou with an attack during the spell's duration, roll a d20 to determine whether the attack instead targets one of the duplicates. If Manitou has three duplicates, you must roll a 6 or higher to change the attack's target to a duplicate. With two duplicates, you must roll an 8 or higher. With one duplicate, you must roll an 11 or higher.
3. Disguise Self. 1st-level illusionManitou can cast Disguise Self at will, altering its appearance to look like a different creature or object of its size or smaller, or back to its true form.

## Actions

Multiattack. Manitou makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage.
