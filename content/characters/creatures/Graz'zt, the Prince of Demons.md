# Graz'zt, the Prince of Demons

**AC** 20 | **HP** 692
**Stats**: STR 22 | DEX 15 | CON 21 | INT 30 | WIS 30 | CHA 26
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.
**Skills**: Arcana +28, History +28, Investigation +28, Religion +28, Insight +19, Medicine +19, Perception +19, Deception +15, Persuasion +15
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Stunned

## Special Traits

Shapechanger.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt can use his action to polymorph&nbsp;into a form that resembles a Medium humanoid, or back into his true form. Aside from his size, his statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment he is wearing or carrying isn&rsquo;t transformed.
Innate Spellcasting.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt&rsquo;s spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 23). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
At will: charm person, crown of madness, detect magic, dispel magic, dissonant whispers
3/day each:&nbsp;counterspell, darkness, dominate person, sanctuary,&nbsp;telekinesis, teleport
1/day each:&nbsp;dominate monster,&nbsp;greater invisibility
&nbsp;Legendary Resistance (5/Day).&nbsp;If Graz&rsquo;zt fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Magic Resistance.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Magic Weapons.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt&rsquo;s weapon attacks are magical.
Regeneration.&nbsp;If Graz'zt has at least 1 hit point, he regains 30 hit points at the start of his turn.
Crown of the Lost Emperor.&nbsp;Graz'zt's size grows to Huge, and he gains a Flying speed of 40 ft. Graz'zt's intelligence increases to 30, his wisdom increases to 30. Graz'zt gains proficiencies in Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, and Religion. If Graz'zt has at least 1 hit point, he uses regenerate. Graz'zt gains resistances to acid&nbsp;and thunder damage, immunity to cold and necrotic damage, as well as immunity to the paralyzed, petrified, and stunned conditions.
Eye of Vecna (Crown of the Lost Emperor).&nbsp;The Eye of Vecna has 8 charges and regains 1d4+4 expended charges daily at dawn. Vecna can cast the following spells from the eye by expending the specified number of charges (spell save DC 18): clairvoyance (2 charges), crown of madness (1 charge), disintegrate (4 charges), dominate monster (5 charges), and eyebite (3 charges).
Hand of Vecna (Crown of the Lost Emperor)&nbsp;The Hand of Vecna has 8 charges and regains 1d4+4&nbsp;expended charges daily at dawn.&nbsp;Graz'zt can cast the following spells from the hand by expending the specified&nbsp;number of charges (spell save DC 18): finger of death (5 charges), sleep (1 charge), slow (2 charges), and teleport (3 charges).
Orb of Demonkind.&nbsp;Once per day, Graz'zt can open a Gate to any plane of his choosing. Three times per day, he can use the spell plane shift.&nbsp;

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt attacks twice with the Wave of Sorrow.
Wave of Sorrow (Greatsword).&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+13&nbsp;to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;20 (4d6 + 6) slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) acid damage.
Know Secrets (Crown of the Lost Emperor) Using the Crown of the Lost Emperor, Ten creatures that&nbsp;Graz'zt&nbsp;can see within 120 feet must succeed on a DC 27 Wisdom saving throw. If it fails,&nbsp;Graz'zt immediately learns its entire history, including any embarrassing or vital secrets it might know.
X-Ray Vision (Eye of Vecna).&nbsp;Using The Eye of Vecna, Graz'zt can use an action to speak its command word. When he does so, he can see into and through solid matter for 1 minute. This vision has a radius of 30 feet. To him, solid objects within that radius appear transparent and don't prevent light from passing through them. The vision can penetrate 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, or up to 3 feet of wood or dirt. Thicker substances block the vision, as does a thin sheet of lead.
Orb of Demonkind. Graz'zt uses the Orb of Demonkind.
Teleport.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt magically teleport, along with any equipment he is wearing or carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.

## Legendary Actions

Graz&rsquo;zt can take&nbsp;5 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature&rsquo;s turn. Graz&rsquo;zt regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.
Attack.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt attacks once with the Wave of Sorrow.
Spells. Graz'zt casts one of his at-will-spells.
Dance, My Puppet!&nbsp;One creature&nbsp;charmed&nbsp;by Graz&rsquo;zt that Graz&rsquo;zt can see must use its reaction to move up to its speed as Graz&rsquo;zt directs.
Sow Discord.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt casts&nbsp;crown of madness or dissonant whispers
Teleport.&nbsp;Graz&rsquo;zt uses his Teleport action.
