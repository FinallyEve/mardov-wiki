# Mordekaiser, The Iron Revenant

**AC** 24 | **HP** 228
**Stats**: STR 26 | DEX 11 | CON 20 | INT 12 | WIS 16 | CHA 18
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Truesight 60ft. (while in Lair)
**Skills**: Athletics +20, Perception +9, Intimidation +16
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Petrified, Poisoned, Stunned

## Special Traits

Reconstitute. If&nbsp;Mordekaiser is destroyed&nbsp;while outside&nbsp;Afterworld (including&nbsp;Realm of Death) his body will reform and his soul shall inhabit it once again 1d100 years later on his throne within his palace.
Sight Beyond Death. Mordekaiser's darkvision is not hindered by magical darkness. If Mordekaiser is in his Lair, he&nbsp;gains truesight to a 60-foot range.
Immutable Form. Mordekaiser is immune to any effect that would alter his form.
Magic Resistance.&nbsp;Mordekaiser has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Legendary Resistance (3/Long Rest).&nbsp;If Mordekaiser fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
Darkness Rise.&nbsp;Knightfall attacks&nbsp;deal a bonus 4d8 necrotic damage (included in attack). Additionally, Mordekaiser can choose to have a 15-foot radius metalstorm encircle him. Creatures that enter the radius for first time on their turn or start their turn&nbsp;in it&nbsp;take 6 (1d10)&nbsp;psychic damage.
Indestructible.&nbsp;Mordekaiser&nbsp;stores&nbsp;an amount equal to half the&nbsp;necrotic damage&nbsp;dealt by&nbsp;Obliterate&nbsp;and Death's Grasp&nbsp;until he uses it to gain&nbsp;temporary hit points.&nbsp;If Mordekaiser suffers Radiant damage he loses 1/4th of his stored shield pool.
Lord Of The Dead.&nbsp;Mordekaiser does not require food, drink, air, or sleep to survive. Additionally, Mordekaiser&nbsp;is immune to&nbsp;features that turn undead. Unless he is incapacitated, undead creatures of&nbsp;his choice within 60 feet of&nbsp;him have advantage on saving throws against features that turn undead.
I Stand Eternal!&nbsp;(Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest). If Mordekaiser is reduced to 0 hit points, he doesn&rsquo;t die or fall unconscious. Instead, he regains&nbsp;180 hit points. In addition, Mordekaiser&rsquo;s&nbsp;Realm of Death's duration refreshes and the effect triggers again, if it is active,&nbsp;it otherwise activates.
Spellcasting.&nbsp;Mordekaiser's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks).&nbsp;He has the following paladin spells prepared:3/Day: hold person,&nbsp;dispel magic.2/Day: counterspell(5th level).1/Day: circle of death (centered on self).

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;Mordekaiser makes three&nbsp;Knightfall&nbsp;attacks.
Knightfall.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+13&nbsp;to hit, reach&nbsp;10 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;18 (2d8 + 10)&nbsp;bludgeoning damage, plus&nbsp;20 (4d8) necrotic damage.
Obliterate (Recharge 5-6).&nbsp;Mordekaiser gathers necromantic energies around&nbsp;Knightfall and slams it down ahead of him. Creatures in a 15-foot cone&nbsp;adjacent to Mordekaiser must succeed a DC 18 Constitution saving throw. Creatures take 48 (8d10) necrotic damage on a failure or half as much on a success.
Death's Grasp.&nbsp;Mordekaiser summons a ghoulish spectral claw&nbsp;in a&nbsp;30-foot long 10-foot wide line adjacent to him. Targets in the line must succeed a DC 18&nbsp;Strength saving throw, taking&nbsp;20&nbsp;(4d8) necrotic damage and be pulled to a space adjacent to&nbsp;Mordekaiser on a failure. On a success, the target takes half damage and is not pulled.
Realm of Death (1/Long Rest).&nbsp;Mordekaiser magically&nbsp;summons&nbsp;up to 8 creatures he can see to his domain, Afterworld, for 1 minute. On arrival, chosen&nbsp;creatures must succeed a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or have all their stats reduced by 2 while within Afterworld. Additionally, a creature's max hit points are reduced by an amount equal to necrotic damage dealt by&nbsp;Mordekaiser while in Afterworld (excluding circle of death). If&nbsp;Mordekaiser slays a creature during this ability's&nbsp;duration, he raises all his stats by 1 permanently, to a maximum of 30.

## Legendary Actions

Mordekaiser can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature&rsquo;s turn. Mordekaiser regains spent legendary actions at the start of&nbsp;his&nbsp;turn.
March. Mordekaiser moves up to his speed.
Strike. Mordekaiser makes one attack with Knightfall.
Within My Grasp&nbsp;(Costs 2 Actions). Mordekaiser targets a creature with&nbsp;Death's Grasp.
Nightfall's Hunger&nbsp;(Costs 3 Actions). Mordekaiser uses&nbsp;Obliterate if it has recharged.
