# Lilly

**AC** 13 | **HP** 25
**Stats**: STR 11 | DEX 16 | CON 12 | INT 2 | WIS 14 | CHA 5


**Condition Immunities**: Paralyzed

## Special Traits

Song of the Doe.&nbsp;As an action the doe may grant up to 4 allies 2d6 inspiration, as a bonus action, the doe may distribute 4d4 good-berries to the inspired targets. If cast on the same targets, each round cry of the doe is cast, the creature gains 1d6 extra inspiration and 1d4 extra good-berries. These extra inspiration die may also apply to damage.&nbsp;
Determined Will. &nbsp;The doe is immune to being paralyzed, while singing.&nbsp;

## Actions

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2 (1d4) piercing damage.
