# Lox

**AC** 16 | **HP** 120
**Stats**: STR 18 | DEX 16 | CON 15 | INT 10 | WIS 14 | CHA 8
**Senses**: Darkvision 60, Tremorsense 20
**Skills**: Athletics +9


## Special Traits

Keen Smell. Lox has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
Rampage. When Lox reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, Lox can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.
Feral Instinct. Lox has advantage on initiative rolls.
Zodiac's Fury. Lox deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage when it hits with a melee attack (included in the attack).

## Actions

Multiattack. Lox makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing damage.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage.
