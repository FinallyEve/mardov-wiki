# Hippo

**AC** 11 | **HP** 68
**Stats**: STR 22 | DEX 7 | CON 16 | INT 3 | WIS 11 | CHA 6




## Special Traits

Hold Breath. The hippo can hold its breath for 5 minutes.
Steadfast. The hippo can't be frightened while it can see an allied creature or one of its calves within 30 feet of it.
Savage Attacks. When the hippo scores a critical hit with its bite attack, it can roll one of the damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical.&nbsp;

## Actions

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5)&nbsp;piercing damage.
