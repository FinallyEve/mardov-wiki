# Star Spawn Monstrosity

**AC** 17 | **HP** 136
**Stats**: STR 20 | DEX 8 | CON 21 | INT 7 | WIS 16 | CHA 9
**Senses**: Blindsight 500ft, Darkvision 60 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +17, Perception +5
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Frightened

## Special Traits

Psychic Mirror.&nbsp;If the&nbsp;Star Spawn Monstrosity&nbsp;takes psychic damage, each creature within 10 feet of the Star Spawn Monstrosity takes that damage instead; the hulk takes none of the damage. In addition, the Star Spawn Monstrosity&rsquo;s thoughts and location can&rsquo;t be discerned by magic.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day).&nbsp;If Star Spawn Monstrosity fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.&nbsp;
Consume Dead.&nbsp;If Star Spawn Monstrosity spends 1 min consuming a body it is able to take a short rest.&nbsp;

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;The hulk makes two slam attacks and one bite attack or 4 tentacle attacks and one bite attack or 2 tentacle attacks, one slam attack and one bite attack. If both slam attacks hit the same target, the target also takes 9 (2d8) psychic damage and must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of the target&rsquo;s next turn.
Slam.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.
Bite.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit: 31 (4d12 + 5) piercing damage.
Tentacle.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled&nbsp;(escape DC 17) and is moved up to 5 feet toward the Star Spawn Monstrosity. Until this grapple ends, the target is&nbsp;restrained, and the Star Spawn Monstrosity can&rsquo;t use this tentacle against other targets. The Star Spawn Monstrosity has four tentacles.
Reaping Arms (Recharge 5&ndash;6).&nbsp;The hulk makes a separate slam attack against each creature within 10 feet of it. Each creature that is hit must also succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone.

## Legendary Actions

The Star Spawn Monstrosity can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature&rsquo;s turn. The Star Spawn Monstrosity regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Bite Attack.&nbsp;The Star Spawn Monstrosity makes one bite attack against one creature it has grappled.
Teleport.&nbsp;The Star Spawn Monstrosity magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying and any creatures it has grappled, up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
Vanish.&nbsp;The Star Spawn Monstrosity magically becomes&nbsp;invisible&nbsp;for up to 10 minutes or until immediately after it makes an attack roll.
