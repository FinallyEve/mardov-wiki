# Inchu

**AC** 16 | **HP** 105
**Stats**: STR 12 | DEX 18 | CON 16 | INT 8 | WIS 14 | CHA 8
**Senses**: Darkvision 60
**Skills**: Acrobatics +8, Stealth +8


## Special Traits

Keen Senses. The mongoose has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or sight.
Nimble Climber. The mongoose has advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks made to climb
1. Gust of Wind. 2nd-level evocationThe mongoose unleashes a line of strong wind 60 feet long and 10 feet wide from its mouth in a direction you choose. Each creature that starts its turn in the line must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 feet away from you in a direction following the line. Any flying creature in the line is instead blown away in that direction until the start of the mongoose's next turn.
2. Blink. 3rd-level transmutationThe mongoose magically teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. Alternatively, if the mongoose can see into the Ethereal Plane, it can choose a target within 10 feet of it and force it to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target is banished to the Ethereal Plane until the start of the mongoose's next turn.
3. Thunder Step. 3rd-level conjurationThe mongoose magically teleports up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. Each creature within 10 feet of the mongoose at both the start and end of the teleportation must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 3d10 thunder damage and is deafened until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't deafened. The thunder can be heard from up to 300 feet away.

## Actions

Multiattack. The mongoose makes three attacks: two with its bite and one with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage.
