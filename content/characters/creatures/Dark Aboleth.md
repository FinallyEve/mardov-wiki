# Dark Aboleth

**AC** 24 | **HP** 490
**Stats**: STR 27 | DEX 19 | CON 25 | INT 22 | WIS 19 | CHA 18
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Truesight 50 feet
**Skills**: History +12, Perception +10
**Condition Immunities**: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated, Stunned, Unconscious

## Special Traits

Amphibious. The aboleth can breathe air and water.
Mucous Cloud. While underwater, the aboleth is surrounded by transformative mucus. A creature that touches the aboleth or that hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is diseased for 1d4 hours. The diseased creature can breathe only underwater.
Probing Telepathy. If a creature communicates telepathically with the aboleth, the aboleth learns the creature's greatest desires if the aboleth can see the creature.
Oily Streak: Upon moving, the Dark Aboleth releases an oily type mucus that spreads across the field, this also works while its standing still up to thirty feet. This counts a difficult terrain for anyone inside, unless you can overcome difficult terrain. Being attacked in this oily field will deal double fire damage.
Split: Upon losing 100 health, the Dark Aboleth will split into two versions of itself at a one size smaller. After it loses 200 more health, it will split again into four at another size smaller.
DarkFire: The Dark Aboleth's attacks hit with fire and darkness (NOTE: Darkness hits any and all living creatures with 10x the amount of damage dealt and is known to be the strongest necrotic enchantment known to man. It also heals undead for 10x the amount of damage dealt.)
Mucus Membrane:&nbsp;The Dark Aboleth is covers in slippery mucus, therefore any and all types of physical attacks are made with disadvantage and only do half damage to the Dark Aboleth.

## Actions

Multiattack. The aboleth makes three tentacle attacks.
Tentacle Swipe:&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit:&nbsp;70 (10d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or become diseased. The disease has no effect for 1 minute and can be removed by any magic that cures disease. After 1 minute, the diseased creature's skin becomes translucent and slimy, the creature can't regain hit points unless it is covered in oil, and the disease can be removed only by heal or another disease-curing spell of 6th level or higher. When the creature is vacant of oil, it takes 15 (2d12) fire damage every minute unless moisture is applied to the skin before 10 minutes have passed. On a successful hit, oil is spread from the aboleth to the target from however long the distance is to its max distance, and is five feet wide.
Dark Napalm: The Aboleth raises it's tentacle, slamming it down forcefully into the ground, any and all creature inside the oils radius, roll a dex save as the black flame bursts uncontrollably, rising high into the air. (Save DC=24). On a failed save, the targets take 4d20 damage. This attack does benefit from the dark enchantment feature and is it's strongest attack.
Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage.
Enslave (3/Day). The aboleth targets one creature it can see within 30 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be magically charmed by the aboleth until the aboleth dies or until it is on a different plane of existence from the target. The charmed target is under the aboleth's control and can't take reactions, and the aboleth and the target can communicate telepathically with each other over any distance.
Whenever the charmed target takes damage, the target can repeat the saving throw. On a success, the effect ends. No more than once every 24 hours, the target can also repeat the saving throw when it is at least 1 mile away from the aboleth.

## Legendary Actions

The aboleth can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The aboleth regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Detect. The aboleth makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
Tail Swipe. The aboleth makes one tail attack.
Psychic Drain (Costs 2 Actions). One creature charmed by the aboleth takes&nbsp;25 (4d10) psychic damage, and the aboleth regains hit points equal to the damage the creature takes.
