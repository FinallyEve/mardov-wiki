# Zodiac of Life

**AC** 19 | **HP** 279
**Stats**: STR 24 | DEX 10 | CON 26 | INT 18 | WIS 22 | CHA 16

**Skills**: Nature +11, Animal Handling +11, Perception +11


## Special Traits

Innate Spellcasting. Kolyma's innate spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit with spell attacks). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

At will: druidcraft, guidance
3/day each: barkskin, entangle, thorn whip
2/day each: plant growth, revivify
1/day each: sunburst, transport via plants, true resurrection

Spellcasting. Kolyma is an 18th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit with spell attacks). Kolyma has the following druid spells prepared:

Cantrips (at will): mending, produce flame, shillelagh
1st level (4 slots): cure wounds, entangle, goodberry, thunderwave
2nd level (3 slots): barkskin, pass without trace, spike growth
3rd level (3 slots): call lightning, plant growth, sleet storm
4th level (3 slots): blight, divination, freedom of movement
5th level (2 slots): commune with nature, greater restoration
6th level (1 slot): heroes' feast

## Actions

Multiattack. Kolyma makes two slam attacks.
Slam. Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
