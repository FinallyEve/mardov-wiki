# Corrupted Solar (Aberrant)

**AC** 24 | **HP** 540
**Stats**: STR 26 | DEX 22 | CON 26 | INT 25 | WIS 25 | CHA 30
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft
**Skills**: Perception +14, Intimidation +24
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned

## Special Traits

Warped Weapons.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar's weapon attacks are magical. When the solar hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 8d8&nbsp; psychic&nbsp;damage (included in the attack).&nbsp;Divine Awareness.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;knows if it hears a lie.&nbsp;Innate Spellcasting.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 25). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:&nbsp;At will: detect evil and good,&nbsp;invisibility&nbsp;(self only)&nbsp;3/day each:&nbsp;blade barrier,&nbsp;dispel evil and good,&nbsp;resurrection&nbsp;1/day each:&nbsp;commune,&nbsp;control weather, plane shift&nbsp;Magic Resistance.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;makes&nbsp;two greatsword attacks.Greatsword.&nbsp;Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+15 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;22 (4d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 36 (8d8) psychic&nbsp;damage.Twisted Slaying Longbow.&nbsp;Ranged Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+13 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage plus 36 (8d8) psychic&nbsp;damage. If the target is a creature that has 100 hit points or fewer, it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or die.Flying Sword.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;releases its greatsword to hover magically in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it. If the Corrupted Solar&nbsp;can see the sword, the Corrupted Solar&nbsp;can mentally command it as a bonus action to fly up to 50 feet and either make one attack against a target or return to the Corrupted Solar's hands. If the hovering sword is targeted by any effect, the Corrupted Solar&nbsp;is considered to be holding it. The hovering sword falls if the Corrupted Solar&nbsp;dies.&nbsp;Healing Touch (4/Day).&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;touches another creature. The target magically regains 40 (8d8 + 4) hit points and is freed from any curse, disease, poison, blindness, or deafness.Blasphemous Touch (4/Day). Melee Weapon Attack:&nbsp;+15 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;40 (8d8 + 4)&nbsp;necrotic damage and must make a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw or be cursed in one of the following ways:

Choose one ability score. While cursed, the target has disadvantage on ability checks and saving throws made with that ability score.
While cursed, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
While cursed, the target must make a Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns. If it fails, it wastes its action that turn doing nothing.
While the target is cursed, your attacks and spells deal an extra 1d8 necrotic damage to the target.

## Legendary Actions

The Corrupted Solar can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.&nbsp;Teleport.&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.&nbsp;Withering Burst (Costs 2 Actions).&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar&nbsp;emits magical, evil energy. Each creature of its choice in a 10-foot radius must make a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) psychic&nbsp;damage plus 21 (6d6)&nbsp;necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.&nbsp;Aberrant&nbsp;Gaze (Costs 3 Actions).&nbsp;The Corrupted Solar targets one creature it can see within 30 feet of it. If the target can see it, the target must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or suffer from short term madness. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target suffers from both short and long term madness, and if the save fails by 10 or more, the target also gains a flaw from the indefinite madness table.
