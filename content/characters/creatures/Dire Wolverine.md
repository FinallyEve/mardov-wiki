# Dire Wolverine

**Stats**: STR 17 | DEX 15 | CON 15 | INT 3 | WIS 12 | CHA 7

Keen Hearing and Smell. The Wolverine has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
