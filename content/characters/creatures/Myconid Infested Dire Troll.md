# Myconid Infested Dire Troll

**AC** 15 | **HP** 212
**Stats**: STR 22 | DEX 15 | CON 21 | INT 7 | WIS 13 | CHA 5
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +5
**Condition Immunities**: Frightened, Poisoned

## Special Traits

Keen Senses. The troll has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or sight.
Regeneration. The troll regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes acid or fire damage, it regains only 5 hit points at the start of its next turn. The troll dies only if it is hit by an attack that deals 10 or more acid or fire damage while the troll has 0 hit points.
Sunlight Sensitivity.&nbsp;While in direct sunlight or sun, the Spore Harvester has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
Spore Umbrella.&nbsp;So long as the Myconid Infected Dire Troll has three or more Puff Spores&nbsp;active, it is not affected by Sunlight Sensitivity, even in direct sunlight.&nbsp;

## Actions

Multiattack. The troll makes five attacks: one with its bite, one to create puff spores, one to consume puff spores, and two with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d8 + 6) piercing damage plus 5 (1d10) poison damage.
Create Puff Spores.&nbsp;The Spore Harvester creates 1d8 + 2&nbsp;Puff Spores.
Consume Puff Spores.&nbsp;The Spore Harvester consumes any number of&nbsp;Puff Spores&nbsp;within 5 feet of it, and gains a +2 bonus to its next damage roll with a Claw for each&nbsp;Puff Spore&nbsp;consumed. Consuming five or more Spores triggers a roll of Regeneration.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d6 + 6) slashing damage.
Whirlwind of Spores and Claws (Recharge 5&ndash;6). Each creature within 10 feet of the troll must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 44 (8d10) slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.Creates 2d10 + 5 Puff Spores
