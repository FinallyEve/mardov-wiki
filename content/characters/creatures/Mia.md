# Mia

**AC** 17 | **HP** 120
**Stats**: STR 16 | DEX 12 | CON 15 | INT 14 | WIS 16 | CHA 12
**Senses**: Darkvision 60
**Skills**: Arcana +5, Insight +5


## Special Traits

Spellcasting. Mia is a 7th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks). Mia has the following spells prepared from the druid spell list:

Cantrips (at will): druidcraft, resistance, thorn whip
1st level (4 slots): absorb elements, entangle, fog cloud
2nd level (3 slots): barkskin, enhance ability (resilience), moonbeam
3rd level (3 slots): protection from energy, sleet storm

Magic Resistance. Mia has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Zodiac's Shield. Mia's presence grants protection to friendly creatures within 30 feet. Whenever a friendly creature within range is targeted by a spell or magical effect, Mia can use her reaction to grant that creature advantage on the saving throw or grant it resistance to the damage (Mia's choice).

## Actions

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.
