# Annis Hag (Coven Variant)

**AC** 17 | **HP** 97
**Stats**: STR 21 | DEX 12 | CON 14 | INT 15 | WIS 16 | CHA 16
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +5, Deception +5


## Special Traits

Innate Spellcasting. The hag&rsquo;s innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16). She can innately cast the following spells:
3/day each: disguise self (including the form of a Medium humanoid), fog cloud
HAG COVENS
When hags must work together, they form covens, in spite of their selfish natures. A coven is made up of hags of any type, all of whom are equals within the group. However, each of the hags continues to desire more personal power.
A coven consists of three hags so that any arguments between two hags can be settled by the third. If more than three hags ever come together, as might happen if two covens come into conflict, the result is usually chaos.
Shared Spellcasting.&nbsp;While all three members of a hag coven are within 30 feet of one another, they can each cast the following spells from the wizard&rsquo;s spell list but must share the spell slots among themselves:
1st level (4 slots):&nbsp;identify,&nbsp;ray of sickness
2nd level (3 slots):&nbsp;hold person,&nbsp;locate object
3rd level (3 slots):&nbsp;bestow curse,&nbsp;counterspell,&nbsp;lightning bolt
4th level (3 slots):&nbsp;phantasmal killer,&nbsp;polymorph
5th level (2 slots):&nbsp;contact other plane,&nbsp;scrying
6th level (1 slot):&nbsp;eyebite

## Actions

Multiattack. The annis makes three attacks: one with her bite and two with her claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) piercing damage.
Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) slashing damage.
Crushing Hug. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 36 (9d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15) if it is a Large or smaller creature. Until the grapple ends, the target takes 36 (9d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of the hag&rsquo;s turns. The hag can&rsquo;t make attacks while grappling a creature in this way.
