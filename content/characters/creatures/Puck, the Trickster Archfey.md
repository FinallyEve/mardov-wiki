# Puck, the Trickster Archfey

**AC** 21 | **HP** 400
**Stats**: STR 10 | DEX 24 | CON 12 | INT 22 | WIS 16 | CHA 26
**Senses**: Truesight 60 ft.
**Skills**: Acrobatics +15, Stealth +15, Insight +11, Perception +11, Deception +16, Persuasion +24
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned

## Special Traits

Legendary Resistance (3/Day).&nbsp;If Puck fails a saving through, he can choose to succeed instead.
Magic Resistance. Puck has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Innate Spellcasting.&nbsp;Puck's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 24). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
At will: charm person, Tasha's hideous laughter
3/day each: confusion, dominate person, suggestion
1/day each: plane shift, Otto's irresistible dance, power word stun
Twinned Spell (2/day).&nbsp;When Puck casts a spell that targets only one creature and doesn&rsquo;t have a range of self, he can target a second creature in range with the same spell. To be eligible, a spell must be incapable of targeting more than one creature at the spell&rsquo;s current level. For example, magic missile and scorching ray aren&rsquo;t eligible, but ray of frost and chromatic orb are.
Heightened Spell (1/day).&nbsp;When you cast a spell that forces a creature to make a saving throw to resist its effects, Puck can give one target of the spell disadvantage on its first saving throw made against the spell.
Wild Magic Surge. Once per turn, roll a d20 immediately after casting a spell or using a spell attack. If that roll is a 1, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table to create a magical effect.&nbsp;If that effect is a spell, it is too wild to be affected by your Metamagic, and if it normally requires concentration, it doesn&rsquo;t require concentration in this case; the spell lasts for its full duration. If that roll is not a 1, then Wild Magic Surge can be triggered on a 1 or 2. This number increases by 1 each time either until a Wild Magic Surge is triggered or until Puck takes a long rest.
Uncanny Dodge. When an attacker that Puck can sees hits him with an attack, Puck can use his reaction to halve the attack's damage against him.
Sneak Attack. Once per turn, Puck can deal an extra 3d6&nbsp;damage to one creature he hits with an attack if he has advantage on the attack roll. He doesn&rsquo;t need advantage on the attack roll if another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it, that enemy isn&rsquo;t incapacitated, and he doesn't have disadvantage on the attack roll.
Joyful Presence.&nbsp;Any non-eladrin creature that starts its turn within 60 feet of Puck must make a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature becomes charmed&nbsp;by the eladrin for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature becomes immune to any eladrin&rsquo;s Joyful Presence for 24 hours.
Whenever Puck deals damage to the charmed&nbsp;creature, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

## Actions

Multiattack.&nbsp;Puck can make two attacks with his Psychic Rapier and one Touch of Chaos attack.
Psychic Rapier. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit:&nbsp;16 (2d8 + 7) piercing damage plus 28 (8d6) psychic damage.
Touch of Chaos.&nbsp;Melee Spell Attack:&nbsp;+16 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.&nbsp;Hit:&nbsp;23 (3d8 + 10) necrotic damage, and Puck can choose one magic item he can see in the target&rsquo;s possession. Unless it&rsquo;s an artifact, the item&rsquo;s magic is suppressed until the start of Puck&rsquo;s next turn.
Wrath of Spring&nbsp;(Recharge 5&ndash;6).&nbsp;Puck conjures a momentary whirl of branches and vines at a point he can see within 60 feet of him. Each creature in a 30-foot cube on that point must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21&nbsp;(6d6)&nbsp;bludgeoning damage and 21&nbsp;(6d6)&nbsp;slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Legendary Actions

Puck can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature&rsquo;s turn. Puck regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.
Fey Step.&nbsp;Puck magically teleports up to 30 ft. to an unoccupied space he can see. Alternatively, Puck can&nbsp;touch one willing creature within 5 feet of him. That creature then teleports instead of him, appearing in an unoccupied space of Puck's choice that he can see within 30 feet of him.
Attack. Puck can make one attack with his Psychic Rapier, with advantage on the roll.
Cast a Spell (Costs 3 Actions). Puck can cast a spell. This counts as one of his daily uses of the spell casted.
