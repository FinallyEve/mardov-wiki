# Maghar of the Sword

**AC** 16 | **HP** 30
**Stats**: STR 16 | DEX 12 | CON 16 | INT 10 | WIS 14 | CHA 11

**Skills**: Athletics +5, Perception +4, Survival +4


## Special Traits

Warforged Resilience. The warforged has advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and is immune to disease. Magic can&rsquo;t put it to sleep.

## Actions

Channel Divinity - Bless Artifact: Sword.&nbsp;Slyvia Hadrin blesses the Sword of a target within a 20ft of his statue. The Blessed Sword grants the wearer +2 to damage on attacks from within range. Only one Sword may be blessed at a time. If the target is also under the effects of the other blessings of Muradin, these effects last until the end of the next long rest. The full blessing may only be granted once per week.
