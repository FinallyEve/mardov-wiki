# The Fey Courts

## Overview

The [[Feywild|Fae Wildes]] are organized into four seasonal courts, each with its own domain and leadership. The courts are deeply entangled with the campaign's politics through [[Reynaldine]]'s machinations and the Convergence event.

## The Four Courts

### Spring Court — Brier Patch
- **Fey Lord:** Evelyandrin of the Spring Court
- **Eladrin Representative:** [[Haden Dar]] (Platinum Seat of Illusionist Covenant)

### Summer Court — Flowerfield
- **Fey Lord:** Juliannia of the Summer Court
- **Eladrin Representative:** Jing Lin (Voice of Potential)

### Autumn Court — Reaper's Bounty
- **Fey Lord:** [[Reynaldine]] of the Autumn Court
- **Role in Campaign:** The most politically active court. [[Reynaldine]] serves [[Vecna]] while pursuing his own agenda — to ascend to godhood and save Gabriel (archangel of kindness).
- **Key Actions:**
  - Hired Annie Hallowbrook to deliver [[Kelik Waystar]] to [[windhaven]]
  - Used [[Xyn]] (Nyx's twin sister) as messenger
  - Threatened to disrupt pestilence shipments to [[rimefang]] as leverage
  - Invoked the Ritual of the Stone Table through Umbara Skuttle
  - True plan revealed Ch. 133 — orchestrated everything for his own ascension
  - "Crippled" Ch. 106; cloak fell revealing leeches forming a portal
- **Queen of Thorns** — Referenced Autumn Court figure

### Winter Court — Snowcrest Sanctum
- **Known Members:**
  - Grumblesin of the Winter Court
  - Silence (Timothy of the Feywild) — Therzadun's vessel
  - Tallow (Squire of Winter)
  - Yeth Hounds
- **Eladrin Representative:** Tumnus Driftwalker (Nic of the [[Winter's Curators]])

> **CRITICAL NOTE:** [[Reynaldine]] (Fey Lord of the Autumn Court) is a DISTINCT entity from [[Reynardine]] (Nyx's patron, "the Madness Bringer"). Similar names, different characters.

## The Convergence (Late Campaign)

All planes began converging into the Feywild:
- Orchestrated by [[Reynaldine]]
- **Stone Table Ritual:** sacrifice + benefactor = sovereignty
- Knights needed: [[Riven Fen|Fen]], [[Josh Coolbreeze|Josh]], [[Varis Laindon|Varis]], [[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]]
- [[Graz'zt]] swapped with Gabriel at the ritual
- [[Bergs Fireforge|Bergs]] forced both himself and [[Graz'zt]] onto the Stone Table
- **Result:** [[Bergs Fireforge|Bergs]] becomes deity of Mardov's Feywild
- Shattering of Stone Table shatters the Feywild and Mardov
