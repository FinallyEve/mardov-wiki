# Vecna Sworn

## Overview

[[Vecna]]'s conquest of [[Mardov]] is organized through four domains of power — the **Four Zeniths**. Each Zenith has a lieutenant, a target, and a nested web of agents. [[Vecna]] rules [[V-Mardov]] (a parallel dimension mirroring the material plane) and commands all four operations.

**Ultimate Goal:** Destroy all keystones, enable [[V-Mardov|V-MORDRED]]'s invasion of the material plane.

## The Four Zenith Operations

### Zenith of Death (Forest's Zenith — Machu)
- **Heart:** [[Heart of the Wilde]] (Emerald)
- **Anchor:** [[Anchor of Death]] (obsidian sword)
- **Eye:** Green Eye of [[Vecna]]
- **Primary Agent:** [[Mordred]] / [[Mordred|Ramses]]
  - [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]] (soul-harvesting front)
  - [[Jorgen]] (lure, turned revenant)
  - [[Golden Reach]] demons ([[mandu|Markus]]/Danelle, incubus/succubus in Argos)
  - Hag Coven (Margaret, Gertrude, Tabitha — brew Pestilence from children's souls)
  - [[mandu]] (corrupt Argos guard captain)
  - Reva (Machu Royal Advisor)
  - Tatiana (the Imposter of Machu)
  - [[jafar]] (Sally's uncle, hired the Dons, placed [[Nathan Yanagan]] as spy)
  - [[Costa Una]] (traitor within STB)
  - Salina (Death Knight Captain), Lord Soth (Death Knight of Vodram)
- **Secondary Agent:** [[Ned'Zailok]] (Lich, Keeper of Death's Anchor in [[V-Mardov]])
  - Betrayed Casaval Dekun (Eladrin warrior)
- **Boss:** Forest Guardian / Forest Titan — awakened by [[Mordred]] using the [[Heart of the Wilde]]. Defeating it transports party to [[V-Mardov]].

### Zenith of War (Mountain's Zenith — Vag'Mahar / Underdark)
- **Heart:** [[Heart of the Forge]] (Garnet)
- **Anchor:** [[Anchor of War]] (hammer-axe-javelin)
- **Eye:** Red Eye of [[Vecna]]
- **Primary Agent:** [[Peraxis Phyrexia]] (Illithilich — THE Central Villain)
  - Created the [[Spell Plague]] (with [[Alhoon Ren]]'s research)
  - Captured the [[Zodiac|Stag Zodiac]] to locate [[Mountain's Zenith]]
  - Holds [[Thorkon Fireforge]] captive; plans Ceremorphosis
  - Beholder relay network for psychic communication
  - Homunculus Thraalm (killed Ch. 66)
  - **Research Arm:** [[Alhoon Ren]] (double agent, secretly taking notes) + [[Alhoon Pollivar]] (Umber Hulk facility)
- **Allied Agent:** [[Oblivion Wayne]] (Vampire Queen of the Drow)
  - [[Ebon Wayne]] (husband, discovered [[Spell Plague]], captured Bat Zodiac)
  - Vampire-converted Drow army
  - Plans overland invasion
- **Allied Agent:** [[Ghost-Eye Thorrin]] (Puppetmaster of [[Vag'Mahar]])
  - Convinced [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] to betray [[Thorson Bronzebeard II]]
  - Then betrayed the deal; hunted all [[forgesworn]]
  - Missing by late campaign
- **Nested Conspiracy:** The Dwarven Civil War
  - Three Thanes: Blackbeard, Brownbeard, Redbeard
  - Three secret police: Blackguard, Truth's Light, Silent Flame
  - **ALL THREE secretly run by A SINGLE MIND FLAYER**
  - Goal: use [[Anchor of War]] to breed better Duergar

### Zenith of Pestilence (Winter's Zenith — Bynar)
- **Heart:** [[Heart of the Faith]] (Sapphire)
- **Anchor:** [[Anchor of Pestilence]] (pan flute / Horn of Winter)
- **Eye:** Blue Eye of [[Vecna]]
- **Primary Agent:** [[rimefang]] (Undead Dragon)
  - Raised by [[Vecna]], given a white dragon body via [[Black Hand Clan]] rebirth ritual
  - Ordered to "open the [[Winter's Zenith]] and finish the Spellplague"
  - Receives pestilence supply shipments
  - Made one last ride as sacrifice (Ch. 113)
- **Allied Agent:** Yuan-Ti / [[Scales of Falak]]
  - Stole the [[Anchor of Pestilence]] from the [[Sunless Citadel]]
  - Ruined the [[Winter's Curators]] (dragon-kin tribe)
  - Leaders: Umangi Shiva, Casstantinople Pserephonie, Lady Sessura Vaught, Mathia Nuum, Bree Jasnar
- **Guardian:** [[Frostragan]] (White Dragon, stole [[Heart of the Faith]])
- **Tiamat Connection:** [[Oblivion Wayne]] gave her soul to [[Tiamat]]. [[Tiamat]]'s avatar awakened late campaign.

### Zenith of Famine (Harvest's Zenith — Tygras Logath / DawnHaven)
- **Heart:** [[Heart of the Progenitors]] (Ruby)
- **Anchor:** [[Anchor of Famine]] ([[Diadem of Dawn]])
- **Eye:** Orange Eye of [[Vecna]]
- **Primary Agent:** [[Reynaldine]] (Fey Lord of the Autumn Court) — **TRIPLE AGENT**
  - Serves [[Vecna]] but has his OWN agenda
  - Wants [[DawnHaven]] cleansed of [[uma]]'s remnants to find the [[Diadem of Dawn|Diadem]]
  - Hired Annie Hallowbrook to bring [[Kelik Waystar]] to [[windhaven]]
  - Used [[Xyn]] as messenger
  - **True Plan (Ch. 133):** Orchestrated bringing ALL villains together at the Convergence + Stone Table so he could ascend to godhood and save Gabriel
  - Invoked the Ritual of the Stone Table through Umbara Skuttle
- **Allied Agent:** [[Logath HellEye]] (Emperor of [[Tygras Logath]])
  - Former gladiator pit champion
  - Made deal with [[Graz'zt]] (the fae demon, now under [[Vecna]]'s command) -- same entity as [[Tygras]]'s patron
  - Causes bloodlust and madness among orcs
- **The Orc-Human War** — manufactured by [[Reynaldine]]/[[Logath HellEye|Logath]]/[[Mordred]]
  - [[Gordan BlackHand]] (Orc Sorcerer, Vecna agent)
  - [[Grimnak Norman|Grim'Nak the Terrible]], Sledge (Orc Chieftains)
- **Sealed Evil:** [[Tygras]] — Ancient tiefling warlord sealed inside the Zenith, feeding off its energy
