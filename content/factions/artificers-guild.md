# Artificer's Guild

## Overview

Founded by [[Sabine Ra]]'s avatar (the [[Raven Queen]]) and entrusted to her husband [[Xerxes]]. The [[Artificer's Guild]] specializes in animancy (the magic of life) — the counterpart to the Circle of the Murder's necromancy (death magic).

[[Mordred]] gave up his phylactery specifically to infiltrate and take out the [[Artificer's Guild]], gaining [[Xerxes]]'s body in the process.

## History

- Founded by the [[Raven Queen]] (Sabine's avatar), entrusted to [[Xerxes]]
- [[Mordred]] infiltrated the Guild by sacrificing his phylactery
- [[Mordred]] corrupted the individual beings that make up [[Xerxes]]
- The party rescues the Guild (Ch. 3-5)
