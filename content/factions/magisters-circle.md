# Magister's Circle

## Overview

The governing magical authority of [[Mardov]]. Contains Bard Colleges, Magic Covenants, and Platinum Seats. Located in [[vartos]]. The Circle enforces the Covenants of Magic established after [[Tygras]]'s destruction — ensuring no being except the Alabaster Seat may possess world-ending power.
