# Scarlet Tunic Brigade

## Overview

The Scarlet Tunic Brigade is a private military company that serves as the campaign's opening deception. On the surface, it is a legitimate mercenary organization that recruits adventurers, defends towns, and covers evacuations. In truth, it is a **soul-harvesting operation** run by the lich [[Mordred]] through his vessel [[Finkle Hollingsworth]] (a Gnome, NOT a Goblin).

The Brigade has harvested approximately **2,000 adventurer souls** by luring recruits through [[Jorgen]] and leading them to ritual chambers beneath cities.

## Goals & Motivation

- **Public Face:** Recruit adventurers, defend frontier towns, maintain order
- **True Purpose:** Harvest souls for [[Mordred]]'s necromantic rituals
- **End Goal:** Gather enough soul energy to facilitate [[V-Mardov|V-MORDRED]]'s invasion

## History

- Founded by [[Mordred]] as a front for soul harvesting
- Provided legitimate military service (e.g., covering [[Tower Bridge]] evacuation against [[Ned'Zailok]])
- [[Varis Liadon|Varis]] enlisted after the Ned'Zailok campaign
- Chapter 0-2: Party recruited, sewer descent, [[Mordred]] revealed, Brigade exposed
- Post-reveal: [[Finkle Hollingsworth]] freed from possession, eventually becomes an ally

## Key Events

- **The Sewer Ritual (Ch. 0-2):** Four-colored waterfall chamber beneath Argos. Ritual to harvest souls. [[Sabine Ra]]'s phylactery discovered.
- **Jorgen's Betrayal (Ch. 0):** Jorgen leads party into the trap, seals them in.
- **Mordred's Reveal (Ch. 2):** "2,000 souls, to be exact... They could have flourished if we hadn't betrayed them."
- **The Revenant (Ch. 7-13):** Jorgen's undead form pursues [[Finkle Hollingsworth|Finkle]], chanting "KILL THE GNOME."
