# The Forgesworn (Mag'har)

## Overview

An ancient dwarven order of master weaponsmiths. Each of the 12 Maghar specializes in creating a different type of fireforged item. Their craft carries the heaviest cost in all of [[Mardov]]:

> **A bit of yerself is imparted in each of yer creations.** There is nothing worth more than yer soul.
> — [[Thorkon Fireforge]]

The [[forgesworn]] were hunted to near-extinction by [[Ghost-Eye Thorrin]] after [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]]'s betrayal was exposed.

## Goals & Motivation

- **Survival** — avoid extinction at [[Ghost-Eye Thorrin]]'s hands
- **Freedom** — end the forced creation of weapons for kings
- **The Warforged Secret** — prevent the creation of more Warforged, which consume dwarven souls

## History

1. [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] serves as royal weaponsmith for [[Thorson Bronzebeard II]]
2. [[Thorson Bronzebeard II|Bronzebeard]] wants to create more Warforged
3. [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] discovers Warforged consume dwarven souls
4. [[Ghost-Eye Thorrin]] convinces [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] to betray the king
5. [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] creates a cursed soul-forged dagger as a "gift"
6. [[Ghost-Eye Thorrin]] betrays [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]], hunts all [[forgesworn]]
7. Most escape; [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] flees with pregnant wife [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]]
8. [[Hlin Blunderbeat|Hlin]] dies giving birth to [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] and an older brother
9. [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]] hides as "Tor Ironforge" in the [[Frozen Forest]]
10. Eventually captured by [[Peraxis Phyrexia]]
