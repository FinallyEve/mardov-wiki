# Therzadun Sworn

## Overview

Servants of Therzadun, the god of destruction. **Separate from [[Vecna]] but entangled** — their goals sometimes align, sometimes conflict. Therzadun is sealed behind Iron Doors at the [[Mountain's Zenith]].

## Members

- **[[Reginald Providence]]** — Lich of Therzadun, [[forgesworn|Maghar of the Belt]]. In true form a Kenku, disguised with "plastic surgery." Wore the cloak of the [[Raven Queen]]. Killed by [[Bergs Fireforge|Bergs]] (Ch. 104-105), unleashing the Phoenix maul.
- **Grumble** — Reginald's apprentice and manservant
- **Vellem the Beholder** — Allied with Reginald against the party
- **Silence (Timothy)** — Therzadun's vessel, unmasked by moonbeam (Ch. 105)
- Refurbished Warforged

## Goals & Motivation

- Free Therzadun from behind the Iron Doors
- Use the [[Anchor of War]] for Therzadun's purposes (conflicting with [[Peraxis Phyrexia]]'s goals)

## Key Events

- **Ch. 104-105:** [[Reginald Providence]] killed. His true kenku form revealed. [[Anchor of War]] silenced. Silence/Timothy unmasked as Therzadun's vessel.
- [[Peraxis Phyrexia|Peraxis]] revealed (Ch. 106) that he wanted to make the new Paragon part of a dwarven god, NOT Therzadun — showing the conflict between Vecna's and Therzadun's agents.
