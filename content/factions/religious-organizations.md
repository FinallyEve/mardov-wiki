# Religious Organizations of Mardov

## Silver Scales of Bahamut

> *"All that is Just is not Righteous!*
> *All that is Righteous is not Just!"*

The highest order priesthood of [[Bahamut]]. They judge the world by measuring deeds through two philosophical lenses: Justice and Righteousness.

## Bronze Curators

Nomadic tribe who travel the wastes in the deserts of [[DawnHaven]]. Elders travel the world after 50 years of service, wandering in penance for their shattered pact with [[Ranar Surefoot]].

### Members
- [[Tiberius D. Rex]] — Messenger, spreading the prophecy of [[Bahamut]]

## Winter's Curators

A ruined dragon-kin tribe of [[bynar]]. Their guardianship was broken when the [[Scales of Falak|Yuan-Ti]] stole the [[Anchor of Pestilence]] from the [[Sunless Citadel]].
