## Party Composition (from 2026 production prompts)

The March 2026 cinematic prompts reveal the full party seated at a tavern table:

> "A long tavern table erupting into simultaneous conversation. Nine characters, all talking at once, the warm amber firelight flickering with their movement."

### Seating/Composition Details (from ensemble prompts)
- **Massive golden-scaled dragonborn paladin** -- dominates one end, broad shoulders like a wall, heavy plate armor catching firelight
- **Gnome diviner** -- smallest at the table, barely visible behind tankard, golden-yellow spiky hair
- **Half-ogre** -- enormous frame towering even while seated, hands that could crush a skull
- **Drow monk** -- hood up, separate from the main conversation, obsidian katana resting against the wall
- **Ranger** -- longbow propped against chair
- **Bard** -- emerald cloak, lute resting beside
- **Druid** -- nature-attuned presence
- **Nyx** -- cobalt blue cloak, barn owl companion

### Group Split (from "reunion" prompts)
The party splits into two groups for extended periods:
- **Group A**: Underground/Deep -- lit by bioluminescent blue-green (#0a0015 base, teal accents), mushroom-forest backdrop
- **Group B**: Surface/Ship -- warm amber twilight (#d4a040), Thornberry Shade
