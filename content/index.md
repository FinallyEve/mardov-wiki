## The Campaign at a Glance

### [[chapters/act-1/index|Act 1 -- The Awakening]] (Chapters 0-15)
The party is recruited by the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]], discovers [[Mordred]]'s soul-harvesting, dies and is reborn with [[Sabine Ra]]'s soul fragments, and liberates [[argos]].

### [[chapters/act-2/index|Act 2 -- The Voyage and the Underdark]] (Chapters 17-41)
Sea voyage to the [[Magister's Circle]], Zodiac revelation, Underdark descent, [[forgesworn]] legacy.

### [[chapters/act-3/index|Act 3 -- The Zenith Wars]] (Chapters 42-87)
45+ chapters of parallel play: **Dwarves** (Vag'Mahar, Mountain's Zenith) and **Tieflings/Distillery** (DawnHaven, Cloud City, Fae).

### [[chapters/act-4/index|Act 4 -- The Final War]] (Chapters 88-102)
Craftkeepers and Distillery converge. The Good Guys Guild forms. Guard'Daemon approaches.

### [[chapters/act-5/index|Act 5 -- The Ascension]] (Chapters 104-136)
The endgame: Anchor ascensions, divine conflicts, the final battle for Mardov.
