# Month of the Bat

The end of Winter is marked by the gnashing of teeth and the beating of war drums. Nine out of ten wars in Mardov History have been fought during this season. Dwarves of the Vag Mahar know this day well, it is the day of their founding and the day of the coronation of a new King, the last of which occurred to crown Thorin Ghosteye to be the next Bronzebeard King. This day is known as Bael' Har or roughly translated to "the day the ruby tempers"
The elves of Machu use this season to hold the Grand Tournament of the Damned, where priests of the Goddess of Death sacrifice themselves in one big battle royal in the shadow of the Forest's Zenith. The winner is chosen as a High Priest and is allowed to live within the Crypt of the Moth as an Undying forever. The Court of the Undying stand in judgement over the tournament and choose one among the fallen to revive before all. These warriors are granted a second chance at ascension. As one more join their ranks each year, and none leave. The Tournament has grown to a revived total of 1000 fallen priests. Each year their power grows. Yet they must find enough fighters to participate, and so they must be bred.
The Drow have their own ritual of rebirth during this month. On the darkest night of winter, the Ritual of Rebirth is preformed at the Reflection Stone. There a contingent of Earth Genasi stand vigillent, guarding the Drow artifact until the last Drow King calls upon the souls within the stone. Until then, this Reflection Stone is activated once a year to withdraw the fallen Drow spirits, within. There, if they are deemed Just, their spirits may once again be reborn into this world to answer the call of their king.
