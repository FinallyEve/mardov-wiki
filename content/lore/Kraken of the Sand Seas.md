# Kraken of the Sand Seas

Oswald Magnacog is the third leader to take up the mantle of the Kraken of the Sand Seas. The position named after the need to have a tentacle in everything, is the highest honor in
Qual' Xar
society. Logistically, a nation of privateers is a nightmare to manage. Oswald rose to power by adding his own signature touch, the Teleportation Network! Originally, a tiny hut filled with 3 portals to the capitals of Machu, Dawnpass and Monethern, the network now greatly outnumbers the Transmuter's Covenant in the Magister's Circle!
The original Kraken was the Halfling Thief named Ranar Surefoot who founded the Qual' Xar and authored the Maturin Oath. He did this by offering a reward to his great quest, to play the Terrapin's Song at the ruins of old Uma, now called Tygras Logath.
The second Kraken was a tiefling, one of the Sons' of Tygras who had been exiled from the Magister's Circle for attempting to study magic. His legacy is the still tense relations with the Platinum Seats of the Magister's Circle due to his inclusion of tieflings and other wayward races into the ranks. With his help, many of the unreachable places of Mardov were accessible, and while there would never be a "soul" within the Magister's Circle. Much of Vartos was still open.
