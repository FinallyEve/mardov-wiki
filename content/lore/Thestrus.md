# Thestrus

Storm Giant owner of Thestrus' Anvil Blacksmithery. Typically wears a protective mask over his face. Built Jack's Bane, a massive two handed sword that has the ability to cut any plant, from which a piece of the sword fell off and was crafted into a one handed sword named Splinter as well.
