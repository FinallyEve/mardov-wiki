# Kolyma The Curator

The First and Last True Curator of the Rose. While the Gods of Creation made the Rose, the tribe of beings who existed before were tasked with protecting each petal of the Rose. Kolyma's body can be found within the Tomb of the First Curator on the isle of Vartos. His mind however is within Eden, a demiplane where he awaits Gard'Daemon.
