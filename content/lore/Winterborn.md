# Winterborn

White, Blue and Silver scaled
Dragonborn
Lead by the Nic of the Winterborn, Tumnus Driftwalker. The Winterborn are some of the few dragonborn tribes to still be at near full strength. They reside in a series of settlement at the base of Mt. Bynar, the capital of which is called Iceharbor. They are famous for their yearly migration from Bynar to the Frozen Forest to mark the beginning of Winter. They travel by boat west of Bynar in large family units, during this time the isle of Bynar is completely shut down by the near frozen water that extends for miles out from the coast.
