# Zepheeron The Thunderfury

Bronze Dragon who is in charge of the Apparatus, also spends time volunteering at
the Ice House
, minting
Electrum
. Hunted by the Abisahi who now control
Volcanis the Scorched Tyrant
.
One of the only possible operators of the Apparatus, along with Temptus (Kelik or his father), also requires one of the elemental seals.
