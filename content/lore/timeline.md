# Master Timeline of Mardov

## Ancient History

- [[Vecna]] and [[Sabine Ra]] — original twin souls, divine jealousy begins
- [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] founds Circle of the Murder ([[Mordred|Ramses]]) + [[Artificer's Guild]] ([[Xerxes]])
- [[Mordred|Ramses]] forces lichdom on [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]]; she crystallizes as Demi-Lich in a phylactery
- [[Tygras]] makes a deal with an arch-fey from [[Vecna]]'s plane; creates a weapon to bridge worlds
- [[Ranar Surefoot]] steals the [[Diadem of Dawn]] during the weapon's use; the weapon backfires
- [[Tygras]]'s superweapon destroys [[uma]], creating the desert; [[Tygras]] sealed in [[Harvest's Zenith]]
- The [[Matrim Pact]] formed: human sailors, gnomes of [[Fae'ish]], and [[Qual'Xar]] half-elves
- Covenants of Magic established after [[Tygras]]'s destruction — no being except the Alabaster Seat may possess such power again
- [[Kolyma]] (Last Curator) establishes Zodiac guardianship of the Zeniths
- The [[forgesworn]] order established; [[Thorin the Bronze|Thorin Bronzebeard]] builds the original Warforged army

### Slide 2

Year 0 - The Rose of Creation

The Rose of Creation was the solution to the ever present conflict between Gods of Destruction and Gods of Creation. It is the culmination of countless Gods and Goddesses’ life's work, and is thusly an irresistible target for Destruction. One such petal on the Rose belonged to The Raven Queen of Fate who made a Pact with Vecna to twin their worlds to their mutual benefit.

---

### Slide 3

The Death Pact

The shared domain of Death would serve as the cornerstone of their agreement. This Pact is largely a mystery, however their specialties point to some grand revelation that will occur at the Fated time of the Raven Queen’s transition from Life to Death. This harvest of the limitless potential of the regent Goddess would culminate in a shared Secret with Vecna, God of Death and Secrets. The end result is a mutually beneficial understanding of What is Beyond the Death of Gods? Each believing they would best the other at the moment of the revelation as they were better equipped to take the advantage, the Pact was sealed.

Mardov and Vodram, twinned worlds, were formed and with them 4 Zeniths capable of wielding the power of the domain of Death.

---

### Slide 4

Zeniths

From the Mountains to the Forest, in Winter or in Harvest, the Zeniths of power await their Anchor and Heart.

There will be War and Death, Pestilence and Famine, but to Anchor the end of Guard’ Daemon, the battlefield will be tallied, its victor declared.

For they wield a secret from beyond the grave of gods. Even in the fires of the Forge or the shadows of the Wilde, the light of Faith and the hope of Progenitors shall strike their Fated Heart.

---

### Slide 5

Bringer of Oblivion

Asmodeus, Bringer of Oblivion only took 100 years to find Mardov, and 12 months to signal the first Guard’Daemon. However, to truely seal Asmodeus away, Kolyma, the First and Last Curator of Mardov had to sacrifice himself to activate the Zeniths and turn them against Oblivion. Unable to fully destroy the God, Asmodeus was sealed in thirteen amulets, to be scattered to the guardians on the edges of existence.

---

### Slide 6

Inheritors of the Mantle

In the Death of Kolyma, the potential he captured to answer the Curator’s Plea was bestowed in four mantles.

The Alabaster Mantle of Arcane Magic

The Ivy Mantle of Natural Magic

The Palladium Mantle of Divine Magic

The Crystalline Mantle of Mortal Magic
