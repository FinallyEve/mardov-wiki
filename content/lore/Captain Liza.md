# Captain Liza

Former Captain of the Thornberry Shade, now the proud owner of the Thornberry Wilde. Her Qual'Xar ship has been a base of operations for the party since they first helped her on her voyage from Machu downstream to
Argos
.
Captain Liza claims that where she is from, Tieflings are called Fae-touched and that her and her brother were taken from their original home and traveled to Mardov after spending a few years on a small boat within the FeyWilde. She holds no claim to her past as her last name has not been revealed. Her brother was lost during the journey to Mardov as the two of them were both eaten by a great serpent who's light was the only thing she saw before being consumed. It is said that she wandered within the belly of the beast until she happened upon a panflute which she blew and wished to be rescued from her multi-year Odyssey. Upon using the instrument, she found herself on the surface of the water, to be rescued by Oswald Magnacog, future Kracken of the Sand Seas.
