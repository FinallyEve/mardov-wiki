# Month of the Owl

The middle of Summer, this is the time of the Summer's Solstice. During this time, it is said that the deserts of Dawnpass flower once a year, the whole wasteland becomes an Eden! This is marked by the night of the Solstice which is called Bloom of Tears, coined after the twin rivers whose banks flood to provide the required water. This phenomena is as old as the desert itself.
