# Autumnborn

Bronze and Black scaled
Dragonborn
Lead by the Malik of the Autmnborn, Tiberius D. Rex. The tribe has been all but demolished since the conclusion of the elemental wars and the death of Juliannia. Despite the fact that this group of Dragonborn follow Reynardine, the sudden murder of the ArchFey of the Spring Court struck Tiberius particularly strong as it was on his watch that both courts lost their true leader. Tiberius was sent to wander the deserts of Dawnhaven in a self imposed penance.
