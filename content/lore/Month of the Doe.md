# Month of the Doe

Middle of Autumn, know for the Autumn Equinox which marks the second Harvest Festival of
WindHaven
. This is the time in which spirits are closest to this plane. On Var'taos, the night of the Equinox, the hobgoblins of the Black Hand clan climb the Valley of Anchors to rest among its hot springs and seek favors from their ancestors.
