# Month of the Turtle

Last month of Autumn, there is a memorial held in the realms of men in remembrance of the fall of Huma. This is a time of giving and family. It is said that the call of Mia as she dove beneath the waves would cause the sealife to glow in her wake. The phosporescent coal which mark its ancient path are all thats left of the great turtle's legacy. Pirates and Treasure Hunters believe that it is possible to trace her route and find the Qual'Xar ruin which is rumored to be on her back. The Qual'Xar also track this pathway, however this is the niche goal of the seekers of the Maturin Song.
