# Artifacts & Magic Items of Mardov

## The Four Anchors

### Anchor of Death (Obsidian Sword)
- **Location:** [[Forest's Zenith]], [[machu]]
- **Wielder (Prophesied):** [[Sprocket Overspark]]
- **Description:** A magic sword thrust in the Forest's Zenith since time immemorial. Originally black steel, now appears made of black glass.
- **Mechanics:** Complex curse system. Rolls 1-5 trigger 4d10 force damage (CHA save DC 15 for half). Long rest: CHA check DC 20 to gain control. When controlled: +2 attack/damage, 10 charges for curse effects (Damage Curse: 2d10 force, 1 charge; Weakening Curse: poisoned + debuffs, 2 charges).
- **Lore:** When the Forest and Desert anchors are both severed, the [[Raven Queen]]'s avatar ceases to exist and [[V-Mardov|V-MORDRED]] is forced back.

### Anchor of War (Hammer-Axe-Javelin)
- **Location:** [[Mountain's Zenith]], [[Vag'Mahar]]
- **Wielder (Prophesied):** [[Daina Fireforge]]
- **Description:** A tri-form weapon that can function as a hammer, axe, or javelin. When [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]]'s axe ascends, it becomes the Anchor of War.
- **Lore:** A hammer capable of creating a quake strong enough to sink [[Vag'Mahar]]. The [[Mind Flayer]] controlling all three secret police forces seeks it to breed better Duergar. Silenced in Ch. 104-105.

### Anchor of Pestilence (Pan Flute / Horn of Winter)
- **Location:** [[Winter's Zenith]], [[bynar]]
- **Wielder (Prophesied):** [[Randal]]
- **Description:** The [[Maturin Turtle|turtle]]'s skeleton, activated by the panflute. By resurrecting [[Mia]] with the panflute, her skeleton is given flesh.
- **Spells:** Anti-inspiration (halve positive inspiration to subtract from enemy rolls), Group inspiration (grants inspiration to whole party). Originates from the [[Sunless Citadel]] (a Yuan-Ti fortress in the Shifting Sands).

### Anchor of Famine (Diadem of Dawn)
- **Location:** [[Harvest's Zenith]], [[Tygras Logath]]
- **Wielder (Prophesied):** [[Tesca Duskhallow]]
- **Description:** The [[Diadem of Dawn]] — an artifact which grants the wielder **one wish** while permanently removing their spellcasting ability after the cast. Connected to [[Tygras]]'s original portal weapon.
- **Lore:** Stolen by [[Ranar Surefoot]] ~2000 years ago. Its theft caused [[Tygras]]'s weapon to backfire and destroy [[uma]]. The [[Dusker's Initiative]] hired [[Tesca Duskhallow|Tesca]]'s parents to find its location.

## Named Weapons & Items

### Nightmare (Sentient Longsword)
- **Owner:** [[Nocturnus Wayne]]
- **Origin:** A mixture of the Eye of [[Vecna]] and [[Vecna]]'s own power manifest. Acts similarly to a Lich's phylactery.
- **In [[V-Mardov|Vecna's World]]:** +2/+2, Darkness (level 4), Blindsight in Darkness, Overwhelming Darkness (grapple), Nightmare attack (8d6 damage).
- **In Our World:** +1/+1, Darkness (level 3), Blindsight in Darkness, Overwhelming Darkness (DC 14 grapple), Nightmare attack (6d6 psychic damage).
- **DM Secret:** Nightmare is sentient (STR 10, DEX 10, WIS 16, INT 14, CHA 20). Level 4 caster with Charm Person, Suggestion, Dominate Person. Attempts to forcibly attune to the nearest person each night (contested WIS check). 25% chance of forcing itself drawn when initiative is rolled.
- **Corruption System:** Each kill = 1 corruption point. Greater Restoration or 3 nights unattuned removes 1 point. At 8 points: compulsion to cut off left hand. At 9: compulsion to cut out left eye. At 10: character becomes the dead Eye of [[Vecna]] (NPC).

### The Hand of Ranar Surefoot
- **Lore:** Connected to [[Ranar Surefoot]], who stole the [[Diadem of Dawn]] and initiated the [[Matrim Pact]].

### Shield of Retribution
- **Owner (Prophesied):** [[Stalwart Little Goldentooth]]
- **Location:** [[Sunless Citadel]]
- **Mechanics:** When combined with the [[Heart of the Faith]], allows channeling Divine Magic without being tied to a deity.

### Zodiac Locket
- **Owner (Prophesied):** [[Brigit Wildfire]]
- **Location:** Bottom of the [[Tomb of the Last Curator]]
- **Mechanics:** Grants unlimited shapeshifts (1-minute cooldown), +2 wisdom. Requires all 12 zodiac forms to attune.

### Raven Queen's Cloak
- **Current Status:** Worn by [[Reginald Providence]] (killed Ch. 104-105), then by [[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]]
- **Mechanics:** Grants Luck feat (3 die rolls per long rest) and ability to channel necromancy.

## Extracted Magic Items

### Gloves of the Wolverine

*Source: NFS/Magic Items/Gloves of the Wolverine.docx*

This item currently has 5/10 charges. Its charge_level is 5.

When you hit with a melee weapon attack, you may activate the Gloves of the Wolverine. On activation the wearer is granted a +charge_level bonus to all strength rolls for 10 minutes, additionally the wearer may pick from the following effects:

- The target suffers 1d12 additional damage on hit, see table below for breakdown of charges effects:

1.  1d4 = 2.5 average

2.  1d6 = 3.5 average

3.  1d8 = 4.5 average

4.  1d10 = 5.5 average

5.  1d12 = 6.5 average

6.  2d6 = 7 average (only goes up by a little because it adds a dice which gives it a bigger crit)

7.  2d8 = 9 average

8.  2d10 = 11 average

9.  2d12 = 13 average

10. 3d8 = 13.5 average

- The target must make a DC 10+charge_level STR saving throw or else be knocked prone.

- The target must make a DC 10+charge_level WIS saving throw or else be frightened

- The wearer is granted advantage on strength checks and is granted +charge_level temporary hit points

To gain a charge at the end of a short rest the wearer must have:

1.  Land a hit on an enemy by more than charge_level twice in a single attack action.

2.  Deal 10\*charge_level damage in a single attack action

3.  Grant a teammate advantage on an attack and they land the final blow of a charge_level CR monster.

4.  The wearer crits twice in a single attack action

All of the above actions are deemed, heroic and worthy of praise by the Gloves. The gloves do factor in the damage done when they are activated.


### Fate -- Nightmare Reforged

*Source: NFS/Magic Items/Fate -- Nightmare Reforged.docx*

The voice within Nightmare has now been replaced with Dusken Wayne, the nephew of Nocturnus and son of Oblivion. Upon being fused with the Raven Queen's blessed blade, his trapped spirit was first to volunteer to help Nocturnus on his quest.

+2 to hit / +1 to damage longsword,

May invoke the Web spell using Dusken's concentration slot. DC 16 Dex save.

May invoke Darkness however instead of blindsight it is websense that guides Nocturnus.

Upon killing a creature using Fate, the slain's memories now serve attuned.

The attuned gains access to the accursed spectre Hexblade class feature.

After the spectre's cooldown finishes or the creature is slain the memory is claimed by the Raven Queen. Upon the blade two names are written, Oblivion Wayne and Ashara Everstar.

Extracting memories that pertain to these two individuals is archived within Fate. So killing those who know of Oblivion grants points towards spells like Locate Creature, Legend Lore, or Scrying. Additionally enough memories will unlock a concentration free Hunter's Mark on these targets when within range.


### How to reforge Nightmare

*Source: NFS/Magic Items/How to reforge Nightmare.docx*

Nightmare is a sword of pure evil from Vodram, it is imbued to a hexblade of Vecna and was claimed in Vodram despite being at the height of its power. Despite being in Mardov out of Vecna's control there is still a ... will within the sword and that will wants to use its wielder to anchor Vecna's influence in this world.

Wretch's sword is a sword of good from The Raven Queen herself. The purpose of the sword is to kill an individual in particular: Ashara Everstar. This swords name is Eulogy. Its power is derived around telling the tale of the slain's life, today it grats the slayer the accused specter hexblade ability.

To reforge the swords and unit them into Fate the following conditions must be met:

1.  Must be done by a master craftsmen who is also skilled with soulforging

2.  Must pick a target for Nightmare and convince a slain soul within Nightmare to take on the contract

    a.  Top contender is Dusken Wayne who will not require a persuasion check if the target is Oblivion

3.  May pick a new flavor of magic to deal extra damage in (fire, ice, radiant, force)

    a.  Can be done by adding a magic item or casting a spell of that damage type during the forging process
