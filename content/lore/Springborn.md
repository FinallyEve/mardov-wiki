# Springborn

The Green and Copper Scaled
Dragonborn
Led by the Eld, Gretta Bloomblossum. This tribe of nomads now wanders the deserts outside Bael' Vun and also makes a yearly migration across the nothern border of the desert at the end of Winter to mark the beginning of Spring. However their tribe does not have a pernament settlement on Mardov as the majority of this tribe spends their time among the Spring Court in the Feywilde assisting
Evelyandrin of the Spring Court
in her war for the Glittering Run.
