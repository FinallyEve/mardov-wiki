# Month of the Rabbit

The middle of Winter, across the land they tell tales of the Rabbit, Niven's the white rabbit who is always late. Parent's tell their children that should the rabbit arrive on time, winter will end before the Month of the Wolverine, but should he be delayed by even a single second. Winter would remain. On the night of the Winter Solstice, it is said that the waters of the Tide Pool boil beneath the waves. Wayward sailors of the Serpent Sea often seek refuge from the harsh winter winds by sailing the pools. Should one stay at noon on the Winter's Solstice, their vessel will melt completely. Treasure hunters seek better and better ways by which to fireproof their ships and find out what happens when the whirlpool in the center stops spinning.
