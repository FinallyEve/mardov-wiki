# Story Arcs & Narrative Structure

## The Overarching Story

The campaign revolves around [[Mordred|Ramses/Mordred]], whose plan unfolds in three stages:

1. **Gets the Avatar of the [[Raven Queen]] to sacrifice her immortality** -- this creates a rift between [[Mardov]] and [[V-Mardov]] where [[Vecna]]'s Avatar rules
2. **Collects [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]]'s gifts to control her power** -- originally wanted to resurrect [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]], his lost love, but after her spirit shattered he pivoted to controlling her fragments. Gave up his phylactery to take out the [[Artificer's Guild]], gaining [[Xerxes]]'s body (corrupting the individual beings that make up [[Xerxes]])
3. **Destroys all the keystones** -- with his newfound body, [[Mordred]] has one goal: bring about the invasion of this realm by V-MORDRED by destroying all the keystones

## Act I — The Awakening (Chapters 0-13)

The party is recruited into the [[Scarlet Tunic Brigade]]. They encounter [[Mordred]] possessing [[Finkle Hollingsworth]], [[Sabine Ra]] is freed from her phylactery, and crystalline heart fragments are deposited in the party. Three prophecies are received from [[Ruth]], the Hags, and [[Mia]], establishing the four-zenith framework.

**Key beats:**
- Party death and resurrection by [[Sabine Ra]] (Chapter 2)
- [[V-Mardov]] rift opens; [[Varis Laindon|Varris]] arrives from the alternate reality (Chapter 6)
- First, Second, and Third Prophecies received (Chapters 9, 12, 13)
- [[Mia]] reveals she wiped the party's memories

## Act II — The Voyage (Chapters 14-40)

The party sails aboard the [[Thornberry Shade]], encounters the zodiac spirits (who bet their immortality on the champions), explores the [[Tomb of the Last Curator]], and splits into parallel tracks.

**Key beats:**
- Tiefling prejudice arc; soul glamours for [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] and [[Tesca Duskhallow|Tesca]] (Chapters 17-19)
- Zodiac spirits revealed; they bet their immortality on the champions (Chapter 23)
- Tomb of the Last Curator; four zenith hieroglyphics decoded (Chapters 24-25)
- [[Peraxis Phyrexia]] revealed; [[Reynaldine]]'s network exposed (Chapters 30-31)
- [[Thorkon Fireforge|Thorkon]]'s letter found inside [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]]'s axe; [[forgesworn]] lore revealed (Chapter 34)

## Act III — The Escalation (Chapters 41-86)

Campaign splits into parallel tracks. Individual character arcs deepen.

### The Cloud City Arc ([[windhaven]])

The party arrives in [[windhaven]] via a portal from the [[Kraken of the Sand Seas]]. Key events:

1. **[[Tiberius D. Rex]]** receives a premonition of the party's arrival
2. **Research at the Windmill Willow**: [[Brigit Wildfire|Brigitte]] researches the zodiacs (astral chart showing the 12 animals, one per month); [[Tesca Duskhallow|Tesca]] researches the [[Diadem of Dawn]]; [[Randal]] researches the gods
3. **[[Leonidas of Summer's Last Light]]** puts the party on trial in his sphinx demi-plane:
   - [[Varis Laindon|Varys]] requests help against [[Vecna]], is granted a vision of [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]] grabbing the [[Heart of the Forge]] and jumping into lava
   - [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] requests help to save [[Xyn]], meets [[Juliannia of the Summer Court]] -- her real patron
   - [[Fen Darkeyes|Fen]] wishes he and his brother never fell under the Aboleth's influence; wish granted
   - [[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]] alone is deemed unworthy (meaning unknown)
   - [[Tiberius D. Rex|Tiberious]] has undergone this process 75 times, making him far older than the 76 he appears
4. **The Cabal of Horns**: [[Randal]] and [[Tesca Duskhallow|Tesca]] discover an underground tiefling society led by [[Winnipeg Redvine]], uncovering a child-kidnapping plot by Lady [[Mathia Nuum]]
5. **Reynaldine's Demand**: The party encounters [[Reynaldine|Reynaldine of the Autumn Court]] collecting Elemental Seals. [[Kelik Temptus]] dies when absorbed into his amulet. [[Reynaldine]] already has Fire and Earth seals; tasks the party with retrieving the Water amulet from [[Lapis Poseidon]]
6. **[[Brigit Wildfire|Brigitte]]'s Stag Form**: Discovers her stag form resembles [[Shi-Shi-Gami|Shi Shi Gami]], with antlers forming the [[Zodiac Locket]]
7. **Cloud City Falling**: The party fights to save Cloud City, encounters [[Zepheeron the Bronze]], battles at Jackfall Landing, and is rescued by [[Captain Liza]] on the [[Thornberry Wilde]]
8. **[[Reynaldine]]'s True Nature Revealed**: [[Randal]] uses Legend Lore and discovers [[Reynaldine]] is actually 4 beings: [[Juliannia of the Summer Court]], [[Graz'zt|Grazz't the Demon Prince]], [[Gabriel the Kind]] (Archangel), and [[Reynaldine|Reynardine, Prince of Harvest]]
9. **[[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] meets [[Xyn]]**: Via Lorelai's magical broach, the twin sisters finally communicate. [[Xyn]] reveals [[Gabriel the Kind]] is her patron (lives in teddy bear "Mr. Happy"), that the [[Mountain's Zenith]] opening released [[Barachiel the Righteous]], and that [[Euclid]]'s book *The Origin of Genasi and the Rebirth of the Progenitors* is the key to the [[Harvest's Zenith]]
10. **The Harvest's Zenith spotted**: [[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]] touches the satellite and sees a hanging upside-down city with the [[Harvest's Zenith]] -- a dual pyramid with 7 floors all rotating out of phase

## Global Conflicts

### The Orc-Human War ([[DawnHaven]])

**Location:** [[dawnpass]]/[[windhaven]]/[[Eavallan]] vs [[Stra'Franar]]/[[Fae'ish]]/[[Tygras Logath]]

**Reveal:** At the [[Magister's Circle]], an envoy from [[dawnpass]] (notably containing zero Half-Orcs -- there have been none in the entire game) requests aid against an army of sorcerers wielding draconic-blood-fueled destruction similar to the Tiefling army that destroyed [[uma]].

**Root cause:** The Orc Chieftain and former Gladiator Pit Champion ([[Logath HellEye|Logath]]) made a deal with the same fae demon as [[Tygras]], causing intense bloodlust and madness. This makes him a target for [[Mordred]], who uses Logath's influence to cut off food from the fertile Harvestlands north of [[windhaven]].

**Connection to the Zenith:** This is the location of the [[Anchor of Famine]], tied to the [[Harvest's Zenith]]. The resurgence of Orc sorcerers matches exactly with when it last rained. The [[Matrim Pact]] was between human sailors of old [[uma]], gnomes of [[Fae'ish]], and [[Qual'Xar]] half-elves -- they promised sanctuary atop the [[Maturin Turtle]].

**The Hidden City:** The city is actually a buried pyramid, the great ziggurat housing the [[Harvest's Zenith]]. Inside, [[Tygras]] himself (or the arcane being he became after his failed ascension) has bid his time feeding on internal zenith energies. Destroying his echo rips a hole in the Fae and reveals the fae demon who gave [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] her powers.

**Nyx's Choice:** The demon was the one who made the pact with [[Tygras]], and [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] was his heir. By destroying the last echo of [[Tygras]], future tiefling children can be prevented. [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] inherits the mark of [[Tygras]] (actually Sable's Beacon, an ancient fae marking). She can either rid the world of "demon children" or rebuild the lost empire by adding tieflings to the [[Matrim Pact]] and reviving the guardian turtle.

**Reviving the Guardian:** Requires (1) removing the [[Anchor of Famine]] from the top of the zenith (guarded by the Harvest Effigy), (2) returning the [[Heart of the Progenitors]] to the crevasse in the middle of [[Tygras Logath]], (3) speaking the words: *"On his back all vows are made, a haven for the continent of dawn paid."* Upon finishing, the turtle skeleton below the bay is revived -- a massive turtle carrying an Atlantean city, serving as THE landing place between [[Mardov]] and the [[Feywild]].

## Character Arc Themes

### [[Sprocket Overspark]] — The Pursuit of Fun

**Question:** Is it possible for responsibility to be fun?

[[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]]'s fate as the next Avatar of the [[Raven Queen]] cannot be downplayed. No matter how he tries, this is his overarching conflict: *"You cannot run from your fate."* To learn this is to understand what it means to become the next [[Raven Queen]].

His rise of infamy: Trixie -> [[Thornberry Shade]] -> [[Qual'Xar]] -> [[Magister's Circle]] & [[Conquis Abbey]] -> Entire Material Plane. The deeds he deems "fun" provide notoriety, making it harder to be underestimated.

### [[Brigit Wildfire]] — Self-Discovery

**Question:** What makes me special?

[[Brigit Wildfire|Brigitte]] is a [[Progenitors|Progenitor]] -- one of the original beings created in Eden. This grants her the ability to open the [[Harvest's Zenith]] without destroying the elemental seals. There is no reason for this other than fate -- the Progenitors were fated to return to help stop [[Gard'Daemon]].

### [[Randal]] — Apotheosis and Pacifism

**Question:** What does it take to become a god?

[[Randal]]'s decision to become a pacifist is valiant but ultimately flawed, for he claimed victory while he still has goals outstanding. His ideal: *"Victory is only satisfying if achieved without violence."*

Foils to his pacifism:
- *"Violence is Victory"* -- [[Logath HellEye|Logath]], Orcish Leader
- *"Peace is what I will achieve after all my enemies have been defeated by my hand"* -- [[Evelyandrin of the Spring Court]]
- *"Some things are worth fighting for"* -- [[Reynaldine]]
- *"Nothing is worth fighting for, I will endure and adapt"* -- [[Grumblesin of the Winter Court]]
