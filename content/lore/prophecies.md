# Prophecies & Zodiac Symbolism

## The Three Prophecies

### First Prophecy — Ruth (Argos, Chapter 9)

> *"THE ANCHORS FALL, THE REALMS ASUNDER*
> *HEARTS OF STONE GATHERED FROM PLUNDER*
> *DEATH INCARNATE KNOCKS AT THE DOOR*
> *BELOW THE BLOOD STAR BEGINS THE FINAL WAR!"*

**Decoded:**
- Vision of what happens inside each zenith — some event has caused this
- Each of the 4 zeniths contain a heart and an anchor; the hearts are being stolen
- [[Vecna]] is death incarnate, and he is coming
- [[Gard'Daemon]] is said to follow a [[Blood Star]]

### Second Prophecy — The Hags (Chapter 12)

> *"DOUBLE BUBBLE TOIL AND TROUBLE,*
> *GARD'DAEMON IS NIGH, THE FIRST ZENITH IS RUBBLE,*
> *PREPARE THE PESTILENCE WITH CHILDREN'S SOULS,*
> *THE HUSKS REMAIN UNTIL THEY'RE TOLD,*
> *DOUBLE BUBBLE TOIL AND TROUBLE,*
> *ANCHORS LIFTED AND HEARTS ARE SMUGGLED"*

**Decoded:**
- The [[Forest's Zenith]] has already been plundered by [[Mordred]] — he had the [[Heart of the Wilde]] and used it to awaken the Forest Guardian (the zenith boss)
- The pestilence is comprised of children's souls — pointing to a crisis of faith
- Anchors must be lifted and recharged at the [[Valley of Anchors]]

### Third Prophecy — MIA (Chapter 13)

Four verses mapping to four Zeniths:

**Harvest Verse:**
> *"As the rain falls on the fallow harvest, and the matrim pact fulfilled, the anchor of famine shall be lifted, the echo of Tygras stilled."*

The people of [[dawnpass]] are under siege; their harvest has rotted due to supernatural drought. The party must find the turtle and play the terrapin song. The echo of [[Tygras]] is the fae-fiend controlling [[Logath HellEye|Logath]]. The [[Anchor of Famine]] is the [[Diadem of Dawn]].

**Winter Verse:**
> *"As the snow drifts over the ruined tribe, and serpent plagues the sea, the anchor of pestilence shall awake, to answer the curators' plea."*

The ruined tribe is the [[Winter's Curators]]. The serpent is a fae eel that swallowed the [[Heart of the Faith]]. Must be recovered to restore order to [[Conquis Abbey]].

**Mountain Verse:**
> *"As the fire bellows on the forgotten forge, and the blood star is found, the anchor of war shall be silenced, as the new paragon is crowned."*

The forgotten forge is the [[First Forge]]. The [[Blood Star]] killed [[Thorson Bronzebeard II]]. The three [[forgesworn|Thane]] secret police forces are all run by the same Mind Flayer. The new paragon is [[Dorrin Hammer]] — the only one who can build/control Warforged.

**Forest Verse:**
> *"As the sunlight warms the forest gate, and madness prisoner freed, the anchor of death shall sway, to bring peace to starcrossed three."*

Must use a sun-reflecting mirror to reach the zenith. Free the king of [[machu]] from madness. The starcrossed three are [[Xerxes]], [[Mordred|Ramses]], and [[Sabine Ra]].

### Tarot Divination (Chapter 26)

Before the divinations began, four tarot cards were drawn with [[Ruth]] and Sheila at the magic giant elk, using his antlers to answer questions. Every time something was right, the papers or words would light up.

The four cards: (1) A tiger, signifying the wind, (2) A forge, signifying creation, (3) A holy knight, signifying faith, (4) A family, signifying [[Progenitors]].

## The Prophecy of the Origin of Genasi and Rebirth of Progenitors

> *"As the Hearts are shattered the planes shall follow.*
> *As the ages turn, the Progenitors shall fade, the harvest fallow.*
> *Once fate takes the path to [[Gard'Daemon|Guard Daemon]], may the pieces be reforged*
> *The price is great, the soul must be fractured for ice, fire, air and earth*
> *Famine locked behind a door of carved iron"*

This prophecy connects the shattering of the [[Heart of the Wilde|Hearts]] to the fading of the [[Progenitors]] and the coming of [[Gard'Daemon]]. The four elements (ice, fire, air, earth) correspond to the four [[Genasi]] bloodlines whose origins are tied to the Zeniths. The "door of carved iron" references the [[Anchor of Famine]] ([[Diadem of Dawn]]) locked within the [[Harvest's Zenith]].

## The Prophecy of the Witches of The Wild

Three stanzas, each witnessed at different moments of the campaign:

### Stanza I — [[Frozen Song]] (Witnessed at [[Sesame Spire]])

> *"What was lost shall now be found,*
> *Entombed in rock which once grew from the ground.*
> *A gift handed down through hands of four,*
> *Only to be returned to he who lost it before.*
> *The power to break the mountains spine,*
> *Frozen in the forest for all to find*
> *The mantle of power thou shall that bear,*
> *The burden of war can still be prepared"*

### Stanza II — [[Birt]] (Witnessed within [[Asmodeus|Oblivion]]'s Throneroom)

> *"On the first day of journey when their adventure seemed so small,*
> *Death found them, yet life followed*
> *On that day the magic of the wilds spoke in three of [[Gard'Daemon|Guard'Daemon]]*
>
> *On the last day of beginnings when cities of the sky fell and cities of the ground rose,*
> *War found them, yet allies followed*
> *On that day the the magic of the other spoke in three of those who could stop it*
>
> *On the first day of Winter when the queens who bind him are recrowned,*
> *Famine found them, yet strength followed*
> *On that day the magic of the progenitors spoke in three of the weapons they would wield*
>
> *On the last day of Winter when the wielder calls upon anchor's horn,*
> *Pestilence found them, yet awakening followed*
> *On that day the magic of the divine spoke in threes of the feats they must accomplish"*

This stanza maps each Horseman to a moment in the campaign's timeline and names the source of power that awakens at each: the **wilds** (Natural), the **other** (Arcane), the **progenitors** (Fae), and the **divine** (Radiant). The "threes" correspond to the three roles at each Zenith: Controller, Champion, and Anchor Wielder.

### Stanza III — The Next Prophecy

> *"War above, war below,*
> *Fourth opened, never closed.*
> *The horn of Winter, reclaimed from snow,*
> *The second harbinger from the past ago,*
> *Wild Magic foretells its path,*
> *Borrowed power returned at last,*
> *A hero's grave, the place it rests,*
> *[[Fenrir]]'s bargain put to the test"*

The "horn of Winter" is the [[Anchor of Pestilence]] (the pan flute). [[Fenrir]] is the [[Wolf|Wolf Zodiac]], tied to [[Bergs Fireforge|Bergs]]. The "fourth opened, never closed" refers to the [[Harvest's Zenith]], which once opened by [[Tygras]] was never fully sealed.

## The Tomb of the Last Curator — Zenith Hieroglyphics

In the [[Tomb of the Last Curator]], the party found four rooms containing hieroglyphics describing the four Zeniths — what has happened, what is happening, and what has yet to come. Each room contained a riddle.

### The Forest Room — [[Anchor of Death]] in [[machu]] (Green Eye of [[Vecna]])

Hieroglyphics depicting:
- A seven-point crown made of sludge, dripping to the floor, creating a small island for a throne
- A triad in ancient garb: one female with raven-black hair, two men — one with unkempt curly black hair, the other with long white hair pulled back ([[Sabine Ra]], [[Mordred|Ramses]], [[Xerxes]])
- A large antlered beast with a glow-stone in its head (the [[Stag Zodiac|Stag]])
- An open doorway bathed in moonlight; the same building without the doorway and without the moon
- Two crystalline shapes flanking a pool — one beneath the water, one above
- A [[Lich]], reaching for a [[Doe Zodiac|doe]]
- A great elk with a locket suspended between its antlers (the [[Zodiac Locket]])
- A [[Rabbit Zodiac|rabbit]] with its head buried in a book, holding a phylactery in one hand and a scythe in the other ([[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]])
- The doe and the elk standing side by side above a forest of mangrove trees

**Riddle:** *"I am the fate of all things, large and small / Many run from me, though I eventually find them all. / Who am I?"* — Answer: **Death**

### The Mountain Room — [[Anchor of War]] in [[Vag'Mahar]] (Red Eye of [[Vecna]])

Hieroglyphics depicting:
- A city with three massive walls carved in the pattern of mechanical dwarves, the largest with a hole in his heart
- A dwarf with red hair, older, missing his right eye ([[Thorkon Fireforge]])
- A crest with a hammer and an anvil ([[bronzebeard]] crest)
- A large bat hanging on a stalagmite; below, a wretched woman drinking what drips from the bat ([[Oblivion Wayne]] and the [[Bat Zodiac]])
- A [[Wolf Zodiac|wolf]] and a [[Wolverine Zodiac|wolverine]], both standing beside a frozen volcano ([[Bergs Fireforge|Bergs]] and [[Daina Fireforge|Daina]])
- A massive forge worked by a glowing red-eyed giant, transparent, with a smaller gaunt old dwarf inside, unconscious
- A shadowy figure on the wall behind a man at a modest forge
- A tentacle rising from a chasm
- All sitting above two massive clashing armies

**Note:** The symbol on the tentacle creature's head matches the symbol between the [[bronzebeard]] districts — the mark of the first Bronzebeard who united the clans.

**Riddle:** *"Children and rich men play me like a game, / But poor men fear even the whisper of my name. / What am I?"* — Answer: **War**

### The Winter Room — [[Anchor of Pestilence]] in [[bynar]] (Blue Eye of [[Vecna]])

Hieroglyphics depicting:
- A serpent with an angler fish lure, enormous enough to swallow ten people (the [[Serpent Zodiac|Serpent]])
- A tribe of snow-colored dragon-born surrounding a great rock (the [[Winter's Curators]])
- A large turtle with a pan flute in the pattern on its back, head and legs tucked within the shell ([[Mia|Maturin Turtle]])
- A great white dragon clutching a blue gemstone ([[Frostragan]] and the [[Heart of the Faith]])
- A dark shadow lurking behind all the townsfolk
- A shield with an inlay for a large gemstone, vacant, wielded by a [[Mongoose Zodiac|mongoose]] (the [[Shield of Retribution]], destined for [[Stalwart Little Goldentooth|Stalwart]])
- A grand citadel in the desert with sunlight all around, but a cloud in the shape of a cobra over the building (the [[Sunless Citadel]])
- A frozen fortress guarded by giants wearing a five-dragonhead tabard

**Riddle:** *"I ride on the wind and roll like a wave, / Those who know me have one foot in the grave. / What am I?"* — Answer: **Pestilence**

### The Harvest Room — [[Anchor of Famine]] in [[Tygras Logath]] (Orange Eye of [[Vecna]])

Hieroglyphics depicting:
- An upside-down city, its gardens glowing faintly (the city on the [[Maturin Turtle]])
- A snow-white tiefling girl with two tall shadows looming behind, both with great owl wings ([[Xyn]] and her patrons)
- A classic red tiefling shaking the hand of a man in a hat and cane — the cane touches the ground but his feet do not ([[Tygras]] and [[Reynardine]])
- A [[Fox Zodiac|fox]] with silver eyes, wings folded from its back ethereal, flowing backward to form the tail ([[Irethia]])
- A large building with suspended orbs revolving around three larger towers, cracked but functional, ancient and brand new (the [[Harvest's Zenith]])
- A woman wearing a headdress on a horse before an army, without a sword; across from her an orc male, pointing at her
- Three mice around a flaming mousetrap — one safe, two burning ([[Tesca Duskhallow|Tesca]])
- A well overflowing with water, a diadem just below the surface (the [[Diadem of Dawn]])
- A burned field patrolled by nightmarish scarecrows and obsidian guards, thunderclouds in the distance

**Riddle:** *"You can read me between the line on many, / Though to find me yourself, you must wade through nothing a plenty. / What am I?"* — Answer: **Famine**

## The Twelve Zodiacs — Kolyma's Twelve

Twelve cosmic animal spirits who serve the [[Raven Queen]]. Each is tied to a player character, a Zenith, and a narrative purpose. The Zodiacs bet their immortality on the champions during [[Gard'Daemon]] — succeed and they ascend, fail and they face eternal death.

| Rabbit | Nivens / Inaba | [[Sprocket Overspark]] |
| Stag | Shi-Shi-Gami / Scythia | [[Brigit Wildfire]] |
| Doe | Lilly | [[Varis Laindon]] |

**Key Events:**
- The Stag (Shi-Shi-Gami) was **captured** by [[Peraxis Phyrexia]]'s Illithid
- Gifted its horn to [[Grenda Far'Tul]] to deliver to [[Brigit Wildfire|Brigitte]]
- [[Peraxis Phyrexia|Peraxis]] suspects the Stag can "truly divine the future"
- Plan: use the Stag as vessel for [[Vecna]]'s latest creation

## Key Zodiac Artifacts

- **[[Zodiac Locket]]** — At the bottom of the [[Tomb of the Last Curator]]. Grants unlimited shapeshifts. [[Brigit Wildfire|Brigitte]]'s ultimate artifact.
- **Stag's Horn** — Contains a projection of [[Vecna]]'s entire council meeting when placed on a magic circle.
- **Bat Zodiac** — Weaponized by [[Ebon Wayne]] to create the vampire hierarchy.
