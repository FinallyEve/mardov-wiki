# Characters from the Point of View of NPCs

How the party looks from the outside -- through the eyes of those who watch them.

---

## As Seen By: [[Kraken of the Sand Seas|Oswald Magnacog, Kraken of the Sand Seas]]

**Who he sees:** Upstarts from the town where one of the oddest captains in his fleet was last seen -- and his daughter is onboard.

**What they did:**
- A gnome wizard ([[Sprocket Overspark|Sprocket]]) used a gift earned by saving his daughter from death on another plane to squash an aboleth in the underdark
- The destruction of the Aboleth led to a massive upjump of treasure -- and they weren't even worried about being split from their friends
- They identified and exposed the Drab Trade hidden within his own city
- A young human bard ([[Randal]]) enacted justice and earned his own [[Qual'Xar]] ship, becoming a captain; his crew includes a previously corrupt member of a treasonous sect
- The bard and the wizard were selected and given a 1/10th chance of being the most magic of beings
- The wizard has the favor of the Kraken's daughter
- The bard is obsessed with the source of his power: Vandelkin Tech
