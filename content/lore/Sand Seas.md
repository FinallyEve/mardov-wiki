# Sand Seas

The headquarters for the
Qual' Xar
Nation. Founded by the Third
Kraken of the Sand Seas
, Oswald Mangacog, the whole city is rumored to behave like a mirage under his whim.
The Dunes serve as a field of portals with preprogrammed "guides" to teach newcomers the new rules while within the city. The first of which is that nowhere in the Sand Seas requires travel to find. Instead the citizen may visualize where they need to go and the sand crabs will deliver traveler there at incredible speeds!
Of note, the party did find an underground trade of Drab slaves within the inn at the center of the market. It seems that the former free-willed people are in high demand as cheap labor, an unfortunate side effect of the spell plague which is tearing its way across Mardov.
