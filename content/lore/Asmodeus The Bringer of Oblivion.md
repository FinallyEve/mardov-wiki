# Asmodeus The Bringer of Oblivion

The strongest God of Destruction. His march towards total Oblivion is so relentless that it led to the creation of the Rose, a feat which required EVERY God of Creation to lend their power. Asmodeus made it his solemn vow to destroy the Rose and put and end to being.
Requiring a single year to completely eradicate his own Petal of Existence, this deity is a threat to all. Good and Evil, Righteous and Cruel, all fear this beings presence. Few things in nature are as constant as Asmodeus' crusade. Little is known of this entity in large part due to his overwhelming destruction of any trace of his victims. His motive is are unclear, however his methods are consistent.
Asmodeus as an entity prefers to multitask by seeding his blood on petals which compete with itself to decide which of the fiends that crawl out his being are worthy of being possessed by him and wielding his power of destruction. It is said that entities worthy of this are granted access to the paradise that he himself has dictated would be the last thing he destroys. Even other Gods of Destruction have decided to submit to the will of Asmodeus for a chance to join the ranks of his elite and reside there. For the more pragmatic in the universe, this is seen as the highest form of self-preservation. Afterall, if you can't beat 'em, join 'em. And NONE have even come close to beating Asmodeus.
Sure, he's been banished from countless petals. But his return is as inevitable as the laws of physics. They can be ... postponed but never rewritten. He is the death that ends even death.
