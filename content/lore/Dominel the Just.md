# Dominel the Just

Rival of
Barachiel The Righteous
, believes in Justice over all. A member of the Triumvirate of Archangels and currently inside the Winter's Zenith.
He carries a book with an eye on the cover and is adorned with a platinum crown. The only time the party has come into contact with this entity has been within the Shadowfell surrounded by a whirlpool of wayward souls. When Sunlight in a Springtime Glade first met his being he was bragging about having plucked him away from his patron, Barachiel the Righteous. In the eyes of this entity death without lasting consequence is a death without JUSTICE. As such, Glade was returned to life, that was inevitable. However, he would be changed by the experience. The change would be the removal of Barachiel's prize for the pact, the dark eye which watches over Glade. Then the tabaxi would be changed to a race of creatures that Barachiel finds the least righteous, a tiefling. However, Dominel does not find subjecting Glade to the influence of an entity he has no notion of, and removes the dark passenger that follows all tieflings from Glade's person. Lastly Glade himself would be given a name more... appropriate of his new identity. However, Sunlight in a Springtime Glade is fated by the Raven Queen and even the power of an Archangel could not alter it.
And so this entity remained there with the Glade's Echo of Tygras having challenged his rival's devotion to righteousness.
