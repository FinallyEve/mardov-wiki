# Euclid

An Owl Aarakocra Wizard who works closely with the Owl Zodiac, Archimedes. A very old individual who has been around since the creation of the Celestial Realm. He got his start as a fledgling archivist working under Gabriel the Kind and worked his way up the ladder to become Gabriel's Keeper of the Divine Seal.
He did so under the condition that he would be safe from Fiends and so he was taught a Forbiddence Spell to keep him safe. Prior to the Collapse of the 7 Heavens, Euclid was approached by a younger and curious Reynardine of the Autumn Court, whom he saw a spark within and agreed to let the Fey Prince study magic under him. Together they spent countless years learning magic and researching the Progenitors. A book of note that the two of them had an argument over was the Origin of Genasi, a tome which indicated the power of the Elemental Evils and the emergence of the Genasi who rose from the ashes of the calamity they caused. During this time, Reynardine would spend large amounts of time out on missions to recover artifacts for Euclid, including donating his own pan-flute which he traded Euclid for safe keeping. In thanks, he offered the Aarakocra a refuge among his people once he assumed his rightful place as FeyLorde of the Autumn Court. "I have entrusted you a power greater than the moon, and in payment for your help guarding it, you will be granted from me a favor and the moon"
Euclid's Library on the moon was named Alexandria, though he would not take up residence there for some time, the library was tethered to the aarakocra's ancestral home on an isle of clouds, Laputa Town. When the 7 Heaven's fell, Euclid retreated back to Alexandria and entrusted Archimedes to transfer the Forbiddence Spell over to their new refuge. During the Elemental Wars that followed, Euclid made an agreement with the old Air Genasi General and follower of the fallen Reynardine that he would allow Laputa Town to be anchored to the newly formed Cloud City under the condition that he be allowed to modify the Forbiddence Spell into a contract that could only be undone by The Ragnarok Protocol. The new Forbiddence Spell would now apply to Reynaldine of the Autumn Court, the being who now inhabited the once spirited Fey Prince. That would apply to the entirety of Cloud City which would be allowed to use the Immovable Rod central to Laputa Town as a contingency plan in case the king were to die. This agreement occurred as a coalition between the Storm King Terrell Temptus, Euclid, and
Leonidas of Summer's Last Light
. All three having reached a peace treaty at the end of the conflict. The Fire Genasi agreed to hand over the Water Genasi princess, Windhaven's Harvest Festival was created as a tithe to the Spring Court, and Leonidas was granted a sanctum which would preserve Juliannia's last moments forever.
Recently, the Ragnarok Protocol was activated, removing the Forbiddence Spell from Alexandria, Cloud City and WindHaven. Stalwart, Nyx and Brigit (using her Owl Zodiac Form) were able to re-activate the spell, however Xyn with her patron
Mr. Happy
stayed behind to finish charging the spell.
