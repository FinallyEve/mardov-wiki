# Cosmology of Mardov

## The Divine Conflict — The Cosmic Engine

The entire world of [[Mardov]] is driven by one act of divine jealousy. [[Vecna]] and [[Sabine Ra]] were the original **twin souls** — both gods of death, but [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] also ruled life and fate. [[Vecna]]'s envy of that additional dominion is the cosmic engine driving every conspiracy in Mardov.

[[Sabine Ra|Sabine]]'s avatar (the [[Raven Queen]]) founded two colleges of magic:
- The **Circle of the Murder** (necromancy/death) — entrusted to her teacher [[Mordred|Ramses]]
- The **[[Artificer's Guild]]** (animancy/life) — entrusted to her husband [[Xerxes]]

[[Mordred|Ramses]] grew jealous and attempted to grant both himself and [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] eternal life through lichdom. When [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] rejected the ritual, [[Mordred|Ramses]] forced it on her. [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] starved her lich form by refusing to consume souls, crystallizing into a Demi-Lich trapped inside a phylactery for centuries.

This created the fracture that [[Vecna]] exploits across the entire campaign.

## Apotheosis — The Path to Godhood

Every deity in the pantheon was the first to uncover a particular universal truth:

| Deity | Universal Truth |
|-------|----------------|
| [[Bahamut]] | "True Justice cannot be attained without Righteousness" |
| Avandra | "To travel is to change" |
| [[Asmodeus]] | "All beings serve." |
| [[Tiamat]] | "Anything may be obtained" |
| [[Vecna]] | "There is no safer place for secrets than a grave" |
| [[Raven Queen]] | "All things serve fate, even death" |

Once a being has uncovered their truth, they may undergo **Apotheosis** — the process of ascending to godhood:

1. **The Miracle**: The being must perform a miracle in service of their ideal. The manner and method determines their domain.
   - Example: [[Vecna]] was a lich; his miracle was the destruction of all living knowledge in his prime plane. The [[Raven Queen]]'s miracle was to determine this fate beforehand and change it — she created the [[shadowfell]] for souls whose fate was unfinished. Thus the stalemate: [[Vecna]] owns the secrets of the grave, yet not all who die go to oblivion.
2. **The Sacrifice**: The being must sacrifice their mortal form and assume responsibility of a **Petal of the Rose**.
   - This invokes the ire of the **Gods of Destruction**
   - Each petal can create infinite more, but each new petal is a shadow of the original — this is why [[vodram]] and [[Mardov]] are twin worlds, spawned from the same prime plane
   - The original split was between the **Gods of Creation** and the **Gods of Destruction** on the plane known as **Prime**

### Reynaldine's Apotheosis Plan

[[Reynaldine|Reynaldine of the Autumn Court]] seeks to collapse all pocket planes (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Material, [[Feywild]], [[shadowfell]], 7 Heavens, 9 Hells) within his domain to gain absolute power over them all. This will be his miracle. His ideal: *"Fate is the death of freedom."* His domain will be Knowledge and Trickery. Once all planes are collapsed, he seeks to segment everything into a Matrix-like paradise.
