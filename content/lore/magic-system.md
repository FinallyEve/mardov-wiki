# Magic System of Mardov

## The Watchful Order

The [[Watchful Order]] is a guild of magicians who govern, teach, and occasionally put down forbidden forms of magic. They serve as the primary regulatory body for magical practice across [[Mardov]].

### Permitted Magic
- Standard elemental magic (fireball, illusions)
- Standard cleric miracles (healing the sick)
- Any magic not associated with forbidden deities

### Forbidden Magic

| School | Associated Deity | Domain |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| Necromancy | [[Raven Queen]] | Death |
| Animancy | [[Vecna]] | Evil secrets, soul manipulation |
| Tyranny / Mind Control / Charm | [[Asmodeus]] | Tyranny |
| Destruction / Chaos | Gruumsh | Uncontrolled devastation |

## The Monkey's Paw Principle

> In the laws of Mardov's universe, **nothing can be gained for free**.

All magic items carry hidden costs:
- **Bag of Holding** — some small percentage of items get lost in the aether between uses
- **Belt of Giant Strength** — causes disadvantage on charm spells (giants are dumb)
- **All Forgesworn creations** — a bit of the creator's soul is imparted in each creation

Dark magic objects are often worshipped by cults and mistaken for deities.

## The Covenants of Magic

After [[Tygras]]'s superweapon destroyed [[uma]], covenants were established to prevent any being (save the [[Magister's Circle|Alabaster Seat]]) from possessing that amount of power again. The [[Magister's Circle]] enforces these covenants through the Platinum Seats.

## The Spell Plague

### Origin
Discovered by [[Ebon Wayne]] (husband of [[Oblivion Wayne]], Vampire Queen of the Drow). Described by [[Alhoon Ren]] as "nothing short of pure genius."

### Mechanism
Without the [[Heart of the Faith]], even humans undergo a "crisis of faith." The plague has an unknown origin — possibly from another plane. Its original purpose has become impossible to determine.

### Research Chain (Alhoon Ren's Discoveries)
1. Ground Umber Hulk eyes + [[Oblivion Wayne]]'s blood = restores individual thought to Illithid hosts
2. Vampirism is the key to liberating mindflayer puppets from the Elder Brain
3. Next generation of Alhoon can now be Ullitharid (elite Illithid)
4. Combined with the Spell Plague: *"dawn of a new empire"*
5. Some humans are immune to the Spell Plague

### Supply Chain
- Hags brew Pestilence from corrupted children's souls (aged 4, developing souls)
- Supply wagons deliver pestilence to [[rimefang]]
- [[rimefang]] ordered to "open the [[Winter's Zenith]] and finish the Spellplague"
- [[Reynaldine]] threatens to disrupt supply lines as leverage against [[Vecna]]

### Who Benefits
- [[Peraxis Phyrexia]] — defeats the dwarves
- [[Oblivion Wayne]] — liberates Illithid for vampire army
- [[Vecna]] — destroys Winter's Zenith barrier

### Who Fights It
- The party
- [[Conquis Abbey]] (Heart of the Faith delivery)
- The [[Winter's Curators]] (ruined dragon-kin tribe)

## The Forgesworn Craft

The [[forgesworn]] (Mag'har) are an ancient order of dwarven weaponsmiths. Their craft carries the heaviest cost of all magic in Mardov:

> **A bit of yerself is imparted in each of yer creations.** There is nothing worth more than yer soul. NOTHING.
> — [[Thorkon Fireforge]]

### The 12 Types of Fireforged Items
Each of the 12 [[forgesworn|Maghar]] specializes in a different item type:

| Maghar | Specialty | Known Holder |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Maghar of the Axe | Axes | [[Thorkon Fireforge]] |
| Maghar of the Hammer | Hammers | [[Hlin Blunderbeat]] |
| Maghar of the Breastplate | Armor | [[Bartrim Hadrin]] |
| Maghar of the Gear | Mechanisms | [[Decan Smithrunner]] |
| Maghar of the Sword | Swords | Evelyn "SteelHardt" Hadrin |
| Maghar of the Belt | Belts | [[Reginald Providence]] (Therzadun Sworn) |

### Warforged
The most extreme expression of Forgesworn craft. Warforged consume **dwarven souls** when created — each machine is a melding of a sacrifice and a great portion of the creator. Only the [[forgesworn]] can create them. [[Dorrin Hammer]] is the only one who can build/control them in the current era.
