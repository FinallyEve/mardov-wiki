# Balazar Keerin

The Archbishop of Bahamut and only Platinum
Dragonborn
. Serves on the
Council of Curators
. He rules from his seat within the Parthenon of Conquis Abbey from which he serves as a moderator between the Scales of Justice and the Scales of Righteousness. Balazar is effectively ageless and is charged with protecting the Heart of the Faith... which was recently stolen.
