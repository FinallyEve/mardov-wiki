# Month of the Fox

The Beginning of Winter, marked by the great migration of
Winterborn
from their villages in Bynar down south through the Frozen Forest of Northern Monethern. There is a large group of musicians who typically create a song of travel by playing a symphony from lanternpost to lanternpost. The Winterborn lend their voices in a song similar to the one posted. It is said that if one manages to whitness the whole symphony at the same time that they would hear the Lamentation of a Goddess, The First Death of Her Creation.
On Bynar, this begins the season-long blizzard which constantly pours from deep within the Dragon's Nest Mountains. This blizzard makes living on the continent all but impossible for most. The great migration is half born out of necessity and half a tradition on behalf of their creation.
