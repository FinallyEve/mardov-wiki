# The Twelve Zodiacs of Mardov

Twelve cosmic animal spirits who serve the [[Raven Queen]]. Each is tied to a player character, a Zenith, and a narrative purpose. The Zodiacs bet their immortality on the champions during [[Gard'Daemon]] -- succeed and they ascend, fail and they face eternal death. Originally established by [[Kolyma]], the First and Last Curator.

## Twin Souls and the Zodiac

The entire campaign revolves around twin souls because the goddess and god of death were the original twin souls:
- [[Vecna]] and [[Sabine Ra]] -- the ORIGINALS. They share a domain. [[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] is also goddess of life, which [[Vecna]] was always jealous of.
- [[Daina Fireforge]] and [[Bergs Fireforge]] -- when twin souls find each other, their combined power increases (the stat with the highest combined bonus gets +1 when they train together)
- [[Nyx Khamestria]] and [[Xyn]] -- [[Nyx Khamestria|Nyx]] was born with her sister as a cyst; the arch-fey took that soul in exchange for powers

[[Sabine Ra|Sabine]] as goddess of life and death is also the goddess of fate -- her powers are pre-ordained. Your time comes when it comes.
