# Firekeeper Morn Loregramar

Elder Orcish Shaman of the Earth Mother. Of the Red Hands of Faeish and credited with finding the Children of Eavallan Forest, sparking a war between the Elves within and the Orcish people. Husband of the leader of the Faeish Tribe, Commander Barberum Loregramar.
His religion has iconography that pertains to the Twelve Zodiacs. Long ago he met a wild human woman attempting to steal a meal from him who he named "Wild Spirit of Fire." Young Brigit took that name to heart and adopted it as her last name, Wildfire.
A kind spirit who is at odds with the raiding nature of the orcish culture. He seems to follow a way of life that is more akin to the Druids than the murder and pillaging that his people find themselves in. Recently recovered by the party as he was attempting to rescue
The Amazing Dr. Grackel
from a brutal fate.
His favorite phrase is Lok'Tava which means "The Earth Mother Provides." This phrase was the password for a druid's grove within the Vodram Feywilde belonging to a Tribe of Progenitors.
When asked, he claimed that the orcs were within Cloud City following the prophecy recovered when his people captured the Children of Eavallan Forest, thanks to a fire spirit! In the prophecy, it claimed that the orcish people would bring down the sky as a way to end a great famine among his people. This was pointed out by a Human Witch who took the form of a great snake.
