# Month of the Stag

This month is marked as the beginning of Autumn, when the leaves begin to fall and form a blanket across the Forests of Monethern. This is also known as the Witching Month, it is said that nights spent within the Eavallan Forest under a moonless sky is to invite the ghosts of ones past to visit. The name for this phenomena is Vespar which is elvish for Haunting.
