# Month of the Pangolin

The end of Summer, marked by the resurgence of the
Summerborn
Dragonborn
who host a Sundance festival nesteled between the banks of the Tears. At Goldentooth Village, the festival has a large banquet and burning of a great Pangolin who is said to have rescued the Summerborn following their tribe losing their home. On this day, the Summerborn share their food with all travellers and urge them to rest themselves in the artificial hotsprings created for the occasion.
