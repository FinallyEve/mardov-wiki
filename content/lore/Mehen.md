# Mehen

Mehen is a psuedodragon hatched from an egg which was taken off of Mathia Nuum, a noblewoman from Windhaven and secret Yuan-ti Cultist from the Scales of Falak. The egg is one of many which were entrusted to high ranking members of the Scales of Falak, legend being that it will hatch to the chosen of Falak, the Serpent Zodiac. However when the egg was recovered by the party following the defeat of Mathia, the egg remained dormant. It wasn't until the egg was brought before the Vodram Knight of the Twisting Meadow, Kreena Longfellow, that answers were found. Kreena demanded the egg from Varys, who's affinity for psuedodragons stems back to a herd his father and him hunted in the Eavallan Forest of Vodram. The skull of one of the beasts still hanging from his belt as a reminder of his father and home. More so than the egg, this skull was demanded by Kreena, as this skull belonged to one of her brood, however upon closer inspection, the egg did not. She directed Varys to entrust the egg to someone from Mardov as the egg did not seem able to bond with him. Fen Darkeyes was the obvious next choice, and when Mehen hatched in his arms beneath a tree which grew from the body of the Vodram Serpent Ananta, Fen was declared worthy of taking up the mantle of the Knight of the Twisting Meadow. The sapphire scaled dragon immediately took a liking to the human ranger, however how many of her brothers and sisters are still held by the yuan-ti? What does the Knight of the Twisting Meadow have to do with the snake-people slavers? Can a Knight of the Twisting Meadows from Mardov work with a Knight of the Dusken Wood from Vodram?
